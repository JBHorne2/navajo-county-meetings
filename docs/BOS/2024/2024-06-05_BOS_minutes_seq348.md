NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, June 5, 2024

Present:
Fern Benally, Vice-Chair; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor, District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V (all appearing virtual)

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:00 a.m. Call To Order:   The meeting was called to order by Chairman Whiting .

NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT Board of Directors Session :
Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6 of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire restrictions beginning June 6, 2024, at 8:00 a.m. :
Kymberle O'Farrell discussed the item. Ms. O'Farrell stated that the White Mountain Fire Coordination Group met earlier this week and has concluded that the conditions outlined in the White Mountain Fire Coordination Group operating plan warrant going into Stage 1 Fire Restrictions. She stated that across the region there have been several human-caused fires as well as a few lightning fires and, in addition, forest fuel conditions are drying out with minimal relative humidity and temperatures are warming up. Ms. O'Farrell stated that at this time, Emergency Management staff were recommending approval of the declaration and moving into Stage 1 Fire Restrictions beginning June 6 at 8:00 a.m.

Supervisor Benally asked if there were any fires in Navajo County. Ms. O'Farrell stated that there are fires in the Apache Sitgreaves Forest and off.

Supervisor Benally made a motion  to approve a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6 of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire restrictions beginning June 6, 2024, at 8:00 a.m., motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :  At 9:05 a.m. the meeting adjourned.

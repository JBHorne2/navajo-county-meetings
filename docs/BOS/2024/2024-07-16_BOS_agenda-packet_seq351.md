Submitted By: Bryan Cook, Sr. Engineering Project Manager II

Presented By: Bryan Cook
Motion before the Board:
PUBLIC HEARING: Sale of public right-of-way and authorization of the Chair to sign the
warranty deed.
Background:
The sale involves abandoned public right-of-way, which is part of a process initiated after
the Board of Supervisors approved ABAN 23-02.
Specifically, this pertains to an un-used stub road within the Scott’s Pine Meadows #2
subdivision.
The abandonment was approved with stipulations outlined in Resolution Number
01-2024.
Stipulations for Completion and Sale:  
Fair Market Appraisal:  
The fair market value of the abandoned right-of-way is appraised at $5,600.
The decision considers the difference in value between adjacent properties with
and without the abandoned right-of-way.
The appraisal was conducted by a third-party Certified Residential Real Estate
Appraiser.
1.
Legal Description:  
A surveyor contracted by the applicant created a legal description, which is
included as part of the warranty deed.
2.
Warranty Deed: 
The warranty deed is part of the agenda item.
3.
Plat Amendment: 
A separate agenda item addresses the plat amendment.
4.
Additional Information: 
Public notice was posted and mailed in accordance with A.R.S.§28-7204.
The applicant, an adjacent property owner, is the sole party expressing interest in
purchasing.
Public Works contacted the only other adjacent property, which expressed no interest.
No additional comments or interested parties have contacted Public Works.

of this Staff Report.

REQUEST DESCRIPTION:
A request for a Final Plat Amendment to allow a portion of abandoned right-of-way to be combined into
Assessor’s Parcel Number 212-10-104 in the Scott’s Pine Meadows #2 Subdivision.
GENERAL CHARACTER OF NEIGHBORHOOD:
The Scott’s Pine Meadows #2 Subdivision is located in the Wagon Wheel area, east of State Route 260.
Properties in this subdivision are a mixture of single and multiple-family zoned, with a variety of single
and multiple-family residences.

<!-- page 18 -->
Board of Supervisors          P a g e2 | 
BACKGROUND/HISTORY:
 June 1st, 1964: Scott’s Pine Meadows Unit Two Subdivision was originally approved by the Navajo
County Board of Supervisors.
 January 9th, 2023: Conditional abandonment of Right-of-Way was granted in Scott’s Pine Meadows
Unit Two Subdivision by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors.
 One condition of this abandonment was that a Plat Amendment be acquired that corrected the
stub road that was abandoned to be private property.
o Due to this requirement, the applicant is bringing forward this Final Plat Amendment
proposal to the Navajo County Board of Supervisors.
o
PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
The proposed project, if approved, would combine the approximately 0.13-acre section of conditionally
abandoned Right-of-Way into APN # 212-10-104, which is currently 0.26-acres in size.
ZONING DISTRICTS:
ARTICLE 14 – (R-3) MULTIPLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL ZONING DISTRICT
Section 1401 – Purpose.
The principal purpose of this zoning district is to provide for multiple-family housing, along with
certain transient residential and non-residential neighborhoods in locations which are suitable
and appropriate taking into consideration existing conditions, including present use of land,
future land use needs and the availability of public utilities.  The minimum parcel size required
is six thousand (6,000) square feet in area and the minimum area required for each dwelling unit
is one thousand (1,000) square feet.  Principal uses permitted in this zoning district include high-
density housing, hospitals and institutions of an educational, religious, charitable, or
philanthropic nature, private clubs and fraternal organizations, resort hotels, Manufactured home
subdivisions and Manufactured Home Parks.
COMPREHENSIVE PLAN:
Comprehensive Plan
Land Use Element
Goal 9: Actively plan to accommodate growth in Navajo County in an appropriate manner.
Policy 9-D: Encourage in-fill opportunities in the County to capitalize on existing
infrastructure and reduce growth-related costs.
 This proposal would allow for in-fill within the Scotts Pine Meadows #2
Subdivision, by making unutilized that has no viable future usage into right-
of-way into private property.

<!-- page 19 -->
Board of Supervisors          P a g e3 | 
Analysis:
This request for a Final Plat Amendment meets the goals and policies of the Navajo County
Comprehensive Plan, as described in this Staff Report.  This proposal would allow for in-fill on property
that is not currently being utilized.
The subject portion of Right-of-Way does not currently have a purpose and will not have a purpose in
the future, due to how the Elk Springs Subdivision to the north was designed.  This portion of Right-of-
Way was intended to connect to future developments to the north.  Directly to the north of this Right-of-
Way is a lot, which already contains a home.  Due to this, the Right-of-Way no longer provides any
potential for connectivity between these developments.
As of July 3rd, 2024, Planning and Development Services Staff have not received any comments in favor
of, nor against, the proposal.
FINDINGS OF FACT:
1. The item has been properly noticed and posted in compliance with Arizona Revised Statutes
and Article 29.
This item was posted in the June 26th, 2024 edition of The Holbrook Tribune.  Postcards were mailed
to neighbors within 300 feet of the property.
2. The proposal is in compliance with the Comprehensive Plan.
The proposal meets all relevant goals and policies within Navajo County’s Comprehensive Plan, as
noted within this report.
3. The proposed use is compatible with the current and likely future uses of properties in the
vicinity of the proposed use.
This proposal is compatible with all nearby uses.
4. The proposed use will have minimal adverse effects on the public health, safety, and general
welfare of properties in the vicinity of the proposed use.
There should be minimal adverse effects on the public health, safety, and general welfare of
properties in the vicinity of the proposal.

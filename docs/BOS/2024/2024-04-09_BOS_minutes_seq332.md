SHERIFF :

a.

Consideration of a proclamation declaring the week beginning April 14th through April 20th, 2024, as "National Telecommunicators Week" in Navajo County, Arizona :
Sheriff David Clouse introduced Caitlyn Montoya, the Lead Dispatcher, indicating that she does a phenomenal job representing her staff and the profession.  He stated that she has worked hard for the members of her team to be awarded benefits that include them as first responders, indicating that it is the dispatchers voice that keeps many of the deputies safe.  He talked about how the dispatchers always know their location, know the type of situation they are in, and that if further assistance is needed, they are there for you.  He stated that it is comforting to have them and that they couldn't do this type of work without their dedication and service.  He stated that when there is a critical incident, a natural disaster or serious law enforcement call Ms. Montoya and her team come in and ensure that they have the communication needed and that they don't go without that service.  He indicated that last month the dispatchers received 3850 administration calls or non-emergency calls and further received 1398 9-1-1 emergency calls, stating that they have to generate those calls, find the resource, and dispatch the call and then stay of the line to assist with giving CPR over the line or giving advice to keep people safe.  He read the proposed proclamation.  Sheriff Clouse stated that the Navajo County Sheriff's Office could not run without our telecommunicators and requested approval of the proclamation.
Supervisor Seymore stated that this is fantastic, indicating that the dispatchers are their voice, their eyes, and their comfort. He stated that the dispatch centers are important and a big key to the safety in our communities.  He thanked Ms. Montoya and her team for all they do.
Supervisor Whitesinger stated that having the proclamations come forward reminds us of some of the things that we take for granted.  She indicated that she is glad that Sheriff Clouse has taken the time to recognize Ms. Montoya and her team and thanked her for her service.
Vice-Chairwoman Benally thanked Ms. Montoya for her service and spoke about the importance of the profession and the many ways that they assist citizens.

Vice-Chairwoman Benally made a motion  to approve a proclamation declaring the week beginning April 14th through April 20th, 2024, as "National Telecommunicators Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore.
Chairman Whiting inquired about training that is receive for this profession regarding emotional strength. He stated that this is an important profession and that he is grateful for Ms. Montoya and her team.
Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Peshlakai being absent.

BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :

a.

Discussion and action regarding a Petition for Review of Proposed Correction Real Property APN 202-18-003Q; Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :
Cammy Darris, Assessor and Joseph Holland, Petitioner, were sworn in by Melissa Buckley.
Joseph Holland stated that he recalls a proposition several years ago that said the value couldn't be grown more than five percent year to year.  He indicated that the assessed value on his property has gone up over twenty-five percent last year and again this year, so he asked for a review.  He talked about some comparable properties in the area and stated that the Assessor's Office indicated that they don't consider it comparable due to foundation issues.  He indicated that due to the huge growth in the assessed value be understand it to be ground to be reassessed.  He stated that there was a minor land adjustment between his and neighboring properties that dealt almost exclusively with the road due to not wanting to be landlocked.  He indicated that he is asking for a review, indicating the higher assessment was not warranted and that it's not keeping with the five percent increase that was supposed to be the historical increase.  He talked about the amount it cost to build his home in 2019, indicating that he understands that the prices have increased but that he thinks the assessed value is high.
Cammy Darris indicated that the home is 5,054 square feet on 1.84 acres located in Snowflake on Crestview Drive. She stated that in Mr. Hollands petition he indicated that the value can't increase more than five percent year to year and that the boundary adjustment that was requested only affected a small area and does not justify the change in assessment as required by statute.  She indicated that she set up a meeting with the Petitioner but received no response, so they answered the petition. She spoke about the change being $485, indicating that the land value was 65,302 and went to 65,787 with the improvement value staying the same.  She stated that the limited property value, which is used for tax purposes, stayed the same. She indicated that the limited value is what is capped at 5% but that the full cash value is synonymous with market and can go up depending on what the market does.  She talked about the comparables that were provided and stated that she provided the statuses for the limited cash value and full cash value, as well as the information regarding the cracks in the other home.  She requested that the FCV remain at $789,633 and the LV at $399,893.
Ms. Darris answered questions from the Board discussing the statutes, limited value, and full cash value.
Mr. Holland asked that his petition be withdrawn, indicating that he had misunderstood and thought that this would affect his taxes going up.
Ms. Darris clarified that her office has no control over the tax amounts, that their valuation will not increase but they can't control the budgets and tax rates.

Chairman Whiting indicated that the petition has been withdrawn.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Peshlakai being absent.

Consideration of a Contract for Professional Services with HDR Engineering, Inc., for Engineering Consultant Contract B23-02-015 for Task 3 Winslow Levee Survey :
William Flake stated that this contract is for an engineering consultant for the Winslow Levee and that the purpose is to perform survey work for the Winslow Levee Design.

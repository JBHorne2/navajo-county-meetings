a.

Presentation and update from Heber-Overgaard Community Resource Network :
A presentation was shown.  John Piva, a Board of Director for HOCRN, stated that the Heber-Overgaard Community Resource Network is a non-profit 501c3 serving the Heber-Overgaard residents and visitors, that it is fully operated by volunteers, that they also operate a thrift store and a community van.  He stated that they were established in 2022 by former Fire Chief McCluskey and local Realtor, Jane Call, that they were originally established to handle emergency and non-emergency requests from the fire and sheriff departments and that their Mission Statement is "To provide coordination of community resources for Heber-Overgaard residents and visitors".  He talked about taking over an existing Thrift store, indicating that it is run fully by volunteers.  He further talked about their community garden project, indicating that they had a local gardening expert as well as two master gardeners who offered guidance when they first started, that they have a twenty-year facility use agreement with the Heber-Overgaard schools for the location, that the space allows for growth and that the volunteers built the garden from the ground up.  He addressed the funds that have been raised from the garden and where the funds came from.  He talked about what all the garden consists of, being able to become a Friend of the Garden (FOG) and the benefits that membership provides and some of the classes that are offered at the garden.  He introduced the members of the HOCRN Board of Directors and talked about the services they provide to the community.  He talked about the future plans for HOCRN.  He talked about their logo, indicating that the wagon wheel represents all of their resources.  He reviewed the statistics for HOCRN for 2023.  He talked about the different ways that the organization can be contacted.
Supervisor Seymore talked about the integrity of the HOCRN organization, indicating that they do what they say they'll do.  He stated that they are one of the greatest organizations that has been put together by people who care about people. He indicated that Heber-Overgaard is the gate to Navajo County.  He talked about the many accomplishments in the community such as the High School Football Team State Championships, the fantastic basketball program, and the community programs for the Senior Center, Fire Department and Chamber of Commerce.  He talked about their 4 th of July and Holiday festivities, indicating that there are no city funds, it is just the citizens who love their community and are doing things on their own to give back to their community. He talked about the need for a program to help the communities, such as Heber-Overgaard, that are doing things to improve on their own to improve the communities who don't receive city/town funds.  He talked about the different requests where help can be provided to give back and help make the community better.  Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Piva and the HOCRN Board for what they do.
Supervisor Whitesinger thanked the HOCRN Board members for being present today and for sharing the work that they do. She spoke of the work that they do and commended each of them for their care and dedication to their community and being willing to give back in so many ways.
Chairman Whiting thanked the HOCRN Board for their service and indicated that this is what we should aspire to do and be in our communities.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 01-2024 , granting a Right-of-Way Abandonment of an unused stub road between lots 214 & 215 and North from the northern edge of right-of way for Wild Game Trail :
William Flake discussed the item indicating that Public Works received an application for an abandonment of a stub road due to there not being a need for an access point through the subdivision.  He stated that this has been advertised and Public Works did not receive any information back either for or against this item. He stated that staff is recommending approval.
Supervisor Seymore indicated that it is his understanding that the two property owners are in agreement to purchase the right of way so there isn't a dispute in the future.
Mr. Flake stated that they are anticipating that and if or when they decide to purchase that, it would come before the Board of Supervisors for the approval.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 01-2024 , granting a Right-of-Way Abandonment of an unused stub road between lots 214 & 215 and North from the northern edge of right-of way for Wild Game Trail, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

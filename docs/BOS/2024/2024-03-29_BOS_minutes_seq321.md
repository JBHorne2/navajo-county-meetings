NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, March 29, 2024

Present:
Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V (all appearing virtual)

Absent:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager (appearing virtual); Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney (appearing virtual); Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Rayleen Richards, Elections Director

8:30 a.m . Call To Order:   Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of the Official Canvass of the results from the March 19, 2024, Presidential Preference Election :
Rayleen Richards requested that the Official Canvass be approved.
Chairman Whiting stated that he noticed that both parties were present for the hand count verification.
Ms. Richards stated that they were and that the hand count matched the machine.

Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the result from the March 19, 2024, Presidential Preference Election, motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Chairman Whiting thanked Elections for the great work they are doing.

2.

ADJOURN: 8:32 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason Whiting, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

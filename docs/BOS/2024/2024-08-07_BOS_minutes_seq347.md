NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 7, 2024

Present:
Fern Benally, Vice-Chair; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V (all appearing virtual)

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney, all appearing virtual; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:00 a.m . Call To Order:   Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration and approval of the Official Canvass of the results for the July 30, 2024, Primary Election :
Rayleen Richards gave a report to the Board of Supervisors on the successful Primary Election held on July 30 in Navajo County. Mrs. Richards stated that out of 66,207 registered voters, 22,637 ballots were cast with a voter turnout of 34.19%. She further explained that 15,072 residents participated in Early Voting and 7,424 residents voted in-person on Election Day, with 141 Provisional Ballots. She indicated that as required by State Statute, a hand count was completed on Saturday after the election of 1% of Early Ballots processed and all ballots from 2 randomly selected vote centers. She reported 100% accuracy after verifying and justification of the machine count with the hand count. She further reported that the Democratic and Republican party chairs supplied the 9 volunteers to help perform the hand count. Mrs. Richards thanked Navajo County Recorder Michael Sample and his staff for the many hours they worked to process all the early ballots by checking voter registration and verifying signatures. She also thanked her Elections staff that worked over 100 hours during Elections week making sure they had the supplies ready and polling places staffed. She commented that her and her staff worked 22 hours straight on Election Day. She further thanked the Volunteers, Poll Workers, and the coordination and support provided by the Democrat and Republican Party Chairs. Mrs. Richards also thanked the Navajo County Sheriff's Office which assigned Deputies to watch over the Elections Office on Elections Day and night and into the next day. She reported that    26 Deputies were assigned to pick up ballots from all the different Vote Centers to ensure their safe and secure transport to Holbrook. Mrs. Richards indicated that Navajo County received a list of questions regarding some voters who casted provisional ballots. She reported that upon investigation, they did find one provisional ballot that had some confusion regarding the last name which caused the ballot to not be tallied originally.  She indicated that they were able to perform further verification and reported that the ballot has been processed and the canvass presented today had been updated to reflect the one additional ballot being counted. Mrs. Richards reported that other than the one exception, the circumstances surrounding each provisional ballot in question had been reviewed by the Recorders Office, the Elections Department, and the County Attorney's Office and that they are confident that the ballots were handled correctly. She went on to report that the investigation confirmed that the systems and safeguards that they have in place are working to prevent double counting, voting more than once, and ensured that only registered Navajo County residents voted.

Jason Moore addressed a couple of questions that came up yesterday regarding voters in suspended status that were required to cast provisional ballots and a request for potential prosecution. Mr. Moore spoke about voter registration and residency of voters. He indicated that the voters address is based on what ballot style they get. He further indicated that if we do not have voters address, we put them in a suspended status and then send them a letter letting them know that we do not have information on their address and need that information before the registration deadline for them to be able to vote. Mr. Moore reported that we did have several voters in the suspended status at the time of the Primary Election, and some of those voters received a provisional ballot. He further reported that some of those voters had addresses outside of Navajo County and other individuals had no address information. Mr. Moore addressed the concern from a citizen regarding the question of criminal prosecution for voters that submitted an early ballot and then went to the polls and were required to cast a provisional ballot. Mr. Moore indicated that our systems work, as the provisional ballot was not counted because the early ballot had already been counted. Mr. Moore further reported that he went through each of the questions raised by the member of the public and we found one that was then counted because of a confusion of the voter's last name. Mr. Moore recommended that the Board of Supervisors approve the official canvass. Jose Lerma, Voter Registration's Office, discussed the number of ballots requested by each political party.

Supervisor Seymore thanked Rayleen for the presentation and all the work, time spent and the accuracy of voting. He further thanked her for answering questions that citizens had as well. Supervisor Seymore asked what two randomly selected vote centers were completed and how many ballots were cast at those vote centers. Mrs. Richards reported that Sun Valley had 24 ballots cast and Show Low Gym had 724 ballots cast. Supervisor Whitesinger sent her appreciation to all those that helped and for further investigating the questions that came in.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve the Official Canvass of the results for the July 30, 2024, Primary Election, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Chairman Whiting expressed his gratitude to Mike Sample and the Recorders Office, Rayleen and her team, the many volunteers in the County and County employees, and the party leadership that helped in this election cycle. He stated that a tremendous job was done. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :   9:27 a.m. meeting adjourned.

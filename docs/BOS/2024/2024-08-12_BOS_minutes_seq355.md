NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 12, 2024

Present:
Fern Benally, Vice-Chair; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Absent:
Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Michael Sample, Recorder, all appearing virtual; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Donna Hurstrom, Chief Deputy Recorder

9:04 a.m . Call To Order:   Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order.

RECORDER :

a.

Consideration of an amendment to the Canvass of the July 30, 2024, Primary Election, originally canvassed by the Board of Supervisors on Aug. 7, 2024, to include one provisional ballot that, upon further investigation, was accepted :
Donna Hurstrom stated that they had one voter who was directed to vote by provisional ballot due to the address of his driver's license not matching his voter registration records.  She indicated that when the ballot was reviewed by the Recorder's Office, that all the voter's parameters were entered into the search field based on the provisional form but that it did not produce a match, resulting in the ballot being rejected.  She stated that after further review it was determined that the form and the voter's registration record had a one number difference for the driver's license.  She further stated that the ballot was then accepted, which resulted in the need to re-canvass the election.  She indicated that the Recorder's Office also reviewed all the rejected provisional ballots to ensure that they were rejected appropriately.

Chairman Whiting thanked the Recorder's Office for taking the time to prove that every vote counts and matters.  He indicated that it is important to make sure that every vote counts and shared his appreciation for it being verified.

Supervisor Peshlakai  made a motion  to approve an amendment to the Canvass of the July 30, 2024, Primary Election, originally canvassed by the Board of Supervisors on Aug. 7, 2024, to include one provisional ballot that, upon further investigation, was accepted, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0 with Supervisor Daryl Seymore being absent.

ADJOURN : 9:07 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason Whiting, Chairman

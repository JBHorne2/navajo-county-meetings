REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

COVID-19 Update : Janelle Linn indicated that COVID cases are starting to surge.  She stated that last week there were 169 new non-tribal related cases reported to the Navajo County Health Nurses, which was an increase from the prior week of 128 cases.  She further stated that the total cases in July were 304, to date in August we are at 502 cases and that there were 47 new cases yesterday.  She indicated that they are experiencing a higher percentage of case in younger individuals. She stated that fifty percent of the cases were in people 40 years old and younger and that twenty-five percent were in children eighteen and younger.  She advised that last week sixty-six percent of the cases were in 40 years old and younger and twenty-five percent were eighteen and younger.  She indicated that they are still seeing a high percentage of cases linked to schools, that of the 47 cases yesterday eleven of them were linked to a school.  She stated that there were five new hospitalizations yesterday.  She indicated that they are still encouraging the vaccine.  She advised that the FDA announced yesterday that they have given full approval for the Pfizer vaccine for anyone 16 and older.  She stated that Summit has shared some concerns regarding the number of hospitalizations they have been receiving and that they are encouraging people, if they choose, to practice mitigation measures when possible by washing your hands, cleaning solid surfaces, consider wearing a mask in large groups, covering your cough and to stay home if they are feeling sick, even if they think it's just allergies.  She stated that when contact tracing there have had several people report that when it started, they felt like it was allergy symptoms.  She further stated that Summit shared that some of the reasons they are at or near capacity is due to staff shortages both local and nationally, that there is an increase in all types of hospital admissions, that respiratory conditions are high this year, that people put off health care in 2020 and have been re-entering the healthcare system so there are more elective surgeries or that they have had a decline in their health.  She indicated that they have a high increase in COVID admissions, and that people are much sicker due to delta variant making hospital stays longer.
Chairman Seymore stated that he appreciates Ms. Linn and her staff for all they do.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

COVID-19 Update :  Janelle Linn stated 324 cases were reported last week, compared to 244 the week prior and that 62 of those were vaccinated.  She stated that the vaccinated individuals have only experiencing mild symptoms.  She indicated that there have been nineteen new hospitalizations reported, with there being eight reported the week prior, that there were two deaths reported last week and two reported the week before.  She advised that sixty-nine of the cases have been related to schools compared to twenty-four the week prior and that they are seeing a spread in the school systems.  She stated that the average age for the cases reported is forty-four.  She advised that so far for this week there have been eight-three new cases reported, one new hospitalization, four deaths and fourteen cases related to a school district.  She indicated that the higher cases continue to be in the lower vaccinated areas.  She stated that the monthly case counts for off-tribal land have been 344 cases in July, 760 cases in August; 699 cases in September and 986 cases in October so far.  She encouraged that people stay home if they are feeling sick and indicated that a lot of individuals have stated that they were experiencing mild allergy symptoms and that it is turning out to be COVID.  She stated that they have been updating the numbers on the Navajo County website COVID-19 dashboard on Mondays.  She advised that the vaccination rate for Navajo County on off-tribal lands is thirty-eight percent, that they have been seeing a decrease through most of the Country but that here they are still seeing an increase and that they are still encouraging people to get vaccinated.  She addressed the new recommendations for booster doses with Moderna and Johnson & Johnson and the recommendations for the Pfizer booster.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote passed 4-0, approving the motion, with Supervisor Benally being absent.

WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKES COUNTY RECREATION IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :   Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to enter into the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote passed 4-0, approving the motion, with Supervisor Benally being absent.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Quarterly Report and Update :  Mark Honberger addressed the numbers from the Labor Day Weekend and indicated that it was a safe and great weekend.  He advised that there next meeting the week prior to Thanksgiving at the Silver Creek Golf Club and invited the Board to attend.  He spoke about the water levels, indicating that early summer it was below the level and then they had the extreme flooding which brought the water up 15 feet in 24 hours but that it didn't go over the overflow level.  He advised that the levels have gone down and that Silver Creek Water District had been using the water for agriculture purposes, they are done using the water for this season and that they will start building up the levels for spring.  He addressed the work that has been completed on the dock floats that had been failing, he indicated that the parking lot lines have been re-measured, giving then an additional four spaces, he stated that they added a mooring that is not on the dock, and that they have replaced some buoys, cables and weights.  He talked about Carr Circle which has playground and basketball court and indicated that they have done some upkeep in that neighborhood and added a picnic table.  He addressed the newsletters and the Facebook postings to increase communication with the community.  He stated that they have implemented the kayak rentals, that they currently have two single kayaks but plan to purchase a double kayak and paddle board for next year.  He stated that they have started wearing staff shirts.  He indicated that they have a weather station at the office now and that the information is on the website.  He talked about the vacation rental placard program that has been implemented again for guests using rentals.  He stated that they have started using the membership software.  He indicated that all visitors have had a safe and fun time at the lake this past season.  He addressed the projects that are still outstanding such as the reminder signs regarding lake safety, painting the curbs, putting gravel on the parking lot, putting disc golf baskets at Carr Circle, inputting swim cove markers, replacing the bark in the playground, having a camp host, installing a lake webcam and the historical sites for the website.  He talked about having an option for credit card payments, the boater handout cards and indicated that they are continuing to work on concessionaire agreements for next year and bringing in some recreational activities.  He addressed the community feedback regarding visitors at the lake, public meetings, emailing response, the website and Facebook posts.  He addressed the efforts of Supervisor Whiting has been working towards with the State Legislators.

Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Honberger for his work on addressing the concerns of the residents.  He inquired what the process is for tracking and resolving the concerns that are brought forward.
Mr. Honberger addressed the process of addressing the concerns.
Supervisor Whiting addressed the Bill that was brought forward for getting a local board that consists of residence of the district but indicated that it didn't pass.  He indicated that they are working on getting it brought forward again.
Mr. Honberger indicated that he will be addressing the Bill at the upcoming meeting and indicated that if there is any additional information that can be provided, he will pass it on.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed her appreciation to Mr. Honberger and Supervisor Whiting for their advocacy for the community.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote passed 4-0, approving the motion, with Supervisor Benally being absent.

He talked about the movie Dear Evan Hansen which addresses mental health challenges.  He indicated that it is something that we need to accept and work on as a community to help make things better, especially through social media.  He thanked Alicia Deets for the Newsletters that she puts out.  He expressed his appreciation to the County employees and to the Citizens of Navajo County for all that they do.  He stated that the City of Show Low will be doing a Community Fast, indicated that he appreciates the support that has been given by Navajo County for that event and that the money goes back to local charities and organizations.

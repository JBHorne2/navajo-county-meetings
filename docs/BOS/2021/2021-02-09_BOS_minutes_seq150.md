Presentation of 2020 Census "Thank You Campaign" for Navajo County and presentation to Bryan Cook for his Outstanding Leadership during the 2020 Census :  Glenn Kephart introduced Tammy Parise and addressed the Census count for Navajo County.  Tammy Parise addressed the Census.  A presentation was shown.  She advised that nationally sixty-seven percent of all household chose to self-respond, which is higher than they had projected and higher than in 2010.  She further advised that this was the first nationwide census that was available online, that eighty percent of the households chose to respond online but that they also had mail and phone responses available.  She indicated that there was no downtime in the internet self-response tool and that there were no data breaches.  She addressed the houses that chose not to respond on their own and the none response follow-up operation.  She indicated that COVID did change the way they worked and that it did set them back in terms of time.  She addressed counting people in nontraditional housing such as hospitals, nursing home and those incarcerated and indicated that they also counted the homeless.  She thanked Navajo County and Bryan Cook for working with their Partnership Specialists and stated that Navajo County is one of their gold partners.  She indicated that the State self-response rate for Arizona was 64.1% and that Navajo County was 34.6%.  She spoke about what to expect next.  She indicated that COVID did delay their ability to work through the data, that they didn't meet the deadline of December 31, 2020, but that they anticipate releasing the State data by April 30, 2021.  She addressed the Post Enumeration Survey, which is happening right now.
Chairman Seymore thanked Ms. Parise for all she did and thanked Mr. Cook for what he did locally.  He also thanked the citizens for their response and indicated that it was great that we had the turn out we did.
Glenn Kephart thanked Ms. Parise for her partnership.  He thanked Mr. Cook for stepping up and taking leadership during the Census and for working with our partners and our social media team.  He showed the plaque that will be presented to Mr. Cook.
Melissa Buckley thanked Mr. Cook for the wonderful job he did.  She stated that he was always able to get the answers when needed and that he had everything ready when needed.
Supervisor Whitesinger expressed her appreciation to Mr. Cook for his leadership and the work he did.
Bryan Cook stated that it was a lot of work and that he's glad it's only every ten years.

c.

Consideration of award of Contract B20-12-015 Redistricting Consultant Services to Federal Compliance Consulting, LLC .:  Jason Moore addressed the item.  He indicated that after the Census has been completed there is an obligation to re-balance the populations in each supervisorial district.  He indicated that when they went through the process in 2010 they hired out an outside consultant who helped to make things run smoothly and professionally.  He advised that they sent out a request for proposals, that there were a couple of different firms that submitted proposals and that those have been evaluated by a team.  He indicated that after the scoring was done, they are recommending Federal Compliance Consulting, LLC and that the principal of the company is Bruce Adelson, who they hired during the last redistricting.  He stated that some differences with this Census compared to the last is that they will not be getting the numbers until later in the process which will slow them down and that in 2010 they did a lot of community outreach, that they are not sure how it will be this year with COVID, but that they will figure out a way to work through it.  He further stated that in 2010 they had to seek approval from the Department of Justice for all the redistricting lines, that there was a case decided by the Supreme Court not to long after that stating that Section 5 of the Voting Rights Act don't apply unless Congress makes certain findings why they should be required in each jurisdiction, and so they are not required to do that right now.  He addressed Mr. Adelson's qualifications and background and indicated that hiring a consultant helps make the process more independent, and if they should ever be challenged in the redistricting process, they will be able to help in that regard.  He indicated that there is an outside contractor that Mr. Adelson's firm will be using for the GIS portion of this agreement.
Supervisor Whitesinger inquired if it is a requirement to contract with a company for redistricting.
Mr. Moore advised that it is a requirement under the law that the County has to do a redistricting every ten years to re-balance the population, that hiring a consultant isn't under the statute requirement but that it is something that most Arizona counties do and that we have done it in the past.
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve the award of Contract B20-12-015 Redistricting Consultant Services to Federal Compliance Consulting, LLC.; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .
Supervisor Peshlakai inquired if they would still need to appoint an Advisory Committee since they have a Consultant.
Mr. Moore indicated that they will still probably recommend an Advisory Committee and addressed the purpose of the Advisory Committee.
Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

COVID-19 Update :  Janelle Linn advised that the numbers continue to decline, that yesterday there were twelve new positive cases and that there were twenty-nine in the last 24 hours.  She indicated that this is a big improvement from having over a hundred positives cases per day.  She stated that they continue to see the same trend across Arizona and with their tribal partners.  She advised that there are 14,936 cases in Navajo County, that 9,736 of those cases have been worked by the Navajo County Public Health Nurses and that there have been 449 deaths in Navajo County.  She stated that there are three variants in the United States right now, one out of the U.K., one out of South Africa and one out of Brazil.  She further stated that so far in Arizona only the U.K. strain has been identified, but that in Navajo County they have not verified any variants.  She indicated that they are saying that the vaccines on the market in the United States are adequate to cover against these stains.  She stated that in South Africa they have been using the Johnson and Johnson product and that it is not highly effective against the South African strain so they have delayed the use of that vaccine and that they will be watching it.  She advised that the roll out and distributions of vaccines in Navajo County has been going well.  She addressed the number of vaccines given.  She stated that the main problem is not having enough vaccine, that they continue to have a shortage but that they have the resources in place to push them out once steady supplies are available.  She advised that they have a team on standby if they need them once they activate their Point of Dispensing Sites at the fire departments.  She further advised that they have eight PODS across Navajo County in Winslow, Joseph City, Holbrook, Snowflake, Heber/Overgaard, Pinetop and two in Show Low and that as soon as they are seeing steady allotments of three to four thousand doses a week they will be able to start the PODS and be able to sustain them.  She stated that she met with ACCCHS and that they have a plan in place to make sure they are considering the high-risk population, the developmentally delayed and long-term care facilities.  She advised that the long-term care facilities are being vaccinated by Walgreens which is going well and that they are working with organizations for the developmentally delayed.  She indicated that they have been able to do one on one training with the POD sites and their staff.  She stated that they appreciate all their partners.  She expressed her appreciation for her staff, indicated that they have been wonderful and continue to do a great job.  She advised that they have been participating with the Navajo Nation in their weekly calls and in some town halls. She indicated that the Apache Tribe partners have reached out this week and have a vaccine team that have volunteered to help if needed.  She stated that they appreciate Tribal partners, the communication they have with them and how their able to work together to serve the residents of Navajo County.
Supervisor Peshlakai expressed his appreciation to Ms. Linn and her team for their continued partnership with the Tribal communities.  He indicated that he appreciates the information provided at the weekly meetings.  He stated that they always go above and beyond for each other as well as provide necessary resources to patients and providers within the county.  He encouraged them to continue to strive for success during this time.
Chairman Seymore stated that we are one of the top five counties for distribution of the vaccine which is wonderful.  He indicated that shows a great effort for Ms. Linn's team.  He stated that we appreciate the patience from the citizens.  He further stated that we are thankful to have those who care about our community by putting forth these efforts.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, October 4, 2021

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:00 a.m . Call To Order:   Chairman Seymore called the meeting to order.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Consideration of and possible adoption of  Resolution Number 29-2021,  approving the sale and execution and delivery of pledged revenue obligations, in one or more series; approving the form and authorizing the execution and delivery of necessary agreements, instruments, and documents; delegating the authority to determine certain matters with respect to the foregoing and declaring an emergency :

Jayson Vowell discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. He further introduced the team from Stifel, Nicolaus & Company, Incorporated, who further answered questions from the Board.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 29-2021 , approving the sale and execution and delivery of pledged revenue obligations, in one or more series; approving the form and authorizing the execution and delivery of necessary agreements, instruments, and documents; delegating the authority to determine certain matters with respect to the foregoing and declaring an emergency, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger. A roll call vote was taken with each member voting in favor of the item. Item passed 5-0.

b.

Consideration of budget amendment in the amount of $17 million to fund payment of bond proceeds to PSPRS and transfer of excess budget capacity of $10 million from Fund 210626 - ARPA ; $5 million from Fund 110191 - Employee Benefits Trust; and $2 million from Fund 110101 - General Fund :

Jayson Vowell discussed the item. He advised the Board of the dollar amounts from contingency funds that would need to be transferred in order to move this forward.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the budget amendment in the amount of $17 million to fund payment of bond proceeds to PSPRS and transfer of excess budget capacity of $10 million from Fund 210626 - ARPA ; $5 million from Fund 110191 - Employee Benefits Trust; and $2 million from Fund 110101 - General Fund, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, asked for permission for Supervisor Peshlakai to sign the documents in the agenda items on behalf of Chairman Seymore. Chairman Seymore indicated that due to timeframe he approved Supervisor Peshlakai signing the documents on his behalf.

ADJOURN :  9:14 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Daryl Seymore, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

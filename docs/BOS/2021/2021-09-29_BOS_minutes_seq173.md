NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, September 29, 2021

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Janelle Linn, Health Director; Jayson Vowell, Finance Director; Rochelle Lacapa, Government Relations Director; Ken Maruyama, Strategic Initiatives Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board

9:00 a.m . Call To Order: Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance was led by Bryan Layton.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Continuing Forward from 2019 Retreat :  Glenn Kephart welcomed everyone and spoke about the previous retreat and how important it was for setting the direction for moving forward.  He talked about the changes in the team and the great success.
Bryan Layton talked about the previous retreat and the results that came from it.  He talked about the concerns from the previous retreat such as economic growth and the unfunded PSPRS liability.   He talked about the accomplishments over the past couple of years.  He stated that they agreed there was need to focus on was infrastructure and that they have been able to focus on them over the past couple of years. He addressed employee investment through compensation and health care.  He talked about the pay increases that were given pre-recession and indicated that they are able to start doing the pay increases again.  Mr. Kephart commented on the mindset of the employees when the 2.5% decrease was done.  Mr. Layton stated that one thing they are focusing on is longevity and performance pay.  He addressed the adjustments for the market going rate by making adjustments to pay and training.  He indicated that it is a work in progress for the retention with different departments.  He stated that they are working on having levels for being able to move up.  He talked about the communication with social media.
Janelle Linn addressed the environmental health program issues during Covid.  She stated that with the two new added positions they are now able to be up to date with program.
Mr. Layton stated that the Boards support means a lot and is appreciated.
Supervisor Whiting inquired about the contract for working with the car dealerships in our area.
Mr. Layton addressed the process for purchasing vehicles in the past and the current process.  Mr. Kephart stated that the local dealers who participated in the bid process were awarded a contract.  Mr. Layton indicated that the only dealer that didn't participate was Horne Chevy.  He stated that aside from the Sheriff's, all vehicles are purchased through local dealers.  He talked about the contract and indicated that next year the bid process will start again. He stated that with the consistent purchasing it has been a good working with the dealers for repairs and warranty work.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that it has been a good prospective to see the growth from where we have been to now.  She indicated that it is important to be financial responsible.

b.

Update on Broadband :  Glenn Kephart indicated that Magellan will provide an update and indicated that it is getting close to the end of the process.  He addressed the completive grant that we have been selected for and are still in the running for.
Melanie Downing gave an update of what they have done with the project for broadband.  She addressed the other providers that they worked with.  She talked about some of the information that they have found with existing fiber lines.  She addressed the new planned fiber projects that are in the works both local and in the region.  She talked about the broadband providers and wireless/satellite companies in the area and what they offer.  She advised that with the needs assessment they talked to many people in area about what their needs are for public safety, healthcare, education/homeschooling, working from home along with having homeschool, economic development and making sure business have what they need to operate and new residents in area that have come in since COVID .   She addressed the survey that was put out and what the results were from the survey, stating that 842 homes and 92 businesses participated.  She advised that some of them participants were satisfied, some were unsatisfied due to price and speed and many were unsatisfied with the service.  She addressed the speed of the service for those who participated, and she provided an overview of the type of connection that people had, 42% having cable, 23% used DSL and only 1% had broadband.  She talked about the locations of those who completed the surveys and the speeds in those areas.  She stated that for the most part the county is seeing low speed and that most are seeing less than 24 per second.  She addressed the gap analysis, indicated that there is fiber available, but it is limited, speeds are not meeting thresholds, there are outages, there is no competition for broadband options and many people are dissatisfied with speed, price and reliability.  She addressed the progress of the high-level design that they are working on and who all it would benefit by increased available service options, connecting county facilities and healthcare, and last mile service.  She highlighted the areas that they are working on high level designs.  She indicated possible partners to work with NACOG, Apache County, Gila County, Hopi Telecom, Navajo Nation, and other coordination with permits/agencies that are also working on broadband expansion. She talked about funding opportunities and the tentative timelines for those funds.  She went through the final/next steps and indicated that they are planning to get the final design by October, get final cost estimate, start to phase on buildout with grant scheduled and to complete and adopt a strategic plan.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired what the next steps would be.
Ms. Downing addressed the next steps and indicated that we need to start coordinating with towns that will have fiber through them and neighboring counties.
Glenn Kephart stated that before getting to that point there will need to be more discussion with the Board as to what the projects could look like, partnership opportunities and funding but that there is still work to be done.  He addressed the neighboring counties positions with partnerships on some of the projects so we can work together.  He advised that staff is having discussions with Magellan on the next steps for future contracts.
A discussion was held on the next steps and possible partnerships.
Jory Wolf addressed SR77 being the backbone for Navajo county to provide access to county facilities but also underserved areas along that route.  He indicated that they will soon have the recommendations for the phased roll out which will include opportunities for other grants.
Rochelle Lacapa inquired if the White Mountain Apache Tribe could be included in the build out plans to know what our tribal partners are doing.
Melanie Downing indicated that they are working with the White Mountain Apache Tribe along with Apache County and that they will incorporate that into high level design.  She further indicated that they are still in planning stages, so they don't have a lot of information to share at this time.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that she appreciates the work and time.
Melanie Downing and Jory Wolf with Magellan left the meeting.
A dissuasion was held regarding the possible partnerships, grant opportunities and a future contract with Magellan.

10:00 a.m. meeting was recessed.
10:21 a.m. meeting resumed.

c.

Legislative Update :  Rochelle Lacapa stated that the infrastructure bill is in flux, that they had hoped to have on the docket this week and that they are not sure when it will be. He indicated that they could be looking at a month before moving forward on it.  She stated that Supervisor Whiting is working on the 4FRI RFP cancellation.  She talked about working with James Candland on Legislative issues, indicated that they will be inviting State Delegates to the Board of Supervisors Meeting on November 9, 2021 and do a Legislative meeting/lunch afterwards.  She advised that she will be requesting help with sending out the invitations to the State Delegates, indicated that she will send a letter and then follow up with a call to staff to make sure there are as many present as possible.   She indicated that she will be working on local legislation for the White Mountain Lakes proposal to get the right language to be able to move forward at the beginning of the session.  She talked about sending proposals out to the Department Heads and Elected Officials for their positions on new legislation that will be addressed and indicated that she hopes this will be helpful when going through the CSA proposals tomorrow.
Glenn Kephart addressed the first proposal and indicated that Janelle Linn and Dr. Kartchner have expressed high opposition to it.
Janelle Linn addressed the concerns that they have with this bill indicated that it is not that it won't work but that it is not best for our County.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired if there is flexibility for discussion with the language.
Mr. Kephart indicated that there is, that it will be addressed on Thursday afternoon and that the proposal can be changed.
Supervisor Whiting addressed the best practice for presentations and changes.
A discussion was held regarding the purpose of the bill, how it could work for some Counties but how it is not good for Navajo County, ATRA'S positions and the current contracts/practices that Navajo County has in place
A discussion held regarding the different positions of the other Bills that will be addressed.

d.

ARPA Update and Discussion : Glenn Kephart addressed how Rochelle Lacapa has taken the lead with ARPA and how the team that has come together to work on the ARPA initiative.  He thanked her for all she has done.
Ken Maruyama thanked everyone for bringing him on the team and indicated that he is excited to be here and that he looks forward to working with everyone.
Rochelle Lacapa provided an updated of the vetting process and where they plan to be in the next couple of months.  She talked about the timeline for the funds/projects. She reviewed the guiding principles for vetting the projects, the county pillars, the final approved vetting process and the form that was sent out for the vetting process.  She talked about the project form that the team is using to determine if the projects should be forwarded to the Board of Supervisors as an eligible project.  She indicated that they are still getting projects and their review process for all submitted projects.  She indicated that the projects that are listing as eligible are not guaranteed to be funded that they are just saying they are eligible.
Glenn Kephart talked about how they plan to work through the eligible projects to be able to determine which projects should be funded is similar to how the budget process works.  He indicated that staff is still working through the process.
Ms. Lacapa stated that she feels it is important for the Board to see all of the submitted projects, not just the ones that are eligible.  She provided an overview of the received projects, indicating that they have received fifty-one proposals, that there are eleven eligible projects, five ineligible, four reserve projects, one partially funded project and thirty that they need additional information on.
Mr. Kephart addressed not having set a deadline for the submittals and indicated that they are requesting guidance for a cut-off date but that if a good idea comes in they can go to the board with the request.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that in either December or January it should be cut-off, when they present it to the Board.
Ms. Lacapa addressed having a date before December or January so it can go to the Board for a funding determination at that time.  She reviewed all of the projects that have been submitted and where they fit in the pillars.
A discussion was held regarding the possible projects.
Ms. Lacapa addressed the ineligible and reserve projects.
A discussion was held on pillars that have not received submittals.
Supervisor Benally inquired if broadband for the students would qualify as a project.
Ms. Lacapa addressed the matter.
Supervisor Benally inquired as to the total cost of the proposed projects to date and the ARPA costs.
Ms. Lacapa addressed the difference in the costs and indicated that not all of the projects are requesting the total cost be covered with ARPA funds.
Ms. Lacapa addressed the next steps of continuing to vet the proposed projects and to start working through project prioritizing.  She indicated that she feels it would be helpful for Board to let the project team know if they are on target or if changes need to be made to help get to the informed decisions.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that she feels it is working well but that one of her biggest concerns would be to communicate why so much was dedicated to a certain project.  She indicated that she would like to see the partnership capacity be part of the requests.
Ms. Lacapa stated that the partnership portion is being tracked and the information can be provided for any requests that they want to see.
Mr. Kephart talked about what information they are planning on providing when they are presenting the packages to the Board for projects.  He talked about creating something that is appropriate for 501c's and addressed the options that have been discussed for bringing the proposed projects to the board.
Chairman Seymore addressed narrowing the number down as to how much will be needed for matching funds and then they will know what is left to start forming top priority submittals.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed her appreciation for all of the work that is being done and the dedication to the organization and the citizens.

e.

County Pillars, Vision and Mission Review and Discussion :   Chairman Seymore addressed the County Pillars, the Vision and the Mission Statement of the County.  He inquired if there are any changes anyone would like to make on the pillars that we have which include County Services, Quality of Life, Public Safety, Economic Development, Transportation, Public Health, Education and the Justice System.  He indicated that he feels when they do the budget, they should focus on pillars for the main guiding principles with the county.  He indicated that if anyone would like to make changes to the guiding pillars or to condense or modify the Mission Statement to let him know.  He talked about the letter that was sent to the Elected Officials requesting feedback on any concerns that they have.  He addressed the feedback that was received, such as having an informal luncheon twice a year for Elected Officials, having office hours for the Board of Supervisors, having an employee luncheon quarterly, doing an event twice a year, one being family oriented and the Board Member visiting the different Departments and recognizing the Departments/Offices on Board days.
A discussion was held regarding the recommendations.
Glenn Kephart inquired as to doing a community service project and indicated that they are planning to have a Christmas party again this year since last year had such good reviews.
A further discussion was held regarding the recommendations.

f.

County Retreat Discussion :   Chairman Seymore addressed future retreats and indicated that he feels like it is good to get together to share thoughts and that it is a great opportunity to collaborate.
A discussion was held regarding the possibility of including the Elected Officials and Department Directors in a retreat.

g.

Summary & Next Steps :  Glenn Kephart talked about the next steps that need to continue.

h.

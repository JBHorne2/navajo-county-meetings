Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to flooding. 
Background:
The weather service indicated that heavy monsoon rains would be falling in Northeastern Arizona,
with the potential for flooding. Rain began on Thursday, July 22, 2021. Heavy rain began
impacting the area of Show Low Arizona the hardest. They had over 4 inches of rain in a very
short time, leaving homes and business flooded. Widespread impacts are potential for Navajo
County through Saturday evening.

Declaration of Emergency 
 
Whereas, the Navajo County Board of Supervisors is authorized by ARS § 26-311 to declare 
an emergency exists when flooding endangers life or property within the unincorporated 
areas of the county; and 
 
Whereas, the National Weather Service has forecasted significant flash flooding due to 
thunderstorms that may be life-threatening and cause significant property damage; and  
 
Whereas, flooding started July 22, 2021 in several areas of Navajo County; and  
 
Whereas, new flooding from the forecasted rains may cause displacement of citizens and 
damage to property in various locations throughout Navajo County; and  
 
Whereas, multiple jurisdictional resources from Navajo County and surrounding agencies 
are working to mitigate the threats to life, safety and property damage.   
 
Now, therefore, the Navajo County Board of Supervisors issues this Declaration of 
Emergency for Navajo County and the Chairman is hereby authorized to take all further 
actions as he deems necessary or appropriate as authorized by ARS § 26-311.

SHERIFF:

a.

Consideration of the Master Customer Agreement between Motorola Solutions, Inc. and Navajo County Sheriff's Office for WatchGuard Video body-worn camera project in the amount of $329,968 : Sheriff Clouse addressed the need for the body worn cameras and the changes with the camera technology over the past several years.  Alden Whipple talked about the trial period that the Sheriff's Office did with body cameras over several months.  He indicated that the WatchGuard system works with the current records management software, that it meets/exceeds the needs for the Sheriff's Office and that it's a better value.

Supervisor Whiting inquired if there are additional costs with this system.  Deputy Whipple indicated there is a five-year commitment.  Supervisor Whiting inquired as to the importance of the hands-free emergency activation.  Deputy Whipple addressed the radio activation.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired if there are additional costs for the cloud storage.  Deputy Whipple addressed the contract that they have selected and the pricing on it.
Supervisor Peshlakai inquired as to the price differences on the quotes.  Deputy Whipple addressed the price differences.
Brad Carlyon inquired if his office could piggyback on the service or if he would need to have his own contract.  Deputy Whipple addressed the matter.
A discussion was held regarding the system and how it can benefit other departments.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the Master Customer  Agreement between Motorola Solutions, Inc. and Navajo County Sheriff's Office for WatchGuard Video body-worn camera project in the amount of $329,968, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .

Chairman Seymore inquired how much more it is going to cost by moving to body cameras.  Deputy Whipple addressed the costs.   Chairman Seymore inquired if the cameras would be compatible with different systems. Deputy Whipple addressed the compatibility.  Mr. Carlyon addressed the redaction of videos and the cost for it.  Deputy Whipple stated that the Arizona Department of Public Safety recently did a test with body cameras and indicated that they had to hire fifteen people to handle the agencies requests.

Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of a Memorandum of Understanding for ChangePoint Mobile Crisis Vehicle donation by the Navajo County Sheriff :  Dawn Wilson addressed the item.  She indicated that Navajo County is becoming known for helping to divert the mental health and substance abuse individuals out of the criminal justice system and into treatment.  She stated that the Sheriff's Office has agreed to donate one of their retired vehicles to ChangePoint for transporting individuals who are required to go to court ordered treatment.  She addressed the funding received through a grant to be able to put logos on the van indicating it is for mobile crisis.
Brad Carlyon addressed the changes with the mental health program since Ms. Wilson has come in.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve Consideration of a Memorandum of Understanding for ChangePoint Mobile Crisis Vehicle donation by the Navajo County Sheriff, motion seconded by  Chairman Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

PUBLIC HEARING : Presentation and Analysis of Proposal to Incur Long-Term Indebtedness pursuant to Arizona Revised Statutes § 11-391 :  Jayson Vowell addressed the Board.  He talked about the presentation that was given on August 10, 2021 regarding the Public Safety Retirement Debt that is  approximately sixteen million dollars and stated that there is seventeen years left to pay it off.  He stated that currently PSPRS is charging 7.3% to pay the money back.  He indicated that this item is regarding different financing options to lower the amount and have some potential savings.  Mark Reader with Stiffel addressed the Board.  Mr. Reader addressed the requirements for a Public Hearing.  He talked about the different options for refinancing to pay off the liability.  He further talked about the historical low interest rates for refinancing at this time.  He indicated that these are taxable bonds so the rate will be a little higher.  He reviewed the savings for the County with doing the refinancing.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Vowell and Mr. Reader and their team on the presentation and work done on this item.
Chairman Seymore thanked Mr. Vowell and Mr. Reader for the work they have done on this item.  He indicated that it is a good financially for the county.

Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the meeting for Public Hearing.  There being no comments, the public hearing was closed.

Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed her appreciation for the work done on this item.

Chairman Seymore made a motion  to proceed to Public Hearing on October 4, 2021 to consider a Resolution for refinancing, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :   Supervisor Whiting made a motion to enter into the Board of Equalization, motion seconded by Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation of APN 212-07-002E & 212-07-002F (Lowes Hiw, Inc.); Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :  Cammy Darris and Kimberly Ward, with Paradigm Tax Group were sworn in by Melissa Buckley.
Cammy Darris addressed the Board regarding the item.  She indicated that this is a discount warehouse property built in 2007.  She stated that the building is 138,315 square feet with an attached greenhouse/garden area and is 10.09 acres in size and is located on White Mountain Road.  She further stated that the property is one building but it's located on two parcels, with half being in in the Lakeside Fire District and the other half being in the Show Low Fire District.  She advised that the full cash value is $8,873,820.00 for the entire property.  She indicated that they made adjustments for depreciation to reflect Arizona Department of Revenue depreciation schedule.  She stated that the building is listed as average grade.  Ms. Darris addressed the information that was used to determine the assessed value.  She advised that the Petitioner's have submitted two separate cost work ups and addressed the differences with both cost work ups.
Chairman Seymore inquired how much they show for the land. Ms. Darris stated that they have $527,041.00. Chairman Seymore inquired if the land is owned and not leased?  Ms. Darris stated that it is owned.
Kimberly Ward addressed the Board regarding the costs work up's that they submitted.  She indicated that they are requesting a small reduction to $7.6 million.
Chairman Seymore inquired if they have requested a decrease in previous years?  Ms. Ward stated that she doesn't have that information and that she will have to get back to the Board on that.   Chairman Seymore requested clarification on the depreciation stated by the Plaintiff's compared to the Assessor's.  Ms. Ward addressed the differences with their work up.
Supervisor Whitesinger inquired about the separation of the two parcels.  Ms. Ward stated that they did that based on the information listed on the county website.
Ms. Darris addressed the reasons that they are staying firm with the $8,873,820.00 price.

Chairman Seymore made a motion  to affirm the Assessor's decision, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion to return to regular session, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

PLANNING & ZONNING :

1.

PUBLIC HEARING: Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 27-2021 approving/denying a special use permit that allows construction of a 250' self-support, wireless communication tower and facility on APN# 201-13-001, Township 14 North, Range 19 East, Section 03 of the GSRM :  Berrin Najad addressed the item and indicated that Cellular One is requesting a special use permit.  She talked about the location for the tower.  She indicated that the existing tower is 295 feet, that it has reached maximum capacity, therefore a second tower is needed, which will be 250 feet and located 40 feet west of the existing tower.  She stated that the area is mostly vacant.  She indicated that they didn't receive calls in opposition or support, that the Planning and Zoning Meeting was held July 15, 2021 and that at the meeting the Commissioners added the stipulation that all the concrete and footers will be removed once the tower is abandoned and that the equipment will be removed.  She stated that staff recommends approval.

Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the meeting for public comments.  There being no comments made, the public hearing was closed.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to adopt of Resolution Number 27-2021 approving a special use permit that allows construction of a 250' self-support, wireless communication tower and facility on APN# 201-13-001, Township 14 North, Range 19 East, Section 03 of the GSRM, with the stipulation that the equipment be removed as recommended by the Planning & Zoning Commission when no longer in use, motion seconded by  Chairman Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

2.

PUBLIC HEARING: Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 28-2021 approving/denying a special use permit that allows construction of a 100 foot monopole, wireless communication tower and facility on APN# 404-04-003U, Township 12 North, Range 22 East, Section 19 of the GSRM :  Berrin Najad addressed the item and indicated that this special use permit is to replace an existing tower.  She addressed the location of the tower.  She indicated that the existing tower is at capacity.  She indicated that they did receive a phone call on this tower but that they were just wanting information on the project.  She stated that the Planning and Zoning Commission held a meeting on July 15, 2021 and recommended approval with the stipulation that the concrete, footers and equipment will be removed once the tower is no longer in use.  She stated that staff recommends approval.

Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the meeting for public comments.  There being no comments made, the public hearing was closed.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to adopt of Resolution Number 28-2021 approving a special use permit that allows construction of a 100 foot monopole, wireless communication tower and facility on APN# 404-04-003U, Township 12 North, Range 22 East, Section 19 of the GSRM, with the stipulation that the equipment be removed as recommended by the Planning & Zoning Commission when no longer is use, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

VICTORY HEIGHTS ROAD MAINTENANCE DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :   Supervisor Whiting made a motion to enter into the Victory Heights Road Maintenance District Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA :

a.
Consideration of Award of Contract #B21-07-022 Victory Heights Road Maintenance District to Cameron Crandell :  John Osgood addressed the item.  He indicated that there are a lot of roads in the county that are not maintained by the County, typically due to not being dedicated to the public or constructed to county standards.  He stated that one of the options that the County offers to neighbors interested in having some planned road maintenance is a county road improvement and maintenance district process.  He indicated that the Victory Heights Road Maintenance District was formed in 2006 and that the most recent contract expired in July 2021 and that the district officials notified the county that they wanted to go to bid again for road maintenance services.  He advised that only one bid was received by Cameron Crandell.  He stated that funds are budgeted in the road maintenance district from fee assessments paid by the property owners.  He stated that both the Victory Heights Advisory Committee and County staff recommend award of the contract to Cameron Crandell.

Chairman Seymore made a motion  to approve Award of Contract #B21-07-022 Victory Heights Road Maintenance District to Cameron Crandell, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion

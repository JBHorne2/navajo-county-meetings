NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, June 21, 2021

Present:
Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman, all appearing telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Catrina Jenkins, Emergency Management Manager; Alicia Deets, Communications Specialist, all appearing telephonically; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board

4:48 p.m . Call To Order.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency :  Glenn Kephart gave an updated on the Wyrick Fire and the evacuations.  He stated that they currently have the resources needed to protect the public.  He addressed the need for the Declaration of Emergency and indicated that it could also help address any new starts, that they have been seeing some throughout the day, since it could cause resources to get spread thin.
4:50 p.m. Supervisor Fern Benally is present telephonically.
Catrina Jenkins thanked the Board for considering the Declaration of Emergency.  She addressed the multiple fires in Navajo and Gila County as well as the surrounding areas.  She indicated that any support issued by the Declaration is appreciated.
Chairman Seymore thanked the Board for being in attendance and thanked staff for all that they have done over the past several days.  He inquired if there are any questions or concerns with the need to declare an emergency.
Melissa Buckley read the Declaration of Emergency.
Chairman Seymore addressed the need for the declaration.
Chairman Seymore made a motion to approve the Declaration of Emergency, motion seconded by Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN : at 4:59 p.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Daryl Seymore, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

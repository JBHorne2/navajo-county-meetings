CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Magnus MacLeod requested that the Board intervene in a conflict regarding a Contract with Navajo County to permit road crossings of aircraft across three roads.  He indicated that staff is issuing right of ways that conflict with the Contract.  He further indicated that the conflicts affect the insurance policy, who's liable and the private property rights in the Contract.  He showed pictures of planes being on the roadways and stated that new signs have been put up saying yield to aircraft.  He indicated that a gate was removed to allow for planes to cross.  He showed pictures of the gate near his property that was removed.  He showed a plat map of the area.  He requested that the Board review the information provided and instruct staff to comply with the Contract.  He advised that he has provided a document and CD with the information he spoke about to staff today.  He stated that he is willing to work with the Department Heads and the County Manager.
Chairman Seymore directed staff to follow up on the matter.

b.

Consideration of a Proclamation, proclaiming April 27, 2021 as "A Day of Remembrance for the Lives Lost to COVID-19" : Alicia Deets addressed the item.  She spoke of reflecting on the past year and the anniversary of COVID-19 having reached our area.  She stated that what everyone thought would be a brief interruption of our normal life turned into a prolonged life alternating event.  She addressed changes that have been made by everyone and the fears and hope that accompanied as it touched every life in Navajo County. She stated that to honor the 525 people who lost their lives due to COVID they have suggested a Day of Remembrance and that every employee wear a white ribbon.  She advised that employee Esther Grass, from Kayenta, stated that she wears her ribbon on behalf of her Aunt and relatives that were lost to COVID, that 2020 was a struggle and that she is thankful to the local leaders, Chapters, the County and the Navajo Nation who did everything in their power to keep them safe.  Ms. Deets advised that a white rose bush will be planted today in the courtyard as a reminder of the residence no longer with us and that they hope that those who visit the rose bush will find a moment of mindful comfort.  She stated that our future remains as hopeful as spring built upon the wisdom of winter.
Chairman Seymore stated that he appreciates all that has been done to put this together by Mrs. Deets and staff.  Chairman Seymore asked that everyone pause for fifty-two seconds and stated that each second will reflect ten lives lost.
Following the moment of silence, Chairman Seymore read the proposed Proclamation.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve the Proclamation, proclaiming April 27, 2021 as "A Day of Remembrance for the Lives Lost to COVID-19", motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.
Chairman Seymore stated we express love and sympathy to the families on behalf of the County.

INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY :

a.

Consideration of approval to purchase the Unitrends backup system to Custom Storage Inc. (cStor) under the GSA Contract #47QTCA19D008G :  :   Ken Dewitt addressed the item.  He gave a background and indicated that this system offers Navajo County the best options for safety, securing, backing up and restoring the counties valuable data in case of physical issues or cyber incident.  He stated that it will give three layers of backup, local, off site within the County and cloud backup.  He stated that this will also meet or exceed the cyber insurance requirements of a 321, which is three copies of a backup, two different locations and one disconnected from the network.  He indicated that IT is recommending approval of item.  He stated that the total cost is $329,511.79, that it will be split over three years for the cost of the system and that he worked with administration and finance on this.

Supervisor Whitesinge r made a motion to approve the purchase the Unitrends backup system to Custom Storage Inc. (cStor) under the GSA Contract #47QTCA19D008G, motion seconded by Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :  Glenn Kephart indicated that it is great to be back in person.  He addressed the items on agenda celebrating the youth and expressing sympathy for the loss of lives with COVID.  He stated that there was a common theme from each of the teams as they talked about learning from each other and about hard work.  He indicated that this will serve them well through life and it's the same attributes we look at when seeing who's a good fit for our team and that there are many of them that will make great future Navajo County employees.
Supervisor Benally thanked everyone for being here.  She reported on working with the Chapters to do some cleanup in her District.  She indicated that over the weekend she did a presentation at the White Mountain Nature Center on Pinon and Juniper trees, which are in her area.  She addressed some of the things that they learned, indicated that she was able to meet new people, see a different part of the County and that she enjoyed her learning experience.
Supervisor Whiting stated that it is good to be here in person and see everyone and that he is looking forward to having more in person meetings.
Supervisor Peshlakai reported on going to community cleanups for Chapters in his area.  He extended his appreciation to Public Works for their coordination and response to his requests.  He stated that after a meeting with Senator Kelly's staff he noticed the litter off one of the exits in Winslow, so he did a cleanup there with another Navajo County employee and that they collected sixteen bags of trash in six hours.  He thanked Public Works for their assistance with that cleanup.  He indicated that it is great to be back in person, that it feels like the first official meeting since it is the first one in person, that it has been very welcoming, and he is looking forward to continuing in person.
Supervisor Whitesinger stated that it is good to be among colleagues and see those who serve our County.  She spoke about the fire at the sawmill in Cibecue.  She indicated that there was a request to provide some resources to the community, and that she is grateful to Mr. Kephart and Ricky Denton for their quick reaction and willingness to serve.  She stated that there is a great quality in the people who work here and that we are working on behalf of our community members.  She indicated that Chief Savage also responded and was able to provide resources.  She stated that one thing she learned is how important our conversations are to be able to have those partnerships with other entities and that the relationships are built by being respectful to those other jurisdictions and by helpful each other.  She indicated that she hopes to continue those important partnerships and that she is grateful to serve in Navajo County among many people who want to serve and are quick to serve when needed.  She spoke about the meeting with Sheriff's Clouse and Deputy Swanty regarding possible future partnerships with Pinetop/Lakeside and the White Mountain Apache Tribe.  She stated that she is learning all the time from great people.  She further stated that it is important to be dedicated and to look for opportunities to serve.
Brad Carlyon stated that it is nice to be back in person.
Chairman Seymore stated that he is thankful for staff and all they have done and accomplished during this time.  He indicated that all departments have stepped up and done a fantastic job.  He stated that it's all about the customer, he addressed the conversations he has had with people regarding them having a good experience when getting their vaccines.  He stated that he appreciates each of the Supervisors and staff for what they do.

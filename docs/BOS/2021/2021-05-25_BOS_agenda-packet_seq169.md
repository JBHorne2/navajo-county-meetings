APACHE COUNTY ARIZONA@WORK NEAZ TITLE 1B
2020-2021 SUCCESS STORIES
• This Apache County client was newly divorced with 8 children. She did not know what she was going to, but knew she wanted 
to go back to school. She was referred to the WIOA program by an older auxiliary women. She applied and was enrolled into 
the Adult program and now she has completed her Bachelors degree and is an RN with the help from the  
ARIZONA@WORK WIOA program.  Her statement was I wish you all knew how much you have helped me! I don’t know 
how to show my appreciation and don’t know what I would have done without this program! 
Success at its best!!!
• This Apache County client is a student who has been working on her Medical Assisting certification through NPC and due to 
COVID she was unable to do an in-person Externship at the end of her schooling.  She didn’t realize how hard that would 
make it for her to get a job as a medical assistant because she had only virtually participated in her Externship.  We have been
working with her to find a   WEX to assist her and helping her apply for various jobs.  She was able to get hired on at Summit 
and they will provide all the training she needs to succeed as a medical assistant and is now working full-time permanent.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring November 2021 as "Native American Heritage Month" :    Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger read the proposed Proclamation declaring November 2021 at "Native American Heritage Month".  She indicated that they appreciate the County in being able to take the time to recognize the many efforts of the Native American's throughout our Nation and the contributions in being able to influence our communities and our nation for the good.  She stated that we recognize the legacy of the Navajo Nation, the Hopi Nation and the White Mountain Apache Tribe in their contributions to our local region.  She further stated that they are grateful for their local history, language and culture.
Chairman Seymore indicated that we appreciate our Supervisors that represent those different areas, Supervisor Benally, Supervisor Peshlakai and Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger.
Supervisor Whiting stated that growing up in this area we sometime fail to understand what a privilege it is to live and experience being a part of those communities and being able to work with the individuals in the tribal communities that are part of Navajo County.  He indicated that the opportunity to be able to focus on the importance to our area and to our Country is greatly appreciated.  He thanked those who worked on bringing the Proclamation forward.

Supervisor Benally made a motion  to approve the Proclamation declaring November 2021 as "Native American Heritage Month", motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :   Supervisor Whiting made a motion to enter into the Board of Equalization, motion seconded by Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

10:30 AM Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 208-06-022C; Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :  The Assessor, Cammy Darris, having previously been sworn indicated that this is a 3200 square foot out building with a livable area built into it on approximately 2.17 acres.  She stated that the Petitioner is requesting that this be changed to their Primary residence.  She indicated that it had been set as a secondary residence due to them having a Surprise, AZ addressed but that they move up here and now have a Show Low, AZ address.  She requested that the classification be changed to Legal Class 3.1 which is primary residence with a full case value of $231,998 and the limited value of $184,932.  She advised that she has talked to the Petitioner's, who indicated that they would not be present, but that they were just wanting the classification changed.

Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger made a motion  to approve the change of Classification to Legal Class 3.1 - Primary Residence with a full case value of $231,998 and the limited value of $184,932 on the Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 208-06-022C, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

10:45 AM Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 202-11-028B; Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :  The Assessor, Cammy Darris, was sworn in by Melissa Buckley.  Ms. Darris indicated that the Petitioner took out a well permit but that due to a delay in getting wells put in there has been no work done on the property.  She stated that the appraiser went by the property, saw a hole and thought that the work had started but that the Petitioner had just removed a tree.  Ms. Darris requested that the full cash value be reverted back to its original value $9,599 for full cash and $4,602 for the limited.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the Assessor's recommendation to revert the full case value to $9,599 and the limited value to $4,602 on the Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 202-11-028B, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion to return to regular session, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Senator Jamescita Peshlakai thanked the Board for inviting the Legislative members to be able to discuss the priorities.  She gave a brief introduction of herself and her family history in the area.  She stated that she is honored to represent the areas in her District.  She indicated that it is an honor to be able to work together.
The Board presented Representative Barton and Senator Peshlakai with a Road Sign as a gift of appreciation.
Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Representative Barton and Senator Peshlakai for being present and for the representation their areas.  She indicated that their efforts and time is appreciated, and the work is important.
Supervisor Whiting stated that their job isn't easy, but that they appreciate the partnership and that they value the time they take to listen and understand the local issues.  He stated we appreciate the advocacy and their representation for our communities.
Supervisor Benally thanked Representative Barton and Senator Peshlakai for being present and for their service in their areas.

BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger made a motion to enter into the Board of Equalization, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

11:00 AM Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 409-21-001B; Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners : The Assessor, Cammy Darris, was sworn in by Melissa Buckley.  Ms. Darris indicated that the issue is the Petitioner is requesting an Ag Valuation but that it requires certain criteria by Statute and is intended to help ligament ranching operations that have large amounts of acreage to be able to afford to keep the land for cattle.  She stated that this property is forty acres located in Lindon, that the land is currently not leased to a qualified party.  She stated that in order to get Ag and be leased you have to have a qualified rancher.  She indicated that he previously had a lease with a qualified rancher but that the rancher then leased his property to someone else which disqualified him as a rancher.  She addressed the process for being a qualified rancher and being able to qualify for Ag Valuation.  She requested that the property remain as Non-Agriculture valuation, with full cash value at $295,677 and limited property value at $216,002.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to deny the Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 409-21-001B, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

d.

11:15 AM Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 309-29-013B; Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners : The Assessor, Cammy Darris, having been previously sworn, indicated that she has talked to the Petitioner and that they agree with the Assessors recommendation.  She stated that this is a 5.10-acre parcel with a single-family dwelling.  She indicated that the basis for the petition was equity, that the Petitioner pulled three other properties in his area and asked the Assessors office to compare those and that she went out and inspected all three parcels and his property.  She requested that the full cash value be adjusted to $550,000 and the limited property value be $463,129.

Vice-Chairwoman made a motion  to approve the Assessor's recommendation to adjust the full cash value to $550,000 and the limited property value to $463,129 on the Petition for Review of Notice of Change APN 309-29-013B, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

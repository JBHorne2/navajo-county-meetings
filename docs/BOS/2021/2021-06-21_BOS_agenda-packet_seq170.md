Title: Emergency Declaration - Wyrick Fire
Submitted By: Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board of Supervisors
Department: Board of Supervisors
Presented By: Glenn Kephart/Catrina Jenkins
Motion before the Board:
Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency. 
Background:
Wyrick Fire - The fire is 3,000+ miles and still very active. The fire continued north and east last
night. A Type 2 Incident Management Team has been approved and is scheduled to take over this
evening at 5pm. USFS, NCSO, H/OFD and more have all been tirelessly working on this fire. 
North Heber/ Overgaard area is under a GO status. This includes the area North of 260 on the
North side of 277 from Mogollon High School to Mogollon Drive. The shelter site will be the
Holbrook High School Wrestling Room. Route 277 and 377 are closed to traffic headed into
Heber. 
Additionally, there is a new start on Fort Apache South of Wagon Wheel- This is a very critical
area and crews and resources are being dispatched.
Recommendation: 
Staff recommends approval.

Declaration of Emergency 
 
Whereas, the Navajo County Board of Supervisors is authorized by ARS § 26 -311 to 
declare an emergency exists due to fire which endangers life or property within the city, 
or the unincorporated areas of the county; and 
 
Whereas, the Wyrick fire that started west of Heber -Overgaard has created an 
emergency situation; and  
 
Whereas, the Wyrick fire is a fire that may force the evacuation of Heber -Overgaard and 
the surrounding communities; and  
 
Whereas, the Wyr ick fire has expanded throughout the day; and  
 
Whereas, multiple jurisdictional resources are on the ground and in the air attacking the 
fire; and 
 
Whereas, additional emergency personnel have been responding to other fires in the 
surrounding areas of Navajo County and will continue to respond to other fires that may 
occur during this emergency declaration.   
 
Now, therefore , the Navajo County Board of Supervisors issues this Declaration of 
Emergency in Navajo County and the Chairman is hereby authorized to  take all further 
actions as may be necessary or appropriate to implement this declaration pursuant to ARS 
§ 26-311.

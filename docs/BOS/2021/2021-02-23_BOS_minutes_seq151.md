ROLL CALL VOTE: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 7-21,  approving the sale and execution and delivery of pledged revenue refunding obligations, in one or more series; approving the form and authorizing the execution and delivery of necessary agreements, instruments, and documents; delegating the authority to determine certain matters with respect to the foregoing and declaring an emergency :   Jayson Vowell addressed the item.  He stated that last month they had an open meeting and presentation to discuss the refunding opportunity regarding the existing debts.  He indicated that looking at market conditions they feel that they are favorable with the opportunity to refund the bonds and that they are looking at a potential to save an estimate of six hundred thousand to nine hundred thousand over the next ten years.  He stated that as part of the process they were required to get a long-term issuer credit rating, for the overall credit worthiness for the County and that they received an AA rating, which is an excellent rating.  He addressed the credit rating.  He advised that staff is asking the Board to approve the Resolution.
Chairman Seymore thanked Mr. Vowell for the presentation and thanked everyone who has contributed to the bond rating.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to adopt  Resolution Number 7-21 , approving the sale and execution and delivery of pledged revenue refunding obligations, in one or more series; approving the form and authorizing the execution and delivery of necessary agreements, instruments, and documents; delegating the authority to determine certain matters with respect to the foregoing and declaring an emergency; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting.  Melissa Buckley did a roll call on the vote.  Supervisor Benally; Supervisor Peshlakai; Supervisor Whiting; Chairman Seymore and Supervisor Whitesinger all voted in favor of approving the motion.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

COVID-19 Update :  Janelle Linn advised that they have had only seven new cases reported to them in the past twenty-four hours and that the previous week was very similar, with an average of nine to twenty cases a day being reported.  She indicated that they had one day last week that had sixty-five cases reported but that they were mostly from one healthcare facility that had a lag in reporting.  She advised that they continue to see a downward trend and that they feel that they are near the bottom of the second spike.  She indicated that they are prepared for the possibility of a third spike once the various strains are identified and that they're assessing it closely in the event they spread through our communities.  She stated that they have been notified that they will be receiving additional funding to continue COVID activities in the form of surveillance, improved data techniques and for vaccine efforts.  As to vaccines she advised that Arizona is about 17% vaccinated, which is on target and that we are about 18% in Navajo County.   She stated that last week due to the winter storms in mid-west, where the vaccine is manufactured, they didn't get the vaccine shipments.  She further stated that they expect to see a big surge this week, that a lot of facilities this week plan to do their regular appointments plus make up for the appointments missed last week.  She advised that Navajo County is ahead of the State average for pushing out the vaccine, especially due to the small allotments they have been receiving.  She stated that they have administered 18,199 vaccines in Navajo County, that 13,279 of those are first doses and that 4,920 are people who have completed their vaccine series.  She stated that they appreciate their Tribal and Healthcare partners for always working with them and for being vaccine sites.
Chairman Seymore thanked Ms. Linn for her report.
Supervisor Whitesigner congratulated Ms. Linn on her new position and for her continued service to our communities.
Chairman Seymore  addressed the presentation given by Ms. Linn to the City of Show Low and indicated that it was very informative.  He stated that he appreciates the Health District and everything that they are doing.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of award of Contract #B20-12-028 Supplying and Delivering Liquid Asphalt Products to Hawker & Evan Asphalt Co., Inc., and Cactus Transport, Inc .:  John Osgood addressed the item.  He advised that this is an annual contract that provides liquid asphalt materials for use in the county's pavement preservation work and maintenance.  He stated that they received two proposals from companies that have been providing these materials to the county for numerous years, and he addressed the award of the contract.  He further stated that the county has the flexibility to award contracts to two bidders for each item.  He advised that the source of funding is the Highway User Revenue Fund.  He further advised that staff recommends awarding the contract to Hawker & Evans and Cactus Transport.
Chairman Seymore inquired as to the different classifications of asphalt and asked if it is different products?
Mr. Osgood advised that they are different products and addressed the differences.
Chairman Seymore inquired as to the amount used per mile.
Mr. Osgood advised that he would try and get an answer as to how many miles are covered for each purchase.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion to approve the award of Contract #B20-12-028 Supplying and Delivering Liquid Asphalt Products to Hawker & Evan Asphalt Co., Inc., and Cactus Transport, Inc.; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Glenn Kephart acknowledge and thanked the Care Committee for all that they do for Navajo County employees.  He addressed the AA credit rating for the County and indicated that it is remarkable for a county of our size.  He thanked the Board for providing the leadership to help navigate the County to being in this position.

He advised our community members to contact DES for that funding opportunity.  He thanked everyone for participating today.  He indicated that we are blessed to have wonderful employees, staff and Supervisors.

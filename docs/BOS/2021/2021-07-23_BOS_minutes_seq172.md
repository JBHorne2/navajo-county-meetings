NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, July 23, 2021

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman, all appearing telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board; Kymberle O'Farrell, Emergency Management; Rochelle Lacapa, Government Relations Director, all appearing telephonically

11:01 a.m . Call To Order :   Chairman Seymore called the meeting to order and thanked everyone for their work with putting this together.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to flooding :  Kymberle O'Farrell gave a brief background and update on the flooding issues.  She indicated that the Show Low area received four inches of rain in a short time leaving several businesses and homes flooded.  She stated that staff is recommending the declaration due to the events.  She read the proposed declaration.
Chairman Seymore asked if the Board had any questions or statements.  There being no response, Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion for Declaration of Emergency due to flooding, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.
Chairman Seymore stated that he appreciates all the efforts that the Emergency Management Team have put forth on working with this.  He addressed the current flooding in the Show Low area and the impact it is having on the businesses and homes.  He thanked everyone for taking the time to be present for this meeting.

ADJOURN :  11:05 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Daryl Seymore, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

 THIS FIRST AMENDMENT TO GROUND LEASE AGREEMENT  
(“Amendment” ) is made and entered i nto as of  this 2nd  day of September, 2021 by and 
between Navajo County, a political subdivision of the State of Arizona, hereinafter 
designated as (“Lessor”), with its principal offices located at 100 East Carter Drive, South 
Highway 77, Holbrook, Arizona 86025; and Cellular One of North East Arizona, a 
division of Smith Bagley, Inc ., a District of Columbia corporation (“Lessee”) , with its 
principal offices located at 1500 S. White Mountain Road, Show Low, Arizona, 85901; 
also referred to herein as “Party” individually, and “Parties” collectively. 
 
RECITALS 
 
 WHEREAS, the Lessor and Lessee are Parties to that  certain Ground Lease 
Agreement (the “Agreement”) dated the 31st day of March 2018 wherein Lessor leased a 
10’x 20’ area (the “Leased Premises”) to Lessee for a Mono Pole  to be constructed by 
Lessee for telecommunication purposes; 
 
 WHEREAS, pursuant to the terms of the Agreement, Lessor owned an existing 
Mono Pole (the “Existing Mono Pole”) at or near the Leased Premises that was in disrepair 
and could not structurally accommodate Lessee’s telecommunications equipment.  Lessor 
and Lessee agreed, inter alia, that Lessee would construct a new Mono Pole (“Lessee’s 
New Mono Pole”) at the Leased Premises ; remove Lessor’s equipment from  Lessor’s 
Existing Mono Pole ; deconstruct Lessee’s Existing Mono Pole; and install the Lessor’s 
equipment on Lessee’s New Mono Pole;  
 
 WHEREAS, Lessor and Lessee acknowledge and agree that the promised events 
stated in the Agreement and in the immediate above paragraph have been fully completed 
to the satisfaction of both Lessor  and Lessee;      
  
WHEREAS, Lessee has entered into an agreement with AT&T wherein Lessee is 
a third-party vendor to AT&T for the installation, construction and maintenance of 
telecommunication equipment for the FirstNet First Responder Network (for which AT&T 
is the awarded contractor of the FirstNet First Responder Network) on designated tower 
sites owned by Lessee.  Further, Lessee’s New Mono Pole is a  designated FirstNet tower 
site;  
 
WHEREAS, Lessee requires an additional 10 feet by 20 feet (10’ x 20’) ground 
space area at the Leased Premises to accommodate the FirstNet telecommunicatio ns 
equipment as depicted on Exhibit B-1 attached hereto and incorp orated herein and the

Motion before the Board:
Request for ARRF Funds Darrin Reed Foundation, Inc. in the amount of $1,250.00
Background:
The Darrin Reed Foundation provides community assistance through local law enforcement
officers.  It provides funds so that officers can provide help for people that they encounter without
it being an expense out of their pockets.  The Foundation helps officers pay for training and
equipment they may need that the Department doesn't pay for to help them fight illegal drug
trafficking.  In addition the Foundation provides gift cards for 5 deserving families at Christmas
on the recommendation of the officers.

two years and begins on October 1 after the primary election at which the precinct committeeman
was a candidate and continues until October 1 after the following primary election at which a
precinct committeeman is elected.

 JUSTICE COURT ADMINISTRATIVE FEES ORDINANCE 
 
 An ordinance adopted pursuant to A.R.S. § 11-251.08(A), establishing  certain 
  administrative fee s for the  Justice Courts in Navajo County  in order to defray Court  
 automation costs, costs associated with  issuing and processing Failure to Appear warrants,  
 and costs associated with collecting civil traffic sanctions in case of default.  
 
SECTION 1:  AUTHORITY / PURPOSE / JURISDICTION / TITLE 
 
1.1 Authority.  Navajo County is authorized by Section 11 -251.08(A) of the Arizona Revised Statutes to 
adopt fee schedules for specific products and services the county provides to the public.  In Arizona 
Attorney General Opinion No. I95-63 (December 18, 1995), the Attorney General determined that A .R.S. 
§ 11-251.08(A) authorizes the Board of Supervisors to “establish fees for any specific products or 
services that the county provides to the public and to direct J ustice Courts to collect any established 
court-related fees.” 
 
1.2 Purpose. It is the purpose of this ordinance to establish an administrative fee schedule whereby Navajo 
County may defray the actual costs of services provided by the Justice Courts in thre e subject areas 
where these costs are a burden to the taxpayers and should be borne by those individuals responsible for 
them: court automation; costs of issuing and executing Failure to Appear (“FTA”) warrants; and costs of 
collecting sanctions in civil traffic ca ses where the defendant defaults in payment.  
 
1.3 Jurisdiction.  This ordinance shall apply to all Justice Courts in Navajo County. 
 
1.4 Title.  This ordinance shall be known as the Navajo County Justice Court Administrative Fees Ordi nance. 
 
 SECTION 2: FEE TO DEFRAY COURT AUTOMATION COSTS 
 
2.1 Basis for Court Automation Fee.   The Justice Courts in Navajo County and the Navajo County 
Superior Court have established a coordinated plan for the automation of cases and ca sh-flow 
management.  The Board of Supervisors has entered into an agreement with the Administrative Office of

SECTION 1:  AUTHORITY / PURPOSE / JURISDICTION / TITLE

adopt fee schedules for specific products and services the county provides to the public.  In Arizona 
Attorney General Opinion No. I95-63 (December 18, 1995), the Attorney General determined that A .R.S. 
§ 11-251.08(A) authorizes the Board of Supervisors to “establish fees for any specific products or 
services that the county provides to the public and to direct Justice Court s to collect any established 
court-related fees.” 
 
1.2 Purpose. It is the purpose of this ordinance to establish an administrative fee schedule whereby Navajo

collecting sanctions and notifying the Motor Vehicle Department in civil traffic cases where the defendant 
defaults in payment.

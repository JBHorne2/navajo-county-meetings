PUBLIC HEARING: FINAL 2021-22 BUDGET PURSUANT TO §42-17104:
Convene a Special Meeting of the Board of Supervisors for final budget adoption.
Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 22-2021 , approving the proposed final budget for fiscal year 2021-22 for Navajo County :   Supervisor Whiting made a motion to convene a Special Meeting of the Board of Supervisors for final budget adoption, motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.
Jayson Vowell addressed the Board.  A presentation was shown.  He addressed the procedures and meetings for getting to the final budget.  Mr. Vowell talked about some of the highlights for the budget, including the recommendation for adopting a flat property tax, revenue growth from sales tax and the healthy financial reserves.  He advised that they have a balanced budget, that they have identified expenditures that address the needs of the Departments and Elected Officials.  He indicated that there is also a healthy contingency fund that will protect against potential downturn and help with cash flow pending the property tax being accumulated.  He indicated that this year they are able to maintain operations in every department, that over the past few years they have had to reduce those services and operations in some cases but that this year they are able to maintain or increase the requests from departments that demonstrated a need to be able to deliver the services to the citizens.  Mr. Vowell stated that Navajo County will have the 4 th lowest property tax rate in the State, that the rate will be reduced from .8657 to .8501 and he addressed the increase in the amount that will be received due to new construction.  He talked the reduce in state shared sales tax and county sales tax in 2021 due to COVID and the unknown effects it would have, but that for FY2022 they will bring it back to a normal level and addressed the forecasted amounts.  He stated that there are a wide range of services provided county wide and addressed the different types of services.  Mr. Vowell advised that staff recommends adoption of budget.
Chairman Seymore thanked Mr. Vowell and his team for their efforts on the budget.
Supervisor Whitesinger stated that she appreciates all that has been done to get to the final budget.  She indicated that conversations have been happening over the past several months to help work towards a balanced budget, to best understand where the money goes, to provide the best services that are mandated and to ensure that the services are provided not only at a minimum but at a quality.  She thanked all of the Supervisors and those who oversee the different parts of the organization for all the consideration and understanding to ensure the importance of balanced budget.  She indicated that we have continued to provide quality service even through the difficulties in the lean years.  She stated that she is glad to see the budget where it is and that we can continue to provide the quality services.
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 22-2021, approving the proposed final budget for fiscal year 2021-22 for Navajo County, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PLANNING & ZONING :

1.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 23-2021  approving/denying a special use permit that allows expansion of an existing boat and self-storage facility at 7837 White Mountain Lake Road, APN # 304-22-007B, T11N, R22E S17, in C-R, Commercial-Residential zoning. Owner: 603 Industrial Drive, LLC (Hartley Turley, Registered Agent) :
Berrin Najad addressed the Board regarding the item.  A Presentation was shown.  She addressed the location of the property.  She indicated that the property is made up of two parts and with this proposal it would be combined.  She advised that currently there are RV and self-storage units on the east side.  She further advised that in phase one the east side would only be self-storage units, that seven additional buildings would be added, and the west side would be RV storage.  She stated that in phase two it would be all self-storage unit in place of the RV storage, depending on the market, if there is a request for more storage units.  She advised that the entrance won't change and that the east side of the property will remain the same.  She stated that the signage will remain the same, that there is existing fencing on property, that some will be changed, and some will be updated.  She advised that there are two residential properties to the south where there will be solid fencing.  She indicated that the applicant would like to add some landscaping on the front, that all utilities are located to the side and that business hours will be 6:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.  She stated that staff has no received any calls or letters in opposition to the project but that there have been some calls inquiring about the project.  She advised that the Planning and Zoning Commission heard the item in May and recommended approval 6-0.  She further advised that the Applicant is present for questions.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion to approve a special use permit that allows expansion of an existing boat and self-storage facility at 7837 White Mountain Lake Road, APN # 304-22-007B, T11N, R22E S17, in C-R, Commercial-Residential zoning. Owner: 603 Industrial Drive, LLC (Hartley Turley, Registered Agent), motion seconded by Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Consideration of Amendment Number Two of the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Silver Creek Irrigation District and the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District :  Mark Honberger addressed the item.  He stated that it has been a very dry year and that the drought is resulting in low lake levels, that they are hoping for rain but that they are planning on how to procced if there is no rain.  He addressed the agreement between the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District and Silver Lake Irrigation District regarding maintaining the lake levels and the amount that is paid each year.  He indicated that the agreement calls for an adjustment in payments if the lake level falls below the twenty-four feet that has been established but that it doesn't define any details for the adjustment.  He talked about the discussions that have been held to determine what happens in the event it does go below.  He addressed the proposed Amendment that specifies the reduced fee that would be paid if the water goes below the twenty-four-foot level and indicated that the amount will be determined at the end of each month.

Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Honberger and everyone who worked together on this item.
Chairman Seymore stated that he recently seen the lake and is impressed with the work that has been done there.
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve Amendment Number Two of the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Silver Creek Irrigation District and the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

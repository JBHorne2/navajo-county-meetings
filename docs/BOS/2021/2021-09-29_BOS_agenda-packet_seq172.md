NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING AND AGENDA
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
Wednesday, September 29, 2021 8:30 a.m. - Noon *WORK SESSION
ONE OR MORE SUPERVISORS MAY ATTEND VIA VIDEO AND/OR TELEPHONICALLY 
The Navajo County Board of Supervisors will hold a WORK SESSION open to the public on Wednesday, September 29, 2021, in
the Prescott/Chino Conference Room at the Prescott Resort and Conference Center 1500 East state Route 69, Prescott, Arizona. 
The Board may meet in executive session pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (3) for legal advice on any item.
To participate in the Board of Supervisors meeting, you may call in at: 
Phone: 1-877-820-7831
Participant Passcode: 6068060#
Persons with disabilities who need accommodation to attend or participate in the meeting may contact the Clerk of the Board’s
office at (928) 524-4053; at least 48 hours prior to the meeting so an accommodation can be arranged.
Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Chairman’s discretion. A copy of the agenda background material
provided to the Board, with the exception of items to be considered in executive session, is available for public inspection at
Navajo County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov
8:00 a.m.- 8:30 a.m. - Breakfast and Ice Breaker - Pursuant to ARS § 38-431.02 and ARS § 38-431 (3), notice is hereby given to
the members of the Navajo County Board of Supervisors and to the General Public that a quorum of the Board of Supervisors may
be present but no official Board of Supervisors action will be taken at this time.
8:30 a.m. Call To Order: Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance

NAVAJO COUNTY REDISTRICTING ADVISORY COMMITTEE MINUTES
Tuesday, October 26, 2021

Present:

Mae-Gilene Begay, Member; Sadie Lister, Member; Ryan Stuart, Chairman; Rick Fernau, Vice-Chairman; Cline Griggs, Member

Staff Attendance:

Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Ryan Taylor, GIS Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

3:07 p.m. Call To Order : Invocation was lead by Mr. Rick Fernau and the Pledge of Allegiance was led by Mr. Ryan Stuart.

Welcome and Introductions : Jason Moore introduce himself and staff which included Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board and Ryan Taylor GIS Manager. The Redistricting Advisory Committee Members introduced themselves which included Sadie Lister resident of Indian Wells Chapter; Rick Fernau; Mae-Gilene Begay; Ryan Stuart; and Cline Griggs.

Election of Chairman and Vice Chairman - Ryan Stuart Chairman and Rick Fernau Vice-Chairman : Rick Fernau made a motion to nominate Ryan Stuart as the Chairman of the Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee, motion seconded by Mae-Gilene Begay. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Sadie Lister made a motion to nominate Rick Fernau as the Vice-Chairman of the Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee, motion seconded by Mae-Gilene Begay. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Training and Legal Requirements for Advisory Committee:   Jason Moore gave a review of the Opening Meeting Law. He indicated that public perception is very important and the Open Meeting Law is applicable whenever there is quorum. Mr. Moore reviewed the requirements of the Opening Meeting Law. Mr. Moore indicated that all agendas and minutes will be published on the Navajo County website, recorded and preserved for history. He reviewed posting locations and what the agenda's must include. Discussion was held on Call to the Public. Mr. Moore indicated that the advisory committee could decide together on how much time to give during call to the public. Mr. Moore discussed minutes and public attendance. He further stated that there will not be any executive sessions due to the transparency of the redistricting process. Mr. Moore discussed the penalties of a violation of the opening meeting law.

Glenn Kephart, County Manager welcomed the Advisory Committee Members and introduced himself. Mr. Kephart indicated that he has been with Navajo County for 6 years and has been the county manager for 4. He stated that he is originally from Pennsylvania and enjoys the White Mountains, which he now calls home.

Review Objectives:   Jason Moore reviewed the objectives the Redistricting Advisory Committee. He indicated that the committee will need to equalize the population of the Districts. Mr. Moore gave a presentation of the Redistricting Process. He indicated that by law redistricting happens after ever census count. Mr. Moore reviewed the current district population numbers as it stands now and the current population issues. Mr. Moore discussed the population variance governed by ARS 11-212, which is our statutory guideline. Mr. Moore explained that the Legislature extended the period of time for a formal new map to be adopted, which is by July 1, 2022. Discussion was held on the timing of the redistricting process. Mr. Moore indicated that we would like to present options to the Board of Supervisors 60 days in advance of July 1 in order for the Board of Supervisors to receive public input. Mr. Moore indicated that we will keep the Board of Supervisors abreast of where we are at in the process. He advised the committee members that they can keep them apprised as well. Mr. Moore continued to discuss the population variance, indicating that we should get as close to 5% as we can. Mr. Moore gave some information on the mapping tool that we will have. Ryan Taylor indicated that he will print off 5 current maps and will have Mrs. Buckley mail them to each member at their home mailing address. Mr. Moore reviewed the existing variance in Navajo County and the 2020 Census. A discussion was held regarding the total deviation between largest and smallest district. Mr. Moore reviewed the race and ethnicity issues. He spoke about the Hopi population, the White Mountain Apache, Navajo, Spanish and black populations. He then discussed the District and minority proportions of each.

Review Census Information:   Mr. Moore reviewed the census information and Resolution Number 10-2021 adopted by the Board of Supervisors. Mr. Moore indicated that we must comply with A.R.S. §11-212 and the voting rights act. Mr. Moore reviewed items 3k, 1-9 of the Resolution. Mr. Moore reviewed the Voting Age, Minority, Majority and the voting age population.

Review Availability for Community Meetings: Mr. Moore discussed the redistricting principles and the public involvement process. He spoke about the community meetings and public information materials. Mr. Moore will be conducting this presentation at each of the community meetings in order to get feedback from our communities. He spoke about what will happen at each of the community meetings and stated that we will conduct two meetings in each current district. He indicated that the committee will then prepare and publish final plans and then adoption will take place by the Board of Supervisors. Mr. Moore spoke about Section 5 of the Voting Rights Act. Mr. Moore reviewed the participant roles to include the Board of Supervisors roles, the committee roles, county staff roles and the consultants. Mrs. Buckley will locate and set up meeting places in each district and will schedule with each member regarding dates and times. Mr. Moore answered questions of the committee regarding community meetings. Mr. Moore spoke about the pandemic and the possibility of virtual meetings. He indicated that he did not want to go that route and would like in person meetings. Mr. Moore continued to answer questions of the committee. Each member will do their own community meetings. He explained again that the goal of the redistricting process is that it is wrapped up by May 1.

Schedule Next Redistricting Advisory Committee Meeting: Mrs. Buckley indicated that she would be in touch with each of the committee members to discuss planning of community meetings and will further stay in touch regarding scheduling of the next Redistricting Advisory Committee meeting.

ADJOURN : At 4:28 p.m. the meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Ryan Stuart, Chairman
Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee
DATED: February 24, 2022

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee

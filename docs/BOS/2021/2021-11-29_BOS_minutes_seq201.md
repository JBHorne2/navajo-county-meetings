NAVAJO COUNTY REDISTRICTING ADVISORY COMMITTEE MINUTES
Monday, November 29, 2021

Present:

Mae-Gilene Begay, Member; Sadie Lister, Member; Ryan Stuart, Chairman; Rick Fernau, Vice-Chairman; Cline Griggs, Member

Staff Attendance:

Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

3:03 p.m. Call To Order : The meeting was called to order at 3:03 p.m. by Chairman Stuart.

Presentation of the Map Training Module :  Jason Moore indicated that Justin Levitt is here and will walk us through the mapping tool. Anyone interested in submitting a map should submit the map to Melissa Buckley.

Mr. Levitt discussed the mapping tool. He indicated that each person should create an account and sign up. He indicated that there is a link to the Navajo County’s mapping tool on the Navajo County Redistricting website. He showed the advisory members where to click on the map. He made a suggestion to make sure to click on the map colors and background map. He indicated that you can be in a view only mode. He demonstrated the paintbrush tool.

3:25 p.m. Cline Griggs is present.

Mr. Levitt demonstrated where to go to view the Statistics. Mr. Levitt discussed how to publish the map you have created and then share the map with Navajo County. Questions were asked and answered regarding the mapping tool. Discussion was held on the mapping tool.

ADJOURN : At 3:51 p.m. the meeting was adjourned.

APPROVED:

Ryan Stuart, Chairman
Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee
DATED: February 24, 2021

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee

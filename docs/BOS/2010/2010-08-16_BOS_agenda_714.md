NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES  
MONDAY, August 16, 2010  
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor was present in the Board Chambers. Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; David 
Tenney, Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present telephonically.  
9:02 a.m.  Call To Order: Supervisor DeSpain called the meeting to order.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC
 
: There were no public comments.  
FINANCE: Consideration and possible adoption of the tax rates to support the adopted 2010-11 budget
a. 
: 
Navajo County Public Schools’ 2010-11 tax rates
b. 
: Linda Morrow discussed the item and indicated that 
tax rates have gone down. Chairman Thompson made a motion  to approve the Navajo County Public 
Schools’ 2010-2011 tax rates as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney . Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
Navajo County 2010- 11 tax rates : James Menlove discussed the item. Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to approve the Navajo County 2010-2011 tax rates as presented; motion seconded by Chairman 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN
 
: At 9:11 a.m. Chairman Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

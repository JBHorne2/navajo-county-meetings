NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, December 28, 2010 
 
PRESENT:  Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and Jesse 
Thompson, Chairman were present in the Board Chambers.  
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Chairman Thompson. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Alberto Peshlakai recognized the Board of Supervisors with a plaque in 
appreciation of the White Cone Senior Center. The Board thanked Mr. Peshlakai and the people of White Cone.   
CONSENT AGENDA: a)  Constables Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1, November; Winslow Precinct #2, 
November; Snowflake Precinct #3, November; Show Low Precinct #5, November; Pinetop- Lakeside Precinct 
#6, November, 2010 b) Resolution Number 60 -10, approving a Special Use Permit for a Family Recreation 
Camp on a 16.01 acre portion of APN 208- 02-001B c) Contract signed by County Manager per Board of 
Supervisors Resolution: Johnson Walzer and Assoc iates, LLC for the Navajo County Holbrook Road Yard 
Facility and Photovoltaic Study Conceptual Architectural/Engineering Services in the amount of $16,900 d) 
Sympathy Letters to Percy Deal and Kendra Dale : Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve the consent 
agenda as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Supervisor Nez pointed out the sympathy 
letter written to Supervisor Percy Deal. He indicated that our hearts and prayers go out to the family. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence: Sheriff Clark recognized Derek Brewer 
on successfully completing the Western Arizona Academy.  
b. Swearing-in of newly elected and re-elected officials: Judge Ruechel swore in the newly elected officials 
and re-elected Officials. Deanne M. Romo, Clerk of the Superior Court; Ralph Hatch, Superior Court 
Judge, Division I; Phyllis Romo, Constable Precinct #1; Madeline Hernandez, Constable Precinct #2; 
Dave L. Wood, Constable Precinct #3; L ita Dixon, Constable Precinct #4; Scott Tipton, Constable 
Precinct #5; Ryan Reinhold, Constable Precinct #6; Evelyn G. Marez, Justice of the Peace, Precinct #1; 
Alison Kolomitz, Justice of the Peace, Precinct #2; Fred L. Peterson, Justice of the Peace, Pre cinct #3; 
Susie Nelson, Justice of the Peace, Precinct #4; and Stephen E. Price, Justice of the Peace, Precinct #5 
were all sworn in. David L. Widmaier, Justice of the Peace, Precinct #6 was unable to be present and 
will be sworn in at a later date.  
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: Gail Calisen reviewed the 
28 item personnel action report. Supervisor Tenney declared a conflict on item #26. Supervisor Nez made a  
motion to approve the personnel action report as prese nted; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

FINANCE:  
a. Consideration and possible approval of sale of scrap metal at sealed bid auction : Mary Springer 
discussed the item. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve the sale of scrap metal at a sealed 
bid auction; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.   
b. PUBLIC HEARING: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 62-10, adopting and 
approving the jail usage fees Ordinance, Ordinance Number 08- 10: Dusty Parsons discussed the item. 
Supervisor Tenney made comments. Supervisor Nez raised some concerns. Brad Carlyon indicated that 
he will bring in some community leaders from the  faith based community, political leaders and leaders 
of the Hopi and Navajo Nation to get some brainstorming done to break the cycle. Chairman Thompson 
shared his thoughts and concerns. Brad Carlyon shared information on the costs. Sheriff Clark addressed 
the question of Chairman Thompson. Chairman Thompson opened up the public meeting, there being no 
comment, closed the public meeting. Judge Kolomitz made comments regarding fees charged in 
Winslow. Supervisor Brownlow indicated his support of what has been presented. He indicated that he 
did get a call from Supervisor DeSpain and he is in support of this  item as well. Supervisor Brownlow 
made a motion to adopt  Resolution Number 62 -10, adopting and approving the jail usage fees 
Ordinance, Ordinance Number 08 -10; motion seconded by  Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
c. 
County Finance Update: James Menlove gave an update on the County Finances.  
PUBLIC WORKS:  
a. Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 63- 10 approving Waste Tire Tipping Fees 
be eliminated : Montana Slack discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Supervisor 
Tenney discussed an issue with a local tire company in the Show Low area. Supervisor Tenney proposed 
the elimination of the fee other than the O ff Road Tire Fees . Dusty  Parsons agreed with Supervisor 
Tenney. Discussion was held. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 63-10, 
suspending the waste tire tipping fees established by Resolution Number 24-07 except the Off Road Tire 
Fee for one year, effective 30 days after this date; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow . Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES  
EMERGENCY MEETING 
THURSDAY, JANUARY 21, 2010 
 
PRESENT: Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; David 
Tenney, Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present telephonically. Also present County Attorney 
Brad Carlyon.   
9:35 a.m. Call to Order by Chairman Thompson.  
Chairman Thompson indicated that t he emergency meeting has been requested by the Navajo County 
Emergency Services Director, due to weather conditions in Navajo County which have caused serious problems 
and have exceeded our resource capacity. An emergency exists because of the severe snowfall and the potential 
countywide flooding resulting from the current storm system.  
Consideration and possible adoption  of Resolution ____ -10, of the Nava jo County Board of Supervisors 
declaring an emergency in the unincorporated areas of Navajo County pursuant to ARS §26-311: Dusty Parsons 
reported on the weather conditions in Navajo County. He reported that Holbrook has had light rain, Heber has 
received 12 inches of new snow, Show Low has received 6 inches of  new snow and is still falling and Pinetop 
has received 2-3 inches of new snow on top of the 12 -16 inches already on the ground. He further reported on 
the water res ervoirs which included Show Low at 6 -8 ft from the spillway, Rainbow Lake at 2ft to top, 
Woodland Lake the water is running over and Pine Lake Meadow and Fool Hollow are being checked on.  Mr. 
Parsons further reported on road conditions including I-17 South of Flagstaff which is closed, State Route 260 
from Payson to Heber is closed, State Route 60 from Globe to Show Low require chains or 4X4, and other  
vehicles are being turned around.  Mr. Parsons continued to discuss the item  and answered questions of the  
Board. Trent Larson indicated that the Army Corps, City of Winslow and the City of Holbrook have been 
notified that Navajo County Flood Control is closely monitoring the Winslow Levee. Supervisor Tenney made 
a motion to adopt Resolution 04 -10, declaring an emergency in the unincorporated areas of Navajo County 
pursuant to ARS §26-311; motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
Chairman Thompson approved the Clerk of the Board to use his signature stamp for the Resolution.  
ADJOURN: At 9: 55 a.m.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
DeSpain. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

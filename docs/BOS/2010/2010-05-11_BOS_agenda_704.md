BOARD OF SUPERVISORS : Presentation to employees retu rning from National Guard Duty : Chairman 
Thompson extended his appreciation to those that have served our C ountry. Caleb Davis, Corban Davis, Janine 
Jones, Timothy Toney, Eric Baldonado, Brian Brown, David Hummer, Scott Martin and Dave Rush were 
recognized for their service to our Country. Chairman Thompson stated that w e pray for each of them and their 
safety.  Sheriff Clark thanked those returning for their sacrifice and service. Dottie Smith Baine sang a song that 
she wrote . Supervisor Tenney thank ed the returning employees for all that they did and continue to do and 
further stated that we look up to each of them and so do all the youngsters out there. Supervisor Nez thanked the 
returning employees. Supervisor DeSpain thank ed the returning employees and welcomed them all home. 
Supervisor Brownlow thanked the returning employees as well as their f amilies. James Jayne gave blessings on 
their service to our Country and welcomed them each home.  
COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE : Supervisor Brownlow reported on meetings held in Washington DC last 
week, indicating that he believes it was a good trip. Supervisor DeSpain passed around pictures of the Woodruff 
Library, he indicated that the people are thankful for the new parking lot. Supervisor Nez reported that Public 
Works has been hauling gravel out to various communities for continued road improvements. He indicated that 
the people o f those communities are very grateful. He further reported on the White Mountain Apache Tribe 
Inauguration. Supervisor Tenney thanked everyone for their condolences for the passing of his grandmother. He 
further reported on a very successful trip to Washin gton DC, indicating that there was a total of 28 members at 
the meeting representing the forest service. Chairman Thompson reported on the drought and a Navajo Taco 
Lunch being held in appreciation of the Board. Mr. Jayne reminded the Board of the upcoming budget meetings 
the afternoon of the 25th and the morning of the 26th.

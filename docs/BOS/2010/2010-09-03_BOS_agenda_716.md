SPECIAL MEETING 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
FRIDAY, September 3, 2010   
 
PRESENT:  Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; and  
Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present.  
9:10 a.m.  Call To Order:  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No public present 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a)Additional 5 year lease term upon identical terms and conditions for the Show Low 
Historical Society to lease Navajo County building located at 561 E. Deuce of Clubs, Show Low, Arizona 
85901 parcel 210-19-016; Lease shall commence on September 20, 2010 and end on September 19, 2015: 
Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve the additional 5 year lease term upon identical terms and 
conditions for the Show Low Historical Society to lease Navajo County building located at 561 E. Deuce of 
Clubs, Show Low, Arizona 85901 parcel 210-19-016; Lease shall commence on September 20,2010 and end on 
September 19, 2015; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the Primary Election conducted 
on August 24, 2010 Johnathan Roes presented the item: Supervisor DeSpain asked if provision and early votes 
have been counted. Johnathan Roes advised they were. Supervisor Nez made a motion to approve the Official 
Canvass of the Primary Election conducted on August 24, 2010; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:14 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

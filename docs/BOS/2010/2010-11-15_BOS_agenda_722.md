NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
MONDAY, November 15, 2010  
SPECIAL BOARD MEETING  
 
PRESENT:  David Tenney, Supervisor and J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor were present. Jonathan M. Nez, 
Supervisor; Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present telephonically.  
9:35 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor Tenney. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.  
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the General Election conducted 
November 2, 2010:  Lisa McKee  discussed the item.  Supervisor DeSpain made a motion  to approve  the 
Official Canvass of the General Election conducted November 2, 2010;  motion seconded by  Chairman 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Consideration and possible approval of the Board of Equalization Hearing 
Officer’s recommendations on hearings conducted on November 10, 2010 for Petition for Review  of Notice of 
Change: Melissa Buckley , Clerk of the Board discussed the item. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to 
approve of the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendations on hearings conducted on November 
10, 2010 for Petition for Review of N otice of Change ; motion seconded by  Supervisor Brownlow. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
ADJOURN: At 9:42 a.m. Supervisor Nez  made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

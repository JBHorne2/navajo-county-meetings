indicated that it would not affect our reimbursements and further indicated again that we are in a 
recovery phase and we will continue to help those that need help. Supervisor Nez  made comments 
regarding his concerns regarding reimbursements and continuing help on the Navajo Nation. Chairman 
Thompson indicated tha t we will continue to utilize our MOA with the BIA and the Nation. Mr. 
Parsons indicated that we will continue to do what we need to do for our County  and if we need to 
declare again, we will. Mr. Parsons further indicated that w e will not put people in ha rm’s way in either 
community. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve declaring an end to the emergency adopted 
by Resolution Number 04 -10 on January 21, 2010 ; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

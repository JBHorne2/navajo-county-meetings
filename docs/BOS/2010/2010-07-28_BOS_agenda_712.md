SPECIAL NOTICE OF EMERGENCY MEETING  
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Wednesday, July 28, 2010 
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor  was present in person; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor ; Jerry Brownlow, 
Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and Jonathan Nez, Supervisor were present telephonically. 
CALL TO ORDER: At 4:31 p.m. Supervisor J.R. DeSpain called the meeting to order.  
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT : Consideration and possible a doption of Resolution Number 47 -10, 
declaring an emergency in support of the  Hopi Tribe, Executive Order #03- 2010 Declaration: emergency 
situation due to heavy rains in First Mesa Consolidated Villages and other Hopi Villages pursuant to A.R.S. 
§26-311: Dusty Parsons discussed the item. Supervisor Tenney  made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 
47-10, declaring an emergency in support of the Hopi Tribe, Executive Order #03-2010 Declaration: emergency 
situation due to heavy rains in First Mesa Consolidated Villages and other Hopi Villages pursuant to A.R.S. 
§26-311; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous to approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 4:40 p.m. Supervisor Nez made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, January 12, 2010 
 
PRESENT:  J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Chairman were present.  
9:08 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Chairman Brownlow. 
Steve Peru, Coconino County Manager, was welcomed by Chairman Brownlow.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Election of Chairman and Vice-Chairman for 2010: Chairman Brownlow indicated that it had been a 
pleasure to be the Chairman and thanked the other Bo ard Members. He further thanked the Clerk of the 
Board and the Board of Supervisors staff, the County Manager, Sheriff, and County Attorney for all the 
support. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to nominate Jesse Thompson as Chairman for the Board of 
Supervisors for the year 2010 and nominated David Tenney as the Vice Chairman; motion seconded by 
Chairman Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion. He further thanked Chairman Brownlow for 
his outstanding chairmanship. Chairman Thompson thanked the Board for their support.   
b. Presentation of Plaque of Appreciation for 2009 Service: Chairman Thompson recognized Supervisor 
Brownlow in appreciation for his committed service to Navajo County as Chairman of the Board of 
Supervisors for the year 2009.  
c. Navajo County Cost Cutting Department Award: Dusty Parsons recognized the Public Defender’s office for 
maintaining consistent performance despite department vacancies.  
d. Personnel Service Awards: Delanna Caraway recognized Darren Carlson, Anna Eggbert, Craig Burtchwell, 
Laurette Justman and Ralph Hatch for their years of service to Navajo County.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No public comment.

Brownlow thanked the citizens and the Sheriff’s office for the presentations  on both sides of the issue.

1625 a reportable transaction fee in the amount of  $3.00, payable in such manner and frequency as the

FINANCE: ADJUDICATIVE HEARING (Protest Appeal of RFP F-10-07-01, Inmate Telephone System):  
The Board, pursuant to procurement regulation R2-7-A905 of the Arizona Administrative Code as adopted by 
Navajo County, shall hear and decide an appeal by Value Added Communications, Inc., from the chief procurement 
officer’s denial of the company’s protest of the award of contract number F-10-07-01, Inmate Telephone System, to 
Securus Technologies.  The hearing shall be conducted in  accordance with the provision s of A.R.S. § 41-1092.07: 
Mary Springer discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Mrs. Springer asked that this matter be put 
out for rebid. Representative Jim Crouch with VAC spoke briefly. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to reverse 
the denial of the protest by the Purchasing office, uphold the appeal and order that the Inmate Telephone System go 
back out for another RFP selection; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

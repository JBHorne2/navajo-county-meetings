NOTICE OF SPECIAL MEETING 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
WEDNESDAY, June 9, 2010 
 
PRESENT: Jesse Thompson, Chairman; David Tenney, Vice-Chairman; J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jerry 
Brownlow, Supervisor; and Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor were present. 
9:00 a.m. : Call To Order: Supervisor Brownlow led the Pledge of Allegiance and the Invocation. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No public comment. 
BUDGET HEARINGS FOR DISCUSSION ONLY:  James Menlove provided further explanation on the 
budget packets provided to the board members. Supervisor DeSpain commented. 
 
a. Facilities Management : Dan Hinz provided budget presentation to the Board.  Supervisor DeSpain  
 commented on the presentation.  
b. Elections: James Menlove presented on behalf of the Elections Department.  
c. Information Technology: Bob Hemming presented his departmental budget to the Board. Supervisor 
 Tenney commented on the item. Supervisor DeSpain commented further. Assistant County Manager 
 Parsons also provided input. Supervisor Brownlow made an inquiry and comment. 
d. Public Fiduciary : Brent Turley made a presentation to the Board on behalf of Sherry Reed. James 
 Menlove provided additional input. Supervisor DeSpain  inquired about certain costs from previous 
 budgets in comparison to current budget.   
e. Workforce Investment Act: Judy Bratcher provided budget information to the members of the Board. 
 She indicated that WIA w ill be providing services without stimulus funds this year. James Menlove 
 provided further input. Supervisor DeSpain commented.  
f. Navajo County Library District: Board of Directors’ Session: Supervisor Brownlow made a motion 
to enter into the Navajo County Library District Board of Director’s Session ; motion seconded by 
Supervisor DeSpain. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Geneva Durkee gave her presentation to 
the members of the Board. Supervisor Brownlow  commented on the item. Supervisor Ten ney also 
commented on the item.  Supervisor Tenney  made a motion to return to regular session; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
g. Navajo County Public Health Services District: Board of Directors’ Session: Supervisor Brownlow 
made a motion to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Dir ectors 
Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney . Vote unanimous approving the motion. Dr. 
Kartchner presented his departmental budget to the Board. Supervisor Brownlow  inquired on one of 
the items. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to return to regular session; the motion was seconded by 
Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
h. Public Works: Homero Vela presented the department budget to the Board. 
i. Navajo County Flood Control District: Board of Directors’ Session: Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to enter into the Navajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors Session; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Nez . Vote unanimous approving the motion. Homero Vela p resented the 
budget for Flood Control District.  Supervisor Brownlow made a moti on to return to regular session; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, November 9, 2010 
 
PRESENT:  Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were 
present.  
8:05 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Melissa Buckley. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No Public Comment. 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Constable Reports: Show Low Precinct #5, October 2010 b)County Attorney Bad 
Check Program Quarterly Report  c)Warrant & Voucher R eport over $1000, October 2010 d)Assessment and 
Tax Roll Correction Resolution e)Contract signed by County Manager per Board of Supervisors Resolution: 
Contract for Professional Services for Subdivision Reconciliation and Automation with Terra Genesis: 
Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to approve the consent agenda as presented; motion seconded by  
Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Consideration and possible adoption of the 2011 Board of Supervisors’ 
Meeting Schedule: Melissa Buckley discussed the item. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to adopt the 
2011 Board of Supervisors’ Meeting Schedule; motion seconded by  Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT: Consideration and possible approval to set a Public Hearing on December 
14, 2010 to take action on the proposed Navajo County Fireworks Ordinance : Dan Hinz discussed the item. 
Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve setting a Public Hearing on December 14, 2010 to take action 
on the proposed Navajo County Fireworks Ordinance; motion seconded by  Supervisor Brownlow. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 8:11 a.m. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

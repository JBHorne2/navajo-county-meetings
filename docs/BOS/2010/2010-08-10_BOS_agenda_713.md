NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, August 10, 2010  
 
PRESENT
 
:  Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; David 
Tenney, Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present.  
9:05 a.m.  Call To Order
 
: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Supervisor Tenney.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC:  Eduardo Coronado indicated that he represents Phyllis Romo, the Holbrook 
Constable. Mr. Coronado indicated that Ms. Romo’s concerns were in regards to agenda item 8a, he indicated 
that there is a disparity in pay between constables in the precincts of Navajo County and wishes the board to 
address it. He further indicated that the Constable in Holbrook is under paid and the raise does not reflect the 
amount of fillings that she makes and is constantly making filings and helping other constables in the County 
and saving the county money by serving inmates in the county jail.   
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Constable Reports: Winslow Precinct #2, July and Pinetop-Lakeside #6, July 2010 
b) Warrant & Voucher Report over $1000 July 2010 c) Review and Authorize the Board of Supervisors to sign 
the Apache/Navajo Counties Regional Sustainability Consortium Memorandum of Understanding for the 
submission of the HUD Sustainable Communities Planning Grant application d) Assessment and tax roll 
corrections: Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to approve the consent agenda as presented; motion seconded 
by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
SUPERIOR COURT: Presentation to retiring Probation Officer Jack Schneider: Arno Hall made a 
presentation regarding Probation Officer Jack Schneider. Mr. Hall indicated that due to health concerns Mr. 
Schneider was unable to be present. Mr. Hall thanked Navajo County on Mr. Schneider’s behalf. Chairman 
Thompson wished Mr. Schneider well.

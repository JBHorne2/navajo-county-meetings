PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and possible adoption of Ordinance Number 01 -10, requiring 
pawnbrokers to electronically report pawn transactions to the Sheriff and to pay reportable transaction 
fees to the Sheriff on a calendar -month basis: Sheriff K.C. Clark discussed the item and explained the 
difference on this Ordinance is on page 1 (i.e. not on re-pawns or extensions). Mr. Carlyon indicated that 
as part of the motion we will need to strike the language “re -pawns or” in the O rdinance. Supervisor 
DeSpain asked what the difference is between the Resolution passed previously and the Ordinance . Mr.

Carlyon advised that we are not changing what we did back in January. Sheriff Clark indicated that this 
is basically putting the rest of the teeth into the ordinance indicating that electronic reporting will be 
used, requiring a photo of the person and the item along with an electronic fingerprint, and consequences 
of not following the ordinance. The item was further discussed. Supervisor  DeSpain asked if we have 
authority to suspend pawnbroker’s license. Mr. Carlyon indicated that yes, Arizona Statute allows you to 
have the ordinance and gives you the teeth that if they ignore the ordinance then you can pull the license. 
Chairman opened the public hearing. Hartley Turley addressed the Board regarding the item. Chairman 
Thompson indicated that the concerns made have been heard and understood. Ben Hatch , owner of a 
pawnshop in Winslow Arizona  addressed the Board  regarding the item. Sheriff Clark addressed the 
Board regarding Mr. Turley’s concerns. Chairman Thompson closed the public hearing. Supervisor Nez 
indicated that some type of coordination needs to begin with the tribal police. Supervisor Nez 
commented further regarding the item and i ndicated support of a task team to look into coordination 
with other law enforcement agencies within Navajo County. Supervisor  Nez made a motion to adopt 
Ordinance Number 01 -10, requiring pawnbrokers to electronically report pawn transactions to the 
Sheriff and to pay reportable transaction fees to the Sheriff on a calendar -month basis with the 
amendment striking “re-pawns or”; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow . Supervisor Brownlow 
indicated that he visited with White Mountain Pawn and the statistics there indicated that half of their 
business is with Native Americans down south and agree d with the comments made by Supervisor  Nez 
regarding Tribal Police. Supervisor DeSpain thanked Mr. Hatch and Mr. Turley for being present. 
Supervisor DeSpain stated that in January the Board ensured that the monies would be used for the 
program and nothing else  and further stated that the Board will review this in a year and make 
modifications if necessary. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

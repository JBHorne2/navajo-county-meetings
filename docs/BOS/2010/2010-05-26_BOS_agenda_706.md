SPECIAL MEETING  
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
WEDNESDAY, May 26, 2010  
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor ; Dusty Parsons, Assistant County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of 
the Board; and Lisa McKee, Elections Director were present in person ; Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor and Jesse 
Thompson, Chairman were present telephonically.    
9:08 a.m.  Call To Order:  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No public comment. 
ELECTION SERVICES: Consideration and possible official canvass of the Special Election conducted 
on May 18, 2010: Lisa McKee discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Supervisor Brownlow 
made a motion to approve the official canvass of the Special Election conducted on May 18, 2010; motion 
seconded by Chairman Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:11 a.m. Chairman Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

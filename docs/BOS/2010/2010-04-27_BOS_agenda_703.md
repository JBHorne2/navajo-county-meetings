JOSEPH CITY FIRE DISTRICT/ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval to convert the Joseph 
City Fire District form a Fire District governed by an Elected Chief and Secretary Treasurer to a District 
governed by a five -person governing board, to be effective beginning with the first terms of office that will

date of H.B. 2666 (to be codified as new A.R.S. 48 -804 (D): Supervisor DeSpain indicated that he has a 
conflict due to his son Rusty DeSpain being the Fire Chief for Joseph City Fire District and left the room 
at 10:07 a.m. Jason Moore discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Rusty DeSpain discussed 
the item further and indicated that this is something that the District has been working towards.  He indicated 
that there is a current advisory board and four of the five m embers are willing to continue. Supervisor 
Brownlow made a motion to approve to convert the Joseph City Fire District from a Fire District governed by 
an Elected Chief and Secretary Treasurer to a District governed by a five -person governing board, to be 
effective beginning with the first terms of office that will commence immediately after the next regular election 
for the Joseph City Fire District and after the effective date of H.B. 2666 (to be codified as new A.R.S. 48 -804 
(D); motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

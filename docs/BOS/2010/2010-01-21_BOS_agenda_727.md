NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ EMERGENCY MEETING ACTION REPORT

conditions in Navajo County which have caused serious  problems and have exceeded our resource capacity. An 
emergency exists because of the severe snowfall and the potential countywide flooding resulting from the 
current storm system.

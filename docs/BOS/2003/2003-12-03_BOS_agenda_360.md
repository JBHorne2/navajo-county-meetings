BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
Special Meeting 
December 3,  2003 
 
Supervisors' Conference Room, Holbrook, Arizona - Time: 1:34 p.m. 
 
PRESENT : Present in Conference Room: Jerry Brownlow, Member, Jimmy Jayne, County Manager; 
June Foard, Deputy Clerk.  Present by conference call: Members Jesse Thompson; Percy Deal (Mr. 
Shumway and Mr. DeSpain were not able to be present) 
 
PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT: Bill Cox, Director; Discussion and possible board action re: 
Approval to increase the per ton cost for asphalt concrete for the paving of the Juvenile Detention parking 
lot. Mr. Bill Cox explained the additional monies the contractor was asking for the job for paving the 
Juvenile Detention parking lot.  The contract amount for asphalt is for $41.75 per ton.  He explained that 
due to the temperature and the need for the job to be completed as soon as possible, the contractor was 
requesting an additional $20.00 per ton for the materials.  The increase would result in a total of $61.75 
per ton. Mr. Deal asked if the funding was available for the project and Mr. Cox reported it is available 
through the Jail Funds.  Mr. Deal made a motion to modify the contract to add the $20.00 per ton for 
asphalt for the paving of the Juvenile Detention parking lot; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
 
.BOARD BUSINESS
: None 
 
There being no further business to come before the Board of Supervisors, the meeting was adjourned 
until Monday, December15, 2003, upon a motion made by Mr.  Thompson; motion seconded by Mr. 
Deal; vote unanimously carried.  The meeting adjourned at 1:50 p.m.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
 
December 23,  2003 
 
Supervisors' Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona - Time: 10:15 a.m. 
 
PRESENT : Pete Shumway, Chairman; J.R. DeSpain, Vice-Chairman; Percy Deal, Member; Jesse 
Thompson, Member; Jerry Brownlow, Member; Mel Bowers, Jr., County Attorney; Jimmy Jayne, County 
Manager; and Kathy Hieb, Deputy Clerk of the Board. (Mr. Deal present by conference call; Mr. 
Thompson appeared by conference call at 11:00 a.m.) 
 
Mr. DeSpain led the Pledge of Allegiance and offered the Invocation. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Pete Shumway, Chairman: 1) Executive Session: Pursuant to ARS 
§38-431.03 (A)(1) for Personnel Matters Re: Possible selection of applicant to fill Finance 
Director position (continued from 12/16/03): Mr. DeSpain made a motion to enter into Executive 
Session; motion seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote unanimously carried. Present in the session were:

Thompson left the meeting at this time due to other commitments) 
 
2) Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Arizona Game and Fish Department, USDA Animal and 
Plant Health Inspection Service/Wildlife Services, White Mountain Apache Tribe, Arizona Counties of 
Graham, Greenlee, New Mexico Counties of Catron and Sierra and the New Mexico Department of 
Agriculture to establish a framework for adaptively managing the Mexican Wolf reintroduction project to 
contribute toward recovery: Mr. DeSpain explained that this MOU will allow for each participant on the 
committee to be “at the table” and vote on the issues involved. Mr. DeSpain  made a motion to approve 
the MOU with Arizona Game and Fish Department and others as presented; motion seconded by Mr. 
Brownlow; vote unanimously carried. (Mr. Deal and Mr. Thompson absent for the vote)  
 
3) Possible changes in Navajo County  Health Insurance Contract with Mountain States: Mr. Jayne 
explained that the employee’s health insurance deductible will increase to $250 as of January 1, 2004. In 
order to try and mitigate this increase for the employees, a proposal has been presented to move in a 
$25 co-payment for office visits, lab and x-ray services, and non-surgical tests. Karen Bray gave an 
example of how this will be a cost savings for the employee: Under the current plan, an employee incurs 
an office visit -$100. The deductible not  having yet been met, the plan would pay nothing, and the 
employee pays $100, leaving another $150 to still pay on his deductible. Under the new proposal, the 
employee pays $25, and the plan pays $75. The $250 deductible would apply only to in/out patient 
hospital services, or any surgical procedure. Under the proposed plan, an employee would have to visit 
his doctor 10 times during the plan year to equal the current deductible. It should be noted that if an 
employee has seen the doctor 10 or more times during the year, and then needs a surgical procedure, he 
would still need to meet the $250 deductible. The co-pays for office visits do not go toward the deductible. 
 It should also be noted that the co-pay applies only to participating providers with BCBS. If the employee 
chooses to go out of network, the services would still be subject to deductible and coinsurance.

would be as follows: projected claims costs (per employee)-$5091; cost to add the $25 co-pay (for 6

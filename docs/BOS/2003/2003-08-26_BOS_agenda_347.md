BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
SPECIAL MEETING 
AUGUST 26,  2003 
 
Supervisors' Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona - Time: 9:00 AM 
 
PRESENT: Pete Shumway, Chairman; J.R. DeSpain, Vice -Chairman; Percy Deal, Member; Jesse 
Thompson, Member; Jerry Brownlow, Member; Mel Bowers, Jr., County Attorney; Dennis Miller, Interim 
County Manager; and Judy Jones, Clerk of the Board. 
 
Mr. DeSpain led the Pledge of Allegiance and offered the Invocation. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS : Executive Session: Pursuant to ARS §38-431.03 (A)(1) for Personnel 
Matters Re: Interview of applicants for the County Manager position: Mr. DeSpain made a motion to enter 
into executive session; motion seconded by Chairman Shumway; vote unanimously carried. 
 
Present: Pete Shumway, Chairman; J.R. DeS pain, Vice -Chairman; Percy Deal, Member; Jesse 
Thompson, Member; Jerry Brownlow, Member; Mel Bowers, Jr., County Attorney; Dennis Miller, Interim  
County Manager; and Judy Jones, Clerk of the Board, Lori Justman, Selection Committee member and 
Doug Muell, Review Committee member.  
 
Mr. DeSpain made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote 
unanimously carried.  After interviewing three applicants; Carlos Rivera, Jimmy Jayne and Ron Lee (one 
applicant withdrew) the Board directed Interim County Manager, Dennis Miller to contact Mr. Jayne to 
make an offer of the County Manager position.  Mr. Miller reported back to the Board that Mr. Jayne 
gratefully accepted. Formal appointment and payroll change will be approved by the Board at their next 
regular meeting on September 2, 2003.   
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :  Executive Session: Pursuant to ARS §38-431.03 (A)(1) for Personnel 
Matters Re: Reconvene Executive Session held on August 11 & 18, 2003: Discuss applicants interviewed 
for the Human Resource position:  Mr. Deal made a motion to enter into executive session; motion 
seconded by Mr. DeSpain; vote unanimously carried.  
 
Present: Pete Shumway, Chairman; J.R. DeSpain, Vice -Chairman; Percy Deal, Member; Jesse 
Thompson, Member; Jerry Brownlow, Member; Mel Bowers, Jr., County Attorney; Dennis Miller, Interim 
County Manager; and Judy Jones, Clerk of the Board, and Doug Muell, Review Committee member. Mr. 
Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Mr. Deal; vote unanimously 
carried.  The Board requested the Clerk to schedule a second round of interviews for the Human Resource 
position.  The Clerk will contact Grady Brown and Gail Sandoval to be present for a second interview on 
Tuesday, September 2, 2003. 
 
EMERGENCY ITEM: The emergency item was at the request of Supervisor Percy Deal.  Supervisor Deal 
requested the Board place an emergency item on the agenda due to the recent rains received on 8/23/03. 
 A main BIA/Navajo Route N61 was completely washed out at Burnt Corn Wash crossing and the culverts 
have washed away, leaving no reliable road to be used for school busses, senior citizen deliveries and 
CHR/Home Attendants.  This emergency situation is impacting approximately 50 families. Funding 
assistance is needed immediately to purchase culverts so the road can be repaired. 
 
The emergency exists pursuant to ARS §38-431.02(D), inability to get this item placed on the Agenda 
prior to notice of the emergency road conditions affecting the health and welfare of more than 50 families. 
 
Mr. Deal made a motion to approve funds from District I Special Road Funds to purchase culverts to 
repair BIA/Navajo Route N61 at Burnt Corn Wash (amount to be determined - $1720 per culvert); motion

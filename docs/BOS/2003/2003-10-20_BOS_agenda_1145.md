NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
BOARD ACTION NOTIFICATION 
MONDAY, OCTOBER 20, 2003 
 
CONSENT AGENDA: Approved 
 
RETIREMENT PLAQUE:  Lurlene James 5 Years of service to Navajo County Citizens since August 1998  
 
SEPT/OCT ANNIVERSARY CERTIFICATES: Presented 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Resolution in Memoriam of Army Specialist Spencer Timothy Karol, who 
Lost his Life while Supporting Operation Iraqi Freedom Continued 
 
HEALTH DISTRICT: Request to purchase equipment needed in the Nutrition Program, funding to come 
from the Nutrition Network grant Approved 
 
HARDROCK COMMUNITY EXPORT PROJECT :  Update on University of Arizona 5 Year Partnership 
regarding the EXPO RT (Excellence in Partnerships for Community Outreach, Research on Health 
Disparities and Training) Project with Hardrock Community Presented 
 
NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRICT: Approve request by the Town of Snowflake obtain 
funds for engineering services contingent upon receiving a revised Scope of Work acceptable to the 
County Engineer Approved $10,000 
 
PUBLIC WORKS: Discussion regarding Navajo County’s position on formulating a standardized process 
for rural consultation with ADOT Supported rural forum approach 
 
HOTEVILLA VILLAGE : Financial assistance from District II Special Project Funds to purchase 
tools/equipment for the Youth/Elderly Construction Project Approved $400 
 
BUILDING & GROUNDS : Extension of temporary custodian for up to 60 days to cover for an industrial 
injury and an employee who will be on vacation Approved 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Board meeting schedule and agenda back-up Approved (2 meetings per 
month) 1st & 3rd Monday.  Agenda back-up will include detailed written description of each item.  
 
COUNTY MANAGER REPORT: Current Events Presented

concerning Bioterrorism Defense Manager Accepted resignation Dr. Ray Nejeres

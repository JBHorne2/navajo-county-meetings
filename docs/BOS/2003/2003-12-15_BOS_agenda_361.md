COUNTY ATTORNEY/SHERIFF: Mel Bowers, County Attorney and Gary Butler, Sheriff: 1) Proposal to 
use funds from the Southwest Border Prosecution Initiative (SWBPI) Award to fund a Criminal Justice 
Grant Administrator: Mr. Bowers that he would like to use $200,000 of the SWBPI funds to fund the 
position of a Criminal Justice Grants Administrator in the Finance Department for a period of two years.  
After that time, the position should be self funded by the percentage allocated for administrative expenses 
in each of the grants. The County already has several Criminal Justice grants that greatly benefit the 
County Attorney, Legal Defender, Public Defender, Sheriff and the Superior Court. There are many 
additional Criminal Justice grant opportunities that a full-time Criminal Justice Grants Administrator could 
explore. Many of the grants and grant opportunities involve more than one Department, so it would be very 
beneficial to have an Administrator who could see the "big picture" and coordinate the grant proposals. 
The Criminal Justice Grants Administrator would have to be an individual with considerable technical 
expertise in this field. The position would involve exploring grant opportunities, writing grant proposals 
and meeting the financial reporting and accounting requirements.  It is felt that this position should report 
directly to the Finance Director. Knowing that the position is expected to be self-funding in two years 
would motivate the Administrator to explore all available grant opportunities. The committee believes this 
proposal is a wise investment with a potential return far in excess of the $200,000 in "seed money" that is 
being recommended. If the program proves to be as successful as we anticipate, it could perhaps be 
expanded to include a broader range of grant responsibilities (i.e., beyond the area of Criminal Justice). 
Mr. Deal made a motion to approve the expenditure of $200,000 from the SWBPI funds for the Grants 
Administrator position; motion seconded by Mr. DeSpain; vote unanimous approving the vote.  Mr. Deal 
requested that a budget for the request be presented at a later date. 2) Proposal to use funds from the

Southwest Border Prosecution Initiative (SWBPI) Award to cover Jail operations personnel costs for the 
first half of FY03-04: Sheriff Butler made a recommendation to the Board that $300,000 of the SWBPI 
funds for funding personnel positions that have been short funded by DOC.  In FY 03/04 Navajo County 
entered into an agreement with DOC to house state prisoners in the County Jail.  Due to the change in 
command at DOC, the county has not received the funding and because the amount in the contract was 
for personnel services and included in the 03/04 Jail Budget, they were able to keep the Jail operational.  
However, due to the issue of payment to Navajo and Coconino Counties being referred to the Legislature 
and that funding not being approved yet, there will be a loss to Jail operations.  The $300,000 is needed 
to keep from freezing positions and closing down a portion of the Jail. If this happens, the number of 
Federal Inmates that could be housed could be affected.  This proposal is consistent with what the 
SWBPI funds should be used for and this would be a reimbursement for funds already expended by the 
Jail.  Mr. Deal made a motion to approve the expenditure of $300,000 from the SWBPI funds for Jail 
Operations and personnel; motion seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the vote

years.  Although the act requirs a plan to be adopted by December 3, 2003, there are not legal 
ramifications if a board does not adopt one.  The legislation says that the county’s current plan would

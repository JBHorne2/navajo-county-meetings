BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
 
SPECIAL MEETING 
MAY 22,  2003 
  
Supervisors' Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona - Time: 3:30 p.m. 
 
PRESENT : J.R. DeSpain, Vice -Chairman; Percy Deal, Member; Jesse Thompson, Member; Jerry 
Brownlow, Member; Lance Payette, Deputy County Attorney; Eddie Koury, County Manager; and  Kathy 
Hieb, Deputy Clerk of the Board. (Chairman Pete Shumway was enroute from meetings in Washington, 
D.C., and was delayed in Chicago) 
 
Mr. Deal led the Pledge of Allegiance and gave the invocation. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS/WORKSESSION:  1) Transition committee recommendations for interim 
County Manager position: (Mr. Shumway was present briefly by conference call, but was disconnected 
approximately 10 minutes into the discussion) Transition Committee Chairman, Dan Hinz, presented 
information regarding the candidates considered by the committee for the position of interim county 
manager. Also included in the information were the minutes from the committee’s meetings and the 
recommendations. Mr. Hinz advised that the committee spent considerable time looking at all aspects of 
this matter. He said that the committee did look at the possibility of utilizing a “head hunter” group to look 
for an interim manager. The cost would be approximately $25,000 initially, plus another 2% per $1000 of 
the salary eventually paid. This seemed to be cost prohibitive.  
Having carefully reviewed all of the candidates, with a paramount emphasis given to the needs of the 
County, the committee recommends hiring Dennis Miller, of Dennis Miller & Associates, as the interim 
county manager. A break-down of the estimated cost was presented: Mr. Miller would be here three days 
a week from May 26-July 14, 2003. Professional services @$75/hour-$14,400; County per diem for hotel 
and meals-$880 and $708, respectively; Total (without mileage)-$15,988. (Mr. Miller is a pilot and flies 
his own plane. Reimbursement for mileage would be $.42/air mile, but the exact mileage is unknown at 
this time.) A county vehicle will be available for Mr. Miller’s use while he is in Holbrook. Mr. Hinz noted that 
Mr. Miller is comfortable in working with the budget, and working with the Legislature. If approved, Mr. 
Miller could start work here as early as Tuesday, May 27 to allow him to work with Mr. Koury and Mr. 
Shreeve before they leave the county employ.  
In discussion regarding the source of funds for payment to Mr. Miller, it was noted that there will be a 
vacancy savings of $24,500 per month. This should be more than adequate to meet Mr. Miller’s salary. 
Mr. Hinz advised that he has spoken with Mr. Miller and he is willing to come immediately, without any 
stipulations. Mr. Deal made a motion to hire Dennis Miller as Interim County Manager as recommended, 
with the cost not to exceed $16,000 plus air and/or road mileage; that he be provided with a county 
vehicle; and that the costs be taken from vacancy savings; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
 
The Board requested that Mr. Payette prepare an appointment letter to Mr. Miller, indicating that he will be 
working at the pleasure of the Board, for the Chairman’s signature. Mr. DeSpain requested that an item 
be placed on the agenda for June 2, 2003 for discussion/action regarding direction for the Transition 
Committee. 
 
2) Tentative approval of retiree health coverage rates:
 Human Resources Director Gilbert Gonzales  
explained that in FY 2001/2002, the Arizona Legislature authorized a “rural subsidy” of $300 and $600 in 
addition to the base subsidies of $150 and $260. That addition was authorized for two years, which will

end on June 30, 2003. In July, 2001, the Board of Supervisors authorized retiree health care premiums of 
$450 for single coverage and $860 for the retiree and family. The subsidies covered the entire premium. 
These rates were renewed in July, 2002. There is a bill, HB 2349, currently under consideration in the 
legislature, which will likely be passed. This bill would allow the continuation of the base subsidy and the 
“rural subsidy” for an additional two years. However, there is a significant change. This change provides 
that all Arizona retirees would be entitled to the base subsidy of $150 and $260, but before a rural retiree 
would qualify for the “rural subsidy” of an additional $300 and $600, the retiree must pay an out-of-pocket 
amount of $125 for single coverage and $425 for family coverage. After these out-of-pocket expenses 
have been paid, the retiree will be eligible for the “rural subsidy” amounts. Mr. Gonzales made the 
following recommendation: If HB 2349 passes: the premium for a single retiree should be $575 (retiree 
base subsidy-$150; required payment-$125; rural subsidy-$300); the premium for retiree and family 
coverage should be $1285 (base subsidy-$260; required payment-$425; rural subsidy-$600). Should the 
bill not pass: Rates should be set at not less than
 the following COBRA rates: retiree-$317.94; retiree 
plus children-$572.98; retiree plus family-$739.80. Mr. Brownlow made a motion to approve the retiree 
health coverage rates as presented; mot ion seconded by Mr. Deal; vote unanimous approving the 
motion.  
 
HUMAN RESOURCES:  Gilbert Gonzales, Director: Approval of payroll actions: Mr. Thompson made 
a motion to approve the payroll changes as presented; motion seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD BUSINESS:  There was no Board business presented. 
 
There being no further business to come before the Board of Supervisors, the meeting was adjourned 
until Monday, June 2 , 2003, upon a motion made by Mr.  Brownlow ; motion seconded by Mr. 
DeSpain; vote unanimously carried.  The meeting adjourned at 4:20 p.m.

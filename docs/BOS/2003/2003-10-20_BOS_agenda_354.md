PUBLIC WORKS; Bill Cox, Director: Discussion regarding Navajo County’s position on formulating a 
standardized process for rural consultation with ADOT: Mr. Cox advised that the State receives about 
$800,000,000 from the Federal Government and a portion is earmarked for freeways, etc. Another 
percentage goes to the various Councils of Government in the state. The 13 rural counties receive only 
1% of those funds, or about $9,000,000. Navajo County sends $5,000,000 to the Federal Government 
from the gas tax, and we get approximately $500,000 back. That money then is distributed between the 
county and the cities/towns. Mr. Cox noted that the Rural Transportation Advocacy Council will be hosting 
a Rural Consultation Forum on October 24th. The purpose of this meeting is to get input from the rural 
elected officials regarding the formulation of a standardized process for rural consultation in Arizona. Mr. 
Cox said he believes that, at a minimum, ADOT’s Transportation Planning Division should have an annual 
meeting with the elected officials with transportation responsibility in each county. In our area, it may be 
appropriate to have a meeting with the members of the White Mountain Transportation Group, the cities 
of Holbrook and Winslow, and representatives from the Native American Tribes within Navajo and 
Apache Counties. Mr. Cox asked for some clear direction from the Board as to what message to take to 
the forum. The Board requested that Mr. Jayne work with Mr. Cox to prepare a memo for the forum and to 
provide a copy, along with more definite figures, to the Board. Mr. Shumway made a motion to request 
that ADOT hold a forum with the White Mountain Transportation Group, the cities of Holbrook and 
Winslow, and the Tribal Governments; and to get the information out regarding the apparent inequities in 
the distribution of federal funds, as presented by staff; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  Judy Jones, Clerk: Board meeting schedule and agenda back-up: Ms. 
Jones presented a proposal to change the Board’s meeting schedule to two meetings per month. This 
would reduce staff time in preparing for meetings each week and will cut down on the Board’s travel 
expenses. There has been concern voiced that the Board and Administration did not take the full 15% 
budget cut. Ms. Jones noted that one position i n Admin, a Secretary IV, will not be filled. Since this 
position previously supported the County Manager, Finance Director and Human Resources Director, the 
Board staff will now absorb those duties. Ms. Jones explained that she is working with the departments to 
provide a summary of agenda requests which, along with an agenda, can be faxed to the Board 
members for review prior to each meeting. Mr. Shumway commented that if we can do a better job and 
save money, then we need to do it. A lengthy discussion then began regarding which day the Board 
meetings should be held. Mr.  Brownlow made a motion to change the Board meeting days to the first 
and third Mondays of the month, with a meeting and/or worksession to be held on the fourth Monday, if 
necessary; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote unanimously carried

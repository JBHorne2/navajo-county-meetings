NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRICT: Bill Cox, Floodplain Manager: Board of Directors 
Session: Approval to participate with the City of Holbrook in building a flood protection levee for the City's 
wastewater treatment plant with the following stipulations: 1) that construction meets all FEMA standards for 
100-year flood protection; 2) that the Holbrook City Engineer provide As-Built plans and certification that the 
FEMA standards have been met: Mr. Cox stated that staff is requesting that the Board approve spending up 
to $81,700 in Navajo County Flood Control District Funds in order to participate with the City of Holbrook in 
building a flood protection levee for the City's wastewater treatment plant.  Recently completed studies 
indicate that the treatment plant i s not adequately protected from flooding in Leroux Wash.  Delph 
Engineering, a consulting firm under contract to the City,  has designed a levee that meets the Federal 
Emergency Management Agency's standards for 100-year flood protection.  (The study and the levee design 
were funded by the flood control district.)  Using Delph Engineering's plans, specifications and contract 
documents, the City recently requested bids and awarded the construction project to Silver Eagle Western.  
The amount of funds requested for this project is the remainder of $120,000 that was originally budgeted for 
a City of Holbrook drainage study.  These funds have been used primarily to address the wastewater 
treatment plant flooding, although this was not the original intent of the study, because adequate flood 
protection for the plant is critical to the City.  The funds requested have been included in the Navajo County 
Flood Control District FY 03/04 budget. Mr. Shumway  made a motion to approve the participation with the 
suggested stipulations; motion seconded by Mr. DeSpain; vote unanimously carried. 
 
PUBLIC WORKS: Bill Cox, Director: 1) Approval of the LTAF II Grant Application for SFY 2004 with the 
required 25% matching funds to be budgeted in the FY 2004/05 General Fund budget. Mr. Cox made a 
presentation as follows on the five agenda requests: The LTAF II funds can be used for transit purposes only. 
 The 2004 funds that have become available are anticipated to be a one-time only distribution. The county’s 
portion of the distributed funds is $88,008 with a 25% matching fund of $22,002 required.  Additional funding 
in the amount of $10,906 has also been made available with an additional $2,726 required of matching 
funds.  We had the opportunity to apply for a total amount of $98,914 in LTAF II funding with a required match 
of $24,728.  However, due to the time constraints to apply for these funds, an application was submitted to 
NACOG for the above listed amounts.  Since the availability of LTAF II funds for SFY 2004 was unexpected 
and local jurisdictions have not budgeted for the 25% matching funds requirement, all jurisdictions receiving 
LTAF II funds will have until June 30, 2005 to expend these funds.  This will allow us time to budget and/or

of the Lone Pine Dam Road project: State statute allows counties’ under a population of 150,000 to expend a 
certain amount on individual projects. Currently the limit for any individual project that can be done with in-
house labor is $179,000 and to date $159,000 in labor has been expended for this project.  ADOT is 
scheduled to deliver asphalt millings to the project in April or May which would reflect a savings of $50,000 or 
more in material cost.  The last phase is 3.1 miles and has been brushed and cleared. It is estimated this 
phase of base preparation will cost approximately $250,000. $500,000 was budgeted to overlay and provide 
base material. Staff is requesting approval to contract the remaining portion with the additional cost to be 
provided from savings in completed projects. Mr. Brownlow made a motion to approve contracting the 
remaining portion of the Lone Pine Dam Project as presented; motion seconded by Mr. DeSpain; vote 
unanimously carried. 4) Approve change order request for Cannon and Associates to complete the Design 
Concept/Environmental Studies on the Chevelon Creek Bridge Rehabilitation Project: Navajo County entered 
into a Professional Services Contract with Cannon and Associates on July 15, 2002 for the preparation of the 
Design Concept Report and completion of NEPA Studies prerequisite to the Rehabilitation of Chevelon 
Creek Bridge.  ADOT requires completion of the following additional tasks for the Environmental 
Determination on this project:  1) Public and Agency Scoping; 2) section 4(F) Documentation of Historic 
Bridge; 3) Programmatic Categorical Exclusion; 4 ) Environmental Determination; 5) Meetings and 
Coordination.  This work was not included in the original scope of work to be performed by Cannon and 
Associates.  Therefore, we need a change order in the amount of $8,200 which includes the additional 
services which are being required by ADOT. Mr. DeSpain made a motion to approve Change Order #1 as 
requested; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote unanimously carried.  5) Request to upgrade AutoCAD 
software to the latest version (version 2004) to continue to receive technical support: AutoCAD is the main 
engineering design platform and the current version (version 2000i) is falling behind in functionality and 
versatility. The software manufacturer has abandoned technical support and licensing for our current version.  
Therefore, the department has been experiencing technical problems due to incompatibility of the old 
version. This item was not budgeted is because the manufacturer was contacted last May to inquire as to 
when they would stop supporting the software and they assured the department they would be supporting the 
software for another year and we have been informed that their support ends December of this year.  The 
cost of AutoCAD Version 2004 is $13,301.20 and will be taken out of the department’s contingency fund. Mr. 
DeSpain made a motion to approve the funding request to purchase the 2004 AutoCAD version; motion 
seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote unanimously carried.  6) Staff & BOS may recommend projects to be 
included in the 2004/05 Capital Improvement Plan five Year Plan  (Study Session): Mr. Cox reviewed the 
proposed Five-Year Capital Improvement Plan so the board would have something to review prior to the 
2004/2005 budget.  He reviewed the overlay history from 1986 to present.  He noted that local public work 
projects are considered on a “Benefit-Cost Ratio.”  He reviewed with the board a “Pay Now or Pay Later” 
graph showing that if maintenance is kept up the cost will stay down and if not the cost continues to increase. 
 Recommendations for Level of  Development (LOD) Categories for County Maintained  Roads was

roads and that needed to be looked at.  Mr. Cox went over the Maintenance Activities and Frequencies for 
Various LOD roads and stated that it is their responsibility to provide a safe and comfortable road system in

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
SPECIAL MEETING 
OCTOBER 28, 2003 
 
 
Supervisors' Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona - Time: 9:05 
 
PRESENT : J.R. DeSpain, Vice-Chairman and Jesse Thompson, Member (present) and Judy Jones, Clerk 
of the Board; Jerry Brownlow, Member and Percy Deal, Member (present telephonically); Pete Shumway (not 
present, attending another meeting previously scheduled). 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: 1) Approval of Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the State of 
Arizona, Emergency Services Management for the Federal Department of Homeland Security, Office for 
Domestic Preparedness Reimbursement Grant Programs: Captain Dunagan explained that there were two 
agreements that were not properly signed, one with the Department of Justice (2002) (State Domestic 
Preparedness Program Grant Funds) and one with the Federal Department of Homeland Security (2003) 
(Domestic Preparedness Reimbursement Grant Programs).  The State had informed Captain Dunagan that 
they did not have a signed agreement for 2002 and the 2003 agreement had been signed by Mr. Jaster 
(Emergency Services Director) but needed to be approved by the Board of Supervisors.  Fortunately if the 
signed agreements reach their office by 10/29/03 they will consider the funding and/or reimbursement of the 
grants. Captain Dunagan will be taking the properly signed agreements to the Phoenix office as soon as they 
are signed today.  Mr.  Thompson made a motion  to approve 2002 agreement (2002-TE-CX-0142 
(08/01/02 - 07/31/04) for the Provision of the U.S . Department of Justice 2002 State Domestic 
Preparedness Program Grant Funds) and the 2003 agreement with the Federal Department of Homeland 
Security, Office of Domestic Preparedness Reimbursement Grant Programs; motion seconded by Mr. 
Brownlow; vote unanimously carried.  
 
2)  Resolution #71 -03 of Support of the Northeastern Counties Coalition's Small Diameter Timber 
Harvesting Project: Mr. Brownlow explained that this Resolution would show support to the Coalition of 
Northeastern Arizona Counties (CNAC) in their efforts to pursue attracting private investment in wood fiber 
processing materials that need to be removed due to the high risk of another tragic forest fire.  Mr.  
Brownlow made a motion  to approve Resolution #71-03 in Support of the Coalition of Northeastern 
Arizona Counties’ Small Diameter Timber Harvesting Project; motion seconded by Mr. DeSpain ;vote 
unanimously carried. 
  
3)  Resolution #72-03 in Support of Invasive Species Management Plan: Mr. DeSpain explained that there 
is a meeting being held in Washington on Wednesday and Thursday of this week.  Navajo County has a 
serious problem with an invasive species namely “Saltcedar” and we want to support the Management 
Committee’s efforts in working on the Plan to assist with all invasive species. Mr. DeSpain made a motion 
to Resolution #72-03 in Support of Invasive Species Management Plan; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson 
;vote unanimously carried.  
 
Mr. Deal requested resolutions be prepared for the California counties that are being effected by the  
wildfires.

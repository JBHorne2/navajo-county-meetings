BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
 
Special Meeting 
December 1, 2003 
 
Supervisors’ Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona- Time: 10:10 a.m. 
 
PRESENT:  Present in Chambers: Vice-Chairman J.R. DeSpain; County Manager Jimmy 
Jayne; Kathy Hieb, Deputy Clerk. Present by conference call: Members Jerry Brownlow; Jesse 
Thompson. (Mr. Shumway and Mr. Deal were not able to be present)  
 
ADMINISTRATION/SPECIAL DISTRICTS: Bob Howell, Accountant: Resolution and Order 
extending the boundaries of the Porter Mountain Domestic Water Improvement District: Mr. 
Howell reported that the property owners were notified of this hearing and no responses, for or 
against, have been received. Mr. DeSpain noted no public comment was offered at this 
hearing. Mr. Brownlow advised he has heard no negative comments from his district regarding 
this issue. Mr. Brownlow made a motion to adopt Resolution #79-03, Extending the 
boundaries of the Porter Mountain Domestic Water Improvement District, as presented; 
motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD BUSINESS:
 Mr. Jayne advised that Mr. Shumway’s wife is in the hospital; flowers 
will be sent. Mr. Jayne also announced that the Show Low High School Cougars won the 3A 
Football State Championship on Saturday night.  
 
There being no further business to come before the Board of Supervisors, the meeting was 
adjourned until Tuesday, December 2, 2003, upon a motion made by Mr. Thompson ; 
motion seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote unanimously carried.  The meeting adjourned at 10:20 
a.m. 
 
APPROVED:

Pete Shumway, Chairman

CONTRACT FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES  
Agreement made as of November 1, 2019, between Navajo County ("County") and Paul Watson 
("Consultant"). 
Whereas, County desires to continue efforts to strengthen the economy thru attracting and 
enhancing industry, job creation, development of a skilled labor force, and tourism and to maintain 
relationships with other agencies such as NACOG, REAL Az Development Council, Arizona 
Commerce Authority, Cities and Towns, and other local, regional, state and natio nal organizations 
and individuals who can assist with those efforts.  
Whereas, Consultant represents that he has the ability to continue those efforts and to coordinate 
with above mentioned organizations. 
Whereas, Consultant desires to assist the County in a transitional period for the continuation of 
these efforts. 
Now, Therefore , the parties agree as follows: 
1.  Services.  Consultant shall provide services in a competent and professional manner as 
 outlined in attached document titled Attachment A, to the  satisfaction of the County. 
2. Consultant's Expertise.   Consultant warrants that it has the ability, authority, capacity and 
 professional expertise to perform these services.  Consultant shall self -perform this 
 agreement and be devoted to the successful  accomplishment of the work to be performed 
 hereunder. 
3. Independent Contractor. Consultant acknowledges that it is an independent contractor.  
 Neither party is or shall be an agent, employee, partner, joint venture or associate of the 
 other. An employee or agent of one party shall not be deemed an employer or agent of the 
 other party for any purpose.  
4. Subcontracts.  Consultant shall not enter into any subcontract with respect to any of the 
 work to be performed hereunder without the County's prior written approval.  All 
 subcontracts shall comply with applicable federal and state laws and regulations and shall 
 impose on the subcontractor substantially the same obligations as are imposed on 
 Consultant by this Agreement with respect to those matters  covered herein.  Consultant is 
 responsible for full performance of this Agreement regardless of whether subcontractors are 
 used or not. 
5. Time for Completion.  Consultant's services shall be performed over the course of six 
 months and can be renewed b ased on the mutual agreement of both parties herein.

Submitted By: Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board of Supervisors
Department: Sheriff 
Presented By: Sheriff David Clouse
Motion before the Board:
Consideration to adopt Resolution Number 26-19, approving the updated Jail Usage Fees for
Municipalities in accordance with the Jail Usage Fees Ordinance, Ordinance Number 08-10 and in
accordance with A.R.S. §11-252.08 and A.R.S. §31-121(D).
Background:
Pursuant to ARS 31-121(D), the costs of incarceration of an individual charged in a municipal
court shall be paid by the city or town in which the charges are filed.  Navajo County’s Jail Usage
Fees Ordinance, No. 08-10 requires periodic review of the fees being charged to responsible
municipalities.  Jail Usage Fees were updated effective July 1, 2019, to help offset increases in the
cost of providing incarceration services in the jail and to defray the costs of providing inmate
medical care.  With the approval of voters to establish a Jail District to help fund the cost of the
Jail, however, the Sheriff recommends that Jail Usage Fees to returned to pre-July amounts until
the Jail District goes into effect in January 2020 at which point Jail Usage Fees can no longer be
charged to responsible municipalities pursuant to ARS 31-121(E).  The Sheriff recommends the
following changes:  the base booking fee be decreased from $75.00 to $50.00, the base daily
housing fee be decreased from $80.00 to $68.41, and the base daily medical services fee be
decreased from $25.00 to $10.00.  The financial impact of lowering the fee from October 1, 2019,
through December 31, 2019, is estimated to be between $70,000 and $100,000.            
Recommendation: 
The Sheriff recommends approval of the updated Jail Usage Fee schedule.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, September 3, 2019

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman, each appeared telephonically

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Paige Peterson, Finance Director; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Christen Haddox, Elections Coordinator

8:34 a.m . Call To Order

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration and approval of the Official Canvass for the August 27, 2019, Special Election :  Christen Haddox requested approval of the Official Canvass for the August 27, 2019, Special Election.  Supervisor Williams thanked the Elections Department, poll workers and all those who helped make this election run smoothly and made a motion to approve the Official Canvass for the August 27, 2019, Special Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Supervisor Thompson requested that the election data be sent to him for his communities.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :  At 8:37 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  September 10, 2019

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

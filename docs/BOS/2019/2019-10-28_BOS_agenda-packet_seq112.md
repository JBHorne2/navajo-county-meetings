NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING AND AGENDA
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
Monday, October 28, 2019 *SPECIAL MEETING
Notice is hereby given, pursuant to A.R.S. 38-431.02, that the Board of Supervisors of Navajo County, State of Arizona, will hold a
Special Meeting on Monday, October 28, 2019, from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. at Harrah's Ak-Chin Conference Center, 15406 N.
Maricopa Road, Maricopa, Arizona in Ballroom B4.  This is not an official Board Meeting and no official action will be taken. 
Persons with disabilities who need accommodation to attend or participate in the meeting may contact the Clerk of the Board’s
office at (928) 524-4053; at least 48 hours prior to the meeting so an accommodation can be arranged. A conference line has been
arranged and can be used by dialing 1-877-820-7831, participant passcode is 6068060#. 
Items on the agenda may be considered out of order, and issues may be revisited throughout the duration of the Special Meeting. At
the Chairman’s discretion, breaks may be called whenever appropriate. A copy of the agenda background material
provided to the Board, with the exception of items to be considered in executive session, is available for public inspection at
Navajo County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, February 21, 2019

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor District IV; and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman each appeared telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Jeff Lee, Public Health Director; and Catrina Jenkins, Emergency Management Director each appeared telephonically; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, was present in person

3:05 p.m . Call To Order:

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of the adoption of Resolution 03-19 Declaring a State of Emergency for the Unincorporated Areas of Navajo County Pursuant to A.R.S. §26-311 :  Jeff Lee indicated that we have come to the conclusion that a declaration of a State of Emergency is necessary by looking at the roadways and possible power outages.  He stated that we will receive funding if the State declares, as well.  He indicated that the Hopi Reservation, Navajo Nation, Gila County, Yavapai County and Coconino County are considering declaring a State of Emergency.  Supervisor Williams inquired as to the information we are receiving.  Mr. Lee stated that the storm has developed further west and is bringing in a colder snow which is increasing the amount of snow for us.  He indicated that it is predicted that Hon-Dah will receive 32-40 inches, Pinetop will receive 31-39 inches, Show Low and Cibecue will receive 10-16 inches and Heber will receive 25-33 inches.  He stated that these increased totals will have an effect on I-40, and Highway 260 has closed from Star Valley to the Rim.   He indicated that a significant amount of snow is going to hit our area.  Supervisor Thompson stated that the northern part of Navajo County is being hit hard right now.  He indicated that we have to rely on the weather service.  Mr. Lee stated that Navajo Nation shows a prediction of 4-8 inches for Chinle, 3-5 inches for Kayenta, 5-9 inches for Hardrock, 8-12 inches for Ganado and 10-16 inches for Buffalo Pass.  Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired whether there is an estimated cost for what this storm could bring, and Mr. Lee indicated that it would depend on how long the storm stays around, if it is a wet snow and how it affects the power and infrastructure.

Supervisor Williams made a motion to adopt Resolution 03-19 declaring a State of Emergency for the Unincorporated Areas of Navajo County Pursuant to A.R.S. §26-311; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN:  At 3:14 p.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

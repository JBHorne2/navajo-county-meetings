NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING AND AGENDA
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
Tuesday, March 12, 2019
ONE OR MORE SUPERVISORS MAY ATTEND VIA VIDEO AND/OR TELEPHONICALLY 
The Navajo County Board of Supervisors will hold a meeting open to the public on Tuesday, March 12, 2019, in the Supervisors’
Chamber in the Navajo County Governmental Complex, Holbrook, Arizona. The Board may vote to meet in a closed executive
session to discuss certain matters as indicated on the following agenda. The Board may meet in executive session pursuant to
A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (3) for legal advice on any item.
Persons with disabilities who need accommodation to attend or participate in the meeting may contact the Clerk of the Board’s office
at (928) 524-4053; at least 48 hours prior to the meeting so an accommodation can be arranged.
Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Chairman’s discretion. A copy of the agenda background material
provided to the Board, with the exception of items to be considered in executive session, is available for public inspection at Navajo
County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov 
9:00 a.m. Call To Order:  Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance
           
1. CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Individuals may address the Board on any relevant issue for up to 5
minutes. At the close of the call to the public, Board members may not respond to any
comments but may respond to criticism, ask staff to review a matter or ask that a matter be
placed on a future agenda.

DW.   We have consistently provided more services to Adults than to Dislocated Workers due to
the rural locations of Navajo, Apache and Gila Counties.  We predict this trend will continue in

WHEREAS, ARS § 48-1404 (B) provides as follows with respect to Special Road  District Board of Directors 
positions : 
 
“If only one person files or no person files a nominating petition for election to fill a position on the board of 
directors of the district, the county board of supervisors, by resolution, may cancel the election for that office and 
appoint the person who filed the nominating petition to fill that position. If no person files a nominating petition for an 
election to fill a district board office, the county board of supervisors, by resolution, may cancel the election for those 
offices and those offices are deemed vacant and shall be fille d as otherwise provided by law. A person who is 
appointed pursuant to this section is fully vested with the powers and duties of the office as if elected to that office .” 
 
         and 
 
WHEREAS, A.R.S. § 16-410 (A) authorizes the Board to take such action no earlier than seventy -five (75) 
days before the election, and it is now less than seventy-five (75) days before the May 21, 2019 Special  Election at 
which the Special Road District Board of Directors will be elected;  
 
         and

Submitted By: Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board of Supervisors

Motion before the Board:
10:00 a.m. PUBLIC HEARING: Consideration and discussion of the proposed Jail District,
reaffirming Resolution Number 13-19, establishing a Jail District in Navajo County for the
purpose of providing a dedicated revenue stream to support the existing jail and relieve the
general fund of the burden of supporting the entire cost of the jail. 
Background:
Significant reductions in services--including in public safety--are needed to balance the budget for
FY2020. In lieu of service reductions, staff have been looking forward to develop options and
have identified an existing funding tool that is available to the County. The establishment of a Jail
District would provide a stable funding source for the daily operation of the County Jail, which
would then provide financial relief to the rest of the General Fund to preserve current services and
jobs.
At the February 12, 2019 Board of Supervisor meeting, the Board heard from several community
members asking the Board to move forward with the establishment of a jail district in Navajo
County. 
After hearing from the public at today's (March 12) Board of Supervisors meeting, should the
Board of Supervisors reaffirm the resolution establishing a Jail District, it would then need to be
approved by voters in a Special Election in August.
Recommendation: 
Sheriff Clouse and staff recommend the establishment of a Jail District in Navajo County.

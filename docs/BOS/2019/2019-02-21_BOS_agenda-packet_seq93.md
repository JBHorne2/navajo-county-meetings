RESOLUTION 03-19 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS, 
DECLARATION OF A STATE OF EMERGENCY FOR THE UNICORPORATED 
AREAS PURSUANT TO A.R.S. 26-311 
 
WHEREAS the Board of Supervisors is authorized by Section 26-311 of the Arizona Revised 
Statues to declare an emergency during natural disasters including significant snow events which 
endanger the life or property of citizens in unincorporated areas of Navajo County; and  
 
WHEREAS a major snow event has threatened the entire region including all of Navajo County 
beginning February 21, 2019 for several days.  
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors as  
follows: 
 
1. Pursuant to A.R.S. § 26 -311, a snow emergency is hereby declared in the 
unincorporated areas of N avajo County. 
 
2. This declaration shall be immediately effective and shall remain in effect 
until further declaration of this  Board. 
 
3. The Chairman is hereby authorized to take all further actions as may be 
necessary or appropriate to implement this declarati on pursuant to A.R.S. §  26-311. 
 
PASSED, ADOPTED AND APPROVED by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors on this 
21st day of February 2019.

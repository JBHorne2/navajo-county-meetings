BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation on Nexus Coalition for Drug Prevention : Vicky Solomon, executive director of Nexus Coalition for Drug Prevention, gave a short presentation on Nexus Coalition for Drug Prevention.  She stated that the two main goals that Nexus has is to reduce substance abuse among youth and establish and strengthen collaboration among the community organizations.  She spoke about the Junior Leadership Academy, Safety Town, Over-the-Counter Safety Medicine training, Drug Diversion, Mpowrd & 2BMpowrd and other drug prevention programs provided. She spoke about Free Naloxone and Free Drug Testing kits that are offered by this program. She spoke about the DEA Drug Takeback Day. Supervisor Williams asked if they present in all Navajo County schools, and Mrs. Solomon indicated that she is working on it. Supervisor Thompson asked if when working with Native American children, does the program take into consideration the medicinal teachings that their cultural have, and Mrs. Solomon indicated that the Native cultural is taught in their Native language. Chairwoman Whitesinger asked about data specific to our County as far as the amount of overdoses in our communities or the number of children affected by substances. Mrs. Solomon indicated that she would get that information to the Board.  She stated that there have been about 9 overdoses in Navajo County since January. She spoke further about the importance of Naloxone.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of the purchase of four 2019 120M2 Caterpillar Motor Graders with a trade-in of four Motor Graders from Empire Machinery in the amount of $1,010,678.80 :  John Osgood stated that this request is for the replacement of three motor graders ranging in age from 29 to 40 years old and another over 50 years old.  He indicated the equipment will be deployed one to Holbrook, one to Heber and two to the South County area. He stated that the source of the funds is the HURF funding that is solely used for highways.  Supervisor Thompson asked about a rent-to-own option rather than purchase, and Mr. Osgood indicated that staff did an analysis and determined that purchasing them was a better business investment than leasing. Supervisor Williams asked about the replacement strategy, and Mr. Osgood indicated that 12-15 years is the normal life expectancy of the graders, and we will be discussing a replacement strategy moving forward. Supervisor Jack thanked Mr. Osgood and made a motion to approve the purchase of four 2019 120M2 Caterpillar Motor Graders with a trade-in of four Motor Graders from Empire Machinery in the amount of $1,010,678.80.  Supervisor Williams indicated that this purchase is needed and that funding comes entirely from HURF funds which can only be used for transportation. Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that these investments are important and thanked Mr. Osgood for keeping the one grader running for over 54 years.Motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

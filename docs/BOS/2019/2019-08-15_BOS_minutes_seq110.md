NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 15, 2019

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I, present in person and; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman, were present telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Jalyn Gerlich, Superintendent of Schools (telephonic); Rayleen Richards, Elections Director; Debra Kester, Treasurer; Paige Peterson, Finance Director

CALL TO ORDER :  8:37 a.m.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS/ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Consideration and adoption of the Fiscal Year 2019-20 property tax rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts :  Paige Peterson indicated that all taxing districts in Navajo County have submitted their budgets from which they have calculated the tax rate necessary to generate the levy that those boards or councils have approved.  She stated that we had an increase in our valuation of nearly 23.8 million which allowed our tax rate to decrease 1.31 cents while keeping a flat levy and adding new construction of $101,000.00.  Superintendent Gerlich stated that, overall, our tax rates for most school districts have decreased slightly or have held steady.  Supervisor Williams made a motion to adopt the Fiscal Year 2019-20 property tax rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 23-19 approving Poll Workers and Polling Place/Vote Centers for the Aug. 27, 2019 Special Election :  Rayleen Richards requested that the Board approve Resolution Number 23-19 as presented.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 23-19 approving Poll Workers and Polling Place/Vote Centers for the Aug. 27, 2019 Special Election; motion seconded by
Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :  At 8:42 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  September 10, 2019

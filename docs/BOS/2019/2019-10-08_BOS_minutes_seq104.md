Presentation to retiring employee, Paul Watson , Assistant County Manager/Economic Development Director :  Eric Scott recognized Paul Watson for his years of service to Navajo County Communities.  He stated that Paul was the town manager for Pinetop-Lakeside for 19 years, town manager for Snowflake for 7-1/2 years and has been with Navajo County for 4-1/2 years  as the Assistant County Manager and Economic Development Director. Mr. Scott indicated that Paul has a strong moral character, stands up for what he believes in, has a calm demeanor, a great sense of humor, and we are happy to have him. Glenn Kephart indicated that we are extremely grateful for what Paul has given to Navajo County for more than 3 decades.  He stated that Paul's character is beyond reproach and expressed gratitude for having the opportunity to learn from Paul.  A brief presentation was shown.  Stephanie Irwin, Mayor of Pinetop-Lakeside, spoke about the mutual respect between Paul and her father-in-law. She wished Paul well on behalf of the Pinetop/Lakeside Council.  Hunter Moore, Governor's Policy Advisor for Natural Resources, stated that friendship and mentorship defines his relationship with Paul. He stated that Paul is a great friend and has affected him and members of his family. He indicated that Paul has been a mentor in sharing his love of public service with him. He stated that Paul's presence and influence has been felt throughout the State of Arizona.  He indicated that Paul has helped to shape policy in this state. He stated that it has been a truly remarkable experience to talk with Paul about things going on in the state. On behalf of the governor, Mr. Moore and this state, he thanked Paul for his service. Chip Davis with Congressman O'Halleran's office thanked Paul for his service and passion on behalf of the Congressman and the Congressman's team.  He stated that Paul is always looking for solutions for his community and other communities.  He indicated that it has been a blessing for Congressman O'Halleran's team to have had Paul's knowledge of this area.  Supervisor Thompson indicated that we are keeping Paul around for a little bit longer.  He stated that Paul is always motivating people.  He thanked Mrs. Watson for sharing Paul with us. He spoke about their friendship. Supervisor Williams echoed what he said in the video and spoke about the ripple effects of Paul's service not only in Navajo County but throughout the state. He indicated that he has always felt like he has been able to trust Paul. He spoke about a foot race that happened after a Board of Supervisors meeting with Paul and Supervisor Whiting. He thanked Paul and his wife for their friendship and influence on him. Vice-Chairman Whiting spoke about their friendship and their work together. He stated that Paul has been a real mentor to him.  He indicated that their relationship changed when he lost his parents, and Paul became a confidante and friend. He thanked Paul's family and appreciated them for allowing us to have Paul. He thanked Paul for all that he has done and continues to do. Supervisor Jack expressed his appreciation to Mr. Watson. He further thanked Paul's family for standing by Paul throughout the years. Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed appreciation for Paul's leadership and his servant heart, kindness, patience, sincerity and genuine care for our community.  She spoke about Mr. Watson's influence and his dedication to the community and his love of family. She thanked Lisa for the great woman that she is and for allowing us to have worked with Paul.  Steve Brophy, President of AZTEC Land and Cattle Company and President of the Apache Railway, stated that his gratitude, as well as the gratitude of AZTEC and Apache Railway, to Paul knows no bounds. He spoke of the effort that Paul went through to save that railway after Catalyst filed for bankruptcy.  He indicated that Paul became the vice-president and treasurer of the railway which did not have enough money to pay for itself.  He indicated that the job was endless phone calls, high stress and no money, which included many trips to Washington, and Paul did it to save jobs for the Apache Railway and the Pig Farm. He spoke about Paul's help with many things. He indicated that Paul has also been an enormous help with the possible egg farm, possible expansion of Smithfield Farms and more agriculture.  He spoke about the importance of saving the railway to industry.   He stated that we owe an enormous debt of gratitude to Paul. Paul Watson expressed his appreciation to all.  He spoke about the Rodeo-Chediski Fire while he was with the Town of Pinetop-Lakeside, the closure of Catalyst Paper Mill when he was with the Town of Snowflake and the impending closure of NGS while he's been with Navajo County.  He stated that through all of those events, what he has learned most was the value of the people and the value of relationships. He indicated that it is in those toughest times that you see the best of men. He indicated that he has been blessed to see the best of people, and that is what he is grateful for.  He thanked his Heavenly Father for the blessings and the people that He has brought into Paul's life. He thanked his wife, Lisa, for her love and support. He thanked his family and his daughter for being here representing his children and grandchildren. He stated that some have said he has been a mentor, but they have been great mentors to him. He expressed his appreciation for his career in the White Mountains and for each of our Elected Officials. He thanked all the staff he has had the opportunity to work with. He also thanked the citizens who give to their communities. Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked all those who were present today.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :  Supervisor Thompson reported on his meetings regarding the census.   Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked Paul and all those who traveled to show their appreciation.  Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that last night she did a welcome for Connecting Forest Industry to Northern Arizona.  She spoke about the efforts of Vice-Chairman Whiting and his dedication and commitment to insuring that forest industry is a viable industry and is looking at forest restoration.

Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

9:00 a.m . Call To Order: Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance was led by Supervisor Williams.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC :  Ramona Dipley, addressed the Board regarding conduct by the Navajo County Sheriff's Office during the search for the Barksdales. She indicated that she and her family were told to get face down on the ground, including children.  She stated that a police department that disregards its oath to uphold the Constitutional rights of the citizens is a danger to the community it serves.  If they continue on the course they are on, it is only a matter of time before the taxpayers pay a penalty of these public servants.  She stated that law enforcement is paid to protect the rights of the people, not to trample on them. Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Ms. Dipley for addressing the Board and explained how Call to the Public works.  Supervisor Thompson thanked the audience for being present and for the support and prayers they receive as elected officials. Supervisor Thompson thanked the Navajo County Fair Board for recognizing him and the people of Navajo County for their support of him throughout the years.

SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration to adopt Resolution Number 26-19 , approving the updated Jail Usage Fees for Municipalities in accordance with the Jail Usage Fees Ordinance, Ordinance Number 08-10 and in accordance with A.R.S. §11-252.08 and A.R.S. §31-121(D) :  Sheriff Clouse congratulated Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting for his recognition as Supervisor of the Year. Sheriff Clouse discussed the item and indicated that in 2014, the prior Sheriff asked for the Board to consider jail fees for incarceration of misdemeanors which was passed; and then in May 2019, Sheriff Clouse asked that those fees be increased due to the increasing costs in jail and medical fees. He stated that he is now asking the Board to adopt this resolution returning those jail fees back to what they were before May 2019; and then in January 2020, these fees will go away. Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked Sheriff Clouse and indicated that this is a sound request.  Supervisor Thompson asked if the Sheriff has an existing contract with the Hopi Nation. Sheriff Clouse indicated that that is a separate contract.  Vice-Chairman Whiting made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 26-19 approving the updated Jail Usage Fees for Municipalities in accordance with the Jail Usage Fees Ordinance, Ordinance Number 08-10 and in accordance with A.R.S. §11-252.08 and A.R.S. §31-121(D); motion seconded by Supervisor Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Presentation of Quarterly Report :  Pat Stevens presented the quarterly report of the White Mountain Lake County Recreation Improvement District.  She stated that to date, there are 26 associate memberships and 1,027 placards issued for the lake. PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of the purchase of two International 10-Wheel Dump Trucks with Snow Plows and Cinder Spreaders from RWC Group in the amount of $537,455.60 :  John Osgood discussed the item indicating that these two trucks will be replacing a 1991 Ford 10-wheel dump truck with snow plow and a 1986 Ford 6-wheel dump truck with snow plow, which are past their useful life.  He stated that the fiscal impact is $537,455.60, which is funded in the Public Works capital outlay budget, and the source of the funds is the dedicated Highway User Revenue Fund.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the purchase of two International 10-Wheel Dump Trucks with Snow Plows and Cinder Spreaders from RWC Group in the amount of $537,455.60; motion seconded by Supervisor Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of approving Attachment "I" to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for the funding of providing aggregate and culvert material for Navajo Route 9065 within the Whitecone Community :  John Osgood discussed the item indicating that Navajo County will be supplying construction material while Navajo Department of Transportation will provide the labor and equipment through contracts for the Whitecone community.  He stated that the fiscal impact is $35,000, which has been budgeted and is available in the District II Navajo Nation CIP funds, and the source of the funds is the dedicated Highway User Revenue Fund.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve Attachment "I" to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for the funding of providing aggregate and culvert material for Navajo Route 9065 within the Whitecone Community; motion seconded by Supervisor Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, October 28, 2019

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor District IV

Absent:
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Paige Peterson, Finance Director; Alberto Peshlakai, Tribal Government Relations Director

Attendees:
Paul Watson, Contractor, Brandon Nee, County Supervisors Association, Peter Aleshire, White Mountain Independent

11:13 a.m . Call To Order : Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance was led by Supervisor Lee Jack .

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

Welcome and Introduction : Glenn Kephart read a message from Chairwoman Whitesinger, stating that she was unable to be present due to her grandmothers passing. Mr. Kephart thanked everyone for being present and gave a brief introduction of today's meeting.

Navajo County's Financial Sustainability : Paige Peterson spoke about our sales tax and state shared sales tax which have recovered to pre-recession levels. She further spoke about State Cost Shifts which we have had a successful partnership with the legislature for relief. Questions were asked and answered regarding the jail district relief for the coal mine revenue loss. Discussion was held on these topics. Mrs. Peterson spoke about our employee healthcare indicating that our plan benefits are increasing and there is a $3.3M savings. There has been no increase in premiums for 3 years in a row to our employees. Discussion was held on employee healthcare and how to communicate with our employees regarding employee benefits and what those benefits are. Mrs. Peterson further spoke about some things coming up regarding retirement, AHCCCS and ALTCS indicating a $500K in ALTCS in 2020 increase, $500K PSPRS 2020 increase and unfunded PSPRS liability. Brandon Nee with the County Supervisors Association, indicated that tomorrow's CSA discussion will include the ALTCS increase and possible resolutions. Discussion was held on the topic of ALTCS payments. Mrs. Peterson indicated that we do have a 5-year sustainable budget plan, we can maintain services even with the loss of coal revenues and we do have limited capacity to invest in the organization. She further spoke about our ability to maintain our fund balance to help manage the unexpected which we have been able to do. Bryan Layton discussed continuing our strong financial and organizational management regarding infrastructure investment. He spoke about our Information Technology, our fleet and our buildings. Mr. Layton further spoke about our current compensation tools for our employees. He discussed our current pay structure, adjustment for market going rate and indicated that 20% of our employees have less than 1 year of service. Mr. Layton spoke about employee investment versus staffing increases. Mr. Layton talked about focusing on our current workforce which would be employee investment with a longevity adjustment, skilled labor structure, merit pay and inclement weather response pay. He then spoke about staffing increases with some partnerships, grants/foundations, innovations and cost recovery. Mr. Layton indicated that we cannot afford to do both. There was discussion on casino grants which Chief Moffitt has applied for. Mr. Kephart indicated that these discussions will continue during our budget process. There was further discussion on employee investment.

Lunch, Activity and General Discussion on following topics:

Communication with the Board of Supervisors : Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board reviewed communication with the Board of Supervisors. She indicated that one of our most important jobs is making sure that Board Members have the information that they need to interact with staff as well as constituents. She reviewed some changes that have been implemented over the past year with regard to office procedure and requested Board member feedback. She thanked each of them for being diligent in their communication with the Board staff and welcomed any new ideas to continue to grow the support that each Board member receives. The Board applauded Melissa and her staff for the work that they do and indicated that the changes that have been implemented have been well-received and appreciated.

Vehicle Purchase Plan : Bryan Layton spoke about vehicle purchases. Supervisors thanked Mr. Layton for reaching out to the local dealers. Discussion took place regarding vehicle purchases.

Code Enforcement and Animal Control : Bryan Layton spoke about Code Enforcement and Environmental Health positions. Public Works and Health District have worked together to come up with a solution to incorporate both positions. Discussion was held regarding the integration of these two positions.

Strategies to improve opportunities for Economic Growth : Paul Watson discussed some strategies to improve opportunities for Economic Growth. He gave some data regarding employment in Navajo County versus statewide. Mr. Watson discussed areas of focus moving forward regarding tourism. Mr. Watson spoke about Navajo County's current regional identity and asked Board members what they felt was unique about our area. Discussion took place about the cooperation we have on and off the reservation, our Native Community, Navajo County having 4 distinct seasons, our diversity; that being geographic as well as cultural. Discussion continued regarding the beauty of Navajo County; that being our diversity, the outdoors (hunting, fishing, hiking), our community celebrations, our community identity. Discussion took place about our natural resources, water, fall colors, seasons, and our majestic mountains. The Board members spoke about having family ties, feeling safe in our communities, having access to rural and remote areas and wide open spaces. Mr. Watson continued the discussion of all the wonderful things that Navajo County has to offer and our regional identity.

Purpose and Mission/Organizational Culture : Glenn Kephart discussed Navajo County's purpose and mission statement. A clip was shown about how to write a mission statement. Discussion was held about our current mission statement and where the "why" is in our current mission statement. Mr. Kephart indicated that we will move forward with coming up with a new mission statement. Mr. Kephart showed a short video regarding a work/life balance. Discussion took place on work life balance.

Communication and Community Trust : Paige Peterson spoke about informing and personalizing county services through our Social Media. A video clip of our current Facebook video was shown and discussed.

Review and Future Items for Discussion : Glenn Kephart reviewed the folders that each Board member received with the Legislative Policy Committee report. Discussion took place regarding a tri-fold newsletter for each district.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC : There were no comments from the public.

ADJOURN : At 2:43 p.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

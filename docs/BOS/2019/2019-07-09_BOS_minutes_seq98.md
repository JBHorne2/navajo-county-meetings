SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Update and Presentation to the U.S. Marshall's Office/MCAT Sex Offender Verification :  Sheriff Clouse discussed the item.  He spoke about the MOU for a joint operation between the Navajo County Sheriff's Office and the U.S. Marshall Service for the sex offender registry compliance check within the Navajo County Sheriff's Office jurisdiction which took place June 17, 18 and 19.  Supervisor Thompson thanked Sheriff Clouse for his presentation and for also making any issue which comes up in the rural areas his issue.  Chairwoman Whitesinger spoke about the importance of ensuring that the public is kept safe and thanked Sheriff Clouse and his team.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Update on the events at the Navajo County Fairgrounds :  Michael Sample discussed the item.  He thanked the Board of Supervisors for the support Navajo County gives to the Fair. He introduced Michelle Stock, the new fair manager and event director.  Mr. Sample spoke about the events which have been held at the fairgrounds this year, which included the Southwest Trappers Association convention.  He further spoke about the Route 66 Livestock Show happening this week.  He indicated that former Supervisor DeSpain will be there and would love to see everyone.  He invited the Board to participate in the Opening Ceremonies on September 11th.  He thanked the elected officials and department directors for allowing their employees to volunteer during the fair and indicated that they could not do it without that help.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Presentation on 2019 Health Fair :   Eric Scott spoke about the events happening here today with the Health Fair. He spoke about the vendors and raffle prizes. He spoke about our Health Guide, Kayli Medina, who helps our employees find options for health care which has provided savings to employees and to the County. He indicated that Ms. Medina has helped in over 32 events resulting in over $35,000 in savings. Supervisor Thompson thanked Mr. Scott for all his work and uniting our employees. Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that the Health Guide is an innovative way to help our employees and is a cost savings.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 20-19 approving/denying a Special Use Permit, with stipulations, to allow for the construction of a recreation vehicle and boat storage facility that will provide uncovered storage spaces for boats and RVs on a portion of a parcel which is zoned C-R, APN 206-15-010A, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 29, in the Overgaard area :  Sandra Phillips discussed the item. She stated that the applicant is requesting a Special Use Permit allowing for the construction of an RV and boat storage facility, and the Planning & Zoning Commission has made a unanimous recommendation of approval.  She indicated that Staff recommends approval of this Special Use Permit under the stipulations as outlined, including a 7' fence.  Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the public hearing; there being no comments closed the public hearing.  Supervisor Williams made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 20-19 approving a Special Use Permit with stipulations to allow for the construction of a recreation vehicle and boat storage facility that will provide uncovered storage spaces for boats and RVs on a parcel which is zoned C-R, APN 206-15-010A, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 29, in the Overgaard area; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 21-19 approving/denying a Zone Change to change their  R1-10 zoning on their property to RU-1, APN 206-02-047A, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 30, in the Overgaard area :  Sandra Phillips discussed the item.  She stated that the applicant is requesting a Zone Change from R1-10 to RU-1 for the purpose of bringing the existing property, which has a garage converted to a guest home by the previous owners, into conformance.  She indicated that there have been no objections, and this change will not impact the neighbors, either negatively or positively.  She stated that the Planning & Zoning Commission has made a recommendation of approval. Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the public hearing. Mr. Walter Royal indicated that he has no objection to his neighbor's request. Chairwoman Whitesinger closed the public hearing.  Sheryl Filliater and Daniel Miller, the applicants, addressed the Board and asked the Board to approve the change.  Supervisor Williams made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 21-19 approving a Zone Change to change their R1-10 zoning on their property to RU-1, APN 206-02-047A, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 30, in the Overgaard area; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

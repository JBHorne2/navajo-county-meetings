COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

at 9:15 a.m., Presentation by Navajo County Attorney Brad Carlyon of a check to the Silver Creek Irrigation District for amounts recovered on the District's behalf through civil asset  forfeiture :  Jason Moore indicated that about 2 ½ years ago, Silver Creek Irrigation District was the victim of a crime of embezzlement in the amount of about $800,000.00. He spoke about the civil asset forfeiture proceedings and indicated that they have recovered $204,270.58 in cash, along with about $10- to $15,000.00 worth of personal property being sold.  He estimated the total amount of recovery will be in the $215,000 to $220,000 range.   He spoke about civil asset forfeiture reform, which is an effort by the legislature to weaken or eliminate civil asset forfeiture throughout the state.  He indicated that this case is an example of why civil asset forfeiture works and without it, this recovery would not be possible.  He stated that in this case, one of the main perpetrators passed away before the criminal investigation was completed; therefore, there were never any criminal charges filed and no conviction or recovery.  He indicated that the law allows for victim restitution.  He introduced Dee Johnson and Vance Muder with Silver Creek Irrigation District and presented them with a check in the amount of $215,000.  Vice-Chairman Whiting asked when the Silver Creek Irrigation District was started, and Dee Johnson indicated that it started in the late 1800's.  Vice-Chairman Whiting inquired regarding how many members are in the District, and Mr. Johnson indicated that there are roughly 400 people that the District serves. Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked the County Attorney's Office for their efforts with the civil asset forfeiture. He also thanked Silver Creek Irrigation District for their patience. Dee Johnson thanked the County for their time and energy they put into this and particularly Mr. Jason Moore.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

At 9:37 a.m., Consideration and adoption of the proposed Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year 2019-20 for Navajo County :  Paige Peterson discussed the FY2020 preliminary budget. She spoke about the FY2020 Summary Highlights, including having a balanced budget despite rising costs of doing business, a flat property tax levy, new construction in the amount of $101,454 and total reductions of $1.2 million.  She stated that due to the limited number of employees in Finance, they cannot monitor grants like they would like to and also will not be able to continue to provide an in-house print shop nor shipping and receiving.  She spoke about our revenue and spending over the current fiscal year, including State shared sales tax which is up 6.57%, County excise tax is up 7.67%, controlled spending and the creation of an appropriate financial contingency.   She spoke about our innovative approach to our health care plan which avoided $3.3 million in claims. She recognized our partners, including our State legislators. She spoke about the efforts of Vice-Chairman Whiting , the Board of Supervisors and Brad Carlyon which brought about 2 successful reliefs:  ongoing elected officials retirement plan relief and the elimination of the Arizona Department of Juvenile Corrections fee; however, the increase to our ALTC payment of $313,000 eliminated those gains.  She expressed appreciation to the Sheriff's Office for their efforts and recognized the strain on jail staff for accepting paying inmates. She spoke about our partnerships with the cities and towns and the difficult conversations about increasing our jail fees effective July 1st. She thanked our elected officials, department directors and employees who worked within their budget.

At 9:45 a.m., Jesse Thompson, District II Supervisor, is present.

Ms. Peterson stated that our General Fund budget is $42,953,712.  She spoke about our financial challenges and uncertainties, including the loss of coal-fired power, a two-year reduction strategy, minimum wage increasing again and capital needs in IT and vehicles. She stated that we have been cutting since the great recession, which has resulted in changes in the services we provide, including the Recorder and Treasurer being unable to provide services in the Show Low area and Finance no longer being able to provide in-house printing and shipping and receiving.     Supervisor Williams thanked Ms. Peterson, her team, the Department Directors and Elected officials for the tremendous job in being team players. He stated that it is important to find a revenue replacement in order to continue to provide services.  Supervisor Williams made a motion to adopt the proposed Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year 2019-20 for Navajo County; motion seconded by Vice-Chairman Whiting .  Supervisor Thompson thanked Ms. Peterson for the insights into the budget.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

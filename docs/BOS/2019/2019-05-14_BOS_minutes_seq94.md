ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Presentation to retiring employee, Jeffrey Nafziger , for his nearly 15 years of service to Navajo County and the Navajo County Health Department :  Eric Scott recognized Jeffrey Nafziger for his retirement after nearly 15 years with the Navajo County Public Health Services District. Jeff Lee, Health District Director, spoke about Mr. Nafziger, indicating that he has conducted about 6500 health inspections in Navajo County, and he will be missed throughout the State by his counterparts. The Board of Supervisors thanked Mr. Nafziger for his service to Navajo County and congratulated him on his retirement.  Mr. Nafziger was presented with a set of coasters.

b.

Health Benefits Update :  Bryan Layton spoke about the employee health plan. Eric Scott gave a presentation regarding the employee health benefits, indicating that in July 2014 Navajo County became self-funded. He indicated that we went out to bid in 2017, and Jason Andrade was selected as our consultant. He stated that in January 2018 we made some material changes to the plan design. Mr. Scott spoke about the actual spend for claims for Navajo County, which has been trending downward since those material changes were made.  He spoke about the positive feedback from employees, including lower bills, appreciation that they can go anywhere and the flat premiums.  He further spoke about critical feedback, including balancing bills, difficulty in navigating through and that the network is confusing.  Mr. Scott stated that in response to the critical feedback, they have made additions to the plan, including implementing a Navajo County Health Guide, Kayli Medina, who has processed 14 life events which has produced over $12,000 in savings to the health plan since December.  He indicated that Ms. Medina helps employees navigate through our health plan when getting procedures and images.  He stated that our list of direct contracts with providers has continued to grow, and Mr.  Andrade was able to finalize our agreement with Honor Health in Phoenix.  He indicated that Mr. Andrade has worked extensively with employees on receiving balance bills.  Mr. Andrade spoke about the benefit positioning, meaning how our benefits compare to the State of Arizona, as well as other government entities in Arizona.  He indicated that we are slightly behind the benchmark by 3% for our benefit plan.  He stated that in order to achieve the benchmark, we would need to increase the employee cost by 170% and the employer would need to decrease its cost by 27%.  He spoke about how we exceed the current benchmark for out-of-pocket maximum and HSA contributions.  Mr. Andrade indicated that when an employee uses the health guide to navigate the system and procure services, we are sharing $250.00 into their HSA account for images and up to $1,000.00 into their HSA account for procedures. The Board thanked Mr. Scott and Mr. Andrade for their work on our health plan.

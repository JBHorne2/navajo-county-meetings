NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, November 15, 2019

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr., Supervisor District I; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman, each appeared telephonically.

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Rayleen Richards, Elections Director; Jalyn Gerlich, Superintendent of Schools

9:03 a.m. Call To Order : Chairwoman Dawnafe Whitesinger

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS ACTING AS THE BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :

a.

Consideration and approval of the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer's recommendation on hearings conducted on November 7, 2019, for Petition for Review of Notice of Change on APN: 304-37-061; 109-07-070D; 205-32-116; 206-23-024; 210-09-023B; 210-33-037A; 409-24-207C and 409-24-131 : Supervisor Williams made a motion to enter into the Board of Equalization; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack . Vote unanimous approving the motion. Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board discussed the item . Supervisor Williams made a motion to approve the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer's recommendation on hearings conducted on November 7, 2019, for Petition for Review of Notice of Change on APN: 304-37-061; 109-07-070D; 205-32-116; 206-23-024; 210-09-023B; 210-33-037A; 409-24-207C and 409-24-131; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Williams made a motion to return to regular session. Motion seconded by Supervisor Jack . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of Official Canvass conducted on November 5, 2019 for Winslow USD, Joseph City USD and Blue Ridge USD : Rayleen Richards, Election Director discussed the item . Supervisor Williams made a motion to approve the Official Canvass conducted on November 5, 2019 for Winslow USD, Joseph City USD and Blue Ridge USD; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :  At 9:08 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  December 10, 2019

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, November 5, 2025

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chair

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board (all appearing virtually)

1:01 p.m . Call To Order:  The meeting was called to order by Chairman Whiting .

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

EXECUTIVE SESSION  pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.03(A)(3) for the Board to receive legal advice from its attorney regarding negotiations of a Development Agreement for renewable energy development :
1:02 p.m. Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to enter into Executive Session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshalaki .  Motion passed 4-0, with Vice-Chair Whitesinger being absent.

b.

RECONVENE  Consideration of the negotiations of a Development Agreement for renewable energy development :
1:37 p.m. Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0, with Vice-Chair Whitesinger being absent.

Chairman Whiting thanked everyone for being present.  He indicated that the Board previously directed staff to work on a development agreement with an attorney that specializes in that process and that the purpose of this meeting was for the Board to be provided with an update on the development agreement and some legal information that relates to it.

ADJOURN : 1:38 p.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason Whiting, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED: November 18, 2025

ATTEST:

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration and approval of a proclamation declaring September 10 through September 13, 2025, as "Navajo County Fair Days" :
Rusty Despain, Navajo County President, introduced Navajo County Fair staff, Mina Henning and DJ Beatty.  He thanked the Board for the partnership with Navajo County.  He stated that they were able to do an economic study this year and spoke about the amount of money generated by the Navajo County Fair for their facility as well as the region.  He indicated that they hold approximately sixty-two events a year, not counting the fair, which is the biggest event.  He stated that the fair will start on Wednesday, September 10 at 5:30 p.m., indicating that he hopes everyone will be able to attend.  He thanked Bryan Layton for the relationship that has been established this year, noting that they have had a lot of new developments this year.  He spoke about the joint city/county asphalt project that was done this year, noting that it will be nice especially during inclement weather.  He stated that they have a new sky box in the arena, which was sponsored by Mr. Hendricks, that they have a new grain bin in the pig barns, and have upgraded to LED's, which was also a joint project with the county.  He talked about how the partnership has been beneficial in place of requesting funds.  He spoke about the need to upgrade the grandstands, as well as expand them, which would be more economical for them.  He thanked the Board for all that they do, stated that the fair starts Wednesday and invited everyone to attend.
Chairman Jason Whiting shared his appreciation to Mr. Despain and the Fair Board for their leadership and effective partnership. He commend the organization for operating with a business-minded approach, balancing growth with financial responsibility, and for prioritizing service to citizens over personal gain. He indicated that the success of the recent county fair is acknowledged as a reflection of this strong leadership and collaboration. He shared his appreciation for the forward-thinking mindset, especially in plans like expanding the grandstands, and emphasized how such actions benefit the community and ensure long-term sustainability.
Supervisor Seymore expressed his appreciation for the continued legacy of past leaders.  He spoke about the importance of teamwork and thanked the many volunteers who make events possible, highlighting the value of youth programs like 4-H, sharing a personal story about how it helped shape his own life. He spoke about the positive impact on the community and commend the current leadership's vision.  The thanked the Fair Board and noting that future collaboration is welcomed.
Supervisor Benally thanked the Fair Board and acknowledged the work they've done. She spoke about the timing of the Navajo County Fair in relation to the Navajo Nation Fair, noting that many of residents in her district attend both fairs.  She expressed appreciation for the improvements made, noting that she is looking forward to the County Fair.
Mr. Despain stated that they've contracted with LA Williams, a well-known voice in the Navajo Nation and active social media presence, to help promote the fair. He indicated that the goal is to reach residents on the reservation, reminding them that they are part of the county and encouraging their participation in the fair.
Chairman Whiting read the proposed proclamation.
Supervisor Benally made a motion  to approve a proclamation declaring September 10 through September 13, 2025, as "Navajo County Fair Days", motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring September 2025 as "National Preparedness" month in Navajo County :
Catrina Jenkins stated that September is National Preparedness Month, and read the proposed proclamation.
Supervisor Seymore expressed gratitude for the strong leadership shown by Ms. Jenkins during times of disaster, acknowledging the behind-the-scenes work and sleepless nights involved. He spoke about the community's involvement and commitment, noting that despite experiencing worse fire seasons than in 2000 and 2012, outcomes have improved due to collective efforts. He thanked Ms. Jenkins for her dedication and leadership in disaster preparedness and response.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger thanked Ms. Jenkins for her efforts in promoting emergency preparedness. She spoke about the importance of being ready for the unexpected and shared her appreciation for Ms. Jenkins' work in helping the community create emergency kits, make family plans, and stay informed through reliable sources.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger made a motion  to approve  a Proclamation declaring September 2025 as "National Preparedness" month in Navajo County, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of award of contract #B25-06-019, Buck Springs Road Equestrian Crossing to A J P Electric, Inc :
Will Flake stated that this is a contract to build a solar-powered equestrian crossing on Buck Springs Road in the Country Club area. He stated that the crossing would allow riders to safely stop traffic without dismounting and can also be used by pedestrians. He indicated that the project received three bids, with JP Electric being the lowest at $77,254.10, which is slightly under the engineer's estimate. He requested approval of the contract.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger thanked those involved for their hard work, research, and background efforts that supported the decision to address this specific public safety need. She expressed appreciation for the thorough preparation that made the project possible.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger made a motion  to approve award of contract #B25-06-019, Buck Springs Road Equestrian Crossing to A J P Electric, Inc., motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members:
Supervisor Seymore spoke about the powerful and healing nature of music, sharing the story behind the hymn "It Is Well With My Soul", written by Horatio Spafford after enduring great personal tragedy. He also highlighted the inspiring journey of Jane Zuki, stage name Knight Bird, who battled breast cancer and touched millions with her song "It's Okay". He spoke about how music offers peace, solace, and strength during difficult times and encouraged people to turn to music for comfort and hope amid life's challenges.

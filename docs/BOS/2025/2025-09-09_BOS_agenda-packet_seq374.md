PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT SERVICES: 
a. PUBLIC HEARING: Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number
26-2025, approving/denying an update to Section 1916 (assisted living
home administrative procedures), Sections 502, 602, 702, and 1917
(guest houses/accessory dwelling units), and Article 23 (definitions) of the
Navajo County Zoning Ordinance: 
A presentation was shown.  Cody Cooper spoke about proposed updates
to Navajo County’s zoning ordinances to comply with recent Arizona state
laws. He indicated that the first relates to assisted living homes, requiring
counties to provide a process for granting variances to the 1,200-foot
separation requirement between group homes for individuals with
disabilities.  He further indicated that this change ensures the county
complies with the Americans with Disabilities Act and the Fair Housing
Act by allowing reasonable accommodations for qualifying homes.  He
stated that they recommend adding a section name reasonable
accommodations under group homes for disabled.
He spoke about the new legislation regarding accessory dwelling units,
also known as guest houses. He indicated that the new law mandates
that counties must allow one attached and one detached unit on any
single-family residential lot, and two detached units on lots that are one
acre or larger. He stated that Navajo County is required to adopt
regulations by January 1, 2026, otherwise, accessory dwelling units will
be permitted without any county oversight.  He indicated that they are
proposing ordinance revisions that allow guest houses in all zoning
districts where single-family residences are permitted, noting these
changes include removing minimum lot size and design similarity
requirements, and standardizing a five-foot setback from property lines to
meet fire safety standards. He further indicated that the county may no
longer impose restrictions like requiring additional parking,
owner-residency, or design matching for accessory dwelling units. He
stated that definitions for guest houses and accessory dwelling units will
be updated to align with state law, and language related to manufactured
and RV parks has been clarified to avoid unintended permitting conflicts. 
He stated that staff would recommend approval of these updates to
ensure timely compliance with the state requirements and preserve the
county's ability to regulate accessory dwelling units. He indicated that no
public comments had been received on the proposal at the time of the
presentation.  Chairman Whiting opened the meeting for public
comments.  There being no comments, the public hearing was
closed. Supervisor Seymore inquired if you are in a subdivision and the

importance of preparing for disasters and emergencies that could happen at any time without warning,

medications, first aid items, and other critical resources; and

strong, connected communities; and

resilience, safeguard lives, and ensure that the legacies they have built will endure;

September 2025 as National Preparedness Month in Navajo County, and call upon all residents to take 
proactive steps to prepare for disasters and emergencies, thereby protecting the life they have built and 
creating a lasting legacy for future generations.

Motion before the Board:
RECONVENE: Consideration and approval of the Proposed Settlement Agreements with G8 Opioid
Manufacturers Alovgen Inc., Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc., Apotex Inc., Hikma Pharmaceuticals USA,
Inc., Indivior Inc., Mylan Pharmaceuticals INc., Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, Inc., and Zydus
Pharmaceuticals (USA) Inc., and to authorize legal counsel to execute participation agreement with the
eight manufacturers.
Background:
Eight companies that are manufacturers of generic opioid medications are seeking a settlement in the
national opioid litigation pending in the State of Ohio.   The specifics of the proposed settlement
agreements will be discussed in executive session.
Recommendation: 
It is recommended that the Board of Supervisors approve the settlement agreements with the eight
manufacturers of generic opioid medications that are seeking a settlement at this time.

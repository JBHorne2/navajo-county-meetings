NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, December 17, 2025

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I (appearing virtual); Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II (appearing virtual); Jason Whiting, Chairman (appearing virtual); Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV (appearing virtual); Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chair (appearing virtual)

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Leah Thomas, Clerk of the Board

11:02 a.m . Call To Order:  by Chairman Jason Whiting.

CONSENT AGENDA:

a.

Amendment #2 of Special Use Authorization with the U.S. Department of Agriculture Forest Service for Tall Timbers Park
Supervisor Seymore stated that this is an agreement that Tall Timbers and the Forest Service have been working on.  He said that the Forest Service reached out and asked for an extension due to the previous government shutdown and that they are acting in good faith and intend to sign this agreement.
Chairman Whiting thanked Supervisor Seymore for sharing
Vote 4-0 approving with Supervisor Benally not voting.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES:

a.

Consideration of entering into a Dark Fiber Indefeasible Right of Use (IRU) Agreement with Frontier Communications of America, Inc.
Grant Evans explained that the item is an indefeasible right of use or IRU agreement with Frontier.  He recalled that in May of 2025, staff brought a proposed pricing structure and IRU agreement template for leasing the Middle Mile Fibers to the board.  Grant said that staff has worked with their commercialization partner, EX2 in negotiating this IRU agreement, in alignment with that pricing structure and IRU template from May. He explained that this IRU or long-term lease agreement is a 20-year agreement for 4 fibers for the entire 106 miles of the Middle Mile Broadband Network.  Grant reminded the board that there is a one-time IRU fee that will be paid at execution of the agreement and an annual maintenance fee that will be paid annually by the grantee on the anniversary of the agreement.  Mr. Evans said that this is the first IRU brought to the board and the first step in bringing improved and enhanced broadband availability to Navajo County and that staff recommends approval in entering into this agreement with Frontier.  Chairman Whiting thanked Grant for the explanation.
Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve, motion seconded by  Vice Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote 4-0 approving the motion with Supervisor Benally not voting
Supervisor Seymore and Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger both thanked Grant and Staff for their work on this agreement.

ADJOURN: 11:06 a.m. meeting adjourned.

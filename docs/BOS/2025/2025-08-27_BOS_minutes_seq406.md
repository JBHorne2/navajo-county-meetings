NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Wednesday, August 27, 2025

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chair

Absent:
Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:00 a.m . Call To Order:
Chairman Jason Whiting called the meeting to order.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS :

a.

Consideration and adoption of the Fiscal Year 2026 Tax Rates and Levies for the County Education District (per ARS 15-997.01) for Navajo County :
Rachael Lewis thanked everyone for being present. She apologized for the mistake and thanked Scott from ATRA for catching it. She stated that listed on the tax rates that were approved in the last meeting for the county education district were based off of last year's qualifying tax rate, 50% of that at 1.5930 and a levy of $15,083. She asked that the Board approve that rate be corrected and changed to 50% of this year's qualifying tax rate, set by statute to 1.5606 with a levy of $14,776 and the remainder of the tax rates and levies remain the same.

Supervisor Seymore  made a motion  to adopt the Fiscal Year 2026 Tax Rates and Levies for the County Education District (per ARS 15-997.01) for Navajo County, motion seconded by Supervisor Benally . Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Peshlakai being absent.

ADJOURN :  At 8:34 a.m. meeting adjourned.

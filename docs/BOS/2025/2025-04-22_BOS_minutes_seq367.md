SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration and adoption of a proclamation declaring the week of May 11th - 17th, 2025, as National Police Week in Navajo County, Arizona :
Sheriff David Clouse thanked Navajo County for annually adopting proclamations recognizing and supporting law enforcement. He indicated that the support extends not only to the Navajo County Sheriff's deputies but to all law enforcement officers serving in the county. He acknowledged their continuous dedication, working 24/7. He noted that National Correctional Officers Week is observed first, followed by Police Week later in the month.  He requested that the Board to adopt May 11–17 as National Police Week in Navajo County, aligning with the national observance proclaimed by President Kennedy in 1962. He stated that May 15th is recognized as National Peace Officer Memorial Day, honoring officers who died in the line of duty, noting that a memorial ceremony will be held that evening in Show Low at 6:00 p.m. He spoke about honoring both fallen and active law enforcement officers and thanked the Sheriff's Office staff for their efforts in organizing and supporting Police Week.  He read the proposed proclamation.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger thanked Sheriff Clouse and expressed gratitude to all who serve the community, highlighting the importance of taking time to appreciate their dedication and the sacrifices they make.

Vice-Chair Whitesinger made a motion  to adopt a proclamation declaring the week of May 11th - 17th, 2025, as National Police Week in Navajo County, Arizona, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .
Supervisor Benally thanked the Sheriff, deputies, staff, and police officers across Navajo County for their service. She spoke about attending a past memorial event in Show Low honoring fallen officers and express appreciation, well wishes, and hopes for everyone's safety and protection.

Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Daryl Seymore being absent.

b.

Consideration and adoption of a proclamation declaring the week of May 4th - 10th, 2025, as National Correctional Officers Week in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe :
Sheriff David Clouse requested that the Board designate May 4–10 as National Correctional Officers Week in Navajo County, recognizing the vital role correctional officers play in the criminal justice system. He spoke of the demanding and often thankless nature of the job and praised the detention staff for maintaining one of Arizona's cleanest and safest jail systems. He gave special recognition to Commander Garcia and his team for upholding national accreditation standards.  He read portions of the proposed proclamation.

Chairman Whiting thanked Sheriff Clouse for recognizing and expressing appreciation for his team and the valuable work they do.

Supervisor Benally made a motion  to adopt a proclamation declaring the week of May 4th - 10th, 2025, as National Correctional Officers Week in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe, motion seconded by  Vice-Chair Whitesinger .

Supervisor Benally thanked the correctional officers, acknowledging the challenges of their job.  She shared a personal connection, noting that family members serve in the role, and express gratitude to all correctional officers and sheriff's department staff for their dedication to protecting the community.

Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Daryl Seymore being absent.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of the award of Contract Number B25-02-005 for Starlight Pavement Reconstruction to PAP, LLC :
Madhav Mundle presented a contract for a pavement reconstruction project in the Starlight Ridge Estates Subdivision, located in District Five.  He indicated that the project involves rebuilding sections of several roads using new materials and asphalt. He stated that four bids were received, and that PAP, LLC was the lowest responsive bidder. He noted that the project is part of the county's CIP program, with a second phase planned. He stated that staff recommends awarding the contract to PAP, LLC.

Vice-Chair Whitesinger expressed appreciation for the efforts to address long-standing concerns about road conditions in the Starlight community. She acknowledged the work done to understand the community's needs and find sustainable solutions for road recovery and long-term maintenance.

Vice-Chair Whitesinger made a motion  to approve the award of Contract Number B25-02-005 for Starlight Pavement Reconstruction to PAP, LLC, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .

Chairman Whiting confirmed that the contract must go to the lowest qualified bidder, in accordance with the county's procurement policy. He expressed a desire for a local preference in bidding but acknowledge that current laws do not allow for such a preference.

Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Daryl Seymore being absent.

b.

Consideration of Attachment D to The Navajo Nation Intergovernmental Agreement for assistance with roadway maintenance materials for the N8065 Road Maintenance Project :
Nicole Reske stated that the Forest Lake Chapter, in coordination with the Navajo Division of Transportation, has requested assistance for the N8065 road maintenance project. She indicated that the unpaved road serves homes, businesses, school buses, residents, and first responders. She stated that the project involves placing aggregate base gravel over 0.8 miles, with an estimated cost of $99,952, noting that there is sufficient capacity in the HURF budget. She indicated that staff recommend approval and that the project aligns with the county's transportation partnership process.

Supervisor Benally expressed gratitude to Nicole and others for their help with the road maintenance project. She spoke of the challenges of muddy roads, especially after rain, and emphasize how graveling stabilizes the road, making it safer for school buses and residents.

Supervisor Benally made a motion  to approve Attachment D to The Navajo Nation Intergovernmental Agreement for assistance with roadway maintenance materials for the N8065 Road Maintenance Project, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Daryl Seymore being absent.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC :
Michellsey Benally, a member of the Navajo Nation, provided an update on recent legislative developments regarding rural groundwater management that could impact the region. She discussed Senate Bill 1520, which proposes the creation of Basin Management Areas (BMAs) for certain rural regions but excludes areas like Navajo County, which lacks similar groundwater management options. She also highlighted Senate Bill 1425 and House Bill 2714, which propose creating Rural Groundwater Management Areas (RGMAs) with local councils but were not heard in committee. She indicated that while these efforts focus on southern and western Arizona, there has been no representation from northern Arizona, leaving the region without management tools for potential groundwater challenges. She encouraged the Board to stay engaged and mentioned an upcoming Rural Groundwater Resilience Workshop on June 18, 2025, that will focus on the Little Colorado River Plateau Basin and urged the Board to advocate for inclusive groundwater management tools for northern Arizona.

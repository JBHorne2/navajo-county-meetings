ARIZONA DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY:  Director Steve Owens 
Update by Arizona Department of Environmental Quality Director:  Director Owens introduced his staff 
and stated that his first meeting as Director ha d been in Navajo County and it related to underground 
storage tanks (UST) and that these important issues involving County constituents are now being resolved. 
 He advised that the Governor gave him a very clear directive to assist rural areas and thus they started the 
Route 66 UST initiative that became a model for other areas, including the EPA.  He cautioned that the last 
date for eligibility for reimbursements related to UST removal is June 30, 2006, requesting that 
Supervisors get this information out to their constituents.  He said his office assists in developing grants to 
provide additional revenues sources and that they provided a grant for Trees for the Rim in the aftermath 
of the Rodeo Chediski fire.  He reported they ar e working to clean up the rubble from the burned JC 
Penney building in Winslow even though it is not an ADEQ issue.  He outlined participation in assisting 
water system management, citing one that his staff is currently working on in Navajo County.  In response 
to a question from Mr. Deal, he stated that the current definition of UST removal reimbursement affects 
incorporated areas of 15,000 or less and does not address unincorporated areas.  He said that even though 
there is still the sovereignty issue to contend with on the reservation, they are exploring clarifying the 
definition to include unincorporated areas.  All Board members expressed their appreciation to Director 
Owens and his staff, especially commending Byron James.

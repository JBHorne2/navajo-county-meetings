BOARD OF SUPERVISORS/COUNTY ATTORNEY:  
 
1) EXECUTIVE SESSION pursuant to ARS§ 38-431.03(A)(1):  Discussion and possible action 
concerning the Interim Public Works Director rega rding personnel issues raised by members of the

2) EXECUTIVE SESSION  pursuant to ARS § 38-431.03(A)(1):  Discussion and possible action 
concerning  the County Manager and personnel issu es raised by members of the Public Works 
Department in a letter dated March 30, 2005. (C ontinuation from Board of Supervisors Meeting on 
April 18, 2005)  
  
Following the Executive Session the Board made the following statement: 
 
The Board of Supervisors has reviewed the two Whistleblower letters submitted by various 
employees in the Public Works Department.  The Board has also met with the County Manager and 
Interim Public Works Director to review those a llegations.  After careful deliberation, the Board has 
determined that further investigation into the allegations of mismanagement is not warranted and would be 
counterproductive.  The validity of allegations concerning open meeting violations and conflict of interest 
will have to be addressed by the Attorney General or County Attorney. 
The Board is in agreement that it is time to move forward in the selection of a new Public Works 
Director.  In retrospect it is now clear that the Board could have proceeded in a more timely manner in the 
selection process.  Many of the misunderstandings and issues that have arisen could have been avoided by 
doing so. 
It is the desire of the Board to hire the best and most qualified candidate to fill this position.  It is 
expected that the screening process will begin at the May 2
nd Board meeting.  Hopefully interview 
schedules will be established at that time.  We unders tand that this matter needs to be resolved in the 
interest of the County and the Public Works Department. 
We invite all employees, Department Heads, and Elected Officials to put aside their differences and 
join the County Manager and Board of Supervisors in moving forward.  The citizens of this County are 
entitled to our best efforts on their behalf. 
 
Sincerely, 
Jerry Brownlow, Chairman 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

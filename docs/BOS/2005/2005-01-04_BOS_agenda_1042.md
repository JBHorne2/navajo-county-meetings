AGENDA ACTION NOTIFICATION 
EMERGENCY MEETING 
JANUARY 4, 2005 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: 
1. Declaration of emergency by Board of Supervisors due to failure or 
damage to Winslow flood control levee and to implement Governor's declaration of emergency relating to 
flood hazard conditions in Northern Arizona.  Resolution #01-05 approved, declaring a flood emergency 
in the unincorporated areas of Navajo County; said declaration to be immediately effective 
retroactive to December 29, 2004 and shall remain in effect until further declaration of the Board. 
 
2. Discussion and possible adoption of such motions, resolutions, orders or regulations as may be necessary 
or appropriate in light of the above-described emergency.  Chairman authorized to sign letter to 
Governor Napolitano requesting assistance from the State of Arizona to help with costs involved 
with Winslow Levee emergency.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
EMERGENCY MEETING 
 
January 4, 2005 
 
Board of Supervisors Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona – Time: 3:05 p.m. 
 
PRESENT:
  Jerry Brownlow, Chairman and Jesse Thom pson, Member (present by conference call); 
J.R. DeSpain, Member; David Tenney, Member; Jame s G. Jayne, County Manager; Lance Payette, 
Chief Deputy County Attorney; Larry Dunagan, Em ergency Services Director; Tom Hieb, Deputy 
Director Flood Control; Dick Young, Interim Co-Director Public Works; Kathy Hieb, Deputy Clerk of 
the Board 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: 1) Declaration of emergency by Board of Supervisors due to failure or 
damage to Winslow flood control levee and to im plement Governor's declaration of emergency 
relating to flood hazard conditions in Northern Arizona; and 2) Di scussion and possible adoption of 
such motions, resolutions, orders or regulations as may be necessary or appropriate in light of the 
above-described emergency : Mr. Dunagan explained that he received a call from the Arizona 
Department of Emergency Management this morni ng. They are aware of the problems we have had 
with the Winslow levee. Under th e Governor’s Declaration of Emergency, the State would pay up to 
75% of our costs to repair the leve e. We can ask to be included in the Governor’s declaration and she 
could amend her declaration. This amended declarati on would include only the Winslow Levee at this 
time; we would have to gather fu rther information regarding the rese rvation damages from the storm. 
Mr. Thompson advised he has been receiving calls from Leupp and Birdsprings regarding storm 
damages.  They have asked to be kept informed a bout any changes in the rive r, etc., so they can be 
prepared Mr. Thompson expressed concerns about the reservation communities  and how they can be 
helped by this declaration. Mr. Dunagan stated he would look try to get additional information 
regarding these concerns. Mr. Dunagan noted that  the Governor’s declar ation includes Coconino, 
Yavapai, Gila and northern Maricopa Counties at th is time. We would need to send a written request 
to be included in the declaration. (The draft letter was read into the record by the Clerk) Mr. DeSpain 
made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the le tter to Governor Napolitano requesting 
assistance from the State of Arizona to help with costs associated with the Winslow Levee emergency; 
motion seconded by Mr. Tenney; vote unanimous approving the motion. (Mr. Deal absent) 
 
Mr. Payette prepared and read a draft resolution, declaring a flood emergency in the unincorporated 
areas of Navajo County. Mr. Tenney made a motion to adopt Resolution #01-05, declaring a flood 
emergency in the unincorporated areas of Nava jo County; motion seconded by Mr. DeSpain; vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
 
BOARD BUSINESS: 
Mr. Jayne advised that well house #1 has a substantial amount of water in it 
and may need some substantial repair. It is unknown at this time exactly what the problem is. He noted 
that a “snow day” may have to be declared tomorrow if the problem is not fixed. The Board expressed 
no objections to this if necessary.

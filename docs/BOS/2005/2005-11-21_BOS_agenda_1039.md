Procedures relating to Commuting Use of County Vehicles:  Ms. Calisen reviewed the provisions of 
the amended Travel Policy.  Mr. Thompson inquired if other elected officials had the opportunity to 
review the policy and Ms. Calisen responded they had and as a result some revisions had been made to 
this document prior to Board review.  Mr. Brow nlow inquired if the Sheriff’s department is 
comfortable with the wording and Karen Bray res ponded that in a conversation with the IRS it was 
determined that officers are on duty when they enter their car but non-certified officers are subject to

relayed this information to the Sheriff’s department and while they are not happy with this they agree 
they must comply with IRS regulations.  Mr. Tenney made a motion  to approve the revision of 
Article 30 of the Personnel Policies and Procedures relating to Commuting Use of County Vehicles; 
motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.

4) Discussion and possible approval of Resolution No. ___ - 05  supporting the City of Holbrook’s 
request to the Arizona Department of Transportation (ADOT) for a Small Area Transportation Study 
and authorizing the expenditure of $12,500.00  HURF funds for Navajo County’s share of the match:  
Mr. Dick Young of City of Holbrook stated that there is considerable interest in economic 
development in the Holbrook area with the comp letion of the hydrology study showing an assured 
water supply for the next 100 years.  He reported there is substantial interest by light and heavy 
industry and residential development in this area, all of which are dependent on transportation.  The 
County and City have been working together on transportation issues and conducting a transportation 
study will support receiving funds such as those need ed for the Obed Bridge.  ADOT finances the 
majority of the transportation studies and request 20% match.  This proposal provides for the City of 
Holbrook contributing 10% and the County contributing 10%.  It is anticipated that the study will not 
cost over $125,000 and will cover the area from Joseph City to the Petrified Forest to Woodruff.  Mr. 
DeSpain advised that he believes it is important to conduct this study and made a motion to approve 
Resolution No. 82 - 05  supporting the City of Holbrook’s reque st to the Arizona Department of 
Transportation (ADOT) for a Small Area Transporta tion Study and authorizing the expenditure of 
$12,500.00 HURF funds for Navajo County’s share of the match; Mr. Thompson seconded the motion; 
vote unanimous approving the motion.

6) CDBG status report:  Mr. Menlove reported there have been several discussions with the Department 
of Housing and NACOG who handle our CDBG gran ts and introduced Margaret Keener and Bob 
Stevens.  Mr. Stevens provided a summary of the Navajo County Rehabilitation investments and 
reviewed it with the Board. He reported that Navajo County is one of the few counties to complete on 
and off reservation homes.  Mr. DeSpain advised it is difficult to assist those who have deteriorating 
houses because of standards set in those cities a nd towns, inquiring if those standards have been 
reviewed or if there has been any trend to change.  He gave the example of a home in District 3 that is 
not up to code and inquired if they could still use these funds.  Mr. Stevens stated the requirements 
keep increasing and the funds do not increase proportionately.  He stated the requirements of the State 
are becoming stricter which raises the cost per house and reduces the number that can be served.  He 
stated he feels the State needs to be reminded that rural Arizona does not have the same resources as 
the municipalities.  He said the State wants insurance and many homes on the reservation can not be 
insured, which limits those who can be funded.  Mr . DeSpain suggested writing a letter to the State 
regarding relief for this issue and invite their cons ideration of the exterior shelter of that home to 
protect the families from the elements even if the interior was not up to specific standards. Mr. Stevens 
stated the support of the Board on this issue would be appreciated.  He stated they would also like to 
consider replacement housing in the future, advising that this is a very expensive process.  Mr. 
DeSpain inquired if it would be appropriate for th e Board, Mr. Stevens and Ms. Keener to draft a 
request for modifications to lower the code require ments to allow an outside cover to ward off the 
elements.  Mr. Brownlow stated the letter itself would have to go on a future Board agenda.  Mr. Jayne 
stated that Mr. Menlove and Ms. Mickle could receive direction to work with Mr. Stevens and Ms. 
Keener for a letter for the next consent agenda.  Mr . DeSpain offered to hand carry that letter.  Mr. 
Thompson stated that the electricity and inside  running water requirements are stringent for the 
reservation and this Board had already requested a waiver.  He said he would support Mr. DeSpain in 
this request for a revision of the State requirements.  He stated that last year some homes needed repair 
under emergency funding and this year he disc ussed the possibility of NACOG partnering with 
reservation communities with Mr. Menlove.  Mr. Stevens reported neither program is set up for 
emergency repairs as going through the bidding process is time consuming and the homes are subject 
to minimum quality standards.  Ms. Keener reported an application for limited housing repair is due in 
January and a letter must submitted 30 days in adva nce stating that the County is interested in 
applying.  Mr. DeSpain proposed that a letter be prepared to satisfy this requirement for giving notice

grant are now complete and Ms. Mickle said they are.  Mr. Brownlow inquired if $593,793 is the total 
amount available and Mr. Stevens stated that it is and they are currently bidding the projects out.  He

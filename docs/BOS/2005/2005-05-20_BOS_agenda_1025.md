BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
 
May 20, 2005 
 
Board of Supervisors Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona – Time:  10:30 a.m. 
 
PRESENT:  Jerry Brownlow, Chairman; Percy Deal, Vice-Chairman; David Tenney, Member; Jesse 
Thompson, Member; J.R. DeSpain, Member; Mel Bowers, County Attorney; June Foard, Acting Deputy 
Clerk of the Board. 
 
EXECUTIVE SESSION: Pursuant to ARS§ 38-431.03(A)(1):  for Personnel Matters Re: Interview of 
applicants for the Public Works Director position:   Mr. Tenney made a motion  to enter Executive 
Session to interview applicants for the Public Works Director Position; motion seconded by Mr. 
Thompson; vote unanimous approving motion.   
 
Mr. Thompson made a motion to reconvene the regular session; motion seconded by Mr. Tenney; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.   
 
Mr. DeSpain made a motion to approve conducting a second interview for Homero Vela and Eugene 
Parsons on June 6, 2005 with letters to be sent to the non-successful candidates; motion seconded by Mr. 
Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
ADJOURN:  There being no further business to come before the Board of Supervisors, Mr. Thompson 
made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Mr. DeSpain; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
The meeting adjourned at 3:20 p.m.

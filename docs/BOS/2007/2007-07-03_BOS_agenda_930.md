d. Consideration and tentative adoption  of proposed Navajo County 2007 -2008 budget : James Menlove 
introduced Tad Spader as the Deputy Director of Finance -Accounting.  Mr. Spader thanked the Board for 
the opportunity to work for the County stating he looks forward to making a contribution to the county.  Mr. 
Menlove reviewed the proposed 2007 -2008 budget stating it is  basically the same as present ed during the 
budget hearings.  He reviewed the budget totalling $119,637,655, reviewing the Summary Budget 
Schedules A-E, Summary by Departments, the General Fund, and Special Revenue Funds.  He reviewed the 
Summary of Tax Levies and Rates, advising the county prim ary tax rate will be reduced from over 62 cents 
to a little over 60 .41 cents.  He advised  fire district assistance tax of ten cents is the maximum allowed by 
statute, the Little Colorado River Flood Control District and Navajo County Flood Control have maintained 
the same tax rate over the last few years; Public Health Services District remains at 19.63 cents; and because 
the general tax property rate is cut by two cents, they recommend a one cent increase for the library district 
tax rate.  He presented the rates for special district taxing entities and reviewed all proposed revenues for all 
districts and funds.  In response to a question from  Supervisor DeSpain, Mr. Menlove stated that the county 
sales tax and the state shared revenue are largest source of  revenues, pointing out other revenues on page 2 
of schedule C.  Supervisor Thompson inquired if the Transaction Privilege Tax includes the State shared tax 
and Mr. Menlove stated the amount listed estimated at $11,505,000 does include the non-Native American 
business tax that goes to the State Department of Revenue who divides it up according to percentages going 
back to County.  Supervisor Deal inquired why, when Indian gaming revenues increased, there is no 
increase listed on Schedule C and there followed a discussion of where all such revenues go and how they 
are divided at the State level through application by cities, towns and counties.   In response to a question 
from Supervisor Deal , Mr. Menlove stated a meeting with the major tax payers and represen tatives of 
ATRA is scheduled for Monday.  James Jayne advised that tax information is routinely provided to the 
major tax payers and  this county has one of the lowest in the State with a good relationship with the major 
tax payers in the State.  Mr. Menlove reviewed the general fund schedule and other special taxing districts 
that are included in the budget book.

CENTER FOR DESERT ARCHAEOLOGY:  Update on Little Colorado River Valley National Heritage 
area designation: Linda Marie Golier conducted a power point presentation regarding this project, providing the 
history, how the designation will leverage grant dollars, the duties of the working groups, public information 
outreach efforts, inviting the board to be available to receive information regarding this project, and the timeline 
of the project.  In response to questions fro m Supervisors, Ms. Golier advised: this  Heritage area would 
probably not purchas e land for the expansion of the Petrified Forest and that the Petrified Forest does have a 
presence in this project ; Heritage areas are established by an Act of Congress ; there have been ten  National 
Heritage areas designated since 2006 and all have very strong private property protection language in their 
designation, that the 2006 group just has to write a letter to opt out; before a Heritage area is enacted, the owner 
must give consent; the entire state of Tennessee is a National Heritage area; she can not address what would and 
would not have to be disclosed on a deed as that is a legal issue;  this designation will probably be introduced to 
Congress in 2009  and then will  work its way through committee ; the main purpose of a Heritage area is to 
promote, conserve or preserve the area; the private property owner has to give written consent and if that owner 
does not want their land included they  can write a letter stating so; the Navajo Nation is gathering information 
about the trading post and they d isseminate information about Native Americans and their communities ; three 
chapters have given letters of support; the Nation’s cultural preservation office is aware of the project; the 
Navajo Nation Department of Tourism has given a letter of support; she met with a sheepherder’s group back in

process; Winslow is provisionally accrediting the Ruby Wash and Ruby Wash Diversion Levee; Holbrook is

delivering bad news , that even though residen ts have always been in a flood plain they will now be on a

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES 
 
FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2007 
 
8:31 a.m. Chairman Tenney called the meeting to order. 
 
PRESENT:  Chairman David Tenney and Clerk of the Board Darlene Fraley were present in the conference 
room at 620 E. McNeil in Show Low, AZ.  Percy Deal, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; J. R. DeSpain, 
Supervisor; Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; and Mel Bowers attended by telephone.   
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS ACTING AS BOARD OF EQUALIZATION: Consideration and possible 
approval of Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendations on hearings conducted on November 14, 
2007 for Petition for Review of Notice of Change :  Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to act as the Board of 
Equalization; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor 
DeSpain reviewed the hearing officer’s recommendations and  made a motion  to accept  the hearing officer 
findings; moti on seconded by Supervisor Brownlow;  vote unanimous approving the motion.  Supervisor 
DeSpain made a motion  to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  Consideration and possible action related to Resolution Number ____ - 07 in 
support of applications for Navajo Nation Fuel Excise Tax Grant and authorization of letters of support to be 
submitted with the applications :  Supervisor Deal reviewed the history of this grant opp ortunity, advising there 
may be a future need to amend the existing MOA.  Mel Bowers suggested minor wording changes on the 
resolution. Supervisor Deal made a motion  to adopt Resolution  #86-07 with recommended changes ; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisors Deal and Thompson 
expressed appreciation to Dusty Parsons and James Jayne for bringing about this cooperation  that will benefit 
school children.   
 
At 8:46 a.m., Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Deal; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

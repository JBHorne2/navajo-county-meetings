SUPERIOR COURT:  Presentation of the 2006 Annual Report of the Judicial Branch : The Honorable 
Judge Nielson reviewed his written 2006 Annual Report. He reviewed the Drug Court program stating 
the current overall percentage of clean drug tests for participants is 98%.  He discussed Fill the Gap 
Funds for new positions in the Justice Courts resulting in closing 59% of the criminal cases within the 
court’s 100 -day goa l.  He conducted a power point presentation reviewing all areas of the judicial 
branch, including case loads, closure rates, increased revenue received, and the  need for additional 
personnel, security updates and probation statistics.  He reported that criminal cases dominate the docket 
and that the judicial branch o perated within the budget for the year.  All s upervisors expressed 
appreciation for the Drug Court Program and its continued success, the recent budget process that went 
smoothly, the new telecommunications program that will limit prisoner transport, and the benefits of the 
new pre-trial services.  They complimented Judge Nielson and the judicial staff for their hard work, the 
improved percentage of cleared cases and the operation of his department.

Board.  J. R. DeSpain, Vice Chairman, was not present.

offered the invocation.

written Personnel Action report; motion seconded by  Supervisor Deal ; vote unanimous approving the

i. The Navajo County hiring process: Shalice Cook reviewed the hiring process as written, responding to 
questions from Supervisors regarding the criminal back ground checks, reference checks,   drug testing, 
the process for disseminating the revised policy to employees/potential employees  and advising that if 
a potential employee disputes the results, they could appeal.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to 
approve the revised Personnel Policy as presented effective January 1, 2008; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.

Commission is to be appointed by the board and discussed its statutory makeup that would include one

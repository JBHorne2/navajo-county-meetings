BOARD BUSINESS:   Supervisor Deal expressed fru stration that the Department of Housing decided to 
disqualify all reservation CDBG projects after many years of funding them and requested this item be on a 
future agenda.  He expressed disappointment that there has not been active county participation in the planning 
of the Indian Health Services’ Hardrock Chapter mass vaccination exercise and expressed appreciation to the 
County Manager James Jayne and Dr. Kartchner for their willingness to meet to rectify this.  He discussed the 
recent Kayenta Town Meeti ng where county employees and visiting dignitaries travelled to Kayenta for a 
meeting, stating that a letter and a check for that meeting were not included in the meeting materials resulting in 
embarrassment and requested more attention be paid to details in the future.  He thanked Dusty Parsons and the 
Justice Court for their support during this meeting.   No other supervisors reported. 
 
At 11:55 a.m., the Board of Supervisors recessed for lunch. 
 
At 1:30 p.m.  Vice Chairman DeSpain  opened the Navajo Count y 200 7 Back Tax Land Auction, advising 
prospective purchasers that: 
 All sales are final 
 The bidder should seek advice on the marketability of title conveyed by Treasurer’s Deed 
 The bidder should examine property before purchasing 
 The bidder should check the Assessor’s map for location of the parcel 
 No warranties or guarantees as to the size or condition of property 
 No refunds will be made 
He strongly encouraged bidders to pay heed to the above warnings, stating there can environmental issues, there 
may not be pins identifying the property and there may not be utilities to the property.  He discussed the bidding 
process and introduced Manny Hernandez and staff from the County Treasurer’s office. Vice-Chairman 
DeSpain gave those present the opportunity to ask questions regarding the properties and there were no 
questions.  During the ensuing auction, all properties with the exception of 109-35-044B were sold.   
 
At 3:09 a quorum reconvened with Supervisor Thompson, Vice-Chairman DeSpain, and Supervisor Deal 
present.  Supervisor Brownlow was not present. 
 
Consideration and possible action to deem all properties sold as auctioned:   Vice-Chairman DeSpain advised 
several properties were bid at less than the minimum due and discussed the authority of the board to accept 
these offers.  Treasurer Manny Hernandez presented a letter to the board advising he considered the $25,000 bid 
on parcel 202-39-008 unacceptable requesting that the bid consider the taxes due of $46,533.72 as a minimum 
acceptable purchase price.  At Supervisors Deal’s request this letter will remain on file in the Clerk’s office as 
part of the permanent record.  Following considerable discussion of all properties that were bid under the 
minimum due, Supervisor Deal made a motion that this board sell, with the exception of APN 202-39-008, all 
properties that were underbid for the minimum due with those who bid less than minimum offered the first right 
of refusal on their properties; the exception property, APN 202-39-008, to be offered for $46,533.72; all unsold 
properties to be listed on the ongoing property list; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous 
approving the motion. 
 
At 3:50 p.m. Supervisor Deal made a motion to adjourn; motion  seconded by Supervisor Thompson . Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

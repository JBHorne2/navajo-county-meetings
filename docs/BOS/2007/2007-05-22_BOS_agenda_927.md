NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2007 
 
 
At 9:04 a.m., Vice Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order. 
 
PRESENT:  Vice Chairman J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor Jesse Thompson, member, and Supervisor Jerry 
Brownlow, member, participat ed by telephone with the phone on speaker for the benefit of all present in the 
Board of Supervisors’ Chambers.  Present in the chambers were:  James G. Jayne, County Manager, Darlene 
Fraley, Clerk of the Board, and Lisa McKee of Elections.  
 
ELECTIONS: Canvass of Official Results of the Timberland Acres Special Road District #1 as conducted on 
May 15, 2007 : Lisa McKee read the results of this election as:  Robert Adler  97, Barbara Miller, 83 and 
Caiaphas Nunnelly, 91 votes for positions on the board.  She advised the tax levy question to produce the sum 
of $70,000 annually or until changed by future election, beginning fiscal year 2007 -08 and ending fiscal year 
2012-13 passed with 89 “yes” votes and 24 “no” votes.  Supervisor DeSpain invited questions and there were 
none.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to approve the Canvass of Official Results as presented; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
ADJOURN: At 9:11 a.m., Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to adjourn, motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow, with motion passing unanimously.

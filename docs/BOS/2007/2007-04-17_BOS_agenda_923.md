Presentation of DFIRM—Map modernization: Homer Vela conducted a power point presentation of the DFIRM 
process and discussed with Supervisors: 
 The deadline for this process was moved up to September 2008 and he has submitted a request for it be 
moved back to the original date of December 2008.   
 All levees will be de-certified and it is the responsibility of the local government to re-certify the levee.   
 Additional flood plains will be identified and property owners in th ose areas will be given the 
opportunity to grandfather flood insurance prior to the maps being issued.   
 The Winslow Levee will be de-certified as it is ethically incorrect to re-certify when there have been two 
piping failures.   
 The levee structure list that will be de -certified and the process to re -certify and the impac t of the 
recertification.   
 The intention and hopeful partnership with Winslow to re-certify the Ruby Wash Levee and Ruby Wash 
Diversion Levee.   
 A railroad embankment east of Holbrook will be de -certified that would impact approximately three 
homes. 
 PAL is a provisional approval to allow governmental entities to collaborate for two years to recertify the 
levees.   
 Different Flood Zones —Zone A, Zone AE, Zone X (C) shaded, Zone X un -shaded, Zone D  with the 
increased implementation of a Zone D as covering tho se areas that were previously undetermined but 
contain possible zone hazards.  He advised there is no flood insurance required for a Zone D but if they 
do obtain it, it is more expensive than if it were a Zone C.   
 The reservation is not covered under DFIR M regulations but they could join the flood study program 
when they would like to do so.

Jesse Thompson, Supervisor Jerry Brownlow, County Attorney Mel Bowers, County Manager James Jayne, and 
Clerk of the Board Darlene Fraley.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:   
a. Consideration and possible approval of a Proclamation Declaring December 3, 2007 the Day of the 
Cougars: Chairman Tenney  recognized the presence of the Show Cougars’ fo otball team, 
congratulating them, Coach Ricedorf and his staff on their State Championship.  Coach Ricedorf, 
Jared MacArthur and Derek Geiger  discussed the season, what they had learned and thanked the 
board for this recognition.   Supervisor Brownlow m ade a motion  to approve a Proclamation 
Declaring December 3, 2007 the Day of the Cougars ; motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain; 
vote unanimous approving the motion. 
b. Bashas and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence : Chairman Tenney  discussed this new 
endeavor to recognize and promote excellence at the County.  He presented a plaque and fruit 
basket to Darcey McKee  of the Health Department and Deputy Recorder Barbara Hunt.  Darcey 
McKee and Barbara Hunt addressed the board and thanked them for this recognition.  
c. Consideration and possible approval of Resolution Number ____ - 07 in opposition of the 
proposed Forest S ervice Travel Management Regulations : All supervisors discussed public 
opposition they had encountered to these new travel management regulations  and the need to 
encourage other counties to adopt a similar resolution to keep these roads open.  Concern was 
expressed that this is one step closer to closing the fo rest service land to the public.  Supervisors 
encouraged the press to print as much information as possible for the public to understand their use 
of forest land is threatened. Supervisor Brownlow mad e a motion  to approve  Resolution 
Number 89 - 07 in opposition of the proposed Forest Service Travel Management Regulations ; 
motion seconded by  Supervisor DeSpain; vote unanimous approving the motion approving the 
motion.

BOARD BUSINESS :  Supervisor Thompson introduced Andre Leonard who was previously a public safety 
officer, welcoming him to this meeting .  Supervisors reported on activities within their districts and issues that 
will impact the county including: the successful stabilization center celebration that took place at the county 
complex, the flu vaccine mass exercise at Hardrock Chapter; the proposed changes to expand the reintroduction 
of the wolf program; holiday parades and festivities taking place within the municipalities; the upcoming Santa 
Cruz Valley National Heritage panel discussion; and the necessity to move forward with opposition to the travel 
management plan.  Appreciation was expressed to staff for their support of activities and issues.  All expressed 
appreciation for the rain and snow that is bringing moisture to the  land.  Mr. Bowers advised the south county 
facility will have a charging attorney and misdemeanor prosecutor on site which will be a real benefit to law 
enforcement at that end of the county.  He thanked all who made this possible.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
SPECIAL MEETING 
AUGUST 14, 2007 
 
 
PRESENT:  Present in the Conference Room at 620 E. McNeil, Show Low, Arizona: David Tenney, Chairman 
and Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the Board .  Vice -Chairman J.R. DeSpain and Supervisor Jerry Brownlow  
participated by telephone through a speaker phone in the Conference Room that made all meeting discussion 
and action audible for  participants and audience members .  Not participating:  Supervisor Percy Deal and 
Supervisor Jesse Thompson.   
 
At 2:04 p.m. Chairman Tenney called the meeting to order. 
 
Consideration and possible adoption of secondary property tax for the Show Low Bluff Community Facilities 
District:  Chairman David Tenney and Clerk Darlene Fraley reviewed the history of this tax rat e adoption, 
stating that  the City of Show Low, acting as the Board of Directors for the Show Low Bluff Community 
Facilities District, adopted a tax rate of $3.55 per hundred subsequent to  the Navajo County Board of 
Supervisors’ adoption of all other county  tax rates.   They explained it is now necessary for the Board of 
Supervisors to perform the ministerial function adopting the tax rate.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to 
adopt the $3.55 tax rate for the Show Low Bluff Community Facilities District; mot ion seconded by Supervisor 
DeSpain; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
At 2:08 p.m., Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

b. Presentation of the published Navajo County FY2005 Audited Financial Statements : James Menlove  
introduced Dennis Miller, financial consultant, and representatives of the Auditor General’s Financial Audit 
Division.  Dennis Mathiesson, Dire ctor of the Financial Audit Division , distributed the  audited FY05 
financial report, advising the County  was financially sound in that year.  He discussed the history of many 
years when the county was not audited and then the negative impact when  GASB rules changed, which was 
touted as the largest change in governmental reporting in 100 years.  He expressed appreciation for the 
county’s willingness to accept assistance in catching up the audits and the Auditor General’s willingness to 
partner with the county to assure this happens.  He advised they are pleased with the progress the county has 
made and that 2006 is expected to be completed soon.  James Menlove, James Jayne and Supervisors 
expressed appreciation to Auditor General Davenport and her staff for their assistance and for their presence 
here today.

g. Community Development Block Grant  
i. Update on CDBG funding in Navajo County: Randy Sullivan provided information regarding progress 
on CDBG projects, advising that four of the projects were denied due to being on reservation lands.  He 
stated the other five projects were coming back for more qualifications  but the applicants were given a 
pre-award letter. He further advised that  because four projects were denied they will change the scope 
on the other five and include administration costs in the revised scope.  He advised the scope of the 
fairgrounds project will be changed to pave the midway and provide handicap accessibility  and that the 
Chairman will sign these revised scopes .  Supervisor Thompson thanked Mary Springer, Rod Ross and 
Randy Sullivan for participating with him in a meeting with Fred Karges, Director, and other staff of the 
Department of Housing where it was explained that while these funds could not go on the reservation, 
they will work to identify other funds that can assist.  Supervisors requested an itemized list of all 
projects that would move forward and discussed the impact of the new policy interpretation.   Supervisor 
Deal expressed extreme disappointment in this interpretation and discussed the negative impact it will 
have on county residents in his district and their trust of governme nt.  He discussed the great need for 
this housing rehabilitation to bring running water and electricity into homes.  Supervisor Deal requested 
that administration and  the board consider the alternative by utilizing county money and other grant 
money to finish what was started as a CDBG project, rather than saying the project can not continue.  
ii. Consideration and possible approval of a letter regarding recent funding decisions impacting Navajo 
County reservation residents : Supervisor Deal made a motion  to approve the letter regarding recent

Thompson. Rod Ross reviewed the letter that stated many reservation residents were disenfranchised by 
this ruling and requesting the initiation of  a dialogue with HUD to allow th is funding source to be 
restored.  Following further discussion, the vote was unanimous approving the motion.

INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY :  Discussion of Advanced Telecom and Broadband Deployment in 
Arizona: Robert Heming  conducted a Power Point Presentation developed after a recent telecommunications 
conference delineating the challenges related to broadband deployment in rural areas and efforts that are being 
made to meet these challenges.   He advised the  digital divide is greater in the tribal communities and tribal 
government was well represented in this meeting.  He discussed barriers to resolving the broadband deployment 
as:  Lack of cooperation ; limited return on investments; access to rights-of-way; lack of l eadership, planning 
and coordination; and lack of f unding.  He discussed a new evolving technology that could be used in rural 
communities to deliver broadband over power lines.  Mel Bowers inquired if there had been studies identifying 
under served -areas in Navajo County  and Mr. Heming stated that an estimated 50% of Navajo County has 
broadband connectivity but on the reservation that is not the case.  He advised that the Chapter Houses have 
satellite connectivity.  He said they need to i mprove connectivity south of I -40 and create connectivity north  of 
I-40 and he believes the Governor is going to promote this connectivity.   Supervisor Thompson inquired if all 
stake holders who are participating in this meeting prepare their own assessme nt and incorporate that into one 
report to submit to the Governor and Mr. Heming responded that is the procedure.  He advised the official 
results of the conference are not yet available.  Supervisor Deal inquired if this whole exercise is headed by the 
Governor and Mr. Heming responded that five tribes were represented in the Arizona Telecommunications and 
Information Council; and ultimately the report will go to the Governor.   Supervisor Deal stated he is glad this 
study is happening, including the rural areas that are left behind; that this will assist in bringing infrastructure to 
these rural areas to make communication, education and economic development more equitable.  He stated if 
there is any communication money available for connectivity, we should  try to stretch it as much as possible to 
keep the Native Americans from falling further behind.  He stated t his is a social injustice and the way to 
equalize that is through connectivity.   He encouraged Mr. Heming to continue emphasizing the need for 
everyone to be brought up to the same level and expressed appreciation to  Mr. Heming for attending this 
meeting and pursuing the issue.  Supervisor Thompson stated he had mentioned to Mr. Heming and the County 
Manager that there is  telecommunications funding f rom Congress  and we  need more information  on it, 
suggesting contact with Representative Kirkpa trick.  Mr. Heming stated several funding sources were 
considered and they are approaching this issue at a state-wide level.  Supervisor Brownlow thanked Mr. Heming 
for the information regarding the broad band policy that may cost the Arizona internet user an additional 50 
cents.  James Jayne s tated this is a Governor Napolitano initiative (GITA).  He expressed appreciation to Mr. 
Heming for following this issue on behalf of the Navajo Coutny citizens.

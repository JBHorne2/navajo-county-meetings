NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
SPECIAL MEETING 
JULY 19, 2007 
 
 
PRESENT:  Present in the Chambers:  Tim Zaremba and Tommy Price of Emergency Management, Darlene 
Fraley, Clerk of the Board and Lorinda Brown, Deputy Clerk of the Board.   David Tenney, Chai rman; Percy 
Deal, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow participated by telephone.  J. R. DeSpain, 
Supervisor, was not present and did not participate by telephone.  All other Supervisors participated 
telephonically through a speaker p hone in the Board Chambers that made all meeting discussion and action 
available to participants in the chambers. 
 
At 4:05 p.m. Chairman Tenney called the meeting to order  and stated it is his preference to match the county’s 
ordinance to the Apache Sitgreaves Forest Service’s that deactivates their emergency restrictions. 
 
SHERIFF’S OFFICE/EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT:   Consideration and possible approval to deactivate 
emergency fire restrictions (Section 7) of the Navajo County Open Fire Ordinance 01 -06 that were activated on 
July 5, 2007: Tim Zaremba of Emergency Management reviewed the press release from the Apache Sitgreaves 
Forest which stated that smoking and campfire  restrictions would be lifted in the entire forest as of 8:00 a.m., 
Friday, July 20, 2007.  He stated it would be difficult to keep county restrictions in place when residents could 
go into the forest and build a fire .  He stated Show Low and Pinetop law enforcement are going to lift their 
restrictions at the same time and he has notified other Navajo County Fire Chiefs.  Supervisor Brownlow 
advised he spoke with Pinetop and Lakeside fire chief s yesterday and they are comfortable with this.  
Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to deactivate emergency fire restrictions (Section 7) of the Navajo 
County Open Fire Ordinance 01 -06 to be effective 8:00 a.m., July 20, 2007; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson.  Supervisor Deal inquired about the moisture level in the southern part of the county and Supervisor 
Tenney responded there has been considerable moisture recently.  Tim Zaremba advised that the Forest Service 
indicated in their press release that the roads are muddy due to the moisture but they still warn campers to take 
caution with their camp fires.  Motion carried 4-0. 
 
Supervisor Deal stated th at following the State’s issuance of a drought emergency proclamation several years 
ago, Navajo County adopted a similar resolution and this action still stands.  He would like action taken so that 
whatever resources may come available that residents are eligible for them.  In response to a comment from 
Chairman Tenney, Tim Zaremba stated he is researching this issue and it will be on the August 7, 2007 board 
meeting.  Supervisor Deal reiterated that he wants the residents to be assured the county and state  will do all it 
can for their benefit to be eligible for any emergency assistance.  
 
At 4:13 p.m., Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Deal; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

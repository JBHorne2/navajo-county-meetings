CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Marc Silliman, a resident of White Mountain Lake, AZ addressed the Board regarding 
the White Mountain Lake s County Recreation Improvement District ’s Advisory Board. He expresse d his 
concerns regarding the bylaws that were drafted stating that the Board of Supervisors should be listed as the Board 
of Directors and that the Advisory Board should be listed as the Advisory Committee. Mr. Silliman indicated that 
he was concerned with the Advisory Committee’s actions at their latest meeting. He stated that several of the 
Advisory Committee members have resigned from the committee  and that Jerry McGraw was voted in as the 
Chairman. Mr. Silliman indicated that Mr. McGraw’s first act of business was to address the apparent conflict of 
interest to have Norma Loeffler, a member of the lake patr ol serve on the committee. He indicated that she was 
legal to be a member of the committee. The end result was that the proposed amendment was agreed to go forward, 
but that  Ms. L oeffler was not able to participate in the remainder of the meeting. Mr. Silliman  wanted a 
clarification of the conditions for th e removal of a committee member . He indicated he felt Mr. McGraw was 
incorrect in his actions of not considering Ms. Loeffler’s vote in the meeting. He asked the Board of Supervisors 
acting as their Board of Directors, to investigate these actions and the terminology written in the bylaws. Mr. 
Silliman indicated their district is not a sub-division of Navajo County, but a subdivision within Navajo County.

Munroe, Alicia Phillips and Gil Alvidrez.  Ms. Phillips addressed the Board regarding educating the youth. 
Ms. Munroe  spoke of the  challenges and rewards of t he program. Mr. Alvidrez  stated that this is a 
“homegrown program” and he spoke of the gratitude from the parents, grandparents and others in support 
of the program  for the youth , ages 10 to 14 . Mr. Alvidrez expressed appreciation to the Board of 
Supervisors for their assistance and  presented the Board  with a poster and a certificate. Chairwoman 
Whitesinger commended the team for making this program successful.

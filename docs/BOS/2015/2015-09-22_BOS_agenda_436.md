Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 36-15, establishing the membership criteria for the 
White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District Advisory Board : Ed Cadenhead addressed the 
item and requested that the bylaws be accepted and put into effect. Brandt Clark, Attorney indicated that he drafted 
the bylaws and gave  them to the W hite Mountain Lakes Advisory Committee. He stated that he believes the 
bylaws are in compliance. Mr. Clark indicated that legal counsel has no issues and he recommended approval of 
the bylaws. Mr. Clark reported that in  1993, the White Mountain Lakes Recreation District was formed by the 
Board of Supervisors, and then two months later, the Board of Supervisors created the Advisory Committee, then 
gave the Committee charge to create the bylaws. He indicated they have been in operation without bylaws since 
1993. He stated  that the bylaws  place the terms  for what the criteria is for indivi dual membership, and the 
fundamental guidelines that an entity needs to have in place, which would address in future times, how vacancies 
are filled. Supervisor Whiting inquired about the duties of the Advisory C ommittee. Mr. Clark stated that the 
Advisory Board only have an advisory role and that they do not create policy and do not ratify rules or regulations. 
Mr. Clark indicated the Advisory Board cannot adopt their own bylaws and they cannot change any bylaws. Mr. 
Clark stated their function is to have a local presence for the Board of Supervisors, and the Board of Supervisors 
are to provide the advisory board with guidance on issues, which arise before the advisory board. He stated that 
everything that comes before the D istrict has to be approved by  the Board of Supervisors, because the Board of 
Supervisors is the governing body. Mr. Clark stated t he Advisory Board deals with the citizens when an issue 
arrives, and then acts as an appellate board to make a decision, then if the citizens don’t like their decision, then 
it falls back to the Board of Supervisor s to act in their capacity  as the governing body  to make a decision . Mr. 
Clark stated  the Advisory Board is there to uphold the current rules and regulations, which  the B oard of 
Supervisors have ratified. Supervisor Whiting made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 36-15, establishing 
the membership criteria for the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District Advisory Board; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Williams. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Williams made 
a motion to return to regular session ; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion.

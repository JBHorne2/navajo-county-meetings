NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, July 28, 2015 
PRESENT: Robert K. Black, Jr., Supervisor; Steve Williams, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jason 
Whiting, Vice Chairman; and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman were present.  
9:08 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Williams.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Brad Carlyon, addressed the Board regarding the loss of employees due to job stress 
and no money  to fund vacant positions. Mr. Carlyon reported on the hardships county departments are 
encountering, by having to do more with less  money. He stated that employees are unable to give more . Mr. 
Carlyon indicated that we need to support other co unties when they go before the state and federal governments 
in support of counties. He asked the Board to support Pinal  County and Mohave County on their federal impact 
issues. He stressed the  need for all of us  to work with AACo and CSA, in  partnering with the 15 Counties for 
support from our legislators . Mr. Carlyon asked the Board to invite our legislators here, write to them , and to 
contact Senate President, Andy Biggs and Speaker of the House, David M. Gowan Sr. to ask for a joint 
appropriations hearing about what is happening to our counties and employee s. Mr. Carlyon asked the Board to 
show our employees that we do care personally and to thank them individually. He asked all the elected officials 
to thank an employee today, because they are giving their all. Mr. Terry Hill, 2677 AZ Highway 77 in Shumway, 
addressed the Board regarding the priority goals for 2015 for the Veterans of Foreign Wars. Mr. Hill indicted he 
brought these goals back from the Veterans of Foreign War’s National Convention in Pittsburg, and he asked the 
Board to review these goals, to see where we stand on the national, state, county and local levels. Mr. Hill stated 
he is on the Department of Arizona’s Veterans of Foreign Wa rs legislative committee and indicated he attends 
the legislative hearings the first three months of the year.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE:  Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager: 
Supervisor Black indicated he attended events which are promoting improved health in his district. He reported

Navajo Nation regarding funding for Navajo Nation roads, which include  the Indian Scho ol Bus Routes. 
Supervisor Thompson reported that he and Homero Vela will attend  the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Los 
Angeles Division’s Change of Command Ceremony, in which Colonel K imberly M. Colloton will relinquish 
command to Colonel Kirk E. Gibbs , at Fort MacArthur in San Pedro, California. He indicated that the Army 
Corps is essential in supporting the Winslow Levee project.  Supervisor Whiting spoke about the Board having 
a united voice at the state legislature, and indicated that he would like to work with staff and other elected officials 
on preparing meaningful talking points . Supervisor Whiting  stated that working with and growing existing 
businesses, along with bringing new industries into our area, will generate additional jobs and revenues to Navajo 
County. He thanked all the Navajo County staff who assisted with the 24th of July Parade and those who attended

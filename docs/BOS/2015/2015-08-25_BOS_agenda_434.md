NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, August 25, 2015 
 
PRESENT: Robert K. Black Jr., Supervisor; Steve Williams, Supervisor; Jason E. Whiting, Vice-Chairman; and 
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman were present. Jesse Thompson, Supervisor was absent. 
9:05 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Whiting.  
      CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Joyce Hill, representing the VFW Ladies Auxiliary, indicated that at their convention 
in July, a vote was passed to allow male members into the VFW Auxiliary, who will meet the same requirements 
as the ladies. Mrs. Hill stated that there are many female combat veterans, whose spouses and sons were not 
eligible to be members of the ladies auxiliary . She reported t his change went into effect on August 21 th. She 
indicated the name will be changed to the VFW Auxiliary.

      COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager : 
Supervisor Black reported that on August 9, Jonathan and Ophelia Nez had a baby boy who weighed 5 pounds, 
4 ounces. Supervisor Black thanked the county staff and the Tomhave’s for preparing him to meet with Governor 
Doug Ducey, Senator John McCain and the Navajo Nation Vice -President Johnathan Nez. He indicated the 
meeting was regarding a letter to legislators, for obtaining funding sources for the Indian school bus routes. He 
expressed his appreciation to Vice-President Nez for extending the invitation for him to meet with Gove rnor 
Ducey. Supervisor Black  reported he attended  a meeting with Supervisor Thompson  at the Spider Mound 
community, on the Hopi Reservation.  He indicated that he will be  stepping down from his role as the Shonto 
Chapter Manager. Supervisor Williams reported he attended the Hangar Dance which was a White M ountain 
salute to veterans. Supervisor Williams  indicated that he, Paul Watson and Supervisor Whiting met with 
Senator McCain’s Chief of Staff regarding county and health issues. Supervisor Williams thanked Paige 
Peterson, Lynda Young, James Menlove, Paul Watson, James Jayne, Brad Carlyon, Chairwoman Whitesinger 
and Supervisor Whiting for the CSA Budget presentation. Supervisor Williams spoke of attending the City of 
Show Low’s Council meeting and the Heber Chamber meeting, where he and Mary Springer, will present “Ready 
Navajo County”. Supervisor Williams  indicated he and his wife celebrated their 16th wedding a nniversary. 
Supervisor Whiting thanked the Navajo County team for the CSA Budget Presentation. He gave updates on the 
Apache Railway, its supporters, and the court hearing to help fund the railway. Supervisor Whiting indicated he 
and Chairwoman Whitesinger  met with Governor Doug Ducey, Senator Carlyle Begay and Senator  John 
McCain during their tour in Round Valley. He gave an update on the NRWG meeting and those involved with 
the 4 -FRI project . He reported on a meeting with Senator McCain’s staff on forest issues and the economic 
importance of the Apache Railway for this area. He stated he spoke at a Town Hall in Snowflake with Senator 
Sylvia Allen. James Jayne, County Manager reported that Seth, his oldest son became engaged to Susan Padilla. 
Chairwoman Whitesinger spoke about the Arrowhead Café’s grand opening, on the Apache Reservation at Fort 
Apache. She indicated it was located in a 200 year old historic building. She stated that John Hopkins partnered 
with the Apache Tribe for the grand opening. Chairwoman Whitesinger reported that the café is in a beautiful 
setting, which provides delicious foods and is helping tribal youth to earn their own way . Chairwoman 
Whitesinger spoke of meeting with Senator Carlyle Begay. Senator John McCain, and Governor Doug Ducey 
regarding the challenges and limitations of the tribe, the Hopi Nation and the County. She indicated she attended 
the First Things First  grantee coordination meeting.  Chairwoman Whitesinger spoke of the  Sunrise Board’s 
upcoming zip-line improvement to the Ski Resort. 
      ADJOURN: At 11:02 a.m. Supervisor Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor

Steve Williams, Supervisor were present. Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman was absent.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Terry Hill addressed the Board of Supervisors . Mr. Hill expresse d congratulations 
to Vice -Chairman Nez for being elected  as Vice-President of the Navajo Nation. Mr. Hill stated the first 
Veteran’s Court in Navajo County will be held on the 4 th Wednesday of May, with Judge Nielson holding the 
first Veteran’ s C ourt hearing. Mr. Hill thanked  Brad Carlyon, County Attorney and Ernie Garcia, Jail 
Commander for their efforts in establishing the Veteran’s Court. Skylar Carenga with SEC Power Corporatio n 
addressed the Board of Supervisors regarding a hybrid power plant project. Mr. Carenga stated he would like 
clarification of ARS 48-1502; a petition for organization of a power district. Vice-Chairman Nez indicated the 
staff will provide the information by the next Board meeting. Billy Weldon, the Arizona  State Commander of 
the Military Order of the Purple Heart, spoke to the Board of Supervisors about Navajo County  passing the 
proclamation to become a Purple Heart County. He indicated this recognizes our nation’s heroes who have lost 
their lives or have been wounded in the war . He reported that Navajo County will make the 13 th county to pass 
the proclamation. Mr. Weldon stated that when  all the Arizona counties have approved this proclamation , the 
Governor will declare Arizona a Purple Heart State.

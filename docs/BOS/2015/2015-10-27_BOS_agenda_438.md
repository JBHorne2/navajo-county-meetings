      RECONVENE: for possible action regarding settlement proposal in Mountain Oaks Senior L.P. v. 
Navajo County, TX -2013-000436, and Fairway Manor L.P., TX -2013-000435: Jason Moore, Deputy 
County Attorney, addressed the Board of Supervisors and recommended that the Board settle in two 
county property tax appeal cases. Mr. Moore recommended that the Board settle the Fairway Manor L.P., 
TX-2013-000435 by reducing the value of the subjects’ property of $3,837,392 for tax years 2009, 2010, 
2011 and 2012 to $1,000,000 for each of those tax years. Mr. Moore also recommended that the Board 
settle the Mountain Oaks Senior L.P., v. Navajo County, TX-2013-000436 by reducing the property value 
from $1.4 million for tax years 2009, 2010, 2011 and 2012 to $800,000. Mr. Moore made a 
recommendation to approve the settlement proposal pursuant to the stipulated judgement as so presented. 
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the settlement proposal for TX-2013-000436 and TX-
2013-000435, pursuant to the stipulated judgements as so presented by the attorney; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Williams. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

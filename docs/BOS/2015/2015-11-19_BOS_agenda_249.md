SHOW LOW CITY COUNCIL 
PINETOP-LAKESIDE TOWN COUNCIL 
SNOWFLAKE TOWN COUNCIL 
TAYLOR TOWN COUNCIL 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
 
Joint Study Session – Thursday, November 19, 2015 
 
PURSUANT to A.R.S. Section 38 -431.02, notice is hereby given to the Show  Low City 
Council, Pinetop -Lakeside Town Council , Snowflake Town Council, Taylor Town 
Council, Navajo County Board of Supervisors, and to the general public, that a JOINT 
STUDY SESSION of the four Councils and the County Supervisors , which will be open 
to the public, will be held on Thursday, November 19, 2015, at 6:00 p.m. at Show Low 
City Hall, Deuce of Clubs Room, 180 North 9th Street, Show Low , Navajo County, 
Arizona.   The agenda for this meeting is as follows. 
 
1. Call to Order. 
 
2. Roll Call. 
 
3. Invocation. 
 
4. Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
5. Reception with Sunrise Park Resort Enterprise Board of Directors . ( Supervisor 
Dawnafe Whitesinger) 
 
6. Update on Regional Dispatch Concept. 
 
7. Update on Regional Geocaching Event. (Mayor Daryl Seymore) 
 
8. Report by Timber Mesa Fire and Medical District. (Chief Bryan Savage) 
 
9. Update on Regional Transit System. (Lisa Robertson) 
 
10. Adjournment.

Ann Kurasaki, City Clerk

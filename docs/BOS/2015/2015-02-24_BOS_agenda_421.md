a. Consideration and possible approval of a Proclamation declaring February 24, 2015 “Day of the 
Cougars”: Supervisor Williams recognized the Show Low Ju nior High School wrestling team for 
winning the State Championship. Coach Tim Williams spoke about the team and their accomplishments, 
including one of their matches receiving a perfect score of 108-0. Every wrestler pinned their opponent 
during one of the tournaments. He spoke of their season  and thanked the support of the parent’s, the 
booster club and the community. Supervisor Williams  spoke about the coaches and  how they have 
taught the team to be champions both on and off the mat. Supervisor Williams read the proclamation to 
the team . Supervisor Williams made a motion  to approve the P roclamation declaring February 24, 
2015 “Day of the Cougars”;  motion seconded by Supervisor Nez . Vote unanimous approving the 
motion. Supervisor Williams presented each team member with a medal.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager: 
Supervisor Williams reported on attending the CSA Board meeting, and the NRWG meeting, which will 
benefit him with addressing matters within his district. Mr. Jayne commented that Supervisor Williams has 
also met with the Planning & Zoning Commissioners within his District. Supervisor Whiting reported on the 
Mexican Wolf issues and the meetings he attended regarding the subject.  He reported on attending the REAL 
AZ meeting and discussing economic development in the region. One important topic is the Apache Railway 
and how it continues to receive emphasis.  Supervisor Whiting discussed a meeting with LPC and Governor 
Ducey’s office and felt encouraged by the open dialogue. He spoke of attending the NRWG meeting which he 
is the Chairman of the committee. The committee is made up of forest officials, Navajo County Supervisors and 
members of the private sector, who are involved in addressing forest matters.  Supervisor Nez thanked staff, 
Melissa, Debra, Sheila, Jimmy, Homero and Bill for all they do in supporting the Board members and 
commented that he appreciates the hard work of the team. He discussed the amount of snow received in areas of 
the reservation.   Supervisor Nez appreciates the blessing of moisture in the region and thanked the staff who 
assists with maintaining the roads. He commented on the election date, scheduled for April 21, 2015 for the 
Navajo Nation presidential election which he has accepted the vice president nomination for Russell Begay. The 
Oath of Office will be on May 12, 2015. Supervisor Nez commented that Supervisor Thompson is in 
Washington DC attending the NACo conference, representing Navajo County. Chairwoman Whitesinger 
spoke of attending the NRWG meeting, and thanked former Supervisor Tenney for being a great supporter to 
the group.  The group is made up of a lot of organizational players involving the forest service, the timber

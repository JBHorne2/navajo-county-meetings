NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, June 2, 2015 
 *SPECIAL MEETING* 
 
PRESENT: Steve Williams,  Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jason E. Whiting, Vice -Chairman; 
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman were present telephonically. Robert K. Black, Jr., Supervisor was absent. 
Call To Order: At 9:01 a.m. Chairwoman Whitesinger called the meeting to order.  
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 17-15, supporting 
the continued operation of the Apache Railway Company : Supervisor Whiting indicated the Apache Railway 
Company based in Snowflake, operates a short line railroad. He reported that the continued operation of the 
Railroad is essential to the economy of Navajo County, employment and prospects for economic development. 
Supervisor Whiting indicated the Snowflake Community Foundation, an Arizona non-profit and sole owner of 
the Apache Railway Company will be going to court today regarding their bankruptcy, in which t he Creditor is 
trying to get the bankruptcy  case dismissed. Supervisor Whiting indicated the public interest would be better 
served, by allowing the Foundation and the Railroad time and control to repay the Lender. Supervisor Thompson 
made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 17-15, supporting the continued operation of the Apache Railway 
Company; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
      ADJOURN: At 9:04 a.m. Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Williams. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
APPROVED:       DATED: June 9, 2015

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Thursday, July 16, 2015 
   *SPECIAL MEETING*  
 
PRESENT: Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman; Jason E. Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Steve Williams, Supervisor 
present telephonically. Jesse Thompson, Supervisor and Robert K. Black Jr., Supervisor were absent. 
9:04 a.m.: Call to Order: Call to order by Chairwoman Whitesinger.  
      TREASURER: Consideration and possible approval of the sixteenth amendment to revolving credit agreement: 
Manny Hernandez discussed the item. Supervisor Williams made a motion to approve the sixteenth amendment 
to revolving credit agreement; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:10 a.m. Supervisor Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Whiting. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
APPROVED:       DATED: July 28, 2015 
 
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

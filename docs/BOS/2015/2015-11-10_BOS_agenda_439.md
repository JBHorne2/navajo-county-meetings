BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Presentation acknowledging the service and commitment of Navajo County employees that  are Veterans 
of the United States Armed Forces : Melissa Buckley showed a presentation of our employees  and their 
family members that are veterans. Chairwoman Whitesinger, Supervisor Thompson and Supervisor 
Williams thanked Mrs. Buckley for the touching video and expressed their appreciation of all our veterans 
and their families. Supervisor Williams spoke about some of the lessons that he lear ned while serving 
his country; the f irst lesson is to make your bed; the importanc e of paying attention  to detail; small 
adjustments; and that we accomplish much more when we are working together for a common goal.  
b. Presentation to Clayton Harvey for his work on “The People’s Farm”: Chairwoman Whitesinger showed 
a presentation acknowledging the work of Clayton Harvey on the “The People’s Farm”. Clayton Harvey 
addressed the Board and indicated that he was proud to accept the award on behalf of the White Mountain 
Apache Community. He acknowledged his ancestors that paved the way for the agricultural industry.  
c. Presentation to Shasta Dazen the 53rd Miss Indian Arizona: Chairwoman Whitesinger recognized Shasta 
Dazen the 53rd Miss Indian Arizona who represented the 23 tribes across Arizona. Ms. Dazen addressed 
the Board regarding her life path. Ms. Dazen reported she received a college basketball scholarship and 
has continued to further her education. Ms. Dazen stated she believes her mission is for the state of Arizona 
and for all Indian Tribes. She reported that she is a product of her native culture and tradition. She indicated 
she currently works with the John Hopkins University and continues to stay on the reservation. Supervisor

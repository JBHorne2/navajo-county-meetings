NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, December 29, 2015 
*SPECIAL MEETING*  
 
PRESENT: Steve Williams, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jason E. Whiting, Vice-Chairman were 
present telephonically; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman and Robert E. Black Jr., Supervisor were absent.  
9:28 a.m.  Call To Order: Vice-Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order. 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS  ACTING AS THE BOARD OF EQUALIZATION : Consideration and 
possible approval of the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendation on hearing conducted on 
December 28, 2015 for Petition for review of Taxpayer Notice of Claim for APN 309 -61-055: Supervisor 
Williams made a motion  to enter i nto the Board of Equalization  Session; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board presented the item. Mrs. 
Buckley read the hearing officer’s decision. She stated the Hearing Officer fou nd that the property in question 
has met the test as described in ARS 42-5070. Mrs. Buckley indicated the Hearing Officer stated the decision by 
the Assessor is found to be consistent and accurate according to his interpretation of the statute and in equity, and 
that it was the opinion of the Hearing Officer that the assessor’s decision should be affirmed by the Board of 
Equalization and a “No Change” be entered in this matter . Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to approve 
the Board of Equalization Hearing O fficer’s recommendation on hearing conducted on December 28, 2015 for 
Petition for review of Taxpayer Notice of Claim for APN 309-61-055; motion seconded by Supervisor Williams. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Williams made a motion to return to regular session; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.    
      ADJOURN: At 9:33 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn, motion seconded by Supervisor 
Williams. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
APPROVED:       DATED: January 12, 2016

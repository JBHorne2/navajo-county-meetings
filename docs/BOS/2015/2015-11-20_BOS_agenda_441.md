NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Friday, November 20, 2015 
 *SPECIAL MEETING* 
 
PRESENT: Steve Williams, Supervisor; Robert K. Black Jr., Supervisor; Jason E, Whiting, Vice-Chairman, and 
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman were present telephonically. Jesse Thompson, Supervisor was absent.   
9:00 a.m. Call To Order: Chairwoman Whitesinger called the meeting to order.  
CONSENT AGENDA:   
a. Department of Corrections Emergency Contract #16-0009-16 Amendment #1 for housing of DOC 
inmates: Supervisor Williams made a  motion to approve  the consent agenda as presented , motion 
seconded by Supervisor Whiting. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS ACTING AS THE BOARD OF EQUALIZATION: Consideration and 
possible approval of the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendation on hearings conducted on 
November 18, 2015 for Petition for Review of Notice of Change: Supervisor Williams made a motion to enter 
in to the Board of Equalization Session, motion seconded by Supervisor Black. Vote unanimous approving the 
motion. Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board discussed the item. Supervisor Williams made a motion to approve 
the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendation on hearings conducted on November 18, 2015 for 
Petition for Review of Notice of Change, motion seconded Supervisor Whiting. Vote unanimous approving the 
motion. Supervisor Williams made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by Supervisor 
Whiting. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
      ADJOURN: At 9:06 a.m.  Supervisor Williams made a motion  to adjourn, motion seconded by Supervisor 
Black. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
APPROVED:       DATED: November 24, 2015 
 
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors 
 
 
 
ATTEST: 
 
Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk

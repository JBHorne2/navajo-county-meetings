      CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Mariclare Hannah from Taylor, AZ. appeared as a private citizen, even though she 
has previously gone before the Board as a Legal Defender.  Ms. Hannah addressed the Board regarding  the Jail 
policy change for visitation. She indicated this visitation policy change has become a hardship to the inmates, 
their families and friends. Ms. Hannah indicated that visits are through a visitation monitor which costs about a 
$1.00 a minute for video visitation.  She indicated that a one hour visit would c ost approximately $60.00. She 
stated the only free way for the inmates and families to communicate is by letter. She stated that many inmat es 
are indigents and that the new policy impacts the poorest population in our county. Ms. Hannah asked the Board 
to study this issue and consider the effect this policy change has on the inmates, their families and friends. She 
stated the moral and ethica l considerations in this case are  more important than any financial gain. She stated 
that there needs to be other cos t effective ways to deal with this. Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Ms. 
Hannah for the information and bringing this issue to the Board’s attention.

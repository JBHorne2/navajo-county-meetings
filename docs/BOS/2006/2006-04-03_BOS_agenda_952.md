NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
MONDAY, APRIL 3, 2006 
 
Chairman Jesse Thompson called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. 
 
PRESENT:  Jesse Thompson, Chairman; J.R. DeSp ain, Member; David Tenney, Member; Jerry 
Brownlow, Member; Mel Bowers, County Attorney; James G. Jayne County Manager, and Darlene 
Fraley, Deputy Clerk of the Board.  Percy Deal, Vice-Chairman, entered the meeting in progress. 
 
Chairman Thompson led the Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC:   Hugo Lusten of Cedar Hills discussed his concerns that the County put 
fences on his private property.  He distributed a document discussing the Dissolution of district (37-
1038) and an editorial entitled “There is No Federal Open Range.”   Chairman Thompson advised 
Mr. Lusten staff would investigate this issue.  Supervisor DeSpain expressed appreciation to Mr. 
Lusten for his appearance. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Voucher list for payment; b) Minutes –January 11, 2005, May 16 & 17, 
2005, May 20, 2005, May 31, 2005, February 22, 2006, March 13, 2006, March 20, 2006; c) Back 
Tax Land Deeds; d) Tax roll corrections; e) Award  of bid to Land America Transnation for title 
searches for 2006 Treasurers’ deeds; f) Interim Permit Liquor License for Steve Carlson of The 
Cabin located at 2763 Highway 260, Overgaard AZ; g) Name existing roads in the Winslow area for 
Bruchman Estates “Rosa Way”, “Calla Way”, “Tulsa Lane”, “Solana Lane”, and “Pasa Way” in 
Section 32, Township 19 North – Range 16 East; h) Sympathy letter for James G. Jayne and Albert 
and Courty Warren; i) Justice Court Reports:  Pinetop #6 January; j) Juvenile Justice System 
Report—February; k) Constable Reports— Wi nslow #2 January/February; Snowflake #3 
January/February; Show Low #5 February:  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to authorize the 
Chairman to sign the items in the Consent Agenda; motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

reviewed her written personnel report with the Board.  Supervisor Brownlow stated he had worked 
with resigning employee Tim Lacey from the Sher iff’s department, and he was an excellent 
employee.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve the Personnel Actions as presented 
and authorize the Chairman to sign the acti ons; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD BUSINESS:  Supervisors reported on District activities, pending legislation and drought 
conditions.  Supervisor Tenney expressed his appreci ation to Mary Springer for her assistance in 
obtaining a mixer for the Rim Country Senior Center.  Supervisor Deal reminded everyone that the 
Navajo Nation is back on daylight savings time and the Hopi reservation remained on standard time. 
 
James Jayne expressed his appreciation for the Board’s condolences and advised they had received 
word that on Friday, Bencita Nelson’s father died.  Mr. Jayne reported that Congressman Renzi’s 
office confirmed that $4.4 million total for Lone Pine Bridge has been appropriated, with the total 
project to be $5.5 million.  He also reported th e times and places where Congressman Renzi will 
speak in Navajo County next week.   
 
Mr. Bowers reported that Drug Court for particip ants who are on intensive probation has been in 
operation for four weeks and so far all participants have been successful.

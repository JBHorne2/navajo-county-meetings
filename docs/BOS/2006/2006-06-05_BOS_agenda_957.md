County Animal Control Ordinance” repealing and superseding Ordinance Number 01-03 as revised May 
19, 1997 relating to the same subject matter:  Dr. Kartchner introduced Ron Jones who reviewed the new 
animal control ordinance.  Supervisor DeSpain thanked Mr. Jones for coming forward with this ordinance, 
advising there have been problems with wolf hybrids in the Cedar Hills area and the previous ordinance did 
not address these animals.  In response to Supervis or Deal’s question regarding the change, Mr. Jones 
advised that they added the section to deal with wolf hybrids including vaccinations and further defined 
cruelty and neglect, and vicious animals.  Chairman Thompson advised that this item was published in the 
Holbrook Tribune as a public hearing and invited public comment.  There was none.  Supervisor DeSpain

NAVAJO COUNTY COOPERATIVE EXTENSION:  Consideration and possible approval of continued 
participation in the Natural Resources Working Group Memorandum of Understanding :  Steve Campbell 
advised that previous Supervisor Lewis Tenney had participated in the organization of this group; discussed the 
focus of the group and the expansion of the group.  He advised a new MOU is needed because those items 
originally outlined have been accomplished.  He reported Supervisors Brownlow and Tenney regularly 
represent the county at this group.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve continued participation 
in the Natural Resources Working Group Memorandum of Understanding; Supervisor Tenney seconded the 
motion. Supervisor Tenney advised this has been an excellent project for a long time and provides a seat for 
Navajo County in managing the forest.  He thanked Steve Campbell and Darlene Fraley for their participation 
in and contributions to this group. In response to a question from Supervisor Deal, Steve Campbell stated that 
the pinon component of forest is almost gone and there is a committee working on a seven segment forest health 
strategy for Arizona that includes pinon/juniper in the hi ghlands.  He advised there is a separate strategy for 
each area and stated that Al Christiansen with the BIA reviews all tribal plans including prevention strategies.  
He discussed his participation in this process and advised of factors resulting from different management 
strategies. Supervisor Brownlow expressed appreciation to Steve Campbell for all he does.  Vote unanimous 
approving the motion. 
 
SHERIFF’S OFFICE:  Consideration and possible approval to authorize the Sheriff’s Office to submit a grant 
proposal to Department of Justice COPS Office for a two year Methamphetamine Program grant for $200,000 
to be used in conjunction with the Navajo County Task Force for two positions and other related expenditures: 
Deputy Commander Casey Clark advised they were notified a year and a half ago that Byrne funds would 
sunset so he, Emery LeBarge and others met with  Congressman Renzi for support and advice regarding 
different grant funds.  He advised this grant is a re sult of that process and the funds would be used for 
equipment, overtime, a secretary, and two surveillance officers for probation all directly supporting the 
methamphetamine program.  Chairman Thompson stated this presentation at Indian Wells was effective and 
expressed appreciation for seeking additional funds.  Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to authorize the 
Sheriff’s Office to submit a grant proposal to Department of Justice COPS Office for a two year 
Methamphetamine Program grant for $200,000 to be used in conjunction with the Navajo County Task Force 
for two positions and other related expenditures; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  In response to a 
question from Supervisor Deal, Deputy Commander Clark discussed the coverage area for this grant as being 
the entire county.  Supervisor Deal requested that this grant cover communities in the Navajo Nation that are 
experiencing tremendous impact of the meth problem.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

1:35 p.m. NAVAJO COUNTY COOPERATIVE EXTENSION:  Consideration and possible approval of 
participation in the development of Community Wildfire Protection Plan for communities within District II, III 
and IV:   Steve Campbell advised that the Bureau of Land Management is providing $25,000 for the 
development of an additional Community Wildfire Protection Plan (CWPP) to address fire issues in the districts 
II, III and IV of the county that will especially address tamarisk and pinon/juniper.  He advised there is a need 
for three additional CWPPs for the  Hopi/Navajo communities, that the Bureau of Land Management will not 
participate in the development of northern Arizona CWPPs and the BIA is mandated to develop a CWPP in a 
joint participation process.  He stated that in the CWPP being proposed today, he anticipates that Holbrook, 
Snowflake, Winslow, Taylor and cthe ounty will each commit $5,000.  Supervisor Deal requested that Mr. 
Campbell send contact information for the reservation area.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve 
participation in the development of Community Wildfire Protection Plan for communities within District II, III 
and IV and direct administration to include the $5,000.00 development cost in the FY2007 budget; Supervisor 
Brownlow seconded the motion; vote unanimous approving the motion.  Supervisor Deal requested that funding 
be reserved for a future CWPPs for northern Arizona. 
 
James Jayne advised it is necessary to schedule a special meeting to address implementing section 7 of the fire 
ordinance on June 8th or June 13th for zones 2 and 3.  He stated Pinetop and Show Low have implemented these 
restrictions and there is an Apache/Sitgreaves forest meeting scheduled at 9:00 tomorrow to implement stage 3 
in the forest.  He further reported neighboring forests have gone to stage 3 restrictions.

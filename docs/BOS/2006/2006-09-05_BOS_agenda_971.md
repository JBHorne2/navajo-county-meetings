GIS/ASSESSOR:  Presentation of transition process from Auto-cad to Geographic Information Systems to more 
efficiently process land splits:  Cammy Darris advised that currently the Assessor’s office utilizes a CAD program 
but in order to facilitate getting the information in a usable format to GIS, they have explored utilizing a Geographic 
Information System.  She reported this will not ease the Assessor’s workload and initially will actually increase the 
learning curve but it is their intent that this will help with public addressing for schools, emergency services, etc.   
Public Works’ employees Judy Needham and Melanie D’Angelo reported that Public Works has been working with 
the Assessor’s office taking classes to learn how to create and edit parcels with ArcGis and they discussed the GIS 
functions that would enable departments to share data ra ther than re-creating it.  Ryan Taylor discussed the 
conversion process advising they would do a batch conversion, processing future developer’s maps the same way.  
He reviewed the conversion steps for each layered map.  He discussed the need for parcel numbers to be assigned in 
order to facilitate the permit process.  He reported it w ill take four days of stra ight processing, not including 
preparation work, to do the conversion.  Anna Ericksen discussed the process as it will impact the Assessor’s office 
displaying schematics of the process.   
 
ELECTIONS:  Acknowledgment of emergency change of polling place in Taylor #2 Precinct from Taylor

motion to approve the change in the polling place as lis ted; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote

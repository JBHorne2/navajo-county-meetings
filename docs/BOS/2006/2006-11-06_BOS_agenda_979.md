SHERIFF’S OFFICE 
a. Consideration and possible authorization for Sheriff’s Office to enter into Training Payback Agreement with 
new hire employees filling certified positions: Sheriff Gary Butler advised that his department has, in the last 
two months, lost six people.  He sa id the department sends the employees to the academy for training at an 
approximate cost of $20,000, and then they go to another agency that pays higher so this department has become 
a training ground for law enforcement in Navajo County.  He reported that other counties have three year 
agreements whereby if money is spent to train a new recruit and that employee voluntary terminates there is a 
pay back provision.  He reviewed the terms of the agreement, advising a similar agreement has been 
implemented in other counties and has held up in court.  He advised Lance Payette had made changes to 
improve the agreement.  Supervisor Deal inquired if ther e is a salary increase at the end of training.  Sheriff 
Butler stated that once the recruits have completed the full training, they will be promoted from Cadet to Deputy 
1 and then after a year to Deputy 2.  Supervisor Deal inquired if a new recruit refused to sign this agreement, 
they are not hired and Sheriff Butler stated that is the case.  Supervisor Tenney stated he felt this is a step in the 
right direction comparing it to military service acad emy training.  Chairman Thompson inquired if the 
certification includes learning more about other federal agencies.  Sheriff Butler stated they do study other 
agencies but it is mainly peace officer training including classes on federal law and tribal law with emphasis on 
enforcing state statutes.  Supervisor Deal inquired if additional training is required in the future and Sheriff 
Butler stated that once an officer is certified and does nothing that jeopardizes that certification, he remains 
certified.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to authorize the Sheriff’s Office to enter into a Training Payback 
Agreement with new hire employees filling certified positions; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
b. Consideration and possible authorization for Sheriff’s Office to enter into Intergovernmental Agreement for law 
enforcement services with Coconino County Sheriff’s Office from 12-01-06 through 12-31-09: Sheriff Gary 
Butler advised that Coconino County has the same limitations that Navajo County has as related to manpower 
and there are remote areas (Forest Lake area) that Navajo County can access easier than Coconino can.  He 
reviewed the terms of the contract.  Supervisor Tenney stated that Supervisor Matt Ryan of Coconino County 
had expressed appreciation for the cooperation that exists between Coconino County and Navajo County.  
Supervisor Tenney made a motion to authorize the Sheriff’s Office to  enter into an Intergovernmental 
Agreement for law enforcement services with the Coconino County Sheriff’s Office from 12-01-06 through 12-
31-09; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the motion.

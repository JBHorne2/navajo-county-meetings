EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT:  Larry Dunagan 
a) Consideration and possible approval by Resolution # ___ - 06 acceptance of the Navajo County Multi-
Hazard Mitigation Plan:  Larry Dunagan displayed the 300 page document that makes up the multi-
hazard mitigation plan and advised that the adoption of this document maintains the County’s funding 
level for multi-hazard emergencies.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve by Resolution 
# 09 - 06 acceptance of the Navajo County Multi-Hazard Mitigation Plan; Supervisor DeSpain 
seconded the motion.  Supervisors asked for and received clarification that there is no current plan in 
place.  Mr. Dunagan stated that September of last year was the deadline for plan submission and the 
State granted an extension and funded the devel opment of the plan.  He reported it takes FEMA 
approximately three to six weeks to review, approve and send the approved document back to the

b)  Consideration and possible approval of Resolution # ___ -06  Declaring a Snow Emergency :  Mr. 
Bowers advised the Board they must declare an emergency to review this item.  Mr. Parsons advised 
the Board that there was significant snowfall th roughout the County and crews have been working 
about 18 hours per day.  The main arteries are open and residential roads must be cleared.  Some of the 
roads are not on the County road system and they are requesting an emergency session to approve a 
resolution to declare a snow emergency.  Supervisor DeSpain made a motion  to enter into an 
emergency session of the Board based on the facts presented regarding the recent snowfall; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
Mr. Dunagan stated that many essential employees could not get to Navapache Hospital so the Sheriff’s 
posse in conjunction with deputies assisted in getting staff to the hospital.  He said emergency vehicles 
were not able to get out in some areas and the s now depth varied from 24” to 48”.  Larry Dunagan 
presented the resolution declaring the snow emerge ncy and a letter to Governor Napolitano requesting 
assistance from the State that may include hiring private contractors.  He said this action is necessary for 
residents and for emergency vehicles.  Supervisor De Spain inquired if the Governor has to declare an 
emergency and Mr. Dunagan stated the letter is from the BOS requesting the State to provide assistance 
and the Governor has the option of approving or disapproving.  He advised he contacted State Emergency 
Management and if approved they will take it to the Governor.  He reported Apache County anticipates 
this action but he has not heard from the other Coun ties.  Mr. Parsons stated the declaration of this 
emergency gives the crews the authority to respond and the County would get reimbursement for this 
action if the Governor approves it.  Supervisor Deal expressed appreciation to the staff for their dedication 
to County residents.  Supervisor Deal requested that an addition be made to the letter to the Governor in 
the second paragraph, 4
th line down, insert the words “and Bureau of Indian Affairs.”  Supervisor Tenney 
made a motion  to adopt Resolution # 10 -06  declaring a Snow Emergency; Supervisor Brownlow 
seconded the motion.  Supervisor Brownlow thanked the Public Works and Emergency Services for their 
immediate response to this snow emergency and requested that County residents be patient.  Supervisor 
Tenney stated he was at the Show Low office to monitor what was happening in the County and thanked 
everyone for their hard work.  Supervisor Deal displayed photos of the Black Mesa area taken yesterday.  
Vote unanimous approving the motion.

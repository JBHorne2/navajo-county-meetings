Director of the county the authority to determine certain terms of a notice inviting proposals for purchase

bonds is subject to the competitive bid process.  He discussed the process that has taken place to date.  He 
advised that effectively a bond is a loan subject to specific conditions with repayment through assessment on 
the properties.  He advised there is a reserve fund and an avenue for penalties for non-payment.   He advised 
this resolution incorporates all action to date and provides for issuance of the bonds through a competitive 
process with the resolution delegating ministerial duties including setting final terms on bonds delegated to 
the financial director of county. He advised they will mail the offer document electronically to prospective 
investors and on August 14 bidders will access the website and lodge secret bids; when bids are closed they 
will tabulate bids to obtain the best interest rate.  He advised there are provisions that the finance director 
may reject all bids if the county is not happy with  the bid on the bonds.   He  reviewed the covenants 
included in the document, discussing the advantage to the property owners for the tax-exempt basis, stating 
that investors are more aggressive about foreclosures in this market.  He advised the board that the Official 
Statement is the disclosure document and it is the most important document being approved today; he 
reviewed the document and stated it will be emailed to  investors when approved.   He discussed factors 
impacting interest; that the bonds will be sold in $25,000 denominations; that brokers are required to keep 
an electronic survey of bonds available to sell; and they must know the accredited investor.  He reported that 
it is difficult to get an independent appraisal for county improvement districts so investors go very 
conservative using assessor information which is less than market value and the interest rate will be higher 
to compensate for that risk.  Mr. Cafiso stated he is still working with staff to assure this document is 
accurate. He advised the proposed resolution is all encompassing; staff may report when all bonds sold; 
there will be no more official actions and they hope to sell by August 14 and close quickly after September 
1.  He expressed appreciation for staff efforts and contributions and complimented Carol Fraley and Dusty

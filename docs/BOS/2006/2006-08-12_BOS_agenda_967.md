NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
SATURDAY, AUGUST 12, 2006 
 
Present: At Navajo County Complex: Supervisor J.R. DeSpain and Clerk Darlene Fraley.  Present by cell 
telephone with speaker phone at the county complex:  Chairman Jesse Thompson, Supervisor Percy 
Deal, County Manager James G. Jayne, County Attorney Mel Bowers, and Public Works Director 
Dusty Parsons.  Supervisors Brownlow and Tenney were not present.  
 
CALL TO ORDER: Chairman Thompson called the meeting to order at 4:40 p.m. advising that this meeting is 
called as a result of rainfall creating an emergency situat ion in the Whippoorwill area.  He advised a request for 
assistance has been received from Johnny Johnson, Inci dent Commander for Navajo  Nation Department of 
Emergency Management, as authorized by an August 7, 2006 Resolution of the Navajo Nation Commission on 
Emergency Management. He reviewed the faxed request for assistance. 
 
Supervisor Deal reported he is at the command center in Whippoorwill, has assessed the flooding situation and 
is requesting the assistance of the county.  He introduced  Sergeant Dempsey Harvey who has set up a station at 
the command center to direct emergency services.  Sergeant Dempsey reported: 
• There have been several rescue missions as a result of yesterday’s ra in and right now there is a heavy 
rain. 
• They had to use horses to do damage assessments due to road wash outs. 
• Several communities are without wate r.  The Nation’s public works department is making repairs to the 
water systems and advises it will take approximately th ree days since they can not get a backhoe to the 
location of the break.  They need tankers of potable water for about three days.  
• Three dams are in danger of breaking and if one breaks, it will create a chain reaction and flood the 
lower valley of Whippoorwill toward Hopi. 
• Two dams did break and released water at Whippoorwill Springs.  
• If evacuation becomes necessary they will utilize th e Pinon Public School gymnasium and there may be 
a need to relocate the incident command to that facility. 
• The main incident command center is in Window Rock and is directed by Johnny Johnson. 
• They are now relying on ATVs for transportation. 
• The Red Cross will be activated. 
 
In response to a question from Dusty Parsons as to what  the BIA has provided, Sergeant  Harvey said they only 
have one motor grader and it can not be used for deep ditches.  In response to a question from Supervisor  
DeSpain, Dusty Parsons advised the c ounty has no tankers that can carry potable water and he would research 
renting a tanker.  He requested and received directions to the site to provide to the rental companies. 
 
Mel Bowers advised that the Supervis ors can authorize county staff to pr ovide support that is available as 
needed after staff has assessed the situation and evaluated where best to put resources.  He stated that for the 
record, this is an emergency meeting of the Navajo County Board of Supervisors pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-
431.02 (D) to address a flooding emergency in northern Nava jo County affecting a large portion of the Navajo 
Nation including the Whippoorwill area, leaving roads washed out and resi dents stranded.  He advised the 
Navajo Nation has requested assistan ce and the county could not provide 24 hour notice of  this meeting due to 
the emergency nature.  He advised that the Clerk of the Board would post appropriate notice of this meeting 
within 24 hours.  He reported action could be taken by resolution or by direction to the County Manager and the 
Public Works Director. 
 
The Public Works Director and County Manager discussed renting potable wa ter tankers from the closest areas 
to the site and strategies and timelines to mobilize staff and equipment. 
 
Supervisor Deal discussed the plight of the people in the area , reporting large numbers of people, some in need 
of medical assistance, are st randed and families are short on food and wate r.  He advised that one 9’ culvert is

washed out leaving approximately 25-30 people unable to get across the washout.  He advised that a blade 
could cut in a temporary road for these people.   
 
The County Manager and Public Works Director reported they will travel to the site this evening and Supervisor 
Deal advised he would arrange lodging in the area for them.  Mr. Jayne stat ed that since the biggest immediate 
issue seems to be potable water, they will begin arrangements to rent equipment to transport water to the area. 
 
Mel Bowers advised this action could be authorized through two motions.  Supervisor Deal made a motion  
that, based on personal observation a nd information received from Nava jo Emergency Management out of 
Window Rock and from Incident Co mmander Sergeant Harvey, the existin g issues do present an emergency 
situation and that the Navajo C ounty Board of Supervisors declares  an emergency; motion seconded by 
Supervisor DeSpain; vote 3-0 approving the motion. 
 
Supervisor Deal made a motion  to direct the County Mana ger and Public Works Direct or to travel up to the 
Whippoorwill area and meet with the incident commander to  further assess the situation and determine needs; 
with authorization to the County Manager and Public Works Director to expend the funds that may be necessary 
to provide assistance for equipment, supplies or for ro ads; motion seconded by Superv isor DeSpain; vote 3-0 
approving the motion. 
 
Chairman Thompson requested that the Whitecone area be assessed also. 
 
ADJOURN:  Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to adjourn at 5:16 p.m.; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Deal; vote 3-0 approving the motion.

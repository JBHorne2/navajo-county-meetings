CITY OF HOLBROOK:  Resolution Number ___ - 06 supporting the City of Holbrook and their application to 
NARBHA to locate a regional detox facility in the City of Holbrook: Chairman Thompson advised the board that he 
has been supportive of legislation for detox centers in northern Arizona.  He advised that other requests for support 
of a grant may be coming before the board.  City Manager David Newlin and Councilman Simmons addressed the 
board regarding the history of the Ad Hoc Alcoholic Tr ansit Committee.  Mr. Newlin discussed the established 
collaborations to support this program and Councilman Simmons read a list of participants in support of the 
program stating they are all working together to address the issue.  He advised various stakeholders have come up 
with funds, provided technically qualified staff and volunteers; and the group is seeing participation they have never 
had before.  He stated the city is asking for a resolution of support.  Chairman Thompson stated rural communities 
will compete for these funds and there is a demonstrated need for a center in north-eastern Arizona.  Supervisor 
DeSpain advised that it is important that both Winslow and Holbrook have a center and understands that funding is 
not available to the reservation.  He stated he wants to assure the City of Holbrook that the board is supportive of 
this project and it might be that later the county may be  supportive of Winslow with their project.  Mr. Newlin 
advised there is no intention in their request to exclude anyone. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to approve 
Resolution Number 67-06 supporting the City of Holbrook and their application to NARBHA to locate a regional 
detox facility in the City of Holbrook; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney.  There was considerable discussion 
of this topic with all supervisors, Mr. Newlin and Mr. Simmons participating.  Discussion points were:   
• The Nation was unable to get this funding as it is state money that can not be used on federal land and the 
final legal definition of federal lands included the re servation.  Assistance is needed to clarify with 
legislators that Indian reservation is not pure federal land but rather a land where certain populations do 
exist as permanent residents including places of business, schools and hospitals.   
• There had been an expectation that the City of Winslow would be a recipient for a detox center.   
• The City of Holbrook is ahead of the other cities that are applying for funding. 
• NARBHA will review all grants and will fund the entity submitting the best outlined proposal. 
• A major player in this issue is the Navajo Nation and there should be representation from the Navajo Nation 
Health Council. 
• Mr. Newlin met with Mr. Largo of Navajo Nation Health a number of times and Mr. Peterson is his liaison, 
sitting on this grant committee. 
• The City of Holbrook will support NARBHA wherever the detox center is located. 
• A united approach to the grant would better insure that a detox center is located in this area. 
• The best location for a center is along I-40 and it is important that it is located in Navajo County. 
• There is no barrier for other entities to submit a request for support from the Board of Supervisors.   
Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Brownlow advised that Chairman Thompson has worked on this 
issue at the State level and thanked him for his dedication to this issue.

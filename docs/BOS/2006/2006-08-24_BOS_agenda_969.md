NORTHLAND PIONEER COLLEGE: Consideration and possible approval of a letter of support for 
Northland Pioneer College program, Education Makes Power : Peggy Belknap, Dean of Workforce 
Development, reviewed the Northland Pioneer EMPower program as a Department of Labor allocation for 
education purposes.  She advised the grant is requesting approximately $4,000,000 for a mobile skills lab 
to meet education and training needs of the Po wer Generation and Supply industry of northeastern 
Arizona.  She discussed the partnerships that collaborated on this grant with submitted letters of support.  
Supervisor DeSpain expressed appreciation to NPC for their efforts on behalf of the workforce of Navajo 
County.   Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to authorize the chairman to sign a letter of support for 
Northland Pioneer College program, Education Makes Power; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney; 
vote unanimous approving the motion.

Mr. Menlove advised that the Clay Springs Pinedale Fire District adopted a budget that would be 
supported by less than the tax rate they requested. The tax rate would be 1.4020; however they requested 
the tax rate to remain the same at 1.6759.  He stated he met with Mel Bowers who advised that A.R.S. 48-
807 requires the board to levy the tax based on the budget submitted by the district.  Supervisor Tenney 
inquired if there has been contact with fire board and Mr. Menlove stated both he and Joan Mickle did 
discuss the issue with them advising the budget drives the tax rate.  Supervisor Tenney inquired if there 
would be a similar situation with NATIVE.  Mel Bowers stated that Lance Payette felt this was a different 
situation because in today’s situation it is the board ’s responsibility to set the rate and in the NATIVE 
situation statute set the limit.     
 
Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve all Navajo County tax rates for all entities as presented; 
motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  PUBLIC HEARING: Consideration and possible board action regarding 
Liquor License Application Amendment #06090043 reflecting transfer of ownership requested by Gloria 
Giansanti for Giansanti’s located at 2381 Highway 260, Lakeside:  Darlene Fraley advised the Board that 
when memos were sent to the departments seeking recommendations, the Sheriff’s office raised a question 
over a previous action regarding a liquor license for the Giansanti’s based on information received from 
the state liquor license department.  Gloria Giansanti and John Giansanti addressed the board, advising that 
when they worked with Gary Martinson to take over the operation they were told they had to file as a 
manager of that license for that property and later f ound out that the law says that if they take over the 
license and reap the benefits they have to have ow nership.  They stated there were two or three liquor 
licenses on that property that created confusion. They said they believe there will be a fine levied against 
Martinson’s operation for not providing appropriate information.  Supervisor Tenney noted the address on 
the agenda was Lakeside and Mr. Bowers stated that  was a clerical error and would not affect today’s 
action.  Mr. Bowers advised that the board only makes a recommendation to the liquor license board and 
the board could consider the non-endorsement by the Sheriff and consider the explanation made today in 
making their recommendation. Supervisor Tenney stated he accepts the Giansantis’ explanation and 
made a motion to recommend approval of Liquor License Application Amendment #06090043 reflecting

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
 THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2006 
SPECIAL MEETING 
 
 
At 8:19 a.m. Chairman Thompson called the meeting to order. 
 
PRESENT:   Present by cell telephone with speaker phone at the county complex:  Jesse Thompson, 
Chairman; J.R. DeSpain,  David Tenney, and Jerry Brownlow, Members; James Jayne, County Manager; 
and Darlene Fraley, Clerk.  Present in the Boardroom were Mel Bowers, County Attorney; Kelly Dastrup, 
Elections Coordinator; and June Foard, Board Secretary.   
 
ELECTION CANVASS: Ms. Dastrup explained the results of the General Elections and asked that the 
Board approve the canvass.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to  approve the canvass as presented; 
Supervisor DeSpain seconded the motion.  Motion unanimously approved.  Supervisor Deal was not in 
attendance. 
 
ADJOURN: Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; Supervisor DeSpain seconded the motion.  
Motion unanimously approved.

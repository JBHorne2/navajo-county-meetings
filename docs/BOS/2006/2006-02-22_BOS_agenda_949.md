NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 22, 2006 
SPECIAL MEETING 
 
 
Navajo County Building at 201 W. Cooley, Show Low, Arizona – Time:  8:31 a.m. 
 
PRESENT:  David Tenney, Member present; Jerry Brownlow, Member present; and Jesse Thompson, 
Chairman, attended the meeting telephonically. Percy Deal, Vice Chairman, and J.R. DeSpain, Member, 
were absent.  Staff also present: James G. Jayne, County Manager; Darlene Fraley, Deputy Clerk of the 
Board.   
 
Acting Chairman David Tenney called the meeting to order and led the Pledge of Allegiance and the 
Invocation. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There was no response to the Call to the Public. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS/ELECTIONS:  Kelly Dastrup: Consideration and possible approval of 
appointments to fill vacancies on the Board of Trust ees of Timberland Acres Special Road District #1:  
Kelly Dastrup advised those present that there are tw o vacancies on the Board of Trustees for this road 
district, one is a one-year term and one is a two-year term. She said that when appointments are made and 
this Board has a quorum, they can determine which person will serve which terms.  Allen Zoellner 
reported that the third person on the Board does not intend to resign and that person believes the office 
becomes vacant when his term of office runs out.  Ms. Dastrup advised that simply because a term runs out 
does not mean that the office is declared vacant; that a resignation must be received.  She stated that if a 
member chooses to resign, the quorum can appoint a replacement until the next election.  She advised that 
the earliest an election could be held is September 2006. Mr. Jayne requested that Ms. Dastrup work with 
these Board members to develop timelines for the election and terms.  Supervisor Brownlow made a 
motion to appoint Allen Zoellner and Barbara Miller to fill vacancies on the Board of Trustees of 
Timberland Acres Special Road Distri ct #1 with terms to be determined at their first meeting; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Tenney; vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
Dusty Parsons stated that Mr. Zoellner had requested that the County maintain the main loop of the district 
until January 2007 and that an IGA would have to be prepared to address this issue.  Mr. Zoellner invited 
Mr. Parsons to their next meeting, which is the first Saturday in March, to discuss the IGA.  Supervisor 
Tenney stated the IGA could then go on the March 13th agenda. 
 
Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to adjourn at 8:50 a.m.; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney; 
vote unanimous approving the motion.

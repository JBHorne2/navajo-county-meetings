NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
 MONDAY, JULY 31, 2006 
 
. 
9:00 a.m .  CALL TO ORDER:  Supervisor DeSpain led the pledge of allegiance and offered the 
invocation. 
 
PRESENT:  Chairman Jesse Thompson and Supervisor J.  R. DeSpain. Supervisor Jerry Brownlow 
participated by the speaker telephone that was in the board chambers; Mel Bowers, County Attorney, 
James Jayne, County Manager, and Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the Board were present.  Supervisors Deal 
and Tenney were not in attendance. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There was no response to the Call to the Public. 
 
SHERIFF’S OFFICE/EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT: Consideration and possible approval to 
deactivate fire restrictions as outlined in the Navajo County Open Fire Ordinance 01-06:  Sheriff Butler 
advised that the need for the fire restrictions has expired and most communities and the forest service have 
pulled their restrictions.  Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to deactivate the emergency fire restrictions 
as outlined in the Navajo County Open Fire Ordinance 01-06 effective immediately; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Brownlow.  Supervisor Brownlow thanked the Sheriff’s office, Larry Dunagan and Sherry Hill 
for their assistance through this process.  Chairman Thompson expressed appreciation for the cooperation 
of the communities.   Sheriff Butler advised that there had been one major fire as a result of lightning at 
Porter Mountain and that it did not do much damage.  Vote unanimous approving the motion 
 
Supervisor DeSpain requested a return to the Call to the Public for projections on the Winslow levee.  Mel 
Bowers advised that if it is anticipated there will be no resulting action and if it is only for informational 
purposes, flooding information can be provided.  Dusty Parsons advised there are flooded roads throughout 
the county and they are monitoring gages on the Colorado River.  He said it is projected for the Winslow 
levee to be up to 16 feet by midnight with monitoring to begin on the levee about 6:00 p.m. tonight.  He 
said there may be low land flooding in different areas.  In response to a question from Supervisor DeSpain, 
Mr. Parsons reported that Mr. Vela is on his way to Woodruff with sand bags but that home is flooding 
only due to the way the house is situated on its lot.  In response to a question from the chairman, Mr. 
Parsons stated he is unsure if there are gages on the reservation. 
 
ADJOURN:  At 9:13 a.m., Supervisor DeSpain made a motion  to adjourn; seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the motion.

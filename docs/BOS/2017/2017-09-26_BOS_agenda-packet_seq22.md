Background:
The Sheriff's Office has trained paramedics within its patrol operations.  For the paramedics to
service the community they need to have "medical direction" from a hospital.  Scottsdale
healthcare Hospitals D/B/A HonorHealth provides this for numerous public safety entities that as
first responders offer paramedic services to their communities.
The term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until either
party hereto gives sixty (60) days' notice in writing to the other party of its intent to cancel and/or
terminate the Agreement.

BASE HOSPITAL AGREEMENT 
 
THIS BASE HOSPITAL AGREEMENT  made and effective on the 8th day of May, 2017 (the 
“Effective Date”) by and between  Navajo County, a political subdivision of the State of Arizona, its 
officers, officials, agents and employees (“County”) and Scottsdale Healthcare Hospitals, doing business 
as HonorHealth, an Arizona nonprofit corporation (“HonorHealth” or “Hospital”). 
 
RECITALS: 
 
WHEREAS, HonorHealth owns and operates five acute care hospitals: Scottsdale Osborn Medical 
Center located at 7400 E. Osborn Road, Scottsdale, Arizona  85251 (“Osborn”); Scottsdale Shea Medical 
Center located at 9003 E. Shea Boulevard, Scottsdale, Arizona 85260 (“Shea”); Scottsdale Thompson Peak 
Medical Center located at 7400 E. Thompson Peak Parkway, Scottsdale, Arizona 85255 (“TPk”); John C. 
Lincoln Medical Center located at 250 E. Dunlap Avenue, Phoenix, Arizona  85020 (“JCL”); and 
HonorHealth Deer Valley Medical Cen ter located at 19829 N. 27th Avenue, Phoenix, Arizona  85027 
(“Deer Valley”); and 
 
WHEREAS, JCL is certified as an advanced life support base hospital by the Arizona Department 
of Health Services (“ALS Base Hospital” or “Hospital”); and 
 
WHEREAS, Hospital believes that acting as a base station for all certified emergency medical 
technicians and individuals licensed or certified to render emergency medical care (“Personnel”) would be 
of benefit to patients cared for at the Hospital, to persons residing in the geographical area of the Hospital, 
and to patients cared for by County for whom the Hospital provides medical direction; and 
 
WHEREAS, Hospital desires to act as a base station for administrative medical direction and on -
line medical direction of County Personnel in accordance with the terms and provisions of this Agreement; 
and  
 
WHEREAS, County desires to utilize the Hospital as a base station for pre- hospital care and on-
line medical direction of County Personnel in accordance with the terms and provisions of this Agreement, 
and 
 
NOW THEREFORE,  in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the mutual covenants and 
promises hereinafter set forth, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of 
which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

Agreement and taking direction from physicians on Hospital ’s staff (i.e., either employed by or

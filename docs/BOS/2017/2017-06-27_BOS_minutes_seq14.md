Consideration and adoption of the proposed Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year 2017-18 for Navajo County :  Bryan Layton discussed the overviews of the proposed budget.  He stated that the 2018 budget is balanced, and we are planning to create a sustainable path for healthcare.  He stated we're projecting a flat property tax.  He spoke about sales tax where we're seeing a continuous positive trend and are therefore budgeting what we see this year.  He stated that we had another strong month in County sales tax and believes that this is our new baseline.  He spoke about the cost of doing business which continues to grow.  He indicated that within the budget, we have over $2.5 million in reductions, and he expressed appreciation to the Departments for their efforts in lowering costs.  He reviewed where the money comes from and where it goes.  He stated that with one-time funds, we were able to establish a true financial reserve account of $240,000.00, which would be used in the event of a financial emergency.  He spoke about having a financially sustainable path for healthcare by using best practices. Chairman Thompson stated that over the past several years, we were able to make adjustments and that we had to take a different direction at this time due to revenue.  He thanked the elected officials for finding other ways to bring in revenue.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Layton for the presentation today and for his work on healthcare.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked Mr. Layton for the presentation and for the effort that he and his team have put in.  Supervisor Whitesinger thanked Mr. Layton and staff for being able to examine our budget capacity and how we're utilizing our dollars to benefit the community.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to adopt the proposed Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year 2017-18 for Navajo County; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger . b.

Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation APN 210-23-016U & 210-02-042C; Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :  Dave Slocum discussed the properties.  He recommended no change to the full cash value.  Cammy Darris stated ARS §42-16106 requires that the County Board shall act on the petition, shall hear testimony presented in person at the hearing and may subpoena witnesses to testify regarding the petition.  Unless all parties agree otherwise, each party shall submit evidence in person, so the property owners need to be present in person to be heard. The Clerk indicated that the property owners called this morning at 10:10 a.m. indicating that they could not be present. Ms. Darris indicated that they were given notice a month ago.  Vice-Chairman Williams  inquired whether they submitted anything for the Board to review.  The Clerk indicated that those items submitted are in front of the Board.  Mr. Slocum indicated that there was a change in full cash value after the previous owner, Mrs. Dean, called and was given a 50-percent obsolescence on the commercial building for hardship for the 2016 year; and in the 2017 year, the value went back up to 188,315, and there was no appeal.  Vice-Chairman Williams indicated that he would like to have the petitioners be heard.  Ms. Darris objected to resetting the hearing as it had already been set.  She asked for an opinion from the County Attorney. Vice-Chairman Williams indicated that he would like to give the petitioners time to be heard.  Brad Carlyon, County Attorney, asked that they move forward with the second parcel so that he has time to review the issue regarding a continuance.  Mr. Slocum discussed Parcel Number 210-23-016U.  He indicated that the owner cited a cost approach but offered no comparables.  He stated he found two comparables for each building and reviewed those comparables which indicate no change to the full cash value.  Mr. Carlyon indicated that the statute is silent on the issue, and we have until October to make a decision.  He indicated that the parties would have a right to appeal the Board's decision to the State Board of Equalization which affords them due process.  Ms. Darris indicated that they have three levels of appeals.  She advised the Board that what they have in front of them is what the proerpty owners submitted to the Assessor's Office.  Mr. Slocum indicated that there has also been a phone conversation in April, and due to the demeanor of the property owner, he is not sure the property owner understood what was said.  Ms. Darris stated that they also have a box they can check to have a meeting with the Assessor's Office in person or on the phone. Vice-Chairman Williams indicated due to the fact the property owners had almost a month to meet with the Assessor's Office and get information in and they still have the right to appeal and have due process, he will motion for approval.  M otion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve the valuation as presented by the Assessor's Office as to APN 210-23-016U; seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion. Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve the valuation as presented by the Assessor's Office as to APN 210-02-042C; seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

To:  " The minimum horizontal distance required between a property line and a structure, or in the event a dedicated ingress/egress easement is interior to the property line, the setback is the horizontal distance between the easement line and a structure. BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration and Acceptance of the Resignation of James Jayne, County Manager effective August 4, 2017 and Direction to Staff to Develop Criteria and a Job Description to be posted for the County Manager position :  Melissa Buckley indicated that August 4, 2017, James Jayne will be stepping down as County Manager and that he has accepted a position with Coconino County as Special Initiatives Director.  She stated that staff is requesting the Board of Supervisors accept the resignation of Mr. Jayne effective August 4, 2017, and direct staff to develop criteria and job description for the position of county manager to be posted.  She spoke about some of the proposed criteria for county manager.  Supervisor Whiting inquired whether the education requirements are the same as other department heads.  Mr. Layton indicated that he will look into that.  Supervisor Whiting inquired as to the requirement for 10 years in county leadership.  Mr. Layton indicated that we are looking for someone with experience in the field and who can provide immediate leadership, and the criteria is consistent with other sitting county manager executive searches.  Supervisor Whiting suggested that the language in the 10 years in county leadership criteria be changed to "preferred" as opposed to "required."   Chairman Thompson indicated that he does not have any problem with the proposed requirements.  Vice-Chairman Williams inquired as to from where the language came.  Mr. Layton indicated that he looked at a number of open searches.   Vice-Chairman Williams  indicated that there should be a little more discretion available and concurs that preferred would be a better word.  Ms. Buckley reviewed the suggested edits to the qualifications for County Manager.  Chairman Thompson confirmed that the candidates would come before the Board.  Mr. Layton requested permission to design the job description and go forward with the application review process and, with the Board's input, design a comprehensive process so the Board can feel comfortable in their selection.  Motion by Supervisor Whiting to accept the resignation of James Jayne and accept the criteria for county manager as modified; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Mr. Layton indicated that he can send the criteria and qualifications individually so that the position can be posted.

The Presiding Justice of The Peace in Navajo County has requested the appointment of Justices of the Peace Judges 
Pro Tempore to serve for the purpose of enhancing the courts ability to process cases. 
 
It appearing the following named person meets the requirements of A.R.S. §22-122, and pursuant to A.R.S §22-
121, and at the request of the Presiding Justice of the Peace,  
 
IT IS ORDERED, that David Antonini is hereby appointed as Justice of the Peace Pro Tempore for the Navajo 
County Justice Courts.  The term of this appointment shall run from November 28, 2017, through July 31, 2018.  
this appointment limits the exercise of the powers granted under A.R.S §22-121 to those occasions where the 
Justice of the Peace Pro Tempore is on Navajo County Justice Court dockets and not for any other purpose or 
function, whether mandatory or discretionary. 
 
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED, that this appointments are conditioned upon the approval of the Navajo County 
Board of Supervisors pursuant to ARS §22-121, and upon the Judicial Branch obtaining appropriate funding from 
whatever source for the compensation due to said appointees pursuant to ARS §22-123 for services as justices 
of the peace pro tempore.

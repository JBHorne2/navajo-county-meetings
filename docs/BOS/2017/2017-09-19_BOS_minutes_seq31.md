NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, September 19, 2017

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I

Staff Attendance:
Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:03 a.m . Call To Order:

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

At 9:03 a.m. Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to enter into Executive Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

EXECUTIVE SESSION : Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (1) for interviews for the County Manager position .

At 12:50 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

RECONVENE :  Direction to staff on matters related to the hiring process of the County Manager position :

RECONVENE :  Discussion and possible action of making a conditional offer of employment to one of the remaining two applicants for the position of County Manager, and direct staff to enter into contract negotiations with the aforementioned applicant :

Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to direct staff to proceed on matters related to the hiring process of the County Manager position as discussed in Executive Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN:   At 12:54 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinge r.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

APPROVED:

Jesse Thompson, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED: September 26, 2017

ATTEST:

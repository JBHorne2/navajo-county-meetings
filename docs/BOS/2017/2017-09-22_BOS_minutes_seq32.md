NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Friday, September 22, 2017 *Telephonic Special Meeting

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Chairman appeared in person; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V all appeared telephonically.

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I

Staff Attendance:
Brad Carlyon, County Attorney appeared telephonically; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board appeared in person.

2:30 p.m . Call To Order :  Chairman Thompson called the meeting to order.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

At 2:30 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to enter into executive session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

EXECUTIVE SESSION : Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (1) for discussion of County Manager employment contract .

At 2:50 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

RECONVENE : Direction to staff on matters related to the hiring process of the County Manager position .

RECONVENE : Discussion and possible action of making a conditional offer of employment to one of the remaining two applicants for the position of County Manager, and direct staff to enter into contract negotiations with the aforementioned applicant .

Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to direct staff to proceed as directed in Executive Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN : At 2:50 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

APPROVED:

Jesse Thompson, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  October 10, 2017

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

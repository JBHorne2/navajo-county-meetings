COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

Presentation by County Attorney, Brad Carlyon to honor Thin Thai for his continued and generous support of child victims of crime in Navajo County :  Brad Carlyon discussed the item.  Mr. Carlyon recognized Thin Thai for his continued and generous support of various groups in the community, including the Navajo County Family Advocacy Center which helps sexually and physically abused children in the community.  He stated that Mr. Thai has never received any publicity for all that he does.   He indicated that it is people like Mr.Thai who make our communities so great.  He recognized Mr. Thai with a plaque in appreciation for his contributions for the Navajo County Family Advocacy Center.  Thin Thai expressed his gratitude for the recognition.  He spoke about having so many people over the years who have helped him, and he is just giving back to the community.  Cindy Reinholtz, Navajo County Family Advocacy Center Board member, expressed her gratitude to Mr. Thai for his big heart and for always helping the Family Advocacy Center.  Ms. Reinholtz invited everyone to attend the September 8th golf tournament at Torreon.  Chairman Thompson expressed appreciation to Mr. Thai for his service and generosity in the community.

Lee Jack, Sr., Supervisor District I is present at 9:14 a.m.

TREASURER :

a.

Consideration and approval of the following items: Receipt of the Annual Report of Collections and Delinquencies in accordance with A.R.S. §42:18002; Settlement of Accounts and Request for Authorization to Invest Funds Pursuant to A.R.S. §35-327 (G):   Debra Kester discussed the item and reviewed the annual report of collections and delinquencies.  She further discussed the settlement of accounts and request for authorization to invest and reinvest County monies.  She stated that the total tax levy is $82,704,359.85 and that as of June 30th, 2017, the Treasurer has collected 96.6 percent of the taxes and at the end of July, the percentage was 97.08.  She stated that the total interest received in the fiscal year was $1,301,511.32 and the total investment balance as of 6/30/2017 was $201,233,812.04.  She stated that we are anticipating approximately a million dollars in back tax land sales, which would equate to $10,000.00 coming into the processing fund.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked Ms. Kester for the work that she and her office does.  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to approve the Annual Report of Collections and Delinquencies in accordance with A.R.S. §42:18002; Settlement of Accounts and Request for Authorization to Invest Funds Pursuant to A.R.S. §35-327 (G); seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, April 25, 2017

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Chairman; Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III

Staff Attendance:
James Jayne, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

Chairman Thompson indicated that Supervisor Jack is on travel for the Navajo Nation Council, and Supervisor Whiting is out sick with the flu.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC :  Luther Lee, representative from Congressman Tom O'Halleran's office.  Mr. Lee spoke about his duties as the Tribal Engagement Representative for Congressman O'Halleran's office.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the week of April 24 - 28, 2017 as the Week of the Young Child :  Supervisor Whitesinger  recognized Jessica Beach and the First Things First Program for their work with our youth.  She spoke about the program and the support they provide for children 0 to 5 and their families.  She read the proposed proclamation.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve a proclamation declaring the week of April 24-28, 2017, as the Week of the Young Child; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Jessica Beach spoke further about the program and thanked the Board of Supervisors for recognizing this week as the week of the young child.  She spoke about the development that takes place in a child's first 5 years.  She spoke about how there are many grandparents raising their grandchildren in Navajo County.  Vice-Chairman Williams and Chairman Thompson made comments and thanked Ms. Beach for being present today.  Ms. Beach invited the County Supervisors to come see the impact of the program at one of their locations.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions :  Eric Scott reviewed the 29-item Personnel Action Report.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve the Personnel Action Report as presented; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Personnel Service Awards: Congratulations to the following employees for their continued service to Navajo County: Geneva Durkee  (Library District) 20 years; Christine Frayer  (Assessor) 10 years; Mariclare Hannah  (Legal Defender) 10 years; Donna Hurstrom  (BOS/Administration) 10 years; and  Michael Sandoval  (Facilities Management) 10 years :  Eric Scott recognized Geneva Durkee, Christine Frayer, Mariclare Hannah, Donna Hurstrom and Michael Sandoval for their continued years of service to Navajo County.  Mr. James Jayne, County Manager, thanked Geneva Durkee for her passion and dedication to the services that she provides throughout Navajo County.  Vice-Chairman Williams spoke about the wonderful examples these employees are and indicated that Navajo County is lucky to have them.

c.

Presentation on Cyber Security and what you can do to protect your computer system : Mary Springer introduced Tina Slankas who is the Deputy Director of the Office of Homeland Security Emergency Management, and indicated that they will be conducting many sessions over the next few days on Cyber Security.  Ms. Slankas gave a presentation on Cyber Security and answered questions of the Board.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked Ms. Slankas for her time today.  Ms. Slankas reviewed her schedule of appointments throughout Navajo County this week.  Chairman Thompson thanked Ms. Springer for her work.  Ms. Springer indicated that she conducts workshops throughout Navajo County every quarter, and Cyber Security is something which was asked for during those workshops.

d.

An update of Navajo County's social media progress over the last year :  Adam Wolfe gave an update on Navajo County's social media progress over the last year.  Mr. Wolfe shared the progress of all Navajo County social media outlets.  He indicated that over the year, there has been an increase of 86.4% for the Navajo County main page, 168% for the Sheriff's Office page, 282% for the Emergency Management page and 155% in followers for the Navajo County Twitter account.  He spoke about the ways we have changed the way we post items.  He spoke of the pages reaching nationwide.  He spoke about looking to improve video capabilities of Board of Supervisors Meetings, the possibility of six second or six word stories, and continuing to keep the public informed and educated.  Chairman Thompson spoke about the way of life of Native Americans and Native American History and indicated that he would like to see more about those items on the social media pages.  Supervisor Whitesinger indicated that this speaks to the transparency and offering communication to the public.  She indicated that this provides different perspectives and the possibility of reaching 300,000 people.  She thanked Mr. Wolfe for his work.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked Mr. Wolfe for the presentation.  He stated that the increase in people reached over this past year is amazing.  Mr. Jayne thanked Mr. Wolfe for what he continues to do for our organization.  Mr. Jayne spoke about the partnership that Mr. Wolfe continues to have with our elected officials, media and department directors to serve the public's interest.

NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Motion by   Vice-Chairman Williams to return to regular session; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of Amendment No. 1 to extend the Supply and Delivery of Culverts and Bands, Contract B16-04-001 with Pacific Ponderosa Co., for one additional year (May 10, 2017 - May 9, 2018 ):  Glenn Kephart discussed the item and requested that the contract be extended for another one-year term.   Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve Amendment No. 1 to extend the Supply and Delivery of Culverts and Bands, Contract B16-04-001 with Pacific Ponderosa Co., for one additional year (May 10, 2017 - May 9, 2018); seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of an Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the Town of Taylor for the Old Shumway Road Project :    Glenn Kephart discussed the item, indicating that this would correct an area of the roadway of Old Shumway within the Town of Taylor.  Mr. Kephart indicated that Supervisor Whiting has been involved in discussions regarding this project.  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to approve Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the Town of Taylor for the Old Shumway Road Project; seconded by Vice-Chairman Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration of Attachment F to the Master Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for Road Projects :  Glenn Kephart discussed the item, indicating that this agreement is in reference to Hopi Route 60.  He stated that this is a very critical route to both the Hopi Tribe and Navajo Nation for school children and medical facilities, and the total length is 13.5 miles.  He indicated that this will need to be done in phases and includes several partnerships with the Hopi Tribe, Navajo Nation, NDOT and Navajo County.  He stated that we are currently working on an agreement with the Hopi Tribe for the first phase of this project.  Chairman Thompson spoke about the economic development in the Hopi area and the need to get this project done and requested the support of the Board.  Supervisor Whitesinger indicated that she appreciates the work that has gone into this project and the partnerships.  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to approve Attachment F to the Master Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for Road Projects; seconded by Vice-Chairman Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKES COUNTY RECREATION IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to enter into the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

Consideration of Resolution Number 09-17 , authorizing the Clerk of the Board to execute certain contracts and administrative functions on behalf of the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District :  Melissa Buckley discussed the item.  She stated that she will provide a quarterly written report to the Board of Supervisors.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to adopt Resolution Number 09-17 , authorizing the Clerk of the Board to execute certain contracts and administrative functions on behalf of the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to return to regular session; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager :   Supervisor Whitesinger gave a report from District V.  She spoke about the Small Counties Forum that was held last week which included representatives from small counties in Arizona and legislators.  She further spoke about the deficiencies and challenges that exist within small counties who are doing more with less.  She stated that the silver lining was hearing lawmakers talk about rural communities.  She indicated that she spoke at the State Board of Transportation on behalf of the White Mountain Regional Transportation Committee and what we can do to support our communities and be able to provide more resources to our communities, with tourism continuing to be important.  She stated that yesterday she had the opportunity to present to the White Mountain Apache Tribe and their council as they had a special transportation meeting and thanked Chairman Thompson for being a part of that presentation.  Vice-Chairman Williams spoke about the article in the Arizona Republic regarding the monies which have been taken from the counties.  He spoke about the tour of the Graham County juvenile detention facilities that he attended with Superior Court Judge, Michala Ruechel; Juvenile Director, Arno Hall; Juvenile Detention Manager, Jake Salas; Assistant County Manager, Bryan Layton; Deputy Commander, Dennis Warren and Public Defender, Michael Penrod.  He also spoke about the same group attending a tour of the Pinal County juvenile detention facility on Friday.  Chairman Thompson  spoke about a meeting with Congressman O'Halleran regarding the Winslow Levee.  He spoke about meetings in the communities of Whippoorwill and Pinon and spoke about the red dog gravel in that area.  He spoke about the meeting with the State Transportation Board.  He also spoke about HURF monies.

ADJOURN :  At 10:25 a.m .  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to adjourn; seconded by Vice-Chairman Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

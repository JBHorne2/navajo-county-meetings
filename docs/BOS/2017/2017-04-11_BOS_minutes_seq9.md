NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES

James Jayne, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

PUSH PROGRAM :

a.

Presentation to April Wellness Warrior, Aleen Hamilton: Eric Scott recognized Aleen Hamilton as the Wellness Warrior for April.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation to Kenadi Stuart, Mylee Demuth and Damon Attakai for their 2017 Elk's Hoop Shoot State and Regional Championships:   Supervisor Whiting introduced Greg Boyce, Arizona Elks Association Hoop Shoot Director.  Mr. Boyce spoke about the Hoop Shoot program and what an accomplishment it is for these three young people to be regional champions out of 2.8 million competitors.  He indicated that they will be going to Chicago next weekend for the national championship and stated that the winners at the national championship will have their names inscribed at the Basketball Hall of Fame.  Each winner spoke about what it means to be a champion and how many baskets they made at the last event.

b.

Presentation regarding the 4th Annual Holbrook Route 66 Festival :  Kathleen Smith, City of Holbrook, spoke about the upcoming 4th Annual Holbrook Route 66 Festival and Gunslinger Car Show, which will be held on Friday, June 9th, and Saturday, June 10th.  She spoke about the Chassis Challenge and the Classic Car show and challenged the members of the Board and County Administration to participate in the Chassis Challenge.  She stated that all municipalities in Navajo and Apache Counties are being invited to attend.  She also indicated that April is "Happy Holbrook" month.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Smith for all she does in the community.

c.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the month of April 2017 "Child Abuse Prevention & Awareness Month":   C.J. Wischmann spoke about Child Abuse Prevention & Awareness Month and the activities that will be held during the month.  He spoke about a program next year which will put an ad in the theaters throughout the State of Arizona to educate the public on how to report suspected child abuse and invite the public to become part of the child abuse prevention council.  Supervisor Whitesinger read the proclamation.  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to approve the proclamation declaring the month of April 2017 "Child Abuse Prevention & Awareness Month," seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Supervisor Whiting and Supervisor Whitesinger thanked Mr. Wischmann for his efforts in combating child abuse in the community.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions : Eric Scott reviewed the six-item Personnel Action Rerport.  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to approve the Personnel Action Report as presented, seconded by  Vice-Chairman Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

County Financial Update :  At 9:49 a.m., Bryan Layton gave a presentation of the County finances.  He stated that County sales tax is at -2.65% below budget expectations and the State Shared Sales Tax is at 0.37% over budget expectations.  He stated that he believes that the numbers will continue in a similar manner for the year.  He further spoke about County facilities and operational costs to continue to operate those buildings.  He spoke about the closure of the Pine Lake Facility in Pinetop-Lakeside, which will save a little over $12,000.00 per year.  He stated that we have partnered with other organizations to continue to provide those services.  He spoke about the current health building and Frontier Conference room at the White Mountain County complex.  There was some discussion on the new space at the White Mountain complex.  Michael Penrod, Public Defender, indicated that the proposed Public Defender's space is sufficient.  Chairman Thompson inquired regarding a meeting space for the public.  Mr. Layton indicated that the Clerk of the Board's office has information for alternate community meeting spaces.  He thanked Paige Peterson for her thoughtful consideration on how we do business in Finance. He spoke about reductions in Administrative Services amounting to a total savings of $252,167.00, which includes eliminating two positions and restructuring the roles and responsibilities, which equals a savings of $96,648.00; leveraging Economic Development together with Workforce Development and split-funding that salary for  a savings of $76,220.00; and restructuring positions and an in-kind match for Emergency Management for a savings of $79,299.00.  He spoke about the tradeoffs that will occur because of the total reductions.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Layton for the information and spoke about how Administrative Services is leading by example.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked Mr. Layton for the update.

SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration of a proclamation declaring April 9 - April 15, 2017 as "National Telecommunicator's Week":   At 9:35 a.m. Sheriff KC Clark spoke about the hard work and dedication of the dispatchers and telecommunicators in Navajo County.  He introduced Donna McMonigal and Jenny Hansen.  Supervisor Whiting read the proclamation.  Motion by Supervisor Whiting to approve a proclamation declaring April 9 - April 15, 2017 as "National Telecommunicator's Week", seconded by Supervisor Jack ,  Ms. McMonigal thanked the Board for the recognition.  Ms. Hansen invited the Board to stop by one of the 911 call centers in the location where they live.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of Agreement 2017-021 with the Department of Public Safety for the transfer of one Virtual Training Simulator :  Chief Jim Molesa addressed the Board regarding the Virtual Training Simulator and indicated that it will be no cost to Navajo County.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked the Sheriff and Chief Molesa for finding a partnership by which we can train law enforcement in the area.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve Agreement 2017-021 with the Department of Public Safety for the transfer of one Virtual Training Simulator, seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of Revocation of authority of Porter Creek Domestic Water Improvement District (DWID):   Rayleen Richards, Elections Director, discussed the item.  Supervisor Whitesinger asked for how long this revocation will last.  Ms. Richards indicated that it would be for a few months.  Vice-Chairman Williams inquired of Mr. Carlyon whether this is the appropriate action to take from a legal perspective, and Mr. Carlyon indicated that it was.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve revocation of authority of Porter Creek Domestic Water Improvement District (DWID) as presented by staff, seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of c all for an election to fill the remaining terms of three (3) vacant governing board positions on the Porter Creek Domestic Water Improvement District on Aug. 29, 2017:   Rayleen Richards, Elections Director, discussed the item.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to approve a call for an election to fill the remaining terms of three (3) vacant governing board positions on the Porter Creek Domestic Water Improvement District on Aug. 29, 2017 as presented by staff, seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

Consideration of the Microsoft Enterprise Agreement starting April 1, 2017 for the next three years:  Ken Dewitt, IT Director, discussed the item.  Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to approve the Microsoft Enterprise Agreement starting April 1, 2017, for the next three years, seconded by Vice-Chairman Williams .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKES COUNTY RECREATION IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams  to enter into the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District Board of Directors Session, seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

Consideration and approval to hire Phil Stratton Electric and Perkins Cinders to fix a broken culvert and electrical repair at 2015 Silver Creek Drive : Melissa Buckley discussed the item.  She spoke about the discovery of the broken culvert.  She spoke about the different quotes which were obtained and requested approval to hire Phil Stratton Electric and Perkins Cinders.  Motion by Supervisor Whiting to approve hiring Phil Stratton Electric and Perkins Cinders to fix a broken culvert and electrical repair at 2015 Silver Creek Drive, seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Ms. Buckley indicated that 2,388 letters were sent out advising property owners of the Community Town Hall on April 27th at 6:00 p.m. at the lodge at White Mountain Lake.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Buckley and her team and indicated that he is looking forward to the town hall meeting.

Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to return to regular session, seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and Assistant County Manager : Chairman Thompson recognized Judge Marez and Constable Tipton for being present at today's meeting.  Supervisor Jack thanked Melissa Buckley and Donna Hurstrom for helping him get on the NACo Transportation Committee.  Supervisor Whiting gave an update on continuing conversations regarding the budget and spoke about individuals coming forward with possible solutions.  Supervisor Whitesinger gave an update on meetings with local townships regarding Navajo County's current financial standing.  Vice-Chairman Williams thanked the reporters who are present for the accuracy of the articles which they have written regarding County issues.  Chairman Thompson thanked Supervisor Jack for his efforts with Navajo Nation in identifying projects in Navajo County.  Mr. Carlyon thanked Mr. Layton for his work on the budget, his professionalism and communication which has helped during these trying times in Navajo County.  Chairman Thompson spoke about the city council meeting in Winslow.

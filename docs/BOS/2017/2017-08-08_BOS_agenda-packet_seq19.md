C.)   Purpose. 
 
The intent of this agreement is to allow member districts to have access to  information 
technology services and cost-effective broadband internet access that would not otherwise be 
accessible to them without cost sharing and cooperative purchasing amongst the organizations. 
The Navajo County Schools Information Technology Consortium is a service provided by the 
Navajo County School Superintendent Office, with the assistance of the Navajo County 
Information Technology Department.  The Consortium will assist in providing solutions for the 
needs of its member Districts.  
 
The parties hereto understand that a substantial portion of the funding for services to be 
provided by the Consortium may consist of E-rate grant funding through the Universal Service 
Administrative Co. (USAC) that is available to the member school districts and libraries.  E ach 
party that is a member to this Agreement will work with the Office of the Navajo County 
School Superintendent and the Navajo County Information Technology Department to apply 
for and obtain necessary grant funding to help support the Consortium’s activities. 
 
D.)  Designation of Fiscal Agent and Consortium Director. 
 
Navajo County School Superintendent shall serve as the fiscal agent for this Agreement.  
A.R.S. 15-301 and 15-302 provide that the Office of County School Superintendent is a State 
Education Service Agency. This authorizes the Navajo County School Superintendent to 
provide program services to school districts. 
 
The Navajo County School Superintendent and the parties hereto have designated the 
Director of the Navajo County Information Technology to be the “Consortium Director.”  The 
Consortium Director shall obtain necessary information regarding the needs of the member 
districts and will coordinate the efforts of the Consortium to serve the common needs of the 
members. 
 
E.)  Cost of Services and Termination of Agreement. 
 
The members of the Consortium shall conduct an annual budget meeting to be 
scheduled prior to March 31 at which planning and a proposed budget will be considered for the 
next fiscal year (July 1-June 30). Each member district shall have a designated representative at 
the budget meeting who can advise on the member District’s needs for the upcoming fiscal year 
and who is authorized to vote on Consortium matters at that meeting, including the adoption of 
a budget for the Consortium.   A budget will be approved for the upcoming fiscal year at the 
budget meeting.  Additionally a fee schedule for each member of the Consortium will be set 
based upon the needs of each member.   Other meetings of the Consortium will be called as 
necessary by the Navajo County School Superintendent or a majority of the member Districts on an as-
needed basis. 
 
The budget to be adopted shall be based upon the services to be provided each member District 
by the Consortium.   If a participating District finds a need to increase services required during the

problem is identified and not resolved member access may be blocked until the problem is 
resolved.  The Navajo County Information Technology Department will be available to help the 
Navajo County School Superintendent  with identifying issues and resolvin g them if needed. 
Members will e nsure CIPA (Child Information Protection Act) filtering compliance when it is

C.)   Purpose. 
 
The intent of this agreement is to allow member districts to have access to  information 
technology services and cost-effective broadband internet access that would not otherwise be 
accessible to them without cost sharing and cooperative purchasing amongst the organizations. 
The Navajo County Schools Information Technology Consortium is a service provided by the 
Navajo County School Superintendent Office, with the assistance of the Navajo County 
Information Technology Department.  The Consortium will assist in providing solutions for the 
needs of its member Districts.  
 
The parties hereto understand that a substantial portion of the funding for services to be 
provided by the Consortium may consist of E-rate grant funding through the Universal Service 
Administrative Co. (USAC) that is available to the member school districts and libraries.  E ach 
party that is a member to this Agreement will work with the Office of the Navajo County 
School Superintendent and the Navajo County Information Technology Department to apply 
for and obtain necessary grant funding to help support the Consortium’s activities. 
 
D.)  Designation of Fiscal Agent and Consortium Director. 
 
Navajo County School Superintendent shall serve as the fiscal agent for this Agreement.  
A.R.S. 15-301 and 15-302 provide that the Office of County School Superintendent is a State 
Education Service Agency. This authorizes the Navajo County School Superintendent to 
provide program services to school districts. 
 
The Navajo County School Superintendent and the parties hereto have designated the 
Director of the Navajo County Information Technology to be the “Consortium Director.”  The 
Consortium Director shall obtain necessary information regarding the needs of the member 
districts and will coordinate the efforts of the Consortium to serve the common needs of the 
members. 
 
E.)  Cost of Services and Termination of Agreement. 
 
The members of the Consortium shall conduct an annual budget meeting to be 
scheduled prior to March 31 at which planning and a proposed budget will be considered for the 
next fiscal year (July 1-June 30). Each member district shall have a designated representative at 
the budget meeting who can advise on the member District’s needs for the upcoming fiscal year 
and who is authorized to vote on Consortium matters at that meeting, including the adoption of 
a budget for the Consortium.   A budget will be approved for the upcoming fiscal year at the 
budget meeting.  Additionally a fee schedule for each member of the Consortium will be set 
based upon the needs of each member.   Other meetings of the Consortium will be called as 
necessary by the Navajo County School Superintendent or a majority of the member Districts on an as-
needed basis. 
 
The budget to be adopted shall be based upon the services to be provided each member District 
by the Consortium.   If a participating District finds a need to increase services required during the

F.)  Term and Termination of Agreement. 
 
This agreement shall be in effect for five years. This I.G.A. may be renewed for successive 
terms of five years upon approval of the governing body of each entity electing to renew it. 
 
Termination of this agreement or partial termination may be accomplished as follows: At or 
prior to each annual budget meeting parties may submit, in writing, notice of termination for the 
upcoming fiscal year. 
 
The parties hereto acknowledge the applicability of A.R.S. § 38-511 regarding cancellation 
of this agreement in the event of a conflict of interest, and said statute is incorporated herein by 
reference. 
 
G.) Procurement. 
 
The Navajo County School Superintendent's Office, utilizing the Navajo County Finance Office 
as needed, is hereby authorized to execute agreements or procurements for the consortium. 
Navajo County Schools Superintendent's Office, shall administer all bidding and purchasing of 
supplies and equipment in conformity with all applicable local, state and/or federal procurement 
statutes and regulations. Navajo County Procurement Policies and Procedures shall apply to 
procurements made by the Navajo County Schools Consortium. Navajo County Schools 
Superintendent's Office may leverage local, state or regional Procurement co-operatives as an 
authorized government entity. Per Title 41 and Article 10, School District Procurement- 41-
2632 and R7-2-1191, cooperative purchasing is authorized. 
 
H.)  Member Responsibilities. 
 
Members agree to make every effort to prevent network abuse that could cause 
blacklisting or liabilities that could  affect all members. This may include but not limited to 
viruses, spam, peer-to-peer or other process that may be considered network or Internet abuse, 
which could be tracked and leave the member networks exposed to the outside world.  If a 
problem is identified and not resolved member access may be blocked until the problem is 
resolved.  The Navajo County Information Technology Department will be available to help the 
Navajo County School Superintendent  with identifying issues and resolvin g them if needed. 
Members will e nsure CIPA (Child Information Protection Act) filtering compliance when it is 
not provided by the Consortium.

I.) Services Provided by the Navajo County School Superintendent through the Navajo 
County IT Department 
 
The Navajo County Schools Consortium will make the following services available to 
member districts: 
 
1.  Networking support for routers, firewalls, logging, intrusion 
detection, microwave and other devices required in providing 
Internet and WAN links to the members. 
2.  The Consortium will negotiate and work with vendors to provide 
Internet access to the members at the best possible price and  quality. 
3.  The Consortium may seek or apply for additional funding to 
lower cost or enhance services and increase programs for district 
members. 
4.  Network Engineers may research solutions for members to resolve 
existing issues or for future recommendations. 
5.  Techs will monitor the network as needed to help in trouble shooting 
and decision making. 
6.  Other services within scope of staff and job descriptions as needed by 
the members. 
 
 Due to the distance involved, when possible any Tech or Engineer work 
may be done either by phone or with remote software. Personnel substitutions may be 
made if someone is not available or it is deemed that a person of lesser experience can 
handle or be guided through the situation. This is not to cut corners but to make the most 
of the people available for the number of miles to cover. 
 
A work request system may be implemented to handle requests. This will help to 
prioritize and schedule work to meet everyone’s needs as much as possible. It will also act as an 
audit trail and a way to compile data that can be used to project future usage or growth. 
 
Charges may be adjusted to make costs most equitable for both Internet charges and hour 
tech/engineer charges. 
 
Charges associated WAN links from the Consortium data center to a participant will be 
the responsibility of the participant unless it is bundled as part of the “Internet Services” at the 
request the member. These links may be discontinued if funds are not available as required.

K.)  Administration of Consortium Business. 
 
The Navajo County School Superintendent, in cooperation with the Navajo County 
Finance Office, shall administer all general financial operations and activities, including 
maintenance of records of sick leave, payroll, vacation and other benefit entitlements and 
reimbursement of approved travel expenses. 
 
Accounts receivables and/or billing for participant members will be handled by the Office 
of the Navajo County School Superintendent.  The SUPERINTENDENT shall establish on behalf of 
the CONSORTIUM a special services fund with the Navajo County Treasurer and shall be responsible for 
all deposits to and disbursements from this fund.   
 
All day-to-day business decisions shall be made  by the Consortium Director (Navajo County IT 
Director) in consultation with the Navajo County School Superintendent.   
 
Significant decisions impacting Consortium members shall require consultation and 
input from the Consortium’s Member Districts. In addition to the budget meetings discussed 
above, the Consortium shall hold regular meetings twice per year, and at other times where 
important decisions need to be made and the Director desires input.  At those meetings, certain 
items are to be presented to the Member Districts for informational purposes and for the member 
Districts to provide direction to the Consortium Director. The Director shall provide Member 
Districts an agenda at least two weeks in advance. Each Member District agrees to ensure 
attendance of a representative to such meetings to vote on relevant items.  The vote for such 
items shall be final. 
 
The Consortium members, in their discretion, may adopt Bylaws consistent with this 
Agreement to govern the Consortium business. 
 
L.)  Office Space. 
 
Navajo County IT will make available office space for the technical personnel used by the 
Consortium. 
 
M.)  Payment by Member Districts. 
 
Payment for services provided to each member District shall be paid to the Fiscal agent at 
the beginning of each fiscal year and no later than 30 days after receipt of invoice. The County 
School Superintendent reserves the right to discontinue services if payment is not received by the 
due date. 
 
Member school Districts agree to budget all necessary funds to carry out their portion of this 
agreement as required by A.R.S. §11-952(B)(3).

15. Definitions:  For purposes of this Agreement, and for the purpose of 
administering this agreement in the future, the following definitions shall 
apply: 
a.) Internet Services: Reflects the amount of Internet bandwidth purchased by 
the entity, this may or may not include cost for physical connection 
(transport) to member(s) location. This will cover getting connection link up 
and kept up, router configuration, and use of common Internet Services as 
requested including network address translation. 
 
b.) Internet Server Hosting: Reflects costs associated with hosting services 
which may include but not limited to Email, DNS, DHCP and Web. 
 
c.) Network Engineer: Upper level tech support for help with network 
design, server maintenance, router and switch maintenance and general 
configuration.

d.) Office Management: Will include general operations of business 
portion of consortium and any assistance desired by the schools in 
routine accounting or purchasing practices. 
 
e.) Operation Cost: All common costs that may be incurred outside of “Base 
Internet Cost”, which may include, training, vehicle maintenance, fuel, 
telephone, electric, air conditioning, testing, servers, travel and salaries 
required to maintain required services. This will normally be covered by 
the per hour rate of all other support services after “Base Internet Costs” 
are met. Hourly rates are adjusted to cover projected services requested. 
 
f.) Base Internet Cost: Purchased Internet charges, transport, equipment, 
maintenance contracts for equipment or software and any other services 
such as web, email, firewall, Intrusion Detection that are common to all 
members and available to be used, or are required for operation. Cost may 
include a portion of salaries needed to operate and maintain and oversee 
these systems. Cost may include a portion of the CIPA filtering or other 
services required by members. These services may be made available from 
the county complex.   
 
g.) Internet charges: Mbs (megabits per second) will be assessed a value as a 
standard, increments of this will be created based on approximation of what 
would be considered fair usage. For instance a T1 is rated at 1.5 Mbs. 
Internet Bandwidth charges will be computed by taking “Base Internet 
Cost” minus “Other Funds” and “Erate Funds” if available to obtain a 
balance. By changing the value assessed to the per Mbs charge which will 
also change charges on all variations of the MBs standard value, the balance 
on the “Base Internet Cost” can be kept at zero, or used to help offset 
“Operation Cost” or to cover projects as requested. 
h.) Tech and Engineer Charges: Hourly rates are based on the balance of 
“Operation Cost” after any “Other Charges” if available are 
subtracted. The hourly value will be raised or lowered based upon the 
number of hours/days selected by the members to clear the balance or 
help offset the “Internet Charges”. 
 
i.) Yearly Charges: Can be anything that a member wishes to have added 
to this agreement that can be handled within personnel or equipment 
limits. 
 
j.) Other Charges: May be revenue generated by support of non-school 
members of the Consortium or other sources.

COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

Consideration and adoption of a Proclamation declaring October 2017 as "National Domestic Violence Awareness Month ":  Lynda Young spoke about the event last night where they lit up the historical courthouse.  She indicated that Domestic Violence Awareness Month evolved from the "Day of Unity" held in October 1981 and was conceived by the National Coalition Against Domestic Violence.  She stated that the intent was to connect advocates across the nation who were working to end violence against women and their children, and in October 1987, the first Domestic Violence Awareness Month was observed and marks the initiation of the first national domestic violence toll-free hotline. She indicated that in 1989, the U.S. Congress passed Law 101-112 designating October of that year as National Domestic Violence Awareness Month.  She stated that Navajo County Attorney's Office is asking the Navajo County Board of Supervisors to proclaim October 2017 as Domestic Violence Awareness Month. She read the proclamation.
Supervisor Whiting made a motion to adopt a Proclamation declaring October 2017 as "National Domestic Violence Awareness Month;" motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion. Chairman Thompson spoke about the importance of talking with your children and family. Vice-Chairman Williams spoke about an event that took place in south county regarding a Domestic Violence issue which resulted in a police officer being shot.  He further stated that domestic violence needs to be at the forefront of our minds.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation to Jihad Nodman, Hopi High School, for taking Second Place in Division 4 Cross Country:   Chairman Thompson recognized Jihad Nodman from Hopi High School for taking second place in Division 4 Cross Country.  He presented Mr. Nodman with a certificate and a medal.  He thanked Mr. Nodman for being a good example to youth in the community.  Vice-Chairman Williams asked Mr. Nodman what he learned from cross country that will benefit him later in life, and Mr. Nodman indicated that he learned about finishing what you start, encouragement for others and to run with a good heart and a good mind.

b.

Presentation to Talon Smith, Joseph City High School, for taking First Place in Division 4 Cross Country :  Supervisor Whiting recognized Talon Smith for taking first place in Division 4 Cross Country.  He presented Mr. Smith with a certificate and a medal.  Mr. Smith spoke about practicing extra by himself.  Supervisor Whitesinger thanked both athletes for being present today and for their outstanding work on the field.

RECORDER :

a.

Presentation to the Indian Wells Veterans Organization:   Doris Clark and Chairman Thompson presented the Indian Wells Veterans Organization with a modular trailer from the Elections Department.  George Clark, Vice Commander, was present for the recognition and thanked the Board of Supervisors and Ms. Clark.

Chairman Thompson spoke about Native American Heritage Month and the recent recognition of the Code Talkers by President Trump.  He indicated that we owe a great deal of debt to these Code Talkers.  He indicated that he was troubled by comments made by our President, and it hurts him to see the division of cultures.  He stated that now is the time to unite, and it takes people of all cultures to make America what it is.

EMPLOYEE RECOGNITION :

a.

Navajo County Recognizes the following employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations:  Jennifer Ober  (Public Works);  Ronnie Hoskins (Public Works); Eileen Rogers (Public Fiduciary); and  Thomas Swaim (Public Works) :  Lea Peterson recognized Jennifer Ober, Ronnie Hoskins, Eileen Rogers and Thomas Swaim for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations.  A short video was shown.  Vice-Chairman Williams spoke about the individual qualities of each employee and thanked them for what they do.  Glenn Kephart thanked each employee and indicated that we recognize them for all of the wonderful things they do and for providing great service to our community.  He stated that they stand there as examples of what it takes to be excellent.  Supervisor Whiting thanked those individuals and spoke about the level of service they provide and how they provide it.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Personnel Service Awards: Congratulations to the following employees for their continued service to Navajo County: Robert Edwards (County Attorney) 15 years; Rebecca Baldwin (Health District) 5 years; Paige Petersen (BOS/Administration) 5 years; Stephanie Wofford (Adult Probation) 5 years; and  Joyce Alexander (Voter Registration) 5 years :  Eric Scott recognized Robert Edwards, Rebecca Baldwin, Paige Peterson, Stephanie Wofford and Joyce Alexander for their continued service to Navajo County.  Chairman Thompson thanked each of the employees for their continued service.

b.

Consideration of the Navajo County Hazardous Materials Response Plan :   Catrina Jenkins and Mary Springer discussed the Navajo County Hazardous Materials Response Plan and answered questions of the Board.  Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve the Navajo County Hazardous Materials Response Plan; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration of an Application for Agent Change/Acquisition of Control of a Liquor License for Robert Harold Holman of Pinetop Country Club, Inc., in Pinetop, Arizona :  Donna Hurstrom discussed the Application for Agent Change/Acquisition of Control of a Liquor License.  She indicated that this facility is located at 6734 Country Club Drive in Pinetop and that we have received approvals from the Sheriff's Office, Health Department, Treasurer's Office and Planning and Zoning.  Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve the Application for Agent Change/Acquisition of Control of a Liquor License for Robert Harold Holman of Pinetop Country Club, Inc., in Pinetop, Arizona; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

d.

Consideration of a New Liquor License for Christian Marcus Otzen, Lorenz Durham Otzen, Jr., Linda Margaret Otzen, Patrick Michael Nackard, Palmer Michael Nackard, Monica Jewel Nackard and James Joseph Davis of Legacy Beverage, LLC, in Show Low, Arizona :  Donna Hurstrom discussed the item of  a new Liquor License for Legacy Beverage.  She indicated that this facility is located at 5102 Wagon Trail in Show Low and that we have received approvals from the Sheriff's Office, Health Department, Treasurer's Office and Planning and Zoning. Cyndy Valdez, attorney for Legacy Beverage, discussed the consolidation of the two corporations.  Vice-Chairman Williams inquired as to whether this consolidation resulted in a change in the number of employees, and Ms. Valdez indicated that at this time, no, but the future remains to be seen.  Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to approve a New Liquor License for Christian Marcus Otzen, Lorenz Durham Otzen, Jr., Linda Margaret Otzen, Patrick Michael Nackard, Palmer Michael Nackard, Monica Jewel Nackard and James Joseph Davis of Legacy Beverage, LLC, in Show Low, Arizona; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

e.

Health Insurance Update :  Eric Scott and Jason Andrade gave a health insurance update for 2018.  They indicated that the employees' financial liability will be staying the same, core benefits are staying the same, and the third-party administrator will change to BAS Health.  Mr. Andrade answered questions of the Board.  He stated that all providers will be in network for employees, and there will no longer be out-of-network providers. He spoke about the personal healthcare navigator, which will help employees find the most efficient provider for services.

b.

Consideration of the purchase of three 2018 120M2 Caterpillar Motor Graders with a trade-in of one 1980 140G Caterpillar Motor Grader from Empire Machinery in the amount of $812,568.92 :  Jeanine Carruthers discussed the item and requested approval.  She indicated that there is a savings by purchasing three and trading in one motor grader as opposed to leasing.  Supervisor Whiting inquired whether this amount was included in the budget.  Ms. Carruthers indicated that $90,000.00 was included and that the funds are available due to HURF dollars.  Glenn Kephart addressed the Board regarding the Public Works capital improvement budget.  Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to approve the purchase of three 2018 120M2 Caterpillar Motor Graders with a trade-in of one 1980 140G Caterpillar Motor Grader from Empire Machinery in the amount of $812,568.92; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Mr. Kephart indicated that the easy route would have been to do what was intended; however, due to Ms. Carruther's due diligence, we found a better way, and he commended her for that.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager :  Chairman Thompson indicated that Supervisor Jack is absent today due to his other position that he holds for the Navajo Nation.  Supervisor Whiting stated that he has received calls from citizens expressing concern about the amount of smoke from prescribed burns.  He spoke about the necessity of controlled burns and how it prevents hazards.  He stated that he has a phone call into the forest supervisor to see if there is something that can be done to accommodate our citizens.  He spoke about having a great Thanksgiving week last week and encouraged us as Navajo County to serve others during the Christmas season.
Supervisor Whitesinger stated that they recently lost an employee due to a job-related accident at Sunrise.  She indicated that they have not been public about it out of respect for the family.  She stated that it has been a significant impact to the Sunrise family in being able to provide the services they need to overcome and helping them cope and provide services that they need and to ensure that the mountain is safe.  She indicated that we continue to send condolences to the family.  Vice-Chairman Williams indicated that ourthoughts and prayers are with Bryan Layton and his family.  He thanked those who participated in the Community Fast in Show Low.  He stated that the fast has averaged about $13,000.00 per year, and the money stays locally to help families in need.  He indicated that as of the end of the fast this year, over $15,000.00 has been raised.  He spoke about the end-of-fast celebration at the Show Low Auditorium where over 300 people attended.  Chairman Thompson spoke about this month as a good time to express appreciation to the people we work with.  He stated that he has been attending veterans organizations and community meetings.  He stated that he is participating in the Arizona State Transportation Board and made a presentation, with Miles Begay's help, on the bus route road conditions on the reservation.  He thanked staff for putting the presentation together.  He stated that he has begun delivering checks to the senior citizen centers in his District.  Glenn Kephart spoke about having heavy hearts as we think of Bryan Layton and his family.  He stated that the arrangements for Mrs. Layton are at 1:00 p.m. this Friday in American Fork, Utah.  Melissa Buckley spoke about the tour of the Historic Courthouse with lunch being served beginning at 11:30 a.m.

ADJOURN :  At 10:30 a.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

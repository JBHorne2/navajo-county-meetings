NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 15, 2017

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Chairman appeared in person; Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman; and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V appeared telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:02 a.m . Call To Order:

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS :

a.

Consideration and adoption of the Fiscal Year 2017-18 property tax rates for all Navajo County Public School Districts :  Jalyn Gerlich spoke about the tax rates and levies and indicated that there are no raises on the tax rates.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to adopt the Fiscal Year 2017-18 property tax rates for all Navajo County Public School Districts; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Consideration  and adoption of the Fiscal Year 2017-18 property tax rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts :  Paige Peterson indicated that the first item is the Navajo County primary property tax rate, and the second will be the property tax rate for all Navajo County special taxing districts.  Chairman Thompson indicated that the information is before the Board.  Vice-Chairman Williams inquired as to whether the Board already voted on the Navajo County primary property tax rate.  Ms. Peterson stated that during the Truth in Taxation hearing, the Board voted whether or not they would increase the rate but did not vote on setting the rate, and this morning is to set the rate as was presented prior, which is an increase of about five cents, 0.8903.  Motion by Supervisor Jack to approve setting the Navajo County primary property tax rate at 0.8903; seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  The Clerk of the Board took a roll call vote.  Supervisor Whitesinger voted yes; Supervisor Jack voted yes; Vice-Chairman Williams voted no; Supervisor Whiting voted no; and Chairman Thompson voted yes.  Motion carried 3 to 2.
Ms. Peterson discussed the FY18 rate for all other taxing authorities in Navajo County.  Motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to adopt the Fiscal Year 2017-18 property tax rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts; seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN : At 9:14 a.m., motion by Vice-Chairman Williams to adjourn; seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

APPROVED:

Jesse Thompson, Chairman

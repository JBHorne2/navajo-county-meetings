NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 31, 2017

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I, telephonic; Jesse Thompson, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III, telephonic

Absent:
Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, Interim County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

10:45 a.m . Call To Order

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS :

a.

Consideration and adoption of the revised 2017-2018 Tax Rates for Navajo County School Districts :  Jalyn Gerlich discussed the item.  She indicated that this is for Joseph City School District.  Supervisor Whiting asked if this decreases the tax for Joseph City, and Ms. Gerlich indicated that yes, it does.  Supervisor Whiting made a motion to adopt the revised 2017-2018 Tax Rates for Navajo County School Districts; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

At 11:12 a.m., Supervisor Whiting and Supervisor Jack were excused and Vice-Chairman Williams and Supervisor Whitesinger were present via Skype.

At 11:12 a.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to move into Executive Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

EXECUTIVE SESSION : Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (1) for Skype interviews for the County Manager position .

At 1:17 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :   At 1:18 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

APPROVED:

Jesse Thompson, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  September 12, 2017

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

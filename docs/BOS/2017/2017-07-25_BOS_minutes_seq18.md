Presentation to retiring employee  Brenda Clark , Navajo County Sheriff's Office:   Eric Scott recognized Brenda Clark on her retirement from the Navajo County Sheriff's Office.  Lt. Don Griffiths spoke about Deputy Clark and her 34-year career with the Navajo County Sheriff's Office beginning in 1983 as a dispatcher.  He stated that she became a deputy sheriff in 1997 and went to the civil division in 2004.  He indicated that she has trained many supervisors and developed many new procedures which helped the division run more efficiently.  He spoke about a time when Deputy Clark developed a program to entice people to pay their delinquent taxes which brought in several hundred thousands of dollars. He stated there have never been any complaints from a citizen for any of the work that Deputy Clark does and that she is very compassionate and understanding with all those she encounters.  He indicated that in addition to her civil duties, Deputy Clark also assists in other areas of the Sheriff's Office, including the Communications Department, and her work never falls behind. He spoke about how she will still be voluntarily assisting the division with the new members after her retirement.  He stated that she has continued to go out of her way during her career for Navajo County and the citizens that she serves and that she is the standard of all that the Navajo County Sheriff's Office stands for.  He thanked Deputy Clerk for her 34 years of service and wished her well.  Chairman Thompson presented Deputy Clark with an engraved vase full of flowers. The Board of Supervisors recognized Deputy Clark.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Deputy Clark for her service and wished her well. Supervisor Whitesinger  thanked Deputy Clark for her service and indicated that she is inspired by Deputy Clark.  Mr. Jayne congratulated Deputy Clark and thanked her for her smiles and kindness and wished her the best.

PLANNING & ZONING :

1.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 21-17 approving/denying a temporary recreational vehicle permit which would allow placement of an RV on the lot on Assessor's Parcel #212-32-015C, a.k.a. Lot 17 in Rainbow Lake Estates and a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 East, Section 26 in the Lakeside area, which is R-3 Zoning:   Sandra Phillips discussed the item.   She indicated that Planning and Zoning Commission has denied the request.  Supervisor Whitesinger asked whether the request is for a temporary permit and they won't be building on the property.  Ms. Phillips indicated that at the time of discussion with the applicant, the applicant was not ready for a building permit as they intend to build but have not decided.  Chairman Thompson opened the public hearing,  Mary Ann Joseph, the applicant, addressed the Board indicating her desire to receive a temporary recreational vehicle permit which would allow placement of an RV on a parcel of land in Navajo County at 1853 Lee Drive.   She spoke about the area.  She stated that it is their full intent to put something nice on the property.   She spoke about her family's history of residence and service in the Show Low area.  She further spoke about property values in the area.  She indicated that she spoke to neighbors east of Arlene Road.  She indicated that they are building, whether it's a mobile home or manufactured home. Supervisor Whitesinger asked about the building permit. Ms. Phillips indicated that all staff is asking is that the applicants apply for a building permit at which time the TRV would be granted, but they have not received any plans to date. Supervisor Whitesinger indicated that she would recommend that the applicant apply for a building permit. Ms. Joseph indicated that they would lose money if they did that.  She asked what would happen if they put out the money and decide to go with a manufactured home?  Ms. Phillips indicated that staff has always worked with homeowners and landowners if they change direction. Chairman Thompson closed the public hearing. Supervisor Whitesinger indicated that one of her concerns is that if we authorize for this RV to be placed on this particular property, it will set a precedent for other RVs to be set on property and that without a building permit it will not allow us to enforce.  She stated that when we're thinking long term in moving RV's, trying to get them removed is difficult, which opens the door for hundreds of other properties to have RVs on them. Supervisor Whitesinger made a recommendation that the applicant apply for a building permit. Motion by Supervisor Whitesinger to adopt Resolution Number 21-17 denying a temporary recreational vehicle permit which would allow placement of an RV on the lot on Assessor's Parcel #212-32-015C. a.k.a. Lot 17 in Rainbow Lake Estates and a portion of Township 9 North. Range 22 East, Section 26 in the Lakeside area, which is R-3 Zoning with the recommendation that the applicant apply for a building permit to ensure that they are able to develop the property the way they choose to develop the property; seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of the appointment of an Interim County Manager:   James Jayne spoke about the qualifications approved by the Board.  Mr. Jayne recommended the appointment of Public Works Director Glenn Kephart, as Interim County Manager effective Monday, August 7, 2017.  Mr. Jayne stated that Mr. Kephart has worked closely with each of the supervisors, elected officials, department directors, tribes, cities and towns.  He indicated that Mr. Kephart does a great job of working with people to find solutions and get things done.  He spoke regarding the education of Mr. Kephart. Chairman Thompson indicated that he works with Mr. Kephart quite a bit, and Mr. Kephart has always been a public relations type of individual. Supervisor Williams indicated that in the time that Mr. Kephart has been with the County, he has been impressive and that he is a dedicated professional, a problem solver, has tremendous work ethic and integrity and embodies what we strive to be in Navajo County. Supervisor Jack indicated that he supports the recommendation and believes that Mr. Kephart has the appropriate qualifications. Supervisor Whitesinger indicated that she supports Mr. Kephart wholeheartedly and that he has been a strength and asset to District V, as well as the districts across Navajo County.  She spoke about him being highly professional and expressed gratitude for the recommendation.  Supervisor Whiting indicated that he has tried to throw Mr. Kephart some curve balls, and Mr. Kephart has never gotten frustrated. He stated that Mr. Kephart is extremely dependable and very responsive. Supervisor Williams indicated that we have a tremendous team at Navajo County and that we have multiple people who could step in as Interim County Manager and expressed gratitude for our team.  Motion by Supervisor Williams to approve the appointment of Glenn Kephart as Interim County Manager; seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Mr. Jayne indicated that Mr. Kephart is really involved in everything we do as a county seven days a week. He agreed with Supervisor Williams's comment that Navajo County has a great team, and Mr. Kephart will be joining the team of Bryan Layton, Paul Watson, Paige Peterson, along with the Board of Supervisors, elected officials, department directors and staff. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Mr. Kephart thanked Mr. Jayne for the vote of confidence. He indicated that it has been an honor and privilege to work with Mr. Jayne.  He stated that he is humbled and honored by this appointment and understands the importance of this commitment and that he is dedicated to carry forth the unending dedication to serve this community.  He spoke about how he is looking forward to working with the team.  He indicated that today is a day to honor Mr. Jayne and what he has built in Navajo County.

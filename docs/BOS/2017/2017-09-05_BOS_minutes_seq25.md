NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, September 5, 2017

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Chairman; Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman, via Skype; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V, telephonic

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III

Staff Attendance:
Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Eric Scott, Human Resources Manager/Risk Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

2:02 p.m . Call To Order

At 2:02 p.m. Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to move into executive session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

EXECUTIVE SESSION : Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (1) for Skype interviews for the County Manager position.

At 2:51 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

RECONVENE : Direction to staff on matters related to the hiring process of the County Manager position:   Staff was directed to bring Marc Ryan and Glenn Kephart to Navajo County for a tour and breakfast, lunch and dinner with members of the Board of Supervisors.

ADJOURN :  At 2:52 p.m., Vice-Chairman Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

APPROVED:

Jesse Thompson, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  September 12, 2017

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

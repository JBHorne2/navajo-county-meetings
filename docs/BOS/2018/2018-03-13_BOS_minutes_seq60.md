BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Recognition of the Winslow Boys' Basketball Team for winning the 2018 3A State Basketball Championship:   Supervisor Thompson recognized the Winslow Boys' Basketball Team for winning the 2018 3A State Basketball Championship.  He indicated that this championship takes him back to 1968 when he graduated from Winslow High School.  He thanked the coaches and the team members for all their hard work and dedication.  He further indicated that this is the Bulldogs' first championship since 1979.  He indicated that the Bulldogs beat the Blue Ridge Yellow Jackets on February 26, 2018, with a score of  79 to 63.  Supervisor Jack spoke about the game and congratulated the coach on a great job.  Head Coach Scott Corum thanked the Board for the recognition and thanked his staff.

b.

Consideration of and approval to appoint John Flynn, Policy Logic, LLC, as District Administrator for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District and approve contract for Mr. Flynn's services:   Brad Carlyon discussed the item indicating that the White Mountain Lake Fire District no longer has a quorum of board members; therefore, by statute, it is the responsibility of the County Board of Supervisors to appoint a district administrator.  He stated that the County Attorney's Office is recommending John Flynn be appointed as administrator and introduced Mr. Flynn.  Mr. Flynn addressed the Board.  He indicated that he has worked for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District through the County Board of Supervisors in the past.  He stated that he also worked with the Timber Mesa Fire Department before it became Timber Mesa and also Mohave, Santa Cruz and Maricopa County fire districts.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Carylon and Jason Moore for their effort on this item.  He further thanked those who have served on the fire district board for their efforts.  Supervisor Whiting made a motion to approve appointing John Flynn, Policy Logic, LLC, as District Administrator for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District and approve the contract for Mr. Flynn's services; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

Consideration and acceptance of FY19 ACJC National Criminal History Improvement Grant Agreement #NCP-17-18-007:   Lynda Young discussed the item indicating that this grant addresses a gap in the fingerprint process to ensure that every defendant has a 10-print fingerprint on file.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to accept the FY19 ACJC National Criminal History Improvement Grant Agreement #NCP-17-18-007; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Mr. Carlyon indicated that the fingerprint machine will go out into the Superior Court hallway where the fingerprinting can be done right after the judge orders the defendant to get fingerprinted.  He spoke about the yellow disposition sheets that follow the defendant which is modified through the system with the fingerprint card.  He stated that this is helpful in many ways for police officers and prosecutors for identifying one's criminal history and will bring Navajo County to almost 100 percent compliance.  Chairman Williams expressed appreciation to the County Attorney's Office for working with ACJC in securing the grant.  Ms. Young indicated that there is a match requirement, and those funds have already been secured.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA

1.

Consideration and approval of Updated Rules and Regulations Manual:   Melissa Buckley introduced Pat Stevens, District Administrator.  Ms. Buckley spoke about the feedback that was received from the community with regard to the updated rules and regulations manual.  She further spoke about the community town hall from which a final draft of the rules and regulations was prepared.  She spoke about notice to the community members.  Ms. Buckley reviewed the proposed changes to the Rules and Regulations Manual.  She stated that they received one comment via email at 8 p.m. last night requesting that the Board defer the vote.  She indicated that this individual has communicated with Ms. Stevens regarding her desire for a gate as well as the placards.  Ms. Buckley stated that that information was discussed with the community members, and it was decided not to go with a gate.  Ms. Buckley requested approval.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Buckley and Ms. Stevens for their efforts.  He stated that he has heard positive feedback from the community, and he loves the idea of the placards.  He spoke about the many opportunities for individuals to participate in discussions.  Supervisor Whiting made a motion to approve the Updated Rules and Regulations Manual; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members:   Glenn Kephart echoed the thank you to Ms. Buckley for her work on the rules and regulations for White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District.  He congratulated Brad Carlyon for being appointed to the Board of Directors of NACo.  Supervisor Thompson stated that he was excited to see Mr. Carlyon get appointed to the Board of Directors of NACo.  He spoke about his participation in the NACo Transportation Steering Committee.  He spoke about how he was not able to attend NACo because of the weather back east.  He indicated he had a meeting with City of Flagstaff and Coconino County Board of Supervisors and talked about lands that the Hopi Tribe is trying to obtain through a 1996 law.  He stated that it has taken a long time trying to identify how they can get certain lands in lieu of federal lands.  He indicated that he will be attending the State Transportation Board Meeting this week and most of it will be relative to Navaio-Hopi resettlement law.   He spoke about a public hearing yesterday in Teesto about transportation projects.  Supervisor Jack thanked staff for supporting the Board of Supervisors.  He thanked Public Works for their work on the Red Dog project.  He stated he also attended the public hearing yesterday regarding the Hopi Tribe selecting land west of Flagstaff.  He spoke about the prospective loss of revenues due to the pending closure of Navajo Generating Station and Peabody Mine.  Supervisor Whiting thanked the Board members, staff and our legislative representatives for their hard work on the bills moving through the legislature. Chairman Williams spoke about the legislative meetings in Washington including the ECO legislative meeting.  He stated that he met with the Forest Service, Senator Flake, Senator McCain's staff, some of our congressman and had a chance to attend a meeting with some of the White House staff, ask questions and bring up issues affecting us in Arizona, including coal energy and Navajo Generating Station.  He thanked Paul Watson for attending.  He spoke about the efforts by Supervisor Whiting in preparation of the meeting with the Forest Service and thanked staff for making all the arrangements. He spoke about the upcoming CSA Small Counties dinner on Wednesday, the CSA Board of Directors meeting on Thursday and the AACo meeting on Friday. He spoke about a nice text he received this morning regarding a Navajo County employee.

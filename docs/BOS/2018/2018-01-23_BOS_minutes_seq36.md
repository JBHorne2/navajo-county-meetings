COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members: Glenn Kephart indicated that he will be attending the Winslow City Council meeting tonight and the Taylor Town Council meeting on Thursday. Supervisor Thompson reported on the Navajo Nation Council Opening Session yesterday. He also reported on Arizona State Transportation Board meeting last week in Sierra Vista. He indicated that they spoke about the name of one of the roads down in southern Arizona, making it an historic road. He spoke about what the State Transportation Board does in relation to roads in the State of Arizona. He spoke about attending many other meetings. He stated that he is grateful for the recognition of having served as a Navajo County Supervisor for 25 years and thanked his family for their support.  He indicated that he could not do it without this team and the trust of the citizens. Supervisor Jack thanked Bill Bess and Public Works for their continued good work on the Red Dog Project. He spoke about the new leadership in Hopi. He further spoke of the Hopi Inauguration and stated that Navajo County was well received. He further spoke of his attendance at the Navajo Nation Winter Session and stated that Navajo County was well received there, as well. Supervisor Whiting thanked the team that attended the Pre-Legislation Meeting and the CSA Board meeting last week. He spoke about the communication between the Board of Supervisors and Legislators on different bills. He stated that he believes we have a good process with regard to speaking with the Legislators.  He spoke about the ECO meeting.  He further spoke about the Northwest Regional Working Group (NRWG) meeting and indicated that the biomass power plant was shut down for a period of time and hope to be operational within the next two weeks.  He asked that we keep them in our hearts and prayers.  He spoke about a meeting with local employees who work at APS at the Cholla Power Plant and felt it was very productive. He further spoke about ongoing meetings with the forest restoration group, which includes the Governor's Office, the regional forester, the state forester and SRP, and they have been looking at state-wide issues as it relates to forest restoration and how we can streamline from the Federal Government to the state to local.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger congratulated Supervisor Thompson and thanked him for his 25 years of service. She further congratulated Supervisor Whiting on his appointment as Chairman of the ECO Board. She spoke about discussions with legislators regarding how bills will affect Navajo County. She spoke about the technology which allows us to tell our lawmakers directly whether we are in support or not in support of a bill. Chairman Williams spoke about attending the NRWG meeting and expressed appreciation to our local industry partners, the Forest Services, neighboring counties, ECO and Supervisor Whiting for his leadership in that area. He congratulated Supervisor Whiting  on his leadership at ECO.  He stated that 3 of the 15 county managers in Arizona are from Navajo County, and we continue to be a leader throughout the state. He spoke of attending the Mayor and Town and City Council Members Meeting for Navajo and Apache County and met with some of our representatives from Legislative Districts 6 and 7.  He spoke of the Pre-Legislative session meeting with legislators from Legislative Districts 6 and 7 regarding how potential legislation will affect Navajo County.  He stated that we were asked to come and present to all of the Rural Caucus  members.  He stated that he and Sheriff Clark sit on the Arizona Criminal Justice Commission and attended that meeting. He stated that he attended the Hopi Inauguration and also the Navajo Nation Tribal Council. He spoke of what a blessing it is to live where we do because of the diversity of cultures that we have. He complimented Bryan Layton and Paige Peterson for their efforts on the presentation which was given to the legislature. He also thanked Glenn Kephart.

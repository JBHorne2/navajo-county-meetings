NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, November 16, 2018

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Steve Williams, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman each appeared telephonically

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Donna Hurstrom, Deputy Clerk of the Board

9:02 a.m . Call To Order:

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of approval of the Official Canvass of the November 6, 2018 General Election:   Rayleen Richards requested that the Board approve the canvass as presented.  Supervisor Whiting made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the November 6, 2018 General Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN:   At 9:03 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Steve Williams, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  November 27, 2018

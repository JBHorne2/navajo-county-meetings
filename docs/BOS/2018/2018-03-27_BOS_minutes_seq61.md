EMPLOYEE RECOGNITION :

a.

Navajo County Recognizes the following employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations:  Rhonda Hatch  (Assessor);  Bryan Sepulveda (NCSO); Doug Hatch (Assessor); and  Ty Stirling (Public Works):   Lea Peterson recognized those employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations.  A short video was shown.  Supervisor Whiting expressed appreciation for those employees for their hard work and dedication.  Chairman Williams made statements about each employee and the qualities they have.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation to Kenadi Stuart and Mylee Demuth for their 2018 Elk's Hoop Shoot State and Regional Championships:   Supervisor Whiting introduced Gregg Boyce, Elk's Lodge Hoop Shoot Administrator for the State of Arizona.  Mr. Boyce explained what the Hoop Shoot is and indicated that there will be 72 competitors in the national finals in Chicago, Illinois, out of 3.2 million kids who started the program.  He stated that this will be Kenadi's second year in a row and Mylee's third year in a row going to Nationals.  He spoke about the relationship and bonds that he has formed with the athletes and their families.  Each champion was presented with a medal and a certificate.  Supervisor Whiting indicated that only 6 of the 72 national finalists made it back from last year, and 2 of those 6 are from District III in Navajo County.  He gave a heartfelt thank you to the families of the athletes and also to Mr. Boyce and the Elks Lodge.  He spoke about the book Talent is Overrated.  Mylee and Kenadi each spoke about working hard and practicing each day to win at this level.

b.

Presentation to Show Low Cheer for winning the Arizona State Cheer and Pom Tournament in Stunt and Show Cheer:   Chairman Williams recognized the Show Low Cheer Team for winning the Arizona State Cheer and Pom Tournament in Stunt and Show Cheer.  Mr. Dave Nicholas, Show Low High School Athletic Director, thanked the Board for the recognition.  He also thanked the coaching staff and the young women.  He spoke about the hard work that goes into the sport of spirit line and the work that these young women do for the enhancement of Show Low High School.  He indicated that they work two-thirds of the school year in addition to keeping up their grades.  Each of the young women stated their name and one thing they have learned from participating in cheer.  Each girl spoke about hard work, determination, teamwork, responsibility, family, focusing on goals and dedication.

c.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the month of April 2018 "Child Abuse Prevention & Awareness Month":   This item was tabled to April 10, 2018.

d.

Consideration and possible approval of all properties sold as auctioned on the back tax land on-line auction and approval to allow the Clerk of the Board to sell the unsold properties over the counter for the amount due:   Melissa Buckley discussed the item.  She indicated that at the close of our auction, 95 properties were sold in the amount of $115,091.67, which is the best auction we've had.  She thanked Donna Hurstrom for all of the work she did.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Buckley and her office for being good stewards of the taxpayer's money.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger made a motion to approve all properties sold as auctioned on the back tax land on-line auction and allow the Clerk of the Board to sell the unsold properties over the counter for the amount due; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Personnel Service Awards: Congratulations to the following employees for their continued service to Navajo County:  Alberto Peshlakai  (BOS/Admin) 10 years:   Eric Scott recognized Alberto Peshlakai for his continued service to Navajo County.  Supervisor Thompson congratulatedMr. Peshlakai for his continued service to Navajo County.  He stated that Mr. Peshlakai is dependable, reliable and has a good character.  Supervisor Jack also congratulated Mr. Peshlakai and thanked him for his many years of public service.  Mr. Kephart thanked Mr. Peshlakai for his work with the Navajo Nation and the service he offers to Navajo County.  Supervisor Whitesinger congratulated Mr. Peshlakai for his ten years of service and thanked him for his contributions to District V.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Peshlakai for the service he offers to all of the communities within Navajo County.

b.

Presentation to retiring employee, Shirley Hunt:   Eric Scott recognized retiring employee Shirley Hunt for her 12-1/2 plus years of service to Navajo County.  Michael Penrod thanked Ms.Hunt for the hard work that she does each day.  He spoke about a time when Ms. Hunt went above and beyond to help a defendant with no family in the area until her family could come and get her.  Chairman Williams congratulated Ms. Hunt on her well-deserved retirement.  Ms. Hunt thanked God and her co-workers and expressed appreciation for all of the great people she has worked with.

c.

Presentation of the Ready Set Go! statewide campaign:   Catrina Jenkins gave a presentation of the Ready Set Go! statewide campaign which included a short video.  Adam Wolfe stated that the Ready Set Go! video will be posted on our social media pages.  He thanked all of those who helped contribute to the video.  Ms. Jenkins spoke about the Ready Set Go! brochures.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Wolfe and Ms. Jenkins for their work on this campaign and discussed the importance of being prepared.  Ms. Jenkins indicated that it was reported that we had seven campfires left unattended last Sunday and spoke about the importance of not leaving a fire unattended and making sure those campfires are out.  Chairman Williams thanked Ms. Jenkins and Mr. Wolfe for their work.

d.

Consideration and approval of a Facilities Agreement Between Navajo County, and Arizona Conservation Corps for a portion of the Pine Lake Facility located at 1852 S. Pine Lake, Pinetop, Arizona:   Bryan Layton discussed the item.  He spoke about the facility and the Arizona Conservation Corps and that this agreement would formalize the partnership.   Supervisor Whitesinger spoke about the type of work that is done by the Arizona Conservation Corps and the services that they provide.  Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve a Facilities Agreement Between Navajo County and Arizona Conservation Corps for a portion of the Pine Lake Facility located at 1852 S. Pine Lake, Pinetop, Arizona; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of Amendment No. Five to the Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for road projects:   John Osgood discussed the item.  Supervisor Thompson spoke about the Navajo Nation School bus routes and the partnership with Navajo Nation to improve this road to make it easier for our students to get to school.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve Amendment No. Five to the Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for road projects; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members : Glenn Kephart spoke about the Joseph City Founder's Day celebration that he attended on Saturday. Supervisor Thompson discussed attending CSA and many community meetings pertaining to the relationship between the Tribes and the County with regard to economic development on the Reservation.  He spoke about the Arizona Transportation Board meeting that took place in Yuma.  He indicated that on April 20,  there will be a public hearing regarding the five-year transportation plan happening in Flagstaff.  He stated that Miles Begay has further information on that upcoming meeting. He spoke about the rehabilitation and conservation of these roads, as well as modernization and expansion.  He stated that we need to review the five-year plan and offer our input. Supervisor Jack thanked the leadership and staff from Navajo County. He spoke of a meeting with Navajo Nation and Apache County and BIA regarding gravel from Peabody. Supervisor Whitesinger spoke about the new court facility in Pinetop-Lakeside. Chairman Williams spoke about the Small County Dinner Forum where four State Representatives, one of our State Senators and APS attended.  He further spoke about meeting with all of the supervisors state wide the following day to continue to discuss legislative issues.  He spoke about discussions regarding the budget and thanked the Department Directors and Elected Officials.  He stated that Judge Ruechel is hosting a Family Resilience Conference tomorrow.

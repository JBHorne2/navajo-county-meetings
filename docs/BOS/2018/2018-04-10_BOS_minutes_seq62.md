SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring April 8 - April 14, 2018 as "National Telecommunicator's Week":  Sheriff Clark discussed National Telecommunicator's week and recognized all dispatchers.  He spoke about their schedule and their lives outside of their careers and the things they hear and go through.  Caitlin Montoya thanked the Board for the appreciation on behalf of the dispatchers.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed her appreciation for all dispatchers county wide.  Chairman Williams read the proposed proclamation.  Supervisor Whiting made a motion to approve a Proclamation declaring April 8 - April 14, 2018 as "National Telecommunicator's Week"; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Sheriff Clark indicated that there will be a live remote on Thursday at the dispatch center.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the month of April 2018 "Child Abuse Prevention & Awareness Month ":  CJ Wischman discussed the efforts to educate the public regarding child abuse prevention and awareness.  He thanked the Board for being one of the leaders of recognizing this effort and providing a proclamation .  Supervisor Whiting read the proposed proclamation.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve a Proclamation declaring the month of April 2018 "Child Abuse Prevention & Awareness Month"; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Chairman Williams thanked Mr. Wischman for being a champion for children for many years.

b.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the week of April 14 - 20, 2018 as the Week of the Young Child :  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger read the proposed proclamation.  Supervisor Jack made a motion to approve a Proclamation declaring the week of April 14 - 20, 2018 as the Week of the Young Child; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Supervisor Thompson thanked all those who work to assist us as parents for educating and teaching our children.  He spoke about teaching his grandchildren.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of Approving Task Agreement No. 4 to Memorandum of Agreement between Hopi Department of Transportation and Navajo County for the Funding of Navajo County Maintenance activities which will not exceed the amount of $30,000 from District ll Hopi CIP Funds:   John Osgood discussed the item.  He spoke about the ongoing capital improvement plan.  He stated that this item, as well as item 7b, represent a collaborative process between Navajo County, the Bureau of  Indian Affairs Western Navajo Agency andthe Hopi Department of Transportation to identify public road maintenance priorities and to initiate, coordinate and complete the work on public roads on native lands.  Supervisor Thompson requested support of the Board for this item and 7b as part of the maintenance program under the responsibility of the Hopi and Navajo.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve Task Agreement No. 4 to Memorandum of Agreement between Hopi Department of Transportation and Navajo County for the Funding of Navajo County Maintenance activities which will not exceed the amount of $30,000 from District II Hopi CIP Funds; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of the BIA Western Agency Task Agreement No. 9 for the purchasing of aggregate material for BIA Route N2 not to exceed $80,000.00:   Supervisor Thompson indicated we have partnered with the BIA in prior years to lay down two inches of gravel on this road, and we would like to do this again on this particular road.  He stated that it is bad when people get isolated during the winter time.  Supervisor Thompson   made a motion to approve the BIA Western Agency Task Agreement No. 9 for the purchasing of aggregate material for BIA Route N2 not to exceed $80,000.00; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration of the Hopi Tribe Department of Transportation Task Agreement No. 5 for the purchase and delivery of aggregate material for Sand Springs Road NR/IR 9751, expenditure not to exceed $20,000.00 :  This item was tabled until the next meeting.

8.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :  Glenn Kephart indicated that the National Weather Service issued an advisory regarding high winds combined with low humidity levels for tomorrow and Thursday, and there will be red flag warnings. He stated that more information can be provided by Catrina Jenkins. Supervisor Thompson reported that on April 6, he attended the Law and Justice Day in Dilkon and at the same time there was a similar event in Kayenta.  He spoke about the Winslow Levee EAP tabletop exercise.  He stated he also attended the NACOG Economic Development Committee meeting with Paul Watson in Flagstaff.  He reported that he will be headed out to the Hopi land today concerning an emergency meeting regarding roadway issues. Supervisor Jack reported that the Navajo Nation's priority is to try to keep Navajo Generating Station going. He stated that this week the speaker of the Navajo Nation Council will go to D.C. to go before a senate hearing of the Natural Resources Committee. He indicated that there are potential buyers of NGS. He stated that the Navajo Nation Council will be convening for Spring Session on April 16 through the 20th and invited all of his colleagues and staff.  He thanked Public Works for continuing the partnerships in addressing road issues in his district.
Supervisor Whiting did not have an update. Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger congratulated the White Mountain Apache Tribe on their recent election and is hopeful for the new leadership.  She wished the new chairwoman, Gwendena Lee-Gatewood, great success in her new leadership role and looks forward to our opportunity to partner with her. She spoke about the Ready Set Go presentation to the Pinetop-Lakeside Town Council.  She stated that Pinetop-Lakeside is looking to be an example to the community on how we prepare our properties and spoke about Wildfire Community Preparedness Day on Saturday, May 5th.  Chairman Williams stated that he attended theVFW 50 th Anniversary Celebration in Show Low.  He spoke about the service that this organization does in the community.  He spoke about attending Arizona Criminal Justice Commission meeting.  He thanked Catrina Jenkins for her efforts in the Ready Set Go campaign.  He thanked Brad Carlyon and Deanne Romo for their time during budget discussions.

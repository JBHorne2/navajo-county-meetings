NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, September 6, 2018

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Steve Williams, Chairman; and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman, appeared telephonically

Absent:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

8:30 a.m . Call To Order:

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of approving Official Canvass of the results for the August 28, 2018 Primary Election:    Rayleen Richards discussed the item.   She indicated that the Secretary of State asked us to canvas before September 7th.  She requested that the official canvas be approved as presented.
Supervisor Thompson inquired whether staff has reviewed the election thoroughly, and Ms. Richards indicated that that is correct.  Supervisor Thompsonmade a motion to approve the official canvas of the results for the August 28, 2018 Primary Election; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN: At 8:32 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Steve Williams, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  September 11, 2018

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk

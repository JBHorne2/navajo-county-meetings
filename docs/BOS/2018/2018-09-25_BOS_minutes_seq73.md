b.

Consideration of accepting/rejecting the County Engineer's recommendation to alter the traffic flow pattern within the Sky Hi Subdivision for flood mitigation purposes :  John Osgood discussed the item.  He stated that residents have been working with the County for years on ways to help reduce flooding concerns.  He indicated that over the past two years, staff has worked collaboratively with the Sky Hi property Owners Association to identify drainage control measures to provide some relief in flood situations.  He stated that the necessity to do something is evident, and the Property Owners Association deserves credit for working towards solutions.  He indicated that a number of residents on North Baron Circle expressed concerns that they were not properly notified of the work at North Skylane Drive.  He stated that we could have done more regarding notification.  He indicated that he is confident that the County is within its rights to take the drainage mitigation efforts we took and did so in the best interests of our residence; however, he feels it is in the best interests of the County and the public to have this discussion today and address any concerns that come up.

Bill Bess showed an overall map of the Sky Hi subdivision.  He spoke about the drainage.  He stated that existing culverts were causing flooding.  He spoke about the existing culverts block the drainage, cause backwater upstream and constricts the flow.  He indicated that the improvements we're looking at now of removing the culverts opened up this channel and does not allow a backwater effect.  He stated that the biggest challenge was the easements.   He indicated that Sandy Phillips has obtained 17 of the 24 easements so we can do this work, and they have also purchased lots to help with construction.  He stated that we want to create a slope in the overall channel to accelerate water leaving the subdivision.  He indicated that before they started this work, they spoke with the Pinetop Fire District to make sure they were comfortable with them altering the traffic pattern and removing one of the three access points to the subdivision, and they received no objections. Ms. Phillips spoke about a 1994 study which recommended increasing existing channel capacity by either widening or regrading the channel to a uniform slope.  She stated that they also looked at steepening the channel.  She indicated that the intent was to see how the malapai would fracture without damaging the adjacent homes.  Chairman Williams stated that the flooding has gone in Sky Hi for years, and the County had gone in on Skylane and altered the flow of traffic there by putting a barricade on either side to open up the channel so water can flow through there and begin to work on getting through the malapai and working on the slope and inquired whether that is a correct summary, and Ms. Phillips stated that that is correct.  Chairman Williams inquired regarding the notice efforts the County did or did not make regarding the flow of traffic.  Ms. Phillips stated when they started to look at this, they attended a Property Owners Association meeting where residents brought up concerns and asked for the County's help.  She indicated that she attended two more Property Owners Association meetings and presented what they could do to help the area.   She stated that for this meeting, they sent postcards to every property owner, and for previous meetings, it was posted on a billboard and on the Property Owners' website.  She stated that they did not realize that all property owners were not part of the Property Owners Association.  Mr. Bess stated that the option of a bridge and more pipes was brought up, but they would be cost prohibitive.  He indicated that this is most cost effective way to minimize flooding.  He stated that the County altered the flow of traffic in February or March.

Ron Thompson, 305 Baron Circle, complimented Ms. Phillips on the work she has done to keep the community informed.  He indicated that he voluntarily gave the County access to come over his property and do the work they needed to do.   He suggested that the community look at a road improvement district.  He expressed his support for everything that the County has done.  Brian Urin, 880 North Baron Circle, stated that due process was not followed for the road closure.  He inquired whether there are any alternatives to closing the roads.  He indicated that there seems to be no master plan for this.  He stated that he met with Brandt Clark and requested a letter showing the legal authority and has not received that.  He indicated that he does not believe the County has exhausted all of their avenues.  Bill Lucas, 612 North Skylane Road, president of the Water Board, stated that he supports whatever it takes to make his property and family safer.  Timothy Provencher, 702 North Skylane Road, current president of the Property Owners Association for Sky Hi Retreat.  He stated that there is a difference of opinion in the community on how the situation is being resolved, and he believes it is healthy for everyone to speak their minds.  He indicated that he was one of the first proponents to want to work with the County and is a proponent to have the County do what they need to do to mitigate the flooding.  He stated that he believes the next phases in order to get that slope will be a huge benefit to the community.  Judy Abers, 802 North Skylane, thanked the Board for everything they're trying to do and expressed her support.  She stated that she believes that everything that is going on is posted, and the method of finding out what's going on is simple.

Brandt Clark spoke about due process and the minimum requirement of the County.  He stated that there is a State statute that requires the County to act when the County is altering, abandoning or creating a county highway, and there is also a statute that requires certain action and notification requirements on the County when the County is abandoning a county street.  He indicated that under the statutory requirements, this would not be considered a county highway.  He indicated that there is no statutory requirement when the County alters a county-maintained street, and this street does not meet the definition of a county highway.  He stated that case law indicates that the Board of Supervisors has expansive powers and authorities to deal with county-maintained streets within their jurisdiction.  He spoke about having discussions with a Mr. Bird four or five months ago regarding the legalities of this and then Mr. Bird sent John Osgood an email asking for the legal authority; however, he cannot draft a legal opinion for a private member of the community.  He stated that there is no statutory requirement that when you alter a county street or public roadway that you have to give notice to the members of the public, and all notifications that have been done are above and beyond what is statutorily required. Chairman Williams clarified that the statute does not require notification for what we did, and Mr. Clark indicated that that is correct.  He stated that the County has complied with and has gone above and beyond what would be required, and there is nothing that requires this to come before the Board of Supervisors.

Mr. Bess stated that there is a culvert crossing on Bonanza which washed out during one event, and they need to do some more engineering.  He indicated that if they put larger culverts in there to allow more flow to come from the north-south into this channel, it would allow water to back flow into that area and spill over and cause more flooding in Baron Circle.  He stated that they will look at armoring that road surface and the slopes. Ms. Phillips stated that the peak discharge for the storms is over 2000 cfs, and any kind of obstruction on Skylane is going to back up.  She indicated that the master plan is the reason they are pursuing all of these easements and as they get these easements, they are able to look at doing more.  She stated that box culverts will still be a restriction, but they can look at it farther on down the road as the slope gets better.

Chairman Williams statedthat flooding has been the biggest concern in that neighborhood since he has been on the Board of Supervisors.  He expressed appreciation to those who allow the County to be on their land to help.  He stated that he believes the biggest concern is safety and preventing flooding in the homes and believes that most of the neighbors are in favor.  He indicated that he supports the county engineer's recommendation.  Supervisor Whiting made a motion to accept the County Engineer's recommendation to alter the traffic flow pattern within the Sky Hi Subdivision for flood mitigation purposes.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Ms. Phillips and Mr. Bess for their time in working with members of the community.  She thanked the community for coming out and sharing their perspectives.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger seconded the motion.  Chairman Williams thanked the neighbors for coming out.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

EMPLOYEE RECOGNITION :

a.

Navajo County Recognizes the following employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations:  Jana Richards  (Assessor);  George Amador (Public Works); and  Michael Tyler (Facilities Management) :  Lea Peterson recognized those employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations.  A short video was shown.  Chairman Williams  spoke about the attributes of each employee and the contributions that they make.  Supervisor Thompson  thanked those employees for the quality work that they do.

SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring May 6-12 "Correctional Officers Week" in Navajo County :  Sheriff Clark spoke about the job duties of all correctional officers and the hazards that they face every day.  He stated that we have the best correctional officers in the state.   Supervisor Thompson read the proposed proclamation.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve a Proclamation declaring May 6-12 "Correctional Officers Week"; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Recognition of Sheriff KC Clark for being named 2018 Sheriff of the Year by the WETIP Foundation :  Glenn Kephart indicated that he had the honor to be present for the award and spoke about the WeTip Foundation.  He spoke about the exemplary performance of Sheriff KC Clark which made him worthy of this award and that this is the second time Sheriff Clark has received this award.  A short video was shown.  The Board of Supervisors spoke about the sound leadership that Sheriff Clark offers his office and congratulated him.  They each expressed their appreciation to Sheriff Clark.  Sheriff Clark thanked his staff, Brad Carlyon, the Board of Supervisors and his deputies, dispatchers, staff and civilians.   Chairman Williams presented Sheriff Clark with a plaque.

b.

Recognition of Constable Phyllis Romo for receiving the "Service Above and Beyond" award from the National Constables & Marshals Association :  Supervisor Whiting  thanked Constable Romo for all that she does and congratulated her for receiving this award.  Constable Romo thanked the Board of Supervisors for the recognition.  She stated that she sits on the Arizona Constables Association Board and is the director for National Constables and Marshals Association.   She expressed gratitude to Navajo County for providing her the opportunity to train and serve.  Chairman Williams  thanked Constable Romo for her decade and a half of service.  Supervisor Thompson  thanked Constable Romo for her service to Navajo County and the kindness that she shows to all our people.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Personnel Service Awards: Congratulations to the following employees for their continued service to Navajo County:  Brandt Clark  (County Attorney)  5 years;  Richard Allen (Public Works) 5 years; Winifred Lincoln (Adult Probation) 5 years; and  Thomas Puckett (Public Works) 5 years :  Katherine Wright recognized those employees for their continued service to Navajo County.

b.

Consideration and possible approval of implementing fire restrictions in Zones 1, 2 and 3 in Navajo County :  Catrina Jenkins gave a short presentation regarding an update on this year's fire season and requested that the Board ratify going into Stage One restrictions in Zones 1, 2 and 3 in Navajo County.  Supervisor Whiting  thanked Ms. Jenkins for her work and coordinating with our partners.  Supervisor Whiting   made a motion to approve implementing fire restrictions in Zones 1, 2 and 3 in Navajo County; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Supervisor Thompson  thanked Ms. Jenkins for her work and for providing the information.  Ms. Jenkins indicated that during Stage One fire restrictions, campfires, which is a warming fire or cooking fire, is restricted to a developed campground or backyard and must be completely cleared of debris for three feet around and above.  She also stated that smoking is only allowed indoors or inside vehicles.  She indicated that these restrictions can be found on our website, and citizens should check current fire restrictions at www.firerestrictions.us.  Ms. Jenkins spoke about the media outreach.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Opportunity Zones Update :  Paul Watson spoke about the qualifications under the tax code which allow us to designate areas as an opportunity zone, which are designated census tracts which allow us to attract investors who can take capital gains and reinvest in opportunity zones and have those taxes reduced or eliminated.  He spoke about the three opportunity zones in Navajo County which were awarded by the Department of Treasury.  The Board of Supervisors thanked Mr. Watson for the update and the work that he continues to do.  Mr. Watson spoke briefly about the Arizona Commerce Authority Meeting.

COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

Presentation of the 7th Annual Sue's Crew 30 Mile Walk for Cancer to be held on May 12, 2018 :  Jim Hillebrecht spoke about the 7th Annual Sue's Crew 30-mile walk.  He stated that the monies raised go to the American Cancer Society in the White Mountains; the American Cancer Society in Cumberland, Maryland; and the Oncology Center at Summit Healthcare.  Anna Atencio spoke about the Sue's Helping Hands program.  She spoke about the application process and indicated that 10% of the monies raised goes to specific individuals who have been diagnosed with cancer.  She stated that informally, other needs have been met with others battling cancer in the White Mountains.  She indicated that information can be found at www.suescrew.org.  She stated that people can also volunteer at one of the stops or sponsor a Porta-John.  The Board of Supervisors thanked Mr. Hillebrecht and Ms. Atencio and indicated that this is very inspiring.

b.

Consideration and approval of a Memorandum of Understanding between Navajo County and Change Point to provide behavioral therapy services to victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, child abuse and child sexual abuse:   Linda Kingery discussed the item and requested approval.  She indicated that just recently, she did two interviews of children, and they and their parents were requesting assistance for therapy.  She spoke about the fiscal impact to the County.   Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger  thanked Ms. Kingery for the work that the FAC does to provide services to victims.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger   made a motion to approve a Memorandum of Understanding between Navajo County and Change Point to provide behavioral therapy services to victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, child abuse and child sexual abuse; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration and approval of Arizona Department of Public Safety Victim of Crimes Act (VOCA) grant agreement No. 2018-360 to provide services to victims of crime in Navajo County in the amount of, VOCA to fund $275,820 and Match requirement to be paid by the County in the amount of $68,955 :  Lynda Young discussed the item and reviewed the services which will be provided with this grant.  She indicated that this grant will provide over 500 direct services for victims with the Family Advocacy Center (FAC).  Brad Carlyon made comments about the services which will be available through this grant.  He spoke about a partnership with Summit Healthcare for them to do the pediatric medical exams.   Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger   made a motion to approve Arizona Department of Public Safety Victim of Crimes Act (VOCA) grant agreement No. 2018-360 to provide services to victims of crime in Navajo County in the amount of, VOCA to fund $275,820 and Match requirement to be paid by the County in the amount of $68,955; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

d.

Consideration and approval of Arizona Department of Public Safety Victim of Crimes Act (VOCA) grant agreement number 2018-361 t o provide services to victims of crime in Navajo County  in the amount of,  VOCA to fund $449,380 and Match requirement to be paid by the County in the amount of $112,346 :  Lynda Young discussed the item and reviewed the services that will be provided with this grant.  She stated that this is year one of a three-year grant.  Brad Carlyon thanked Sheriff Clark for his help on this matter.   Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger  thanked Ms. Young for her work in securing these grants to serve the victims in the County.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger   made a motion to approve Arizona Department of Public Safety Victim of Crimes Act (VOCA) grant agreement number 2018-361 to provide services to victims of crime in Navajo County in the amount of, VOCA to fund $449,380 and Match requirement to be paid by the County in the amount of $112,346; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

e.

Consideration and approval of Arizona Criminal Justice Commission's Grant Number IFBC 13-15-004 to aid in the timely reporting of case disposition and sentencing records, in the amount of $40,000 :  Lynda Young discussed the ACJC Grant which will allow the County Attorney's Office to interface their case management system with the DPS system which will aid in keeping criminal histories timely.  Supervisor Whiting   made a motion to approve Arizona Criminal Justice Commission's Grant Number IFBC 13-15-004 to aid in the timely reporting of case disposition and sentencing records, in the amount of $40,000; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

f.

Consideration and approval of Work Order from County Attorney's Case Management Vendor, LegalEdge, for the Arizona Disposition Reporting System Interface Customization for the ACJC IFBC Grant #13-15-004, in the amount of $40,000: Lynda Young discussed the item.   Supervisor Thompson   made a motion to approve a Work Order from County Attorney's Case Management Vendor, LegalEdge, for the Arizona Disposition Reporting System Interface Customization for the ACJC IFBC Grant #13-15-004, in the amount of $40,000; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Consideration of approving the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between the White Mountain Apache Tribe Division of Transportation and Navajo County for assistance in road projects :  John Osgood discussed the item and requested approval.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger  thanked Mr. Osgood for providing this MOA and for the discussion on how we can work together to strengthen the services that we have and serve our constituents.   Supervisor Thompson
made a motion  to approve the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between the White Mountain Apache Tribe Division of Transportation and Navajo County for assistance in road projects; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members : Glenn Kephart thanked the Navajo County Attorney's Office and Lynda Young for their efforts on the grants that were just approved. He further sent a shout out to all our Elected Officials during this legislative session for looking out for our interests with regard to the bills being considered by our legislators.  He stated that we are making a difference through their efforts. Mr. Carlyon discussed the possible impacts on the County to fund education.  He indicated that the National Association of Counties Western Region meeting is coming up in May and that Supervisor Tommie Martin will be the newly elected president of the Western Region Council.  He stated that registration is open for the annual conference coming up in July. Supervisor Thompson spoke about the Law and Justice Day in Dilkon.  He further spoke about the CSA and Small Counties meetings. He stated that he also attended the Sedona Transportation Organization meeting, and they are continuing to do studies on how to deal with heavy traffic. He spoke about the 5-year transportation plan presented by the State Transportation Board.  Supervisor Jack thanked his colleagues and Navajo County staff for working hard within our communities and for those projects on which they continue to work.  He stated that this week, the Navajo Nation will be meeting in Albuquerque with a potential buyer of NGS. Supervisor Whiting spoke about the Hoop Shoot National Championship which took place in Chicago and indicated that Kenadi Stuart placed 7 th  in her age group, and Mylie Demuth placed 9th in her age group.  He stated that Holbrook's baseball team beat Payson and Winslow last night and are now ranked number one in the East Region going into the playoffs. He spoke about everybody's efforts in speaking to the legislature and how we got what the Counties were pushing for in the budget that they were trying to move forward; however, with the Governor discussing teacher raises, since there is not a funding mechanism, everything else is off the table. He spoke about how the legislators need to find a solution, and it is important that we continue to communicate with our legislators. Supervisor Whitesinger spoke about the White Mountain Regional Transportation Committee meeting and priorities were set out that will be relayed to the State Transportation Board.  She stated that the last day to provide comments is June 5 at 5:00 p.m.  Chairman Williams expressed appreciation for all of the effort that has gone into the items on the agenda by team members and the continued work of supervisors and other Elected Officials and our management team.

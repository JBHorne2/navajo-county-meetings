NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, January 9, 2018

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jesse Thompson, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Steve Williams, Vice-Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:02 a.m . Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Vice-Chairman Williams .

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Election of Chairman and Vice-Chairman for 2018:   Supervisor Whiting made a motion to elect Steve Williams as Chairman and Dawnafe Whitesinger as Vice-Chairwoman; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Chairman Williams expressed appreciation to his fellow Board Members for their trust in him to serve as Chairman and expressed gratitude to serve with Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger.  He stated that he hopes to represent the Board in a way that Supervisor Thompson has done.

b.

Presentation of a Plaque of Appreciation for 2017 Service to Chairman Jesse Thompson : Chairman Williams indicated that Supervisor Thompson is the longest standing supervisor in the State of Arizona for 25 years, serving as chairman six times. He stated that he has always found Supervisor Thompson to be kind and respectful and a man of integrity.  He indicated that he is better because of his association with Supervisor Thompson and that our county is better because of Supervisor Thompson.  He expressed appreciation to Supervisor Thompson for being Chairman this past year.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed appreciation to the Board for electing her as vice-chairwoman and stated that she looks forward to the coming year.  She expressed her gratitude to Supervisor Thompson for being an example to her on how to be a public servant and thanked him for all his service to the Board and Navajo County. Supervisor Whiting indicated that this is the second time he has been on the Board under Supervisor Thompson's leadership. He stated that Supervisor Thompson works tirelessly in his efforts to represent the people and works well with all of the other elected officials.  He stated that he appreciates Supervisor Thompson's service and the manner in which he leads. Supervisor Jack commended Supervisor Thompson for the many years that he has served and expressed appreciation for the great things he has done in partnering the Navajo Nation and Navajo County with our transportation issues and that Supervisor Thompson continues to impress him. Glenn Kephart thanked Supervisor Thompson and congratulated Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger and Chairman Williams on their appointment. He indicated that it was helpful and comforting coming in as a new manager under the breadth of experience of Supervisor Thompson and that he is also a caring person. The Board presented Supervisor Thompson with a plaque of appreciation. Supervisor Thompson indicated that this Board, the Elected Officials, Department Directors and staff all work as a team. He stated that he is proud of all of the Supervisors. He indicated that we all have our own thoughts and ideas on how to deal with certain issues, and we all work together. He indicated that he has concerns for each of our Board members and wants them to take care of themselves emotionally, spiritually and be in good health.  He stated that many challenges will come our way, but we need to stay in good health, and he hopes that our Creator will continue to bless us.  Supervisor Thompson asked to be excused by 10:00 to attend a funeral.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC :  There were no comments from the public.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation to retiring employee, Lynn Decker, Juvenile Probation :  Eric Scott recognized Lynn Decker for over 32 years of service to Navajo County.  He stated that Mr. Decker is a man of character and did a great job working with Juvenile Probation.  Lynda Wilson recognized Mr. Decker and spoke about his work with Navajo County Probation.  She stated that we will miss Mr. Decker's insight and integrity.  Mr. Decker expressed gratitude for his time at Navajo County and indicated that he appreciated the opportunity.
Chairman Williams indicated that the juvenile system is rehabilitative in nature, and he can only imagine the impact that Mr. Decker has had for three decades.  He thanked Mr. Decker for his dedicated service and what he has done for Navajo County.  Mr. Scott presented Mr. Decker with a set of coasters as a token of gratitude.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager : Supervisor Thompson reported that he will be going down to Phoenix for the Tribal Transportation Unity Caucus Meeting and State Legislative Day.   He indicated that they have some legislation for the Reservation to be introduced to identify some legislative bills. Supervisor Whiting thanked Supervisor Thompson for his service, and stated that he looks forward to the leadership of Chairman Williams and Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger.  He spoke of the LPC meetings that will begin this Friday, and he will keep everybody aware of what is happening. Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger gave a special accolade to Representative Descheenie for his invitation to meet with him on the floor yesterday for opening session. She reported that over the Christmas break, we were able to give over 200 jackets to troubled children with the partnership of Three Precious Miracles from the Valley and Bii'Nalwod.  She thanked Sheriff Clark for lending his Santa suit and thanked everyone from Navajo County who donated jackets. Chairman Williams indicated that the 5 Board of Supervisors will be in Phoenix next week meeting with several of our State legislators.

One Stop Operator Agreement 
 
 
This Agreement is by and between the Northeastern Arizona Local Workforce Development 
Board (hereinafter referred to as the LWDB), constituted under terms of the Workforce 
Innovation and Opportunity Act of 2014 (WIOA), and the designated One Stop Operator for the 
LWDB. 
 
Purpose 
 
The purpose of the Agreement shall be to designate Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona 
as the LWDB’s One Stop Operator as provided for under WIOA Section 121 (d), and to describe 
in general terms the roles and responsibilities of the parties to the Agreement, procedures for 
modifications of Agreement, compliance requirements and other related purposes. 
 
Navajo County, as the Chief Elected Official for the LWDB has the authority to enter into this 
Agreement under the provisions of A.R.S. § 11-952. 
 
Terms of Agreement 
 
This Agreement shall become effective November 16, 2018 and shall remain in force until it 
terminates on June 30, 2020, unless circumstances require modified Agreement prior to the 
termination date. 
 
Termination of Agreement 
 
Any party to the Agreement may withdraw from the Agreement by giving written notice of 
intent ninety (90) days in advance of the effective withdrawal date.  Notice of withdrawal shall 
be given in writing to all parties of the Agreement.  
 
The Agreement may also be terminated in the event that grant funding upon which this 
Agreement depends is withdrawn by the State of Arizona or the federal government.   
 
Notice is also hereby given that this agreement is subject to cancellation pursuant to the 
provisions of A.R.S. § 38-511 in the event of the discovery of a conflict of interest.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) 
Between 
Navajo County Public Health Services District, Arizona Health Zone SNAP-ED Program 
and 
Southwest Food Service Excellence LLC 
and 
Window Rock Unified School District 
 
 
 
This Memorandum of Agreement (“MOA”) is between the Navajo County Public Health Services District, 
by and through its Arizona Health Zone SNAP-ED Program (“the Health District”), Southwest Food Service 
Excellence LLC (“Southwest”), and Window Rock Unified School District of Apache County (“the School 
District”). 
 
I. PURPOSE & SCOPE 
 
The purpose of this MOA is to clearly identify the roles and responsibilities of said parties related to 
The collaboration in increased fruit and vegetable consumption, physical activity levels and address food 
safety for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) eligible families in Navajo and Apache 
Counties. 
 
II. BACKGROUND 
 
The Arizona Health Zone SNAP-ED Program is administered by Navajo County Public Health Services 
District. The purpose of the Program is to increase fruit and vegetable consumption, increase physical 
activity levels, provide federal nutrition food service information to SNAP eligible youth and families 
throughout Apache County.  
 
The school district operates seven schools within its boundaries. Southwest is a private company that 
provides nutrition consulting and planning services to the School District and, in cooperation with the School 
District, serves approximately 2,240 participants within the unincorporated community of Window Rock, 
AZ. The mission of Southwest Food Service LLC is to equip preschool to adult participants and families 
with the skills, knowledge, and attitudes they will need to pursue their life goals responsibly and creatively in 
a rapidly changing society.

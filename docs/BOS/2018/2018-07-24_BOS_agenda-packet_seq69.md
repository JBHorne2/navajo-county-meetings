noticing the addition of the STOP sign.
Navajo County does not maintain South Woodside Trail and the STOP sign will be installed
within the existing Right-of-Way of White Mountain Lake Road.

Re:  City of Show Low, Arizona – Application for BUILD Funding – Scott Ranch Road and 
Bridge Project 
 
Dear Secretary Chao: 
 
I write today in support of the City of Show Low’s application requesting $9,891,988 Better 
Utilizing Investments to Leverage Development (BUILD) Discretionary Grant funding for 
construction of the Scott Ranch Road and Bridge Project.  The project will establish a bridge and 
roadway over Show Low Creek at the heart of the region’s commercial district and increase 
accessibility to Summit Regional Medical Center which responds to the healthcare needs of more 
than 90,000 permanent and seasonal residents in Navajo and Apache counties, annually.  In 
addition, the project will reduce congestion on State Route 260 by creating a more direct route to 
the region’s primary business and commercial district.   
 
Transportation improvement needs in this area are identified in the 2007 Southern 
Navajo/Apache County Sub-Regional Transportation Plan, which recognizes Scott Ranch Road 
and Bridge Project as a key element of the region’s transportation improvement plan.  The 
purpose of this project is to provide an east-west connection between SR260 and Penrod Road 
with a bridge over Show Low Creek and connecting roadway to improve traffic circulation, 
promote economic development throughout the region and create sustainable jobs for local 
residents. 
 
The Scott Ranch Road and Bridge Project is a regionally significant roadway and bridge 
connection project that will positively impact the entire region by linking the Show Low 
Micropolitan Statistical Area with important medical, economic and retail centers throughout the 
Navajo County region.  It will connect two major north-south roadways and improve multimodal 
connectivity within the region’s largest population centers for: 
 
• Continued economic development and improved quality of life for local residents. 
• Direct accessibility to the region’s primary commerce district and medical community. 
• Safety to enhance emergency services response time and provide an alternative route 
during forced evacuations.

This grant award will provide immediate and direct benefits in two ways: 1) improve critical 
infrastructure for the region, and, 2) provide much-needed jobs for residents. 
 
I request that you give the City of Show Low’s BUILD application all due consideration.

Motion before the Board:
Ambulance Dispatching Agreement between the Sheriff's Office and Holbrook EMS
Background:
The Sheriff's Office operates a dispatch center which includes a 911 public safety answering
point.  Presently the Sheriff's Office dispatches ambulance service calls to Holbrook EMS.  The
Sheriff's Office and Holbrook EMS wish to continue this practice.  It is mutually agreed that the
Sheriff's Office will provide dispatching of Holbrook EMS ambulance units on a 24 hr basis and
Holbrook EMS will pay to Navajo County a fee of Thirty Dollars ($30) per call.
The fee will be paid in quarterly payments and invoiced within 30 days at the end of each quarter. 
This agreement will end June 30, 2019 and shall be automatically renewed for additional one-year
periods unless terminated.

AMBULANCE DISPATCHING AGREEMENT 
 
 
 THIS AGREEMENT is entered into as of the __ day of July, 2018, between 
Navajo County, a Political Subdivision of the State of Arizona, ("COUNTY") and 
Holbrook, EMS, ("HOLBROOK EMS"). 
 
WITNESSETH: 
 
 WHEREAS the Navajo County Sheriff’s Office has authority pursuant to A.R.S. 
§11-441 to es tablish and regulate the law enforcement within t he COUNTY and to 
prescribe their powers and duties, and carries out twenty -four (24) hour dispatch 
services in that regard; and  
 
 WHEREAS th e Navajo County Sheriff’s Office operated a 911 Public Safety 
Answering Point Center and is responsible for answering all 911 emergency telephone 
calls in throughout Navajo County; and 
 
 WHEREAS the Navajo County Sheriff’s Office presently dispatches calls  for 
ambulance services to HOLBROOK EMS , and the Navajo County Sheriff’s Office and 
HOLBROOK EMS jointly desire to continue this practice; and 
 
 NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, 
the parties mutually agree to jointly exercise their common powers as follows: 
 
1. Purpose.  This agreement is made to provide for dispatching of HOLBROOK 
EMS Ambulance units on a twenty -four (24) hour basis in the HOLBROOK EMS 
service area. 
 
2. Service.  COUNTY hereby agrees to dispatch call s received by the Navajo 
County Sheriff’s Office for ambulance service within the HOLBROOK EMS 
service area to HOLBROOK EMS authorized personnel on a twenty -four (24) 
hour basis. 
 
3. Compensation and Budget .  HOLBROOK EMS agrees to pay the COUNTY for 
services rendered for calls of service. As full and complete compensation for the 
services to be provided hereunder, HOLBROOK EMS shall pay to NAVAJO 
COUNTY a fee of Thirty Dollars ($30.00) per call. Said fee shall be paid in 
quarterly payments, within thirty d ays following invoicing at the end of each 
quarter. 
 
 A call for service shall be defined as any call where a field unit is sent to a 
location of an incident or to assist a citizen, or any time a field unit initiates 
activity that would, otherwise, have resulted in the dispatch of a field unit. 
 
HOLBROOK EMS shall be responsible for all radio equipment costs, including 
acquisition and maintenance of radio equipment specifically required to dispatch 
its agency, in addition to all radio receiving equipment.  Said equipment shall be

<!-- page 18 -->
returned to HOLBROOK EMS in the event this Agreement is terminated for any 
reason. 
 
4. Adjustments to Compensation .  The compensation referred to in paragraph 3 
above may be adjusted annually based upon the actual costs to COUNTY.  This 
adjustment shall be calculated as of January 1 of each calendar year based upon 
the change in the actual costs over the past calendar year. The parties will jointly 
calculate the change, if any, in the compensation early enough in the calendar 
year so that the adjusted compensation figure can be included in the fiscal year 
budget process for approval. The adjusted compensation will be effective upon 
the new fiscal year to which it applies.   Any adjustments to compensation shall 
be as mutually agreed upon by the parties.   
 
5. Duration.  The initial term of this Agreement shall end June 30, 2019.  This 
Agreement shall be automatically renewed for additional one -year periods unless 
terminated. Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing writte n notice 
to the other party at least one hundred and twenty (120) days before the effective 
date of termination. In the event of termination, the fee payable pursuant to 
paragraph 3 shall be prorated through the effective date of termination. In 
addition, the COUNTY may suspend the performance of dispatch services during 
any period when HOLBROOK EMS has failed to make payment as required by 
paragraph 3. 
 
 
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have entered this Agreement the day and year 
first written above.

guidelines in Workforce Ari zona Council Policy 01-2016 entitled Workforce Innovation and 
Opportunity Act (WIOA) Local Governance.  
 
The CEO must appoint business representatives that meet the criteria established by the 
Workforce Arizona Council on behalf of the Governor.  
 
The authority to appoint members to the LWDB lies solely with the CEO. 
 
A. Terms 
LWDB members are appointed for a fixed and staggered 3 -year term. Members may be 
reappointed upon completion of their term.

LWDB appointments must be made by the Navajo Board of Supervisors as designated CEO.

LWDB members who no longer hold the position or status that made them eligible LWDB 
members must resign or be removed by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors as 
designated CEO immediately upon notification to the LWDB Chair of the change of status as 
a representative of that entity. 
 
E. Mid-Term Appointment 
LWDB members replacing out-going members mid-term will serve the remainder of the 
out-going member term. 
 
F. Vacancies 
A minimum of four business representatives from each county will serve on the LWDB. 
That balance in LWDB representation shall be maintained when filling vacancies. 
Recommended nominees shall be submitted by the appropriate organizations to the Navajo 
County Board of Supervisors, as designated CEO, for formal appointment. LWDB vacancies 
must be filled within 120 days of the vacancy. Navajo County Board of Supervisors, as 
designated CEO, is authorized to make all reappointments of members. Reappointments 
must be made within 120 days of the term expiration. In the event a vacancy cannot be 
filled within 120 days, the Administrative Entity must request a waiver in writing to the 
Director of the Workforce Arizona Council with an explanation of why a vacancy was not 
filled in the 120-day timeframe and a description of the process underway to fill the 
vacancy. The Administrative Entity must maintain written approval of the waiver request 
by the Director of the Workforce Arizona Council and will be monitored according to the 
process outlined in their approved waiver request.

LWDB members must be removed by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors, as

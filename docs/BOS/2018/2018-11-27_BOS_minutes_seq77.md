c.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring November 2018 as "Native American Heritage Month":   Supervisor Thompson stated that every Native American is proud of who they are and their tradition and culture and how they have contributed to the growth of America and their communities.  He indicated that today we are celebrating Native American month and that Navajo County has the honor of having three Native American communities:  Navajo Nation, Hopi and the White Mountain Apache Tribe.  He spoke about the many contributions that Native Americans have made to the rest of the world in many fields, including agriculture, medicine, music, dance, language and art and have also served with valor in all of the wars from the Revolutionary War, World War II as Code Talkers, to the current conflicts.  He stated that as we reflect on the sacrifices by our ancestors, he gives thanks to Native American leaders currently serving and those who have served before and encouraged all people to celebrate their heritage, to give thanks to ancestors and a future where all children receive access to quality education and expanded school choices on tribal lands.  He spoke about striving to improve roads and to preserve the land, water and mineral rights in building up the Native American communities.  He stated that he celebrates the Native American Nations, Tribes, villages, chapters and communities for their respect of Mother Earth and natural resources.  He spoke about continuing to maintain the values and practices of our forefathers as peaceful and humble caretakers of our lands, cultures, language and religion.  He read the proposed proclamation.
Supervisor Jack made a motion to approve a Proclamation declaring November 2018 as "Native American Heritage Month"; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vice - Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Supervisor Thompson for making certain that we recognize Native Americans in our county.  She spoke about the struggles and challenges which face Native Americans.  She further spoke about the contributions of Native American tribes which have enhanced our communities.  Chairman Williams stated he respects and appreciates his colleagues on the Board of Supervisors for their work in their communities and expressed gratitude for living in a region with diverse cultures.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Acceptance of Sheriff Clark's Notice of Retirement effective February 12, 2019 and future appointment of David Clouse upon his retirement :  Sheriff Clark stated it his intent is to retire on February 12th.  He requested that the Board consider a motion with the intent to appoint David Clouse.  He spoke about the leadership qualities of Sergeant Clouse.  Supervisor Whiting thanked Sheriff Clark for his service.  He stated that it has been an honor and a privilege working with Sheriff Clark and that he values the relationship.  He stated that he admires and appreciates the work that Sergeant Clouse does and the leader that he is.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Sheriff Clark for his service and what she has learned from him about being a leader.  She expressed gratitude for the opportunity to spend time with Sergeant Clouse and stated that he has a servant's heart.  Chairman Williams stated that this is a well-earned step, and Sheriff Clark has served the County faithfully for three decades.  He stated he respects the work that Sheriff has done.  He stated that he does not believe it's proper to make a formal motion at this time on the acceptance or future appointment but to wait until the February 12 date, and Mr. Carylon confirmed.  Chairman Williams stated that the Board gives great weight to the recommendation of outgoing elected officials as to who they believe should be appointed in their place.  Supervisor Jack thanked Sheriff Clark for his service to the Native American communities.   Supervisor Thompson stated that the Native American community has gotten to know the Sheriff, and they gave him a name of Dark Brown Moustache.  He expressed appreciation to Sheriff Clark.  Sheriff Clark stated that Sergeant Clouse has a long history with the Sheriff's Office as his grandfather was the Sheriff who hired Sheriff Clark,  and his great-grandfather was a deputy.  Sergeant Clouse thanked the Board for the vote of confidence and kind words.  He stated that Sheriff Clark has been a great mentor over the past ten years and appreciates everything that Sheriff Clark does.  He stated that Sheriff Clark has worked tirelessly over the past 32 years on behalf of Navajo County.  He stated that he commits to work with the Board, work with the County and will put the County first as Sheriff.  He thanked Sheriff Clark for his confidence to fulfill his commitment.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of Resolution Number 31-18 , amending the Navajo County Addenda and Additions to the 2015 International Building Code and the 2015 International Residential Code, with an effective date of June 11, 2019 :  John Osgood discussed the item. He introduced Rick Denton, Rob Douglas and Donald Perkins indicating that they have worked on this item, along with Bill Bess and Sandra Phillips. He stated that throughout this year, the building industry professionals have been made aware of this item and have been given the opportunity to make comments and changes have been made based on those comments. He spoke about the key objectives of updating the building codes from a risk management standpoint and from the perspective of our building division staff. He further spoke of the next steps, which would be to study the 2018 International Building Code (IBC) and International Residential Code (IRC) for potential adoption in the future. Mr. Osgood recommended approval of the addenda and additions document which includes adoption of the 2015 versions of the IBC and IRC to be effective June 11 of 2019. Supervisor Thompson commended the Public Works staff for completing multiple tasks on top of their regular duties in order to implement this. Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired as to how many projects is this going to impact, and Rick Denton indicated that we have about 1200 permits this year; however, the current permits would stay under the 2003 code, and then any new permits issued from today forward would go under the 2015 code. Chairman Williams indicated that what he is hearing is that there has been conversations with the construction industry and fire departments with no resistance to updating the codes. John Osgood indicated that there have been many conversations and interaction and some of those comments have been incorporated. Chairman Williams inquired regarding some of the highlights of things that will change.  Robert Douglas indicated that wall integrity, more prescriptive methods for the community, more square footage, and drywall got better.  He indicated that this would put us above State standards. Supervisor Jack thanked Mr. Osgood and his team for the work that they've put into this.  Mr. Denton stated that this code is specifically for residential and commercial building.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 31-18 , amending the Navajo County Addenda and Additions to the 2015 International Building Code and the 2015 International Residential Code, with an effective date of June 11, 2019; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

positions:
“If only one person files or no person files a nominating petition for election to fill a position on the board of 
directors of the district, the county board of supervisors, by resolution, may cancel the election for that office and 
appoint the person who filed the nominating petition to fill that position. If no person files a nominating petition for an 
election to fill a district board office, the county board of supervisors, by resolution, may cancel the election for those 
offices and those offices are deemed vacant and shall be filled as otherwise provided by law. A person who is 
appointed pursuant to this section is fully vested with the powers and duties of the office as if elected to that office.”
         and

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING 
(MOU) BETWEEN
NAVAJO COUNTY
AND
NORTHEAST ARIZONA FAMILY RESOURCE CENTER DBA FAMILY ADVOCACY CENTER
WHEREAS, Navajo County, Navajo County’s Attorney Office and the Northeast Arizona Family 
Resource Center dba Family Advocacy Center have come together to collaborate and to make an 
application for OVW Fiscal Year 2018 Rural Sexual Assault, Domestic Violence, Dating Violence, 
and Stalking Program Solicitation grant; and
WHEREAS, the partners listed below have agreed to enter into a collaborative agreement in which 
Navajo County Attorney’s Office will be the lead agency and named applicant and the other 
agencies will be partners in this application; and
WHEREAS, the partners herein desire to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding setting forth 
the services to be provided by the collaborative; and
WHEREAS, the application prepared and approved by the collaborative through its partners is to 
be submitted to the Office on Violence Against Women on or before January 31, 2018;
1.0 PARTIES
1.1 This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) provides a binding agreement 
between Navajo County through the Navajo County Attorney’s Office 
(hereinafter referred to as “County”) and Northeast Arizona Family 
Resource Center dba Family Advocacy Center (hereinafter referred to as 
“FAC”) for their respective responsibilities and rights in providing services 
to victims of sexual assault, domestic violence, dating violence, and 
stalking. Navajo County is authorized to enter into this MOU under A.R.S. 
§ 11-952 for the purposes of exercising joint powers or contracting for 
services. FAC is empowered by A.R.S. § 11-951 to enter into this 
Agreement and has authorized the undersigned to execute this Agreement 
on behalf of FAC. County and FAC may collectively at times hereinafter be 
referred to as the “Parties,” or each individually as a “Party.”
1.2 The FAC was established in 2011 to provide a coordinated, multidisciplinary 
and humane approach to cases of child abuse and sexual assault. The FAC 
offers a welcoming homelike atmosphere, and puts children at ease when 
conducting forensic interviews; this in turn increases the effectiveness of 
the investigative process. The FAC is a safe, secure, and caring 
environment that focuses on the needs of the victims they serve, and assist 
in addressing and overcoming the trauma of their experience. 
1.3 The Navajo County Attorney’s Office is an Arizona Constitutionally created 
office and was created when Arizona received Statehood in 1912.

<!-- page 47 -->
2
2.0 PURPOSE
2.1 This Agreement outlines the responsibilities of each of the Parties as to their 
respective responsibilities and rights in providing victims, across their life 
span, with the protection and services they need to pursue safe and healthy 
lives, while improving communities’ capacity to provide justice for victims 
and hold offenders accountable. The Parties agree to cooperate and assist 
each other in meeting the mutual obligations and duties of each office 
because it is good public policy and benefits the citizens of Navajo County 
and of the individuals who utilize the services of the FAC.
3.0 PARTNERSHIP
3.1 Parties will continue to work with Winslow Police Department, Holbrook 
Police Department, Snowflake Police Department, Show Low Police 
Department, Pinetop-Lakeside Police Department, Navajo County Sheriff’s 
Office, Family Advocacy Center, Alice’s Place (Domestic Violence Shelter), 
White Mountain Safe House (Domestic Violence Shelter), and the Family 
Advocacy Center to ensure the goals and objectives of this grant are met.  
4.0 TERM OF AGREEMENT:
4.1 Agreement shall become effective upon approval and execution by the 
Parties for a period of five (5) years. Upon mutual agreement between the 
parties, this agreement may be renewed up to two (2) additional five-year 
terms.
5.0 RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTIES
5.1 COUNTY Responsibilities:
5.1.1 Provide all upfront expenses to be reimbursed. 
5.1.2 Provide two dedicated County Attorney Prosecutors, that specialize 
in sexual offenses, to oversee and prosecute sex based offenses. 
5.1.3 Provide onsite personnel to monitor in-person interviews while they 
are conducted. 
5.1.4 Coordinate investigatory efforts between multi-jurisdictional law 
enforcement agencies for sexual assault, domestic violence, dating 
violence, and sexual stalking.  
5.1.5 Provides office space utilized by the FAC and the Prosecuting 
Attorneys and project staff.
5.1.6 Provide training to law enforcement agency personnel as it relates 
to sexual assault, domestic violence, dating violence, and sexual 
stalking. 
5.1.7 Ensure the information obtained from forensic interviews is used by 
law enforcement in investigation efforts.

3
5.1.8 Assure that the services provided at the FAC are coordinated 
with and integrated into the overarching purpose of this MOU. 
5.2 FAC Responsibilities:
5.2.1 Provide one forensic interviewer who will work with victims of 
sexual assault, domestic violence, dating violence, and sexual 
stalking. 
5.2.2 Provide office space and interview rooms for members of law 
enforcement, social services, and the County Attorney’s office to 
watch forensic interviews live, when possible. Alternatively, provide 
viewing rooms for these individuals to watch the recorded interview
when live viewing is not practical.
5.2.3 Provide information obtained from sexual assault, domestic 
violence, dating violence, and sexual stalking investigations to the 
County Attorney’s domestic violence attorneys for prosecution 
efforts. 
5.2.4 Assure that the services provided at the Center are coordinated 
with and integrated into the overarching purpose of this MOU.
5.3 Commitment to Partnership:

3.21 Policy:  
Sick leave is authorized time off from work by an eligible employee for illness, injury, or 
for doctor, dental and health care provider appointments and other medical purposes 
relating to the employee or his/her immediate family member.  Sick leave is not to be 
considered a substitute for annual leave. and is not an entitlement.  
3.22 Coverage:  
This policy applies to all classified and unclassified positions.  Nothing in this policy 
modifies or waives the “at will” status of an unclassified employee.
3.23 Eligibility: 
A. All County employees are eligible for sick leave with pay. except the following:
1. regular full-time and part-time employees who have been employed fewer 
than 30 days;
2. regular part-time employees hired after January 1, 2014 who work fewer 
than 1,560 hours per year;
3. temporary, emergency, volunteer and seasonal workers;
3.24 Sick Leave Requests:
A. In order to receive compensation while absent due to sudden illness or injury, an 
employee shall contact and request for sick leave from his/her Department 
Director, Elected Official or immediate supervisor prior to, or within one hour of the 
beginning of his/her regular work day or shift.  The employee must follow-up by 
submitting a Leave Request form to the Department Director, Elected Official or 
immediate supervisor upon return to work.  
B. The employee shall submit a Leave Request form to the supervisor well in advance 
of any scheduled treatment or doctor's appointment.
C. Failure to notify the responsible supervisor may result in denial of sick leave, and/or 
disciplinary action up to and including discharge.
D. An employee may request to use accrued annual leave after sick leave is 
exhausted.
3.25 Sick Leave Accrual:
A. All eligible, full-time employees will earn 12 days annually or 8 hours of sick leave 
per month.  Earned sick leave hours are credited to an employee two pay periods 
each month in accordance with the annual schedule distributed by Payroll.

Title: Lot Purchase for Flood Improvement Project
Submitted For: Bill Bess, County Engineer/Assistant Public Works Director 
Submitted By: Sandra Phillips, Assistant County Engineer/Flood Control Manager
Department: Public Works
Presented By: Sandy Phillips 
Motion before the Board:
Consideration of the purchase of Lot 222 of Sky-Hi Retreat #2 for Flood Improvement Project for
the amount of $4,500.
Background:
Sky-Hi was subdivided in the 60's and the drainage channels that were constructed were not
covered by a drainage easement.  The County has been working with the residents to correct this
situation.  This lot does not have a buildable area and does not percolate for septic.  The Flood
Control District is investigating the option of opening this channel crossing, by removing the
culverts in front of this lot, which would help the flooding in this subdivision.  If this roadway is
made a dead end, a cul-de-sac at the end for vehicles to turn around would be needed.  The
purchase of this lot would accommodate this cul-de-sac.  County Staff has confirmed with Timber
Mesa that this is a viable option as there are two (2) other roadways to access the northern portion
of this subdivision.
The appraisal for this lot came in at $3,000; however, the property owners have been offered
$6,000 from a private party.  Therefore, staff feels the purchase price of $4,500 is reasonable.
Recommendation: 
Staff recommends the purchase of this lot by the Flood Control District in the amount of $4,500

Neighborhood Description
Subject is located outside of the incorporated limits of the Town of Pinetop-Lakeside, but the location is referred to as Pinetop.  Not being within
the incorporated limits of Pinetop-Lakeside, it is under the jurisdiction of Navajo County for zoning, police and fire protection, street
maintenance, and other government services. 
Subject is located in the single-family development of Ski-Hi-Retreat.  It is a small subdivision surrounded by the Apache-Sitgreaves National
Forest.  The subdivision appeals to those using the property on a seasonal basis as well as year-round those wanting year-round occupancy. 
Numerous houses were moved onto the site from other locations, but they are not manufactured, as deed restrictions do not allow manufactured
dwellings.  This is not a tract development, so all properties have custom improvements which created a wide variety of housing designs, gross
living areas, quality of construction, and other physical features.  Because of the wide array of houses, the development does not have a true,
defined predominant value.  All properties were constructed at different times so there is not a true, defined predominant age. 
Site Description
A public sanitary waste system is not available to this development, so septic tank systems are in use as the typical for waste disposal system. 
However, the appraiser confirmed with the listing agent that a percolation test was performed on the lot, but it did not pass the requirements set
forth by Navajo County.  Since the test did not pass, a building permit would not be issued for a residential structure.  Another building could be
constructed, but could not have plumbing facilities requiring an on-site sanitary waste system.  The information regarding the percolation
provided by the listing agent is assumed to be correct, but if not, the appraiser reserves the right to review the appraisal and amend as
necessary. 
Because the property will not percolate, it would be necessary to install an alternate waste system such as a sand mound system, but the cost
of this type of system, and others, is not financially feasible for this lot, as these systems can cost from $15,000 to $25,000 depending upon
various factors such as soil conditions and size of the system required.
Subject is also adjacent to and encompasses a drainage channel at the rear which diverts water away from the interior of the development. Per
the aerial map provided by Navajo County, a portion of the lot encompasses a significant portion of the lot rendering the majority of it
non-useable.  The topography of the useable area appears mostly level, but without physical evidence, it is unknown if water would drain off.
Comments on Market Data
Sales of unimproved lots have been limits in the development.  Listings are not considered in excess, but are more prevalent than sales. 
Properties have sold adjacent to the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest that are the most appealing and have a superior marketability and
values than those not adjacent to the forest.  
Research of MLS records dating back to 2014 reported only 2 closed sales and there are 3 current active listings with 1 being the Subject.  List
prices for the other 2 are $28,700 and $29,500.  Both have been on the market for approximately 6 months.  In driving the development, several
other lots are for sale by the owner outside of MLS.  The overall quantity of listings is considered in excess of the demand.
The appraiser completed research for any sales of properties in the regional market that would not percolate in an attempt to support a value
difference compared to those that would.  As best as could be determined, there is a 50% difference between one that would percolate
compared to one that would not.  Lots that do not percolate have limited marketability and a longer marketing time unless an adjacent property
owner would purchase one so to add to the size of their existing property.
Other sales viewed:Lots 126 (.29 ac) & 137 (.25 ac), adjacent lots, sold in 10/2017 for $34,000.  Lots 54 & 54A (.32 ac) sold in 01/2017 for
$43,000.  This is a heavily wooded lot adjacent to the national forest. 
All passed a percolation test. Comparable #2 is adjacent to the national forest which creates added privacy, appeal, and value.
All comparables applied are dated, but time adjustments could not be proven because the overall data is just too limited to support any upward
or downward movement in value.
The subject lot has approximately 60%-70% if total area that is not useable attributed to the drainage channel and it does not percolate, both of
which significantly restrict the utility, thus the value.  Adjustments are applied for the lack of percolation and loss of utility.
As typical, the lots (as all) sold for different prices, thus adjusted to different values.  In reconciling the comparables, Comparable #3 is given
most weight because it has the lowest combined adjustments.  It is reported that no lots were found having sold with the exact same issues,
thus there is a degree of inaccuracy in the opinion of value.  However, the appraiser is considering that the subject lot has a significant lack of
utility that would not allow the construction of a residential dwelling or significant utility/auxiliary structure, so the value is highly affected.  Placing
most weight on Comparable #3 the opinion of value by this approach is$3,000.

EMPLOYEE RECOGNITION :

a.

Navajo County Recognizes the following employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations: Salli O'Hair  (Sheriff);  Sandra Wagner (Assessor); and  Allan Loen (Public Works):    Lea Peterson recognized those employees for their excellence in personal performance far exceeding organization expectations.  A short video was shown.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed appreciation tothose employees for their dedication.  Chairman Williams spoke about the qualities of each employee and expressed his appreciation to them.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation to Adam Henderson, Blue Ridge High School, for winning the Division III State Wrestling Championship in his weight class:   Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger recognized Adam Henderson for winning the Division III State Wrestling Championship in his weight class.  Chairman Williams indicated that Adam is also in the running for Valedictorian.  He further spoke of Adam's accomplishments.  Adam addressed the Board regarding his favorite takedown and indicated that he's learned through wrestling that everything takes time and commitment.  He also thanked the Board for the recognition.

b.

Presentation to Breck Williams and Nik White, Show Low Junior High School, for winning the 2017-2018 State Junior High School wrestling title in their respective weight classes :  Chairman Williams recognized both Show Low Junior High School winners and Show Low High School winners.  He spoke about the team and the coaches.  Breck Williams and Nik White addressed the Board regarding their favorite takedown and indicated that hard work and dedication is what they have learned most from wrestling.

c.

Presentation to Jackson Allen and Westin Keime, Show Low High School, for winning the State Wrestling Championship in their respective weight class :  Chairman Williams recognized Westin Keime for winning the State Wrestling Championship in his weight class and spoke of Westin's football career, as well.  Westin addressed the Board regarding his favorite takedown and indicated that he has learned from wrestling that you will never regret the hard work.  Chairman Williams recognized Jackson Allen for winning the State Wrestling Championship in his weight class and indicated that this is Jackson's fourth championship.  Jackson addressed the Board regarding his favorite takedown and indicated that he has learned from wrestling the benefit of hard work, patience and consistency.  Chairman Williams recognized the parents, as well.  Coach Williams spoke of the student athletes and that their goal as coaches is to create good young men with character.  Supervisor Jack thanked the young men who have achieved greatness in their sport and congratulated the parents and coaches.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :  Glenn Kephart indicated that Ken Dewitt, Navajo County IT Director, has been reappointed to represent County Supervisor Association on the Supreme Court Commission on Technology.  Supervisor Jack spoke about Winslow High School boys' basketball winning the State 3A Championship last night.  He thanked staff for all of the work that they do.  He indicated that he has been visiting the communities in his district and informing them regarding different bills which we support and oppose.  Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger reported on a conference she attended entitled Day for Visionaries and Innovators.  She stated that she was impressed and empowered by what is possible and can be created.  Chairman Williams spoke about the legislature continuing to be in session, and we are continuing to work to communicate with our legislators and champion those bills which benefit our community and oppose those bills which cause difficulty for us.  He further spoke about the upcoming NACo Conference being held in Washington D.C.  Chairman Williams thanked our County Manager, elected officials, employees and team members.

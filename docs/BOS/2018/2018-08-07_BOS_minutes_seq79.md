NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 7, 2018 *SPECIAL MEETING - TELEPHONIC

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Supervisor District I; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Steve Williams, Chairman; and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chairwoman, appeared telephonically

Absent:
Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; and Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, were present in person

11:45 a.m . Call To Order:

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of adoption of Resolution Number 14-18,  approving Poll Workers and Polling Places/Vote Centers for the Primary/General Election :  Rayleen Richards discussed the item.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 14-18 approving Poll Workers and Polling Places/Vote Centers for the Primary/General Election; motion seconded by Vice-Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :   At 11:47 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Steve Williams, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
THURSDAY, MAY 29, 2008 
 
 
9:03 A.M.   Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order. 
 
PRESENT:  Present in the Navajo County Board of Supervisors' chambers at 100 E. Carter, Holbrook, 
Arizona: Chairman J.R. DeSpain,  Lisa McKee, Elections Director and Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the 
Board.  Supervisor Jesse Thompson, Supervisor Percy Deal and Supervisor Jerry Brownlow participated 
by telephone through a speaker phone in the chambers that made all meeting discussion and a ction 
audible for participants and audience members.  Not participating:  Supervisor David Tenney. 
 
ELECTIONS:  Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the Special General 
Election conducted May 20, 2008 : Lisa McKee reviewed the resu lts of the May 20th election advising 
the City of Winslow and City of Show Low are canvassing their own elections.  Election results were 
read into the record and will be recorded.  The summary is as follows: 
 Sun Valley Fire District: James Flynn (15) and Linda Stoffer (19) elected as board members 
resulting in the successful recall of Howard Brown and Robert Marshall. 
 White Mountain Lake Fire District -both questions related to building codes passed with a 
majority vote. 
 Winslow Unified School question related to bonding passed with a majority vote. 
 Timberland Acres Domestic Water Improvement District resulted in William Deer (65) and Carol 
Prochter (55) elected to their board. 
 
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to canvass the election results as presented; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
ADJOURN: At 9:07 a.m. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by 
Chairman Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.

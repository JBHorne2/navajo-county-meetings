NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, OCTOBER 21, 2008 
 
PRESENT:  J. R. DeSpain, Chairman; Jerry Brownlow, Vice -Chairman; Percy Deal, 
Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and David Tenney, Supervisor were present. 
 
9:05 a.m. Call To Order : Supervisor Deal led the Pledge of Allegiance and offered the 
invocation. 
 
Chairman DeSpain recognized the County Treasurer, Manny Hernandez , and presented him with 
a birthday cake.  Everyone sang Happy Birthday. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Andy Jacobs of  Policy Development Group (PDG), 3636 N. Central 
Avenue #590, Phoenix, AZ 85010, spoke to the board regarding Arizona Department of 
Transportation (ADOT) statewide transportation planning. He announced that two public 
meetings will be held on November 13, 2008, at 10:00 a.m. and 5:30 p.m. in the Public Meeting 
Room at JB’s in Show Low to discuss the ADOT statewide transportation framework studies, a 
30-50 year look at transportation needs across the state.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES 
FRIDAY, MAY 23, 2008 
 
1:06 P.M.   Call to Order 
 
PRESENT:: J. R. DeSpain, Chairman; Tim Zaremba, Emergency Management; and Darlene Fraley, 
Clerk of the Board were present in the chambers.  Percy Deal, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; 
David Tenney, Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Vice -Chairman, participated by telephone through a 
speaker phone in the chambers that made all meeting discussion and action audible for participants and 
audience members. 
 
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT :  Consideration and possible action to rescind the May 20, 2008 
Board of Supervisors decision to implement fire restrictions for Zones 2 and 3 in Navajo County :   Tim 
Zaremba advised that yesterday afternoon the Apache/Sitgreaves Forests resci nded fire restrictions and 
it is his recommendation to follow the lead of the forest service. Jerry Brownlow made a motion  to 
rescind the May 20, 2008 Board of Supervisors' decision to implement fire restrictions for Zones 2 and 3 
in Navajo County; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson.  Supervisor Tenney inquired if there is 
any indication when the Forest Service will again put fire restrictions in place and Mr. Zaremba stated 
there is no tentative date.  Supervisor Brownlow advised he and Supervisor Tenn ey will meet with Fire 
Chiefs Tuesday at 10:00 at the Linden Fire Station.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
The Board of Supervisors' meeting adjourned at 1:12 p.m.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
MONDAY, AUGUST 18, 2008 
Mogollon Complex Conference Room  
2188 W. Country Club Drive 
Overgaard, Arizona. 
 
l:03 p.m.  Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order and led the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
PRESENT:  J. R.  DeSpain, Chairman ; Jerry Brownlow , Vice-Chairman; Percy Deal , Supervisor; Jesse 
Thompson, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; Mel Bowers, County Attorney; James Jayne, County 
Manager; and Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the Board.   
 
Call to the Public:  No response 
 
Introduction of Elected Officials and County Administration :  Chairman DeSpain introduced all elected 
officials, county administration and the Heber-Overgaard Unified School District S uperintendent and 
recognized others in the audience including th e architect and builder thanking all for their attendance.  He 
expressed appreciation that this building was complete and would provide expanded services to local residents.  
He then turned the chair over to Supervisor Tenney. 
 
Recognition of contributions made to the construction and opening of the Navajo County Mogollon 
Complex:  Supervisor Tenney welcomed all who were present, reviewed events leading up to this opening, 
thanked voters for their support of Proposition 400 so this facility could be funded and presented certificates of 
appreciation to all who contributed to the completion of this building.  Special appreciation was given to Mary 
Springer for her outstanding contribution to this facility. 
 
Adjourn:  At 1:28 p.m. this meeting adjourned by consensus.

10:08 a.m.  Call to Order: Chairman DeSpain led the Pledge of Allegiance and offered the invocation. 
 
PRESENT:  J. R. DeSpain, Chairman; Jerry Brownlow, Vice-Chairman; Percy Deal, Supervisor, Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor , Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the Board, Mel Bowers, County Attorney; and 
James Jayne, County Manager. 
 
Consideration and possible adoption of the tax rates to support adopted 2008-2009 budget: 
a. Navajo County 2008-2009 tax rates :  Lauren Sedillo advised the Board of Supervisors that a ll required 
publications and hearings related to tax rates and the levy were appropriately conducted , the tax rates tha t 
are being presented today are for all county taxing entities including the schools , and all rates are included 
in the documentation presented.  She stated that as  has been indicated in previous meetings, the county tax 
rate did go down  and the Qwest settlement did impact the Navajo County levy and  tax rate.  She stated the 
final county tax levy is $5,246,080 and the final tax rate is $0.5775.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion 
to adopt the Navajo County tax rates as presented; motion seconded by Superviso r Tenney; vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
b. Special Districts’ 2008 -2009 tax rates —Board of Directors’ Sessions :  Ms. Sedillo reported that the tax 
rates for the Special Districts remained constant with the 2007-2008 tax rate with the following exceptions:   
 White Mountain Lakes Recreation District lot assessments increased on more than 3.3 acres to 
$38.56 and on less than 3.3 acres to $12.85 to support the budget increase. 
 Due to an increased valuation, Victory Heights Maintenance District tax rate  decreased to $1.3721 
and Silver Creek County Road Improvement District decreased to $0.9893.  
 Silver Creek Prevention District tax rate decreased to $0.1002 due to a reduced budget amount.

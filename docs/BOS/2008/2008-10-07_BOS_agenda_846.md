BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:    
a. Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excel lence: Darlene Fraley, outgoing Clerk of 
the Board, was recognized by the board and staff for her service to the county. She was presented 
with a certificate of appreciation along with plants and a fruit basket and each member of the board 
individually expressed his thanks and wished her well. 
b. Personnel service awards: Keith Payne, Wilfred John, James Pruitt, Leann Baker, James Jayne and 
Gail Calisen were recognized for their years of service to Navajo County. 
c. Consideration and possible appointment of Interim Clerk of the Board of Supervisors : James Jayne, 
County Manager, discussed the item and made the recommendation that Lorinda Brown be named 
Interim Clerk of the Board effective October 16, 2008, at entry level for the position.  Supervisor 
Tenney made the motion  to appoint Lorinda Brown as Interim Cler k; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and action related to  Personnel Action report : Gail Calisen, Human 
Resources Director, reviewed the 32 item Personnel Action report.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to 
approve the Personnel Action report as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney.  Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.

ADMINISTRATION: 
a. Consideration and possible approval of amendment of the Navajo County Personnel Policy 
Section 5.15 through Section 5.17 to include the establishment of the Accident Review  Board: 
Tim Norton, Risk Manager for Navajo County, discussed the item and introduced Doug Muell, 
Director of Loss Control for Arizona Counties Insurance Pool (ACIP), who responded to 
questions from board members and the County Attorney. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion 
to approve an amendment to the Navajo County Personnel Policy, Section 5.15 through Section 
5.17 to include the establishment of an Accident Review Board; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.   
b. Update of 2008 -2009 revenues and expenditures :  James Menlove, Finance Director, presented 
the Revenue Budget Performance Report for the first quarter of the fiscal year and responded to 
questions from the board.  No action was taken by the board. 
c. Presentation of Public Safety issues relating to county jail : Rod Ross , Government Relations 
Administrator, along with Sheriff Gary Butler, Chief Deputy Bernard Huser and Commander 
Matt Searles, gave a power point presentation on public safety issues relating to the co unty jail.  
No action was taken by the board.

BOARD BUSINESS:  Report from Board Members:  Chairman DeSpain listed the upcoming board related 
events as follows: October 20, 2008, Kayenta Community Meeting; November 13, 2008, Special Board 
Meeting to Canvass the General Election; Saturday, October 11, 2008 , NATC Dedication ; December 2 -5, 
2008, Arizona Association of Counties (AACo) Annual Conference in Glendale, AZ.  Supervisor Tenney 
attended the White House Conference on Wildlife Conservation Policy in Reno, NV , called for by President 
Bush, to establish a 10 -year Plan.  Supervisor Thompson indicated he is spending time on the Jeddito 
graveling project and expressed his appreciation for the continued partnership between the Navajo Nation 
Department of Transportation, Navajo County, and the Hopi Tribe.  The re is a continuing problem with 
regards to obtaining water for the roads and thanked Leo Sheppard and Dusty Parsons for their help.  
Supervisor Deal announced a preparedness meeting in Pinon on October 15, 2008, with the Bureau of 
Indian Affairs, Navajo Nation Department of Transportation, Navajo County and the schools.  The purpose 
is preparation in order to avoid snow removal emergencies, responding to flooding, etc.  He also indicated 
that a briefing session was held for incoming Supervisor of District I, Jonathan Nez, who is a member of the 
Navajo Nation Council who he feels will be bringing new energy to the position.  He stated Mr. Nez left the 
meeting feeling totally welcomed by Administration and Navajo County in general.  County Manager, 
Jimmy Jayne , announced that Melissa Buckley has joined the Administration team and asked everyone 
make her welcome.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
TUESDAY, JULY 28, 2008 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD CHAMBERS 
 
2:07 p.m.  Call to Order 
 
PRESENT:   J.R. DeSpain, Chairman, Dusty Parsons, Assistant County Manager, and Darlene 
Fraley, Clerk, were present in the board chamb ers.  David Tenney, Supervisor, Jerry Brownlow, 
Vice Chairman, and Percy Deal, Supervisor, participated by telephone.  Supervisor Jesse 
Thompson was not present.  A speaker telephone was available in the chambers that made all 
meeting discussion and action audible for participants and audience members.   
Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number ____ - 08 of the Navajo County 
Board of Supervisors declaring a flood emergency in the unincorporated areas of Navajo County 
pursuant to ARS § 26-311:   The Clerk of the Board read the proposed resolution into the record.  
Dusty Parsons discussed emergency conditions that had already arisen related to public safety as 
a result of washed out non -county-maintained roads.  In response to Supervisor Tenney , Mr. 
Parsons stated that at this time there are about 30 -40 miles of roads in Concho, 3 -4 miles in Sun 
Valley and several reservation roads  needing emergency maintenance , estimating that 
approximately 200 miles could meet the criteria for emergency mainte nance through the end of 
August.  
Supervisor Deal expressed appreciation for administration addressing the problem on the 
reservation, describing collapsing roads that include bus route s, stating there are six to seven 
different roads on the Hopi and Navaj o reservations that need emergency maintenance.  He 
advised that the BIA is not equipped to respond.  Stating that he supports this declaration , 
Supervisor Deal made a motion  to approve Resolution Number 49 -08 declaring a flood 
emergency in unincorporated areas of Navajo County pursuant to ARS §26-311; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.   
Nell Wijnaendts  of the Sun Valley area  stated that she lives on the Sun Valley road that is 
washed out and a 90 year old woman also lives down that road . She stated t hat neither of them 
could get out initially and they were out of potable water, that Chairman DeSpain was able to 
come in and provide water.  She said that today she ro de a 4-wheeler down the still impassable 
road to the front of the trading post, walked past the wash out and came into town with Donna 
Lineberry.  
Al House stated he lives in Chevelon Canyon Ranch that is 18 miles from SR 377.  He said there 
was an old bridge across the wash that has been condemned so the county blades off to the side 
of that  bridge.  H said this works during g ood weather but when water floods the road this 
crossing becomes impassable.  He suggested throwing in some culverts and covering them with 
concrete.  Dusty Parsons discussed impact studies that would have to be done and  that the road 
would have to be built up to county standards before the road could be accepted for permanent 
maintenance.  Chairman DeSpain  further explained that only action can be taken on a non -
county road for emergency measures when safety and welfare are threatened. 
Chairman DeSpain called for the vote that was 4-0 in favor of the resolution.

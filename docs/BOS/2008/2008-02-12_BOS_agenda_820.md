NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
SPECIAL MEETING 
FEBRUARY 12, 2008 
 
 
PRESENT:  Present in the  Navajo County Board of Supervisors’ Conference Room at 100 E. Carter, 
Holbrook, Arizona: Chairman J.R. DeSpain, Lisa McKee, Elections Director  and Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the 
Board.  Supervisor Jesse Thompson, Supervisor David Tenney and  Supervisor Jerry Brownlow participated by 
telephone through a speaker phone in the Conference Room that made all meeting discussion and action audible 
for participants and audience members.  Not participating:  Supervisor Percy Deal. 
 
At 8:09 a.m. Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order. 
 
ELECTIONS:  Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the 2008 Presidential Preference 
Election conducted February 5, 2008:  Lisa McKee reviewed the election results advising that Democrat Hillary 
Clinton garnered the most Democratic votes winning 3834 representing 51.31% of the vote and Republican Mitt 
Romney won 4065 votes representing 53.64% of the votes.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve 
the Canvass of the 2008 Presidential Preference Election conducted February 5, 2008; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
At 8:12 a.m., Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES

PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Chairman ; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry 
Brownlow, Supervisor and Percy Deal, Supervisor are present.  
 
9:03 a.m. Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor Brownlow.

would like to address the drug problems in the White Mountains with a Christian bas ed drug treatment program

NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT Board of Directors Session: 
a. Consideration and possible approval of Memorandum of Agreement dated October 1, 2008, 
between Arizona Wing of the Civil Air Patrol (AZWG,CAP) and Navajo County Public Health 
Services District for mutual support in the event of a Public Health Crisis : Supervisor 
Brownlow made a motion to enter  into the Navajo County Public Health Services District 
Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson.  Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  Dr. Wade Kartchner  discussed the item.  Supervisor Deal asked for a 
clarification if the MOA included the Navajo Nation.  Dr. Kartchner responded that both the 
Navajo Nation and the Hopi Tribe have arranged for their own regional distribution site in 
Gallup, NM therefore the shipments from Washington, DC go directly to that site and only the 
White River Apache Tribe is included in this MOA.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to 
approve the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.   
b. Consideration and possible approval of Professional Services contract with Regina Rich, RDH,

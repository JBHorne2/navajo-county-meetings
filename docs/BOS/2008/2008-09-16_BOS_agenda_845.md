ADMINISTRATION:   
a. Consideration and possible approval, as authorized by ARS §§ 11 -951, 11-952 and 11 -952(L), of  an 
IGA between the City of Show Low and Navajo County for the county court collection staff to perform 
the collection duties for the City Magistrate Court; to continu e the practice of the county adjudicating 
criminal misdemeanor cases that are filed by City police officers with the Show Low Justice Courts; and 
to provide for compensation from the City to help defray costs associated with the adjudication of these 
criminal misdemeanor cases: James Jayne addressed the board advising of the following: five of the six 
county justice courts also serve as city magistrate s, saving money for taxpayer s and creating efficiency; 
last year four of the five municipalities  agreed to partner with the county to help defray costs of 
misdemeanor prosecution and defense  and each agreed to provide $15,000 ; the City of Show Low has 
requested a formal agreement to quantify the a greement that has been in place;  Winslow has chosen not 
to pay a portion of the expenses; the Public Defender and County Attorney’s office participates in the 
adjudication of these cases with funding from the general fund; the courts have agreed to act to quantify 
the current practice of collection services; Emery LaBarge had contacted him yesterday with the concern 
that this agreement does not charg e attorney fees at the JP court level and the Clerk is setting up a 
meeting with the Presiding Justice of the Peace, him and  Ms. LaBarge to address these issues .  Mel  
Bowers clarified the fee issue is not the county attorney’s issue but is for public defense.  He discussed 
the history of involving municipalities when he and the County Manager negotiated with cities to see if 
they were willing to take back prosecution and defen se of misdemeanors or assist with cost s.  He stated 
he felt the fee arrangement with Show Low was appropriate, that the city is getting more than its 
money’s worth . He stated that since the City of  Winslow, on advice of counsel,  has not agreed to 
payment a t some point the Board may have to make a decision of whether the county  can provide 
services without a fee.  He further stated that Navajo County is one of the last counties to provide th is

PUBLIC WORKS:   
a. Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number ____ - 08 for the approval of the Final Plat 
and Financial Assurances for the Northwood Pines, Phase I subdivision : Bill Fraley recognized Frank 
DaMato stating this is an important project for the county and this subdivision will be the first user of 
Mogollon Domestic Water Improvement District.  He oriented the board to the project site through the 
use of maps.  He reviewed the staff report and the history of the process stating they are ready to request 
that the board act on Phase I of the final plat.  He advised this phase encompasses 126 lots on 91.17 
acres.  Andy Romance of Mountain Standard, project engineer, reviewed the variances the developer is 
requesting.  He stated that three requested variances relate to private streets within the phase as included 
in the staff report. Mr. Fraley stated these variances still comply with emergency services requirements, 
provide adequate, safe and comfortable streets for primary users , and address the innovation neede d to 
respect the topography of the varied terrain .  He said the f inal variance pertains to the plat, deviating 
from the requirement of a subdivision having consecutive lot numberings.  He advised the tentative plat 
did have consecutive lot numbering for the lots and advised that even though that is no longer the case  
because of the phase construction , emergency services depend on addressing rather than lot numbers so 
staff is comfortable with this variance .  In response to questions, Andy Romance discussed code 
requirements as related to the topography and pointed out the vertical relief the developer had to contend 
with in this area when developing roads.  In response to a question from Supervisor Tenney, Bill Fraley 
confirmed that the board has granted suc h variances in the past.  Supervisors expressed concern that 
residents will come back in the future, as some have recently, to request the county take these roads in 
that are not up to county standards.   Mr. Fraley stated that this is a gated community that will have a 
Home Owners’ Association and the roads are paved and private.  Mr. Romance advised that the tentative 
plat was approved prior to the new subdivision code being in place , that they had tried  to bring 
everything up to the new code st andards to  support county goal s and f ound it would be extremely 
difficult to comply with the new code fully .  Mr. Fraley  introduced the developer, John Prescott, who 
advised the Home Owners’ Association  will be in place with dues dedicated to  upgrades and 
maintenance of the roads.  Supervisor Tenney said that he believes this will be a very good project and is 
an integral  part of the new sewer system that is going in since it will help the sewer system work.  
Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 62 - 08 for the approval of the Final 
Plat and Financial Assurances for the Northwood Pines, Phase I subdivision with stipulations as listed in 
the staff report; motion s econded by Supervisor Deal; vote unanimous approving the motion. Homero 
Vela thanked Bill Fraley and Linda Elliot for their efforts in bringing this project forward.

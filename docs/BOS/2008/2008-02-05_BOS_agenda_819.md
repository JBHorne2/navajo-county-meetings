NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES

and offered the invocation.

PUBLIC WORKS: 
a. Navajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors Session :  Consideration and possible 
authorization for the Chairman to sign a letter to the Army Corps of Engineers urging the Corps to 
participate in efforts to remediate  flooding and rehabilitate the watershed in the Lower Silver Creek 
Area of Navajo County :  Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to go into the Navajo County Flood 
Control District Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  Homero Vela provided information from the Corps visit October 29, 
2007 to Silver Creek.  He advised that while Schoen’s Dam has impressive flood protection the reduced 
flow eliminated flushing flows so the channel continues to accumulate a large amount of sediment.  He 
distributed information regarding this project advising staff requests that a letter be sent requesting that 
the Corps assist in finding a way to remediate and stabilize the channel. Supervisor Tenney thanked Mr. 
Vela for his leadership with the Silver Creek Watershed Council.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion 
to approve this letter as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson who thanked Mr. Vela 
for providing information to Bird Springs ’ residents.  Mr. Vela reviewed the process to involve the 
Corps in a project advising staff will start working with Silver Creek area residents for th e justification 
that must be provided to the Corps .  Mr. Vela advised he would bring back a full report on dams in the 
south county area at a future meeting. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Supervisor Deal made 
a motion  to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote unanimous 
approving the emotion.

c. Hilltop Drive Improvement District :  Carol Fraley reviewed the boundaries of the proposed 
Improvement District. 
i. PUBLIC HEARING regarding the formation of Hilltop Drive Improvement:  Chairman DeSpain 
opened the public hearing . Bob Bixby addressed the board stating he lives within the boundaries 
of the proposed district .  He stated he is not against the district but he had heard several rumors 
and last week wrote a letter with many questions that he wanted answered. Mr. Parsons addressed 
each question in the letter and following discussion, Mr. Bixby stated his  questions were 
answered and he does not object to the improvement district.   Patricia Hamilton who stated she 
lives within the  district inquired what an estimate of the cost would be and Mr. Parsons stated 
they will not have  definite figures until engineering designs are drawn , that  with county 
participation, normal costs range between $3,000 and $7,000 but costs could be added depending 
on homeowner s’ requests.  Carol Fraley reviewed the format ion process for those present and 
advised staff will  have neighborhood meetings to keep them informed.  Supervisor DeSpain

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
SPECIAL MEETING 
JANUARY 29, 2008 
 
PRESENT:  Present in the  Navajo County Board of Supervisors’ Conference Room at 100 E. Carter, 
Holbrook, Arizona : James Jayne, County Manager; Mel Bowers, County Attorney;  Lance Payette, Chief 
Deputy County Attorney; Gary Butler, County Sheriff, Bernard Huser, Chief Deputy Sheriff; Tim Zaremba, 
Division Commander; Dusty Parsons, Public Works Director; and Lorinda Brown, Deputy Clerk of the Board . 
Chairman J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor Percy Dea l, Supervisor Jesse Thompson, and Supervisor David Tenney  
participated by telephone through a speaker phone in the Conference Room that made all meeting discussion 
and action audible for participants and audience members.  Not participating:  Supervisor Jerry Brownlow 
 
At 4:00 p.m., Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order. 
 
County Attorney Mel Bowers advised this emergency meeting is authorized under A.R.S. § 38 -431.02(D) due 
to welfare-threatening emergency conditions in unincorporated areas of the co unty caused by recent weather 
conditions.  This is the result of an unusually warm storm that dropped a lot of rain in Navajo County resulting 
in heavy snow melt and unanticipated run -off.  As a result the county has experienced flooding conditions 
which requires immediate action by the Board  as stated in the agenda .   The Board was further advised that a 
resolution of the Navajo Nation was passed yesterday declaring a state of emergency on the Nation due to 
extreme inclement weather conditions caused by re cent heavy snow and rain.  There was discussion of similar 
emergency conditions in other areas of the county including Hopi Tribal lands.  Supervisor Deal asked for a 
clarification of the term “unincorporated  areas” which was given by the Chief Deputy Coun ty Attorney Lance 
Payette who then proceeded to read the Resolution.   
 
Consideration of a r esolution of the Navajo County Board of Supervisors declaring a flood emergency in the 
unincorporated areas pursuant to ARS § 26 -311: Supervisor Deal expressed his appreciation to Mel Bowers 
and Lance Payette for their efforts in drafting the resolution then made a motion to approve  Resolution 
Number 11 -08 declaring a flood emergency in the unincorporated areas of Navajo County affected by the 
current severe weather; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
Supervisor Deal indicated that District I has several areas of concern including broken water lines leaving 28 
families without water for three days as well as severe complete washouts on certain roads where assessments 
are being completed.  Supervisor Thompson stated that District II has severe muddy road issues and that the 
Little Colorado River is being watched for flooding.   Supervisor Deal said Kayenta School Superintendent  
Larry Wallen has asked for a coordination meeting between the government entities and requested the Public 
Works Director be in attendance. 
 
At 4:20 p.m., Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Deal; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

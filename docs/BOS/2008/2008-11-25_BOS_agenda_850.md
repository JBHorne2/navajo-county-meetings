NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
SPECIAL MEETING 
NOVEMBER 25, 2008 
 
 
PRESENT:  Present in the  Navajo County Board of Supervisors’ Conference Room at 100 E. Carter, 
Holbrook, Arizona: Chairman J.R. DeSpain and Lorinda Brown, Interim Clerk of the Board .  Supervisor Jesse 
Thompson, Supervisor David Tenney and  Supervisor Jerry Brownlow  participated by telephone  through a 
speaker phone in the Conference Room that made all meeting discussion and action audible for participants and 
audience members.  Not participating:  Supervisor Percy Deal. 
 
At 1:32 p.m. Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS ACTING AS BOARD OF EQUALIZATION:  Consideration and possible 
approval of Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendations on hearings conducted on November 17, 
2008 for Petition for Review of Notice of Change .  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to enter into the Board 
of Equalization; m otion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote u nanimous approving the motion.  Lorinda 
Brown presented the recommendations of the State Board of Equalization on hearings conducted on November 
17, 2008, noting that ten (10) hearings were heard either telephonically or in person.  Supervisor Tenney made 
a motion to accept the recommendation of the State Board of Equalization on hearings conducted on November 
17, 2008 for Petition for Review of Notice of Change .  The  motion was seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; 
vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
At 1:45 p.m., Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

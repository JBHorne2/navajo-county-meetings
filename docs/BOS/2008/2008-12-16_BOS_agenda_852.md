BOARD  OF SUPERVISORS: 
a. Slide Shows: – Slide shows were presented by Mary Jane Springer and Harry Begay honoring 
Supervisor Deal’s years of service to Navajo County.    Supervisor Deal thanked Ms. Springer 
and reviewed his 27 years as Supervisor on the Board as he asked for support of a continuing 
partnership b etween Navajo County and the Navajo Nation.  As an expression of gratitude 
Supervisor Deal was presented with beautiful gifts from the Board, elected officials and staff  
including a painting of horse and ride r and a Pendleton blanket  embroidered with the C ounty 
Seal, his name and Board of Supervisors.  
b. Swearing In of Newly Elected and Returning Officials :  Honorable Judge Wing presided over 
the swearing in of the newly elected and returning officials for the following:  the Board of 
Supervisors; Jerry Brownlow, J.R. DeSpain, Jonathan M. Nez, David Tenney, and Jesse 
Thompson:  Superior Court; Judge Michala M. Ruechel and Judge Carolyn C. Holliday: Elected 
Officials; Bradley Carlyon, Cammy Darris, Laurette “Lori” Justman, Linda Morrow, Manuel 
“Manny” Hernandez, Kelly “KC” Clark:  Justice of the Peace  Susie Nelson and Constables 
Dave L. Wood and Lorenzo Yazzie. 
c. Recognition of Retiring Officials:   Chairman DeSpain recognized Carolyn Castillo, Chief 
Deputy Clerk of the Superior Court, Sheriff Gary But ler, County Attorney Mel Bowers.  Judge 
Dale Nielson was in court and unable to be in attendance.   
 
Supervisor Deal spoke about the Navajo ceremony and prayer of the Beauty Way and presented a plaque to the 
members of the Board; David Tenney , Jesse Thomps on, Jerry Brownlow, J.R. DeSpain, as well as  Dusty 
Parsons, Jimmy Jayne, and Homero Vela.   Supervisor Deal also thanked Dusty Parsons and presented him with 
a school bus plaque for his efforts in restoring the SAFETEA -LU transportation legislation language  and 
assuring the reservation school bus route maintenance fund. 
 
Supervisors Tenney, Thompson, Brownlow,  and Nez recognized Supervisor Deal and thanked him for his years 
of service and wished him well.  Representative Albert Tom was welcomed and he passed on good wished from 
the Navajo Nation.   Senator Allen welcomed the newly elected officials and thanked Supervisor Deal for all the 
wonderful things he has done for the county and presented him with a State of Arizona pin.  Everyone in 
attendance was invited to an Open House in the Code Talkers Room. 
 
ADJOURN: 11:15 a.m.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to  adjourn.  Motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

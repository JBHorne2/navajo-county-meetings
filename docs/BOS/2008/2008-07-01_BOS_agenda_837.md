BOARD BUSINESS :  Chairman DeSpain  outlined upcoming special board meetings —September 2 at the 
Mogollon Complex at 1:30 p.m., and September 11 and November 13 at 1:30 p.m. in the chambers.   
Supervisors discussed meetings and issues impacting their districts including the drought and the anticipated 
resulting disagreement over available water on the reservation ; studies being conducted on water availability 
and the resulting domino effect when flooding  occurs that included a meeting with county representatives, U of 
A, NAU, USGS, Navajo Nation Council Delegates, and Dilkon and Teesto officials ; the opening of detox 
centers in Holbrook and Winslow ; and appreciation for staff’s facilitation of road  improvement districts  and 
new development.  Mr. Parsons reported that the county general fund was negatively impacted less  than 
anticipated by the State budget with the  big hit com ing to HURF fun ds, a little less than $1,000,000.  H e 
discussed other budget impacts.  Supervisor DeSpain requested staff submit a  written overview of the State 
budget.  Mr. Parsons reported slurry seal will begin on North Park and six miles  of the Navajo County portion 
of SR 99, stating there is no longer  a shared maintenance agreement with Coconino C ounty but Navajo County 
does continue to do snow removal.

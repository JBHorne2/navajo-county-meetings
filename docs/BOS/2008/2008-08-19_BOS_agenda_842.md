now the only realistic project is the small Silver Lake Boulevard project.  He stated it is staff’s intent  to 
re-bid in the spring reporting that he spoke  to the low bidder who understands this issue.  He said staff 
will specifically bid the Silver Lake Boulevard project when all easements  are obtained .  Sup ervisor

County Attorney Mel Bowers advised that there are criminal justice issues that will have a serious fiscal impact 
on counties and municipalities.  He discussed the imp act of the DPS lab fees that were imposed after local 
budgets were established and the impact photo radar will have on county judicial systems with minimal 
financial support.  He expressed concern that APAC funds were swept by the legislature and these fun ds 
provide mandatory training for county personnel.  He said the criminal justice system is taking a serious hit and 
cities and counties must absorb the impac t, requesting that these impacts be monitored .  Mr. Jayne thanked Mr. 
Bowers for his comments, agr eeing with the points that he made.  He further discussed the financial impact of 
State budget shifts especially as related to the prisoner shift issue that will come back before the board.

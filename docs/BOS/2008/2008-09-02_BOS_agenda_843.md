c. Consideration and possible amendment of the Navajo County Personnel Policy Section 5.15 through 
Section 5.17 to include the establishment of the Accident Revie w Board : Tim Norton  reviewed this 
request stating it would standardize processes and subsequent recommendations following an employee 
accident.  During discussion in which all supervisors participated, Mr. Norton explained the make up of 
the committee, the  process and timing  and advised that Lance Payette had reviewed and re -written 
portions of this policy .  Mr. Bowers expressed concern regarding the lack of expertise of committee 
members, advising they may not understand  something very technical or may not  be knowledgeable 
about personal injury laws , causation, fault or estimations of economic liability , and confidentiality and 
5th amendment issues.  Supervisor Tenney pointed out a uniform policy and review board will provide 
that employees are treated more consistently for the same type of incident.  It was agreed that this policy 
will again be reviewed by legal counsel and come back before the board at a later date.

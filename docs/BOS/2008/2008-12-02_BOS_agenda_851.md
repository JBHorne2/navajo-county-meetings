a. Consideration and possible a doption of the Central Navajo County Community Wildfire 
Protection Plan (CWPP) : Tommy Price  discussed the item and introduced members of the

Chief; Dale Call, Snowflake Assistant Fire Chief; Steve Campbell, University of Arizona Navajo 
County Cooperative Extension; and Lloy d Wilmes, Sitgreaves Communities Wildfire Protection

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
TUESDAY, JUNE 10, 2008 
 
3:12 P.M.   Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order. 
 
PRESENT:  J. R. DeSpain, Chairman; Jerry Brownlow, Vice -Chairman; Percy Deal, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; David Tenney , Supervisor; James Jayne, County Manager; Mel Bowers, County Attorney; and Darlene 
Fraley, Clerk of the Board.  
 
County Manager James Jayne reported: 
 He was notified Sunday morning that Senator Flake, a friend of Navajo County, had passed away.   
 He worked with the County Attorney’s office on the process to fill the vacancy under State Statute  and appreciates 
Mel Bowers’ and Lance Payette’s diligence and counsel. 
 Yesterday it was reported in the Arizona Republic that Senator Flake died of a heart attack and that is not  the case.  
The Sheriff’s office is still determining the cause of death  but it is i mportant to the family that it is noted for the 
record that the death was related to the broken ribs and his fall. 
 Statute offers guidance for two different scenarios:   
o If there are 3 0 elected Precinct Committeemen in the  party of the deceased legislator then the Precinct 
Committeemen meet and bring a name forward.  Navajo County has only 17 elected Precinct 
Committeemen. 
o Since Navajo County does not meet the 3 0 elected Precinct Committeemen threshold, there is a second 
scenario where the Board of Supervisors forms a Citizens’ Panel.  There is n o guidance in statute for the 
formation of the panel. S upervisors of Districts III, IV and V fall within Legislative District Five and 
determine the size of the panel.  S taff suggested these supervisors bring recommendations for the 
Citizens’ Panel to this meeting.   
 In a detailed county press release, it was reported the Citizens’ Panel will meet Thursday morning at 9:00 a.m. in 
Taylor Town Hall, the center of the affected districts, and will agree upon three names of registered  Republicans who 
reside in Navajo County .  The Board will r econvene here Friday at 2:00 p.m. for review of the three names the 
Citizens’ Panel submits. 
 A supervisor had questioned the accelerated timing.  Calls were received from the Senate President’s office discussing 
critical issues that the legislature is addressing and the Secretary of State’s office outlining the statutory timeline.  The 
presented schedule meets statute and by selecting the person to fill the seat on Friday rather than next Tuesday, will 
allow the Senate to have a full work -week next week.  The staff is sensitive to Senator Flake’s legacy and knows that 
Jake Flake, also, would want the process to go on.  Efforts are being made to act responsibly while being respectful of 
Senator Flake. 
 
Mel Bowers explained that he also was c oncerned about the appearance of being insensitive by the short time frame but 
recognizes this process is governed by ARS 41 -1202.  He reiterated the two processes , acknowledging the precinct 
committeemen selection does not apply.  He discussed the timeline as outlined in  statute, stating that these are maximum 
timelines and the presented schedule meets these timelines.  He advised the names submitted by the panel to the Board of 
Supervisors must be qualified elector s of the district and county , reside in the county and be the  same part y as the 
deceased—Republican. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:   
a. Establishment of a Ci tizens’ Panel with duties to include determining a recommendation of three registered 
Republicans who reside in the Navajo County portion of District 5 to fill the vacant Senate Seat in District 5 who will 
then submit this list to the Navajo County Board o f Supervisors for their action :  Chairman DeSpain explained that 
this panel will meet 9:00 a.m. Thursday June 12 th in Taylor at the town hall.  Mr. Jayne clarified the Citizens’ Panel 
members do not have any requirements as to residency, party or being a r egistered voter but the t hree names brought 
forward by the panel have to be qualified electors, Republicans and reside in Navajo County.   Supervisor Tenney 
made a motion to move forward with the staff recommendation that supervisors from Districts 3, 4, and 5 appoint up 
to three people to be on the Citizens’ Panel; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  Chairman DeSpain stated he 
consulted with Supervisor Thompson on one member’s name  and Supervisor Deal stated he agreed that Supervisor 
Thompson should have the right to select one of the three from District III since a portion of Winslow is in District II.

Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
b. Appointment of membership of the Citizens’ Panel : Chairman DeSpain requested the Supervisors provide the names 
for the Citizens’ Panel.  Super visor Thompson recommended Mayor Robin Boyd of Winslow.  Chairman DeSpain 
recommended Holbrook Mayor Jeff Hill and Snowflake Mayor Kelly Willis.  Supervisor Tenney recommended Alice 
Franco Anderson , Brad Click of Taylor /Shumway and Jack Latham  of Show Low.  Supervisor Brownlow  
recommended Karla Bowen, Greg Schalow and Larry Vicario.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to appoint these 
nine citizens to the Citizens’ Panel to conduct a meeting on Thursday  where they will determine thr ee names to be 
submitted to the Board of Supervisors for Friday’s meeting; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson ; vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
 
Supervisor Deal  discussed the Native American culture as related to respect for the deceased, especiall y those of 
extremely high profile, stating that the Friday meeting would fall within the time frame he would normally be holding 
tribute.  He acknowledged there is also a need for expediency due to the critical position Senator Flake held and state 
statutes, stating that this creates a clash of culture and requested all non -Native members respect the conflict this creates 
for him and Supervisor Thompson.  All Supervisors spoke of their respect for Senator Flake, the respect with which he 
treated others, the outstanding way the Senator represented rural counties, and the concern they have regarding the speed 
of this process while acknowledging they must comply with state statutes.   Supervisor Tenney reported he, James Jayne, 
and Rod Ross are trying to meet with Mary Louise Flake this afternoon to convey the sympathy and support of the board.   
He said he will also inquire if there is anyone Senator Flake may have considered as a legislator. 
 
Leeann Arganbright  addressed the board advising that her mother, Senat or Jarrett, passed away during the legislative 
session two years ago .  She described the situation and how the Senate and staff treated her family with tremendous 
respect and kindness.  She said that as a family they understood the process and with a heritage similar to Senator Flake’s 
she is confident his family would like his work to continue as expeditiously as possible.  She stated that through the 
Senator’s leadership, as well as through the board’s concern, the family will be well cared for.   
 
Mr. Jayne reported the Clerk of the Board will be at the Citizens’ Panel  to take minutes as it is an open meeting and he 
intends to attend the beginning  of the meeting and then let the  panel deliberate.  Supervisors requested the clerk notify  
each of the panel members to let them know the schedule and when the panel makes a selection of three names to invite 
those candidates to Friday’s meeting.   
 
ADJOURN:  At 4:07 p.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by  Supervisor Brownlow; 
vote unanimous approving the motion.

9:00 a.m.  Supervisor DeSpain c alled the meeting  to order, led the  Pledge of Allegiance and offered the 
invocation.

Deal, Supervisor; Mel Bowers, County Attorney; and Darlene Fraley, Clerk of the Board. Present at 150 
Fleurey Street, Prescott, AZ and participating by telephone:  Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; David Tenney,

PUBLIC WORKS:  
a. Consideration and possible approval of Resolution Number _____-08 to extinguish a 50’ ea sement 
between parcels 304-32-120 and 304-32-119 in the White Mountain Lake area :  Carol Fraley reviewed this 
request advising no protests  had been received .  Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to adopt Resolution 
Number 21 -08 extinguishing a 50’ easement bet ween parcels 304 -32-120 and 304 -32-119 in the White 
Mountain Lake area; motion seconded by Supervisor Deal; vote unanimous approving the motion. 
b. Consideration and possible approval of Resolution Number _____-08 authorizing the limited use of county 
resources to assist the City of Holbrook with urgent street repairs: Dusty Parsons advised that several weeks 
ago the County Manager and he had a study session with the City of Holbrook to address street issues 
within Holbrook and believed a partnership between t he county and the city would be beneficial to Navajo 
County citizens.  Holbrook Mayor Jeff Hill addressed the Board extending condolences to the Brownlow 
family.  He expressed appreciation for the support offered through this partnership.  In response to a  
question from Supervisor Deal, Mr. Parsons advised the city will provide the funding and county equipment 
and personnel will be used. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve Resolution Number 20-08 
authorizing the limited use of county resources to a ssist the City of Holbrook with urgent street repairs; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney; vote unanimous approving the motion. 
c. PUBLIC HEARING:  Consideration and possible approval of Resolution Number _____-08 to grant the 
Special Use Permit for a Guest Ranch as requested by Ronald Curtin for parcel 404-79-019, in Township 11 
North, Range 23 East, Section 13 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Chamisa Ranches area : Bill 
Fraley advised that Mr. and Mrs. Curtin are here representing the project. He oriented the board to the site, 
reviewed the staff report  and reported  the Planning & Zoning Commission presented a unanimous 
recommendation of approval. Mr. Fraley advised that the access road to this property goes through Apache 
County and they did advis e Apache County residents at the commission hearing that the county will not 
improve the Apache County road.  Chairman DeSpain opened the public hearing.  Ron Curtin P.O. Box 
2456, Show Low, AZ stated he is the owner of the subject 40 acre  parcel along wit h two other 40 acre 
parcels contiguous to that.  He oriented the board to his project including individual cabins . He responded to 
questions regarding fire protection stating t hat the appropriate documentation of the required protection  is 
on file.  Chairman DeSpain closed the public hearing.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to approve  
Resolution Number 22-08 granting a  Special Use Permit for a Guest Ranch as requested by Ronald Curtin 
for parcel 404 -79-019, in Township 11 North, Range 23 East, Section 1 3 of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian in the Chamisa Ranches area ; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney; vote unanimous approving 
the motion. 
d. LZ Angel Solar Airpark:

the runway length as it looks like it can land a jet; whether the sewer will get into their water system as

project is on septic and about 90% have a treatment system; the 500’ runway will accommoda te a light

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  Consideration and possible approval to authorize the future signing by the 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors of Resolution Number 1 -08 of the County Supervisors Association of 
Arizona and the Arizona As sociation of Counties urging opposition to the Incarceration Shift Proposal : 
Supervisor Tenney and Supervisor DeSpain explained this is a joint resolution of CSA and AACo to protect 
counties from the negative funding impact of the proposed legislation.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to 
authorize the future signing by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors of Resolution Number 1 -08 of the 
County Supervisors Association of Arizona and the Arizona Association of Counties urging opposition to the 
Incarceration Shift Proposal; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES 
TUESDAY, November 4, 2008 
   
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Chairman; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry 
Brownlow, Supervisor and Percy Deal, Supervisor are present.  
 
9:03 a.m. Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor David Tenney. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No response to Call to the Public. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Reappointment of Joel Lawson to the Planning & Zoning Commission for District 
III; b) Appointment of William “Bill” Rawlings to the Planning & Zoning Commission for District V; 
 c) Approval and acceptance of dedication plat by the RFK Silver Creek Corporation of AZ dedicating a portion 
of Silver Lake Boulevard as a public roadway; d) Letter of support for AD EQ continuation; e) Superior Court 
Report: August 2008; f) Constable Reports:  Kayenta #4 September; g) Justice Court Reports: Winslow #2 
September; Snowflake #3 September; Show Low #5 September; Pinetop #6 September 2008 
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the items on the Consent Agenda as 
presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence:  Clyde Halstead, Misdemeanor Prosecutor, was 
recognized for his outstanding accomplishments for the County Attorney’s Office in prosecuting a high profile 
case.  John Swanson was recognized for his contributions to the Finance Department and for being the ultimate 
team player.  
 
Personnel service awards :  Dave Rodriguez, Daniel Laumann, Patricia Hollins, Nancy McAllester, Michael 
Duran and Jerry Padilla were recognized for their dedicated years of service to Navajo County. 
 
Shalice Cook introduced Leonard Brown as Interim Public Defender. 
 
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: Gail Calisen reviewed the 
33 item Personnel Action Report. Supervisor Tenney expressed his appreciation to those employees on 
military leave to serve our country and made a motion to approve the Personnel Action Report as presented; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKE RECREATION DISTRICT BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ SESSION:  
Consideration and possible approval of  issuance of letter of termination of New Horizons Villas dock contract 
Supervisor Tenney made a motion to enter into the WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKE RECREATION 
DISTRICT BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ SESSION; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.   John Hagar presented the item and updated the board on the chronology of 
events regarding the New Horizons Villas dock contract and conditions of the ramp area and requested issuance 
of a notice of termination.  Deputy County Attorney Lance Payette responded to questions from the board.  
Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve the issuance of letter of termination of New Horizons Villas 
dock contract  based on the reasons presented ; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Brownlow.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
FINANCE: Consideration and possible action related to Navajo County’s election to receive a share of the 
State payment under the Secure Rural Schools Act, including county allocations for school districts and Title 
II/III projects through adoption of :  Finance Director James Menlove and Superintendent of Schools Linda 
Morrow discussed the item and responded to questions from the board.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS ACTING AS BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :  Consideration and possible 
approval of Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendations on hearings conducted on September 22, 
2008:  Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to enter into the Board of Equalization ; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Lorinda Brown presented the recommendations 
of the State Board of Equalization on hearings conducted September 22, 2008.  Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to accept the recommendation of the State Board of Equalization; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to return to regular 
session; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
PUBLIC WORKS:  
a. Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number ____ - 08 approving the Final Plat 
for the Cedar Mesa Ridge Subdivision as presented :  Bill Fraley presented the item  and 
introduced Mr. Phil Canally, Manager of Cedar Vista Properties.  Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to adopt Resolution 67 -08 approving the final p lat for the Cedar Mesa Ridge 
Subdivision as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
 Supervisor Deal entered the meeting at this time. 
b. Navajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors’ Session : Consideration and possible 
approval of a Professional Service Contract with J. E. Fuller Hydrology and Geomorphology, Inc 
in an amount not to exceed $45,000 :  Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to enter the Navajo 
County Flood Control District Board of Directors’ Session; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Homero Vela introduced Trent Larson who 
presented the item  and responded to questions from the board regarding field maintenance and 
in-house training.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve the Professional Service 
Contract with J. E. Fuller Hydrology and Geomorphology, Inc. in an amount not to exceed 
$45,000; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
Supervisor Tenney made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
BOARD BUSINESS : Report from Board Members : Supervisor s Tenney,  Thompson and Deal gave reports 
from their districts.  Two weeks ago James Menlo ve, Finance Director, was the keynote speaker at the New 
World Systems annual Executive Conference in Scottsdale.  Rod Ross represented the county at the signing 
ceremony for the Dry Lake Wind Project.

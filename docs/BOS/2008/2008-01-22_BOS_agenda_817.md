c. Report of Navajo County financial status : Supervisor DeSpain commended James Menlove for his efforts 
on the Navajo County Fair where he volunteered assisting with their accounting procedures.  Mr. Menlove 
presented a plaque that the Fair Board presented to Navajo County  and Supervisor DeSpain sugg ested that 
this plaque hang in Mr.  Menlove’s department .  Mr.  Menlove discussed the state of the county finances 
comparing it to  the State, cities and towns and other counties that are in hiring freezes and dire financial 
straits.  He discussed the conservativ e budget approach that Navajo County takes to the budget process by 
budgeting only 95% of the D epartment of Revenue  estimated revenue and that for  all other revenues they 
budget what was received in prior fiscal year.  He reported that at the end of December the county received 
48% of state shared revenue and 51.2% of county revenue so in those areas the county is  on track ; 
licenses/permits and fines/fees for the courts are significantly below where they were last year.  The county 
is 57% through the year and ha s spent 41% of the budgeted expenditures.  He advised the state is 
considering shifting $2 million of the shortfall from the State to Navajo County discussing where those cuts 
would be taken.  He reported ASRS contributions are decreasing slightly but  the other four retirement 
programs are increasing substantially and he anticipates no insurance increase for the employee.  He advised 
that he anticipates with state cost shifts, the 5% employee raise, increased retirement costs and other 
mandated increases, there will be increased budgeted expenditures of $1.3 million.  He advised that no new 
FTEs for FY09 , regardless of funding source , will be brought to board  and the county will curtail  capital 
purchases to a significant extent to help balance the budge t.  Supervisor Tenney inquired if there had been 
any indication from the insurance consortium if the county is on track to become full fledged member  and 
Mr. Jayne stated that the actuarial numbers will be out in February and the Board will receive an update.  He 
stated the health plan is now fiscally solid from a bankrupt situation.  Supervisors thanked Mr. Menlove and 
Mr. Jayne for their conservative approach and foresight in the budgeting process which puts the county in a 
better position than most.

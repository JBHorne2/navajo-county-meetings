SHERIFF’S OFFICE:  Employee recognition:  Sheriff Butler advised this is the first citation of this type given 
from his department in the Sheriff department’s history.  He introduced Detective Sergeant Tim Webster  and 
read the citation as a criminal investigation award recognizing his dedication, criminal inv estigation techniques 
that resulted in solving several complicated criminal cases  in many areas, effectiveness against the Aryan 
Brotherhood; tireless efforts and skills resulting in many dangerous criminals in prison; lead ing the  battle 
against meth labs  and solving a  heinous murder crime .  He p resented a medal for Excellence in Criminal 
Investigations to Detective Sergeant Webster .  Sergeant Webster addressed the board, stating that  none of this 
would be possible if he did not have the assistance and support of agencies in Navajo County , that the support 
of the Sheriff’s office, ATF and local agencies is outstanding.   Supervisors expressed appreciation to Sergeant 
Webster for his outstanding efforts on behalf of Navajo County citizens.   In response to a qu estion, Sheriff 
Butler and Sergeant Webster discussed the prison gang of Aryan Brotherhood , how it evolved from prison life 
and a local murder case where witnesses were threatened during the trial, and were subject to drive -by 
shootings.   
 
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT:  Update on Navajo County Multi -Hazard Mitigation Plan :  Tim Zaremba  
reported the Navajo County Multi -Hazard Mitigation Plan requires annual review in order to receive certain 
federal mitigation funds. He advised he is currently updating appendix G a nd following any type of disaster 
staff will review and update it again.  He said the plan will be available electronically to anyone who wishes to 
read it and it will be completely re -written in 2009-10. In response to a question from Supervisor Tenney about 
fire restrictions as related to the  monsoons, Mr. Zaremba stated that another week of rain such as we have had 
will end the fire season.

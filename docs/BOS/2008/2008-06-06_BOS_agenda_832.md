NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
FRIDAY, JUNE 6, 2008 
 
2:35 P.M.   Call to Order 
 
Present:  J.R. DeSpain, Chairman, James Jayne, County Manager, and Darlene Fraley , Clerk, were present in 
the board chambers .  David Tenney, Supervisor , Jerry Brown low, Vice Chairman , and Jesse  Thompson, 
Supervisor, participated by telephone.  Supervisor Percy Deal was not present .  A speaker telephone was 
available in the chambers that made all meeting discussion and action audible  for participants and audience 
members.     
 
PUBLIC WORKS:  Consideration and possible action to amend Contract #B07-06-025 with Hawker & Evans 
Asphalt, Co. for supplying and delivering liquid asphalt products for a CRS -2 price increase from $464.72 to 
$571.70 per ton  with material to be us ed for the City of Holbrook Chip Seal project , Spur 277  and 
miscellaneous county roads :  James Matteson advised the Board that this price increase request  is due to the 
dramatic increase in the price of oil and asphalt  and is significantly below what could  have been requested 
through the escalation clause  of the contract .  He stated the approval of this increase will facilitate starting the  
chip seal program in Holbrook, Spur 277 at Abitibi and other county roads.  Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to approve the amendment to Contract #B07 -06-025 with Hawker & Evans Asphalt, Co. for supplying 
and delivering liquid asphalt products for a CRS -2 price increase from $464.72 to $571.70 per ton with 
materials to be used for the City of Holbrook Chip Seal project, Sp ur 277 and miscellaneous county roads ; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow; vote unanimous to approve the motion.   
 
ADJOURN:  At 2:38 p.m., Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney; vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, February 28, 2012 
 
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Jerry 
Brownlow, Supervisor; and J.R. DeSpain, Chairman.  
9:03 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor Thompson. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no comments from the public. 
CONSENT AGENDA: a) Minutes: February 14, 2012  b) Constable Reports: Snowflake P recinct #3 , 
December 2011; Holbrook Precinct #1, Winslow Precinct #2, Show Low Precinct #5, Pinetop -Lakeside 
Precinct #6, January 2012  c) Assessment and Tax Roll Correction d) Fill the Gap grant application for funding 
to cover Pro Tempore Judges pay for D ivision II of the Superior Court until Judge Higgins takes office, and 
purchase an additional Polycom device for use in the Superior Court  e) Approve the appointment of Justices of 
the Peace Pro Tempore and Juvenile Hearing officers f) Letter to U. S. Fore st Service regarding a change in 
permit with Tall Timbers Park in Overgaard to allow the sale of alcoholic beverages during events sponsored by 
the Heber/Overgaard Chamber of Commerce  g) Letter to ADEQ in support of the Air Quality Permit for the 
Cholla Po wer Plant  h) Sympathy letter to Laurette Justman : Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to  
authorize the chairman to sign the items in the consent agenda as presented ; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
BOARD OF SUPERV ISORS: RSC Rental Corporation  and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence:  
Chairman DeSpain stated that the item was pulled. 
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: David Coolidge reviewed 
the 10 item personnel action repor t. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the 
personnel action report as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion.   
 
FINANCE:  
a. Consideration and possible adoption of Fiscal Year 2012 -13 Budget Guide lines: James Menlove  
discussed the item . Chairman DeSpain inquired about the response from the other elected officials. 
Supervisor Brownlow inquired about number 3 in the guidelines. James  Menlove answered the 
questions of the Board. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the Fiscal Year 2012 -13 
Budget Guidelines; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.   
b. County Finance Update /State Legislative Update : James Menlove  discussed the item . Supervisor 
Tenney provided the State Legislative Update. 
c. Navajo County recognizes the Arizona Office of the Auditor General:  Chairman DeSpain stated that the 
item was pulled.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Boa rd members, County Attorney and County Manager : 
Supervisor Tenney reported that he is waiting on a contract from 4FRI sometime in March or April and that he 
had the privilege of being the Governor’s guest at the presidential debate Wednesday February 22nd.  Chairman 
DeSpain made an inquiry about the fireworks issue regarding fire dang ers. Supervisor Thompson reported he 
was getting ready for the legislative conference and looking forward to his trip. Supervisor Nez advised that the 
gravel project has receiv ed a few delays due to the weather but it is progressing. Supervisor Nez also reported 
that the Shonto Youth Center is looki ng for services and/or funding and Hardrock is the first community to be 
graveled. Supervisor Brownlow advised that the water settlement passed for White Mountain Apache Tribe to 
proceed with the water treatment for the area. Brad Carlyon advised that last Wednesday he and Sheriff Clark 
hosted an Elder Abuse conference at Solterra in Show Low and they were going to try to host another one in the 
I- 40 corridor area. Mr. Carlyon and Sheriff Clark also hosted a teen drinking video contest through the schools 
and have another video and poster contest coming up in April for the Victim’s Rights Symposium . All seniors 
in the area schools  have also been engaged to help with the Family Advocacy Center raffle and will win funds 
to assist with their graduation night. Homero Vela stated that he will travel Thursday to attend meetings 
regarding the Winslow Levee in Los Angeles with the Army Corp of Engineers. Supervisor Thompson  
requested a sympathy letter for the family of Joe H. Yazzie from Tolani Lake area who recently passed away.  
ADJOURN: At 10:18 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by  
Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PLANNING & ZONING:  
a. Public Hearing: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 38-12, approving the 
proposed Special Use  Permit for Overgaard Compassion Care for a Medical Marijuana Dispensa ry 
in the Heber/Overgaard area: Homero Vela presented the item. Supervisor Thompson commented 
on the item expressing his concerns regarding misuse. Supervisor Brownlow  opened the Public 
Hearing. Mr. Jeff Quinn commented on the application. Supervisor Tenney asked Mr. Quinn about 
his comments regarding the availability of locations . Robert F ern 311 W Strafford, Chandler spoke 
in favor of the approval of the item. Supervisor Thompson asked the consequence regarding abuse 
of the system. Mr. F ern answered the question. Supervisor Brownlow inquired about letters/emails 
regarding this item, 5 in favor and 18 against were received. Supervisor Brownlow asked for those 
opposed. David Wheel  who resides at Lot 2288 Airpark Circle, expressed his concerns regarding 
safety, liability and concerns regarding the runway near his home. June Call 1980 Little Overgaard 
expressed her concerns for the community safety. Jerry Call 1980 Little Rd, Overgaard expressed his 
concerns for the safety and general welfare of the community. Valerie Houseman  2351 Hellcat 
Circle, Overgaard inquired about the business plan and would like to know how they are backed 
financially as she is concerned they will not generate enough profit to support the business. 
Supervisor Thompson inquired if these concerns were brought to the planning and zoning meeting. 
Mr. Vela responded that many were and they were addressed. Record keeping is managed by the 
state not the county. David Houseman 2351 Hellcat Circle expressed that his concerns against this 
issue and the legality of it. Amanda McLaws 14898 Bigler Circle; business address of 2275 Hwy 
260, Overgaard expressed her concerns regarding the dispensary as a mother, business owner and 
citizen. Scott McLaws 14898 Bigler Circle; business address of 2275 Hwy 260, Overgaard 
expressed his concerns regarding this item. Ed Meyer 2272 Hwy 260 , Skyhawk Investments, spoke 
in opposition of this item and the impacts regarding his investments in property  in the area. Bob 
Greco 2788 Constellation, Mogollon Airpark speaking as representative for Mogollon Airpark and 
opposed to the item. Lorin De Porter 3340 Buckskin Canyon Rd spoke in opposition to this item. 
Eric Duthie Town Manager of Taylor 425 Papermill  Rd. expressed the opposition of this item on 
behalf of the Town  of Taylor Town Council. Dr Seth Black is the director and will be relocating to 
the area. Supervisor Tenney  advised he does have legal questions for the county attorney . 
Supervisor Tenney mad e a motion to enter into executive session; motion was seconded by

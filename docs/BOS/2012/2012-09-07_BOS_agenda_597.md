NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
FRIDAY, September 7, 2012  
**Special Meeting**  
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Chairman was present in person. Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; and Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor were present telephonically. David Tenney, Supervisor was not 
present.   
At 9:34 a.m. Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order.   
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the Primary Election conducted 
on August 28, 2012 : Johnathan Roes discussed the item. Supervisor Nez  made a motion to approve the 
official canvass of the primary election conducted on August 28, 2012; motion second ed by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: A t 9:37 a .m. Supervisor Nez made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

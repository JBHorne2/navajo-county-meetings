NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, April 10, 2012 
 
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Jerry 
Brownlow, Vice Chairman; and J.R. DeSpain, Chairman were present.  
Chairman DeSpain welcomed Sheriff Clark and former Supervisor, Lewis Tenney.  
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Supervisor David Tenney.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Hartley Turley addressed the Board regarding the pawn fee.  
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: March 27  b) Constable Reports: Show Low Precinct #5, March 2012  c) 
Justice Court Reports: Holbrook Justice Court Precinct #1, March; Winslow Justice Court Precinct #2, March; 
Snowflake/Taylor Justice Court Precinct #3, March; Kayenta Justice Court Precinct #4, March; Show Low 
Justice Court Precinct #5, March; Pinetop -Lakeside Justice Court Precinct #6, March, 2012  d) Clerk of the 
Superior Court Report March 2012 e) Conduct precinct-based election for Pinetop Fire District on November 6, 
2012 to elect 3 positions with a 4 year term beginning January 2013 and ending December 2016  f) Sheriff 
Office vehicle lease contract with Kansas State Bank for 4 years on six 2012 Dodge Durango’s with Police 
Uplift g) Amendment to the Agreement between the U.S. Forest Service and Navajo County to allow for the 
sale of alcoholic beverages at the Tall Timbers Park in Overgaard for events that are sponsored by the 
Heber/Overgaard Chamber of Commerce h) Sympathy letter to Mary & Jack Springer : Supervisor Brownlow 
made a motion  to approve the consent agenda as presented; motion seconded by  Supervisor Tenney . Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
HUMAN RESOURCES:  
a. Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Acti ons: David Coolidge reviewed the 17 item 
personnel action report. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the 
17 item personnel action report; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Chairman DeSpain indicated that 
number 11 is not a relative of his. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
b. Consideration and possible approval of the 2012 Health Insurance Rates : Lois Fleener reviewed the 
2012 Health Insurance Rates. She indicated that this has not been sent out to the Department Directors  
or Elected Officials. Mr. Jayne indicated that the employee related expenses have been included in these 
rates. He further indicated that County is paying 80% of the benefit. Discussion was held. Supervisor 
Tenney made a motion to approve the 2012 Health Insurance Rates as presented ; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
SHERIFF: Presentation to the Navajo County Sheriff’s Office Dispatchers for National Public Safety 
Telecommunications Week 2012: Sheriff Clark recognized the Navajo County Communications Specialists. He 
presented each with a certificate and a ticket to the salad luncheon.

approving the motion.

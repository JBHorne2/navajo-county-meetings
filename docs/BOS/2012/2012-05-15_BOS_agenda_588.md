NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, May 15, 2012  
*SPECIAL MEETING* 
 
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor and Jerry Brownlow, Vice -Chairman were present in person. Jesse 
Thompson, Supervisor and Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor were present by telephone.  
10:03 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor Tenney.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.   
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT:  
a. Declare an emergenc y situation to enact Ordinance 01 -06 section 7 of the Navajo County Fire 
Ordinance: Dan Hinz gave a brief update on fires in the state. Mr. Hinz requested that the Board declare 
an emergency situation to enact Ordinance 01 -06 section 7 of the Navajo County  Fire Ordinance. 
Supervisor Tenney asked about propane cooking. Mr. Hinz indicated that yes you can have propane 
cooking. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to declare an emergency situation to enact Ordinance 01 -
06 section 7 of the Navajo County Fire Ordinan ce; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
b. Consideration and possible approval of implementing fire restrictions for Zones 1, 2, and 3 in Navajo 
County: Dan Hinz discussed the item. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve implementing 
fire restrictions for Zones 1, 2, and 3 in Navajo County ; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 10:16 a.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

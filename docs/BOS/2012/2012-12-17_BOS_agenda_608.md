NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
MONDAY, December 17, 2012 
SPECIAL MEETING  
PRESENT: Jesse Thompson, Supervisor and J.R. DeSpain, Chairman are present in person. Jonathan M. Nez, 
Supervisor is present telephonically. Homero Vela is present.  
8:33 a.m. Call To Order: Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.  
PUBLIC WORKS: Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement with the Navajo 
Nation and Navajo County for Road Projects : Homero Vela discussed the item and requested approval. 
Supervisor Nez thanked Homero for his work on this project and for the partnership between the Board of 
Supervisors and the Navajo Nation to benefit the local region. Supervisor Nez made a motion to approve the 
Intergovernmental Agreement with the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for Road Projects; motion seconded 
by Supervisor Thompson. Chairman DeSpain asked if the Navajo Nation is completing the crushing. 
Homero Vela discussed the item further. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 8:39 Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
 
 
APPROVED: 
 
Jonathan M. Nez, Chairman

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
TUESDAY, September 11, 2012  
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Jerry 
Brownlow, Supervisor; and J.R. DeSpain, Chairman were present.  
9:01 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor Tenney. A moment of 
silence was offered for the 11th Anniversary of September 11.  
Recorder Laurette Justman and Treasurer Manny Hernandez were recognized for being present.  
Supervisor Thompson offered comments about the 11th anniversary of September 11th. He spoke of the power of 
the media and the great County in which we live. He spoke of the people in the plane on that day that was 
headed towards the Capitol and their sacrifice that they all gave.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no comments for the public.   
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: August 28, 2012  b) Juvenile Probation Report August 2012  c) Clerk of 
Superior Court Report August 2012  d) Justice Court Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1, August 2012; Winslow 
Precinct #2, August 2012; Snowflake -Taylor Precinct #3,  August 2012; Kayenta Precinct #4, August 2012; 
Show Low Precinct #5, August 2012; and Pinetop -Lakeside, August 2012  e) Amended Intergovernmental 
Agreement, Contract #DE111011 -001 Amendment 5 between the Arizona Department of Economic Security 
and Navajo C ounty Board of Supervisor for WIA PY12/FY13 allocated funding in the amount of $508,320  f) 
Intergovernmental Agreement, Contract #DE111011 -001 Amendment 7 between the Arizona Department of 
Economic Security and Navajo County Board of Supervisors for WIA PY 11 incentive funding in the amount of 
$42, 485.00  g) Contract #08 -99-0017 with US Immigration and Customs Enforcement for the detention and 
care of persons detained at the direction of ICE  h) Amended Intergovernmental Agreement between the 
Sheriff’s Office and Cities of Winslow and Holbrook, Towns of Snowflake and Taylor and Pinetop -Lakeside 
regarding Byrne grant funding for FY13  i) Adopt Resolution Number 26 -12, canceling the election and 
appointing the persons listed on Exhibit A who filed nominating peti tions or a nomination paper for a write -in 
candidate for Governing Board for Winslow USD #1, Holbrook USD #3 (Ward 3, 4 and 5), Snowflake USD #5, 
Heber/Overgaard USD #6, Whiteriver USD #20, Cedar USD #25, Blue Ridge USD #32,  NAVIT  Districts – 
Joseph City  USD #2, Snowflake USD #5, Heber/Overgaard USD #6, and Whiteriver USD #20 because the 
number of candidates who filed were equal to or less than the number of positions open for each school district  
j) Adopt Resolution Number 27 -12, cancelling the election for the Joseph City Sanitary District and 
Heber/Overgaard Sanitary District  k) Adopt Resolution Number 28 -12, cancelling the election for a Precinct 
Committeeman position  l) Adopt Resolution Number 29 -12, cancelling the election for the Navapache 
Hospital District m) Adopt Resolution Number 30 -12, cancelling the election for the Clay Springs/Pinedale 
Fire District, Heber/Overgaard Fire District, Linden Fire District, McLaws Rd. Fire District, and Woodruff Fire 
District n) Adopt Resolution Number  31-12, cancelling the election for the  following Domestic Water 
Improvement Districts: Heber DWID, Joseph City DWID, Ponderosa DWID, Porter Creek DWID, Woodruff 
DWID, Three-O-Three DWID, Wonderland Acres DWID and the Clay Springs DWID  o) Adopt Resolution 
Number 32-12, cancelling the election for the NPC Community College District Governing Board  p) Name an 
existing road in the Show Low/White Mountain Lakes area to “Pence Drive” in Section 5, Township 11 North, 
Range 22 East: Supervisor Tenney made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the items in the consent 
agenda as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Consideration and possible approval of a proclamation declaring September 12 through September 16, 
2012 as “Navajo County Fair Days ”: Fair Board President  Michael Sample addressed the Board. Mr. 
Sample introduced members of the Fair Board; Valerie Slade, Leah Thomas, and Melissa Buckley. Mr. 
Sample also introduced Mina Henning employee of the Fair Board and the Fair Event Director. Melissa 
Buckley read the proclamation. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve the proclamat ion

by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
b. BOARD OF EQUALIZATION: Consideration and possible approval of the property owners’ appeal 
to re-classify property to class three properties (owner occupied), waive assessed penalties and liens 
related to this recent reclassification action, and direct the County Assessor to re -classify the properties 
to class three (owner occupied), pursuant to A. R.S. §41-12003 for the following properties: APN 403 -
07-286; APN 210 -47-016; APN 205 -32-045; APN 109 -25-266; and APN 202 -53-193: Supervisor 
Tenney made a motion to enter into the Board of Equalization session; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Melissa Buckley discussed the item and 
answered questions of the Board. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve  the property 
owners’ appeal to re -classify property to class three properties (owner occupied) waive the ass essed 
penalties and liens related to the recent reclassification action and direct the County Assessor to re -
classify the properties to class three (owner occupied) ; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to return to regular session; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADMINISTRATION: Legislative Update: Hunter Moore spoke of the CSA Summit in Cochise  County and 
gave a brief update on the high level priorities. Counties have been called upon in various way s to help with the 
state’s budget  problems. Navajo County has contributed about a million dollars more than what we have 
collected. Mr. Moore reviewed the restoration of HURF funds. Impacts to counties have been about 100 million 
dollars. Supervisor Thompson spoke of the importance of HURF funds to the reservation schools. Mr. Moore 
indicated that we will push the point for the rural areas. Supervisor Brownlow asked what would happen if the 
one percent sales tax fails . Hunter Moore responded. Supervisor Tenney mentioned that there was  not a lot of 
strategic talk in that area. Chairman DeSpain received positive input in regards to the County’s presentation on 
Proposition 101. Supervisor Thompson asked about the propositions and if any of them affect Navajo County. 
Supervisor Tenney advised that only Prop 204 would affect Navajo County.  
FINANCE: Budget Update:  James Menlove discussed the county’s budget. We continue to maintai n and 
watch expenditures very closely. Projected to be in the black at the end of the year $73,000. State shared 
revenues are a head of the previous year at 3.3%. We continue to struggle with General Fund with our Vehicle 
License Tax which is down 6.3%. With that we are expected to be short $6000.  
PUBLIC WORKS: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 38 -12, approving an 
Intergovernmental Agreement with the State of Arizona, Department of Transportation for the 
Woodruff/Snowflake Bridge Rehabilitation project: Homero Vela discussed th e Resolution approving the IGA 
for the Woodruff/Snowflake Bridge Rehabilitation project. The ADOT rating for this bridge is 40%. The federal 
portion of this project is $1,250,000.00. The counties match portion o f the project is $139,553.00. The design 
will take approximately 18 months. Award of a bid before construction starts  is about two years out . 
Supervisor Brownlow asked if this project will be in the new ADOT project management system ; Bryan Cook 
indicated that this will be managed by Jeff Davidson and will be in the old system. Homero Vela advised that 
the cost of making this bridge two lane would more than quadruple the cost of this project and that is why there 
was no expansion of the width of this bridge . The rehabilitation of the bridge is to ensure the safety of the 
bridge and keep it open to the public. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 38 -
12 approving an Intergovernmental Agreement with the State of Arizona, Department of Transportation for the 
Woodruff/Snowflake Bridge Rehabilitation project ; motion seconded by  Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, March 27, 2012 
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; and Jerry 
Brownlow, Vice Chair; and JR DeSpain, Chairman were present in person. 
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Brownlow.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC:  Jamie Chapman addressed  the Board regarding the Woodruff area . He requested 
signs and more Sheriff’s Office Patrol. Stephani and Arturo Bamondi from P eople Helping People addressed 
the Board and gave some information regarding their company.  
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: March 13, 2012 b) Constable Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1, February; 
Pinetop/Lakeside Precinct 6, February; and Snowflake Precinct #3, February, 2012 c)Name an existing road in 
the Snowflake/Taylor area to “Top Ridge Trail” in Section 22, Township  12 North, Range 23 East d) Adoption 
of Resolution Number 07 -12, cancelling the election for the Overgaard Townsite Domestic Waste Water 
Improvement District scheduled for May 15, 2012 and deem Jason “Billy” Fitzsimmons and Jerry Call elected 
and 1 position vacant e) Extension of the BIA Contract to hold juvenile detainees, Contract #A11PC00015 to be 
extended from April 1, 2012 to March 31, 2013 f) Re-sign a new and revised easement agreement form with the 
Forest Service; the Board has previously approved t he acceptance of the Forest Service road easements for FS 
roads 916, 916A, 332, and 332A via Resolution Number 18-11 g) Reappointment of Evelyn M. Meadows to the 
Planning & Zoning Commission for District I h) Reappointment of Randy Murph to the Planning & Zoning 
Commission for District III  i) Reappointment of Rick Slone to the Planning & Zoning Commission for District 
IV j) Approval of the one year reappointment of Ann Jeffers, Esther Peterson and Connie Bergstrand; as well as 
a two year term for the four new Board members, Sadie Lister, Michael Larsen, Brad Richel and Codi Stinnet to 
serve on the Navajo County Extension Advisory Board beginning on April 1, 2012 k) Adoption of Resolution 
Number 08-12, supporting the White Mountain Apache Tribe’s efforts to fund necessary equipment upgrades 
at Sunrise Park Resort :  Supervisor Tenney  made a motion  to approve the consent agenda as presented; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Presentation of plaqu e to retiring employee Lance Payette : Chairman DeSpain stated that the item was 
pulled. 
b. Presentation of plaque to retiring employee Don Walker: Dr. Kartchner presented a retirement plaque to 
Don Walker. Supervisor Brownlow, Supervisor Thompson, Supervisor Nez, and Chairman DeSpain  
thanked Don for his years of service. 
c. Presentation to Nestle Purina PetCare Company : Sheriff KC Clark with K-9 Deputy Murray and K -9 
Deputy Jones presented a certificate, Navajo pot and Navajo County bag to Jeff Gamet  thanking him 
and Purina for their support. 
d. Presentation by SRP on the Navajo Generating Station: Barry Drost from SRP gave a presentation to the 
Board.  
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions. David Coolidge presented 
the 14 item personnel action report. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign 
the Personnel Action Report as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the 
item.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS: Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental 
Agreement between the Navajo County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Agency and Coconino 
County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Agency in order to expend ARRA funds :  Linda Morrow 
discussed the item:  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve the Intergovernmental Agreement 
between the Navajo County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Agency and Coconino County 
Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Age ncy in order to expend ARRA funds; motion was seconded 
by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ASSESSOR: Consideration and possible approval of Settlement Agreement between Snow Creek Development 
and Navajo County regarding 2010 assesse d value of 14 Navajo County parcels:  Cammy Darris requested that 
the Board enter into executive session to discuss settlement negotiations.  
EXECUTIVE SESSION pursuant to A.R.S. § 38 -431.03(A)(3)-(4) for the Board to receive legal advice from its 
attorneys and to consult with and give instructions to its attorneys concerning pending litigation and possible 
settlement of pending litigation:  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to enter into executive session  pursuant 
to A.R.S. § 38 -431.03(A)(3)-(4) for the Board to receive legal advice ; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. At 10:20 a.m. the Board retired  into executive session. 
Discussion is held. Supervisor Nez was not present.  
RECONVENE for discussion and possible B oard action of Settlement Agreement between Snow Creek 
Development and Navajo County regarding 2010 assessed value of 14 Navajo County parcels : At 10:32 a.m. 
the Board reconvened  in regular session.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to approve the settlement 
agreement between Snow Creek Development and Navajo County regarding 2010 assessed value of 14 Navajo 
County parcels; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney . Supervisor Thompson, Chairman DeSpain, 
Supervisor Tenney and Vice Chairman Brownlow were in favor  of the motion.  Supervisor Nez abstained 
from the vote, as he was not present for the discussion. Vote passed approving the motion.   
COUNTY ATTORNEY: Consideration and possible approval of Contract Number A12PX90988 issued for 
Forensic Interview Services between BIA and Navajo County : Brad Carlyon discussed the item. Supervisor 
Brownlow made a motion to approve Contract Number A12PX90988 issued for Forensic Interview Services 
between BIA and Navajo County , motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous to approve the 
motion. 
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass conducted on March 13, 2012 for 
Timberland Acres Special Road District and White Mount ain Lake Special Road District : Johnathan Roes 
discussed the item. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of March 13, 2012 
for Timberland Acres Special Road District and White Mountain Lake Special Road District ; motion seconded 
by Supervisor Tenney. Vote Unanimous approving the motion.  
SHERIFF: US Marshall’s Contract Presentation and possible approval of contract : Sheriff Clark advised that 
the contact has not arrived and will represent this item at a future meeting. Chairman DeSpain stated that the 
item was pulled.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager  
Supervisor Tenney reported on additional information regarding the legislature and County issues. Supervisor 
Thompson reported on the continuing projects for each C hapter and expressed his concerns regarding wate r 
needs for the upcoming season and that the graveling projects are preceding. Supervisor Thompson advised 
Keith Begay from Tolani Lake has passed away and requested a letter of sympathy for the family. Supervisor 
Nez commented on the redistricting and tha nked everyone involved. Supervisor Brownlow thanked Homero 
and his crew for their assistance during the snow. County Attorney Carlyon advised that he and Sheriff Clark 
attended the Shonto General A ssembly and was very impressed with the tourism program. He further reported 
on the 3rd Annual Victim Rights Symposium coming up in April . Mr. Carlyon further reported on the Family 
Advocacy Centers Memorial Day event, the Navajo County Drug Coalition  and other grants administered by 
the County Attorney’s office. Mr. Carlyon stated that additional cuts in funding are anticipated. County 
Manager Jayne clarified that finance is reporting on FY2012 and will be ending in the black but the additional 
year will be tougher. Mr. Jayne invited everyone to the April 13 Honor Guard fund raiser at the Holbrook 
complex. Mr. Jayne reminded everyone that professional photographs will be taken on April 10.

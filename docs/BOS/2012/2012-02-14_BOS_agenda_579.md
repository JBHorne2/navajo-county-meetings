TREASURER: Consideration and possible approval of abatement of personal property taxes : Manny 
Hernandez discussed the item. The abatement cleans the tax rolls for mobile homes or manufactured homes that 
have been burned, torn down, or demolished. Statute 4219118 states when the County Treasurer, County Sheriff 
and County Attorney are unable to locate both the personal property on which taxes have been assessed and the 
person owning the property after a diligent search by the Sheriff, the tax may be deemed uncollectable pur suant 
to this section. Mr. Hernandez brought to the attention of the Board an additional item on a back property 
located in Winslow that the County Assessor was able to take off three years of taxes. The County Assessor is 
only able to remove up to three y ears property taxes . Mr. Hernandez request ed that the B oard remove two 
additional years of taxes, 2006 and 2007. The dollar amount for the removal of taxes is $5.00 and $4.00 
respectively. The request for the abatement of the tax is because the property is condemned and a parcel number 
should have not been created. Manny Hernandez  answered questions of the Board. Supervisor Brownlow 
made a motion to approve the abatement of personal property taxes ; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.

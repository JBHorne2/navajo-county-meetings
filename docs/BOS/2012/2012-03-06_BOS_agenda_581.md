NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, March 6, 2012  
SPECIAL MEETING 
 
PRESENT: Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor were 
all present telephonically. J.R. DeSpain, Chairman was present in person.  
9:03 a.m. Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation. Navajo County Recorder Laurette Justman 
led the Pledge of Allegiance. Chairman JR DeSpain gave the Invocation. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.  
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Approval for County Attorney’s Office to enter into contract #NIC11 -12-003 with 
Arizona Criminal Justice Commission under the NCIS Act Records Improvement Program for overtime for 
$6,263 to assist in clean -up of disposition forms  b) Approval for Clerk of Superior Court’s Office to enter into 
contract #NIC11-12-004 with Arizona Criminal Justice Commission under the NCIS Act Records Improvement 
Program for overtime for $3,966 to assist in completion of disposition forms  c) Approval for Navajo County 
Justice Courts to enter into contract #NIC11-12-005 with Arizona Criminal Justice Commission under the NCIS 
Act Records Improvement Program for overtime for $5,569 to assist in clean -up of disposition forms  d) 
Approval for Sheriff’s Office to enter into contract #NIC11 -12-006 with Arizona Criminal Justice Commission 
under the NCIS Act Records Improvement Progr am for overtime for $3,664 to assist in completion of 
disposition forms: Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to authorize the Chairman to sign the items in the 
consent agenda as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the 2012 Presidential Preference 
Election conducted February 28, 2012 : Johnathan Roes discussed the item. Supervisor Nez made a motion to 
approve the Official Canvass of the 2012 Presidential Preference Election conducted February 28, 2012; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:09 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
APPROVED:       DATE: March 13, 2012

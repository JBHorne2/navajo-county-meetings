NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, April 3, 2012  
Special Meeting  
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Chairman was present in person. David Tenney, Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor was present telephonically. Sheriff Clark, Commander Garcia, Randy Weems, Hunter Moore, 
Homero Vela, James Jayne, and Melissa Buckley were present in the Board Chambers.   
10:01 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Chairman DeSpain.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.  
SHERIFF: Consideration and possible approval of Intergovernmental Agreement #08 -99-0017 with US 
Marshall’s Service for housing of up to 120 federal detaine es at $59.00 per inmate per day : Sheriff Clark 
discussed the item . Sheriff Clark indicated that the rate is f ixed for 36 months from the effective date of the 
IGA; there will be 100 males and 20 females; there may be a medical copay for the detainee; and there will also 
be a reimbursement of medical transport at an hourly rate of $23 per transport officer plus mileage if the inmate 
needs to be transported to a medical facility . This IGA will become effective upon the date of signature of both 
parties and remains in effect unless terminated by eith er party with written notice. Sheriff Clark answered 
questions of the Board members. Sheriff Clark indicated that Commander Powell will overnight the IGA and 
also email it to them and as soon as they sign it they will start making arrangements to start sen ding inmates. 
Sheriff Clark indicated that these will be m inimum security inmates. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to 
approve the Intergovernmental Agreement #08 -99-0017 with the US Marshall’s Service for housing of up to 
120 federal detainees at $59.00 pe r inmate per day ; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson . Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 10:10 a.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS: Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental 
Agreement between the Arizona Supreme Court, the Navajo County School Superintendent and the Navajo 
County Board of Supervisors; for Title I and other associated funds for juvenile detention education services : 
Linda Morrow discussed t he item and asked for approval. Supervisor  Brownlow made a motion to  approve

Superintendent and the Navajo County Board of Supervisors; for Title I and other associated funds for juvenile 
detention education services; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Ms. 
Morrow also thanked Peabody Coal for their partnership with Navajo County. Supervisor Thompson also is  
grateful for the partnership with the schools.  
ADMINISTRATION: Update on the Navajo County Five Ye ar Strategic Plan : Strategic Plan Team  member, 
Tim Norton gave a brief presentation to the Board regarding the Strategic Plan. Mr. Norton indicated that t he 
strategic plan is being communicated to all C ounty employees. Supervisor Brownlow, Supervisor Thompson 
and Supervisor Nez made comments. Mr. Jayne indicated that this is a living document and thanked the team 
members. Mr. Jayne  also indicated that the Strategic Plan is on the  County webpage  and t he C ounty is 
encouraging the public to view and provide feedback.  
FINANCE: Consideration and possible approval of professional services contr act in the amount of $10,900 for 
JWA Architects for Concept design and build/remodel costs for Jail improvements : Mary Springer discussed 
the item and gave a brief presentation regarding jail facility needs regarding the medical, kitchen and laundry 
facilities at the Navajo County Jail. Sheriff Clark introduced Commander Ernie Garcia and Lieutenant Lea 
Land. Chairman DeSpain made comments. Sheriff Clark discussed the item further. He indicated that the

PUBLIC WORKS: NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRICT Board of Directors Session: 
Consideration and possible approval of the Navajo County Flood Control District to reimburse the Woodruff 
Fire District, for an amount not to exceed $12,600, for repairs made by the Woodruff Fire District to an existing

the motion. Trent Larson discussed the item. Chairman DeSpain discussed the item further. Mr. Gerald Scor se 
discussed the item indicating that this area is at risk and expressed his appreciation to Navajo County for their

Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Thompson reported on the  Opening Day  Ceremony of the 50 th Legislature and the Arizona 
Department of Homeland North Regional Advisory Council Meeting . Supervisor Nez indicated his appreciation of 
the partnership with Supervisor Thompson. Supervisor Brownlow reported on the snow and the skiing in his 
district. Chairman DeSpain thanked Linda Kor and Dave Roberts for always being good to us . Hunter Moore 
indicated that the legislative event was very well attended  and stated committee meetings were held last night. 
Brad Carlyon indicated that he did issue a policy statement for perso nnel after the ceremony. Supervisor  
Thompson discussed Arizona’s Centennial and Steve Brophy with the Arizona Cattle Growers' Association.  
ADJOURN: At 10:30  a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

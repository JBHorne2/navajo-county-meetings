NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
WEDNESDAY, October 31, 2012  
**Special Meeting**  
 
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Jerry 
Brownlow, Supervisor; and J.R. DeSpain, Chairman.  
8:35 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor Tenney.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC : David Newlin with the Little Colorado River RC&D spoke regarding the 
continuation of the Apache R ailway. Mr. Newlin recommended preserving the railway. Paul Watson, M anager 
of the Town of Snowflake  spoke to the Board regarding the economic impact of the closing of Catalyst  Mill. 
Mr. Watson indicated that if condemnation is the way to get Catalyst to communicate than that is the wa y we 
need to go. Keith Watkins with Arizona Commerce Authority spoke to the Board regarding Apache Railway . 
He indicated that the Arizona Commerce Authority has not received any notice of intent to abandon the Apache 
Railway line. The Arizona Commerce Authority is watching this with keen interest. Kerry Ballard, Councilman 
of Snowflake spoke to the Board regarding the possible condemnation of the Apache Railway. He indicated that 
distribution centers need rail. Tom Porcharsky, City Councilmen of Snowflake spoke to the Board regarding the 
Apache Railway  and indicated that he is in favor of condemnation. Dale Patton , City Attorney of Winslow  
spoke regarding the economic development of Navajo County and suggested that the County consider an 
adoption of a count ywide economic development plan. Steve Brophy, President of AZTEC Land and Cattle  
spoke to the Board in favor of condemnation. David Brown, Attorney for AZTE C introduced Mr. Nick Patton 
whom has joined Brown & Brown. Mr. Brown s poke to the Board regarding c ondemnation and c onstitution 
eminent domain and the power to condemn. Mr. Brown urged the Board to con sider listening to your legal 
counsel. Jared Hatch spoke to the Board regarding a rush to a decision. He indicated that it is unfortunate to see 
what has occurred with Catalyst however he indicated that they have the right to do what they are doing. He 
believes in free enterprise and his view is to help and assist and attract business. We are here to govern not force 
the hand said Mr. Hatch. Senator Sylvia Allen indicated that we need to make sure that hate  or fear does not 
drive our decisions. She indicated that the railroad does not belong to you it belongs to Catalyst. Senator Allen 
indicated that if  private property rights do not exist than liberty does not exist either. Senator Allen indicated 
that she does not think that condemnation is the right course for taxpayers. Vern Turley , resident of Snowflake 
Arizona spoke to the Board regarding the Apache Railway . Eric Duthie, Town Manager of the Town of Tayl or 
spoke on behalf of Real Az Corridor . Mr. Duthie cleared the record that the email that was sent out was not 
from Real Az Corridor.  
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. EXECUTIVE SESSION pursuant to A.R.S. §38 -431.03(A)(3) for the Board to receive legal advice 
from its attorneys regarding possible Board action for condemnation /purchase of the Apache Railway : 
Supervisor Tenney made a motion to enter into Executive Session; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion at 9:38 a.m.  
RECONVENE for discussion and possible Board action for condemnation /purchase of the Apache 
Railway: At 10:22 a.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to return to regular session; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Chairman DeSpain turned 
it over to the Board for discussion. Supervisor Tenney stated that his position is that it is not our duty to 
enter into an eminent domain and condemn someone’s property. He stated that what is going on is a

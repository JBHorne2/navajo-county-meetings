NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2012 AT 9:00 A.M.  
EMERGENCY MEETING 
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Chairman was present in person. David Tenney, Supervisor; Jerry Brownlow, 
Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Supervisor were present telephonically. Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor was not 
present. Treasurer Manny Hernandez, County Attorney  Brad Carlyon and Recorder Laurie Justman were 
present.   
 
At 9:02 a.m. Chairman DeSpain called the meeting to order.  
 
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Amended Canvass of the Primary Election 
conducted on August 28, 2012 : Johnathan Roes indicated that after the canvass  on Friday, there was an error 
discovered. Mr. Roes asked that we comply with A .R.S. §16-645 and re-canvass the election. Supervisor 
Brownlow asked what the error was. Mr. Roes explained that there was a batch of ballots that had not been 
counted. He further explained that those ballots have now been counted, that the numbers have changed slightly 
however there were no changes in the outcomes of the  results. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to 
approve the official amended canvass of the primary election conducted on August 28, 2012; motion seconded 
by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
ADJOURN: At 9:05 a.m. the meeting was adjourned by Chairman DeSpain.

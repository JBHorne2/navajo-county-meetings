NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, August 28, 2012  
 
PRESENT: JR DeSpain, Chairman; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and David Tenney, Supervisor; were present 
in person. Jerry Brownlow, Vice-Chairman; and Jonathan Nez, Supervisor were present telephonically.  
 
9:01 a.m. Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Chairman DeSpain 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: Regular Meeting on August 14, 2012 and Emergency Meeting on August 
16, 2012   b)  Constable Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1, July, 2012; Winslow Precinct #2, July, 2012; and 
Pinetop-Lakeside Precinct #6, July, 2012 c) Appoint Blaine Hatch to the Disciplinary Appeals Review 
Commission to replace resigned member Jerry Keeley d) Resolution N umber 25-12, approving the lease 
agreement for two copy machines for the Navajo County Jail  e) Letter to the Honorable Jared Blumenfeld 
regarding the Arizona Regional Haze Federal Implementation Plan by the Environmental Protection Agency  f) 
Name an existing road in the Heber/Overgaard area to “Spring Creek Lane” in Section 30, Township 12 North, 
Range 17 East  g) Renewal Option Year 1/Modification Number 1 for Contract Number A12PX90988 for 
Forensic Interview Services for the BIA -Office of Justice Services : Supervisor Tenney  made a motion to 
authorize the chairman to sign the items in the consent agenda as presented ; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: United Way Parking Spot for the month of September:  Chairman DeSpain 
picked the name of Angela Keene for the United Way Parking Spot for the month of September.  
 
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: David Coolidge reviewed 
the 22 item personnel action repor t. Chairman DeSpain declared a conflict on item 13, the person listed is his 
daughter. Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to approve the personnel action report as presented; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Tenney . Vote unanimous with the exception of Chairman DeSpain not voting on 
number 13. 
 
NAVAJO COUNTY HEALTH DISTRICT Board of Directors Session: Supervisor Tenney made a motion 
to enter into the Navajo County Hea lth District Board of Directors  Session; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
a. CONSENT AGENDA: Amendment #3 of the Contract #ADHS 12 -007893, the Public Health Emergency 
Preparedness Program : Supervisor Tenney made  a motion  to approve the Consent item  as presented ; 
motion was seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
b. Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental Rental Agreement between Navajo County 
Public Health Services District and the Kayenta Unified School District for classroom space for August 1, 
2012 to June 30, 2013 in the amount of $774.20 per month :  Mr. Jayne discussed the matter. Mary Tyler 
presented the item. Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to approve  the Intergovernmental Rental 
Agreement between Navajo County Public Health Services District a nd the Kayenta Unified School District 
for classroom space for August 1, 2012 to June 30, 2013 in the amount of $774.20 per month ; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT: Consideration and possible approval of a Proclamation declaring 
September as National Pr eparedness Month 2012 :  Dan Hinz presented the item. Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to approve the Proclamation as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS: Consideration and possible approval of election of “State payment” 
and a 15% allocation to Title II funding for 2012 Secure Rural School funding : Tami Phillips and Homero Vela 
presented the item. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve the election of “State payment” and a 15% 
allocation to Title II funding for 2012 Secure Rural Schoo l funding; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Tenney thanked Tami  Phillips and the efforts of 
the staff to track these funds.  
PUBLIC WORKS:   
a. Public Hearing: Consideration and possible adoption of Ordinance Number 02-12, approving the 
proposed amendments to Sections 2001 and 1509 of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance : Homero Vela 
presented the item. Supervisor Tenney inquired about the public objections or concerns.  Mr. Vela advised 
that there were none. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve the adoption of Ordinance Number 
02-12; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion 
b. Consideration and possible approval of revised Amendment #3A to the Intergovernmental Agree ment 
between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County : Homero Vela presented the item.  Supervisor Thompson 
made a motion  to approve  revised Amendment #3A  to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the 
Navajo Nation and Navajo County ; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion. Supervisor Thompson commented on the item.  
c. Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement between the Kayenta Unified 
School District  and Navajo County : Homero Vela presented the item.  Supervisor Thompson made a 
motion to approve  an Intergovernmental Agreement between the Kayenta Unified School District and 
Navajo County; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion 
d. Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement between the Pinon Unified School 
District and Navajo County : Homero Vela presented the item.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to 
approve an Intergovernmental Agreement between the Pinon  Unified School District and Navajo County ; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion . Supervisor Nez thanked 
the Board for their support.  
COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager 
Supervisor Nez advised there was nothing to report from District I. Supervisor Brownlow commented on the 
Primary Elections and encouraged everyone to head out and vote. Supervisor Tenney advised they have 
continued to meet with Representatives and Senators to push Legislative issues for Navajo County. Supervisor 
Tenney thanked everyone for their support. Supervisor Thompson  wished the Tenney family well as they 
continue to deal with their loss. Chairman DeSpain encouraged everyone to get out and vote.  Mr. Jayne 
discussed a recent meeting regarding County perspectives pertaining to funding. 
ADJOURN: At 9:37 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES  
TUESDAY, January 25, 2011 
 
PRESENT: Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; J.R. 
DeSpain, Supervisor; and David Tenney, Chairman were present.  
9:03 a.m.  Call to Order
Supervisor Nez spoke of the distinguished honor of serving as Speaker of the Navajo Nation for two weeks 
until the new Speaker was appointed.  
: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Supervisor Nez. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: July 28, 2010; January 4, 2011 b) Warrant & Voucher Report over $1000, 
December 2010 c) Constable Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1, December; Snowflake Precinct #3, December; 
Pinetop-Lakeside, December, 2010 d) Arizona Attorney General to represent Navajo County in Arizona Tax 
Court tax appeal Snowflake Power v. Arizona Department of Revenue, involving Centrally Valued Property 
(Snowflake biomass facility for tax years 2010 and 2011 e) Resolution Number 02-11, approving 
representation of Navajo County by Arizona Attorney General in all tax appeals involving Centrally Valued 
Property f) Re-appointment of the Justices of the Peace Pro Tempores and Re-appointment of Juvenile Hearing 
Officers g) Correct wrong funding source identified for installation of emergency generator to enable 
continuation of operations in the case of a power outage h) Sympathy letter to Michael Sample i) Rename 
existing road in the Winslow/Chevelon area from “Cosby Rd” to “Jack Rabbit Road” in Section 9 & 10, 
Township 18 North, Range 18 East j) Resolution Number 03-11, canceling the election for Misty Mountain 
Domestic Water Improvement District, for which the number of candidates filed was equal to or less than the 
number of positions open for each District k) Resolution Number 04-11, canceling the election for White 
Mountain Lake Special Road District #2 scheduled for March 8, 2011 and appointing Richard Steffes with a 
three (3) year term to the Board of Directors as he was the only candidate who filed nomination papers for the

Precinct Committeeman to fill an existing vacancy in the respective precinct: Dorothy Jean Tyler m) 
Reappointment of Robert K. Black, Jr. to the Planning & Zoning Commission for District I n) Reappointment of 
Carol Davis to the Planning & Zoning Commission for District II o) Reappointment of Wendell DeCross to the 
Planning & Zoning Commission for District V p) Reappointment of Chuck Teetsel to the Planning & Zoning 
Commission as an alternate for District V q) Letter to Corbin Newman Regional Forester regarding Navajo 
County’s continued support for the Four Forest Restoration Initiative r) Letter endorsing the County’s 
solicitation of the Justice Reinvestment Initiative: Supervisor Thompson made a motion to authorize the 
Chairman to sign the items in the consent agenda as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

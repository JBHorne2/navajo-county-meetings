NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, March 8, 2011 
  
PRESENT
 
: Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; and J.R. DeSpain, Vice-Chairman were 
present in person. David Tenney, Chairman was present telephonically.  
9:11 a.m.  Call To Order
 
: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Vice-Chairman DeSpain. Vice-
Chairman DeSpain also ran the meeting due to Chairman Tenney being in Washington D.C. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC
 
: There were no public comments.

Agreement 11-LE-11030121-002 Modification Number 1, to increase the reimbursement from $10,000 to 
$12,742 i) Resolution Number 08-11, canceling the election for the White Mountain Summer Home Domestic 
Water Improvement District, for which the number of candidates filed, was equal to or less than the number of 
positions open for each District j) Sympathy letter to Greg Repine and Jackie Walker

made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the items in the consent agenda as presented; motion

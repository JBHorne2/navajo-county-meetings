NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, January 4, 2011 
PRESENT: Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; David 
Tenney, Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present.  
9:00 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor DeSpain.  
The Board of Supervisors sang Happy Birthday to Supervisor David Tenney. Chairman Thompson made 
comments.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Election of Chairman and Vice -Chairman for 2011 : Supervisor Nez  made a motion to elect David 
Tenney as Chairman and JR Desp ain as Vice -Chairman for 2011 ; motion seconded by Supervisor 
DeSpain. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Nez  made comm ents. Supervisor 
DeSpain indicated that Chairman Tenney is also the President of CSA and made further comments.  
b. Presentation of Plaque of Appreciation for 2010 Service : Chairman Tenney  presented the outgoing 
Chairman, Jesse  Thompson with a plaque of appre ciation. Supervisor Thompson  made comments 
regarding the economy including housing, high unemployment and all of the people that are hurting at 
this time.  
County Manager Jayne thanked former Board of Supervisor member Mr. Lewis Tenney for being 
present. Mr. Tenney made comments. Chairman Tenney thanked his father for his presence and kind 
words. He further shared some words of wisdom that his dad said to him “remember when you sit in that 
seat to be yourself but at the same time respect the position”.    
Presentation on behalf of the Employer Support of the Guard and Reserve : Show Low City Councilman 
Jack Latham introduced Pam Rodriquez who is a retired Colonel from the National Guard  and the new 
Community Service Director for Navajo County. Mr. Latham, Ms. Rodriquez, and Sergeant Caleb Davis 
recognized Sheriff K.C. Clark as a patriotic employer for contributing to National Security and 
protecting Liberty and Freedom by supporting employee participation in America’s National Guard and 
Reserve Forces. Sheriff Clark made a few comments. Mr. Latham thanked the Board of Supervisors for 
the working relationship that the City of Show Low has with the County. Lance Payette re-introduced 
Community Service Director, Pam Rodriquez. The Board welcomed Pam.  
c. Personnel Service Awards: Tammie Begay, 5 years; Veronica Dale, 5 years; Ivette Williams, 5 years; 
Michelle Hunt, 1 0 years; Manuel Hernandez, 10 years; and Doris Clark, 20 years were all recognized  
for their years of service.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.   
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: June 8, 2010 and June 9, 2010  b) Constable Reports: Winslow Precinct 
#2, December and Show Low Precinct #5, December 2010  c) Cooperative Law Enforcement Agreement #09 -
LE-11030121-001 Forest Service, U.S. Department  of Agriculture (Apache -Sitgreaves National Forests) 11 -
LE-11030121-002 for 2011 Financial and Operating Plan  d) Letter requesting Governor Brewer to grant and 
approve concurrent criminal jurisdiction on that portion of the Petrified Forest that lies withi n Navajo County e) 
Sympathy letter to Donna Hale : Supervisor DeSpain made a  motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the 
items in the consent agenda as presented ; motion seconded by  Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.

HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions : Shalice Cook reviewed 
the 36 item personnel action report. Supervisor Brownlow inquired about item number 21 and item number 31. 
Shalice indicated that number 21 is due to a funding reduction. Supervisor Brownlow indicated that Lauren 
Sedillo has done a great job in Finance and will be missed. James Jayne clarified number 31, indicating that Ms. 
Sedillo accepted a job with Northland Pioneer College and introduced number 23 , Hunter Moore , Navajo 
Counties new Government Affairs Director. Mr. Moore indicated that he is excited to work for Navajo County. 
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the personnel action report as presented; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT Board of Directors Session: 
Consideration and possible approval of a Professional Services Contract with Michelle Jaramillo, for Dental 
Assistant services through the Dental Sealant Program effective J anuary 1, 2011 to December 31, 2011 in the 
amount of $14.00 per hour :  Supervisor DeSpain made  a  motion to enter into the Navajo County Public 
Health Services District Board of Directors Session ; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote  unanimous 
approving the motion . Mary Tyler discussed the item. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the 
Professional Services Contract with Michelle Jaramillo, for Dental Assistant services through the Dental Sealant 
Program effective January 1, 2011 to December 31, 2011 in the amount of $14.00 per hour; motion seconded by 
Supervisor DeSpain. Vote  unanimous approving the motion . Mr. Jayne spoke regarding the New Health 
Department building flooding issues. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to return to regular session; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.   
FINANCE: Consideration and possible award of contract F11 -12-07 to purchase and install  new emergency 
generator for South County Complex offices located at 550 N. 9 th Place, Show Low , Arizona to the lowest 
responsive and responsible bidder : Mary Springer discussed the item and handed out a bid report to the Board. 
Mrs. Springer asked  that the contract be awarded to  Phil Stratton Electric  in the amount of $62,172.88. 
Supervisor DeSpain  made a motion to award contract F11 -12-07 to Phil Stratton Electric in the amount of 
$62,172.88 to purchase and install new emergency generator for South County Complex offices located at 550 
N. 9 th Place in Show Low Arizona; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the 
motion.   
PUBLIC WORKS:  
a. Consideration and possible authorization for the Public Works Department to sell a steel building in the 
amount of $9,500.00 to the Navajo County Fairgrounds : Bill Bess  discussed the item. Supervisor

Fairgrounds for the amount of $9,500.00 ; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.   
b. Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 01 -11, in support of the Black Mesa 
Review Board’s application for Fuel Excise Tax funds from the Navajo Nation Department of 
Transportation for a Feasibility Stu dy for road improvements to N41 : Bill Bess discussed the item. 
Supervisor Nez added comments and made a  motion to adopt Resolution Number 01 -11 in support of 
the Black Mesa Review Board’s application for Fuel Excise Tax funds from the Navajo Nation 
Department of Transportation for a Feasibility Study for road improvements to N41 ; motion seconded 
by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
c. NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRIC T Board of Directors Session: Consideration 
and possible approval of five grant application submittals : Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to ente r

a. Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners : Melissa Buckley swore in the Assessor Cammy 
Darris.  
Discussion and possible action regarding Petition for Review of Notice of Claim APN 212 -26-154G for 
Brian G. Fieber: Assessor Cammy Darr is discussed the item. Mrs. Darris indicated that 2009 and 2010 
cannot be petitioned because the y were not the owners at that time. Based on the information supplied 
by the petitioner the assessor recommended a reduction in the full cash value to $127,154 and a limited 
value to $125,280. The decision would roll over to 2011 and then go back to full cash in 2012. Mrs. 
Darris answered questions of the Board. Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to uphold the Assessors 
recommendation; motion seconded by  Supervisor DeSpain. Vote unanimous approving the motion.   
Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement for Court and Criminal Justice 
Services: Dusty Parsons discussed the item. He indicated that we have had an IGA which currently each 
community has paid the county $15,000 per year along with court costs. Brad Carl yon discussed the 
item further and answered questions of the Board. Mayor Willis of Snowflake spoke to the Board in 
opposition of this item. He further s poke about the revenues that the county already receives from the 
cities. Jordy Fuentes Vice Mayor of Taylor spoke in opposition of this item. Dale Patton, attorney for the 
City of Winslow spoke against the item. Chairman Tenney made comments. Discussion was held. 
Supervisor Brownlow made a motion to delay this item until the 2 nd meeting in April to further discuss 
and work with the cities and towns on this item; motion seconded by  Supervisor Thompson. 
Supervisors Thompson, DeSpain and Brownlow voted for the motion.  Chairman Tenney voted 
against.

request for a waiver. Ch airman Tenney opened up the public hearing . Mr. Menlove asked about the property 
taxes and would like information regarding those numbers provided. Mr. Osborn addressed Mr. Menlove’s 
concern. Scott Osborn addressed the Board.  Supervisor DeSpain asked about income to the property owners. 
Mr. Osborn indicated that they get a shar e of the gr oss revenue of the project. Chairman Tenney closed the 
public hearing. Supervisor Thompson discussed the written letter from Itasca Small regarding the project. 
Supervisor Nez discussed the Commission ’s vote and the public meetings. Supervisor Nez discusse d his 
concerns. Greg Loper responded to the concerns . Supervisor Nez indicated that we have supported all the wind 
projects since my term and the questions is what next and how much of this projection of revenue is correct .  
Mr. Osborn addressed the concerns and indicated that public information meetings were held along with several 
trips to the Indian Wells Chapter and District. Discussion was held regarding setbacks. Supervisor DeSpain 
made a motion to include the waiver of the setbacks as requested by the developer; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Nez.  Supervisor Nez indicated that he is  concerned with the discussion that needs to happen with 
the Navajo Nation and would like confirmation that the discussions took place with the Nation. Homero Vela 
asked for clarification of the waiver. Discussion was held regarding the waiver. Supervisor Thompson reiterated 
Supervisor Nez concerns regarding the impact on the Navajo N ation. Chairman Tenney made comments 
regarding the setbacks and the Ordinance approved by the Board. Greg Loper addressed the concerns  and 
possibilities. Discussion is held regarding blanket waiver regarding setbacks. Supervisor Brownlow thanked Mr. 
Osborn for his work with the  community. He discussed the ordinance and the public meetings regardi ng the 
Ordinance. Supervisor Brownlow stated that he feels that the Board should follow the O rdinance already in 
place. Chairman Tenney indicated that he feels that the biggest issue was the proximity to sight , noise and 
vibration to residences and feels t hat this is different because there are not any of those issues and probably 
won’t be. He indicated that previously the  wind farms near Antelope Valley residents  thought that they would 
be impacted. Supervisor DeSpain indicated that his motion stood to app rove the project as presented with 
the waiver as requested by the developer specifically  item 3a2 along with documentation indicating that 
the developer has met with the Navajo Nation Tribal Government regarding this project; Supervisor Nez 
indicated that he did second the motion as previously stated. Vote was 4 ayes and 1 nay.  Supervisor 
Brownlow opposed. Motion passed.

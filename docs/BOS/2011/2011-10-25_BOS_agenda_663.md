requested that Assessor Parcel Numbers 207 -16-047G; 304-25-153, and 409-16-006F be removed from 
the rolls . Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to remove  Assessor Parcel Numbers 207 -16-047G; 
304-25-153, and 409 -16-006F; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez . Vote unanimous approving the 
motion. Supervisor DeSpain proceeded with the 2011 Back Tax Land Auction.  
Supervisor DeSpain advised prospective purchasers of:  
 All sales are final 
 Seek advice on the marketability of title conveyed by Treasurer’s Deed 
 Examine property before purchasing 
 Check the Assessor’s map for location of the parcel 
 No warranties or guarantees as to the size or condition of property 
 No refunds will be made 
At 1:45 p.m. Supervisor DeSpain explains that Parcel #105-33-333 minimum bid is $150.00. Treasurer 
Hernandez indicated that the taxes alone are  $117.54, the title search was  $75.00, to mail it out was

Supervisor Brownlow asked if we turn this bid down will this property still be  for sale over the counter. 
Mr. Carl Glassmeyer addressed the Board indicating that what their offering is something and something 
is better than nothing. Discussion is held. Supervisor Nez is concerned about County’s investment.

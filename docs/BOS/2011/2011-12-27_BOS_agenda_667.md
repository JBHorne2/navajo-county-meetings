NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT Board of Directors Session: 
Consideration and possible approval of a Memorandum of Agreement with Chinle Unified School District and 
South West Food Service Excellence : Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to enter  into the Navajo County 
Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session ; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. Mary Tyler introduced Cherilyn Yazzie who discussed the item and answered 
questions of the Boar d. Supervisor Brownlow expressed a concern that we are spending funds for Apache 
County when these funds should be spent in Navajo County. Mary Tyler indicated that Cherilyn is providing the 
training to the staff there and that is all. Mary Tyler indicated Cherilyn is paid out of grant dollars from Arizona 
nutrition network and they are aware of what we would like to do to serve  a low income population. She further 
indicated that we are just assisting them with some training and materials. Supervisor Thompson and Supervisor 
Nez made comments. Supervisor Nez made a motion to approve the Memorandum of Agreement with Chinle 
Unified School District and South West Food Service Excellence ; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion  to return to regular session; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

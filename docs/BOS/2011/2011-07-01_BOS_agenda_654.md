NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
*SPECIAL MEETING*  
FRIDAY, JULY 1, 2011 
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Vice Chairman is present in the Board Chambers; David Tenney, Chairman; Jesse 
Thompson, Supervisor; Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor are present.  
5:09 p.m. Call To Order: Supervisor DeSpain opened the Special Meeting and indicated that he will allow the 
public to make public comments.  
Ken Flowberg addressed the Board in opposition of granting the application for fireworks. Sherry Lloyd spoke 
in favor of the application.   
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT:  
a. Report of current fire restrictions and possible reconsideration of Heber/Overgaard commercial 
fireworks permit: Dusty Parsons gave a report of the current fire re strictions. Mr. Parsons explained that 
the weather is an ex treme wind of 97% and recommended  denying the permit. Supervisor DeSpain 
indicated that Sheriff Clark supports the decision. Chairman Tenney thanked everyone for being present 
and commented  on the fireworks display in the Heber/Over gaard area. He f urther indicated  that he 
supports the decision of the Sheriff and Mr. Parsons; however he does feel that we should review our 
Ordinance at a later time to allow local fire chiefs to review. Supervisor Brownlow indicated that he 
agrees with the recommendati on. Supervisor Thompson also supports the recommendation due to dry 
weather conditions. Supervisor DeSpain indicated that we can look at the Ordinance at a later date. 
Dusty made further comments. NO ACTION.  
ADJOURN: At 5:32 p.m. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to adjourn ; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Brownlow. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

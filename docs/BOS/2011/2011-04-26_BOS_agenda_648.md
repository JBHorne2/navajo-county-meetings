NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, April 26, 2011 
 
PRESENT: Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; J.R. 
DeSpain, Supervisor; and David Tenney, Chairman were present.  
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Supervisor Brownlow.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Kathy Hemingway addressed the board regarding groundwater in her area and 
indicated that there would be a speaker tonight on groundwater quality.  
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: April 12 b) Constable Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1, March and Pinetop-
Lakeside Precinct #6, March  2011 c) High Intensity Drug Trafficking Area (HIDTA) Project Domestic 
Highway Enforcemen t HT20 -10-2719 Grant Adjustment Notice  d) Tax exemptions filed pursuant to A.R.S.

EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT: Briefing on the fire at Catalyst : Dan Hinz, Chief Pat Hancock and Tiffany

applause for his efforts during the Catal yst Fire. Mr. Vawter expressed his p rofound gratitude to the executive 
management, emergency management, public works, sheriff’s office, and local  responding fire departments,

during the fire. He also thanked the Sheriff. Supervisor Thompson spoke of the operation at the Catalyst Mill 
which has been a good neighbor to others and now we have been able to help them.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager : 
Supervisor Thompson indicated that he will be attending  a child abuse prevention program in F lagstaff. He

appreciation for all the work on the Catalyst Fire. Jimmy  Jayne thanked Marla for her hard work on the IGA

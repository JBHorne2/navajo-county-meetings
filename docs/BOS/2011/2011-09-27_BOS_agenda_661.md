NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES

Tenney, Chairman were present. Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor was present by telephone.  
9:03 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor DeSpain.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No comment from the public. 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: June 28, July 9, July 2 6, August 15, August 23, and September 13, 2011  
b) Justice Court Reports:  Winslow #2, August and Kayenta #4, August, 2011  c) Constable Reports: Holbrook 
Precinct #1, August; Winslow Precinct #2, August; Snowflake Precinct #3, August; Show Low Precinct #5, 
August; and Pinetop -Lakeside Precinct #6, August 2011  d) Clerk of Superior Court Report  August 2011  e) 
Grant Adjustment Notice – ACJC Byrne Grant DC -12-001 f) Authorization for Clerk of the Board to request 
undercover plates g) Sympathy letter to Juanita W ilson and James “Andy” Ronken  h) Letter to Chief Tom 
Tidwell regarding Four Forest Restoration Initiative’s current status  i) Letter to Charlie Denton with the United 
States Forest Service regarding Navajo County Comment on Timber Mesa/Vernon WUI Fuels Red uction & 
Forest Restoration Project  j) Budgeted Senior Center Agreements for Contributions for Food and Nutrition

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. RSC Rental Corporation  and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence : Melissa Buckley discussed the 
skit performed by some Navajo County Elected Officials at the grand opening of the fair and showed the 
video to the Board. Supervisor DeSpain presented certificates to the County Employees that volunteered 
for this years’ 80th Annual Navajo County Fair.  
b. Consideration and possible approval of a Proclamation declaring the month of October 2011 as “United 
Way Challenge” mont h: Linda Morrow discussed the item and introduced Cindy May and Brenda 
Hazlett with United Way of Northern A rizona. Cindy May addressed the Board regarding the United 
Way Challenge. Brenda Hazlett thanked Navajo County for the support given to United Way.   
Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to approve the Proclamation declaring the month of October 2011 
as “United Way Challenge” month; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson . Vote unanimous 
approving the motion. 
c. Presentation of the new outpatient health clinic-Telehealth Cart (Dan Roper) Item Pulled. 
d. BOARD OF SUPERVISORS ACTING AS BOARD OF EQUALIZATION : Consideration and 
possible approval of the Board of Equalization Hearing Officer’s recommendations on hearings 
conducted on September 6, 2011 for Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation : Supervisor 
DeSpain made a motion to enter into the Board of Equalization Session; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Melissa Buckley discussed the item and 
answered questions of the Bo ard.  Supervisor Brownlow made a motion  to approve the Board of 
Equalization Hearing Officer s’ recommendations on hearings conducted on September 6, 2011 for 
Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation ; motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain.  Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.   Supervisor Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; 
motion was seconded by Supervisor Nez.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

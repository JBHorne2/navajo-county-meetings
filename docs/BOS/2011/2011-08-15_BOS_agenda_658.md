SPECIAL MEETING 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
MONDAY, August 15, 2011 
  
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor  was present in person. Jerry Brownlow, Supervisor ; Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; and David Tenney, Chairman were present telephonically.  
9:07 a.m.  Call To Order: Supervisor DeSpain called the meeting to order. Supervisor DeSpain led us in the 
pledge of allegiance. James Jayne gave an invocation.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: No public comment. 
FINANCE: Consideration and possible adoption of the fiscal year 2011 -2012 property tax rates for all 
applicable governmental entities within Navajo County : James Menlove discussed the item and indicated that 
there is one cor rection with the  Heber/Overgaard Fire District tax rate of 1.5637. He indicated that the 
Heber/Overgaard Fire District adopted the specific tax rate of 1.5660. Linda Morrow discussed the school 
district tax rates. Chairman Tenney made a motion  to adopt the  fiscal year 2011-2012 property tax rates  for 
all applicable governmental entities within Navajo County  with the correction of Heber/Overgaard Fire District 
tax rate of 1.5660; motion seconded by Supervisor Brownlow.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:16 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Chairman 
Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

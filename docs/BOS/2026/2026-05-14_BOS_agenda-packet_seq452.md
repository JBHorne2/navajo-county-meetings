Title: Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger to enact Stage 1 Fire
Restrictions
Submitted For: Catrina Jenkins, EMP Director 
Submitted By: Catrina Jenkins, EMP Director
Department: NC Public Health Services District Division: Emergency Management
Presented By: Catrina Jenkins
Motion before the Board:
Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6
of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire
restrictions.
Background:
The White Mountain Fire Coordination Group has initiated discussions regarding the prevailing
conditions throughout the region.  Weather forecasts indicate that no significant precipitation is
expected in the near future; long-range projections also anticipate below-normal rainfall and
above-normal temperatures during the remainder of May and June until monsoonal activity
starts. Furthermore, multiple fires of human origin have occurred in various areas throughout
the region. In light of these factors and other elements of our operational plan, the WMFCG
recommends that Stage 1 Fire Restrictions be implemented in coordination with our regional
partners on Tuesday May 12, 2026 at 6:00 a.m. 
Fire prevention starts at home, all residents, visitors, and businesses are urged to educate
themselves and share this information with others on the measures necessary to prevent
human-caused wildfires. Such measures include refraining from constructing, igniting, or using
wood, coal, charcoal, or briquette fires  except in areas designated for that purpose, such as
authorized campgrounds or locations on private property that are free of debris. Additionally,
smoking materials and fireplace ashes must be disposed of responsibly, vehicles kept in good
repair, tow chains should not be allowed to drag, and all mechanical activities—such as
welding, grinding, or cutting—should be conducted only in safe areas and on days when wind
speeds do not exceed 10 mph. These and other mitigation strategies are critical to protecting our
community from dangerous and costly wildfires.

Declaration of Emergency
WHEREAS the Navajo County Board of Supervisors is authorized by ARS §26-311 to declare  
that an emergency exists due to wildfire which endangers life or property within the city, or the  
unincorporated areas of the county; and
WHEREAS the Navajo County Emergency Manager is recommending enacting Emergency Fire  
Restrictions,  as  set  forth  under  Navajo  County  Ordinance  01-19  Section  6  which  outlines 
Emergency Fire Restrictions for Navajo County, to begin on May 12, 2026, at 6:00 a.m.; and
WHEREAS the threat of wildfire in Navajo County can have a catastrophic impact to the lives,   
property and welfare of the citizens and visitors of Navajo County and as such Navajo County  
has a legal and ethical obligation to protect the health, safety and welfare of the citizens and  
visitors of Navajo County; and
WHEREAS the people, lands, infrastructure and economic activities within the boundaries of  
Navajo County are under imminent threat from wildfire due to strong winds, relatively high  
temperatures and extremely dry conditions.
NOW, THEREFORE the Navajo County Board of Supervisors issues this Declaration of 
Emergency in Navajo County and the Chairman is hereby authorized to take all further 
actions as may be necessary or appropriate to implement this declaration pursuant to ARS
§26-311.

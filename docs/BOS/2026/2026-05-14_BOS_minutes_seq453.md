NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, May 14, 2026

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I (appearing virtually); Alberto Peshlakai, Supervisor District II (appearing virtually); Daryl Seymore, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Vice-Chair

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney (appearing virtually); Catrina Jenkins, Emergency Manager; Leah Thomas, Clerk of the Board

8:46 a.m. Call To Order:   Chairman Seymore  called the meeting to order.

1.

NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :
Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion  to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by Vice-Chair Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0 with  Supervisor Whiting  being absent.
8:47 a.m. Supervisor  Jason Whiting  is present virtually.

a.

Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6 of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire restrictions :
Catrina Jenkins reported that during Tuesday’s fire restrictions call, the group agreed to recommend moving into Stage 1 fire restrictions because conditions are worsening. She indicated that a declaration of emergency is being requested so that the county can initiate fire restrictions under Navajo County Ordinance 01-19, Section 6.  Ms. Jenkins read the proposed Declaration of Emergency.  She stated that they will distribute information about the fire restrictions as soon as the other jurisdictions in the region—including the Forest Service and state land—have approved their proclamations and ordinances, and that they will work to get this information out as quickly as possible once all approvals are in place.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger  made a motion  to approve a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6 of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire restrictions, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.
Vice-Chair Whitesinger made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.
Chairman Seymore  thanked Ms. Jenkins for all she does.

2.

ADJOURN :  8:51 a.m. meeting adjourned.

d. Economic Update: Paul Watson gave an update of the Economic Development efforts for the area. He spoke 
about the REAL Az Development Council indicating that at the annual meeting a couple of weeks ago, the 
executive committee was selected as follows : Jason Whiting, Chair; Steve North, Economic Development 
Director of City of Show Low, Vice Chair; Diana Morgan , Apache County,  Secretary/Treasurer; Paul 
Ramsey, interim City Manager of St. Johns , Member at Large; and Brian Gardanier, local regional manager 
for APS , Member at Large . He stated that REAL AZ is seeking quotes for brochures and managing the 
website.  He indicated that they are looking for tradeshows that are conducive to the markets which Navajo 
County is trying to attract  and will be sending delegates to those tradeshows . He spoke about the need for 
better broadband and the creation of the Better Broadband Forum.  He indicated that Ken Dewitt is leading a 
group of IT directors from Navajo and Apache Counties . He spoke about receiving a grant from Arizona 
Commerce Authority which they are looking to combine with additional funding they’re seeking through 
Summit Regional Medical Center to do an infrastructure analysis for the region to determine what broadband 
infrastructure we have and what the weaknesses are and provide a strategic plan to improve .  He thanked 
Olivia Todd for her work on the grant. He gave an industry update as follows:  Apache Railway now has 25 
full-time employees and is self-sustaining at this point and rea dy to do business with other industries , Novo 
Power has approximately 40 full-time employees, Novo Star has approximately 20 full-time employees, Tri 
Star Logging has approximately 48 full -time employees, Forest Energy has approximately 30 full -time 
employees, and Arizona Log & Timber Works has approximately 6 full-time employees.  He thanked the 
members of the Board , specifically Chairman Whiting, for their efforts  regarding the forest industry . He 
spoke briefly about cleaning and thinning the forest , with about 18,000 acres being projected for this next 
year. He spoke about the following potential new industry:  Project West, which is an egg farm, could employ 
approximately 300 employees ; Project Falcon, which could employ approximately 450 employees; and 
Project Diamond Star, which could employ approximately 145 employees.  He spoke about some possible 
projects on the natural resource side. He stated that he also serves on the Northeast Arizona Regional Dispatch 
Center Council and indicated that interviews have been conducted and a conditional offer of employment has 
been tendered and accepted for Regional Dispatch Director . Supervisor Williams thanked Mr. Watson for 
the update and his efforts. Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Watson for looking out for our ci tizens and 
bringing jobs to our communities.

e. Update on Fire Restrictions : Mary Springer indicated that monsoons have arrived and as of Thursday, July 
21st, 2016, at noon, restrictions were lifted for Navajo County, Apache -Sitgreaves, Apache County, and th e 
cities and towns. She spoke of the Baldwin Fire, four to five miles south of Heber, approximately 276 acres; 
and the Elk Fire around Porter Mountain / Turkey Mountain area , approximately 450 acres, which will be 
allowed to burn for resource benefit up to 1700 acres.   She spoke about how both fires were lightening caused. 
She indicated that there will be smoke in the areas. She stated that 25 to 30 lightning strikes have caused fires 
in the area over the weekend.

WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKES COUNTY RECREATION IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT: Board of 
Directors Session:  Supervisor Williams  made a motion to enter into the White Mountain Lakes County 
Recreation Improvement District Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
a. Consideration and possible abolishment of the current boat dock policy for White Mountain Lakes County 
Recreation Improvement District: Jerry McGraw indicated that for several months, the Advisory Committee 
has worked on a new boat dock policy  and indicated that the 2002 boat dock policy was designed to not be 
obtainable in that the homeowner would create the boat dock , give it to the White Mountain Lakes County 
Recreation Improvement District who would then lease it back to the homeowner. Chairman Whiting invited 
those who wished to speak on this topic to speak at this time  regarding Items 9a and 9b .  Pat Sarcoz, 2047 
Silver Creek Drive, White Mountain Lakes , spoke in favor of private boat docks. She spoke about being a 
real estate broker and is familiar with the properties, and there is only a small segment of the community who 
an even have a private dock. She spoke about the difference in property taxes for those who have property on 
the water.  She spoke about those who conducted a survey gave out wrong information and that people were 
told that their taxes would go up significantly if the private boat dock policy was approved. She indicated that 
she owns 22-24 properties and that the results of the survey were misleading . She thanked the Board for the 
time that Navajo County has taken out for the community.  She spoke about how the contention over this issue 
is affecting people who are looking to move into the community.  John Hager, 1909 Ponce Place, Show Low, 
spoke in opposition to the private boat dock policy. He indicated that the current boat dock policy on Page 2, 
Item A, indicates that the community should have been notified 10 days prior to any meeting about docks. He 
further stated that he is in favor of getting rid of the 2002 boat dock policy, but believes that the new policy 
is worse than the 2002 policy. He spoke of the specifications in the proposed boat dock policy and that they 
are inferior and that the public has had very little input.  He spoke about the risk of accidents with more docks 
around the lake.  He spoke about the mailed survey with 1630 responses with 802 against private docks, 424 
in favor of private docks, and the remaining 404 indicated they don’t care or were returned blank or 
undeliverable.  Chairman Whiting indicated that there have been quite a few public meetings held on this

<!-- page 5 -->
issue over the past year.  Mr. Hager stated that the meetings get posted the morning before the meeting. Brandt 
Clark indicated that the portion regarding notification on Page 2, Item A, of the 2002 boat dock policy refers 
to an application for a boat dock under the policy, but is not applicable to modifying or abolishing the policy, 
which would fall under public notice requirements in the State statute . Bill Kaiser, 8377 Lakeview Drive, 
Show Low, indicated that he is an Advisory Committee member and drafted the new boat dock policy with 
other committee members, community members and Brandt Clark. He spoke in favor of the new boat dock 
policy and abolishment of the 2002 boat dock  policy. He spoke about t he requirements set forth in the new 
boat dock policy for an applicant.  He spoke about a poll which was done in the White Mountain Independent, 
which indicated that 50 percent were in favor of people having private boat docks , and 25 percent did not 
care.  He stated that the purpose of the Advisory Committee is in place to hear the concerns of the community 
and believes they have done that. He spoke of Item Number 3 of the Rules and Regulations which indicates 
boat docks are allowed.  He stated that at the June Advisory Committee Meeting, the new boat dock policy 
was originally approved by a vote of 5 in favor, 1 against, and 1 absent.  He stated that Brandt Clark reviewed 
the policy for a second time, made revisions, and that policy was approved at the July 20, 2016, special 
meeting by a vote of 4 in favor, 2 against, and 1 absent.  Supervisor Whitesinger inquired regarding the steps 
taken to get the public to participate in the boat dock policy and the number of homeowners in the area . Mr. 
Kaiser indicated that the public was informed by letting it be known it was on the agenda for the committee 
meetings and that there has been pretty good attendance at the meetings.   He indicated that the area has 2400 
taxpayers and that each taxpayer pays $42.00 which goes into a fund that is used to provide maintenance for 
public docks and the ramp, the kiosk, the administrator, lake patrol, and the Taylor Water Board. He indicated 
that currently there are two people with lakefront property who are desiring a dock.  Chairman Whiting 
inquired about the new boat dock policy  not being a blanket approval for anyone desiring a boat dock . Mr. 
Kaiser indicated that each proposed applicant is unique and will be considered on its own merits. Jerry 
McGraw recommended that the Board approve the abolishment of the current boat dock policy. Brandt Clark 
stated that there has been proper notice, and there is no issue with the Board proceeding on this issue.  
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to abolish the current boat dock policy for White Mountain Lakes 
County Recreation Improvement District ; motion seconded by Supervisor Williams . Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
b. Consideration and possible approval of White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District 
Private Boat Dock Policy and Specifications : Supervisor Whitesinger indicated that, certainly, this can be 
very volatile and that people are very protective of their property and want to ensure that their rights are being 
protected, their property is being protected, and they are freely able to do the things that they desire to do. She 
stated that based on the background and understanding the information, the Advisory Board has done their 
job in making sure that the public was informed , having public meetings and crea ting a forum to provide 
opportunities for the public to speak and that the Board needs to support moving on. Supervisor Whitesinger 
made a motion to approve the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District Private Boat 
Dock Policy and Specif ications; motion seconded by Supervisor Williams. Chairman Whiting  thanked 
everyone for their work done on this issue. He thanked Melissa Buckley and Brandt Clark for their work on 
this issue. He spoke about the meetings that he has attended and those who spoke in favor of and against and 
that there has been confusion amongst the community. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

item. He indicated that the boat dock application has been reviewed by the Advisory Committee and that the 
boat dock has met specifications and recommended that the Board approve the Gierst boat dock application. 
Pat Sarcoz spoke in favor of the Gierst boat dock application. She spoke about how Mr. and Mrs. Gierst want 
to give to the community.  John Hager spoke in opposition of the Gierst boat dock application. He spoke about 
the 1630 responses that were received, including the comments that were put on the postcards. He spoke about

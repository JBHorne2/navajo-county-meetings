PRESENT: Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; Robert K. Black Jr., Supervisor; Steve Williams, Supervisor; 
Jesse Thompson, Vice-Chairman; and Jason E. Whiting, Chairman were present.  
9:03 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Williams.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC:  Tom Poscharsky, Mayor of Snowflake addressed the Board about  a letter that he 
previously sent to the Board regarding  a possible Bill that would authorize a private corporation without taxing 
power. He indicated this bill would be an option to help the economic development in the county. He spoke of a 
redundancy in power  for the county. He stated  the County could approve  this at a later date if the proposed 
legislation is passed. Mayor Poscharsky spoke about different companies and what they offer. Skyler Careaga, 
with SEC Power Corporation, addressed the Board regarding a proposed  legislative bill regarding the possible 
formation of a power district. He spoke about the hours of downtime on the grid system and the possibility of a 
second option for power in the county. He asked the Board to write a letter in support of this possible legislation 
which he read aloud. Mr. Careaga indicated this would support bringing infrastructure to Navajo County to expand 
and provide increased availably for vital services like power, intern et connectivity and first responder 
communication.

c. Native American Heritage Month : Adam Wolfe gave a short presentation on Native American Heritage 
Month. Supervisor Whitesinger  expressed thanks to Mr. Wolfe for recognizing the Native American 
community. She spoke of tribal relations and that there are positive relationships that do occ ur, including 
public safety and fire mitigation . She thanked Mr. Wolfe for taking the time to bring education to Native 
American Heritage month . Supervisor Thompson  indicated that by acknowledging these months brings 
education and cooperation in the working relationships that we have between Native American tribes and off-
reservation communities. He stated that we have come a long way, and we live together in order to move 
forward to help one another.  
d. Video of Remembrance for Show Low Police Officer Darrin  Reed: Adam Wolfe showed a video of 
Remembrance for Show Low Police Officer Darrin Reed. Supervisor Williams spoke about the outpouring 
of love and support throughout the communities. He spoke about the procession and the thoughts that he will 
never forget. He expressed appreciation to Officer Reed’s family, Show Low PD, law enforcement and the 
community. He spoke about how tragedy unites this community, and he expressed gratitude to live where we 
live. Supervisor Thompson thanked his fellow supervisors and our administrative personnel who were able 
to attend the funeral. He spoke about how we help one another. He spoke about those who put themselves in 
harm’s way to serve and protect.  Supervisor Whitesinger spoke about all of us having our own ties to the 
tragedy and our own hurts. She expressed her gratitude for the support that was shown and the support to the 
Show Low Police Department, the community of Show Low, and Officer Reed’s family. Chairman Whiting 
spoke about how the communities came together  and the outpouring of love and support . He spoke about 
being humbled during the procession.  He indicated that a lot of things were accomplished that day, including 
respect being paid to the fallen officer and to his fellow officers and that parents were teaching their children 
to have respect for those who serve us. He spoke about the tribute from the Show Low football team.

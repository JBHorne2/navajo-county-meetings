NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Friday, June 24, 2016 
*EMERGENCY MEETING* 
 
 
PRESENT: Robert K. Black Jr., Supervisor; Steve Williams, Supervisor; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; and 
Jason E. Whiting, Chairman were present telephonically. Jesse Thompson, Vice-Chairman was present in person. 
8:58 a.m. Call To Order: Chairman Jason Whiting called the meeting to order. 
Consideration and possible approval of a  Declaration of Emergency in support of the post fire flooding effects:  
Mary Springer, Emergency Manager presented the item.  She asked the Board to approve the item. Supervisor 
Thompson made a motion to approve the Declaration of Emergency in support of the post fire flooding effects; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Black. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
ADJOURN: At 9:02 a.m. Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Williams. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
APPROVED:       DATED: June 28, 2016 
 
Jesse Thompson, Vice-Chairman 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

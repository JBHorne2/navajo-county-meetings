PUBLIC WORKS: 
a. NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRICT Board of Directors Session: Supervisor Williams 
made a motion to enter into the Flood Control District Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by  
Supervisor Thompson . Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Consideration and possible approval 
supporting the United States Army Corps of Engineers’ Waiver Request to revise the schedule and increase 
the cost for completion of the Little Colorado River Feasibility Study and signed Chief’s Report for the 
Winslow Levee project : Glenn Kephart discussed the item .  He stated that for several y ears, the Board of 
Supervisors, our communities, the City of Winslow and staff have been working in partnership with the Army 
Corps of Engineers to redesign and rebuild a new levee.  He spoke about the project being well received at 
the public hearing.  He stated that additional time needs to be added to the project and additional money needs 
to be spent to get to the Chief’s Report.  He asked for the Board’s support of the item. He recognized 
community members and members of the Advisory Committee:  John McCauley; Winslow City Manager , 
Steve Pauken; Chad Meunier, State Parks Representative; and Homero Vela, who has dedicated so much to 
this project.  He further introduced Colonel Gibbs from the Army Corps of Engineers and his team. He stated 
that Colonel Gibbs has indicated his support to this project. Supervisor Thompson thanked Mr. Pauken and 
Bob Schlesinger, Winslow Council member for being here.  Colonol Gibbs introduced his Planning chief Ed 
Demesa; and the new project manager, Priyanka Wadhawan.  Colonel Gibbs stated that the partnership with 
Navajo County and the City of Winslow throughout this project is the best partnership that the Army Corp s 
of Engineers has.  He spoke about the necessity for the waiver for more time and funding. He stated that he 
expects to resume work as early as December if the waiver is granted.  He stated that this project is important 
to our district and our region and to him. He stated that this project is the Army Corps of Engineers’s number 
one priority.  He indicated that he is excited, because the project is supported all the way through headquarters. 
He spoke about his new project manager, Priyanka Wadhawan.  Ms. Wadhawan indicated her excitement to 
be a part of the team and to take the study to completion. She presented a Power Point presentation regarding 
the path to complete, schedule and cost .  She spoke about the importance of the Chief’s Report.  Colonel 
Gibbs spoke about the funding and timing of this project.  He spoke about the cost share and the ways that the 
Army Corps can work with the County.  He stated that additional funding and more time  is needed and the 
support of this Board is necessary to complete the study. He indicated that this project will compete well. He 
stated that this is about a $60 million  project once it gets to construction, and the federal government will 
provide about two-thirds of that cost. He indicated that the Board has his commitment and that he will get the 
project to the Chief’s Report before he leaves . Vice-Chairman Thompson thanked Colonel Gibbs and his 
team for being present today. He indicated that their presence shows their commitment  to the project . He 
further stated that Navajo County would request that the Army Corps minimize the cost to Navajo County 
where possible. He thanked Mr. Pauken and Mr. Schlesinger for being present. Supervisor Williams asked 
James Jayne regarding Flood Control monies and if there are monies available . Mr. Jayne indicated that the 
local cost share for construction would be a partnership between Navajo County and the City of Winslow.  He 
stated that, likely, the Flood Control District will have to borrow a portion of the money for Navajo County’s 
share and then be repaid through Flood Control District revenues.  Supervisor Williams inquired as to when 
the money is expected.  Mr. Jayne stated that any borrowing by the Flood Control District for Navajo County’s 
50% share of the local match would be encumbered at that time.  In the meantime, the dollars are in Flood 
Control and have been planned for b etween now and when we initiate construction.  Mr. Jayne thanked 
Colonel Gibbs and his team for being present today , which shows a clear indication of their commitment to 
us. He further thanked the State Parks, City of Winslow, and the Public Works team for their commitment to 
this project , and Mr. Vela . Vice-Chairman Thompson  indicated that this project is a safety issue and 
protection of property.  Winslow City Manager , Steve Pauken, addressed the Board reiterating the 
commitment from the Mayor, members of Council and the community to this project. He indicated that safety

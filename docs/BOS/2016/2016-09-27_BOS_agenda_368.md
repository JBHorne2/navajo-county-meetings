COUNTY ATTORNEY: Consideration and possible approval of a Proclamation declaring October 2016 as 
Domestic Violence Awareness Month in Navajo County : Lynda Young requested that the Board recognize the 
Domestic Violence Awareness proclamation and spoke of the statistics of domestic violence and the devastating 
consequences of domestic violence.  Ms. Young read the proclamation.  Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion 
to approve the Proclamation declaring October 2016 as Domestic Violence Awareness Month in Navajo County; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Williams.    Vice-Chairman Thompson stated that domestic violence is not 
part of a healthy family and expressed sympathy for all those who get caught up in domestic violence situations.  
Brad Carlyon spoke of the staggering statistics of domestic violence.  He said that off-reservation Navajo County 
averages three deaths a year due to domestic violence.  He stated that combatting domestic violence  takes 
prosecution, incarceration, education, and resources available for treatment.  H e spoke about the good 
organizations throughout the County which partner with law enforcement to educate.  He stated that on Monday, 
October 3, 2016, at 6:00 p.m., they will be kicking off an educational campaign at the old Historic Courthouse in

domestic violence.  Supervisor Williams  thanked Mr. Carlyon and his office for their efforts.  Supervisor

PUBLIC WORKS:   
a. PUBLIC HEARING:  Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 23-16, 
approving/denying a zone change from Rural -20 to Rural -5 on 15 parcels comprising approximately 238.7 
acres within Section 35, Township 11 North, Range 23 East, of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Show 
Low area: Bill Bess discussed the item. Mr. Bess indicated that surrounding parcels have been notified , the 
Planning & Zoning Commission recommended approval by a vote of 7-1 at the meeting on August 18, 2016, 
and n o one appeared in opposition at that meeting. Mr. Bess indicated that the staff report shows more 
information. Peggy Saunders addressed the Board regarding the notification of parcels owners, indicating that 
15 parcels wanted the zoning change, 7 parcels did not respond; one parcel indicated no zone change; 2 did 
not want their zoning changed but did not care about the others , and one wanted his zoning changed but did 
not respond with the proper paperwork. Ms. Saunders indicated that only those who indicated that they wanted 
their zoning changed will be affected if approved. Mr. Bess spoke about the surrounding state land and private 
property and that the 300-foot notification was extended into the private sector land. He stated that the original 
survey done was in 1970 and revised in 1971  and the zoning ordinance was adopted in 1974 but was not 
recorded until 1977; and that had it been recorded prior to 1974, these lots would have been grandfathered in. 
Supervisor Williams asked about the location of the property. Mr. Bess explained where the property is 
located, further indicating that this property is within District III.  He stated that the zone change will a llow 
people to build a structure on a 5 -acre property when they could not before. Vice-Chairman Thompson 
opened the public hearing, and there being no comment, closed the public hearing. Supervisor Whitesinger 
made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 23-16 approving a zone change from Rural -20 to Rural-5 on 
15 parcels comprising approximately 238.7 acres within Section 35, Township 11 North, Range 23 East, of 
the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Show Low area ; motion seconded by Supervisor Williams . 
Supervisor Black asked why the two parcel owners said no. Ms. Saunders indicated that one wanted to sell 
her property in one piece; however, this zone change will not impact her parcel. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion.

ago when Native Americans would come off the reservation and work together to create an impact.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Presentation to Brock Slade and Baylend Stephens for winning the Division IV 
State Wrestling Championship in their respective weight classes: Supervisor Williams indicated that the wrestlers 
were unable to be here today. He indicated that we will be mailing the certificates and medals to them. Supervisor 
Williams spoke of both young men. At 10:40 a.m., Supervisor Williams recognized Baylend Stephens and Brock 
Slade for winning the Division IV State Wrestling Championship in their respective weight classes. Mr. Baylend 
Stephens indicated that his favorite takedown is the Bear Hug and that wrestling has taught him that you will not 
be successful unless you have hard work. He is going on to  attend Phoenix College on a football scholarship.  
Brock Slade addressed the Board stating that the ankle pick is his favorite takedown and that wrestling has taught 
him that you can’t be successful in  life without hard work. He further indicated that he plans to serve a mission 
for his church for two years and then attend college.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE:  Report from Board members, County Attorney and County Manager : 
Supervisor Black gave a report from District I. He spoke of the support that he offer s to campaign “The Naked 
Truth” and how prevalent abuse is in our communities.   He spoke about financial difficulties facing the senior 
centers.  He further reported about concerns regarding limited resources to train staff on teaching children with 
learning disabilities . He thanked Bill Bess and Danny Shurter for their efforts with the Red Dog project.  
Supervisor Thompson gave a report on District II. He reported on a com munity walk in which he participated. 
He indicated that he has been asked to be the speaker at a High School function for Native American Students in 
Joseph City during their graduation banquet.  He stated that he had members of his family pass away. Chairman 
Whiting gave an update on District III. He spoke about APS and their company moving forward. He reported on 
REAL Az and the recent meeting that they had that was based around the Forest Industry, which included a brief 
tour of the Novo Bio Plant. He further spoke about the budget. Supervisor Thompson asked about the lottery 
funding. Mr. Jayne indicated that the $150,000.00 has been in the base budget, and there has been a push to 
include three other counties, and Navajo County is included.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Thursday, September 8, 216 **Special Meeting** 
 
PRESENT:  Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; Robert K. Black, Jr., Supervisor; Steve Williams, Supervisor; 
Jesse Thompson, Vice-Chairman; and Jason E. Whiting, Chairman, were all present telephonically.   
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order:  Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order.   
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the Primary Election conducted 
on Au gust 30, 2016:  Rayleen Richards asked for approval of the Official Canvass of the Primary Election 
conducted on August 30, 2016. Supervisor Williams made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the 
Primary Election  conducted on August 30, 2016 ; motion seconded by Vice-Chairman Thompson . Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. Vice-Chairman Thompson  indicated that he has reviewed the u nofficial 
results. Supervisor Williams thanked the Elections Director and the Recorder for all the work that they did.   
ADJOURN: 9:01 a.m. Supervisor Williams made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
APPROVED:       DATED:  September 13, 2016 
 
Jason E. Whiting, Chairman

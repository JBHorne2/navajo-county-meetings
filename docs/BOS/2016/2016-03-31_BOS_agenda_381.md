NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Thursday, March 31, 2016 *SPECIAL MEETING* 
 
PRESENT: Robert K. Black Jr., Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; Steve Williams, Supervisor; Dawnafe 
Whitesinger, Supervisor; and Jason E. Whiting, Chairman were present telephonically. 
9:05 a.m. Call To Order: Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order. 
      ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval  of the Official Canvass of the results from the March 22, 
2016 P residential Preference Election:  Rayleen Richards, Elections Director presented the item. Supervisor 
Williams made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the results from the March 22, 2 016 Presidential 
Preference Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:08 a.m. Supervisor Black made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
APPROVED:       DATED: April 12, 2016 
 
 
Jason E. Whiting, Chairman 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

attend the State Transportation Board Meeting where she discussed Lion Springs and advocated for our 
community on Route 60.  She spoke about chairing the White Mountain Regional Transportation Committee and 
the discussion to try to build a collective response to the State’s efforts to take the dollars the Count y needs to

expressed appreciation for teachers.  Supervisor Black gave a report from District I.  He t hanked the County 
Manager, Elections, Assessor, and the Sheriff’s Office for coming out to Kayenta Justice Day .  He expressed 
appreciation for those who participated in graduation activities .  He reported that he attended the AZ4A Aging

Carlyon, Sheriff Clark, Paul Watson , and Chairman Whiting also presented to the group .  He congratulated 
Skyler Jayne for winning a competition for weight lifting which qualified him to participate in the national

BUDGET HEARINGS FOR DISCUSSION ONLY: The following departments were heard beginning at 10:10 
a.m. until 12:24 p.m.:   Board of Supervisors/Administration, Assessor, County Attorney, Judicial - Superior 
Court/Clerk of the Court/JP Courts/Adult and Juvenile Probation/Juvenile Detention, Recorder/Voter 
Registration/Elections, Sheriff/Jail, Superintendent of Schools, Treasurer, Facilities Management, Information 
Technology, Legal Defender, Library District, and Northeast Arizona Innovative Workforce Solutions.  The 
following departments were heard beginning at 1:11 p.m.:  Constables, Public Defender, Public Fiduciary, Public 
Health Services District, and Public Works/Planning & Zoning/Flood Control. 
a. Board of Supervisors/Administration: Bryan Layton presented an overview of the 2017 preliminary budget .  
He indicated that  the 2017 preliminary budget is balanced,  and there is no increase in the general fund tax 
levy.  He spoke about  revenue challenges and the creative solutions which have been found .  Supervisor 
Thompson thanked Mr. Layton, Administration, elected officials, and department heads for their efforts in 
putting together the budget.  Supervisor Williams thanked Mr. Layton for the presentation and the time that 
the team and the department heads have put together.  Supervisor Whitesinger inquired as to whether the 
co-location in Show Low includes the Courts, and Mr. Layton indicated that it does not.  Chairman Whiting 
thanked the Administrative Team , department heads, and elected officials  for their creativity in finding 
solutions.

b. Assessor: Cammy Darris presented the Assessor’s budget.  She spoke about the services her office provides 
for the County and that she is looking to move the Assessor’s Office more into digital and mobile. Supervisor 
Thompson expressed appreciation to Ms. Darris for working within her budget. 
c. Constables: Ryan Reinhold, Scott Tipton, Phyllis Romo, and Suzanne Smith spoke about the Constables’ 
services and budget.  Mr. Reinhold indicated that there was not a line item for out-of-state travel, which will 
need to be added in the amount of approximately $3,000.00 due to Ms. Romo’s election to the Board for the 
Arizona State Constables Association and Board of Directors for the National Constables Association. 
d. County Attorney: Brad Carlyon discussed the compensation factor for attorneys.  Joel Ruechel  spoke about 
the schooling required to become an attorney , the pressure of the job of an attorney , and not being able to 
keep good attorneys.  Josh Osborn and Sarah Wolfe spoke about why they are leaving Navajo County.  Jason 
Moore spoke about student loan debt that a ttorneys have and the difficulty in recruiting and retaining 
attorneys.  Mr. Carlyon urged the Board to find a solution to retain quality attorneys.  Supervisor Williams 
expressed his respect and appreciation to Mr. Carlyon and the attorneys who presented today.  He indicated 
that he believes the role of the Board of Supervisors is not to micromanage departments but to set general 
policy and direction for the County and work in partnership with department heads and elected officials.  Mr. 
Carlyon indicated that reallocating non-mandated program money to salary would be a short -term solution, 
not long-term.  He expressed appreciation to the Board for their willingness to work with elected officials and 
department heads.  Supervisor Thompson thanked Ms. Wolfe, Mr. Osborn, and Mr. Ruechel on the jobs they 
have done.  He spoke about how important some of the programs are  and that he will continue to work with 
legislators.  Chairman Whiting  thanked Mr. Carlyon and his staff and expressed an understanding for 
employees who are making the decision to leave.  He spoke about how the conversation with the Legislature 
continues throughout the year. 
e. Judicial: Superior Court/Clerk of the Court/ JP Courts/Adult and Juvenile Probation/Juvenile Detention :  
Presiding Judge, Michala Ruechel, provided a budget book to the Board of Supervisors.   She spoke about the 
services and budget for the Judicial Branch.  She spoke about how the Judicial Branch is asking for less and 
trying to do as much or more with what they have.   Supervisor Williams thanked Judge Ruechel for the 
presentation and what she does.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES 
TUESDAY, May 26, 2009 
   
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry 
Brownlow, Chairman are present. Jonathon Nez, Supervisor is present appearing by video conference.

regarding the new experience of the video conference. Supervisor Nez recognized Daniel Peaches, NPC

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Consideration and possible appr oval of a Proclamation Declaring May 26, 2009 the Day of the Lady 
Mustangs: Supervisor Nez discussed the item. Supervisor Nez made a motion to  approve Proclamation 
declaring May 26, 2009 the day of the Lady Mustangs ; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson.  Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Nez made a  presentation to the Lad y Mustangs. Coach 
Robert Nash and Monument Valley High School Athletic Director Jacob Holiday mad e comments 
regarding the season and congratulated the Lady Mustangs. The Lady Mustangs sang Happy Birthday to 
Supervisor Nez.  
b. Consideration and possible approval of a Proclamation Declaring March 20, 2009 the 100 -Year 
Anniversary of the Navajo National Monument: Supervisor Nez discussed the item. Supervisor Nez made 
a motion to  approve Proclamation declaring March 20, 2009 the 100 -Year Anniversary of the Navajo 
National Monument; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
Supervisor Nez made a presentation to the staff of the Navajo National Monument. Supervisor Thompson 
thanked the Superintendent of the Park and congratulated them on their 100-year anniversary.  
c. Recognition of Retiring Chief Deputy Assessor, Lola Sibcy : Lola Sibcy was recognized for her years of 
dedicated service to Navajo County.

outstanding work with the Recorder’s Office.   
e. Navajo County Cost Cutting Award : Perry Denton, Larry Ponikvar and Kelly King were recognized for 
their cost savings ideas totaling $53,000 to date.  
f. Certificate of Appreciation for Linda Kor, Tribune News, for her time and commitment in covering Navajo 
County events: Linda Kor from the Holbrook Tribune News was recognized for her diligence in reporting 
in Navajo County.

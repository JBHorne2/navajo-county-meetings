NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES 
TUESDAY, July 28, 2009 
   
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Chairman were 
present in the Board Chambers. Jonathan Nez, Supervisor appeared via video conference and Supervisor David 
Tenney was absent.    
 
9:04 a.m.  Call To Order: The Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Supervisor Thompson.  
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Lewie Lacanski addressed the Board regarding road issues. Rhoda McGuire 
addressed the Board regarding wind farm development. Ms. McGuire gave the Clerk of the Board 26 pages of 
signed petitions and thanked Supervisor DeSpain for coming to the Cedar Hills Senior Center Meeting on 
August 10, 2009 at 6:00 p.m.  
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  Sympathy letter to Verna Yazzie; Minutes: July 14, 2009, November 4, 2008 and 
November 18, 2008; Letter to Holbrook Elks Lodge regarding their performance at the ritual competition; 
Application for temporary extension of premises/patio permit to the Arizona Department of Liquor Licenses 
and Control for Dana Saar dba Campfire Hospitality, LLC for an Oktoberfest fund raiser to be held September 
19-20, 2009; Intergovernmental Agreement for lease renewal for Navajo County Workforce Investment Act 
office space in the Arizona Department of Economic Security offices in Show Low and Winslow; Letter to 
Chris Knopp, Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, regarding proposed travel management plan 
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the items on the consent agenda as 
presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: Gail Calisen reviewed the 
13 item Personnel Action Report.  Supervisor Thompson inquired about the individual in item number 2 & 5. 
Ms. Calisen indicated that those two items involve the same individual and the action taken was due to failure to 
appear to work. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to approve the Personnel Action Report as presented; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT Board of Directors Session:  
Consideration and possible approval of new agreement for laboratory services with Center for Disease 
Detection (CDD) Women's Health Lab, to provide STD testing and reproductive health screening services to 
Navajo County Residents effective July 1, 2009 thru June 30, 2011: Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to 
enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Dr. Wade Kartchner discussed the item. 
Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to approve the new agreement for laboratory services with the Center for 
Disease Detection Women’s Health Lab, to provide STD testing and reproductive health screening services to 
Navajo County Residents effective July 1, 2009 thru June 30, 2011; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to return to regular session; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence: Diana Gates 
was recognized for her outstanding accomplishments. Diana thanked the Board and stated that she was pleased 
to work for Navajo County. Supervisor DeSpain and Supervisor Thompson congratulated Diana and thanked 
her for all her hard work.

Number ____-09 to support and sponsor the application of the Linden Fire District with the AK-Chin Indian 
Community to receive Tribal-State Gaming Compact monies to be used to fund a new attack truck for the 
Linden Fire District along with twelve turnouts: Jason Moore and Carol Lewis discussed the item and answered 
questions of the Board. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 54-09, to support

WORKFORCE INVESTMENT ACT: Consideration and possible approval of new contract with the Arizona 
Department of Economic Security and Navajo County Workforce Investment Act for $417,906 for PY09/FY10: 
Judy Bratcher discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to

Investment Act for $417,906 for PY09/FY10; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion. Mr. Jayne indicated that Judy has done a great job with this program and with the 
summer youth program and believes that it is a well thought out budget.  
 
ADMINISTRATION: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number ____-09 supporting the 
establishment of a liaison from the Department of the Interior, Bureau of Indian Affairs to NACo that will 
facilitate communication and information sharing with NACo and individual counties: Rod Ross discussed the 
item. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 55-09 supporting the establishment 
of a liaison from the Department of the Interior, Bureau of Indian Affairs to NACo that will facilitate 
communication and information sharing with NACo and individual counties; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Nez; Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
Supervisor Nez left the meeting at 9:45 a.m.  
  
FINANCE:  
a. Consideration and possible approval to extend the current CableOne franchise agreement to September 15, 
 2009 to allow time for public comment and to negotiate a new long-term agreement (Mary Springer) Item 
 pulled on July 28, 2009. 
b. PUBLIC HEARING: TRUTH IN TAXATION PURSUANT TO A.R.S. §42-17104: Consideration and 
 possible approval of property tax levy as presented with tax rates to be set at the August 17, 2009  Special 
 Board of Supervisors' meeting - Roll Call Vote: James Menlove discussed the item and answered 
 questions of the Board. Chairman Brownlow convened the public hearing and with no public comment, 
 closed the hearing. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to approve the property tax levy as presented 
 with the tax rates to be set at the August 17, 2009 Special Board of Supervisors’ meeting; motion 
 seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Roll Call Vote: Supervisor DeSpain, Supervisor Thompson and 
 Chairman Brownlow all voted “aye” and the vote was unanimous approving the motion.   
c. PUBLIC HEARING ON THE FINAL 2009-2010 BUDGET PURSUANT TO A.R.S. § 42-17104:  
 Chairman Brownlow opened the public hearing for the Final FY 2009-2010 budget and with no public 
 comment, closed the hearing.

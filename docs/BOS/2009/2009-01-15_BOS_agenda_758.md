NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ CHAMBERS 
THURSDAY, JANUARY 15, 2009 
SPECIAL MEETING 
 
3:06 p.m. Call to Order: Chairman Brownlow called the meeting to order.  
 
PRESENT:  Jerry Brownlow, Chairman; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Dusty Parsons, Assistant County 
Manager; and Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney were present in the board chambers.  Jesse Thompson, Vice-
Chairman; Jonathan Nez , Supervisor;  J.R. DeSpain , Supervisor; and David Tenney, Supervisor  participated by 
telephone. A speaker telephone was available in the chambers that made all meeting discussion and action audible 
for participants and audience members.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Consideration and possible appointment of a District Administrator  for the 
White Mountain Lakes Fire District : Deputy County Attorney, Jason Moore  advised the Board that the White 
Mountain Lakes Fire District Governing Board, which is a three member board now has three vacancies. Under 
Arizona law when that occurs the Bo ard of Supervisors is obligated to either appoint a District Administrator 
within 30 days or wait and call for a S pecial District election. Mr. Moore indicated that leaving the district 
without a governing body during that period of time  would not be helpf ul to the district especially because of 
the fact that the dist rict is currently facing some fiscal  challenges that need to be addressed immediately. It is 
the recommendation of the County Attorney’s office and the County Manager that the Board of Supervis ors 
accepts the resignations submitted by Larry Mullins and Robert Riester and appoint Dusty Parsons , the 
Assistant County Manger as the Fire District Administrator for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District , to have 
all powers and duties of the Fire Distr ict Board under A.R.S. 48-803(b). Supervisor DeSpain indicated that Sun 
Valley Fire District was in a similar circumstance and with the help of the Navajo County Finance Director, 
James Menlove, is now doing well. Supervisor DeSpain further indicated that this is the appropriate thing to do 
and he would appreciate the Board M embers support . Supervisor DeSpain  made a motion  to accept the 
resignations submitted by Larry Mullins and Robert Riester and appoint Dusty Parsons, the Assistant County 
Manger as the F ire District Administrator for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District, to have all powers and 
duties of the Fire District Board pursuant to A.R.S. 48 -803(b); motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson; vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
Dusty Parsons thanked James Menlove and Dirk Gardner for the work they have done so far and advised the 
Board that Navajo County would continue do all that we can to get White Mountain Lakes Fire District running 
effectively.    
 
ADJOURN:  At 3:12 p.m., Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion.

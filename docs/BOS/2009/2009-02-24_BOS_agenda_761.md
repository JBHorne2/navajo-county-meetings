NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, February 24, 2009 
   
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jonathan Nez, Supervisor; and Jesse 
Thompson, Vice-Chairman. 
 
9:03 a.m. Call to Order: 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Linda Morrow, School Superintendent, asked the Board of Supervisors for their 
continued support for Schools when working with the legislature. Mrs. Morrow encouraged Board members to 
keep our students in their hearts and stated that we cannot continue to have the budget woes of this State bore 
on the backs of our children.  Board members sang Happy Birthday to Linda Morrow. James Menlove, Finance 
Director, introduced the new Financial/Systems Analyst, Rulon Hemingway.  
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Juvenile Justice System January, 2009 report ; b) Constable reports: Winslow 
Precinct #3, January; Snowflake Precinct #3, January ; Show Low Precinct #5, January ; and Lakeside Precinct 
#6, January; c) Consideration and possible certification of revenue collected by Superior Court, Clerk of the 
Court and all Justice Courts ; d) Sympathy letter for Fran McCarroll  and Bill Bennet t; e) Consideration and 
possible approval of the Federal Equitable Sharing Agre ement with changes in Agency and Governing Body 
head; f) Thank you letters : Show Low Superintendent, Kevin Brackney; Show Low High School Principal, 
Farrell Adams; Show Low City Manager, Ed Muder; Show Low Mayor, Rick Fernau; Pilot, Danny Ortega; 
White Mou ntain Honor Guard, Bart Mosely; ADOT Regional Manager, Rick Long & Assistant Jesse 
Hitchcock; ADOT Regional Manger, Earl Craig & Assistant Diana Armijo; ADOT Regional Manager, Bruce 
Cooper & Assistant Jonny Judd; Steve Pysz; and Tim Norton ; g) Minutes: February 10, 2009; h) Consideration 
and possible approval of contract amendment 01 to increase WIA funds by $33,000.00 ; i) Consideration and 
possible approval of contract amendment 01 to increase WIA funds by $33,000.00 : Supervisor DeSpain made 
a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the items on the Consent Agenda; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion. Brad Carlyon, County Attorney, advised that item i, WIA funds 
should have been a contract amendment of an increase of $ 19, 312.00 and believes that it was a typographical 
error. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to change item i to $19, 312.00 ; motion seconded by Supervisor 
DeSpain. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
  
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: Gail Calisen , Human 
Resources Director, reviewed the 21 item Personnel Action report . Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to 
approve the Personnel Action report as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez; Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence: Shirley Hunt 
was recognized for her outstanding accomplishments. Ms. Hunt thanked the Board and indicated her 
appreciation for being a Navajo County employee.

Arizona Public Service Plant Manager, who spoke about the plant activities and the agenda item.

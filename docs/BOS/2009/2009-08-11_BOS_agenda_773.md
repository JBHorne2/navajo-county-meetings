NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, August 11, 2009 
   
PRESENT:  Supervisor DeSpain; Supervisor Nez; Supervisor Thompson; Chairman Brownlow were present in 
the Board Chambers. Supervisor Tenney was present by telephone.  
 
9:05a.m. Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation were led by Chairman Brownlow. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Supervisor DeSpain thanked the Public Works Director Homero Vela and his staff 
Greg Loper, Norm Cody and Linda Elliott for their attendance and hard work at the public meeting with the 
Cedar Hills residents regarding the Wind and Solar generation. He indicated that he was very appreciative of all 
that was involved in this public meeting.  
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Minutes: January 13, 2009 and July 28, 2009; b)Law Enforcement and Boating 
Safety Fund (LEBSF) grant project  from the State of Arizona through the Arizona State Parks Board in a total 
amount of $68,458.95 for FY 09/10 to the Navajo County Sheriff’s Office c) County Attorney Bad Check 
Program Quarterly Report for period ending June 30, 2009 d) Application for tax exe mption “Request for 
Redemption of Waiver” for Joe Hernandez e) Certify revenue collected by Superior Court, Clerk of the Court 
and all Justice Courts f) Legal Representation Agreement for the Public Safety Public Retirement System Local 
Board g) Letter in opposition of the Clean Water Restoration Act h) Name an existing road in the 
Heber/Overgaard area “Gambles Way” in Section 31, Township 12 North, Range 17 East i) Application for 
temporary extension of premises/patio permit to the Arizona Department of Liquor Licenses and Control for 
Raynita Carlson dba The Cabin Bar & Grill for a Cornhole Tournament fundraiser for cancer to be held the 
following dates: August 15 & 16; August 19; August 22 & 23; August 26; August 29 & 30, 2009 j) Warrants 
and Vouchers Report over $1000: July, 2009 k) Juvenile Justice System Report July, 2009 l) Clerk of Superior 
Court Report July, 2009 m) Constable Reports: Holbrook Precinct #1 July, 2009 n) Justice Court Reports: 
Holbrook #1, July; Winslow #2, July; Snowflake #3, July; Kay enta #4, July; Show Low #5, July; Pinetop #6,

presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence: Shanda Breed was recognized for being 
named Arizona Probation Training Officer of the Year from the State of Arizona. Supervisor DeSpain 
indicated that we have great employees and thanked Shanda for her work with Navajo County.  
b. Navajo County Cost Cutting Department Award: Elections Director, Lisa McKee and the Elections 
Department were recognized for practicing frugality and flexibility in the Elections Department in the 
time of budget cuts and for doing more with less.  
c. Personnel Service Awards: Mark Crandell, Richard Engler, Stephen Julian, Debbie Serna, Greg Kroeger 
and Gloria Hatton were recognized for their years of service to Navajo County. Supervisor DeSpain 
thanked Human Resources for continuing this program.  
d. Thank you letter to Holbrook Safeway Greg Duran, Manager: Supervisor Thompson and Sheriff Clark 
recognized Holbrook Safeway and Greg Duran, Manager for their cooperation and generous donation of 
bottled water to the reservation communities of northern Navajo County. Supervisor Nez also thanked 
Mr. Duran.  
 
HUMAN RESOURCES: Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions: Gail Calisen reviewed the 
11 item Personnel Action Report. Supervisor Thompson asked a question about the resignations. Ms. Calisen 
advised that the individuals either returned to school or relocated. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to 
approve the Personnel Action Report as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.

FINANCE:  
a. Consideration and possible adoption of Operating Budget Policy, Capital Budget Policy, Revenue 
Policy, Reserve Policy and Debt Policy: James Menlove discussed the item. Supervisor Thompson 
made a motion to adopt the Operating Budget Policy, Capital Budget Policy, Revenue Policy, Reserve 
Policy and the Debt Policy; motion seconded by Supervisor DeSpain who asked if these policies were in 
line with our audit. Mr. Menlove indicated that these policies will be included in our budget document 
for FY2010. Brad Carlyon had a concern about the Operating Budget Policies which was discussed. 
Supervisor Thompson asked for further explanation and indicated that more review needs to be made. 
Supervisor Nez asked for clarification about the policy for budgeted expenditure carry over. Mr. 
Menlove indicated that the intent is that our grants need to be self supporting along with all grants and 
programs and answered further questions of the Board members. Mr. Jayne proposed adding the words 
“or be leveraged to the maximum extent possible”. Mr. Jayne advised that the Grant Administrator starts 
on August 24
th.  Supervisor Thompson amended his motion; Supervisor DeSpain asked that this item 
be tabled. Mr. Menlove answered further questions. Supervisor Nez indicated that he supports this item 
being tabled to the next agenda. Supervisor Thompson withdrew the motion. Chairman Brownlow 
tabled the item.

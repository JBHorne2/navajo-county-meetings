620 E McNeil, Show Low Arizona 
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
FRIDAY, October 23, 2009   
 
PRESENT: David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Chairman were 
present at 620 E McNeil in Show Low Arizona.  
10:00 a.m.  Call To Order:  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There was no response to Call to the Public.  
PINETOP FIRE DISTRICT/COUNTY ATTORNEY:  Consideration and possible approval of a Grant In-
Aid Agreement between the Tohono O’odham Nation and Navajo County on behalf of the Pinetop Fire District: 
Jason Moore discussed the item. Stuart Bishop thanked the Board. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to 
approve a Grant In-Aid Agreement between the Tohono O’odham Nation and Navajo Count y on behalf of the 
Pinetop Fire District; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN:
 
 At 10:12 a.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

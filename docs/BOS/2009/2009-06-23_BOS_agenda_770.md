NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, June 23, 2009 
   
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jonathan Nez, Supervisor; Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Chairman.   
 
9:05a.m. Call To Order:  The Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Chairman Brownlow. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Enzo DiMartino, owner of Enzo’s Italian Restaurant in Snowflake and Richard G. 
Young of Holbrook, AZ spoke to the Board regarding Ordinance No. PHSD 01-09, the Public Health Services 
District Licensing Fees Ordinance passed on June 9, 2009.  Mr. DiMartino stated he has less than 30 seats in his 
restaurant and his fee increased 300% and requested the Board reconsider the ordinance and postpone the fee 
increase until the economy has improved.  Mr. Young indicated that the increase is excessive and that small 
businesses will be adversely affected.

e.    Consideration and possible adoption of cost saving measures that may include salary reductions and 
furloughs.  James Menlove discussed the item and provided information showing budget projections which 
explained the budget deficit of $700,000 to $800,000 , and after presenting the two options, asked that some 
type of cost savings measure be considered, including furloughs.  Erin Amos from Taylor spoke to the board 
and suggested that other options be considered , one of wh ich would be input from a citizen’s committee. 
County Attorney Brad Carlyon , Assessor Cammy Darris, School Superintenden t Linda Morrow  and Judge 
Ruechel recommended Option B.  Judge Ruechel also suggested  that employees be kept informed with  
quarterly financial reviews and raised the question as to how 24/7 shift operation employees will be treated.  
The County Manager responded that the Sheriff had provided a list of those employees  affected.  Also 
included are the Chief Deputies of elected officials.   
 
Supervisor Tenney made the motion that a temporary 2.5% reduction in salary be imposed for the 
upcoming fiscal year and that each employee, where the salary reduction is imposed, receive 52 hours of 
additional personal time.  Excluded from the 2.5% salary reduction are the three categories: 
1. Chief Deputies of the Elected Officials; 
2. The list of patrol officers and detention officers submitted by the Navajo County Sheriff’s Office; and 
3. The list of Juvenile Detention officers submitted by the Navajo County Superior Court and the two 
medical examiners who work for the Navajo County Health Department.  
The motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

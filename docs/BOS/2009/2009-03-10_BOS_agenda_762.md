NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, MARCH 10, 2009 
 
PRESENT: Jerry Brownlow, Chairman; Jesse Thompson, Vice-Chairman; and Jonathan Nez, Supervisor were 
present.  
 
9:05 a.m.  Call to Order: Supervisor Nez led the Pledge of Allegiance and offered the invocation. 
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC:   No response. 
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  :  a) Application for a special event liquor license for a fund raising event on April 18, 
2009 for St. Anthony School at Pinetop Country Club, 6734 Country Club Drive, Pinetop; b) Reissuance of 
Redemption Checks; c) Letters to Congresswoman Kirkpatrick requesting support to fund Army Corps Feasibility 
Study for t he Winslow Levee Rehabilitation, Pinon Road Yard,  and Obed Bridge; d)  Letter to Senator Kyl 
requesting support to fund  the Army Corps Feasibility Study for the Winslow Levee Rehabilitation; e) Sympathy 
letters for Mark Herrington and Rick Slone; f) Cell phone stipend changes; g) Justice Court reports: Holbrook #1, 
February; Winslow #2, February; Snowflake #3, February; Ka yenta #4, February; Show Low #5, February; Pinetop 
#6, February, 2009; h) Contract Amendment Keefe Commissary Network, LLC; i) Contracts signed by County 
Manager per Board of Supervisors resolution: Contract Extension #B07 -010-001 VitalChek and Navajo Coun ty 
Library District Contract with SirsiDynix in the amount of $1,500.00  
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the items on the Consent Agenda; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
  
Human Resources: Consideration and action related to Personnel Action report : Gail Calisen summarized the 
37 item Personnel Action Report . Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the Personnel Action 
Report as presented ; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.   Chairman 
Brownlow explained that the six layoff positions in the Judicial Branch were due to state budget cuts. 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  
a. Quality Emulsions and Navajo County Recognizing Excellence: Cherlyn Yazzie, Program Manager for 
the AZ Nutrition Network, along with her staff, was recognized by Mary Tyler for bringing nutrition 
education to second and fifth graders from Kayenta to Cibecue and everywhere in between.   
b. Navajo County Cost Cutting Awards: Dan Hinz recognized Leanne Baker from Facilities Management 
for her suggestion to convert C-fold paper towels to roll dispenser paper towels at a cost savings to the 
county of $5,600, 60% of the annual budget for paper towels. 
c. Personnel Service Awards: Delanna Caraway recognized Nancy Farr, Craig Urling, Joshua Reizer, 
Darin Foshee, and Donette Goodman for their years of service to Navajo County. 
 
ADMINISTRATION/COUNTY ATTORNEY:  
Consideration and possible approval of a contract with John Flynn, PolicyLogic, LLC., naming him the successor 
District Administrator for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District and relieving Dusty Parsons:   Jason Moore , 
Deputy County Attorney, discussed the item.   He recommended that Dusty Parsons, who has fulfilled his initial job 
duties, be released from his duties as District Administrator for the White Mountain Lakes Fire District .  Mr. Moore 
recommended that John Flynn of PolicyLogic, LLC  be appointed.  Mr. Flynn  has over twenty ye ars of fire district 
experience. Larry Messick, Chairman of the White Mountain Lakes Fire District Advisory Committee, addressed the 
board and recommended the appointment of John Flynn.  Mr. Flynn then addressed the board and stated his 
qualifications.

ELECTIONS:  
a. Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 10-09 canceling the election for Timberland 
Acres Domestic Water Improvement District scheduled for May 19, 2 009 and appointing Sharon Shields, 
with a four (4) year term to the Board of Directors as she was the only candidate who filed nominating 
papers for the one (1) vacant position : Following Election Director Lisa McKee’s review of this item, 
Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 10-09 cancelling the election and 
appointing Sharon Shields with a four year term to the Board of Directors ; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Nez.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
b. Consideration and possible ad option of Resolution Number 11-09 canceling the election for Timberland 
Acres Special Road District #1 scheduled for May 19, 2009 and appointing Brian Shields and Caiaphas (CD) 
Nunnally both with a three (3) year term to the Board of Directors as they were the only candidates who filed 
nominating papers for the two (2) vacant positions:  Lisa McKee discussed the item.  Supervisor Thompson 
made a motion to adopt Resolution 11 -09 as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez.  Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
 
SHERIFF: Consideration and possible approval to enter into Agreements for Professional Services for 
appointments as Alternate Medical Examiners with up to eight physicians for medical examiner services : Chief 
Deputy Bernard Huser reviewed the item and introduced Brock Bevel l, Medical Examiner for the county, who put 
the information together.  Currently the Medical Examiner does not have the authority to perform autopsies which 
are being done in Tucson.  Supervisor Nez made a motion to enter into agr eements for professional services  for 
appointment as Alternate Medical Examiners with up to eight physicians for medical examiner services; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Thompson.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
PUBLIC WORKS:   
a. Consideration and possible approval of the amended lease agreement for the boundary of the Navajo County 
business site in the Kayenta Township : Homero Vela discussed the item.  Supervisor Nez made a motion 
to amend the lease agreement for the boundary of the Navajo County business site in the Kayenta Township; 
Supervisor Nez made the statement that he had run this item by the Navajo Nation Department of Justice and 
for the county’s information there are certain business site leases in Kayenta that are still under the auspices 
of the Navajo Nation, however, this is an older lease that is under the Kayenta Township.  It will go through 
the Navajo Nation Department of Justice before signature of the Navajo Nation President.  Supervisor 
Thompson stated a contingent from Navajo Coun ty made an onsite visit and seconded the motion. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
b. Consideration and possible award of Contract #B09 -01-014 purchase of one Crack Sealer to Crafco, Inc. in 
the amount of $58,288.54 : Homero Vela  discussed the item , which f ulfilled Navajo County bidding 
procedures, and recommended approval.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to award contract B09-01-
014 for the purchase of one Crack Sealer to Crafco, Inc. in the amount of $58,288.54; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Nez.  Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor Nez proposed a question on due 
diligence in the procurement process which was answered by County Attorney Brad Carlyon. 
c.    Navajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors’ Session: Consideration and possible approval to         
enter into a Joint Funding Agreement with the United States Geological Survey for water resources 
investigations and the maintenance agreement for 3 Surface -Water Ga uging Stations:  Supervisor Nez 
made a motion to enter into the Na vajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors’ Session; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Trent Larson 
discussed the item.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to approve Navajo County Flood Control

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS: Consideration and possible approval of an amendment to 
Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) #29881 between the Arizona Supreme Court, Administrative Office of the 
Courts (AOC), the Navajo County Board of Supervisors through th e Navajo County School Superintendent, and 
the Navajo County Superior Court through the Juvenile Court; for Title I and other associated funds for juvenile

Administrative Office of the Courts (AOC), the Navajo County Board of Supervisors through the Navajo County

FINANCE: 
a. Consideration and possible adoption of updated travel policy :  Mary Springer discussed the changes 
and updates in the travel policy.  She introduced Bencita Nelson who is instrumental on a day to day 
basis in the implementation of the policy.   Supervisor Thompson made a motion  to approve the 
updated travel policy as presented; seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous appro ving the 
motion. 
b. Consideration and possible approval to ratify execution of $54,200 contract with Maximus 
Consulting Services, Inc. dated as of February 10, 2009, for County -wide user fee study identifying 
potential sources of revenue for County departments:  Mary Springer discussed the item ; Supervisor 
Thompson made a motion  to ratify the contract; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion. 
c. Budget Update:   Finance Director James Menlove addressed the board regarding a summary as to 
where the county currently stands financially and how the county can remain fiscally sound.   Mr. 
Menlove responded to a question from Supervisor Thompson regarding audit status  and a question 
from Supervisor Nez regarding the availability of an econ omist.  Further the County Manager 
responded to an inquiry by Supervisor Nez with regards to the possibility of a part time economist on 
staff.  No action. 
 
BOARD BUSINESS: Report from Board Members : Supervisor Thompson indicated that there was a very 
healthy discussion with the Hopi Tribe in a meeting set up by Supervisor Nez  and attended by chapter officials 
from District I.  There was also a meeting with a veterans group and they are very interesting in creating further 
partnership with the county.

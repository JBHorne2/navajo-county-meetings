e. Fiscal Years 2009/2010 Budget Updates: James Menlove and Dusty Parsons discussed the item and 
answered questions. Mr. Menlove reviewed the revenue budget report which was handed out to the Board 
members. The reports gave estimated figures as to the Legislative and the Governor’s budget, and the hits 
taken to Navajo County by both budgets. Mr. Menlove indicated about a $700,000 shortfall in the 2009-
2010 budget.  James Jayne asked Mr. Menlove to clarify the new amounts shown. Mr. Menlove indicated 
that the vacancy savings, utility cost savings and the four day work week have now been included in this 
amount and the amount was reduced by $100,000. Dusty Parsons gave a brief overview of the decline in 
revenue and County sales tax. Mr. Parsons discussed the options. Mr. Parsons discussed a possibility of 
furlough time, 2 hours per pay period and further indicated that he will likely be coming back on June 23 
and make a recommendation. Mr. Parsons indicated that we are trying to save jobs. Mr. Menlove indicated 
that Board policy is to set aside 10% of our budget and indicated that we may not be able to make our 
payroll if we tap into that contingency. We are at a minimum as required to keep functioning financially as 
a county. Marlin Gillespie addressed the Board regarding the option of furloughs. Mr. Gillespie indicated 
that furloughs could be disastrous to some of the employees. Mr. Gillespie encouraged the Board to 
consider furloughs as a last resort. Sheriff Clark, Assessor Cammy Darris, Recorder Laurie Justman, 
Treasurer Manny Hernandez, Clerk of the Court Juanita Mann and County Attorney Brad Carlyon 
addressed the Board regarding the option of furloughs and asked the Board to look at other options with 
the last option being furlough hours. Assessor Cammy Darris read a letter into the record from 
Superintendent of Schools, Linda Morrow asking the Board that furloughs be the last resort. Mrs. Darris 
indicated that as a whole they would rather have a furlough than a layoff. LaRee Saline, Dan Hinz, Doris 
Clark, Debra Kester,  David Shondee and Barbara Hunt addressed the Board regarding the option of 
furloughs. James Jayne indicated a cell phone stipend and take home vehicle list will be available to the 
Board on the 23rd of June.

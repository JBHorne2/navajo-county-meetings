NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES 
WEDNESDAY, May 27, 2009 
   
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jonathan Nez, Supervisor;  Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; and Jerry Brownlow, Chairman were present.  
 
9:02 a.m.  Call To Order: 
 
Finance Director, James Menlove, reviewed the fiscal year 2009/2010 Budget Guidelines adopted by the Board 
on February 10, 2009. 
 
BUDGET HEARINGS FOR DISCUSSION ONLY 
All proposed budgets available for review through the Finance Department. 
 
Judicial Branch: Judge Ruechel reviewed the Judicial Branch budget which included the Superior Court, Clerk 
of the Superior Court, Adult Probation, Juvenile Probation, Juvenile Detention and all of the Justice Courts 
(Holbrook, Winslow, Snowflake, Show Low, Pinetop/Lakeside and Kayenta). She outlined the cost increases 
which included salaries, mental health costs and the triennial audit which are mandatory every three years. She 
indicated that overall the Judicial Branch had a decrease of $4,862.00 from fiscal year 2009 budget.  
 
Constables: Finance Director James Menlove and County Manager James Jayne reviewed the Constables 
budget and indicated that their budget remains the same as last fiscal year.  
 
Treasurer: Treasurer Manny Hernandez reviewed the Treasurer’s budget indicated that adjustments were made 
for temporary wages, postage, duplicating, travel, publishing and advertising.  He indicated that by printing the 
tax bills in state there will be a savings.  
 
Superintendent of Schools: Superintendent of Schools Linda Morrow reviewed the schools budget. She 
indicated that 96% of her budget covers salary or salary related expenses. County Attorney, Brad Carlyon, 
thanked Linda Morrow, Jason Moore and Manny Hernandez for ensuring that Show Low School District and 
Blue Ridge School District were able to make payroll last Friday and the remainder of the fiscal year.  
 
Recorder: Chief Deputy Recorder Barbara Hunt indicated that Recorder Laurie Justman was not present due to 
illness. She further indicated that the Recorder’s budget has remained the same except for the cellular phone 
which was dropped in the interest of saving money.  Supervisor DeSpain extended his appreciation and best of 
health to the Recorder.  
 
Assessor: Assessor Cammy Darris indicated that the Assessor’s budget increased .34 cents; Supervisor DeSpain 
extended his congratulations on the retirement of Lola Sibcy Chief Deputy Assessor. James Jayne County 
Manager thanked the Assessor for her partnership in helping us with the software and finding solutions for the 
Superintendent’s Office.  
 
Sheriff/Jail: Sheriff KC Clark reviewed the Sheriff’s and the Jail’s budget. The Board of Supervisors extended 
their appreciation to Sheriff Clark. Supervisor Nez advised the Board of the Sheriff’s Navajo name “Dirty 
Mustache”.

County Attorney: County Attorney Brad Carlyon reviewed the County Attorney’s budget. He indicated that he 
is still working hard to figure out Special Fund balances. He indicated that there are some systematic problems 
in the system that we have been using. The Board offered their support to Mr. Carlyon and encouraged an open 
dialogue for new philosophy.  
 
Board of Supervisors/Administration: Finance Director James Menlove reviewed the budgets for the Board 
of Supervisors and Administration. He indicated that the revenues are still declining and Finance intends to 
bring forth an update the first meeting in June.  
ADJOURN: At 10:38 a.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.

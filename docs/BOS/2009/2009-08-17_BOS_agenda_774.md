NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
MONDAY, August 17, 2009 
   
PRESENT:  J.R. DeSpain, Supervisor and Jerry Brownlow, Chairman were pr esent in the Board Chambers. David Tenney, 
Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, Supervisor were present telephonically.  
 
11:09 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation was led by Supervisor DeSpain  
 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Special event liquor license for a Car Show, Labor Day and Fall Festival to be held on August 22 nd 
& August 23rd, September 5th & 6th; and September 19, 2009, with events to be held at 2068 Lumber Valley Road, Overgaard, 
AZ as requested by the Sons of the American Legion Squadron #86  Supervisor DeSpain made a  motion to authorize the 
Chairman to sign the items on the consent agenda as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.  
 
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of one (1) polling place change for the upcoming Special General election 
on November 3, 2009: Lisa McKee discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Supervisor DeSpain made a 
motion to approve the consolidation of the Whiteriver one and two election sites for the November election and to have the 
Elections Department move forward with the application to make it a permanent elections site; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Thompson. 10:15 a.m. Jonathon Nez, Supervisor is present appearing telephonically. Vote unanimous approving the 
motion.  
 
FINANCE:  
a. Consideration and possible award of contract for services in an amount not to exceed $21,000 for legislative advocacy 
and support for the Transportation Investment Generating Economic Recovery (“TIGER”) grant for the Southern 
Navajo County Regional Corridor: Mary Springer discussed the item and answered questions from the Board. 
Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to award contract for services in an amount not to exceed $21,000 for legislative 
advocacy and support for the Transportation Investment Generating Economic Recovery grant for the Southern Navajo 
County Regional Corridor; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
b. Consideration and possible adoption of the tax rates to support the adopted 2009-10 budget:    
i. Navajo County Public Schools’ 2009-10 tax rates: Linda Morrow discussed the item and answered questions 
of the Board. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to adopt the rates as printed; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Thompson. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ii. Navajo County 2009-10 tax rates: Lauren Sedillo discussed the item and answered questions of the Board. Ms. 
Sedillo advised that the county is required by law to assess the state equalization property tax today; that the 
legislature is considering repealing the tax as part of its budget negotiations, but that no such action has yet 
occurred. She further indicated that if the situation changes, the board will then have to take action to comply 
with the law. Ms. Sedillo indicated that the State Equalization property tax is .3306. Supervisor DeSpain 
made a motion to adopt the primary rate of .5594 for Navajo County; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion. Supervisor DeSpain made a motion to adopt the 2009-2010 tax rates 
as presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
ADJOURN: at 11:35 a.m. Supervisor Thompson made a  motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

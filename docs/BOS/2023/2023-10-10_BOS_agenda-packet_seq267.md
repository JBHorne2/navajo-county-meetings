b. Consideration of adoption of Resolution Number 34-2023, adopting the
new voting precincts maps that were adjusted to meet the New Board of
Supervisor Districts pursuant to A.R.S. 16-411 :
Jason Moore stated that they are not asking for final approval today,
because they still need to make some final adjustments to the Justice of
the Peace precincts and there can’t be a finalized map until Justice Court
precincts are lined out.  A presentation was shown.  He indicated that Title
22 allows the Justice Court precincts to be redrawn at any time.  He
stated that they are also requesting that there not be a final approve due
to concerns from some citizens with the precinct lines and that they would
like to sit down with leadership of both political parties in Navajo County to
get input on what they would like to see for the lines.  He provided some
background regarding the redistricting process from 2010, the multiple
precincts that were in place at that time and the legislation that was
passed in 2011 to adopt voting centers.  He stated that when you use the
precinct-based voting system the precincts were more important than they
are today.  He talked about the frustration of voters going to a voting
precinct to then be told they were in the wrong precinct and then having to
go to a different precinct to be able vote, not being able to vote or if they
insisted on voting at the wrong location being issued a provisional ballot
and that ballot not counting due to it being cast in the wrong precinct.  He
stated voting centers allow voters to cast their vote at any voting center
within Navajo County.  He talked about some provisions in the law for
combining precincts under certain circumstances when no other precinct
is available, indicating that precincts are drawn for administrative
purposed only and help the Elections Department and Recorder put on
the election and not for the benefit of a person or political party.  He stated
that the reason this is before the Board now is due to redistricting having
taken place earlier this year and it being necessary to adjust the precinct
boundaries accordingly and the statute saying is has to be done by

differences between the various counties, and indicated that the precincts
we have are already included within election district boundaries of the

Arizona that puts a minimum or maximum number for precincts that you
can have or the number of people that you can have within a
precinct.

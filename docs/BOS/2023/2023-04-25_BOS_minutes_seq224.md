SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

Item 6b was addressed out of order.

a.

Presentation of Lifesaving Awards to Detention Deputies Garrison Yazzie and Adrian Barrera , and Medical Staff Josh Anderson and Beth Miller , for life-saving efforts at the Navajo County Detention Facility and Presentation of Sheriff's Commendations to Detention Sergeants Marty Boisvert and Christopher Young, Detention Deputies Brian McElrath, Dalin Neff, and Nathan King, and Medical Staff Angie Colwell, Alicia LaDuke, and Noreen Bridges :
Sheriff David Clouse talked about the different roles that the detention staff handle each day.  He stated that detention staff lives by the slogan care, custody and control and indicated that no matter what situation someone has found themselves in the detention staff takes care of everyone.  He talked about the issues with fentanyl and how, no matter how much they try to keep the jail safe from contraband, it has at times made its way in.  He indicated that a few years ago they were seeing an increase with fentanyl exposure in the jail, and he talked about the measures that were taken to try to help eliminate the contraband coming in.  He addressed the situation that led to the lifesaving efforts and the response from staff, stating that they conducted lifesaving efforts for 45 minutes until the inmate was taken from the facility.  He recognized each of the individuals for their lifesaving efforts and heroic actions. He indicated that they put care, custody, and control into every day.  He presented the staff with awards for their actions. He stated that it takes 24-hour care and constant vigilant watch over three hundred inmates every day and he thanked them for their great work.

b.

Consideration of a proclamation declaring the week of May 7 - May 13, 2023, as "National Correctional Officers' Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe :
Sheriff David Clouse talked about the history of National Correctional Officers Week and indicated that Navajo County has recognized it for many years to honor the profession of the Correctional Officers.  He indicated that there is a need for a well-staffed jail, not only for their safety but also for the safety of the inmates.  He talked about the training he attended that was focused on the operations of a jail and indicated that sheriff's from across the country are of the same mindset that you can't learn enough about the complex operations that happen in a jail.  He stated that he is grateful to the jail staff.  He read the proposed proclamation.  He requested that May 7-13, 2023, be proclaimed as National Correctional Officers Week.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve a proclamation declaring the week of May 7 - May 13, 2023, as "National Correctional Officers' Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe, motion seconded by Supervisor Benally.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration of agreement on the receipt of funds through the AZ Department of Public Safety Border Strike Force for the purchase of 6 fully equipped patrol vehicles :
Chief Deputy Brian Swanty talked about the proposal that the Sheriff's Office submitted to the Arizona Department of Public Safety through the Border Strike Force Fund that is to combat human and drug smuggling in Arizona and that they have been awarded approximately $582,000.  He stated that the contract is for the purchase of six fully equipped patrol vehicles, two for the canines and four for the drug task force detectives.  He requested approval of the contract.

Supervisor Whitesinger thanked the Sheriff's Office for looking out for the citizens and providing services that are needed.  She stated that she is grateful for all they do.
Supervisor Seymore talked about the dedication of the Peace Officers Memorial and commended the Sheriff and Sheriff's for their service and the work they did on the memorial.  He thanked them for their efforts and for all they do for Navajo County.
Supervisor Benally thanked the Sheriff's Office for all they do.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to approve an agreement on the receipt of funds through the AZ Department of Public Safety Border Strike Force for the purchase of 6 fully equipped patrol vehicles, motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

He thanked staff for all that they do to make things happen.

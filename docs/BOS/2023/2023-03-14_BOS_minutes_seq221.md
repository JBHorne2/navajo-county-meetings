REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the month of March 2023 as "National Nutrition Month 2023" :
Janelle Linn introduced Ivy McCullough, the WIC Division Manager and Registered Dietitian.  A presentation was shown.  She introduced the WIC Team, indicating that they are all an asset to the program. She talked about the services that the WIC program provides.  She stated that the program serves approximately forty-three percent of all infants born in the U.S. and twenty percent of all children up to their fifth birthday. She indicated that the program helps with nutrition security during critical stages of growth and development.   She talked about the caseload for the monthly participation over the past few years.  She advised that they have offices in Show Low, Snowflake, Holbrook, Winslow, Heber and a satellite office in Pinetop and that the services are available to all residents of the county if they qualify based on income eligibility, category and nutritional risk.  She talked about how they provided services remotely during COVID and indicated that they will continue to provide remote services when possible.  She reviewed the average numbers for services provided to woman, infants, and children in the form of a food package since 2019, indicating that they anticipate the numbers to increase, and she thanked the Board for their support of the program.  She advised that Navajo County was recognized this year for the largest increase in redemption rates.  She reviewed the structure of the WIC benefits indicating it is more than a food assistance program.  She stated that nationally for every dollar spent on WIC approximately $2.48 is returned in healthcare cost savings.  She talked about the 2023 special projects that are planned.  She talked about the partnership with the AZ Farmers Market Nutrition Program that is new this year, indicating that they are currently working with the farmers markets in Show Low, Snowflake/Taylor and Winslow to be added to the list.  She addressed things that they will be working on going forward.
Supervisor Seymore thanked Ms. McCullough for all that they do with the WIC program.
Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked the team for all that they do.  He talked about the health crisis and indicated that what they are doing matters.  He talked about his appreciation for the partnership with the Farmer's Market and that they hope to find ways to partner with them to get the information out to the communities.
Glenn Kephart addressed the increase in numbers for the program and the health benefits.
Ms. McCullough thanked her team and indicated she appreciates the relationships that they have with their clients.  She read the proposed Proclamation.

Vice-Chairman Whiting made a motion  to approve a Proclamation declaring the month of March 2023 as "National Nutrition Month 2023", motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Motion passed 4-0 with Supervisor Whitesinger being absent.

Vice-Chairman Whiting made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Supervisor Whitesinger being absent.

COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

Consideration of award of Contract B23-01-003 Juvenile Diversion Services for Navajo County Attorney's office to Advent Financial Systems, LLC :
Brad Carlyon indicated that this contract for critical thinking skills for kids who will be a part of a program.  He stated that currently in the juvenile justice system a juvenile who gets in trouble for lower-level offense can be diverted by the Probation but still appears in the system for having been charged.  He stated that this program allows law enforcement officers who encounter juveniles who commit a limited crimes to initiate consequences at that time if the parents and victims agree to it.  He indicated that the program will be monitored through the County Attorney's Office.  He talked about how the program works.  He stated that if a kid can do the diversion program it will keep them out of the system and provide quicker consequences.  He talked about starting the program in one district to work out the kinks and then they will expand the program county wide.
Vice-Chairman Whiting expressed his appreciate to the County Attorney's Office for looking for ways to partner and for seeing the bigger picture and finding solutions to accomplish it.
Supervisor Benally inquired if the program will also be on the reservation.
Mr. Carlyon talked about how it would be limited due to the State not having jurisdiction over Native Americans on reservation land.

Vice-Chairman Whiting made a motion  to approve award of Contract B23-01-003 Juvenile Diversion Services for Navajo County Attorney's office to Advent Financial Systems, LLC, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Supervisor Whitesinger being absent.

FACILITIES MANAGEMENT :

a.

Consideration of a grant agreement between the Arizona State Parks and Navajo County to accept $100,790.00 from the FY2023 Heritage Fund Historic Preservation Grant Program for Pinedale Elementary School Restoration Project (Phase I), and authorize staff to sign the grant agreement :
Ken Maruyama stated that the Pinedale Elementary School is a nationally registered historic place and that it is culturally significant is the Pinedale community.  He advised that it needs some repairs and restoration work, that Navajo County hired JWA Architects and that the work will be conducted in two phases.  He stated that they were successful in receiving a grant from the State's Historic Preservation Heritage Fund Program for phase one of the project. He requested approval of the item and acceptance of the grant.
Supervisor Seymore talked about the phases of the project and thanked staff for the work done on this.  He stated that Navajo County had the highest grading on the grant.  He indicated that this project will be a great benefit to the community.
Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked Supervisor Seymore for his leadership on this project and thanked Mr. Maruyama and the team for the work done on this item.
Mr. Maruyama talked about those on the team that have worked on the grant efforts.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve a grant agreement between the Arizona State Parks and Navajo County to accept $100,790.00 from the FY2023 Heritage Fund Historic Preservation Grant Program for Pinedale Elementary School Restoration Project (Phase I), and authorize staff to sign the grant agreement, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Whiting .  Motion passed 4-0 with Supervisor Whitesinger being absent.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Consideration of  award of contract number B22-11-009 Public Health Medical Consultation/Alternative Medical Examiner Services to Wade Kartchner, MD, MPH, Public Health Partners, LLC :
Janelle Linn addressed the bid process, indicating that they only received one proposal for this contract, that the proposal was evaluated and received a cumulative score of 291 out of 300.  She stated that the provider is highly qualified and has more than ten years in public health and medical examiner services.  She stated staff recommends approval.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve award of contract number B22-11-009 Public Health Medical Consultation/Alternative Medical Examiner Services to Wade Kartchner, MD, MPH, Public Health Partners, LLC, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Seymore thanked Ms. Linn and her team for continuing to find qualified providers for services.

2.

Consideration of award of contract B22-11-014 Nurse Practitioner Services to Jaskiranjit Hanson, MSN, FNP-BC :
Janelle Linn stated that this contact is to provide services in the areas of reproductive health, STI'S, HIV, immunization consulting and to perform physical exams associated with those services.  She talked about the bid process, indicated that they received two proposals for the contract, that they were evaluated and that the evaluation committee is recommending consideration of approval to Jaskiranjit Hanson.  She stated that staff recommends approval.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve award of contract B22-11-014 Nurse Practitioner Services to Jaskiranjit Hanson, MSN, FNP-BC, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Ms. Linn for the time spent on selecting those that will best serve our communities.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :
Supervisor Seymore wished everyone a Happy New Year.  He stated that he is grateful for those who are caring and having hopeful prospective in life.
Supervisor Whiting reminded the Board that the Legislative Reception will be next Wednesday at the CSA building.  He encouraged the Board reach out and invite our delegation.  He indicated that the Legislative Session started yesterday and looking forward to this it and that he appreciates the leadership and relationships that continue to develop while working with them on behalf of the citizens and help them understand the impact at a local level.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai stated that he is attending the Navajo Nation Inauguration today along with other County Officials and staff.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked everyone for the representation throughout the State at the different events and stated that they appreciate those partnerships.  She talked about how each of us play an important part, whether in life, at work, in our families or relationships in the communities, we all have an important role.  She wished everyone a Happy New Year.

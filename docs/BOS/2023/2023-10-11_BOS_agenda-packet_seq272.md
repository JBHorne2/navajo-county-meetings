Navajo County - For Official Use Only
Public Official vs. Private Citizen
• For current Elected Officials, campaign season can be tricky to 
navigate
• To avoid violating the law or the perception of impropriety, extra 
caution is useful
• Clearly know when you are a public official versus a private citizen
• Public Official = County Supervisor
• Private Citizen = Candidate for County Supervisor

<!-- page 7 -->
Navajo County - For Official Use Only
Good 
Stewards of 
Public 
Resources
• Arizona Revised Statutes prohibits use of 
public resources for private gain
• ARS 13-802
• County resources cannot be used to support 
campaign activities 
• County resources include:
• Facilities, equipment, personnel, or supplies
• Clearly delineating time between public 
official and private citizen allows:
• The county to maintain public trust 
• The county to be good stewards of public 
resources
• Prevents violation of state law

Navajo County - For Official Use Only
Background
• The American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) of 2021 provided direct 
payments to state, tribal and local governments to help address the 
negative economic impacts associated with the COVID19 pandemic. 
• In January 2022, the County Board of Supervisors approved a 
funding package of $21.1 M:
• $18.7 M for county-wide projects, 
• $1.35 M for community partnerships, and 
• $1 M for project management and contingency. 
• The funding package leverages another $18.1 M of contributions 
and partnerships from other sources:
• a total expected project value of $42.1 M for our communities.

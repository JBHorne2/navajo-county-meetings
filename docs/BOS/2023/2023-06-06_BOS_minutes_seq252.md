NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, June 6, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:08 a.m. Call To Order: The meeting was called to order by Chairman Peshlakai at 9:08 a.m.

CONSENT AGENDA:

a.

Minutes: Executive Session Minutes, May 23, 2023: Supervisor Jason Whiting made a motion to approve the Executive Session Minutes from May 23, 2023; motion seconded by Supervisor Daryl Seymore. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:

a.

EXECUTIVE SESSION  pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.03 (A)(1) for the Board to review semi-finalists information for the County Manager position. Supervisor Jason Whiting made a motion to enter into Executive Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Daryl Seymore . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

RECONVENE:  Consideration and possible action concerning semi-finalists for County Manager position. Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to move to the next step of in-person interview with candidate number 1 and candidate number 2; motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore.  Motion passed in favor 4-1, with Supervisor Benally voting against the motion.

Supervisor Seymore recommended that as we bring these finalists in, that we bring them in for a final interview next Tuesday and that we coordinate some type of open house setting with the finalist, Elected Officials and others so that others can meet with those candidates. He further recommended that the Board be ready to discuss contract stipulations at the next executive session as we move forward. Mr. Carlyon concurred with Supervisor Seymore's recommendation and indicated that the Board can direct staff on how to proceed with contract negotiations.

ADJOURN 10:46 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

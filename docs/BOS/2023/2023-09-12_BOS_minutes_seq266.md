b.

Consideration of award of Contract #B23-06-014 Code Enforcement Facility (Architect) to JWA Architects, LLC :
Grant Evans stated that this item is for the architectural and engineering design contract for the Code Enforcement Facility that will be constructed in Taylor.  A presentation was shown.  He talked about the reason for the project being needed due to the lack of workspace for animal control, environmental health, and the medical examiner for the specialized mandated services to the communities and lack of meeting and training space.  He indicated upon completion those needs will be met as well as having all the services in one facility, having a centralized location and being able to leverage and utilize existing county property.  He further indicated this will be purpose-built for the users and the specialized services they provide which will help with their workflow.  He stated that currently the County leases workspace at the Show Low Airport and cooler space for the medical examiner and that they\will be able to exit or reduce those spaces once the facility is complete.  He indicated that they will build a Sheriff's substation as part of the facility which will allow for them to relocate from Silver Creek Facility and will allow that area to be remodeled and repurposed for training and meeting space as well as flex offices where the substation is currently located.  He reviewed the floor plan for the new facility, indicating that they have been collaborating with the Sheriff's Office to meet the needs for their services.  He stated that the building will be approximately 9100 square feet.  He talked about the location of the facility, indicating that they will have its own driveway and entrance to separate them from the Public Works operations.  He talked about the estimated schedule for the design and bid phase as well as the construction phase.  He talked about the project funding which includes some ARPA funds allocation and a 2.2 million one-time allocation from the general fund for capital projects. He addressed the RFQ process that was conducted and indicated that JWA was the highest scoring respondent.  He stated that staff is recommending approval.

Vice-Chairman Whiting made a motion  to approve award of Contract #B23-06-014 Code Enforcement Facility (Architect) to JWA Architects, LLC, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Presentation regarding Navajo County Precincts :
Renee Rowles stated that she is a registered voter living in Taylor and stated that she is going to talk about if Navajo County precinct definitions are in compliance with ARS 16-411.  She talked about what a precinct is and the requirements for complying.  She reviewed portions of ARS 16-411 and talked about the definition of a precinct, precinct boundaries, and political subdivisions.  She talked about the survey done in 2004 by the United States Election Assistance Committee regarding precinct sizes across the United States and addressed the varying sizes of precincts in Navajo County.  She compared Navajo County precinct sizes to other precincts within the State of Arizona.  She addressed the difference between precincts and precinct parts.
She provided some examples for different precincts.  She talked about reasons for dividing precincts and the need for additional representation in some of the areas.  She talked about some of the political subdivisions in Navajo County, including incorporated municipalities, school districts, congressional legislative districts, the Board of Supervisors and Justice of the Peace districts.  She indicated that she made a public records request to determine why the number of precincts were changed in 2015 and addressed the model for which the change was made.  She stated that there are instances where people are assigned an incorrect ballot and indicated that the larger the precincts are it does create confusion for voters with the ballots.  She provided some recommendations for precinct boundaries and requested that the Board take into account the statute when voting on precincts.
Chairman Peshlakai thanked Ms. Rowles for her presentation and for providing the information.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the information that Ms. Rowles and Ms. Greene provided and the knowledge that they shared.   He recommended that the information be looked at and considered prior to voting on precincts to see if it could improve voting as well as be better for the citizens.
Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked Ms. Rowles for the presentation indicating that it was very informative.

2.

Presentation regarding the 2023 Wildfire Season :
Catrina Jenkins provided an update on the wildfire season.  A presentation was shown.  She talked about the White Mountain Fire Coordination Group and the unified approach that is taken prior to entering into fire restrictions.  She stated that it's a multi-agency approach and that they meet weekly during the driest part of the season to evaluate the core criteria, including forest conditions, weather patterns, visitors to the area and other criteria prior to the group voting and that they then proceed to evaluate and watch conditions.  She talked about the different people they work with during fire season to be able to get unified messages throughout the region.  She further talked about the fires that were close call this year in the county, indicating that only one of them required a set status.  She indicated that due to the monsoons starting they were able to lift the restrictions in mid-August.  She stated that they need the citizens to continue to be prepared and do the work needed to allow public safety officials to protect our homes and businesses.  She talked about the alert system and the ready/set/go program.  She thanked the Board for their continued support.

Chairman Peshlakai thanked Ms. Jenkins for the work that she and her team do with keeping the public informed.

10:05 a.m. Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation of APN 210-36-004B (Charles Corbett); Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :
Cammy Darris, Assessor; Jana Richards, Appraiser, and Charles Corbett, Applicant, appearing via Zoom, were sworn in by Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board.
Mr. Corbett stated that he is contesting the property valuation.
Ms. Richards provided some history on the parcel, indicating that it's a vacant two and half acres located within the boundaries of Show Low.She indicated that currently the property is receiving a thirty percent reduction for its typography issue, based on a 2017 appeal, it was reduced.She indicated that data historically sells for fifteen percent lower in this area and that reduction was also applied to the property.She talked about the location of the property and the price that other properties in the area sold for and what the average price per acre is compared to the applicant's property.She stated that they did a field check on the property to check the severe slope mentioned by the applicant and that most parcels in that area have slopes.She talked about the properties in the area and the improvements that have been made.She indicated that Mr. Corbett stated in his petition that the property is unsellable unless combined with a neighboring lot and addressed similar properties that are currently listed for sale.She addressed the letter that the petitioner provided from a realtor indicating that the property is unbuildable due to the slope indicating that recent sales data shows that is not the case.She stated that the Assessor's recommendation is no change to the parcel.
Mr. Corbett stated that his petition was submitted on the basis of the market sales approach.He talked about the sales comparison approach and addressed the reasons that were provided to him for the denial of his request.He further talked about the assessments since 2014, indicating that he doesn't know where the reduction stated by the Assessor's Office is at.He addressed the percentage amount that the assessment has gone up each year.He talked about the offer amounts that he has received for the property, indicating that the realtors will not list the property.
Ms. Darris talked about the law changes in 2015.
Supervisor Seymore shared his appreciation for presentations.  He inquired what the petitioner's purpose was when he bought the property and how long it has been on market to sell.
Mr. Corbett stated that the property was a gift and that he didn't have on market but that he has been in contact with realtors in case there is any interest for it.
Supervisor Seymore inquired as to the valuations from the Assessors.
Ms. Richards stated that the full cash value listed is $73,192 and that the petitioner is requesting $25,000 on his full cash and limited.
Supervisor Seymore recommended trying to find a mediation point and suggested a compromise for around $40,000.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve valuation at $40,000, motion seconded by Vice-Chairman Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Ms. Darris stated that it will set the full cash value at $40,000 and the limited value will remain at $29,539.

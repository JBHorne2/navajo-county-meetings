NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, July 11, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney, appearing via Zoom; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

Chairman Peshlakai thanked the City of Winslow staff for their efforts in helping to coordinate this meeting.

10:06 a.m. Call To Order: Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance was led by  Chairman Alberto Peshlakai .

PUBLIC WORKS :

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to enter into the Navajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRICT  Board of Directors Session :

1.

Consideration of the Design Agreement with the Department of the Army to initiate pre-construction, engineering and design (PED) for the Little Colorado River at Winslow Flood Risk Management Project :
Madhav Mundle stated that the signed agreement will establish the role and responsibilities of the federal government partners, US Army Corps of Engineers and nonfederal sponsor, Navajo County Flood Control District, for performing detailed reconstruction engineering design work, including progression of plans and specification for the initial construction contract for the project.  He indicated that the details of the agreement, including cost sharing, and the scope of work are in the packet.
John Osgood stated that executing agreement represent the most and tangible milestone to date in the life of this critical flood mitigation project.  He stated that the negotiating process for getting to this point has been beneficial in the joint project development and established a strong working relationship and shared objective to deliver the design and construction of the project.  He talked about how they will be holding frequent team meetings to address issues and challenges as they arise and to continually look for efficiencies in terms of shorting the design schedule, indicating that the first meeting will take place in thirty to forty-five days.  He talked about the fiscal impact of the agreement and the cost share split for the design of the project.  He acknowledged all those who have contributed to this project over the 15-year journey to get to this point, stating that they have worked hard to make a reality.
Colonel Balten stated that this is a true partnership in how they plan to deliver this project and indicated that their team will be out as soon as possible to have the first meeting to understand the dire need for this project.  She stated that thanks to the congressional members in the state of Arizona last year they were able to get authorized over $65 million for the project, which includes design and construction.  She indicated that they will do cost updates if needed to complete this, but stated that right now it looks like they have what's need to finish the project.  She stated that she's proud of team they have and the collaboration of city and county and that she appreciates the welcome from the community.  She indicated that she is excited about supporting everyone in delivering the project and getting a levee that is certified by FEMA to provide protection to the community.
Supervisor Benally thanked everyone who has worked on this project, including the City of Winslow, Public Works, the Army Corp of Engineers, and former Supervisor Jesse Thompson for the role that they have played in this project and getting it to this position.  She talked about advocating for the funding from the state.  She shared her appreciation to everyone involved.
Supervisor Whitesinger extended her gratitude, indicating that there is a great pride in being one of the valued partners and being able to witness such a great endeavor and everyone's contributions.  She stated that she is grateful to all that have played a key role in helping this come to be.
Supervisor Seymore thanked everyone that was here today and everyone that has worked so hard to make this to happen.  He talked about keeping people involved in the project due to their knowledge.  He stated that this is a milestone that isn't achieved often.  He thanked everyone for the work done on this project, stating that it is a great accomplishment.
Mayor Cano stated that gratitude is the first thing that comes to mind when thinking of how it will benefit the community, indicating that it's because everyone came together that has made it happen.  She stated that if the approach going forward is in the same matter that they will be able to accomplish this.  She thanked everyone that has contributed in any way.  She indicated that she is looking forward to jobs it will create and working with the Army Corp, the county and all the partners.
Chairman Peshlakai recognized all those who have put forth so much effort.  He thanked everyone for being present to witness such a momentous occasion.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to approve the Design Agreement with the Department of the Army to initiate pre-construction, engineering and design (PED) for the Little Colorado River at Winslow Flood Risk Management Project, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

Chairman Peshlakai recognized Colonel Balten on her retirement and presented her with a plaque on behalf of the Board.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, June 28, 2023

Present:
Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V, all appearing via Zoom

Staff Attendance:
Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Catrina Jenkins, Emergency Management Manager; Jayson Vowell, Finance Director, all appearing via Zoom

8:30 a.m. Call To Order: Chairman Alberto Peshlakai  called the meeting to order.

NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT Board of Directors Session :
Supervisor Seymore made a motion to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by Vice Chairman Whiting . Motion passed 4-0 with  Supervisor Fern Benally  being absent.

REGULAR :

1.

Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6 of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire restrictions :
Catrina Jenkins stated that at the White Mountain Fire Coordination Group meeting on June 26, 2023 it was determined that they have reached the level of danger in the forest and surrounding areas to go into Stage One fire restrictions.  She requested approval of proposed declaration.

Vice-Chairman Whiting made a motion to approve a Declaration of Emergency due to extreme fire danger and to enact Section 6 of the Navajo County Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Fire Restrictions, to enter into Stage 1 fire restrictions, motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0 with  Supervisor Fern Benally  being absent.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to return to regular session, motion seconded by Vice-Chairman Whiting .  Motion passed 4-0 with  Supervisor Fern Benally being absent.

ADJOURN :  8:33 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

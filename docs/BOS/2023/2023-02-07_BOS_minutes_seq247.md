NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, February 7, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District VI; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

8:30 a.m. Call To Order: Chairman Alberto Peshlakai called the meeting to order.

SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

EXECUTIVE SESSION pursuant to A.R.S §38-431.03(A)(4) for the Board to receive legal advice from its attorney regarding settlement negotiations in U.S. District Court of Arizona, Case No. CV-20-08055-PCT-JJT Peterson Vs. Navajo County :
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to enter into Executive Session; motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.
The Board of Supervisors convened in Executive Session in a private Zoom room.
The Board returned to open meeting on Zoom.
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

RECONVENE: Authorize Attorney to proceed with Settlement Agreement in U.S. District Court of Arizona, Case No. CV-20-08055-PCT-JJT Peterson Vs. Navajo County as discussed in executive session :
Supervisor Seymore made a motion to proceed with Settlement Agreement in U.S. District Court of Arizona, Case No. CV-20-08055-PCT-JJT Peterson Vs. Navajo County as discussed in Executive Session; motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

The meeting Adjourned at 8:59 a.m.

APPROVED:

Jason Whiting, Vice - Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED: February 28, 2023

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

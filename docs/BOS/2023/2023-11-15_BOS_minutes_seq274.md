NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, November 15, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV

Absent:
Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:00 a.m. Call To Order:  Chairman Peshlakai called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m.

ELECTIONS:

a.

Consideration of the Official Canvass of the Election conducted on November 7, 2023, for Holbrook USD #3, Snowflake USD #5, and Blue Ridge USD #32:
Rayleen Richards discussed the item indicating that the election went very smoothly and requested that the Board approve the Official Canvass of the Election conducted on November 7, 2023.  Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve the canvassing of the election; motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN:  At 9:02 a.m. the meeting was adjourned.

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  December 12, 2023

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

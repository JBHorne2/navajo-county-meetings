Consideration of  approval of Contract with eX 2 Technology, LLC (eX2) for the Design, Construction, Maintenance and Marketing of Fiber Optic Infrastructure and to authorize change order authority to the County Manager for 2.5% of the System Price amount and authorize the County Manager to execute the necessary documents :
Grant Evans addressed the Board indicating that this project will be very monumental and transformational for the communities.  A presentation was shown.  He reviewed the process leading up to now, including the ARPA funding and the funding from the ACA grant, indicating that the ACA funds haven't been released yet but that they are working on it.  He talked about the network route map, stating that there are a couple of changes, one being that our partner has designed it to be a hundred percent underground and that there is a change with the deviation north of Show Low.  He reviewed the route that the network will follow, indicating that following the major corridors is by design to provide the ability for last mile providers.  He indicated that the connecting point at APS is critical because it provides the connection point needed to get back to the data center in Phoenix.  He talked about the strategic plan that was developed by Magellan and the redundancy option which would enable critical public service and public safety connectivity.  He stated that the redundancy component part of the project is not included in the current contract but they wanted to explore and consider all options.  He advised that they have been in discussions with the City of Winslow on their needs for improved broadband and connectivity.  He talked about how the network lay out is good for possible future connection points and that there are opportunities to connect to the Hopi and Navajo tribes that have existing project infrastructure, to Apache County's infrastructure, the White Mountain Apache Tribe's existing infrastructure and broadband project and possibly Gila County.  He stated the future connection points aren't by mistake and are a critical and important piece of how this connects with the greater region.  He reviewed the tentative schedule of work for design and construction.
Vice-Chairman Whiting thanked Mr. Evans and the team for the work done on this.  He talked about the importance and need for broadband in our region and how it will allow for future growth and development.  He stated that he appreciates the different connection points and how they are collaborating with those around us.  He inquired, after the eighteen-month period, what it looks like for citizens and what they can expect.
Mr. Evans stated that this will provide the backbone for the last mile connections and indicated that they have had a lot of interest already from internet service providers.  He advised that the middle mile will be critical in being successful for final mile funding.
Ashley Dodds inquired as to access to rural areas.
Mr. Evans indicated that the project will be built on the routes addressed and that they will then go out for bid on the last mile service providers that builds the infrastructure to the homes.
Mike, White Mountain Lakes, inquired about using different routes and going above ground to be able to get access to their community.
Mr. Evans indicated that the area is a marketable location and that last mile providers will want to provide service to the community.  He indicated that those are things that will be weighed as they get into the detail design and the cost of routes to certain locations.
Mr. Kephart stated that the purpose of the project is to be able to create infrastructure for all parts of the county to be able to be provided service.
Buzz Essel, White Mountain Lakes, inquired that once everything is done if there will be meetings held so the public can be provided with an understanding, get direction of what to do and be able to voice concerns.
Mr. Kephart indicated that this will be an open, transparent and inclusive process.
Vice-Chairman Whiting indicated that they appreciate all the work that has been done on this project over the past three years.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to approve the contract with eX 2 Technology, LLC (eX2) for the Design, Construction, Maintenance and Marketing of Fiber Optic Infrastructure and to authorize change order authority to the County Manager for 2.5% of the System Price amount and authorize the County Manager to execute the necessary documents, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Chairman Peshlakai being absent.

1.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 04-2023 , approving/denying text amendment to Article 25 of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance regarding the prohibition against placement/movement of mobile homes 20 years old or older :
Cody Cooper addressed the item, indicating that last year the county updated the IRC and IBC to the 2018 version to update the modern outdated safety standards.  A presentation was shown.  He advised that Planning and Zoning were operating on the bare minimum threshold prior to June 15, 1975, for manufactured homes and that a twenty-year rolling basis is being proposed.  He talked about the proposed amendments which would ensure the safety of manufactured homes that are being moved into or around the county.  He stated that the HUD rehabilitation certification does verify dwelling safety and creates safety awareness for residents.  He advised that HUD allows jurisdictions to set their own threshold on the manufacture home age.  He talked about HUD being able to reimburse homeowners up to $1500.00 of their costs for those below the federal poverty guidelines for rehabilitation.  He advised that fees for rehabilitation through the State Office of Manufactured Housing comes in two tiers based on income criteria.  He stated that the Planning and Zoning Commission unanimously recommended approval at their January meeting and that staff would also recommend approval.
Supervisor Seymore addressed having a policy for any type of transported structure having requirements.  He stated that he appreciates the work staff has done on this item.

Vice-Chairman Whiting opened the public hearing.  There being no comments, the public hearing was closed.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 04-2023 , approving text amendment to Article 25 of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance regarding the prohibition against placement/movement of mobile homes 20 years old or older, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Chairman Peshlakai being absent.

2.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration of the adoption of Resolution Number 05-2023 approving/denying a text amendment to Article IX, Navajo County Subdivision Regulations and Addition of Section 9.4 - Exceptions; Plat Alterations :
Berrin Nejad talked about the current regulations for plated properties in a sub-division which was adopted in January 1969 and has only had a few small amendments in 2007 and 2021.  She indicated that based on the CC&R's property owners were able to split by just recording it with the County Recorder's Office but that in 2018 Navajo County adopted the minor land division process which now required the splits to go to Planning staff for review and were rejected in the already final platted subdivisions.  She advised that this caused property owners to complain.  She stated that staff and an Ad Hoc Task Force explored and worked on the problem for several months and that there findings were several counties already do the lot-split process in existing and already plotted sub-divisions, Navajo County has evaluated it for a long time and feels with the conditions such split with be acceptable and that after a work session the Planning and Zoning Commission determined staff should find a solution.  She talked about the proposed amendments.  She advised that the Planning and Zoning Commission unanimously recommended approval at their January meeting.  She stated that staff recommends approval of the amendments.

Vice-Chairman Whiting opened the public hearing.  There being no comments the public hearing was closed.

Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the work done on this item and addressed the proposed changes.
Mischa Larisch addressed the proposed changes and the reason for the changes.
Supervisor Seymore indicated that this will be a great asset, that it could cause challenges for some people, but that it is a great direction for the development of the county.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 05-2023 approving a text amendment to Article IX, Navajo County Subdivision Regulations and Addition of Section 9.4 - Exceptions; Plat Alterations, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0 with Chairman Peshlakai being absent.

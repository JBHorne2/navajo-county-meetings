CALL TO THE PUBLIC :
Steven Slaton provided a handout.  He talked about an invasion after Title 42 was dropped, indicating that it will affect Navajo County.  He stated that the handout has good information.  He talked about the different locations that are currently being affected.  He proposed that Sheriff Clouse come up with task force and a plan to deal with the issue.  He addressed concerns for issues that can happen.
Supervisor Seymore requested that Sheriff Clouse meet and discuss the issues more in depth with Mr. Slaton.
Kathy Henderson Essel, White Mountain Lakes, talked about the proposed cell tower in the community.  She further talked about studies that show how the towers reduce home rates, how it will affect those in the proposed area and the loss that it will cause in the community.  She addressed how HUD has them do an assessment to see if a home is in the cell tower range and indicated that FHA is not allowed to do a mortgage if they are within the fall zone.  She talked about the tower heights and the properties that would be located within the fall zone.  She addressed the EMF liability insurance and inquired who will be holding the coverage.
Ashley Dodds, White Mountain Lakes, addressed concerns of the community members with the proposed cell tower.  She indicated that they are fearful of the potential health hazards and the devaluation of homes there.

PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT :

1.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 13 - 2023 approving/denying fee schedule for services provided to the public by the Planning and Development Services Division of the Public Works Department in connection with Planning, Zoning and Building Related Reviews and Permits :
Mischa Larisch stated that last year the Board of Supervisors adopted the 1028 International Building Codes but that a new fee schedule was not adopted. He stated that the last time a new fee schedule was adopted was in 2010.  He indicated that a critical part of the fee schedule is the building valuation data.  A presentation was shown.  Mr. Larisch indicated that Planning and Development has reviewed other jurisdictions fee schedules and that they found most to adopt IBC and IRC standards for construction and building valuation data and the permit fee structures.  He reviewed the findings for different municipalities in the area.  He talked about how they currently do valuations and the differences between the 1997 schedule and the 2018 schedule.  He talked about the delegation agreements that they have with the different agencies.  He stated that staff is recommending approval of the fee schedule.

Chairman Peshlakai  opened the meeting for public comments.  There being no comments, the public meeting was closed.
Supervisor Seymore inquired when this would go into effect.
Mr. Larisch stated that it would go into effect the new fiscal year.
Supervisor Seymore requested that the fee increase be advertised prior to going into effect.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 13 - 2023  approving fee schedule for services provided to the public by the Planning and Development Services Division of the Public Works Department in connection with Planning, Zoning and Building Related Reviews and Permits to be in effect July 1, 2023, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

Supervisor Seymore stated that with the increases we need to make sure that we are providing services at a level expected.

2.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and possible adoption of  Resolution Number 14 - 2023  approving/denying an update to the Comprehensive Plan of Navajo County :
Cody Cooper stated that staff has been working to update the comprehensive plan, that was previously adopted in 2011, for approximately six months and that they have worked with some consultants a little over a year to find where they had deficiencies or issues with the plan in general.  A presentation was shown.  He stated that the goal is to satisfy the requirements of ARS 11-824(c) and that with those goals they need to adopt some new portions of the comprehensive plan, with the most major being a proposed hearing process for major amendments.  He addressed the proposed changes for the hearing process, the updates to the population figures, the changes made to make the plan more readable for the general public, and changes that were updated for the goals and policies.  He stated that they are recommending approval of the updates to the Navajo County Comprehensive Plan.

Chairman Peshlakai  opened the meeting for public comments.  There being no comments, the public hearing was closed.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 14 - 2023  approving an update to the Comprehensive Plan of Navajo County, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.
Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and possible adoption of  Resolution Number 15 - 2023  approving/denying a Special Use Permit Amendment from an RV Park and RV storage facility to a two-phase RV, trailer, and mini-storage facility on a vacant 4.30-acre parcel in the Overgaard area, APN# 206-02-012C, Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 30 of the Gila and Salt River Base and Meridian :
Cody Cooper stated that this is a special use permit amendment. A presentation was shown.  Mr. Cooper stated that the property was originally approved for an RV park and storage facility in 2022, that the approval was with a different applicant, but that the wording of the previous resolution only provided a six-month approval.  He indicated that the current applicant is looking to purchase the property and is requesting a special use permit amendment as to the use of the property.  He stated that the original approval was for 25 RV park spaces and 20 RV storage spaces and that the currently applicate is proposing a two passed development, phase one would have 200 storage units and an acre of land would include 44 RV and trailer storage spaces, then phase two would remove the RV and trailer storage and add an additional 100 storage units and add a commercial center on the west of the property.  He stated that they have received no comments from the public either in favor or against and that staff would recommend approval subject to the conditions submitted to the Board.       Logan Rogers, applicant, addressed the board regarding the proposed project.
Supervisor Seymore inquired if the applicant would be opposed to having a condition on the special use permit that every five years the property would need to be reinspected.
Mr. Rogers indicated that he does not oppose that condition.

Chairman Peshlakai  opened the meeting for public comments.
Lisa Santoro, Bison Ranch 2341 Quarter Horse Trail, requested the physical address of the proposed facility.
Mr. Rogers provided the address for the property.
Chairman Peshlakai closed the public hearing.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 15 - 2023  approving a Special Use Permit Amendment from an RV Park and RV storage facility to a two-phase RV, trailer, and mini-storage facility on a vacant 4.30-acre parcel in the Overgaard area, APN# 206-02-012C, Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 30 of the Gila and Salt River Base and Meridian, with a special conditions that it will be reinspected every five  years for compliance with landscaping and site appearance, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

Joseph Holland, applicant, addressed the Board regarding the proposed project.
Supervisor Seymore inquired if the applicant would be opposed to having a condition in place for reinspection every five years to check for compliance.
Mr. Holland stated that he doesn't have an issue with the condition.

Chairman Peshlakai  opened the meeting for public comments.
Ronald Longenbaugh addressed pros and cons with development issues in the area of the proposed project.
Chairman Peshlakai closed the public hearing.

Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 16 - 2023 approving a Special Use Permit to develop a self-storage facility with four banks of new storage units on a vacant 1.2-acre parcel within the White Mountain Lake area of Show Low. APN # 304-19-584A, Township 11 North, Range 22 East, Section 15 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, with a special conditions that it will be reinspected every five years for compliance with landscaping and site appearance, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

5.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and possible adoption of  Resolution Number 17 - 2023, approving/denying a Special Use Permit to expand use of a permitted primary dwelling to include indoor storage of contractor's equipment on a 0.44-acre parcel located in Overgaard, APN # 206-27-020, Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 33 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian :
Sondra Starke stated that this item is for the Kenison Contractor Yard, that the property is one parcel totaling approximately .44 acres and that they are requesting a special use permit to expand the use of a permitted primary dwelling to include indoor storage for contractors' equipment.  A presentation was shown.  She addressed the location of the property, the proposed project, the site plan and the fencing/landscaping on the property.  She stated that they have received two phone calls and six emails in opposition to the project.  She stated that the Planning and Zoning Commission recommended approval 7-1, with an additional stipulation that the applicant put up a solid fence at least six feet in height, which is also a condition recommended by staff.  She stated that staff recommends approval.
Brian Kennison, applicant, addressed the Board regarding the proposed project.  He stated that it is important that they be close to Bison Ranch, that they are always on call and that they would have their equipment readily available.
Supervisor Seymore inquired if the applicant would be opposed to having a condition in place for reinspection every five years to check for compliance.
Mr. Kennison indicated that he does not have any issues with that condition.

Chairman Peshlakai  opened the meeting for public comments.
Ben Mayer, Bison Ranch, stated that he hopes that Board will approve the item.  He indicated that it will help improve the look of the property without having the equipment all around.
Diane Dollin, 2271 SR 260, stated that by allowing the building it will help to contain and store the equipment.  She inquired as to the primary dwelling.  She requested that there be an inspection in 2 years to make sure that everything is in place and then to continue with a 5-year inspection after that.
Chairman Peshlakai closed the public hearing.
Supervisor Seymore inquired if applicant would be okay with 2-year inspection.
Mr. Kennison stated that he does not have any issues with the condition.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 17 - 2023, approving a Special Use Permit to expand use of a permitted primary dwelling to include indoor storage of contractor's equipment on a 0.44-acre parcel located in Overgaard, APN # 206-27-020, Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 33 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, with a special conditions that it will be reinspected every two years for compliance with landscaping and site appearance, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Motion passed 4-0 with Vice-Chairman Jason Whiting being absent.

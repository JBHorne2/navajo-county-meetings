NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, June 20, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

9:02a.m. Call To Order: Chairman Alberto Peshlakai called the meeting to order.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

EXECUTIVE SESSION : Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (1) for discussion regarding interviews of County Manager position and County Manager employment contract :
Supervisor Daryl Seymore made a motion to enter into executive Session. Motion seconded by Supervisor Jason Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

10:11 a.m. RECONVENE : Discussion a nd possible action of making a conditional offer of employment to one of the remaining two applicants for the position of County Manager, and direct staff to enter into contract negotiations with the aforementioned applicant:
Supervisor Dawnafe Whitesinger made a motion to return to regular session. Motion seconded by Supervisor Daryl Seymore . Vote 4-0 with Supervisor Jason Whiting being absent for the vote.

Supervisor Daryl Seymore  made a motion  to direct staff to move forward with the candidate as discussed in executive session. Motion seconded by  Supervisor Dawnafe Whitesinger .  Vote 4-0 with Supervisor Jason Whiting being absent for the vote.

ADJOURN :  At 10:12 a.m. meeting adjourned .

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  July 18, 2023

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

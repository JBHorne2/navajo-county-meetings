NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Thursday, August 17, 2023 *Special Meeting

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V, all appearing via Zoom.

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, County Manager; Jayson Vowell, Finance Director; Jalyn Gerlich, Superintendent of Schools; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, appearing via Zoom.

1:01 p.m. Call To Order:  Chairman Alberto Peshlakai  called the meeting to order.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS :

a.

Consideration and approval of the submitted FY 2024 Tax Rates and Levies for school districts :

Jalyn Gerlich, Superintendent of Schools, recommended that the Board of Supervisors approve the FY 2024 Tax Rates and Levies for School Districts in Navajo County, as presented.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the submitted FY 2024 Tax Rates and Levies for school districts, motion seconded by Supervisor Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN:  1:02 p.m. meeting adjourned .

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:   September 12, 2023

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk

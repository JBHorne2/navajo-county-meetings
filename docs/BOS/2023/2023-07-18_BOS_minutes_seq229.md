Consideration and approval to implement a fee for service for CPR and First Aid certification and be recognized as a Training Center :
Amy Stradling stated that they have a Child Fatality Review Committee and a Fatality Review Committee and talked about the number of times that they heard that CPR was not initiated due to family members not knowing how or not being confident in their skills of it.  She stated that CPR is a life saving measure, that they want to take a proactive stance and look at Public Health offering it as a community resource.  She stated that they don't have grant funding to cover it at this time but that they do have certified instructors that could facilitate the course, but that they would be asking to charge a small fee to offer the service.  She talked about classes being cancelled at other entities and that they want to make sure it's a sustainable community service that can be offered.  She stated that there is a babysitting course as well, for children under the age of eighteen that they can learn CPR and basic first aid skills.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 24-2023,  approving/denying a Zone Change from A-General to R-3 to allow for a 17-space manufactured home park in Lakeside on a 3.48-acre portion of APN# 212-26-119A, 212-26-119C, 212-26-119D, and 212-26-119E :
Sondra Starke indicated that this a zone change request, that the property is located in Lakeside, that there are four parcels totaling approximately 4.99 acres, with 3.48 acres being used for development project.  A presentation was shown. She indicated that the land is current A General and that the zone change is requesting to change it to R-3.  She talked about the general zoning for the land in the neighborhood.  She stated that there is a similar manufactured home park located south of the site, which is zoned R-3.  She stated that the proposal is in compliance with the comprehensive plan that the proposed zoning has been deterred compatible with current and likely future uses.  She stated that there are minimal adverse effect on public health, safety, and general welfare of the area.  She indicated that they have received one public comment, with concerns about drainage, flooding, light pollution and urbanization.  She stated that staff recommends approval and that at their June 15, 2023 hearing the Planning & Zoning Commission voted 7-0 for approval with an added condition that the owner shall begin development within a time period, if the development has not begun during that time period the applicant shall be required to re-enter the public hearing process which it would be determined if an extension granted or if the property would be reverted back to its previous zoning classification.

Chairman Peshlakai opened the meeting for public comments.

Marilyn Baca stated that she lives in the community and talked about the zone change making it difficult for those in the A general zone.  She addressed concerns with the amount of traffic, indicating that there is one way in and one way out.  She further addressed concerns with emergency vehicles being able to get into the area.  She stated that she is not in favor of this due to the issues stated, the high concentration of people in a rural area, there not being enough roads, and things being to capacitated.
Supervisor Whitesinger inquired where Ms. Baca lives in the area.
Ms. Baca stated that she lives on the corner of Flag Hollow and Hi Joe Roads.
Laura Galusha, appearing via Zoom, 834 Flag Hollow Road, talked about concerns with access to the community, indicating that they could possibly do some improvements on Gold Oak Road for better access.  She addressed concerns with the traffic issues, especially if there was an emergency evacuation.  She further addressed the traffic issues with machinery coming in and out during the development.  She stated that she has concerns with the access and egress to the community.
Allanna Jackson, 2556 Parkinson Road, appearing via Zoom, stated that she sent a letter for public comment.  She addressed her concerns with draining, traffic and safety issues.
Mary Miranda, 1038 Flag Hollow Road, Lakeside, stated that she has concerns with the traffic and that she shares the same concerns as Ms. Baca.

Chairman Peshlakai closed the public hearing was closed.

Cody Cooper stated that the zone change and Special Use Permit are separate process and that the zone change stands on its own.

this request is in the Lakeside area, that the applicant is proposing that the project would be in two phases.  He indicated that the first phase being for a seventeen spaced manufacture home park stated that the Planning and Zoning Commission, at their June 15, 2023, meeting, approved the item 6-1, with a condition that the easements be verified prior to any activity related to the special use

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, June 26, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

8:00 a.m. Call To Order:   Chairman Alberto Peshlakai called the meeting to order.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

EXECUTIVE SESSION: Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (1) for discussion regarding County Manager employment contract :
Supervisor Daryl Seymore made a motion  to enter into Executive Session; motion seconded by  Supervisor Jason Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

8:46 a.m. RECONVENE:  Discussion and possible direction to staff to continue with contract negotiations on employment contract for County Manager position. DIRECTED COUNTY ATTORNEY TO CONTINUE CONTRACT NEGOTIATIONS AS DISCUSSED IN EXECUTIVE SESSION :
Supervisor Dawnafe Whitesinger made a motion  to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Jason Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Daryl Seymore made a motion  to continue negotiations on employment contract for County Manager position; motion seconded by  Supervisor Jason Whiting . Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN: At 8:47 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED:  July 18, 2023

ATTEST:

Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

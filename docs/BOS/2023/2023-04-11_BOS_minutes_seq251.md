NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS'
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Tuesday, April 11, 2023

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Chairman; Jason Whiting, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V (at 1:08 p.m.)

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

Attendees:
Madhav Mundle, Miles Begay, Grant Evans, Jason Vowell, Rochelle Lacapa

12:32 p.m. Meeting Called to Order: Chairman Peshlakai called the meeting to order.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Capital Improvement Plan Overview:  Bryan Layton gave an overview of the Capital Improvement Plan and the purpose of that plan.

b.

Broadband Update: Grant Evans discussed the middle mile broadband network. He gave an update on the ACA Grant. He indicated that we have been informed by the ACA that we are approved, awaiting final grant agreement from ACA to review and bring in front of the Board for approval. He further spoke of the kickoff meeting held in Holbrook March 29. He indicated that they have started on the design and that is progressing well. He reported that eX2 does not yet have County approval to start ordering materials. They have advised us if we do not place this order soon our pricing and schedule could, and likely will be impacted. We are awaiting direction from the ACA on whether we can complete this task with our local match monies while the grant agreement is being finalized and executed. He stated that they would like to do a white tent ground-breaking in Holbrook, possibly in September after State budget cycle and Federal delegation legislative sessions are wrapped up. He spoke about the Microwave/Redundancy still being worked through, the plan to meet with eX2 the week of April 10th to hopefully get final pricing numbers on the microwave option and to discuss other viable options that we see for redundancy.

Bryan Layton spoke about Communications. He spoke about the Public Safety communications and First Net. They have been very happy with connection in the Northern Area and believe that this is getting a great reach.

c.

Transportation Capital Improvement Plan:  Madhav Mundle discussed Partnership Projects in each of the Board of Supervisors Districts. Miles Begay discussed partnerships projects in each district from motor grader purchases, culvert replacements, equipment operators and roadway repairs. Mr. Begay discussed each project separately and gave the status updates of each.

At 1:08 Supervisor Whitesinger entered the Board Chambers.

Mr. Mundle reviewed a new CIP Project Worksheet which includes the Title/Details, Project Number, Project description and benefits, project funding and expenditures, project location along with any pictures. Mr. Mundle spoke about a few projects in and around the county. He further spoke about the challenges and opportunities that we are faced with. He spoke about the Gravel Road Maintenance and Pavement Preservation. He said that his goal is to have something done by August 2023 for the pavement preservation. We want to fix these pavements when they are in the maintenance, repair, and restoration timeline and not in the reconstruct timeline. He spoke of another challenge with our Bridge rehabilitation and fleet modernization programs. He spoke about the Obed Bridge and discussed a specific instance when I40 shuts down, people use that bridge. He then spoke about the fleet modernization program and the equipment that is at the end of its useful life. He indicated that this is also a budgeting tool. He talked about discretionary and competitive programs that we are looking to explore or have explored. Some of those programs were Az Smart Grant, Highway Safety Improvement Program, Off System Bridge Program and the Bridge Formula Program.

Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Mundle for keeping track of our roads and the work being done. Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Mundle and indicated that he would like to see these listed as priorities and then how are we setting money aside for those bigger 5-year project plans so that we do not have to come up with the money all at one time.

d.

Facilities Capital Improvement Plan:  Bryan Layton spoke about our current facilities. He spoke about the guiding principles for facility renovations and new construction. He spoke about access to services, safety and security, sustainability, community engagement, and staff development.  Mr. Layton spoke about the new Fiix CMMS Software that Facilities Management is currently using, which is a computerized maintenance management system implemented in 2020. This software tracks, plans and documents actionable facility data. It allows for dispatch of personnel and work orders. Mr. Layton then discussed the recent achievements of the Facilities Capital Improvement Plan. He reviewed the renovations of the Sheriff's Office Training room and the Pine Lake Substation. Mr. Layton reviewed the parking lots and streets of the Holbrook Complex and the work done there. Mr. Layton went on to discuss the LED Lighting Conversion, the High Efficiency HVAC Units, and Sewer Pipe Repair.

e.

Update on Approved ARPA Projects:  Mr. Layton discussed County Complex Renovations in Holbrook. He indicated that it would be a phased approach so that our services will remain open. He indicated that we can begin construction this summer. Mr. Layton discussed the renovations for the Taylor Code Enforcement building. He indicated that the medical examiner, animal care services, environmental health and the Sheriff substation will be able to share this space. Mr. Layton indicated that there is also a fuel site out there and easier to access. Mr. Layton further discussed the re-purpose of the Silver Creek Sheriff Substation.

Supervisor Seymore asked about the budget for the renovations. Jayson Vowell indicate that the dollars are there and feels comfortable with what has been presented. He indicated that this would all be at the discretion of the Board. Mr. Vowell indicated that we are feeling comfortable with this along with the ARPA funding as discussed. Discussion was held about funding.

Mr. Layton discussed Public Health Satellite Locations. He discussed the improvement needed to the physical security and functionality of the satellite offices. He spoke about pausing on the headquarters location and continuing to look for data that would prove where the location should be.  Discussion took place about data points regarding the location of the Health Department headquarters. Mr. Layton discussed the options that exist for growth. He showed a map of the locations in the Show Low area that Navajo County owns. He also showed a map of the Holbrook Complex and the area of land that exists in Holbrook.

f.

Follow-up Discussion and Direction to Staff:  Supervisor Seymore indicated that he liked the options.

ADJOURN: At 2:38 p.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Alberto L. Peshlakai, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

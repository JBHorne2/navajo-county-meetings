BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Recognition of those who contributed to County Health Week for their hard work and dedication to make all of the events a success through the different communities in Navajo County :   Vice-Chairman Alberto Peshlakai  talked about the Proclamation for Navajo County Health Week and the 5K run/walks that were held in each of the Districts.  He stated that it wouldn't have been a success without the Navajo County Team.  He talked about the Planning Team who worked on the event from the start and indicated that he wanted to recognize the Team for all of the hard work that they put into planning the events and making them a success.  He recognized each of the employees that were part of the Navajo County Health Week and presented those present with a certificate of appreciation.  He talked about the outreach to the community to let them know of the services and programs that are offered by Navajo County and stated that he hopes that this can be an annual event.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Vice-Chairman Peshlakai for his leadership in getting this started for the organization.  She presented him with a certificate of appreciation.
Supervisor Benally thanked those who participated in the event that was held in Kayenta.  She talked about the individuals that assisted with that event.  She thanked Vice-Chairman Peshlakai for all of the planning put into this and thanked those who participated in the event.
Chairwoman Whitesinger talked about the participation from North County to South County.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Presentation to Bonita Chase on her Retirement from Navajo County after 21+ Years of Service :  Glenn Kephart talked about Bonita Chase working in probation for the past 21 years and indicated that a lot of her work was behind the scenes but that it didn't go unrecognized. He shared a few comments from Eric Scott indicating that Ms. Chase was one of the first people Mr. Scott meet and that he stated she has always been kind, caring, has a great sense of humor and was always willing to step in and help.  Mr. Kephart thanked Ms. Chase for all that she has done.
Ashton Lesueur talked about Ms. Chase's time in Probation, her strong work ethic, indicating that she was always at work, kept probation running during pandemic, is great baker, loves sewing and crafts.  She stated that Ms. Chase will be greatly missed and that they appreciate all she has done.
Bonita Chase addressed the Board, stating that she has had a good time working in the Probation Department.  She talked about the different positions that she held while working in Probation.  She stated that she will miss everyone.
Mr. Kephart presented Ms. Chase with a vase for recognition of her 21 years of service.

b.

Consideration of a grant agreement between Navajo County and Chilchinbeto Chapter :  Ken Maruyama addressed the item, indicating that this is a project that was approved as part of the ARPA funding projects in January.

Supervisor Benally indicated that she is thankful that this funding can be provided to Chilchinbeto for the project.  She stated that the Community is very thankful for the funding and have been very proactive is getting the project going.

Paul Matson, Chilchinbeto Chapter President, addressed the Board regarding the project, indicating that the funding will be used towards the wastewater upgrade.   Eugene Badonie addressed the Board regarding the project.

Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked them for appearing and providing information on the proposed project.

Supervisor Benally made a motion  to approve the grant agreement between Navajo County and Chilchinbeto Chapter, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration of a grant agreement between Navajo County and the City of Show Low :  Ken Maruyama addressed the item.  Justin Johnson addressed the Board regarding the project that the City of Show Low will use the funding for, indicating that it will be an all-inclusive playground and splash pad for those with disabilities and that it will be wheelchair accessible.

Supervisor Seymore talked about the project, indicating that it's a need that needs to be filled and that it will be remarkable for those with disabilities.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve the grant agreement between Navajo County and the City of Show Low, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring the week of May 15-21, 2022 as National Public Works Week in Navajo County :  John Osgood addressed the Board regarding the item, stating that the American Public Works Association has sponsored National Public Works Week for over 60 years and that communities use this as a reminder of the importance of public works in their daily life.  He talked about the theme Ready and Resilient, and stated that their team is always ready to serve the communities and that they are resilient as ever in their abilities to recover from disasters and challenges.
Catrina Jenkins addressed the Board regarding how the Public Works Department and the Emergency Management Department works so closely in the communities and addressed the different projects that that they work together on.
John Osgood further addressed the partnership between the two Departments. He acknowledged the members of the Public Works Team that are present as well as those that are in the field.    He read the proposed proclamation.

Supervisor Seymore thanked the Public Works Department and those who work all hours during the emergencies.
Supervisor Whiting thanked the Public Works Team for all that they do and indicated that he appreciates their willingness to do everything they do.
Supervisor Benally thanked the Public Works Team and Emergency Management Team for all that they do during the emergencies throughout the communities.
Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that she appreciates all that the Public Work's Team does and that she's grateful for the work they do and contributions that they make to our communities.

Chairwoman Whitesinger made a motion  to approve a Proclamation declaring the week of May 15-21, 2022 as National Public Works Week in Navajo County, motion seconded by Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Presentation of TRACKS Annual Report :  Lynn Krigbaum addressed the item.  She stated that TRACKS has been around since the 1980's and that there are approximately 500 members.  She indicated that it's free to join, that there are no membership fees and that you can join online.  She stated that they work from April to November, that it's approximately 3 hours a week, that they do maintenance work and build new trail and that it is very rewarding.  She indicated that it offers several activities, including trail working, hiking, bicycling, cross country skiing, photography, horseback riding and more that people can be a part of.  Ms. Krigbaum talked about the accomplishments over the past year and indicated that the trail crew has donated over 2,037 hours which is a savings of $54,877.00 to the Forest Service due to it all being volunteer hours and that the hours from the other parts of the group were 11,124 hours valued at $299,681.00.   She thanked the Board for the support they have given to TRACKS and talked about the upcoming work that will be done on the trail system.

Supervisor Seymore thanked Ms. Krigbaum for all that they are doing to expand TRACKS throughout the State.  He indicated that he has been on the trails and that the markers in our area make it easy to follow.
Ms. Krigbaum talked about the emergency responder diamonds that they put in and indicated that it makes it easier and quicker to respond when help is needed.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Krigbaum for the leadership that has been demonstrated and indicated that they are doing a great job.   He stated that the number of volunteer hours is commendable.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Ms. Krigbaum for coming to present today and indicated that they appreciate all of the hours of dedication to the community.  She indicated that the trails ways are important not only to our local recreation but also to tourism.

b.

Consideration of a Liquor License for Amy S. Nations at Al & Diane's Red Onion Lounge located at 1931 Highway 260, Overgaard, AZ :  Leah Thomas addressed the item, indicating that all Departments have submitted their approvals.  She indicated that staff recommends approval.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Consideration of the development of an 18-hole disc golf course on County property surrounding the Holbrook County Complex to complement and enhance the recreational opportunities provided to the public :  Torel Nichols addressed the item and provided a brief background about disc golf.  A presentation was shown.   He stated that disc golf has grown tremendously.  He indicated that the disc golf course would be open to the public, enhance what is already at the County Park and that it would have a unique landscape and views.  He talked about the proposed course that has been designed and some of the views from the course.  He talked about the phone app for disc golf.  He addressed the costs for the baskets and the launch pads and the different types of tee pads/launch pads.
Bryan Layton talked about the item, indicated that no staffing would be needed due to everything being done through the app.  He talked about the cost sharing through the general funds and wellness funds.  He stated that this would be a good opportunity for future wellness challenges for employees.  He indicated that it would be low maintenance and stated that there will be trash barrels placed around the course.  He talked about liability concerns and stated that our provider doesn't feel there are any major concerns.  He indicated that in speaking with regional partners they have indicated that they have very minimal liability concerns with their courses. He stated that staff recommends approval.
Jared Robinson addressed the Board regarding the item.
Supervisor Whiting thanked them for the presentation.  He indicated that it is something that the employees will enjoy and that he can see this as a win for employees and for the public.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai thanked them for the presentation.  He stated that there are a lot of benefits for this, that it is a great activity and will be a great addition to the complex.
Supervisor Seymore stated he thinks that this is a great marketing tool to bring tourism into our community.  He thanked them for the work done of this project.
Chairwoman Whitesinger talked about the project and indicated that she sees many people using the course near her home.  She talked about what she has learned about disk goal and how it will provide options for recreational activities for the youth in our area.

Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion  to approve the development of an 18-hole disc golf course on County property surrounding the Holbrook County Complex to complement and enhance the recreational opportunities provided to the public, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COOPERATIVE EXTENSION :

a.

Presentation of Cooperative Extension Programs and Ongoing Events : Mike Hauser addressed the item.  A presentation was shown.  He provided a brief summary of what Cooperative Extension does.  He talked about the hours of volunteer service that have been contributed and the different programs that are done through 4-H other than the agriculture projects.  He advised that they have two new Extension Agents who will share a summary of some of their programs.  Margene Bowden talked about the programs that she oversees, the outreach that they have done and indicated that they were able to expand their outreach in 2021 even with COVID.  She indicated that they have been working with the food systems, especially on developing gardens, and that they are hoping to expand the physical wellness activities.  Anita Thompson talked about the programs that she oversees and the outreach that she has provided in her areas.  She indicated that they are hoping to bring more agribility, which provides assistance to those with disabilities who work in agriculture, to the area.

Supervisor Seymore thanked them for what they offer to the youth in the County.  He talked about attending the fair and the difference it makes for the youth.
Supervisor Whiting thanked them for the presentation and indicated that they appreciate what they do for the youth.
Mr. Hauser thanked the Board for the time and support.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked them for the presentation and talked about the importance of the agriculture side and how it helps the youth.  She indicated that they appreciate them taking the time to invest in our community and help the youth with the different activities that they support them with.

PLANNING & ZONING :

1.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution 02-2022 approving/denying a special use permit that allows construction of an RV and boat storage facility on 4.64 acres, located north of State Route 260 and west of Burton Road, in the Linden area. APN# 409-22-007S, T10N, R21E, S06 of the GSRM :  Berrin Nejad addressed the Board regarding the item.  A presentation was shown. She stated that this is a request for a Special Use Permit for an RV/Boat storage, that the property is zoned as commercial-residential and the units will only be allowed with Special Use Permits.  She talked about the layout for the storage units, indicating that it will have 146 lots, that they entrance will be an electronic gate off of Burden Road, that it will be 24 hours, that a lighting system at the gate to provide security but that it will only be on the property and not be a nuisance to the neighbors.  She talked about the wall that will be placed on the property and indicated that the property owner meet with the residence regarding the wall height, that there was a discussion in the Planning & Zoning meeting about the height, that it was decided that the fence will be a six foot high solid wall and property owner will gift some trees to the residence.  Pictures were shown of the property.  She stated that staff and the Planning & Zoning Commission recommend approval.  Ryan Mell, the applicant, thanked Board for their consideration.  He talked the entrances, the wall and the trees that they are gifting.
Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the meeting for public comments.  There being no comments, the public meeting was closed.
Supervisor Seymore commended the Applicant on the design.  He addressed the project layout and inquired if the entrances will be paved? He talked about the signage being up to code. He thanked the applicant and staff for the work done on this item.
Mr. Mell indicated that the entrance will be paved.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt Resolution 02-2022 approving a special use permit that allows construction of an RV and boat storage facility on 4.64 acres, located north of State Route 260 and west of Burton Road, in the Linden area. APN# 409-22-007S, T10N, R21E, S06 of the GSRM, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, December 2, 2022

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Vice-Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District VI; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman, all appearing via Zoom

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Rayleen Richards, Election Director, all appearing via Zoom; Michael Sample, Recorder; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board

2:30 p.m . Call To Order:    Chairwoman Whitesinger called the meeting to order.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of an amendment to the Canvass of the November 8, 2022, General Election, originally canvassed by the Board of Supervisors on November 28, 2022, to include one ballot that has been found to be misplaced :
Brad Carlyon stated that the Navajo County Attorney's Office believes that the election laws require a count of every vote that is cast and that they have advised the Board to have this Special Meeting to revise the canvass include the ballot.
Rayleen Richards, Elections Director, stated that she can answer any questions that the Board may have.
Supervisor Whiting inquired as to what happened.
Michael Sample, Recorder, stated that the ballot was found in a small portable drop box that is located at the Recorder's Office during the time of early voting, that the ballot was dropped on the last day of early voting, which was November 4, 2022, that it was emptied at the end of that day.  He further stated that the ballot was found earlier this week when a member of the Personnel Commission came to borrow the portable box and he noticed there was something in there when he was getting it out of their vault.  He indicated that he is not sure how it was missed and that they believe it may have been caught in the lid of the box.    He advised that once the ballot was found they contacted the County Attorney's Office and Elections Director to make sure that they followed proper procedures to make sure the ballot was counted since it was voted during the early voting period.
Supervisor Whiting thanked both Ms. Richards and Mr. Sample for bringing this forward once it was found and that the proper channels were addressed to make sure that every vote counts.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked everyone who has quickly responded to the critical and important manner.  She indicated that she is proud of the way this it has been handled, that the citizens rely on us to provide professional and quality service, and that staff was able to protect that vote.  She stated that she is grateful for the professionalism in making sure that every vote counts.
Jason Moore addressed the motion that will need to be made.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to amend the Canvass of the November 8, 2022, General Election, originally canvassed by the Board of Supervisors on November 28, 2022, to include one ballot that has been found to be misplaced and was not counted in the original canvass, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :   2:36 p.m. meeting adjourned.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Presentation regarding the decision to rescind the emergency fire restrictions throughout the region :  Catrina Jenkins stated that we have received a lot of rain and that the forecasts are showing for more rain in the future.  She further stated that the emergency release components went from the 97 th percentile to the 50 th percentile and that most of the local responders that had been deployed have returned.  A presentation was shown.  Ms. Jenkins indicated that they look at the data for the seven-day outlook for fire potential, the monthly outlook and the energy release components when making decisions on restrictions.  She stated that this year was a record low number for human caused starts. She indicated that at the beginning of the season the fire potential was very high.  She reviewed the data from the past week showing that the temperature outlooks and precipitation are better.  She talked about the drought monitor and the early monsoons.   She further talked about the Task Force that meets weekly and coordinates all decisions regarding restrictions.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Jenkins on the partnerships and leadership that she provides to areas around the region.  He thanked her for the data she presented and indicated that it makes it easy for our citizens to understand the reason for going in and out of the restrictions.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the communication and leadership from Ms. Jenkins.
Supervisor Benally thanked Ms. Jenkins for the information she provides.
Chairwoman Whitesinger expressed her appreciation to all those involved in the collective coordination and conversations around the decision about restrictions.  She stated that she is grateful and knows that the citizens are grateful for the information that is provided to be able to respond quickly to support our communities.  She further stated that the constant communications are helpful and important to understand the certain levels of fire restrictions to keep the citizens and those visiting safe.
Ms. Jenkins thanked the Board, Glenn Kephart and her team for all of their support.

SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration and approval of a Sheriff's Office mobile application as a part of the already approved Public Safety Communications ARPA project :  Lt. Alden Whipple stated that at the beginning of the year they presented the Public Safety Communications Project to be funded with ARPA funds and that they are ready to purchase the mobile application that would allow the Sheriff's Office to communicate more efficiently and freely with member of the public and allow them to share information faster across different, multiple platforms. A presentation was shown. He stated that they want to increase the transparency and increase citizen engagement.  He talked about the mobile application and the tools, options and features that would be available for the public to access to get information immediately.   He stated that some of the goals that they wanted to accomplish are to continue to improve communication with the citizens/public and make it easier for them to receive information and provide information. He talked about a situation in California were an similar application helped law enforcement be able to reach out to the public to get information such as photos and videos regarding an incident, which allowed them to be able to identify the suspects and witnesses, put a case together and find those who were responsible.  He stated that another goal is to increase the partnerships and communications with the public and increase engagement.  He talked about the statistics for law enforcement using platforms like this to address challenges with public safety and emergency services.  He stated that the application also pushes out emails and SMS messages, that they can post most wanted and warrants lists, that it can be used to address sex offenders and provide information on them, help with inmate searches, which will reduce the numerous phone calls to the 911 center from people wanting jail information and provides a staff directory which allows the public to immediately get contact information for specific deputies and command staff.  He further stated that they can create a post and share it to every social media platform with one click instead of having to create different posts.
Supervisor Whiting stated that he likes the ability for the public to be able to increase communications with the Sheriff's Office and that some of the tools in the application would be a big benefit.  He thanked Lt. Whipple for the presentation.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve a Sheriff's Office mobile application as a part of the already approved Public Safety Communications ARPA project, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PLANNING & ZONING :

1.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 23-2022 , approving/denying text amendments to Articles 3, 4, 25, and 28 of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance, regarding Use Permits and Guest Houses, addition of section to Article 25 :  Cody Cooper stated that they are proposing an amendment to the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance regarding guesthouse and use permits.  A presentation was shown.  He stated that the reasons for the amendment would be to bring the Board of Adjustments into compliance with State Law, to expedite an approval or denial, which is effectively the review time for the public, and to continue the considerations that they have in maintaining the intent of their zoning ordinance.  He talked about the role and authorities of the Board of Adjustments, stating that currently there is nothing in the ordinance regarding use permits or guest houses.  He addressed the current zoning districts which use permits can be allowed for guest houses and the proposed zoning districts.  He talked about the guest house permitting by jurisdiction to show where the rest of the state is compared to Navajo County, stating that eight of the fourteen counties permit by right, which is what they are proposing, where staff reviews the applications; that three of the counties are administratively permitted, which means that they have some form of administrative hearing; two counties permit by special use permit, which means that it would go in front of the Planning and Zoning Commission and then the Board of Supervisors; and that one county did not have any data.  He talked about the current process for the permits, indicating that they come into Planning and Zoning for review and then go before the Board of Adjustments, which takes approximately six to eight weeks from acceptance of the application for the approval or denial. He further talked about the review process by the Planning and Zoning Department.  He stated that with the proposal, the applications will still come to the Department for review, that it will still be the same standard of review, but with taking out the hearing it will take it from a six to eight weeks period to a two week process and will bring the Board of Adjustments into compliance with what they do.  Mr. Cooper stated that staff is recommending approval.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the work done on this.  He inquired if a house is built and then a guest house is built on the same lot if there is anything that says they can't sell the guest house.
Mr. Cooper stated that typically the houses are tied together and sold together, otherwise, they would have to separate and split the lot, which would be an extra process for them.
Supervisor Seymore addressed the challenges that are created with the required square footage that is required for some of the lots.
Brandt Clark addressed the different processes and requirements if someone wished to split off a portion of a lot.
A discussion was held regarding the concerns.
Mr. Cooper stated that they understand the concerns.  He addressed the minimum requirement for the 10,000 square foot.
Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired how many applicants request a guest home on their property.
Mr. Cooper stated that it averages out to twenty to thirty annually.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt  Resolution Number 23-2022 , approving text amendments to Articles 3, 4, 25, and 28 of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance, regarding Use Permits and Guest Houses, addition of section to Article 25, with a recommendation to come back in the future with a little more definition of what guest home is and is not permissible, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

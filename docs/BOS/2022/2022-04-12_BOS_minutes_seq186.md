SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Consideration of a Contract with Novo BioPower, LLC., for access to a boiler at its biomass power facility :  Chief Deputy Bryan Swanty addressed the item, indicating that the contract is for a partnership to help them be more efficient in day to day obligations.  He talked about the process for evidence to be destroyed after it is no longer needed for the case/trial.  He stated that currently they burn the evidence in a burn barrel which is time consuming and indicated that with this contract they will save time for destroying the evidence.  He further stated that the evidence will still be destroyed through the same process but that it will put in the boiler at Novo Power instead of a burn barrel.

Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Worsley for his partnership and indicated that he appreciates the work done on this item.
Supervisor Whiting inquired of County Attorney Brad Carlyon if there are any concerns from the County Attorney's Office with this process.
Brad Carlyon indicated that they do not have an issue and that this is a common method for destroying evidence.
Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that they appreciate the service through this contract and thanked Mr. Worsley for the partnership.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve a Contract with Novo Bio Power, LLC., for access to a boiler at its biomass power facility, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Item 2h: Proclamation declaring the week beginning April 10 through April 16, 2022, as "National Telecommunicators Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe :  Sheriff David Clouse addressed the item.  He introduced Felicia Bennett, Lead Dispatcher.  He talked about the history of the proclamation.  He addressed the statistics for Northern Navajo County and stated that in 2021 Navajo County Dispatch received 92,319 calls to the dispatch center, with 19,026 being 911 calls and 10,000 calls being for a Navajo County Sheriff Deputy to be dispatched to an event.  He talked about the different types of calls that are received daily.  He stated that they appreciate the efforts of the Dispatchers.  He read the proposed proclamation.

Supervisor Seymore talked about the variety of calls that the Dispatchers receive and stated that they appreciate all that the Dispatchers do.
Supervisor Whiting stated that they appreciate the Dispatchers and all that they do.  He further stated that he hopes that all of the communities participate in recognizing this week.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Sheriff Clouse for bringing this proclamation to the Board and stated that as citizens not everyone thinks about all that is done in the background with law enforcement.  She talked about the teamwork it takes to respond to the different situations.  She thanked them for their service and their love for the communities.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve a Proclamation declaring the week beginning April 10 through April 16, 2022, as "National Telecommunicators Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

2.

Presentation of the 2022 Wildfire Season Outlook and Preparedness Actions in Navajo County :  Catrina Jenkins addressed the item. A presentation was shown.  She talked about the Pre-Wildfire Season Kickoff Meeting that they hold every year with all of their partners to make sure they are all of the same page and updated contact information and indicated that after that meeting, they have weekly discussions to addressed conditions and outlooks.  She stated that they communicate with the citizens and share the information with surrounding area media partners to allow those visiting to know what's happening.  She talked about the trap line which is local business that help promote messaging. She talked about the drought situation this year compared to last year and indicated that the drought plays a big part in wildfire.  She indicated that June is the most threatening time of the year, that we are in a wildfire threat year-round, but that it's just more likely now.  She talked about the White Mountain Fire Restrictions Coordinating Group, who makes the decision for going in and out of restrictions every year and the different things they look at when considering potential fire dangers.   She further talked about the Ready, Set, Go promotion.  She stated that they are always planning.
Neil Traver, with APS; Sheriff Clouse and Chief Savage and Deputy Chief Savalty, with Timber Mesa Fire each addressed how their agency is working to be fire ready.

Supervisor Whiting thanked Ms. Jenkins and her team for all that they do.  He addressed the different things that APS does for fire prevention and he thanked them.  He indicated that the Sheriff's Office has done a great job as well.  He inquired if they have businesses that help partner financially.
Ms. Jenkins stated that they don't have businesses that partner financially but that they do have businesses that are tied into the program.  She stated that Navapache is doing work in South County that is similar to what does APS.
Supervisor Whiting talked about the different partnerships that are involved.
Ms. Jenkins stated that the insurance companies have been a big partner of Fire Wise Program.
Supervisor Seymore stated that it's been a great partnership and indicated that he appreciates the Ready, Set, Go promotion.
Supervisor Benally thanked the team for their work with fire prevention.
Supervisor Peshlakai inquired what the coverage area is for Timber Mesa.
Chief Savage addressed the coverage area.
Ms. Jenkins stated that when restrictions go into place, they apply to everyone.
Chairwoman Whitesinger shared her appreciation for all that the team does. She stated that it can be challenging to work with so many organizations and bring them together but indicated that you can see everyone is working together on the same mission and importance of that for our region.  She stated that she is appreciative of the work and of those who are committed and dedicated to our communities.

Vice-Chairman Peshlakai made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

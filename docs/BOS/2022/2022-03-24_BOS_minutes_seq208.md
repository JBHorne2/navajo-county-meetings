NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, March 24, 2022

Present:
Mae-Gilene Begay, Member, District I; Sadie Lister, Member, District II; Ryan Stuart, Chairman, District III; Rick Fernau, Vice-Chairman, District IV, appearing virtually; Cline Griggs, Member, District V, Not Present

Staff Attendance:
Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Ryan Taylor, GIS Manager

12:40 p.m . Call To Order:   Chairman Stuart called the meeting to order.

Call to the Public : There were no comments from the public.

Consideration and approval of the Redistricting Advisory Committee Minutes from February 24, 2022: Ms. Begay made a motion to approve the Redistricting Advisory Committee minutes from February 24, 2022; motion seconded by Mr. Fernau. Vote unanimously passed 4-0 with one member absent.

Discussion and review of maps submitted by the Redistricting Advisory Committee Members: Mr. Stuart indicated that we would like to narrow down our list of submitted maps in order to take at least four to our second round of community meetings. Mr. Stuart asked staff to review the new maps in which the advisory members have not seen yet. Ms. Begay indicated that there are 14 maps that have been submitted and through our attorney, he indicated that some of those maps submitted did not meet the qualifications in terms of voting rights act, communities of interest, deviation and so forth. Ms. Begay requested that we do not belabor those maps that didn't meet the qualifications and just review the maps that have met the qualifications. The committee reviewed the Fernau Revised map, the NNHRC D map, and the RAC Stuart map. Ms.Lauren Bernally addressed the Redistricting Advisory Committee regarding the NNHRC D map. Discussion was held on all the maps submitted.

Discussion/Consideration/Action on maps to circulate during second round of community meetings:  Mr. Fernau indicated that it would nice to take the NNHRC D map and either the Stuart or Fernau map and blend them somehow to incorporate the changes that we would like to see without affecting the Navajo or Hopi Nation. Chairman Stuart asked Ryan Taylor if he would be able to blend these maps into a fourth map. Mr. Taylor indicated that he could do that. Discussion was held. Mr Fernau asked if the names of the maps could be taken off and just numbered. The committee discussed the option of number the maps, Map 1, Map 2, Map 3, and Map 4. Mr. Fernau made a motion that NNHRC D map would be named Map 1, Map 2 would be the Fernau Revised Percy Deal map, Map 3 would be the RAC Stuart map and Map 4 would be the conglomeration; motion seconded by Ms. Begay. Vote passed 4-0 with one absent for the vote. Mr. Moore spoke about the conglomeration of a Map 4. Ms. Begay made a motion to move forward with the four maps into the second round of community meetings as discussed; motion seconded by Ms. Lister. Vote passed 4-0 with one absent for the vote.
Mr. Fernau thanked staff for all of their work.
Discussion was held regarding the second round of community meetings.
The next Redistricting Advisory Committee meeting will be held on May 16 at 3:00 p.m.

ADJOURN : Meeting adjourned at 1:15 p.m.

APPROVED:

Ryan Stuart, Chairman
Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee
DATED: May 16, 2022

ATTEST:

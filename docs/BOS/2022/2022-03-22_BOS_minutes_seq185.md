Consideration of a Proclamation, proclaiming April 2022 as "National County Government Month" :   Vice-Chairman Peshlakai addressed the item.  He indicated that the month of April is dedicated as National County Government Month which provides the County with an opportunity to provide outreach to the citizens on what County Governments is and provide information on the operations, programs and services that are offered.  He stated that one of the days is designated as County Health Day.  He read the proposed proclamation.

Vice-Chairman Peshlakai made a motion  to approve a Proclamation, proclaiming April 2022 as "National County Government Month", motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Vice-Chairman Peshlakai extended his appreciation to the team in coordinating the activities that they are planning for April.  He indicated that during the week of April 18, 2022, which is designated as the County Health Day, they will be holding a 5K walk/run in each of the five Districts.  He stated that they look forward to this promotion of health and wellness as part of the National Counties Health Day.  He advised that on Monday, April 18, 2022 there will be a 5K in Holbrook at 12:00 p.m. and Winslow at 5:00 p.m.; on Tuesday, April 19, 2022 in Kayenta at 4:30 p.m. MST/5:30 p.m. DST and on Friday, April 22, 2022 in Pinetop at 12:00 p.m. and Show Low at 5:30 p.m.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked all of those who have contributed to this and stated that they look forward to the month and share how the County impacts our region.

d.

Consideration of a Liquor License from Jeffrey Craig Miller for The Cabin Bar & Grill located at 2763 Highway 260, Overgaard, AZ : Leah Thomas addressed the item.  She indicated that three of the Departments submitted approvals.  She further indicated that the Sheriff had some concerns regarding the noise levels and late hours, that she reached out to the applicant, who stated that the owner was willing to comply with any noise level requirements and would provide her contact information to the Sheriff's office, and that the Sheriff then stated he didn't have any issues with the application.

Supervisor Seymore commented on the difference is value for the two liquor license applications.  He addressed the part of the application referring to claims, denials or issues having a rubber stamp answer indicating that they are unaware but could have issues.  He inquired of County Attorney Brad Carlyon if due to that acceptance is left up to the State.
Brad Carlyon, County Attorney, indicated that they could request clarification if they wished and stated that this process is for the Board to make a  recommendation but that the ultimate decision is with the Arizona Department of Liquor License.
Jeffrey Craig Miller stated that his company serves as Agent for people applying as liquor license, that they represent and file paperwork for the actual owner.  He indicated that they don't always hear if there were issues but that they work with the owners to ensure that they receive the proper training and that the permits and licenses are updated and renewed on time.  He addressed the reasons for the difference in the value.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve a Liquor License from Jeffrey Craig Miller for The Cabin Bar & Grill located at 2763 Highway 260, Overgaard, AZ, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of award of contract #B22-02-007 Supplying of Various Roadway Materials to Hatch Construction and Paving, Inc.,  Perkins Aggregates, Inc., Perkins Cinders, Inc. and Schuck Development effective March 28, 2022 :  John Osgood addressed the item, indicating that the purpose of this contract is to supply the road maintenance operations with the basic materials needed for maintaining and repairing gravel and asphalt roadways, drainage structures and cinders for snow response.  He indicated that awarding contracts to multiple local vendors who provide materials at multiple locations throughout Navajo County is financially and operationally beneficial to the County.  He addressed the bid process and the price increase of the materials.  He stated that the source of funds is the Highway User Road Funds.  He indicated that staff recommends approval of the item.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the award of contract #B22-02-007 Supplying of Various Roadway Materials to Hatch Construction and Paving, Inc., Perkins Aggregates, Inc., Perkins Cinders, Inc. and Schuck Development effective March 28, 2022, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of Resolution Number 9-22 , adopting the 2018 International Building Code and International Residential Code and Amending the County's Addenda and Additions :  John Osgood acknowledged Chief Building Officer, Rob Douglas, Highway and Building Division Manager, Ricky Denton and Jennifer Ober, indicating that they are the ones who, along with their teams, manage the processes and interpreting and enforcing the International Building Codes. He stated that it has been Navajo County's intentions to align the building codes with other agencies in the region in order to reduce confusion for the customers who do business in multiple areas.  He indicated that most jurisdictions through the State have adopted their codes on a 6-year schedule.  He stated that staff is recommending updating to the 2018 version at this time for the reasons stated above and to ensure that they construct safer buildings, which encounter less damage and lower insured losses from catastrophes.  He addressed the changes from the 2013 to the 2018 version.  He requested that a 90-day transition period be given once approved for implementation of the codes.
Supervisor Seymore indicated that they don't want to become overly cumbersome with adoption of the codes and that they are clear with what they plan.
Ricky Denton addressed and indicated that they have taken that into consideration.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve Resolution Number 9-22 , adopting the 2018 International Building Code and International Residential Code and Amending the County's Addenda and Additions, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

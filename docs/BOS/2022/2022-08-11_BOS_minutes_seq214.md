NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, August 11, 2022

Present:
Alberto Peshlakai, Vice-Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District VI; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman, all appearing by phone

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Michael Sample, Recorder; Rayleen Richards, Elections Director, all present in the Board Chambers; Jalyn Gerlich, Superintendent of Schools, appearing by phone

9:00 a.m . Call To Order: Chairwoman Dawnafe Whitesinger called the meeting to order.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS :

a.

Consideration and approval of the submitted 2023 School District Tax Rates : Jalyn Gerlich addressed the item, stating that she is available to answer any questions on the submitted 2023 School District Tax Rates.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the submitted 2023 School District Tax Rates, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Fern Benally being absent for the vote.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration and approval of the Official Canvass of the August 2, 2022, Primary Election :  Rayleen Richards requested approval of the August 2, 2022 Primary Election.
Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that a Summary of the Election was sent out.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the efforts put into this item and indicated that it is a difficult task in a short time to put together.  He inquired if the non-partisan voters have the same opportunity to vote by mail-in ballot, noting the low turnout.
Ms. Richards indicated that the Recorders Office sends out a 90-day notice to all those that are not participating in a party that gives them the option to choose a ballot and that once the receipt is returned to the Recorders Office they mail them a ballot.
Supervisor Seymore inquired about the late early voting results.
Ms. Richards stated that late early voting is the early ballets that are turned in on election day.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the Official Canvass of the August 2, 2022, Primary Election, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman  Peshlakai .  Motion passed 4-0, with Supervisor Fern Benally being absent for the vote.

Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked everyone for work done on this item and the smooth Primary Election.

ADJOURN :  9:06  a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

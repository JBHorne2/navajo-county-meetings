REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Consideration of approval to Direct Select under the Emergency Procurement procedures to contract with Wexford Health Sources, Inc. for Inmate Health Care Services :  Sheriff David Clouse addressed the Board regarding the item, indicating that this contract agreement which would provide medical services in the jail.  He stated that on February 11, 2022 he received written notice from Advanced Correctional Health that they wished to opt out of the existing agreement effective March 13, 2022 since they are not able to meet the needs due to not being able to hire the staff required for the 24-hour onsite coverage and the increase of the off-site ER transports.  He addressed concerns with the off-site visit increases and indicated that if it continued it could cause constraints with the budget.  He stated that Advanced Correctional Health gave a 30-day notice which will be effective March 13, 2022.  He indicated that they have tried to work out the issues with ACH and that they indicated that they did not wish to continue due to not being able to provide the services that are being paid for.   He stated that they have been reaching out to other vendors that could provide services for the jail starting March 14, 2022 and that contact was made with Wexford Health.  He advised the Wexford is operating at two other counties is Arizona and that the Sheriff's in both counties have had good remarks about their services and indicated that they are meeting the needs of their contracts.
Deputy Chief Ernie Garcia talked about the needs for medical care in the jail.  He indicated that due to time restraints not able to put out an RFP, that they talked to several companies to see if they could secure an emergency contract and that Wexford was the only one that indicated that they could help in the short time period.  He advised that they are offering to do a cost-plus contract for the year, which means that only services that they can provide will be paid for.  He indicated that they are in State and have adequate resources that they can draw from in surrounding communities.  He addressed the reasons for originally contracting with ACH and provided a background of the medical needs and costs for the jail medical.
Supervisor Whiting inquired as to the amount of increase there has been for off-site visit.
Deputy Chief Garcia indicated that there were 65 transport for a period of twelve months, with 33 of them being in the past 90 days.
Supervisor Whiting inquired why Wexford would be a good fit for Navajo County.
Deputy Chief Garcia indicated that they have a long standing relationship in the State or Arizona, that they have been with Yavapai for eighteen years, that the jail has worked with them in the past and that Arizona County Insurance Pool, the agency who provides the insurance, approves and endorses Wexford.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Sheriff Clouse and Deputy Chief Garcia for their leadership on this item and acting quickly to address the issue.
Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that she appreciates the work that the Sheriff's Office has done and the quick work to mitigate the issue to make sure we are in compliance and meeting the needs of those who are in the jail.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve the Direct Select under the Emergency Procurement procedures to contract with Wexford Health Sources, Inc. for Inmate Health Care Services, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to out of the Navajo County Jail District and move into the Navajo County Public Health Services District, motion seconded by  Supervisor Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 06 - 2022 approving/denying a Master Development Plan amendment request to the previously approved Master Site Plan in 2006, also known as Woodland Village, for up to 52-lot manufactured residential subdivision : Berrin Nejad addressed the Board regarding the item, stating that this is an amendment to an existing Master Site Plan approval, that was approved in 2007, and that the property is located at Woodland Road south of Highway 260. She stated that the property is approximately 9.6 acres and is located in Lakeside.  She indicated that the property was rezoned from a C-R to a Special Development, that it had 47 lots for villas and that the new proposal is requesting for 52 lots averaging 4400 each.  She indicated that they new proposal has two access points and is a wider roadway.  She advised that one of the stipulations in 2007 was that it have site-built homes only and that the new proposal is for manufactured homes.  She stated that staff received one call, with the main concern being with flood issues from the past years and that they also submitted a letter which has been provided to the Board. Ms. Nejad indicated that the Planning & Zoning Commission heard this item at their January 20, 2022 meeting and that they recommended 3-1 approval.
The applicant, David Kutis, addressed the Board regarding the proposal and what has already been approved.  He indicated that there is a housing crisis on the mountain, that it is almost impossible to find housing and that what is available is not affordable.  He talked about the location and what they will offer.  He indicated that they are requesting to change from site build to manufacture homes and stated that they can offer housing faster and at a more reasonable price.  He advised that they are not offering the homes, that they will just develop and sale the land.  He addressed Exhibit A which talks about the cost and Exhibit B which talks about the lot coverage.  He talked about the common areas that will be offered.  He indicated that their Engineers have worked with the County to address the drainage issues in that area and stated that it will help the surrounding areas with their flooding issues.

Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the meeting for public comments.

Kay Hamby, 1830 Lee Rees Drive, inquired if the existing sewer will be enough for the additional homes. She indicated that in the past there have been a lot of sewer issues.  She stated that she lives in the area and that there have been issues with sewer and drainage.
Mr. Kutis addressed the sewage and indicated that their engineering will be working closely with them.
Ms. Hamby inquired as to the existing utility assessments, indicated that the easement access to major power and sewer will be reduced.
Bill Bess addressed the access indicated that those will be reviewed as part of the overall development to ensure that they have access to those.

Supervisor Seymore indicated that he has some questions and concerns with the design.  He inquired if they are planning on any garages, if the parking will be mostly on the street and what the width of the roads will be?
Mr. Kutis indicated that they are not planning on garages, that the parking will be in front of the homes and that there will be a 50-foot road width.
Supervisor Seymore indicated that the original plan didn't call for any changes on Woodland Road but that he would like to see a new impact/traffic study to see if anything has changed before it comes back with the final plan.  He talked about having an updated flood plan and drainage on Lee Rees Drive.  He talked about the previous zone with the requirement on site-built homes in 2007 and indicated that if nothing happened in five years it should have reverted back to its original zoning.  He inquired if the applicants are willing to make the CC&R's require, if they approve manufactured homes, that they be on a permanent foundation?
Mr. Kutis indicated that they are planning on requiring that they be on a permanent foundation.
Chairwoman Whitesinger addressed concerns regarding the concerns of the residents and the increased traffic in the area.
Mr. Bess addressed the parking and flood concerns raised by Supervisor Seymore and Chairwoman Whitesinger and indicated that they are doing a right of way survey from Lee Rees Drive.

Supervisor Seymore indicated that they would also like for the CC&R's to be recorded with the final plan and that in the CC&R's it state that the homeowners are responsible for maintaining and keeping the retention basins  and any improvements are needed that it's the responsibility of the home owners association.
Mr. Kutis indicated that they are planning on that.

There being no additional comments the Public Hearing was closed.

Melissa Buckley indicated that the comments that she received have been provided to the Board.

Supervisor Seymore addressed the concerns with the traffic, the flooding and the CC&R's.
Ms. Nejad indicated that the concerns mentioned will be addressed at the final plan stage.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 06 - 2022 approving a Master Development Plan amendment request to the previously approved Master Site Plan in 2006, also known as Woodland Village, for up to 52-lot manufactured residential subdivision, motion seconded by  Chairwoman Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Consideration of a contract with The WLB Group in the amount of $88,095 to prepare engineering construction plans for replacing the existing (3) corrugated metal pipes (CPM) with multiple precast Concrete Box Culverts (CBC) to increase the drainage flow capacity under Bourdon Ranch Rd. : Bill Bess addressed the item, indicating that when the Millet Swale breached in July with all of the flooding that it created an opening that let water down the channel where it hit Bourdon Ranch road where the existing culverts underneath and caused a backup due to the pipes not being large enough. He advised that with this proposal they will survey and prepare a design for the installation of pre-cast concrete cast culverts which will allow more water underneath the road and minimize the amount of flooding that can occur.  He indicated that they have also been coordinating with Silver Creek Flood Control District's design efforts for the Millet Swale Dam and the stream to create a solution for the residents in the area.  He stated that staff recommends approval.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the contract with The WLB Group in the amount of $88,095 to prepare engineering construction plans for replacing the existing (3) corrugated metal pipes (CPM) with multiple precast Concrete Box Culverts (CBC) to increase the drainage flow capacity under Bourdon Ranch Rd., motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

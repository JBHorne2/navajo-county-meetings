SHERIFF :

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation proclaiming the week of May 1 - May 7, 2022, as "National Correctional Officers' Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe :  Deputy Chief Swanty addressed the item.  He stated that the first week of May has been designed to recognize Correctional Officers.  He indicated that half of the Sheriff Office staff is at the jail and that they are not seen as often as those on the streets but that the work they do is critical to the Court system.  He stated that they maintain approximately 300 inmates daily and talked about care that is required for those individuals on a daily basis.  He talked about the management of the jail staff by Lieutenant Warren, Lieutenant Perkins and Deputy Chief Garcia.  He read the proposed proclamation.

Supervisor Whiting thanked the jail staff for the work they do.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve the Proclamation proclaiming the week of May 1 - May 7, 2022, as "National Correctional Officers' Week" in Navajo County, Arizona, in honor of the men and women whose diligence and professionalism keep our county and citizens safe, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Deputy Chief Swanty stated that Court Security staff also provides support as part of the management of the jail.

PUBLIC WORKS :

PLANNING AND ZONING :

1.

PUBLIC HEARING:  Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 15 - 2022,  approving/denying a Special Use Permit for Overgaard RV Storage with 140 units :  Cody Cooper addressed the item.  A presentation was shown.  He talked about the location of the site, indicating that its 3.56 acres and that it is zoned Commercial-Residential.  He talked about the project for a 140 space RV storage facility, the site plan, the intended fencing that will be put up, the intended land scaping and signage that will be put up.  He stated that staff received opposition from one individual on this item and that they received nine letters of support.  He stated that the Planning and Zoning Commission heard this item at their April 21, 2022 meeting and recommended approval with the following conditions: drainage and grading plans for all new sites will be completed and approved by Public Works prior to building permits being issues; a storm water prevention plan will be prepared and a copy of the notice of intent shall be submitted as part of the grading plan permit.  He stated that staff recommends approval.

Supervisor Seymore stated that this project is something that is changing the landscaping in different communities.  He addressed the importance of the landscaping and fencing be followed through with.  He indicated that he is thankful for the project and hopes that it is successful.

Chairwoman Whitesinger opened meeting for public comments.  There being no comments, the public hearing was closed.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 15 - 2022, approving a Special Use Permit for Overgaard RV Storage with 140 units, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

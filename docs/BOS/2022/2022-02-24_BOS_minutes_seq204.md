NAVAJO COUNTY REDISTRICTING ADVISORY COMMITTEE MINUTES
Thursday, February 24, 2022

Present:

Mae-Gilene Begay, Member; Sadie Lister (VIRTUAL), Member; Ryan Stuart, Chairman; Rick Fernau (VIRTUAL), Vice-Chairman

Absent:

Cline Griggs, Member

Staff Attendance:

Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Ryan Taylor, GIS Manager; Isaura Nez, Navajo Interpreter

3:05 p.m. Call To Order : Pledge of Allegiance was led by Ms. Mae-Gilene Begay.

Call to the Public : There were no comments from the public.

Consideration and approval of the Redistricting Advisory Committee Minutes from October 26, 2021, and November 29, 2021 : Ms. Begay made a motion to approve the Redistricting Advisory Committee Minutes from October 26, 2021, and November 29, 2021; motion seconded by Rick Fernau . Vote passed unanimously 4-0 with one member absent.

Discussion of First Round of Community Meetings and Public Input Received : Jason Moore indicated that this is an opportunity for each member of the Redistricting Committee to provide us with information that they received from the public during the first round of community meetings. Chairman Stuart stated that he attended two meetings with very limited participation with no specific feedback. He indicated that he also participated in the virtual meeting which had the best participation with community input with recommendations on drawing lines. Chairman Stuart indicated that the first round of community meetings was disappointing. Ms. Begay spoke about three meetings, Kayenta, Pinon, and Hardrock. She indicated that the participation was disappointing and indicated that she sent out several text messages and emails inviting community members to attend. Ms. Begay stated that she is not sure what to do to get participation. Mr. Fernau indicated that the participation was from staff with 100% attendance. Mr. Fernau thanked staff for their participation and further thanked Mr. Moore for showing him how to use the mapping tool. Ms. Begay indicated that the CDC precautionary measurers may have caused low attendance. Mr. Moore stated that staff was aware of those concerns as well, and we did everything possible to get information out to our communities. He stated that he is hopeful that the second round of community meetings will be better attended. Chairman Stuart thanked staff for all that have done and continue to do.

Discussion and review of current maps submitted for the Redistricting Advisory Committee's consideration : Jason Moore stated that 6 maps were received prior to today, which came from various sources. Mr. Moore spoke about the strengths, weaknesses and problem areas of the maps. He indicated that all the maps can be viewed by going to the redistricting website. Mr. Moore discussed the NNHRC B Map. He addressed the division of tribal lands; the City of Holbrook; and the Heber/Overgaard area. Mr. Moore discussed the NNHRC C Map. He spoke about how the population demographics indicating that the numbers look good. He addressed how it splits the City of Winslow, the City of Holbrook and Heber/Overgaard, which are considered communities of interest. He further indicated that it divides the Hopi Reservation. Ms. Begay talked about the division on the Hopi Reservations. Supervisor Benally inquired about the communities on the Hopi Reservation. A discussion was held regarding the Hopi Reservation split. Mr. Moore discussed the Percy Deal map. He talked about the statistics with the proposed map. He reviewed the changes this would bring with the adjustments he made. Mr. Moore talked about the Joseph City Only Map and indicated that if you want to keep the supervisor districts the same as they are today and do the smallest change to the map, then moving Joseph City to District One would work. Mr. Moore reviewed the Navajo County Green Map indicating that it is not compact. He stated that trying to get the supervisor districts to make them compact is virtually impossible. He reviewed the demographics with the map indicating that the Native population in District Two is below what is set forth by the Voting Rights Act. He indicated that it splits a community of interest in the Heber/Overgaard area. He addressed the Show Low and Pinetop/Lakeside areas. Mr. Fernau indicated that it splits Show Low/Pinetop/Lakeside. Ms. Begay addressed the Jeddito area. Mr. Moore reviewed the Purple Map. He reviewed the demographics indicating that it is below the targeted number for the native population in District II. He indicated that it divides Holbrook into two, which could be a potential problem. Ms. Begay talked about the prison population being added into District II and keeping Holbrook from being split. He reviewed the division in the Pinetop-Lakeside area. Mr. Moore talked about Clark Navajo County Map. He reviewed the demographics. Supervisor Benally indicated that this map would remove her from her District. He reviewed the divisions in the different Districts. Mr. Moore talked about the revised Percy Deal Map indicating that the native population for District Two needed to be increased. He reviewed the changes that were made. He talked about the native population for the demographics. Mr. Moore indicated that we have now seen 8 different scenarios and that they all have strengths and weaknesses. He stated that these are some different ideas to consider. Bruce Adelson stated that he thinks that this is early in the process, that the requirements were pointed out and that there are some choices that can be difficult but that Mr. Moore did a good job laying it out.

Redistricting Members share ideas for drawing new Supervisorial District/NPC precinct maps : Supervisor Benally requested that the demographics for Holbrook be reviewed further. Mr. Moore reviewed the demographics. A discussion was held regarding the demographics for the Holbrook/Joseph City area.
The Committee recessed at 4:40 p.m.
The Committee reconvened at 4:47 p.m.
The Committee reviewed the maps discussing the pros and cons of each map. Mr. Moore indicated that they will need to be careful with the communities of interest. Mr. Fernau indicated that they need to prioritize the things that need to be prioritized. Chairman Stuart indicated that communities of interest may have to be divided but that they need to comply with the voter rights act. Discussion was held regarding priorities. Ms. Begay addressed her views on the different priorities. She indicated that communities of interest is high priority. Ms. Lister addressed her views on the priorities. She indicated that the NNHRC maps do divide Hopi. She indicated that the maps are good but that they need to work on hitting the 10% or 5%. She indicated that communities of interest are the highest priority. She suggested that we should get Hopi's input. Chairman Stuart inquired if the Hopis have the ability to submit a map like that of the Navajo Tribe. Jason Moore indicated that they do not have the ability to do their own map like NNHRC did. They can use the DRA tool. He addressed ways to get the position of the Hopi Tribe. He indicated that they could try to have a meeting on the Hopi Reservation. Discussion was held on the second round of community meetings and reaching out to the different tribes. Ms. Begay indicated that they should reach out to the tribes to get their input. Chairman Stuart indicated that they should reach out to the communities that they represent to get their input. Mr. Fernau spoke about the Resolution passed by the Board of Supervisors which lists the top priorities. Discussion was held regarding the priorities set forth in the Resolution. The committee continued to discuss the individual maps. Percy Deal addressed the Committee regarding the maps submitted by the Navajo Tribe indicating that it splits the Hopi and Navajo Reservations. He stated under the current districts it is off set. He stated that with his experience with the Hopi Tribe they will not be okay with the split. Mr. Fernau agreed with Mr. Deal's assessment. Ms. Begay addressed the Hardrock area indicating that there are not Hopi’s living in those communities, that the Navajo’s who have permission are living there, and she believes the Hopis' would be willing to work with us. Leonard Gorman addressed the Committee regarding the maps that he submitted on behalf of the Navajo Nation Human Rights Commission. He indicated that their top priority is the VRA everything else is below. He talked about keeping chapter boundaries as a part of communities of interests in the Navajo and Hopi Nations. He talked about the communities surrounding the Navajo Nation indicating that there are many Navajo families that live in the surrounding areas including Winslow, Holbrook, Joseph City and Sun Valley. He further indicated that his main interest are Districts 1, 2 and 5. Chairman Stuart thanked Mr. Gorman for his comments. The Committee reviewed the Percy Deal Map and discussed the pros and cons of this map. Mr. Deal indicated that with this map he was trying to keep the communities of interest altogether and also to respect the chapter boundaries. He also stated that it keeps the Hopi altogether in District II. He indicated that he is interested in Districts 1, 2 and 5 and that he would like to see those numbers kept intact. Chairman Stuart thanked him for his comments, his previous service and his map submission. Discussion continued on the Percy Deal map. The Committee reviewed the Joseph City only Map. Chairman Stuart indicated that this map is the least deviation to the current districts. Mr. Moore indicated that the biggest concern with this map is the 8% deviation and that they are hoping to be closer to 5%. He talked about the Voting Rights Act numbers. He indicated that District 5 needs to be brought up to at least 51% to be in compliance with VRA. Discussion was held regarding the VRA in District 5 and population in the other districts. The Committee talked about the Green Map. Chairman Stuart indicated that the deviations are fine, but this map splits Heber-Overgarrd, doesn’t recognize supervisor districts and took District 2 Native Population under 7%. He indicated that the lines are fairly clean however when we look at the statistics there are some problems with that map. Ms. Begay addressed breaking the communities of interest. The Committee addressed the Purple Map, which splits Holbrook. The Committee addressed the Clark Map, indicating that we would be unseating at least two Supervisors and some communities of interest would be divided. The Committee addressed the Revised Percy Deal Map which indicates that it achieves the greatest number of objectives. Chairman Stuart indicated that this map draws on most of the guidelines that they have set and that it would be a good map to start working on. He indicated that it should be one of the proposed maps to bring back to the communities for the second round of meetings. Chairman Stuart requested that the members take this map and with the input from their communities they can start tweaking it.

Discussion/Consideration/Action on maps to circulate during second round of community meetings : Jason Moore reviewed the options for selecting maps prior to the second rounds of meetings. Chairman Stuart suggested that each of the committee members take the information received today, submit a map based off of that information, and then have one more RAC Meeting prior to going out to the second round of meetings. Ms. Begay indicated that she would like all members to be present in person to allow for a good discussion. The suggestion was discussed by the committee. Ms. Begay made a motion to have another Redistricting Advisory Committee meeting on March 10, 2022, with all members being present in person and at which time each committee member will present a map of their choice to the committee,  motion second by Ms. Lister . Vote passed unanimously 4-0 with one member absent. Chairman Stuart indicated that the maps should be published so that they are able to be viewed by staff prior to the meeting. Jason Moore requested that as the members publish their individual maps that they should also be sent to Melissa so that they can be placed on the website. Chairman Stuart indicated that all maps should be sent to Melissa by March 9 for placement on the website.

ADJOURN : At 5:42 p.m. the meeting was adjourned.

APPROVED:

Ryan Stuart, Chairman
Navajo County Redistricting Advisory Committee
DATED: March 24, 2022

ATTEST:

Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Vice-Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration of Task Agreement No. 27 to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Bureau of Indian Affairs- Navajo Region and Navajo County that authorizes funding assistance for Fort Defiance Agency in providing aggregate material for BIA public Route 6 :  John Osgood stated that approval of this item continues a very successful partnership that facilitates road repair and drainage projects on public tribal roads and school bus routes.  He indicated that the specific request comes from the Indian Wells community for an emergency culvert repair on Navajo Route 6.  He advised that following a meeting last week, that BIA & NDOT have initiated an emergency erosion backfill effort to repair a box culvert to prevent further erosion.  He stated that in a joint effort BIA forces will provide the labor, NDOT is providing the heavy equipment and that Navajo County has been asked to provide the aggregate materials.  He indicated that the source of funding is Highway User Revenue Funds and that the fiscal impact is $5000.00. He stated that staff recommends approval.

Vice-Chairman Peshlakai thanked staff for the work done on this.  He showed pictures of the erosion around the culvert.  He stated that it is a major route, that they consider it the southern entrance to the Navajo Nation, that it is a highly traveled route and that it's a school bus route for the Holbrook Unified Schools and that at least six buses travel that road twice a day.  He talked about how the permit was able to be expedited due to the great partnerships.
Supervisor Whiting inquired if this will help with the care of the road to improve the safety.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai indicated it would.
Supervisor Whiting thanked all of those involved.
A discussion was held regarding the pictures shown and the work that will be done.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve Task Agreement No. 27 to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Bureau of Indian Affairs- Navajo Region and Navajo County that authorizes funding assistance for Fort Defiance Agency in providing aggregate material for BIA public Route 6, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKES COUNTY RECREATION IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :   Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to enter into the White Mountain Lakes County Recreation Improvement District Board of Directors Session, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Quarterly Report and Update :  Mark Honberger addressed the item.  A presentation was shown.  He addressed the community meeting that was held November 18 indicating that everyone was receptive to the information that was provided on the improvements that have been made, the possibility and idea of having a Local Board, the possible contract renewal with the Silver Creek Irrigation District and the water level updates.  He talked about work tasks that have been completed such as repairing the failing dock floats, remeasurement of the parking lot lines, the wet mooring, replacement of several buoys, adding a basketball court and added picnic tables.  He indicated that they have implemented regular newsletters and Facebook postings.  He stated that they now have kayak rentals, shirts for the staff and the online weather station. He addressed the vacation rentals parking passes that will now have a small fee, some of the additional signage that has been put up and the membership database.  He talked about the work that is planned for 2022 such as continued district assessment, the regular newsletters/Facebook postings/quarterly meetings, the curb painting project, the parking lots and disc golf, swim cove markers and playground bark.  He further talked about repairs that are being done to the facilities.  He addressed the credit card payments, inventory tracking, switching their website, getting a food truck to come in during the summer season and bringing in recreational classes.  He further addressed community feedback and ways that the office can be reached, which can all be tracked now.

Supervisor Seymore indicated that he appreciates all the work that is being done.
Supervisor Whiting addressed the issues with communications, the meeting that was held and the improvements that have been made.  He thanked Mr. Honberger for the work he has been doing.  He addressed the Bill that is being worked on that would allow the District to have a local board and indicate that those who are in support of the bill need to call and email Representative Blackman and possibly reaching out to the Legislature.  He addressed having a meeting with the Irrigation District.
Supervisor Benally thanked Mr. Honberger for his presentation and requested that he provide pictures in future presentation to allow the Board to see some of the improvements.

Supervisor Peshlakai made a motion  to return to regular session, motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :  Glenn Kephart congratulated Melissa Buckley on her 13 th Anniversary as Clerk of the Board.  He thanked Supervisor Seymore for his year of leadership.  He congratulated Chairwoman Whitesinger on her appointment.
Supervisor Seymore talked about working on improving by one percent each day and the improvements that can be accomplished by the end of the year.  He indicated he is looking forward to 2022.
Supervisor Benally thanked everyone for their leadership. She indicated that we should accomplish the things we can moving into 2022 and continue to work towards our goals.
Supervisor Whiting stated that the Arizona Legislature opened yesterday and that we will have a chance to monitor what they are doing and engage in the conversations.  He further stated that LPC will start meeting every Friday and encouraged that staff be provided with any input that should be included in the report.  He talked about working with CSA to be able to communication better and make sure that Navajo County is heard and indicated that he will keep everyone in the loop moving forward.  He indicated that he looks forward and appreciates being able to work with the Board in the coming year, that there are things that they have in common and care about but that he values the differences of the Board and that he listens to and loves to learn and support each Supervisor in their District.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai wished everyone a Happy New Year.  He indicated that looks forward to working with staff and Supervisors this year.  He stated that 2021 was an amazing year even with the challenges, but that it didn't stop them, and that we will continue to press on.  He indicated that he was happy to be able to go down and meet some of the Legislatures and that he looks forward to being able to work on regional and local projects with them.
Chairwoman Whitesinger indicated that it has started off as an interesting year at the Legislature due to the numbers of Legislators who have stepped down but think that it's a positive thing that there are a lot of eager people to serve in their new roles.  She stated that there is a need to build relationships to ensure that they can advocate for not only our Counties but all the Counties through the State.  She talked about improving by one percent as stated by Supervisor Seymore and indicated that even one small change can impact our day.  She further talked about being more positive and starting with one small change. She thanked Public Works for the works they have done on the roads during the storms.

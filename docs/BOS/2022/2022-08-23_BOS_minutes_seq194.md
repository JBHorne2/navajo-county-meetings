b.

Presentation regarding Navajo County's benefit offerings and Edison Health program :  Eric Scott addressed the item and addressed the benefits offered by Edison Health Care.  He stated that it is a program that offers second opinions and surgeries at their smart care medical centers or centers of excellence around the country.  He further stated that the smart care centers are known for being the best in this specific are of medical care and allows for the best doctors and best care in the locations that specializes in their need.  He indicated that through the direct contracts that Edison has with the providers and care centers, they are able to offer concierge care with all the travel and transportation arrangements coordinated by their team.  He talked about the savings since bringing Edison on board.  He stated that Mark Lamb was the first employee that used the program.
Mark Lamb addressed the Board regarding his experience with Edison and thanked the Board of Supervisors.  He thanked Ron Smith with the Care Committee for the hours that were donated to him.  He stated in October 2020 he found out that he had cancer.  He talked about his experience with finding out he had cancer, the visits to the doctors telling him that there was nothing that could be done and his experience with Edison Health Care through cancer and heart failure which have both had successful outcomes for him.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai thanked Mr. Lamb and Mr. Scott for the presentation.  He stated that Mr. Lamb's story was remarkable and inspirational and that hearing the firsthand account of the support that was provided and of everyone involved adds more to our beliefs to stand strong.
Supervisor Seymore talked about knowing Mr. Lamb for many years and the example that he is.   He thanked staff for the work they do to help others and be able to provide the services.
Supervisory Benally thanked Mr. Lamb for sharing his story.

COUNTY ATTORNEY :

a.

Navajo County Opioid Education Presentation :  Brad Carlyon stated that the item is on behalf of the Navajo County Criminal Justice Coordinating Council.  He indicated that the opioid settlement funds are coming to the County and to the municipalities and indicated that they need to figure out how best to use the money to help fight the opioid epidemic and other drugs.  He indicated that there are some stipulations for using the funds, one category being education and the other category being treatment.  Mr. Carlyon talked about when the Criminal Justice Committee started and how they shared the information on their website and through a radio show but indicated that they didn't have the resources needed to do a lot more.  A presentation was shown.  He further talked about the drug of choice being opioids, the program that was originally stated and what they would like to do with it now to educate more people and give them tools and resources.  He addressed the rehabilitation part of the program. He talked about possible partnerships with the municipalities.
Jason Cash addressed the Board regarding things that the Probation Officers experience with the opioid epidemic.  He stated that along with education they need to be able to expand the treatment opportunities.
Mr. Carlyon stated that they feel that these are two very strong ideas and that they would like to see the Board to set up a committee to start vetting ideas for how to use the money to benefit the citizens of Navajo County and make a difference.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai inquired if they have been involved with the Tribal Opioid and Substances Conference and indicated that he would provide information to them regarding the conference.
Mr. Carlyon stated that he would love to attend and make the additional contacts to reach out to and learn of other resources that they can use to benefit the citizens.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the work that has been done on this.  He talked about how Generation Z is learning by watching rather than by listening.  He stated that the Board will address further at their retreat.
Supervisor Benally thanked Mr. Carlyon and Mr. Cash for the presentation.  She talked about the loss of a family member through drug and alcohol use.  She indicated that she would like to share this information with her communities to help those who are having issues with the epidemic.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 30 - 2022 approving/denying a Special Use Permit Extension to continue operation of a glamping recreational camp located on  APN#  404-51-008, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 23 East, Section 1 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian :  Cody Cooper addressed the item.  A presentation was shown.  He stated that it is a special use permit for a glamping site that was previously approved in 2017 for five years and that the applicant is now requesting an extension.  He addressed the location of the site, the request from the applicant, the number of camping sites and the improvements that have been made.  He showed pictures of the site.  He talked about the residents that were notified of the application in Navajo and Apache County.  He stated that they had received one objection after the Planning and Zoning Commission Hearing, which was held on July 21, 2022, and that at the meeting it was unanimously recommended for approval of the item subject to the conditions that staff had raised.  He stated that Planning and Zoning is recommending approval of the special use permit subject to the Commission's recommendation of granting the permit in perpetuity.
Steve Romania, the applicant, addressed the Board regarding the site.  He addressed the improvements that have been made and talked about the glamping sites.

Vice-Chairman Peshlakai opened the meeting for public comments.  He stated that they have received a copy of the objection.  There being no comments, the Public Hearing was closed.
Supervisor Seymore inquired if it would be a problem if the permit was just approved for five years.
Mr. Cooper indicated that it is at the discretion of the Board.
Supervisor Seymore talked about the reasons for wanting to have a time limit.
Supervisor Benally inquired if the person who objected was notified of the meeting and given the Zoom information.
Melissa Buckley stated that she sent them the information yesterday.  She further stated that they are not on Zoom.
Supervisor Whiting indicated that he agrees with the five-year approval.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 30 - 2022 approving a Special Use Permit Extension to continue operation of a glamping recreational camp located on APN# 404-51-008, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 23 East, Section 1 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, with the provision that they will need to apply for an extension in five years, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Vote passed 4-1, with Supervisor Benally objecting to the item.

Consideration of Coordinated County Design and Style Guide Development :  Bryan Layton addressed the capital renovations that they are doing and indicated that they have had conversations with the architects regarding the signage in Navajo County.  A presentation was shown.  He talked about how the county is currently identify county facility and letting the public know what services are available at the different facilities and indicated that there is a lot of room for improvement.  He addressed how coordinated design reflects a consistent identity.  He stated that he went to the different locations and looked at the outdoor signage for Navajo County and addressed the issues with the current signage at many locations and how inconsistent it is. He talked about the inconsistent signage located inside of the different locations.  He indicated that there are twenty different styles of outdoor signage and over ten different styles for indoors.  He talked about the different styles used for letterhead, website banners, social media, and email signatures.  He stated that this is important because it's a visual representation of values of the organization, that is sets the tone, has specific feelings, creates and ease of use and sets accountability.  He indicated that for the County identity there is no clear of consistent signage for residents to identity county facilities, know what services are available or understand how to access services.  He talked about the action plan and next steps.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Layton for taking the lead on this item.  He inquired if this will be done as signage needs to be renovated or upgraded.
Mr. Layton indicated that it would be done as needed but that they would start with the digital process first.

c.

Consideration of an Arizona Broadband Development Grant agreement between Arizona Commerce Authority and Navajo County and accepting a grant award of $9,734,083.06 for the County's middle-mile broadband infrastructure project with local match of $9,000,000 :  Ken Maruyama addressed the Board regarding the item.  He stated that in July the Arizona Commerce Authority announced the award winners for the Arizona Broadband Development Grant.  He stated that Navajo County was one of the grant awardees and was awarded $9,734,083.06, with a local match of $9,000,000 from the County's ARPA Funds.  He stated that staff recommends approval.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to approve an Arizona Broadband Development Grant agreement between Arizona Commerce Authority and Navajo County and accepting a grant award of $9,734,083.06 for the County's middle-mile broadband infrastructure project with local match of $9,000,000, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, November 28, 2022

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Vice-Chairman; Jason Whiting, Supervisor District III; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District VI; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Michael Sample, Recorder; Rayleen Richards, Election Director; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, all present in the Board Chambers

9:00 a.m . Call To Order: Chairwoman Whitesinger called the meeting to order.

CONSENT AGENDA :

a.

Arizona Department of Administration Agreement Number DBFFY23L22CH31310, for one time payments for eligible Sheriff's Deputies

b.

As required by the final ARPA guidelines and in consultation with the State Auditor, this action updates the FY22-23 Budget to match ARPA revenues to expenditures in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP).  This is a procedural action only and does not allocate new funds nor does it modify the Board of Supervisors' approved ARPA program :
Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to authorize the Chairman to sign the items in the consent agenda; motion seconded by  Supervisor Whiting .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of the Official Canvass of the results for the November 8, 2022, General Election :
Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that there are some individuals who wish to speak regarding the election and that they will each have three minutes to speak.
Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that t
Lisa Green, Chairwoman for Navajo County Republican Committee, advised the Board that during Early Voting on October 26, 2022, at the Kayenta Chapter in the Red Butte Precinct a poll observer indicated that the location had the KTNN radio station playing which included heavily pro democratic ads running.  She further advised that on October 28, 2022, she went to the Kayenta Chapter house to observe and approximately ten minutes after arriving they again turned on the KTNN radio station, that they had two speakers on the check in table between the poll pads, that three songs were played and then a ford ad began, and they turned the radio down when two county election employees came in to pick up ballots.  She indicated that playing live radio inside the 75-foot limit in electioneering and addressed the statutes concerning electioneering and the consequences.  She stated that there were ten days of early voting in the Red Butte precinct and there is no way to know how many voters were influenced by electioneering during that time.   She further stated that it is imperative that all statues are followed equally.
Sandee McKinley, 307 S. Otto Dr., Snowflake, talked about concerns with issues in Maricopa County that disenfranchised voters across the state and in Navajo County.  She indicated that many voters would like the counties to unite and withhold certifying the election until the investigations are resolved for the recounts and lawsuits.  She reviewed ARS16-531 and addressed concerns with poll workers who have changed parties since 2020, not having an equal number from each party at the polling places and having an equal number of inspectors in the various precincts from the two largest political parties.  She indicated that all laws need to be followed during the election.
Don Haggard, 2062 Grey Squirrel Trail, Overgaard, talked about concerns he has with the voting machines being comprised by algorithms, stated that he thinks it would be best to verify the votes prior to the certification and that if voters could get the cast vote records it would give citizens confidence that the election was free, fair, accurate and secure.
Dyan Flyzik,8539 E. Concho Highway, Snowflake, stated that she emailed and hand delivered a letter setting forth reasons why she believes the certification should be postponed.   She addressed the statutory mandate to have a recount due to the minor differences in votes for two positions.  She indicated that it is her understanding that a threat was made by Katie Hobbs that a lawsuit will be filed by those who do not certify the election and addressed concerns with Ms. Hobbs running for Governor while overseeing the elections as current Secretary of State.  She addressed concerns with not having a recount.  She requested that the Board defer and postpone the certification.
Donna Shurwin, 1207 S. Forest Ln., Pinetop, requested that the certification be deferred pending the completion of the lawsuits and addressed the reasons for the request.
Jerry Hubbard, 2726 Covered Wagon Trail, Overgaard, stated that he was a poll worker for primary and general election.  He stated that he has been voting for fifty years and that for most of those years voting was done in person on election day, with an ID and a paper ballot.  He stated that elections are now a mess and talked about concerns with voter suppression and disenfranchisement.  He requested that the Board delay the certification until the completion of the lawsuits.
Chairwoman thanked everyone for taking the time to come and share their positions.
Jason Moore addressed ARA16-642 regarding the certification of the election and why it has to be canvassed by November 28.  He talked about the reasons set forth in the statute that would allow for a delay of the canvass but indicated that since there are no missing results that the Board does not have discretion to not follow the law.  He addressed the litigation for the Attorney General race and indicated that the lawsuit does not ask for a delay of the canvass, that it asks that if the court should find the results different from those in the original canvass that there be an order to amend the canvass.  He stated that there are no legal reasons to postpone the canvass.
Rayleen Richards requested that the Board approve the official canvass results of the November 8, 2022, general election.
Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired if there are any questions regarding the election being canvassed.
Supervisor Seymore requested that some of the issues from the public be addressed, such as the radio being played, and inquired if anything that happened would make a difference with proceeding with the canvass.
Michael Sample, Recorder, addressed the Board regarding the issues of the radio being played at the polling place, indicating when it was brought to their attention, they advised the poll workers that it was not allowed.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates the concerns of the citizens regarding the voting machines and requested that the certification of the voting machines be addressed.
Rayleen Richards stated that they are certified, and that she has sent the information regarding the certification to the Board previously.
Melissa Buckley stated that there are members of the public that have questions.
Jason Moore advised that he is not able to give legal advice to the public, and that is his understanding that it was Chairwoman Whitesinger's intent that it be for any questions from members of the Board.  He stated that the public may ask any questions that they have to an elections law attorney.
Chairwoman Whitesinger  stated that it her intent for it to be questions from the Board of Supervisors.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to approve the Official Canvass of the results for the November 8, 2022, General Election, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :   9:20 a.m. meeting adjourned.

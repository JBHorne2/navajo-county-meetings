SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Recognition of Chief Deputy Garcia for receiving the Jail Commander of the Year Award by the Arizona Sheriff's Association :  Sheriff David Clouse stated that they would like to recognize Chief Deputy Ernie Garcia on receiving the Arizona Sheriff's Association Jail Commander of the Year for 2022.  He further stated that in early June there was an Annual Conference in Scottsdale for all 13 Counties and that he was unanimously voted from the Sheriff's Association and his fellow Jail Commanders for the award.  Sheriff Clouse advised that Mr. Garcia was nominated by Chief Deputy Brian Swanty and his Lieutenants that work for him for the work he has done.  He indicated that Mr. Garcia has over 30 years of detention experience, that he has been with Navajo County for over ten years and that he has done a phenomenal job.  He talked about a lot of the recognition coming from his mitigation efforts in dealing with COVID over the past couple of years and stated that he appreciates his leadership efforts in that.  He stated that he has done a great job with different jail contracts.  He further stated that he has a management style that is very fitting for a detention facility and does a great job.  He thanked Mr. Garcia for everything that he does.
Supervisor Seymore stated that Mr. Garcia has done a great job and that they appreciate his efforts.  He indicated that there are challenges but that they hear very little complaints.  He thanked him for his service over the years.
Ernie Garcia stated that he can only be recognized based on the leadership that he has and that it is a testament to the Sheriff Clouse and Chief Deputy Swanty that his team is able to accomplish what they do.  He further stated that he is part of a Team that does a phenomenal job and that it couldn't be done without them.
Chairwoman Whitesinger congratulated Mr. Garcia, his Department and the Sheriff's Office.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :
Supervisor Seymore addressed the comment from Chief Garcia regarding his award being because of more than just his efforts.  He stated that one of the greatest things he appreciates about Navajo County is the team efforts.  He talked about an abatement that took the team efforts of the Sheriff's Department, County Attorney's Office, Public Works and Planning and Zoning and indicated that the citizens in the area were thankful, which let them know if was the right decision.  He wished everyone a happy 24 th of July and stated that our heritage in these communities is important and goes back for many years.  He further stated that he is thankful for the freedoms that we have in our Country that we were able to celebrate over the 4 th of July.
Supervisor Benally reported on the transitioning of the Chapters between District One and District Two.  She talked about the campaign efforts for the Navajo Nation Delegates.  She stated that she is grateful for the Chapters that she has served and worked with.  She thanked Ken Maryama for reaching out to some of the Chapters regarding some grant opportunities that didn't qualify for the ARPA funds.  She reported on the Broadband meeting that she attended regarding the Navajo Nation and thanked Coconino County Supervisor Lena Fowler and the other delegates that put the meeting on.  She indicated that any Broadband that they are able to construct on the Navajo Nation would be appreciated.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai stated that he has been reintroducing himself to some of the Chapters that are now in District Two and establish contact with them.  He thanked staff and the Holbrook School District for hosting the Navajo Nation Broadband Initiative Meeting and the work put into it.  He thanked Chairwoman Whitesinger for her support in attending and for delivering the welcome address at the meeting.  He stated that he has been attending planning and regular Chapter Meetings, that he was able to attend the Hopi Wellness Event and reacquaint himself with the Hopi Leadership.  He stated that they have two 5K events remaining, one on Tuesday, July 26 at Homolovi State Park and the final event will be in conjunction with the Navajo County Health Fair on July 28 at the County Complex.  He further stated at both events will start at 5:15 p.m. and that the walk/run event will begin at 6:00 p.m.  He invited all who are able to attend.  He thanked Janelle Linn and her staff, and all those involved in helping support the health and wellness initiated for District Two and stated that next year they would like to have an event in the majority of the communities for District Two.  He talked about the partners that have help promote and will continue to promote the event.  He stated that there will be a special guest on July 28, President Jonathan Nez, will be attending and participating in the event.  He indicated that anyone who would like to set up an information booth is welcome to share with the public what Navajo County has to provide.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Vice-Chairman Peshlakai for the invitation to the Navajo Nation Broadband Initiative Meeting, stated that there were great conversations led by Coconino Supervisor Lena Fowler and that this was the third meeting that they have talked about the impacts that broadband has on the Navajo Nation.  She reported on attending the Real AZ Meeting that is Chaired by Supervisor Whiting , where they talk about initiatives to support business growth and opportunities within the region.  She talked about the discussion on the broadband grant that Navajo County was awarded, what it means for the County and stated that they are excited about the grant and the support it will provide to our region for education, health initiatives and public safety.  She addressed the support that was received from individuals who wrote the grant, the letters from the cities, towns and tribal communities and stated that they are grateful for the support with the grant to help get Navajo County to this point.

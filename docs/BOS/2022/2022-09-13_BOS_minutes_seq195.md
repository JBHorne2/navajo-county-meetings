BOARD OF EQUALIZATION :

a.

Discussion and action regarding Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation of APN 409-26-010J (Edwards Revocable Trust - James G. Edwards Trustee); Swearing in of Assessor, Appraisers and Petitioners :  Nathan Conley, appearing via Zoom, and Cammy Darris were sworn in by Clerk of the Board, Melissa Buckley.  Mr. Conley stated that the subject property is 5300 acres of pastureland with a long history of agriculture use and that it lost the agriculture designation last year due to previous rancher that leased the property having passed away.  He indicated that the owner entered into a new lease with Timberline Cattle Company, that the petitioners have provided the agriculture application, copies of the lease on the property as well as the additional information that was requested by Assessor's Office, including copies of their USDA forest leases and a few brand certificates.  He stated that Timberline Cattle Company is one of the oldest, largest, and most established ranches in Arizona, that one of the brand certificates has a brand number of 16 meaning that it was the 16 th brand certificate issued by the Department of Agriculture and that it was issued prior to Arizona becoming a State.  He indicated that they have met all of the requirements for over one hundred years.  He further indicated that he provided some additional information on the property showing that it is fenced and has a water retention tank that is full.  He stated that the Assessor requested a statement indicating that the property won't be used for own personal use, which was provided, stating that the property is leased to Timberline Cattle for their sole and exclusive use.  He further stated that there is a letter from Mr. Terry Dobson regarding the intended use of property.  He indicated that the property is also located close to Timberlines Cattles main ranching operation.
Cammy Darris stated that the property is 52.94 acres and has a 2480 square foot single family dwelling on it and a detached garage.  She stated that the full cash value was determined to be $1,054,733, which is based on the sale of similar properties.  She stated that the reason for lost agriculture is not just because the property sold, she indicated that when a property is sold the agriculture classification is taken away and the new owner has to reapply.  She further stated that this property sold in September 2021 for $1,125,000, that they owner was sent notice that the classification was removed, that they received a few calls inquiring about the previous holders agriculture information but that it was not provided due to being confidential.  She indicated that they received the petition in March 2022, that their office requested additional paperwork but that it was not provided so the petition was denied.  She indicated that the statute states that the land has to be used primarily as agricultural land and that it has to have a reasonable expectation of profit.  She addressed the paperwork that is required and what they have received.  She stated that they walked the property, up and down the fence line, and saw no evidence of cattle.  She talked about the lease amount compared to the purchase amount.  She stated that it appears they are trying to get around a fair valuation result in a much lower tax by getting the agricultural.  She stated that they are requesting about an eight percent reduction.  She requested that the property remain non-agricultural with the full cash value remaining at $1,054,733 and a limited value of $646,179.
Supervisor Seymore inquired what the valuation was when it was agricultural and what they previous owner did when he had the property.
Ms. Darris stated that she didn't have that information for the value and that the previous owner was part of a family ranch.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to deny the Petition for Review of Real Property Valuation of APN 409-26-010J (Edwards Revocable Trust - James G. Edwards Trustee).

Ms. Conley requested rebuttal to the Assessors presentation.  He stated that the  property has had an agriculture designation for many years.  He talked about previous uses of the property and indicated that the house is not habitable.  He stated that Timberline just took over lease and that they will be moving cattle to the property later this year.  He talked about the agriculture structures that are located on the property.  He talked about the Dobson family being a well-established family.
Supervisor Seymore indicated that the property sold for $1.1 million, that they failed to show agriculture use at this time but that it doesn't mean they can't come back and apply again.
Mr. Conley stated that it's a new lease, that cattle ranchers move the cattle around regularly based on weather and season and that they have plans on moving cattle to the property in October.
Supervisor Seymore stated that they could come back once property in use and that just having the lease doesn't satisfy the requirement.  He stated that until use of the property is proven they can't approve and petition.
A discussion was held regarding the statutes related to this issue.
Brandt Clark addressed.
Jason Moore addressed the Board regarding the statute.

PUBLIC HEARING : Consideration of a Liquor License from Chantel Marie Masi for Pine Meadows Country Club located at 2209 Country Club Drive, Overgaard, AZ. :  Leah Thomas addressed the item, stating that Planning and Zoning and the Treasurer and indicated that the application meets the Departments requirements, that the Sheriff indicated that they don't have a problem with the application and that the Health Department has indicated that the applicant is not in compliance with the Departments requirements at this time.  She stated that she has attempted to contact the applicant and has not been successful in reaching her.  She stated that staff would ask that the Board consider the Department recommendations when making a recommendation.

Chairwoman Whitesinger opened the meeting for public comments.  There being no comments, Chairwoman Whitesinger closed the public hearing.
Supervisor Seymore inquired of the County Attorney if they need to be in compliance with the Health ordinance to get a license.
Brandt Clark indicated that he is aware if they have to be in compliance.
Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired as to the duration for trying to contact the applicant.
Ms. Thomas stated that the application was received in June, that it was originally scheduled to go before the Board in August, that she attempted to contact the applicant in July and right before the first August meeting the applicant did contact her and request an extension of the applicant going before the Board.  She further stated that over the past couple of weeks she has tried to get in contact with the applicant and that it has been unsuccessful.
Chairwoman Whitesinger inquired the motion was to die what would be the process for the applicant to get approved in the future.
Ms. Thomas stated that the applicant would have to reapply for the license if it was not approved by the Department of Liquor.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve a liquor license from Chantel Marie Masi for Pine Meadows Country Club located at 2209 Country Club Drive, Overgaard, AZ.
Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that the motion dies for lack of a second and inquired if there is another motion.
Supervisor Whiting inquired what would happen if they were to table the applicant to allow the applicant to work with the Health Department.
Ms. Thomas stated that the Department of Liquor contacted her and indicated that they are ready to move forward and that they are waiting for the Board's recommendation.

Supervisor Whiting made a motion  to deny a Liquor License from Chantel Marie Masi for Pine Meadows Country Club located at 2209 Country Club Drive, Overgaard, AZ, motion seconded by  Supervisor Seymore .  Motion passed 4-0 with Supervisor Benally being absent for the vote.

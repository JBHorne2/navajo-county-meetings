SHERIFF'S OFFICE :

a.

Presentation by Sheriff Clouse to Mr. Jamie Stuck for life saving actions taken on July 18th, 2022 :  Sheriff David Clouse talked about the life saving actions that Jamie Stuck took.  Sheriff Clouse introduced all those who responded to the incident.
Chief Savage addressed the Board regarding Mr. Stuck and thanked him for what he did.
Ms. Carol Michaels thanked Mr. Stuck for his actions in saving her life.
Mr. Stuck talked about the need for people to learn CPR so that they will be able to help in situations such as this.
Sheriff Clouse stated that the Sheriff's Office will be joining with Timber Mesa for a PSA regarding CPR classes and how to do CPR.
Sheriff Clouse presented Mr. Stuck with a certificate and a plaque in recognition of his life saving actions.
Supervisor Whiting thanked Mr. Stuck for his actions and for sharing his message.
Supervisor Benally thanked Mr. Stuck for his actions and stated that it was very heroic.
Chairwoman Whitesinger thanked Mr. Stuck and talked about his example.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :

a.

Presentation to Retiring Public Works Director, John Osgood : Eric Scott addressed the Board regarding John Osgood, indicating that he has done a great job in structuring the Public Works Department.  He stated that the employees in the Department being of the consensus that he has been one of the best Public Works Directors and that they are honored to have served under him.  Mr. Scott stated that Mr. Osgood has been with Navajo County for four and a half years and has been in public service for over thirty-five years.Glenn Kephart addressed the Board regarding Mr. Osgood and talked about the opportunity he had to celebrate with Mr. Osgood when he received the Gabe Zimmerman award.  He talked about the outstanding person that Mr. Osgood is.
Jeanine Carruthers thanked Mr. Osgood for his support throughout the years.  Bill Bess shared memories of working with Mr. Osgood.
Jesse Thompson shared some memories about Mr. Osgood and thanked him for all of the work that he has done to help the County and the Communities within Navajo County.
Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Osgood for his service and dedication.
Supervisor Benally thanked Mr. Osgood for the work that he has done.
Supervisor Whiting stated that he enjoyed working with Mr. Osgood.  He talked about his sincerity for the work he did, the people who worked with and for the citizens.  He wished him the best in his retirement.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai stated that it has been an honor and privilege to work with Mr. Osgood.
Chairwoman Whitesinger stated that they have been honored to work with Mr. Osgood.
The Board presented Mr. Osgood with a clock and a Pendleton blanket in recognition of his service.
Vice-Chairman Peshlakai talked about the tradition of presenting Pendleton blankets to those who retired and stated that they are used to provide protection, comfort and security.
Former Supervisor Thompson presented Mr. Osgood with a gift.
A presentation was shown.
Mr. Osgood thanked the Board, Glenn Kephart and the Public Works Team.  He talked about his time with Navajo County and indicated that he cherishes what he has learned.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion  to adopt the Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Property Tax Rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts, motion seconded by  Vice-Chairman Peshlakai .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

WHITE MOUNTAIN LAKES COUNTY RECREATION IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Quarterly Report and Update :  Mark Honberger addressed the item, reporting on visitor attendance at the lake during the holidays, the Community Meeting, and provided a water level update.  He talked about an algae bloom in the community but indicated that it has not affected the basic lake water and that the lake and beaches are open and that they do warn if there is algae present not to go in the water.  He reported on kayak rentals, concession sales, their Facebook page, the activities provided at Carr Circle and the Newsletters.  He addressed the Community Feedback that they have received and how the public can contact them.

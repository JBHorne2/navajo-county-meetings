NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS'
EMERGENCY MEETING MINUTES
Tuesday, May 5, 2022

Present:
Fern Benally, Supervisor District I; Alberto Peshlakai, Vice-Chairman; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District VI; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Chairwoman, all appearing telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, appearing telephonically; Catrina Jenkins, Emergency Management Manager; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board

10:34 a.m . Call To Order:    Chairwoman Dawnafe Whitesinger  called the meeting to order.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration of a Declaration of Emergency due to wildfire conditions and enacting Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Restrictions :  Catrina Jenkins stated that the rest of the region has entered into fire restrictions due to the weather conditions.  She addressed the current outlook, stating that there is a potential for critical wildfire danger and possible red flag conditions.  She requested that the Board consider the Declaration of Emergency that would allow Stage One restriction throughout Navajo County and the unincorporated areas off tribal land.  She stated that the City of Show Low and the Town of Pinetop-Lakeside have also enacted restrictions, along with the State, BLM and the Federal Forests.   She further stated that Ft. Apache and the White Mountain Apache Tribe are in year round restrictions.
Ms. Jenkins read the proposed Declaration of Emergency.

Vice-Chairman Peshlakai made a motion  to approve the Declaration of Emergency due to wildfire conditions and enacting Ordinance 01-19 Emergency Restrictions, motion seconded by  Supervisor Benally .  The motion was approved 4-0 with  Supervisor Jason Whiting  being absent.

ADJOURN : 10:40 a.m. meeting adjourned.

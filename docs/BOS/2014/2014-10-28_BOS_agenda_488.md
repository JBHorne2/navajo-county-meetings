NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, October 28, 2014 
 
PRESENT: Jason E. Whiting, Supervisor; Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; 
David Tenney, Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present.  
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Nez.  
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Administering of the Oath of Off ice to Supervisor Jason E. Whiting : Judge 
Michala Ruechel administered the Oath of Office to Jason E. Whiting. Supervisor Whiting addressed the 
public and thanked the Board of Supervisors for giving him the opportunity to serve as a Board of Supervisor, 
District III . He spoke about his family  who has lived  in this area for many generations. He expressed 
appreciation to his parents, wife and family for all their support. He indicated he looks forward to working 
with everyone in his new capacity. Supervisor Whiting introduced his family and thanked everyone for being 
there. Chairman Thompson spoke of the process that it took to get to this point and indicated that there were 
many good people that applied for this position. Chairman Thompson welcomed Supervisor Whiting to 
the Board.    
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Karen MacKean addressed the Board regarding the voting act and stated that the 
Recorder has continued to have early voting sites on the  tribal reservations. Ms. M acKean spoke of the  
Kirkpatrick campaign’s solicitation of votes. She indicated that no other voting sites were set up in other parts 
of Navajo County. Ms. MacKean stated she felt this was unconstitutional. She asked the Board to put a hold 
on these ballots, and to have this issue put on the agenda of a future Board of Supervisor’s meeting. Chairman 
Thompson indicated that our intention and position has always been to  get people to vote. Steven Slaton 
addressed the Board  and indicated he is a member of Americans for Prosperity. He expressed concerns 
regarding early voting on the reservation. He stated  that he felt this is in violation of the  United States  
Constitution. Mr. Slaton reported that the Early Ballots that are sent out, need to be checked more carefully 
for those individuals who no  longer reside in the County. Mr. Slaton  indicated he was concerned about the 
assistance given to individuals who cannot read at the polls. He said he felt many individuals were being told 
and influenced on how to vote. Terry Hill addressed the Board regarding Veterans Day on November 11th. He 
indicated at 10:00 a.m. in Show low, VFW Post 9907 will be conducting a Veteran’s Day Ceremony at the 
Veteran’s Memorial Monument by the l ibrary. Mr. Hill welcomed all to attend. Mr. Hill indicated the Elks 
Lodge 209, the VFW Post 9907, and the American Legion Post 76, all of Show Low, will be holding the 25th 
Annual Veterans Dinner on November 8th. He stated all veterans of the county are invited to the dinner, which 
will begin at 6 p.m. at the Elks building on Whipple in Show Low. Pat Sarcoz addressed the Board regarding 
the White Mountain Lakes Recreation Improvement District. She spoke about her concerns and situations of 
the Lake P atrol, and the rules and regulations regarding the use the lake. Mrs. Sarcoz  stated she owns 25 
properties in the  White Mountain Lake s District. She reported herself, many of the renters, and property 
owners have been denied access to the lake, if they are late on their taxes. Mrs. Sarcoz requested the Board to 
look into her concerns of mistreatment , for herself and many other individuals who are tenants. Chairman 
Thompson asked that the staff speak with Mrs. Sarcoz . Norma Chestnut thanked the Board of Supervisors 
for allowing her the chance to address issues with the White Mountain Lakes Recreation Improvement District 
regarding deeded property owner’s denial of the usage of the White Mountain Lake privileges; specifically 
with the Advisory Chairman of  the Board, the Lake Patrol, and the members of the Board.  Mrs. Chestnut 
indicated a  formal complaint letter dated 7/14/ 2014, was sent to various Navajo County departments , 
including the Navajo County Attorney’s office, which was signed as received, by the various offices, by return

      WORKFORCE INVESTMENT ACT : Consideration and possible appro val of a merger with Gila County : 
Susan Tegmeyer addressed the Board regarding the process of the merger with Gila County, with the Navajo and 
Apache County Workforce Partnership.  Ms. Tegmeyer indicated the State has been looking to merge 
organizations and to consolidate programs. She stated this merger is beneficial for the counties who share similar 
needs and demographics  to share resources and administrative expenses . She reported the State has provided 
$50,000.00 to assist with the merger, and there will be no additional cost to the County.  She indicated Gila 
County has a great program  and the sharing of ideas and “best practices” is good in terms of growth  for the 
program, in doing a better job of serving our clients. Ms. Tegmeyer indicated she looks forward to working with 
Gila County as their goals, mission, and team mesh with our vision. James Jayne, County Manager thanked Mrs.

FINANCE:  
a. Notice of CDBG Funding Cycle: Lynda Young addressed the Board and gave a review of the Fiscal Year 
2015-2016 Arizona Department of Housing , Community Development Block Grant program funding 
cycle. She reported Navajo County will receive approximately $370,000.00 this funding cycle. Ms. Young 
reported that Navajo County will be applying for thes e funds, which will be used to benefit low income 
persons in the areas, eliminate slum blight , or ad dress an urgent need in the communities . Ms. Young 
indicated there will be two public hearings , to gather public input  on the use of the  Community 
Development Block Grant funding for specific projects. She stated this hearings will be held on Monday, 
November 3rd. She stated one hearing will be held in Show Low,  at the Frontier Conference room from 
3:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. , and the other hea ring will be at the Navaj o County Complex in Holbrook from 
6:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. p.m. Ms. Young indicated the staff will present the suggested projects to the Board 
of Supervisors for approval , after which the staff will work with  the Northern Arizona Council of 
Governments directly for their assistance. Ms. Young stated the projects decided upon will then be brought 
to the Arizona D epartment of Housing for approval, after which the process will be started. Ms. Young 
reported that four CDBG pro jects have b een completed  within the 2014 Fiscal Y ear, for the Senior 
Centers. She indicated they are finishing the Snowflake Academy project, with the use of CDBG funds. 
b. County Finance Update : James Menlove presented the first  budget update  for Fiscal Y ear 2015 . Mr. 
Menlove stated the County is holding its expenses. He indicated that the bottom line projection is early in 
the year and the County is conservative  at this point. Mr. Menlove stated he hopes to be in the black by 
the end of the year. Chairman Thompson thanked Mr. Menlove  for his efforts  and indicated that w e 
continue to look for additional revenues. Supervisor Tenney indicated the County usually projects lower 
than the State. Supervisor Tenney stated we are trying to find ways to fill the gap  with other revenue 
sources.

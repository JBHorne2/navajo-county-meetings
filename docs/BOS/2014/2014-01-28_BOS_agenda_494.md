ADMINISTRATION: Consideration and possible approval of Resolution Number 03-14, requesting Congress 
to immediately provide full funding for the Payment -In-Lieu-of-Taxes program: Hunter Moore presented the 
item. Mr. Moore indicated the resolution is seeking congress to provide full funding to the PILT program for the 
Fiscal Year 2014.  Mr. Moore stated the los s of PILT would mean over $1.4 million  to Navajo County in the 
current Fiscal Year . Hunter Moore stated he and Supervisor Tenney  met with Senator McCain  and other 
legislators to help restore PILT and to include a renewal of the PILT program for FY 2014 in the final Farm Bill 
conference report and to advocate for sustainable funding in the future. Mr. Moore indicated t he farm bill must 
be passed by the Hou se and the Senate and he stated we need to continue lobb ying for this in years to come.  
Supervisor Allen stated she appreciates the efforts’ of the County  to help restore PILT funding . Supervisor 
Allen made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 03 -14, requesting Congress to immediately provide full 
funding for the Payment-In-Lieu-of-Taxes program; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, September 30, 2014 **SPECIAL MEETING** 
PRESENT: Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Chairman 
were present in person. Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor was present telephonically.  
9:00 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Whitesinger.  
      CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Supervisor Tenney  thanked everyone for coming. Supervisor Tenney made a 
motion to table the call to the public; motion second by Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion. 
      BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Consideration and possible approval of the selection process for the Navajo 
County District III Board of Supervisor vacancy : Chairman Thompson discussed the item and thanked all of 
the persons who have submitted letters of interest in becoming a Superviso r of District III. Supervisor Tenney 
introduced the selection process for the Navajo County District III Board of S upervisor vacancy. He noted the 
selection process has been approved by the County Attorney’s office. Supervisor Tenney indicated there were 
20 applications submitted for the position, with 19 applicants meeting the qualifications for the selection process. 
He noted that by statue, all applicants must be a registered Republican as required.  Supervisor Tenney asked 
Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the B oard to pass out a copy of the proposed timeline s and proposed essay questions 
along with a list of all qua lified candidates. Supervisor Tenney reviewed the proposed t imeline in detail. 
Supervisor Tenney proposed that the candidates submit answers to the series of essay questions for the B oard 
members to review and select the top five candidates. Supervisor Tenney stated those candidates with the most 
interest will be notified for an interview with the Board. He indicated that in the event there is a tie between two 
or more candidates, this will expand the total of candidates in the pool , who will be interviewed. The interviews 
will be held following the regularly scheduled Board of Supervisors meeting on October 14, 2014. Once the 
interviews are completed, the Board will then deliberate the candidates and determine the appropriate course of 
action. Chairman Thompson  indicated if there are any  questions, please contact the Clerk of the Board. 
Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion  to approve the selection proc ess for the Navajo County D istrict III 
Board of Supervisor vacancy; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
ADJOURN: At 9:17 a.m.  Supervisor Tenney  made a motion to adjourn ; motion seconded by  Supervisor 
Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
APPROVED:       DATED: October 14, 2014 
 
Jesse Thompson, Chairman

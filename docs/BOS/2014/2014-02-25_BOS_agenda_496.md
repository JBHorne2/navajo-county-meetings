FINANCE:  
a. County Finance Update : James Menlove discussed the budget and stated at the end of this  Fiscal Year 
we will be in the black over 613,000  dollars. He indicated the b udget challenges for the FY15 are the 
uncertainty of PILT funding and the loss of the inmate housing contracts in our jail and other bills at the 
State Capitol which will impact our county. Supervisor Allen and Supervisor Tenney expressed that it 
is important to lobby for PILT funds in the future. 
b. Consideration and possible approval to fund  the Employee Annual Leave Sell -Back Programs A & B : 
Paige Peterson discussed the item. County Manager James Jayne discussed the item further indicating 
that this is at the employee’s discretion  and answered questions of the B oard. Supervisor Allen asked 
how much is this going to cost the county. Mrs. Peterson indicated for  every employee that is eligible; 
Option A will cost approximately $55,777, of that approximately $34,247 will be paid from the general 
fund.  The first half of Option B will cost approximately $164,389 of that approximately $97,726 will be 
paid from the general fund.  At the extent possible, the sell back will be paid for with the employ ee’s 
current funding source. Mrs. Peterson indicated we  will work with departments to ensure they don’t  
exceed budget capacity. Supervisor Tenney asked if this is the item that was brought before the Board 
in October. Mr. Jayne indicated that the Annual Leave Sell -Back Program was approved at the October 
Board of Supervisor s meeting with the e xpectation of the County being able to fund thi s.  He reported 
we now have the authorization to fund plans A & B. Mr. Jayne stated that this is a partnership with the 
employees to buy down their excess leave over a two year period. He indicated this will limit the payout 
in the future  and will also limit the future liability of the county when an  employee separates service 
from the county either though leaving or retirement. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve to 
fund the Employee Annual Leave Sell -Back Programs A  & B; motion seconded by Supervisor Allen. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.   James Menlove clarified that employees must qualify for either 
A or B and that the cost to the county has been included in the budget as reported in his update.

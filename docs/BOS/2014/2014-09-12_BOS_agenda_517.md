NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ ACTION REPORT 
Friday, September 12, 2014 **SPECIAL MEETING**

between Navajo County acting as fiscal agent for the Eastern AZ Counties Organization and the Apache -
Sitgreaves National Forest for the treatment of 726 acres which burned during the Wallow fire in the Big  
Lake area, and the supply of approximately 17,000 tons of biomass to the White  Mountains wood industry

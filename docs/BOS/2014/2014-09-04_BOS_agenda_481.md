NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Thursday, September 4, 2014  
**SPECIAL MEETING ** 
 
PRESENT: Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Sylvia Allen, Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present 
in person. Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; and David Tenney, Supervisor were present telephonically.   
9:06 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Thompson.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments 
      ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the Primary Electi on conducted 
on August 26, 2014 : Johnathan Roes reviewed  the results of the primary election. Mr. Roes indicated that  the 
voter turnout was higher than in previous elections.  Mr. Roes indicated this was the first time we utilized our E-
poll books. Supervisor Allen made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the Primary Election conducted 
on August 26, 2014 ; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motio n. Chairman 
Thompson expressed appreciation to Laurie Justman and staff. 
      ADJOURN: At 9:10 a.m. Supervisor Nez made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by  Supervisor Allen. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
APPROVED:       DATED: September 9, 2014 
 
Jesse Thompson, Chairman 
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

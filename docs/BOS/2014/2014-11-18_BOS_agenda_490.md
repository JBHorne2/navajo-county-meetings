NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MINUTES 
Tuesday, November 18, 2014 
**SPECIAL MEETING ** 
 
PRESENT:  Jesse Thompson, Chairman was present. Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor 
and Jason E. Whiting, Supervisor were present telephonically. Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor was absent. 
9:10 a.m. Call To Order: Chairman Thompson called the meeting to order. 
ELECTIONS: Consideration and possible approval of the Official Canvass of the November 4, 2014 General 
Election: Johnathan Roes, Election Director discussed the item. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve 
the Canvass of the November 4, 2014 General Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous 
approving the motion. 
ADJOURN: At 9:42 a.m. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
 
APPROVED: 
 
David Tenney, Chairman 
 
 
ATTEST: 
 
Melissa W. Buckley, Clerk of the Board 
 
 
 
DATE: January 13, 2015

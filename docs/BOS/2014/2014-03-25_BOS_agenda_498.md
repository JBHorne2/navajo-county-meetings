      EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT : Consideration and possible approval of the repeal of the Outdoor Fire 
Ordinance Number 01-13 and possible adoption of a new Outdoor Fire Ordinance, Ordinance Number 01-14: 
Mary Springer introduced Jim Zornes , Forest Supervisor of Apache Sitgreave s National Forest. Mr. Zornes  
addressed the Board regarding fire conditions. He indicated that it is very dry and that there will be spring winds 
which is a concern to the forest service. Mr. Zornes indicated that if there is not any more measurable precipitation 
that there may be restrictions prior to Memorial Day. Mrs. Springer discussed the item and answered qu estions 
of the Board. Supervisor Allen  and Supervisor Tenney  thanked the staff for their efforts and expressed 
appreciation to Jim Zornes.  Supervisor Tenney  made a motion  to approve the repeal of the Outdoor Fire 
Ordinance Number 01-13 and possible adoption of a new Outdoor Fire Ordinance, Ordinance Number 01-14; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Allen. Vote unanimous approving the  motion. Kirk Webb, Fire Marshall with 
the Lakeside Fire Department made comments. Mr. Webb indicated that March 30 through April 5 is Southwest 
Wildfire Awareness Week.

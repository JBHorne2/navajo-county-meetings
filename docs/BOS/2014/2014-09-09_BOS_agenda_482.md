NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Tuesday, September 9, 2014  
PRESENT: Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and 
Jesse Thompson, Chairman were present.  
9:00 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Tenney.  
Chairman Thompson indicated that there has been a fire that has hit the Chapters of Teesto and Dilkon. He 
requested that item 2c be moved to the end of the meeting.  
 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC : George Campeau addressed the Board regarding  safety issues of public vehicle’s 
using an easement road, above highway 260.  Seven property owners live on this easement  and are all having a 
problem with people speeding on this road and trying to avoid the DUI check points.  This has caused the traffic 
to increase between 1 and 2 AM due to the DUI checkpoints.  We are asking Navajo County accept the road as a 
County road and prepare it for high traffic and pedestrian traffic.  We as property owners are financially 
responsible for this and we cannot  be financially responsible for the public. Mr. Campeau asked the road b e 
deemed as a county road or dee m it as a private road for property owners and utility easement  access only. 
Chairman Thompson asked that he speak with Homero Vela, Assistant County Manager/Public Works Director. 
Supervisor Whitesinger stated her appreciation for Mr. Campeau bringing this issue to the Counties attention 
and the board will spend some time investigating the situation and prepare a response to the property owners.  
Tom Poscharsky addressed the Board of Supervisors, on behalf of Snowflake and Taylor and thanked Supervisor 
Allen for her service to Navajo County. He indicated she was able to get work done for the Snowflake Academy 
and other projects that were important to the communities . Wade Carlisle thanked Supervisor Allen  for her 
service to District III and the City of Holbrook as well as NACOG.

b. Consideration and adoption of a procedure for filling the vacanc y for Supervisor in District III : Brad 
Carlyon, County Attorney  discussed Title 11 of State S tatute on the required qualifications to fill th e 
District III open seat. He explained in A.R.S. 16-230 notwithstanding any other statute, an appointment is 
made from the same political affiliation. Mr. Carlyon put together some suggestions to widely seminate a 
good group of qualified people.  Requirements are the applicants submit a resume and a letter of interest 
to include meeting the qualifications of the office.  A notice soliciting applications from eligible residents 
of District III will be published as soon as possible in the White Mountain Independent and Holbrook 
Tribune News to fill the vacancy.  
Mr. Carlyon suggested notifying Political Party Chairpersons however it will not be necessary to notify 
the Democratic Party Chairman. He also suggested setting a special meeting towards the end of the month 
to review the applications to appoint or take further action. The procedure was outlined by Brad Carlyon. 
Vice Chairman Tenney asked for a Clarification of A.R.S. 16-230 (A) (2), about the timing of the vacancy 
occurring within the first 2 years of term.  Mr. Carlyon clarified that due to the timing of the vacancy,

there is not time to meet the nomination requirements as a write in candidate for the upcoming November 
elections.  Vice Chairman Tenney asked about item #5 of the procedures.  Mr. Carlyon explained that all

30 to either decide on a candidate or outline a process for selecting a candidate.  An agenda item will be 
drafted to give broad discretion to discuss appointment or if you need to adopt a selection proces s.  The

FACILITIES MANAGEMENT : Presentation on allocation of building space : Bret Starns gave a brief 
presentation of the allocation of 23000 sq. ft. of building space. He indicated that the old public works building 
will now be allocated to Probation along with High Desert Health Care; the old Auto shop will be allocated to the 
NCSO for impound and evidence storage. The old Holbrook Road yard at McClaw’s Road will be used for storage 
of paper goods for Facilities Management and storage for the Library District, IT will also have some room there 
and County Auction will be stored within this facility. He spoke of the moves in the Library District to McLaws 
Rd facility.  The downtown office where the Justice Court is currently will be allocated  to the Clerk of Superior 
Court; Mr. Starns spoke of the County Complex Basement where cooperative extension currently resides will be 
made into a Grand Jury room; a portion of the old Probation office will be demolished and the remainder of the 
building for cooperative extension.  Mr. Jayne thanked Bret Starns and Jason Owens for putting this presentation 
together.  Mr. Jayne indicated that the adult probation department across the parking lot is a challenge.  Mr. Starns 
indicated that this will be done in six months. Supervisor Nez recalled an agreement that the County has with the 
City of Show Low for a g ym usage and asked about a wellness center for Central County. Mr. Starns indicated 
that there are 1200 square feet that is allocated for a wellness cent er. Mr. Jayne thanked Mr. Starns for his 
leadership. Mr. Jayne indicated that the Board recently approved the agreement with the City of Show Low and 
indicated that there will be a design for a gym for Central County. The two restrooms will be redone and m ade 
into showers.  At the racquetball court located on the main County complex; those bathrooms will be renovated 
as well.

e. Public Hearing: Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 20-14, approving a Special 
Use Permit for AT&T to construct a 190 ft. Cellular Tower on APN 109-23-007: Trent Larson discussed 
the item. P&Z Commission recommended approval and staff recommends approval. Mr. Larson spoke of 
the conditions of the Special Use Permit. A recommendation that came from the P&Z Commission is that 
the tower be lit, however FAA guidelines do not require it to be lit. Mr. Larson reviewed the pros and cons 
to the tower being lit. Staff does not require the tower to be lit and there being no comments, closed the 
public hearing.  AT&T representative, G arett Jonilonis addressed the Board regarding the Special Use 
Permit.  
 Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 20-14, approving a Special Use Permit 
for AT&T to construct a 190 ft. Cellular Tower on APN 109-23-007, with no condition of lighting; motion 
seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
Friday, September 12, 2014 **SPECIAL MEETING** 
 
PRESENT: Jonathan M. Nez, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and Jesse Thompson, Chairman were 
present in person. Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor was present telephonically.  
At 12:00 p.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Tenney.    
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS: Discussion and Possible Action re: a one-year Stewardship Agreement between 
Navajo County acting as fiscal agent for the Eastern AZ Counties Organization and the Apache -Sitgreaves 
National Forest for the treatment of 726 acres which burned during the Wallow fire in the Big Lake area, and the 
supply of approximately 17,000 tons of biomass to the White  Mountains wood industry : Supervisor Tenney 
discussed touring the San Juan Fire with the 4FRI members and Hunter Moore. Supervisor Tenney reported they 
spoke with the Forest Supervisor, Mr. Jim. Zornes, who indicated that there is money allotted  for a biomass 
project. He proposed applying for the funds , as Navajo County is the fiscal agent  for ECO. Pascal Berlioux, 
Executive Director of the Eastern Arizona Counties Organization, discussed the item and indicated that ECO has 
conceptually approved the agreement. Mr. Berlioux indicated the Stewardship Agreement with the Forest Service 
will need to be done by Tuesday, September 16th. Mr. Belioux stated there is a draft agreement at this time, which 
will be completed by September 16 th. Mr. Berlioux asked that James Jayne, County Manager execute this  
agreement. Supervisor Nez made a motion to approve a one -year Stewardship Agreement between Navajo 
County acting as fiscal  agent for the Eastern AZ Counties Organization and the Apache -Sitgreaves National 
Forest for the treatment of 726 acres which burned during the Wallow fire in the Big Lake area, and the supply 
of approximately 17,000 tons of biomass to the White Mountains wood industry; motion seconded by Chairman 
Thompson. Vote u nanimous approving the motion , with the review of James Jayne, County Manager, Brad  
Carlyon, County Attorney and James Menlove, Finance Director.  
ADJOURN: At 12:12 p.m. Chairman Thompson made a motion to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
APPROVED:       DATED: October 14, 2014 
 
Jesse Thompson, Chairman

b. Consideration and review of the p roposed Lakeside Fire District, Linden Fire District and Show Low 
Fire District Merger: Supervisor Tenney addressed the merger of the Lakeside, Linden and Show Low 
Fire Districts. He indicated there has been an interest in this merger for many years and they are working 
towards its completion. Supervisor Tenney indicated the merger will benefit the citizens and taxpayers 
within the districts with a better fire service and lower tax rates . He indicated the B oard has received 
notification from the  fire districts to move forward with the merger  pursuant to ARS §48-820. 
Supervisor Tenney stated the Board is here to review and offer written comments on the proposed 
merger, and to act formally on this issue. Chief Savage indicated this discussion is to notify the Board of 
Supervisors of the merger for their review and written comments . He indicated that this has been  a 
cooperative effort  between the fire districts. Chief Savage stated this has been 10 to 12 years in the 
making to bring forth a more effective and efficient fire service.  Chief Savage discussed  the scheduled 
times for the Public Hearings for Show Low, Lakeside and Linden. Supervisor Whitesinger expressed 
appreciation to Chief Savage for his efforts in the  process and the acceptance of the merger. Supervisor 
Whitesinger indicated this  merger will better serve the public and support our communities to save 
money, by serving as one function working together. Chairman Thompson indicated that the policies 
and state laws have been followed in regards to the merger. Supervisor Tenney spoke in support of the 
item and expressed appreciation to Chief Anderson of Linden Fire District . He indicated this merger 
shares the same concerns for all the people of the areas and thanked the districts f or moving forward 
with good faith for all concerned in  Linden, Show Low and Lakeside. Supervisor Allen indicated that 
the consolidation of the districts and the r eduction in taxes is a great thing  for the three different 
communities. Chief Savage spoke of the organization of the merger and the employees who have been 
remarkable in working together to create and an organizational chart that w ill best serve the public 
interest. Chief Savage reported that a fter the public hearing s and a unanimous deci sion to merge, the 
next move is for the various boards to meet to select a new board wi th representation from each board . 
James Jayne spoke from a  county perspective and thanked the fire chiefs and those  in attendance for 
being regional leaders  and their e fforts in the process  to become more efficient. Supervisor Tenney  
proposed that he, Hunter Moore , Government Relations Director  and Jason Moore , Deputy County 
Attorney work together to  prepare and  submit the  written comments  for the upcoming  fire district 
meetings. The Board was in agreement.

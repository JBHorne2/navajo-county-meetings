NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT: Mary Tyler, Interim Director: 
Board of Directors Session: 1) West Nile Virus update:  Mr. Shumway made a motion to enter into a 
Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by Mr . Deal; vote unanimous approving the motion. Ms. 
Tyler reported that to date, there have been 108 positive equine (hor se) cases in Arizona, with 5 in 
Navajo County. There have been 96 positive avian (bird) cases in Arizona, with 5 in Navajo County. 
There were 249 positive mosquitoes in the state, with 10 in Navajo County (3 in Winslow; 1 in Polacca; 
3 in Holbrook; 3 in Woodruff). Of the 387 human cases  (with 10 deaths) reported in the state, there was 
one case (one death) in Navajo County. Ms. Tyler noted  that because horse owners are more alert to the 
symptoms of West Nile Virus, many horses survived the season. No action taken; informational only.

2) Discussion and possible board action re: Approva l of final settlement of Winslow Levee flood 
litigation: Mr. Payette explained that la wsuits were filed against the county fo llowing the floods in 
Winslow in 1993. After the trial, Navajo County was found to be 40% responsible for the damages. Both

ADMINSTRATION: James Menlove, Finance Director: Authoriz ation to enter into a contract to 
purchase new administrative computing software from New World Systems (NWS): Mr. Menlove began 
by explaining that the current soft ware is inadequate for the county’ s needs, and is only available to 
finance staff. It is inaccessible by other departme nts and therefore, duplicate books are being kept. New 
World Systems is designed for government needs and is  state of the art technol ogy with a rich browser-
based interface. Mr. Menlove noted th at it will take 7 months to fu lly implement the system, but NWS 
will help up integrate and train personnel to use the system to the fullest extent possible. Mr. Menlove 
advised that while he did not do a formal Request for Proposal (RFP), he did do an extensive search for 
a vendor who would meet our needs. Mr. Shumway made a motion to enter into an executive session 
for legal advice, pursua nt to ARS § 38-431.03 (A)(3); motion s econded by Mr. Deal; vote unanimous 
approving the motion. Present in th e executive session were: Chairman  J.R. DeSpain; Vice-Chairman 
Jerry Brownlow; Members Percy Deal, Jesse Thom pson, Pete Shumway; Deputy County Attorney 
Lance Payette; County Manager James G. Jayne; Finance Director James Menlove; Accounting 
Supervisor Joan Mickle; Deputy Clerk Kathy Hieb. Mr. Thompson made a motion to return to regular 
session; motion seconded by Mr. Shumway; vote unanimous approving th e motion. Mr. Menlove 
withdrew his proposal and will submit an RFP. Th is matter will be brought back to the Board on 
December 6, 2004. Mr. Deal made a motion to authorize the RFP for administrative computing 
software as presented; motion seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the motion.

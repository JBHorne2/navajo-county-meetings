BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
 
October 14, 2004 
 
Supervisors’ Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona – Time: 9:05 a.m. 
 
PRESENT: J.R. DeSpain, Chairman; Jerry Brownlow, Vice-Chairman; Jesse Thompson, Member; Pete 
Shumway, Member; James G. Jayne, County Manager; Lance Payette, Chief Deputy County Attorney; 
Kathy Hieb, Deputy Clerk of the Board. (Percy Deal absent due to prior commitment) Mr. DeSpain 
led the Pledge of Allegiance and the Invocation.

  No comments were offered.

Superintendent, who passed away on October 11, 2004; 2) Back Tax Land Deeds: Justin Mattea; Walter

items in the Consent Agenda; motion seconded by Mr. Brownlow; vote unanimous approving the

RECORDER/ELECTIONS: Kelly Dastrup, Elections Coordinator: Approval to purchase cassette tape 
players and headsets for use in the Navajo Nation precincts to assist voters with translation services: Ms. 
Dastrup explained that funding for this purchase w ill be reimbursed from a Health & Human Services 
Grant. The purchase will include 30 cassette players and 30 headsets, which will be used for Navajo 
Language tapes for the Navajo Reservation Precincts. Mr. Thompson made a motion  to authorize the 
purchase of cassette players and headsets as pres ented, in the amount of $1074.44; motion seconded by 
Mr. Shumway; vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
PUBLIC WORKS: Dusty Parsons, Deputy Director Highways: Resolution  Approving an exchange of 
portions of the old McLaws Road alignment with the abutting land owners; Authorizing the Chairman to 
execute and deliver Quit Claim Deeds to the exch anged portions; and reserving for the County a public 
utility easement over certain of the exchanged portions : Mr. Parsons advised that McLaws Road was 
realigned two years ago to eliminate a dangerous cu rve. The old alignment is  no longer necessary for 
public road purposes. Mr. Shumway made a motion  to adopt Resolution #104-04, approving an 
exchange of portions of the Old McLaws Road alignment with abutting landowners; authorizing the 
Chairman to execute and deliver Quit Claim Deeds to the exchanged portions ; and reserving unto the 
County a public utility easement over certain of the exchanged portions; motion seconded by Mr. 
Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion. (Mr. DeSpain declared a conflict and did not vote 
as he has a grazing lease on portion of the Aztec Land & Cattle Company property)

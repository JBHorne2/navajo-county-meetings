BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NAVAJO COUNTY, ARIZONA 
 
Special Meeting 
February 9, 2004 
 
Supervisors’ Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona-Time 10:10 a.m. 
 
PRESENT:  Present in Chambers: Chairman J.R. DeSpain; Vice-Chairman Jerry Brownlow; 
Members Jesse Thompson and Pete Shumway; County Attorney Mel Bowers; Deputy Clerk 
Kathy Hieb. Present by conference call: Member Percy Deal. (County Manager Jimmy Jayne 
was excused due to his attendance in the Insurance Task Force meeting) 
 
Mr. Thompson led the Pledge of Allegiance and offered the invocation. 
 
ELECTIONS: Mary Kelley, Elections Director: Canvass of the Presidential Preference 
Election held on February 3, 2004: Ms. Kelley reported that 18.7% of registered voters turned 
out to vote in this election. There were 23,511 registered voters, with 4392 votes cast. The 
results were as follows: Lyndon LaRouche-50 votes; Fern Penna-25 votes; Keith Brand-32 
votes; Huda Mohammad -19 votes; Bill Wyatt -42 votes; Ray Caplette -9 votes; Carol 
Moseley Braun-20 (pulled out of the race prior to election); Al Sharpton-37 votes; Wesley K. 
Clark-1203 votes; Dennis J. Kucinich-36 votes; John Edwards-255 votes; Evelyn L. Vitullo-8 
votes; Dianne Barker-17 votes; Dick Gephardt-27 votes (pulled out of the race prior to the 
election); William Barchilon -20 votes; John F. Kerry-1488 votes; Howard Dean-419 votes; 
Joe Lieberman -661 votes. Mr. Shumway made a motion  to accept the canvass of the 
Presidential Preference Election held on February 3, 2004, and to declare John F. Kerry the 
winner in Navajo County; motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote unanimous approving the 
motion. 
 
BOARD BUSINESS:  There was no business presented. 
 
There being no further business to come before the Board of Supervisors, the meeting was 
adjourned until Tuesday, February 17, 2004 , upon a motion made by Mr. Shumway; 
motion seconded by Mr. Thompson; vote unanimous approving the motion. The meeting 
adjourned at 10:25 a.m.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
*SPECIAL MEETING* MONDAY, May 20, 2013 
 
PRESENT: Sylvia Allen,  Supervisor and Jesse Thompson, Vice -Chairman were present in the Board 
Chambers. Jonathan M. Nez, Chairman and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor were present telephonically.  
9:02 a.m.: Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance led by Supervisor Allen.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments. 
CONSENT AGENDA:  a) Letter to Federal Officials regarding the Kayenta Healthcare Facility : Supervisor 
Nez made a motion to approve the consent agenda as presented ; motion seconded by Supervisor Allen. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT:  
a) Consideration and possible approval of a D eclaration of Emergency and implementation of Ordinance 
01-13 section 7 – Emergency Fire Restrictions: Mary Springer discussed the item and indicated  that the 
current conditions throughout Navajo County  are in high fire danger right now  with the lack of 
significant rainfall . Supervisor Allen  made a motion  to approve the Declaration of Emergency and 
implementation of Ordinance 01 -13 section 7 – Emergency Fire Restrictions ; motion seconded by 
Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
b) Consideration and possible approval of implementing fire restrictions for Zones 1, 2 and 3 in Navajo 
County: Mary Springer asked the Zones 1, 2, and 3 be impl emented with fire restrictions effective May 
23, 2013 at 12:01 a.m. Supervisor Allen made a motion to approve the implementing fire restrictions 
for Zones 1, 2 and 3 in Navajo County; motion seconded by Chairman Nez. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion.  
SHERIFF: Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement with the Department of 
Corrections for housing DOC inmates (Sheriff KC Clark ) Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board indicated the  
DOC Contract has not bee n received from the D epartment of Corrections. She reported that the Sheriff has  
asked that this item be pulled and that a Special Meeting be called when the contract is received for approval.  
TREASURER: Consideration and possible approval of Personal Property Tax abatements per A.R.S. §42 -
19118: Manny Hernandez discussed the item and indicated there are a significant number of personal property 
taxes on the books for mobile homes and equipment that have been determined by the Navajo County Sheriff’s 
Office and the Treasurer department to be uncollectible for various reasons  including the inability to locate 
either the personal property or the property owner . Treasurer Hernandez  then spoke about busin ess personal 
property Chapter 2 and indicated small businesses are not really affected by this however the larger businesses 
are.  Supervisor Allen made a motion to approve the Personal Property Tax abatements per A.R.S. §42-19118; 
motion seconded by Supervisor Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: At 9:22  a.m. Supervisor Allen made a motion  to adjourn; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

WORKFORCE INVESTMENT ACT : Update on the Workforce Investment Act Program in Navajo and 
Apache Counties: Carla Fails and Andrea Harings gave an update on the Workforce Investment Act Program  in 
Navajo and Apache Counties. Ms. Fails indicated the WIA is a federally funded program that is specifically aimed 
at youth, adults and dislocated workers by providing core service such as job search and placement assistance. 
Supervisor Tenney asked about the Summer Youth program and Ms. Fails reported there were 11 youth in the 
program this year. Andrea Harings addressed the Board regarding the summer youth program and their efforts in 
creating meaningful partnerships with regional businesses including economic development organizations, NPC, 
school districts, tribes, municipalities, employee and faith based organizations and the State.   Supervisor 
Thompson indicated that he does report to the economic development program  of Northern Arizona Council of 
Governments (NACOG) and thanked them for their  update. Mr. Jayne thanked Ms. Fails and Ms. Harings  for 
their support with this program and the opportunities that will come forth  to offer more services to the business 
community.

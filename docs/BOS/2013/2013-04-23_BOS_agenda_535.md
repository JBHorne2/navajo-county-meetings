b. Discussion and possible action regarding Petition for Review of Taxpayer Notice of Claim APN 309-46-
001A: Assessor Cammy Darris reviewed the appeal periods and further reviewed the process of a Notice 
of Claim and indicated a Notice of Claim can only be changed as a result of a property tax error. The 
concrete mentioned was a factual error and had been previously addressed by the Assessor. The 
petitioner was unhappy with the increase of the ir last year tax evaluation and was  still appealing the 
valuation which is not a notice of a factual error. Ms. Darris indicated a Notice of Claim cannot be used 
for this and reported the petitioner should have gone through the earlier appeals process . Supervisor 
Allen inquired if Brad Carlyon, County Attorney agreed and he indicated he did concur.  Supervisor 
Tenney made a motio n to deny the  Petition for Review of Taxpayer Notice of Claim APN 309 -46-
001A; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.  
c. Discussion and possible action regarding Petition for Review of Taxpayer Notice of Claim  
M870012901: Assessor Cammy  Darris reported  that this is the same  scenario as the last claim and  
indicated the petitioner cannot file to dispute the valuation under the Notice of Claim which can only be 
used for factual errors. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to deny the Petition for Review of Taxpayer 
Notice of Claim M870012901 ; motion seconded by Supervisor Allen. Vote unanimous approving the 
motion.

g. Public Hearing : Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 12-13, approving a 
Special Use Permit for Apache District Wind Farm, LLC to place a temporary meteorological tower 
with a height not to exceed 200 feet, to measure wind for the potential of developing an alternative 
power (wind) generating facility on a site located approximately 13 miles southwest of Holbrook : 
Chairman Nez  opened the Public Hearing. Trent Larson discussed th e item. He indicated t he land 
owner Steve Brophy with Aztec Land and Cattle Company and developer Eric Simon of Apache District 
Wind Farm were present and available for questions. Eric Simons thanked the Board for allowing him to 
apply for this Special Use Permit and spoke in favor of this item. He indicated the proposed tower would 
give the company more data to determine if the wind resource would be enough for the commercial 
project. Mr. Simons indicated that it takes a minimum of one year however they wo uld like to gather 
more data and has ask ed for a three year period.  Supervisor Allen indicated that she received a letter 
from Itasca Small and then read the letter in regards to  wind towers causing health issues . Mr. Carlyon 
indicated that he disagrees wi th the letter.  Mr. Larson responded that the  Planning and Zoning 
department has looked into these issues previously and that at this point they are just measuring wind 
with a meteorological (MET) tower. Chairman Nez indicated that Planning and Zoning  had considered 
the item and recommended approval. Chairman Ne z closed the Public H earing. Supervisor Allen 
made a motion  to adopt Resolution Number 12 -13, approving a Special Use Permit for Apache 
District Wind Farm, LLC to place a temporary meteorological tower with a height not to exceed 200 
feet, to measure wind for the potential of developing an alternative power (wind) generating facility on a 
site located approximately 13 miles southwest of Holbrook ; motion seconded by  Supervisor Tenney. 
Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Board members expressed their admiration and appreciation to Mr. Jayne.

d. Consideration and possible approval of a proclamation declaring September 17, 2013 Constitution Day :

e. Presentation of Novo Power, LLC: Hunter Moore introduced  Arizona State Senator Bob Worsley and 
his son Brad Worsley who is the President and CEO of Novo Power . Mr. Brad Worsley spoke about the 
acquisition of the former Snowflake Power Plant, the Catalyst Paper Mill  and also the buying of 7,500 
acres surrounding the plant.  He addressed what they are doing with No vo Bio Power. He said the plant 
creates enough megawatts of power to furnish electricity to about 20,000 homes. He spoke of the 
economic impact which includes ; 36 direct employees , it has created 100 jobs indirectly and does 
business with 100 local supplie rs. Senator Worsley answered questions of the Board and indicated he 
feels this is mutually beneficial for Navajo County and NOVO Power.  He reported they spent millions 
of dollars buying these assets and they want to get them back into operation to provide jobs.  
f. Presentation from the President of the American Lands Council: Utah Representative Ken Ivory 
explained the constitutional and legal basis for the transfer of public lands fro m federal to state 
ownership and how that will benefit the states and counties in the west, in their ability to take care of the 
health, safety and welfare of its citizens.  He asked the Board to consider joining the American Lands 
Council to help move the message forward to secure  the rights of  life, liberty and property.  
Representative Ivory answered questions of the Board and invited them  to enter their voice into the 
Legislature process and invited the Board  to attend the Multi-State Strategy Conference on Octobe r 2nd 
in Park City, Utah.

      HUMAN RESOURCES : Consideration and possible approval of Personnel Actions : Kimberly Eavenson  
reviewed the 29 item personnel action report.  Supervisor Thompson asked for more information on item #25. 
Brad Carlyon, County Attorney  indicated that the Board oversees the Human Resources  department. He stated 
the influence of the Board is in setting the budget; however they cannot oversee various employees that work 
for an Elected Official. Mr. Carlyon indicated the Board could not veto another Elected Official’s decision over 
the employees hired or dismissed by them. He suggested the Board could address any concerns or questions to 
that Elected Official.  Mr. Carlyon indicated the sheet they are approving is a clarification of payroll. Chairman 
Nez indicated that there is no direct family relation with the individual even though the last name is the same as 
one of the Board members . Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve the personnel  action report as 
presented; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

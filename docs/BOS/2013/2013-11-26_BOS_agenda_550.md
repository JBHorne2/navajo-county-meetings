NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, November 26, 2013 
 
PRESENT: Sylvia Allen, Supervisor; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and Vice-
Chairman Jesse Thompson were present. Chairman Jonathan M. Nez was not present due to a death in his family.  
9:00 a.m.  Call to Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Tenney.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments.  
CONSENT AGENDA: a) Minutes: October 22, 2013 and November 12, 2013 b) Constable Reports: Holbrook 
Precinct #1, September; Snowflake Precinct #3, October; Show Low Precinct #5, October; and Pinetop Precinct 
#6, October 2013 c) Contract signed by County Manager per Board of Supervisors Resolution: Revised Contract 
for Professional Services between Navajo County and Stantec Consulting Services, Inc.  d) Letter to 
Congresswoman Kirkpatrick regarding Gravel and Road Improvement Project for the Navajo Nation – Red Dog 
Partnership Team e) Sympathy letters to Judge David Widmaier, Juanita Mann, Laura Sanchez , Paul Saiz, and 
the family of Charles Youvella f) Letters to Senator Flake and Senator McCain regarding Senate action to restore 
the health of both our National Forests and the gateway communities adjacent to them g) 2nd Amended Addendum 
to the June 30, 2010 Memorandum of Understanding for Mexican Wolf Conservation and cover letter h) Navajo 
County scoping comments on Removing the Gray Wolf from the list of endangered and threatened wildlife and 
maintain protections for the Mexican Wolf by listing it as endangered; and proposed revision to the nonessential 
experimental population of the Mexican Wolf i) Tribute to former state lawmaker, Mr. Ben Miranda: Supervisor

c. Presentation and consideration of a letter of support for the Cherenkov Telescope Array - Arizona

presentation regarding the Cherenkov Telescope Array. Mr. Hall reported on the project and its benefits

PUBLIC WORKS: Recognize the Public Works team for their contribution in constructing the access road and 
building pad for the Public Works Complex : Homero Vela, Public Works Director/A ssistant County Manager  
gave a presentation on the construction of the New Public Works Complex.  He stated the access road required 
the use of 12,000 cubic yards of material. He stated the build-up material was applied and compacted to meet a 
compaction st andard of 95%. Mr. Vela reported the pad required survey work, material testing, project 
management of course material hauling, application of water and material placement. He reported the team 
completed this work in 4 weeks allowing for the work to proceed as planned on schedule. Homero Vela recognized 
Bill Sturgeon for his work on the construction of the new Public Works Complex access road and building pad. 
Russell McCray, Deputy Director Highways recognized David Clark, Pride Spurlock, Terrance Yazzie, Rodney 
Stump, Sam Whipple, Shelby Beeler, Bill Wilbanks, Martinez Mike, Rick Beeler, Bob Ripley, Bill Barnard, Dan 
Lewis, Brett Schmitt, Wilmer Dickson, Lary Griffin, Wilfred John, Tom Puckett, Chester Robinson, Frank Reed 
and Rick Denton. Bill Bess, County Engineer/Assistant Public Works Director recognized the engineering team 
Jeremiah Loyd, Rick Denton, James DeSpain, Jason Owens, Glenn Hoskins and Dane Hancock.

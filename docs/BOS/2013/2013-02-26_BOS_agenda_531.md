PUBLIC WORKS : Public Hearing : Reconsideration and possible adoption of Resolution Number 07 -13, 
approving a Special Use Permit to include the stipulations presen ted by staff, for Overgaard Compassion Care 
to operate a Medical Marijuana Dispensary in the Heber/Overgaard area : Trent Larson gave a brief presentation 
on the request from Overgaard Compassion Care for a Special Use Permit to operate a Medical Marijuana 
Dispensary in an existing commercial office building located on Hwy 260 in Overgaard.  This is a second 
attempt by the applicant to attain a Special Use Permit.  The Board previously denied the Special Use Permit on 
November 27th, 2012. Mr. Larson stated that the site which the applicant selected for their establishment met all 
State and local requirements and was in compliance with AZDHS security and inspection rules, zoning and site 
location as required in the Zoning Ordinance which was adopted by the Navajo County Board of Supervisors on 
February 22 , 2011 . James Jayne indicated that the Sheriff’s Office does not support the medical marijuana 
policy; however they will follow the law as set forth.  Adam Trenk gave a presentation on behalf of the 
applicant, Overgaard Compassion Care.  The Supervisors asked questions of Mr. Trenk in which he addressed 
their concerns. Chairman Nez opened the meeting for public comment.  In the Public Hearing the following 
spoke against the item; Don Brown, June Call, Jerry Call, Scott McLaws, Amanda McLaws, David E. Weil, 
David Hausmann, Valorie Hausmann, Lorin Porter, and Paul Kulas.  Heather DeCarlo s poke in favor of the 
item. Mr. Trenk spoke of this issue in length and addressed the concerns  of the community and concluded with 
statements regarding the regulations  and the S tate law which  has b een passed.  Chairman Nez thanked the 
applicant a nd the comments from the public, then closed the Public Hearing. The S upervisors discussed the 
issue. Supervisor Tenney made a motion to deny t he adoption of Resolution Number  07-13, approving a 
Special Use Permit to include the stipulations presented by staff, for Overgaard Compassion Care to operate a 
Medical Marijuana Dispensary in the Heber/Overgaard area; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
THURSDAY, February 7, 2013 
*SPECIAL MEETING*  
 
PRESENT: Sylvia Allen, Supervisor was present in person. Jonathan M. Nez, Chairman ; Jesse Thompson, 
Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; and Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor were present telephonically.  
1:33 p.m. Call to Order: Supervisor Allen called the meeting to order. 
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: There were no public comments. 
PUBLIC WORKS: Consideration and possible approval of a revised Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with 
the Navajo Nation and Navajo County for Road Projects : Homero Vela discussed the item  which makes three 
revisions to the original I GA that was approved on December 12, 2012  by the Board of Supervisors .  
Attachment A has been revised to increase the excavating and crushing of red dog material in an amount not to 
exceed $200,000.00. The Navajo Nation Department of Transportation will be reimbursing the county for those 
expenses associated with crushing of the red rock material. By Navajo County working with the vendor as well 
as Navajo Nation DOT trying to get this IGA revised t o increase the quantity of material and dollars, this will 
minimize the costs of the project . By making the decision today  we would avoid a $25,000 mobilization cost. 
Chairman Nez thanked Homero Vela for his partnership with the BIA and Navajo Nation and his assistance in 
this agreement. Chairman Nez made a motion to approve the Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the 
Navajo Nation and Navajo County for Road Projects ; motion seconded by  Supervisor Thompson. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.  
ADJOURN: at 1:38 p.m.  Supervisor Tenney made a motion to adjourn ; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Nez. Vote unanimous approving the motion. 
 
 
APPROVED: 
 
Jonathan M. Nez, Chairman

b. Consideration and possible approval of an agreement with Consult -A-Doc benefit for Navajo County

c. Consideration and  possible approval of revised employee benefits package : Kimberly Eavenson 
reviewed the benefits survey sent out to all employees. She further reviewed the proposed strategy for 
the employee benefits package. Navajo County health care costs are going to increase 6.5% in the next 
fiscal year.  In an effort to try and find a balance and help mitigate the fiscal impact on both the 
employee and the employer, staff is proposing a revision to the current employee bene fits package for 
the FY13-14 plan year that would eliminate the Core Plus, and Co -Pay plans and reduce the employer 
contribution rate to the Health Savings Accounts associated with the High Deductible Plans.  The 
potential cost savings to the em ployer will be approximately $400,000 for FY2013-14. Mr. Jayne spoke 
to the revised employee benefits package which was arrived at with consultation and efforts of  the 
Strategic Planning Team, Personnel Commission and Senior Staff and indicated we will have 
department meetings during the first week of April  with employees regarding the budget and revised 
benefits package. Supervisor Whitesinger expressed appreciation to the staff for their efforts in looking 
out for the best interests of the employees. Mrs. Eavenson indicated open enrollment begins on April 22, 
2013 and will take effect July 1, 2013; however the deduction will come out the month of  June for the 
month of July.   Supervisor Tenney said he recognizes the economic conditions in the state require s 
change and indicated this proposal does a good job of the count y being fiscally responsible while  still 
providing benefits to the employees . Supervisor Tenney made a motion to approve  the revised 
employee benefits package;  motion seconded by  Supervisor Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving 
the motion.

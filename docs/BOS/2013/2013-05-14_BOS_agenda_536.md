  SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS : Consideration and possible approval of an Intergovernmental 
Agreement between the Navajo County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Agency and the Apache 
County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Agency in order to expend Race to the Top funds: 
Superintendent Linda Morrow presented the item and indicated that this  is an IGA that was already approved 
for Coconino County in partnership with the Northeast  Regional Training Center. She reported t wo years ago 
the 15 County Superintendents came together to form a voluntary group of Regional Centers. She indicated 
Navajo County is the  fiscal agent for the Northeast AZ Regional Service Center  with Coconino County and 
Apache County.  They applied for and received a top grant and this fund goes though her office to be disbursed. 
She indicated that these funds are offered to provide improving the knowledge of the classroom teacher which 
helps get their training and workshops at a local level . She indicated these classes teach math, reading, writing, 
history and civic studies. She reported this IGA is intended to create a way for the County School 
Superintendents both in Apache County and Navajo County to expend Race to the Top funds received from the 
Governor’s Office in fiscal year 2012 -13. Mrs. Morrow indicated that she is as king for approval to give funds  
back to Apache County that has already been expended. Supervisor Allen inquired how much money is going 
to Apache County.  Linda Morrow reported  they had $125,000 to divide equally among the three counties and 
that over four years the grant is for $500,00 0. Mrs. Morrow related they have trained over  125 teachers so far 
this year. Chairman Nez opened the call to the public and  indicated that the public will have four minutes to 
address the Board.  Brendee Peterson, Misty  Brimhall, Julie Bert , Bobbie Hall, Martin L ynch, Lowry Flake, 
Karen MacKean, Janet Reynolds, William Faurot, Brenda Johnson, Terry Hill, Joyce Hill and  Wayne Jones all 
spoke in opposition of Common Core as did Supervisor Allen. Linda Morrow addressed some of the thoughts 
and concerns  that were spoken about during the call to the public  and expressed  by Supervisor Allen . 
Superintendent Morrow indicated that this does support Common Core however she stressed she was here today 
to get an IGA approved for monies that  was spent by a previous superintendent in Apache County and not 
applied for, so the current superintendent is seeking the reimbursement. Supervisor Tenney indicated that there 
is nothing in the S tate laws and statues that give the Board of Supervisors any control over school education.  
He reported he believes the changes should be made at the l ocal School B oards, the State Board of E ducation 
and at the State Legislature. Supervisor Tenney praised the public who spoke and indicated if asked he would 
go to the School and State Boards and State Legislature to make these requests and help fight for this along with 
them, but reported the Navajo County Board of Supervisors is not the place to address this. He indicated that he 
did not believe the Board of Supervisors can affect the Common Core by refusing to pass money through to 
Apache County.  Supervisor Whitesinger  commended those that spoke and voiced their opinions in 
representation of their families and communities and for the protection of their children . Supervisor Allen  
spoke strongly against the Common Core State Standards Initiative and indicated that we need  to speak out, 
take a stand and start by voting no. Supervisor Thompson spoke in regards to education and our children in the 
remote areas  and how they are being educated . He indicated he appreciates the w orking relationship and 
communication with the homes,  schools and community.  Chairman Nez  indicated he appreciates all the 
citizens who are fighting for the advocacy of our children and indicated that in District I and District II  they are 
debating to implement their native language into their schools. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve 
the Intergovernmental Agreement be tween the Navajo County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service 
Agency and the Apache County Superintendent of Schools, Education Service Agency in order to expend Race 
to the Top funds; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger . Chairman Nez , Supervisor Thompson , 
Supervisor Tenney and Supervisor Whitesinger voted in favor of the motion. Supervisor Allen voted against 
the motion. Item did pass.

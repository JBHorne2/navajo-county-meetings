NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’ MINUTES 
TUESDAY, December 10, 2013 
 
PRESENT: Sylvia Allen, Supervisor; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor; David Tenney, Supervisor; Jesse 
Thompson, Supervisor; and Jonathan M. Nez, Chairman were present in person.  
9:03 a.m.  Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance and Invocation led by Supervisor Whitesinger.  
CALL TO THE PUBLIC: Terry Hill of Show Low, addressed the Board. He reported that he, County Attorney 
Brad Carlyon, Judge Ruechel, Rich Thompson the commander of VFW 9907 have all been involved  to instated 
getting a Veterans Court in Navajo County. Mr. Hill expressed appreciation to  Supervisor Thompson and Mr. 
James Jayne for asking him to put together an Honor Guard for a funeral at Hardrock on December 1. Mr. Hill 
stated that this was the 30th funeral this year in which the Post presented honors to Veterans. Supervisor Tenney 
and Supervisor Allen presented Mr. Hill and the VFW Post 9907 with a donation to support the expenses of the 
Honor Guard’s services. James Jayne recognized Chairman Nez for three of his recent marathon races that he 
participated in. Mr. Jayne thanked Chairman Nez for the example he is setting. Chairman Nez thanked the local 
newspaper for their article and he spoke about his healthy lifestyle  initiative. He spoke about the  recent loss of 
his brother. Chairman Nez indicated that he has run in 6 states and hopes to run in each state throughout the 
United States. The Board offered their congratulations to Chairman Nez.

JUVENILE PROBATION/COUNTY ATTORNEY: Consideration and possible acceptance of the award for 
the Drug Free Communities Grant Proposal submitted by the Navajo County Drug Project. Navajo County is 
proposed to be the fiscal agent for the Drug Free Communities Grant. The new positions will be a part of the 
Navajo County Attorney’s Office who will administer the grant. Arno Hall discussed the item. Supervisor Allen 
inquired about the status of Navajo Coun ty’s drug problem. Mr. Hall reported  he believes that we are doing a 
good job educating citizens. County Attorney Brad Carlyon offered up some information regarding the new kinds 
of drug coming out. Supervisor Allen indicated she would like to see the Health Department educate young 
mothers regarding the effects of their drug use upon their newborn bab ies. Supervisor Allen made a motion to 
approve the award for the Drug Free Communities Grant Proposal submitted by the Navajo County Drug Project 
Navajo County is proposed to be the fiscal agent for the Drug Free Communities Grant. The new positions will 
be a part of the Navajo County Attorney’s Office who will administer the grant; motion seconded by Supervisor 
Whitesinger. Vote unanimous approving the motion.

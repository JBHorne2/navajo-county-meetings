CALL TO THE PUBLIC : Terry Hill extended an invitation to the Board  of Supervisors to attend the State 
Superintendent of Schools John Huppenthal’s Town Hall meeting to be held at the VFW on Oct. 23rd. The subject 
would be the Common Core Standards  which are being implemented in the Arizona schools . He indicated 
November 11th is Veterans Day and invited everyone to the VFW’s Show Low  Post ceremonies at the Veterans 
Memorial at the City Hall in Show Low. Nick Lund, President of TRACKS  addressed the Board and reported 
that a group of ap proximately 350 volunteers build and maintain the White Mountain Trail Systems which is 
about 200 miles in the Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, with most of it being within Navajo County. He stated 
that this trail system is a major economic factor in the County  with the visitors it attracts.  He expressed 
appreciation to Supervisor Whitesinger for attending  and giving a welcome to  the 18th annual tour of the White 
Mountains where they had 650 mountain bike riders. He further spoke about the  safety improvement project 
which is being implemented to the Trail Systems. He indicated about every quarter mile they put up new trail 
marker diamonds with each diamond having  a unique code on them, which allow GPS capabilities to assist all 
the regional emergency responders and dispatchers. He reported this should help anyone who is lost or hurt t o 
have a quick response for assistance and to be located easily within approximately five minutes or less. Mr. Lund 
reported TRACKS has presented this to the Arizona State Committee on T rails and has recommended this type 
of model be approved for the State of Arizona . He reported the Arizona Game & Fish Department ’s Heritage 
Fund Grant, plus the County and Hon-Dah have provided financial support to help pay for the diamonds and the 
efforts involved in the project.

     ADMINISTRATION: Consideration and possible approval of the modifications to  the Holbrook Complex to 
allow for consistent security control: Homero Vela and Bret Starns reviewed the modifications being made to the 
Holbrook Complex allowing for consistent security control.  The North East entrance will be used by the 
employees via a card reader and closed to public access. This would require all public access to use the court 
entrance and have everyone go through the security screening. They reported Marla Randall, Court Administrator 
and Judge Ruechel were consulted in regar ds to the use of their security and the utilization of this space.  
Supervisor Allen inquired about the cost of having a metal detector and officers to sec ure the administration 
building. Mr. Vela indicated that we would have to hire three security officers and purchase the equipment. Brett 
Starns indicated the building modifications for the most part, can be accomplished by our Facilities Management 
Team and that we would use existing staff, so no additional personnel would be needed.  Mr. Jayne indicated that 
we are looking at opening up the whole entrance to create more room and easier access in and out of the building. 
Mr. Jayne further commented about the options that have been looked at for 3 FTE’s and the cost of the machine 
which he reported the costs are fairly substantial. Supervisor Tenney expressed his appreciation to Marla Randall 
and Judge Ruechel for their help in solving the problem of security measures.  Terry Hill spoke in opposition of 
the need for more security in administration and making it more difficult for the public to do business and access 
the Treasures, Assessor and Recorders office and especially making it difficult for those who need  handicap 
accessibility. Supervisor Tenney addressed Mr. Hill’s questions and reported the needs of security is especially 
needed in the courts. Supervisor Allen reported that there is also need of security at the Board meetings and that 
our citizens can go around the current security. Mr. Jayne specified  that employees are currently helping  to 
accommodate those in wheelchairs and that we will continue to be  accommodate and be  sensitive to all the 
citizen’s needs. Supervisor Allen made a motion  to approve the modifications to the Holbrook Complex to 
allow for consistent security control ; motion seconded by Supervisor Tenney. Vote unanimous approving the 
motion. 
FINANCE:  
a. County Budget Update : James Menlove  discussed the County Budget and answered questions of the 
Board. 
b. Consideration and possible approval of Fiscal Year 2013 -14 One -Time Salary Payment Plan: James 
Menlove acknowledged Paige Peterson for her efforts with this plan. He indicated that FY 2013-14 is the 
6th year that our employees have not had any adjustments to their salary base. Mr. Menlove recommended 
that we do a onetime salary payment which is a $500.00 amount that is for all permanent employees that 
receive benefits as of November 1st. He stated employees that were hired after November 1st of 2012 will 
receive a prorate d percentage of the one -time $500.00 payment . Mr. Menlove indicated that temporary 
and contract employees are not eligible for the one-time salary payment and part-time employees who are 
not eligible for benefits are also not eligible for the one-time salary payment. He reported the cost will be 
a cost of about $282,000.00 about $500,000.00 total costs to the General Fund which has been approved 
in the budget which included capacity for a 2% salary increase . Mr. Menlove reported the payment w ill 
be included in the regular employee paychecks dated November 22, 2013.  Supervisor Tenney thanked 
Mr. Jayne and Mr. Menlove for their work on this. Supervisor Tenney made a motion  to approve the 
Fiscal Year 2013 -14 One -Time Salary Payment Plan ; motion seconded by Supervisor Allen. Vote 
unanimous approving the motion.

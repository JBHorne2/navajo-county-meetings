NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, March 27, 2020

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Vice-Chairman, telephonic; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II, telephonic; Jason Whiting, Chairman, in person; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V, telephonic; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV, in person

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager, in person; Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager, in person; Brad Carlyon, County Attorney, telephonic; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board, in person

9:00 a.m . Call To Order: Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance was led by Chairman Whiting.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Update to Navajo County's Strategic Response to COVID-19 : Chairman Whiting expressed appreciation to what everyone is doing to keep themselves, their families and neighbors healthy and safe.  He expressed admiration to the many businesses throughout Navajo County for their leadership as they find ways to work through this.  He expressed gratitude to our wonderful schools and teachers for their outreach and genuine concern for our children and how they are modifying how they engage their students so they can continue to learn and grow.  He stated that County services are being modified to keep everyone working and delivering essential services.  He indicated that the County is open for business, but differently.  Bryan Layton discussed the internal limitations that have been made. Mr. Layton spoke about the importance of keeping our employees and residences safe, which, in turn, will help keep our families safe.  He spoke about cleaning tools that have been deployed to departments. He stated that the County remains opened for business, and we are just a phone call or email away.  He stated that about 50 percent of our workforce can telecommute, other staff are working in very small groups, and we are staggering shifts.  He spoke about caregivers whose children's schedules have changed.   He further spoke about limited public walk-in access and that our employees are still working and ready to provide necessary services.  He stated that we have provided a quick reference guide on our website and social media with departments and phone numbers; and if someone is not sure where to start, please call 928-524-4000, and Melissa Buckley and her team will listen and direct you to the right department.  He indicated that for the time being, the Courts are present as they are under the guidelines of the Office of the Supreme Court.  He stated that at some point, there may come a need to have an in-person meeting with county staff, and that can be done by appointment.  Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Layton and all staff for continuing to do their part and helping us stay abreast of information. Chairman Whiting spoke about the department heads and elected officials and thanked everyone for their leadership in helping their employees and customers stay safe while still providing services.

CALL TO THE PUBLIC :  Melissa read a letter from Kenneth Cail which was submitted prior to the meeting.  Chairman Whiting stated that the Board cannot respond to call to the public, and directed Ms. Buckley to provide Mr. Cail contact information for Superior Court.

NAVAJO COUNTY PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICES DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to enter into the Navajo County Public Health Services District; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Update on COVID-19 : Health Director, Jeff Lee, gave an update on COVID-19. He expressed gratitude for his staff for the selfless hard work that they have put in during this time.  He stated that the numbers are updated at 9:00 each morning when they receive the information from Arizona Department of Health Services.  He indicated that they expect the numbers to rise significantly over the next couple of weeks and encouraged all to stay calm and follow those guidelines and recommendations.  He stated that the Emergency Operations Center has been operational since Tuesday.  He spoke of the PPE items that we have and how we will disseminate them which he hopes will get us through a critical surge until another shipment comes through. Mr. Lee thanked the Board, Administration and all those who have given their support to the Health Department and are working together through this time. Supervisor Seymore asked if the number of those with the virus remained constant from yesterday, and Mr. Lee stated that it has.  He thanked Mr. Lee and his staff for all that they are doing.   Chairman Whiting thanked Mr. Lee and his team and expressed appreciation for the updates. Supervisor Whitesinger asked for a summary of Mr. Lee's update.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ELECTIONS :

a.

Consideration of approval for the Official Canvass of the March 17, 2020, Presidential Preference Election :  Rayleen Richards addressed the Board.  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the March 17, 2020, Presidential Preference Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES :  Chairman Whiting declared a conflict of interest and asked Supervisor Seymore to take over the meeting.  He left the room at 11:00 a.m.

a.

Consideration of award of Contract B20-01-007 Employee Benefits Consultant to The Mahoney Group : Eric Scott discussed the item. He discussed the RFP process. Mr. Scott spoke about the $4.5 million in savings to Navajo County in 2019 alone. He stated that we continue to innovate with our healthcare plan and have many ideas for the future, along with our health guide, Kayli Medina.  He indicated that we were able to negotiate with the Mahoney group for the same price and no cost to administer the plan.  He recommended approval of this item. Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Scott and stated that their evaluation process seems very fair. Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to award Contract B20-01-007 Employee Benefits Consultant to The Mahoney Group; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson . Vote unanimous approving the motion. Approved 3-0

Supervisor Seymore turned the meeting back over to Chairman Whiting at 11:05 a.m.

FACILITIES MANAGEMENT :

a.

Consideration of award of contract B20-01-010 Navajo County Historic Courthouse, Re-Roof, Contract #B20-01-010 to Restore Pro, Inc. :  Bret Starns, appearing telephonically, discussed the item. He stated that two bids were received, and at this time staff recommends award of the contract to Restore Pro, Inc., as the lowest responsive bidder.   Supervisor Seymore stated that he wants to make sure that we were very clear on the specifications and that both bidders are bidding the exact specifications of the product, and Mr. Starns indicated that he would ensure that they are in compliance with the architect's recommendation. Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to award Contract B20-01-010 Navajo County Historic Courthouse, Re-Roof, Contract #B20-01-010 to Restore Pro, Inc.; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS :

a.

Contract with Cellular One for a microwave backhaul service for improved radio performance :  John Osgood discussed the item.  He stated that by utilizing this microwave backhaul service, Navajo County will have dedicated space on the most reliable microwave system in Apache and Navajo County.  He stated that staff recommends approval of this item.  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve a Contract with Cellular One for a microwave backhaul service for improved radio performance; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 14-2020 the acceptance of Little Doe Trail and Twin Pines Trail into the Navajo County Maintenance System as primitive roads :  John Osgood discussed the item and stated that staff recommends approval of this item.  Supervisor Whitesinger made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 14-2020 the acceptance of Little Doe Trail and Twin Pines Trail into the Navajo County Maintenance System as primitive roads; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Lee Jack, Sr., District I Supervisor, is present at 11:19 am.

c.

Consideration of Task Agreement No. 20 to Memorandum of Agreement between the Bureau of Indian Affairs Department of Transportation-Navajo Region and Navajo County for the funding of purchasing pavement rehabilitation material :  John Osgood discussed the item.  He indicated that the fiscal impact of this item is $20,000, and the source of funds is the Highway User Revenue Fund, which can be used solely for transportation-related projects.  He stated that staff recommends approval of this item.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve Task Agreement No. 20 to Memorandum of Agreement between the Bureau of Indian Affairs Department of Transportation-Navajo Region and Navajo County for the funding of purchasing pavement rehabilitation material; motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NAVAJO COUNTY FLOOD CONTROL DISTRICT: Board of Directors Session :  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to enter into the Navajo County Flood Control District Board of Directors Session; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

1.

CONSENT AGENDA :

a.
Award JE Fuller Contract to install a mass notification system near the Little Colorado River Levee at Winslow :  Supervisor Thompson highlighted the importance of this life-saving measure to alert the people of Winslow.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to authorize the Chairman to sign the item in the consent agenda; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

2.

REGULAR AGENDA :

a.
Consideration of award of contract with JE Fuller to coordinate, procure and install the custom sensor in the Schoen's Dam principal outlet intake tower :  John Osgood stated that all three of these flood control items are important to our routine and non-routine response and flood control management.  Mr. Osgood spoke about the Schoen's Dam and stated that this item will greatly improve the operation of the dam.  Supervisor Seymore indicated that he has had several people report that this gate is locked open and can't be closed.  Sandra Phillips stated that it is not broken, but it got stuck open, and this item will alert staff if the gate is open.  Supervisor Seymore stated that this is a safety mechanism to help us know the position of that gate.  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to award Contract with JE Fuller to coordinate, procure and install the custom sensor in the Schoen's Dam principal outlet intake tower; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.
Consideration and award of contract to Atkins North America, Inc. to determine the high water elevation of Rainbow Lake, which is identified as a Special Flood Hazard Area, Zone AE :  John Osgood discussed the item.  He stated that the fiscal impact is $26,620, adequate funds have been budgeted and approved in the Navajo County Flood Control budget and staff recommends approval.  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to award Contract to Atkins North America, Inc. to determine the high water elevation of Rainbow Lake, which is identified as a Special Flood Hazard Area, Zone AE; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members : Glenn Kephart thanked the Board for the leadership it has shown through this crisis to make sure that we move forward as a cohesive team throughout Navajo County and their support for staff so that we have the resources we need.  He spoke about the amazing team we have who have stepped up and how every one of the supervisors have reached out personally to see how staff is doing.
Supervisor Seymore thanked our citizens throughout Navajo County whom have been excellent to work with. He stated that our communication has been great as if we were fully staffed. He indicated that everyone has done a super job. He encouraged our citizens to look to another source for help. He stated that this Sunday, he is asking citizens globally to fast and pray and look to the source of our Heavenly Father and our Savior, Jesus Christ, that we might be protected during this time and stated that he will be doing that on Sunday. Supervisor Whitesinger expressed appreciation to everyone and stated that she is always impressed by the people she works alongside with and is filled with gratitude.  She expressed her support and appreciation to Jeff Lee and his team and thanked them for all of their efforts. She further thanked administration. Supervisor Thompson expressed appreciation to all of our first responders in addressing the health issues we have. He stated that he has continued to maintain communication, even though he is apart right now.  He encouraged everyone to be safe. Supervisor Jack sent his appreciation to all the frontline people that are out there fighting this health issue. He indicated that his district has been hit hard in Chilchinbeto. He indicated that Navajo Nation leaders have been out and are making things happen. He sent his prayers. Chairman Whiting expressed appreciation to the team that we have from top to bottom. He stated that he is proud of the response he has received from the businesses for their support of the measures being taken. He concurred with Supervisor Seymore and encouraged that we all take a moment to unify and pray. He thanked his fellow supervisors during this tough time.

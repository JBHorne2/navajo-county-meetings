REGULAR AGENDA:

1.

Update on COVID-19:   Jeff Lee advised that the risk of exposure remains extremely high in Navajo County.  He advised that as of this morning there have been 6,881 cases and 259 deaths in Navajo County and 3434 new cases reported in Arizona today.  He stated that we must all be better with following the CDC recommendations which include social distancing, wearing a facial covering, staying home when possible or if you're feeling sick, avoiding large gatherings, coughing or sneezing into a bent elbow or tissue and disinfecting frequently touched surfaces.  He stated that we are trending in the wrong direction and that if it keeps up within a few weeks we will be back in the red zone for schools and high-risk businesses.  As to the vaccine he indicated that the trials are showing a ninety percent effective rate, that they don't have a positive time frame for the vaccine to be available in our area but that they are planning for as early as the end of November.
Supervisor Thompson addressed how each of us have had someone who has been affected by the virus and the impact it has had on those effected.  He indicated that he hopes the vaccine will be available soon.  He further indicated that it is up to us to be responsible.
Supervisor Jack inquired how safe the vaccine will be and possible side effects.  He further inquired how they know it is 90 percent effective.
Jeff Lee addressed the safety of the vaccine and indicated that as it becomes available, they will receive safety data sheets from the CDC and the FDA that list possible side effects, allergic reactions and other details, so they can educate the public on it.
Chairman Whiting asked Mr. Lee to thank his team and let them know that they appreciate their efforts.  He indicated that they know they are doing their best to partner with the different municipalities, the schools and the county and help everyone be prepared, understand what to do and make the best decisions.

Supervisor Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:

a.

Consideration of a Proclamation declaring November 2020 as "Native American Heritage Month":   Supervisor Thompson addressed the item.  He indicated that Native Americans are very strong and generous and despite many challenges and disappointments they always strive to make their lives and the lives of their families better.  He stated that he appreciates the forefathers and the grandparents who laid the foundation to build on.  He further stated that they depended on the cornfields and animals for stability and to provide for their families.    Supervisor Thompson presented the Proclamation declaring November 2020 as "Native American Heritage Month".
Supervisor Jack thanked Supervisor Thompson for presenting the Proclamation.  He indicated that he thinks that the Proclamation is very important, that we live in a diverse county and that we should recognize each other's cultures and traditions.
Chairman Whiting indicated that he appreciates the diversity we have in Navajo County and the opportunity we have to learn from each other and come together and understand each other's point of view.  He stated that there is strength when there is diversity.  He further stated that we should recognize not only the culture but the contributions that the Native American communities make.

Supervisor Thompson made a motion to declare November 2020 as "Native American Heritage Month; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS:

a.

Consideration of Attachment I to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County (Contract No. CO10070) for road projects :  John Osgood addressed the item and provided a background of the agreement.  He indicated that approximately 45,000 tons is crushed and ready to go on the Chapter roads and that NDOT has contracted with a contractor to load, haul and deliver the existing material.  He stated that NDOT and the Nation believe that the funding that they previously provided for the new crushing would be better used for placing the existing material.  He advised that Public Works supports the request to return the funds.  He further advised that the fiscal impact of the item is neutral as the funding provided by the Nation to the County remains in the same project.
Supervisor Jack indicated that they had numerous meeting on this project, that the project had been delayed and that it is a priority to get the gravel to the Chapters.  He requested that the Board support the new plan to be able to place the material.

Supervisor Jack made a motion to accept Attachment I to the Intergovernmental Agreement between the Navajo Nation and Navajo County (Contract No. CO10070) for road projects; motion seconded by Supervisor  Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING AND AGENDA
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
Friday, November 13, 2020 *SPECIAL MEETING
ONE OR MORE SUPERVISORS MAY ATTEND VIA VIDEO AND/OR TELEPHONICALLY 
The Navajo County Board of Supervisors will hold a special meeting open to the public on Friday, November 13, 2020, in the
Supervisors’ Chamber in the Navajo County Governmental Complex, Holbrook, Arizona. The Board may vote to meet in a
closed executive session to discuss certain matters as indicated on the following agenda. The Board may meet in executive
session pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.03(A) (3) for legal advice on any item.
We continue to maintain physical distancing and will conduct Board of Supervisor meetings by conference call. Board
Members, staff and members of the public may call and participate in the meeting by following these steps:
Conference Call number: 1-877-820-7831
Participant Passcode: 9933555 #
Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Chairman’s discretion. A copy of the agenda background material
provided to the Board, with the exception of items to be considered in executive session, is available for public inspection at Navajo
County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov

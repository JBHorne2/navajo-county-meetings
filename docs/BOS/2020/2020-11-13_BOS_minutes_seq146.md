NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, November 13, 2020

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Vice-Chairman; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV, all appearing telephonically.

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Brandt Clark, Deputy County Attorney; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board; Rayleen Richards, Elections Director; Christen Haddox, Elections Coordinator, all appearing telephonically

9:01 a.m . Call To Order:  Roll call done.

ELECTIONS:

a.

Consideration of Approving the Official Canvass of the results for the Nov. 3, 2020 General Election:
Rayleen Richards addressed the Board regarding the item.  She advised that on November 4, 2020, Elections staff met with the six members of the Democratic and Republican Parties.  They conducted a hand count of drawn ballot batches and two of the polling places that were randomly drawn by the Chairmen of both parties.  The count was one hundred percent accurate.  She indicated that the information on that can be found on the Secretary of States website.  Mrs. Richards requested that the Board approve the official canvass of the General Election as presented.
Supervisor Thompson inquired if everything went well with this election.  Mrs. Richards advised that everything went well.  Supervisor Jack inquired if there were any issues with getting poll workers.  Mrs. Richards advised that there were no issues, that they had a lot of people willing to help with the General Election and that they had to turn some away.

Supervisor Jack made a motion to approve the Official Canvass of the results for the Nov. 3, 2020 General Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

Chairman Whiting thanked Mrs. Richards and her team and indicated that they did a great job.
Supervisor Seymore thanked Mrs. Richards.
Supervisor Thompson thanked Mrs. Richards for all she did.

ADJOURN: 9:08a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason E. Whiting, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Monday, August 17, 2020

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV, all appearing by phone.

Staff Attendance:
Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Jason Moore, Deputy County Attorney; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Jalyn Gerlich, Superintendent of Schools; Jayson Vowell, Finance Director

1:00 p.m . Call To Order: Roll call was taken and Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order at 1:00 p.m.

ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES:

a.

Consideration and adoption of the Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Property Tax Rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts:

Jayson Vowell discussed the item.  He indicted that this is the third and final step in the budget process and that the amounts are consistent with what was presented in the adopted budgets.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the 2020-2021 Property Tax Rates for all Navajo County Taxing Districts; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS:

a.

Consideration and adoption of the Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Tax Rates for all School Districts for Navajo County:

Jalyn Gerlich discussed the item.  She indicated that there are five districts that have decreasing rates, one is slightly increasing and one that will remain the same.  Ms. Gerlich requested the Boards consideration and adoption of the tax rates for the school districts in Navajo County.

Supervisor Seymore inquired what district is taking a slight increase.  Mrs. Gerlich advised that it is Joseph City and that it is due to their assessed valuations decreasing.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the 2020-20201 Tax Rates for all Schools Districts for Navajo County; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN   1:05 p.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason E. Whiting, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors

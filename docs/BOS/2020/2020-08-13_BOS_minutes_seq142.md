NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Thursday, August 13, 2020

Present:
Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV, all appearing by phone.

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Leah Thomas, Deputy Clerk of the Board; Rayleen Richards, Elections Director, appearing by phone; Michael Sample, Recorder, appearing by phone

Attendees:
Rochelle Lacapa, with Congressman Tom O'Halleran's Office, appearing by phone

11:31 a.m . Call To Order:   Roll call was taken and Chairman Whiting called the meeting to order at 11:31 a.m.

CONSENT AGENDA:

a.

Designate Jayson Vowell as the Navajo County Chief Financial Officer to submit the Annual Expenditure Limitation Report:

Chairman Whiting asked if the Board wanted further clarification on the consent agenda item. There was no discussion.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the item on the consent agenda; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote was unanimous approving the motion.

ELECTIONS:

a.

Consideration of approval of the Official Canvass of the Aug. 4, 2020 Primary Election:

Rayleen Richards addressed the Board regarding the item and indicated it was a difficult election due to COVID-19.  Mrs. Richards requested that the Board approve the Official Canvass of the Primary Election as presented.

Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the Offical Canvass of the August 4, 2020 Primary Election; motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN 11:36 a.m. meeting adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason E. Whiting, Chairman
Navajo County Board of Supervisors
DATED: August 25, 2020

RESOLUTION 13-20 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE NAVAJO COUNTY ARIZONA, BOARD OF SUPERVISORS, 
DECLARING A LOCAL EMERGENCY 
 
WHEREAS, the Navajo County Board of Supervisors is authorized by Arizona Revised 
Statutes §26-311, to declare a local emergency in Navajo County due to a natural calamity; and  
WHEREAS, the World Health Organization declared a Public Health Emergency of 
International Concern on January 30, 2020, the United States Department of Health and Human 
Services declared a Public Health Emergency related to the COVID-19 outbreak on January 31, 
2020, and the World Health Organization officially declared a pandemic due to COVID-19 on 
March 11, 2020, and the Governor of Arizona declared an emergency due to COVID-19 and 
issued Executive Order 2020-07 providing Proactive Measures to Protect Against COVID-19 
and on March 13, 2020 the President of the United States, Donald Trump, declared a National 
Emergency concerning the Novel Coronavirus Disease (COVID-19) Outbreak; and 
WHEREAS, Governor Doug Ducey and Superintendent Kathy Hoffman, on March 15, 2020, 
announced a statewide closure of Arizona schools from Monday, March 16, 2020 through 
Friday, March 27, 2020 and subsequently extended the closure date through April 10, 2020; and 
WHEREAS, Governor Doug Ducey issued Executive Orders on March 20, 2020, ordering the 
closure of Bars, Movie Theaters, indoor gyms and fitness clubs and restricted restaurants to take 
out and delivery options only; and 
WHEREAS, the County continues to plan and prepare for event and facility closures, modified 
staffing plan, work-from-home programs, and enhanced communications to employees to ensure 
for effective Continuity of Operations of County Government through the activation of the 
County Continuity of Operations plan, and plans to provide additional support to assure that 
affected businesses are fully educated and aware of resources available to help transition through 
these tough economic challenges; and  
WHEREAS, COVID -19 poses a serious public health threat for infectious disease spread to the 
residents and visitors of Navajo County if proper precautions recommended by public health are 
not followed; and 
WHEREAS, throughout the nation and State of Arizona, public health and health care systems 
have identified precautions and interventions that can mitigate the spread of COVID -19; and

<!-- page 4 -->
WHEREAS, it is necessary and appropriate to take action to ensure the spread of COVID-19 is 
controlled and that the residents of the Navajo County remain safe and healthy; and 
WHEREAS, multiple samples for the CoVid-19 virus originating in Navajo County were tested 
at the Arizona State Public Health Laboratory and results were found to be a presumptive 
positive for COVID-19 and  the County increased operations to account for additional resources, 
logistics and tracking in support of the County Health Emergency Opera tions Center (HEOC) as 
needed; and   
WHEREAS, the above facts give credence to the conditions of extreme peril and public health 
emergency; and the declaration of a local emergency will enable Navajo County to provide 
emergency resources, request additional resources and receive mutual aid from other political 
sub-divisions and entities;  
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF 
SUPERVISORS that a local emergency is hereby declared to exist throughout Navajo County 
due to extreme peril and the public health emergency created by the COVID-19 illness; and be it 
further 
RESOLVED that, during the existence of said local emergency, the powers, functions and 
duties of the emergency organization of this County shall be those prescribed by state law, by 
ordinances and resolutions of Navajo County and the County Emergency Operations Plan. 
This resolution shall be effective immediately upon adoption due to the foregoing emergency 
and shall remain in effect until rescinded by the Board of Supervisors. 
 
PASSED AND ADOPTED this 23rd day of March, 2020.

NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS' MINUTES
Tuesday, March 23, 2020

Present:
Lee Jack, Sr, Vice-Chairman; Jesse Thompson, Supervisor District II; Jason Whiting, Chairman; Dawnafe Whitesinger, Supervisor District V; Daryl Seymore, Supervisor District IV, each appearing telephonically

Staff Attendance:
Glenn Kephart, County Manager; Bryan Layton, Assistant County Manager; Melissa Buckley, Clerk of the Board; Jeff Lee, Health Director; Eric Scott, HR Director

11:06 a.m . Call To Order:

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS :

a.

Consideration and adoption of Resolution Number 13-2020 , declaring a local emergency throughout Navajo County due to extreme peril and the public health emergency created by the COVID-19 illness :  Glenn Kephart introduced Resolution Number 13-2020 and gave a brief description of what has gone on since the onset of COVID-19.  He stated that the resolution provides us an opportunity to receive federal funds as they become available and increased flexibility to respond in a timely manner as things are quickly ever change.  He indicated that staff is recommending adoption of the resolution.  Chairman Whiting indicated that this resolution will allow federal reimbursement funds to flow down to the local level and gives us proper legal authority.  Supervisor Seymore stated that it also allows us the ability to have procedures in place prior to closing the building.  Supervisor Thompson inquired whether this is consistent to the actions which have been taken by the White Mountain Hopi and Navajo and whether communications will be increased at a higher level, and Mr. Kephart indicated that it is consistent with that, as well as what other counties are doing.  He stated that communications have been occurring at the highest level prior to this resolution and will continue after the resolution, it just increases our flexibility to respond if we need to do something for our community outside of normal policy.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to adopt Resolution Number 13-2020 , declaring a local emergency throughout Navajo County due to extreme peril and the public health emergency created by the COVID-19 illness; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

ADJOURN :  At 11:14 a.m., the Board adjourned.

APPROVED:

Jason E. Whiting, Chairman

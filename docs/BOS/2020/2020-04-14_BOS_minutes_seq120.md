REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Update on COVID-19 :  Jeff Lee gave an update on COVID-19.  He stated that we are seeing a continued steady rise of cases throughout Navajo County.  He indicated that as of this morning, there are 355 confirmed positive cases.  He spoke about recently moving the Emergency Operations Center to Martinez Hall at the Fairgrounds.  He thanked those at the Fairgrounds for allowing them to utilize the space and helping them set it up.  He stated that they have two national guard members whose main job will be working with the EOC for situational awareness for Apache County, Navajo County and Coconino County and also will help within the EOC with resource management and distribution.  He indicated that the forecast modeling shows that mitigation efforts which are in place are working which have resulted in the peak hitting Arizona being pushed back to April 30th.  He spoke about the importance of continuing to follow the recommendations.  He stated that this allows our healthcare partners extra time to prepare for when the surge does hit.  He indicated that they submitted a resource request to the Arizona Department of Emergency Management for site surveys on two alternate care site locations in Winslow and Show low.  He stated that currently, there are three sites which have been surveyed in Arizona:  old Walgreens warehouse in Flagstaff, St. Luke's Hospital in Phoenix and the convention center in Tucson.  He stated only one of those sites has been approved for a build-out, which is St. Luke's Hospital.  He indicated that on Sunday, they were notified by the Arizona Department of Health Services that we will receive another shipment of PPE, and they will send out another needs survey to our healthcare and first responders in order to get that equipment to them as soon as possible.  He thanked Healthchoice Arizona which will be sending us 3,000 surgical masks to push out to our partners.  He stated that there are positive indicators that the curve is flattening and mitigation measures are working, so now is the time not to let up.  He indicated that we need to continue to follow the recommendations.  Supervisor Thompson asked about the location of the site in Winslow, and Mr. Lee stated that it is probably the NPC campus.  Chairman Whiting expressed gratitude for the surgical masks which are coming in.  He spoke about those who have provided cloth masks.  He inquired why the State is not reporting those who have recovered, and Mr. Lee stated it's a challenging thing to report.  He stated that they are working to develop a way to accurately report those recoveries.  He indicated that some of the challenge is that multiple organizations are performing case investigations, continuous changes in the CDC guidelines, and there are individuals who are not following the traditional track of the virus.   He stated that they want to agree statewide so that we're all reporting the same way; and once they can report that accurately, they want to get that out.  Chairman Whiting inquired whether Navajo County has test kits for county- administered tests, and Mr. Lee Health stated that the Department receives testing supplies and pushes them out to healthcare partners immediately.  He indicated that they do not collect samples at the Health Department.  Chairman Whiting inquired regarding who gets tested, and Mr. Lee stated that it is up to the healthcare provider, and there are different matrixes for the different labs.  He indicated that the best way to know if you're eligible to be tested is to contact your healthcare provider or healthcare facility, have them do an initial triage, and they will determine whether testing is appropriate.  Chairman Whiting inquiredregarding the process now for first responders and medical professionals to get tested, and Mr. Lee stated that testing is encouraged to prioritize those healthcare workers and first responders and those in critical positions, but the process is the same regarding contacting their healthcare provider or healthcare facility who will do a quick triage over the phone to determine whether testing is needed.  He indicated that they are considered a priority group.  Chairman Whiting thanked Mr. Lee for his presentation and further thanked Mr. Lee and his team for their efforts.

Supervisor Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

Consideration of the resignation of Doris Clark, Navajo County Recorder effective Friday, April 17, 2020, and appointing Michael Sample to fill the remainder of the Recorder's term :  Doris Clark discussed the item.  She spoke about her time serving as County Recorder.  She requested that the Board accept her letter of resignation and appoint Michael Sample to fill the remainder of her term.  She expressed confidence that Mr. Sample will do an excellent job.   Supervisor Whitesinger thanked Ms. Clark for her leadership and work to serve our communities.  Supervisor Thompson stated that Ms. Clark has done an excellent job representing the County by having direct communication with the people she represents.  Supervisor Jack thanked Ms. Clark for the good work that she has done.  Supervisor Seymore stated that Ms. Clark has done a fantastic job and thanked her for her service to the County.  Chairman Whiting thanked Ms. Clark for her efforts and her partnership with the Board in finding the best ways to find solutions for our citizens.  He spoke about the integrity of Mr. Sample and his ability to lead.  Supervisor Seymore   made a motion to accept the resignation of Doris Clark, Navajo County Recorder, effective Friday, April 17, 2020, and appointing Michael Sample to fill the remainder of the Recorder's term; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.  Ms. Clark thanked the Board for their kind comments and stated that she was honored to serve with great elected officials.  Mr. Sample thanked Ms. Clark for her recommendation and having the confidence in him to do the job.  He stated that he looks forward to working with the great team in the Recorder's Office and serving the citizens of Navajo County.

COUNTY BUSINESS UPDATE: Report from County Manager, County Attorney and Board members :  Glenn Kephart expressed appreciation to the entire Navajo County team for smoothly modifying operating procedures and coming together as one team during these challenging times.  He spoke about the leadership of the Board.  Supervisor Seymore expressed appreciation for the opportunity to serve during this unprecedented time and being part of this great team.  He spoke about the unity we have in our communities, our counties and the state, as a whole.  He thanked our health department and sheriff's department for their work.  Supervisor Thompson stated that he has seen this type of emergency before.  He indicated that it was a big challenge, but we came through it, and he knows that there are a lot of prayers taking place, and there is hope that things will get better.  He expressed appreciation to the whole Navajo County team.  He thanked those on the front lines.  He stated that he is attending all of his meetings telephonically and thanked the supervisors and Administration.  Supervisor Whitesinger expressed gratitude for those who continue to serve us.  She sent prayers and thoughts to the Navajo Nation.  She spoke about the importance of our Tribal communities.  Supervisor Jack concurred with the sentiments of his fellow supervisors.  He spoke about the challenges of the Chapters and their needs for food, firewood, water and supplies.  He stated that there are three communities that are in a hot spot in his District.  He thanked everyone for their prayers. Chairman Whiting stated that there is a lot of good which is occurring during this time.  He spoke about the leadership of the Board members, elected officials and department heads in the way that we're doing business.  He stated that they are working to find the timeframe to move forward appropriately.  He indicated that he is proud of the County for looking for ways to continue to provide services in a safe and responsible way.  He thanked Mr. Lee and his team for their efforts.  He recognized the businesses and expressed appreciation to the Workforce Development team for keeping everyone apprised of the federal and state relief available and how it applies to our businesses.  He encouraged everyone to continue with their prayers.

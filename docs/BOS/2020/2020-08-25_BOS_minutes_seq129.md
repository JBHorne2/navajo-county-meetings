REGULAR AGENDA:

1.

Consideration and approval of a Proclamation declaring the month of September as "National Preparedness Month":   Catrina Jenkins addressed the Board regarding the item and indicated that this year the theme is "Disasters Don't Wait, Make Your Plan Today".  She advised that through the month of September they will be reaching out to citizens to make sure they are prepared for any disaster.  She further advised that they will be reaching out to their partners to have them help share this message.
Supervisor Seymore stated that he appreciates all that Mrs. Jenkins and her department do, indicated that this is a great proclamation and that it is always good to be prepared.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the item and declare the month of September as "National Preparedness Month"; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

2.

Consideration and approval of Intergovernmental Agreement 2021-045 Navajo County (COVID-19 Complaint Referrals) with the Arizona Department of Health Services for the purpose of off-setting the salary of one Environmental Health Inspector during the COVID-19 pandemic:   Jeff Lee indicated that this is a new grant agreement between the Health Department and the Arizona Department of Health Services.  Mr. Lee stated that this will be set up through the Environmental Health Program, where any complaint or concern on an establishment due to the new COVID restrictions will be responded to and worked through and that this program will help offset the cost of one of their staff members.  Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the Intergovernmental Agreement 2021-045 Navajo County COVID-19 Complaint Referral with the Arizona Department of Health Services for the purpose of off-setting the salary of one Environmental Health Inspector during COVID-19 pandemic; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

3.

Consideration and approval of Intergovernmental Agreement, Contract Number IGA2020-035, for grant funding for Empower PLUS 2.0, a nutrition based training program, with the use of the Go NAPSACC platform, to be provided to qualifying childcare centers in Navajo and Apache Counties:   Jeff Lee indicated that this is a new program to Northern Arizona and that this is to help support early childhood educators to improve their practices and policies and to help meet health related practices through the purpose of lifelong healthy living.  Mr. Lee further indicated that this program will be incorporated through the First Things First Childcare Health Consolation Program.  Supervisor Thompson inquired if this is for all Navajo County or if the tribes have their own program.  Mr. Lee indicated that this includes on reservation and off reservation childcare facilities for Navajo and Apache Counties.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve Intergovernmental Agreement Contract Number IGA2020-035, for grant funding for Empower PLUS 2.0, a nutrition based training program, with the use of the Go NAPSACC platform, to be provided to qualifying childcare centers in Navajo and Apache Counties; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

4.

Consideration and approval of Intergovernmental Agreement, Contract Number IGA2021-045, to create a scalable and sustainable comprehensive disease investigation division focused on enhancing the level of disease surveillance, detection, and rapid response in Navajo County:   Jeff Lee stated that this is a new Intergovernmental Agreement and that it is part of a larger grant, the Arizona Department of Health Services received specifically for COVID related activities.  Mr. Lee indicated that this funding will help sustain the current level of staffing that was brought on for COVID related response through February 2022.   Mr. Lee stated that this will also allow them to expand their current disease surveillance outside of COVID-19 and enhance the level of service they can provided to many of their partners throughout the County. Supervisor Jack made a motion to approve Intergovernmental Agreement, Contract Number IGA2021-045, to create a scalable and sustainable comprehensive disease investigation division focused on enhancing the level of disease surveillance, detection, and rapid response in Navajo County; motion seconded by Supervisor Seymore.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

5.

Update on COVID-19:   Jeff Lee stated that there is positive news to report and that the sacrifices and actions are paying off.  He indicated that the numbers over the past couple of weeks have lowered and that we are trending in a positive direction.  Mr. Lee stated that we need to continue to follow the personal precautionary measures and that if we continue to follow these measures we will continue to trend in a positive direction.  Mr. Lee indicated that we have seen what a small lapse in our actions can cause, that in late May, early June and in July we had a significant rise in cases that put a stress on the health care system.  Mr. Lee advised that now is the time to start thinking about flu season and to start planning for that.  Mr. Lee indicated the Health Department as well as other healthcare partners are scheduled to receive the flu vaccine in the next few weeks and that it is crucial to get a flu vaccination to avoid another stressor on the healthcare system and to avoid a potential for a flu/COVID coinfection.  Supervisor Seymore inquired if Mr. Lee could provide current numbers.  Jeff Lee advised that as of this morning the total cases for Navajo County are 5,523 and the total cases for Arizona are 199273.  Mr. Lee further advised that in the past forty-five days in Navajo County that for the first thirty days they were seeing fifty to eighty new cases per day and that in the past two weeks it is an average of six new cases per day.   Chairman Whiting thanked Jeff for his presentation.  Supervisor Thompson inquired how Navajo County is doing compared to other counties in Arizona.  Jeff Lee indicated that we are doing very well and that Northern Arizona as a whole is doing very well.  Mr. Lee advised that compared to other counties we have had a much faster reduction in new cases and that he contributes that to the actions and precautions people are taking.  Supervisor Thompson thanked Mr. Lee.

Supervisor Thompson made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

PUBLIC WORKS:

a.

Consideration of the purchase of one International 10-Wheel Dump Truck with Snow Plow and Cinder Spreader from RWC Group in the amount of $268,727.80:   John Osgood addressed the item and advised that they are replacing vehicles and heavy equipment that have reached the end of their useful life. Mr. Osgood indicated that this item is one of most utilized and versatile pieces of equipment in the fleet and that this piece of equipment will replace a dump truck put in service in 1989.  Mr. Osgood further indicated that this equipment is used for snow response, for weather related repairs to roads and for hauling material for maintenance work.  He advised that the State of Arizona has a contract with RWC Group for cooperative governmental use.  Mr. Osgood indicated that the source of funding for the purchase is the Highway User Revenue Fund.  Mr. Osgood advised that staff recommends approval of the item.  Supervisor Thompson inquired if old vehicles can be donated to NDOT in Dilcon or Pinon.  Mr. Osgood advised that they are getting some trade-in value and addressed the issue of gifting and donating related to the funds but indicated that they can look into it.  Supervisor Thompson addressed the issue of not having proper equipment to maintain the roads and indicated that this could be a future partnership worth looking into. Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the purchase of one International 10-Wheel Dump Truck with Snowplow and Cinder Spreader from RWC Group in the amount of $268,727.80; motion seconded by Supervisor Jack.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

b.

Consideration of the purchase of two 1-1/2 ton Pick Up Trucks with Dump Bed, Plows & Cinder Spreaders from RWC Group in the amount of $202,938.98:   John Osgood advised that these vehicles will replace two vehicles put into service in 1994 and 2003 and that the primary purpose for this equipment would be for snow response, weather related repairs and for hauling material for maintenance projects.  Mr. Osgood indicated that the source of funding is the Highway User Revenue Fund which can be used solely for transportation related purposes. He advised that the State of Arizona has a contract with RWC Group and that the contract is available for cooperative use.  Mr. Osgood indicated that these international model vehicles provided by RWC can read diagnostics codes via an on demand connect system verses having to take the vehicle into the shop for diagnostics which saves time, travel and money.  Staff recommends approval of the item.   Supervisor Seymore made a motion to approve the purchase of two 1-1/2 ton Pick Up Trucks with Dump Bed, Plows and Cinder Spreaders from RWC Group in the amount of $202,938.98; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson.   Supervisor Whitesinger commented on the importance of the equipment replacement to the organization and indicated that the purchase will help our communities.  She thanked the members of Public Works for all that they do.  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

c.

Consideration of award of Contract #B20-07-015 Pavement Markings to the lowest responsive, responsible bidder, Sunline Contracting, LLC:   John Osgood advised that the county maintains approximately 730 miles of roadway with about 320 miles being paved.  He indicated that this is an annual contact for pavement markings which are used to refresh faded and lay out new striping and cross walks on county roads. Mr. Osgood addressed the bidding process and indicated that the bid is being awarded to Sunline Contracting, an existing contractor that they have good experiences with but that this is not a renewal. Supervisor Thompson inquired if any of the minority contractors bid for these types of jobs.  Mr. Osgood addressed the bidding process and indicated that he can research if any of them were minority owned businesses.  He indicated that they can see who is on the list and see if they can expand it to include minority and women owned businesses.  Supervisor Thompson indicated that it would be good for them to participate in the bidding process.  Supervisor Thompson made a motion to approve the award of Contract #B20-07-015 Pavement Markings to Sunline Contracting, LLC; motion seconded by Supervisor Whitesinger.   Vote unanimous approving the motion.

a.

REGULAR AGENDA :

1.

Presentation - update on the current activities related to the 2019 Coronavirus :  Jeff Lee, Health Director, gave a presentation regarding the Coronavirus. Mr. Lee indicated that this is a low risk virus to the United States according to the Center for Disease Control (CDC), and they do not think that we will see the effects that some other countries are seeing with this virus.  He stated that as of yesterday, in the U.S., there are 12 cases, with one case in Maricopa County.  He indicated that in Navajo County, we are on weekly conference calls with the CDC and Arizona Department of Health Services (ADHS) where they get information on incoming travelers, isolation and quarantine. He stated that they will start having a weekly internal planning meeting so that if we do have a suspect case, we will be able to properly handle it.  He indicated that they will also increase social media presence to communicate with the public which will be updated weekly.  Mr. Lee stated that prevention is key, which includes washing your hands, using hand sanitizer, avoiding close interaction with those who are sick, stay home if you are sick and communicate with your primary health care provider.  He indicated that the Coronavirus is a serious virus; however, we are still in the midst of flu season, and we have had 17,000 cases of flu in Arizona and 23 flu-related deaths in Arizona since October 1st. Supervisor Seymore thanked Mr. Lee for the information and stated that he appreciates having a better understanding of this. Supervisor Thompson inquired whether we know who from the County or the State have traveled to China, and Mr. Lee stated that we do not know the exact number of travelers into China.  He indicated that we do get notified before a traveler comes back to the County from China.

Supervisor Seymore made a motion to return to regular session; motion seconded by Supervisor Thompson .  Vote unanimous approving the motion.

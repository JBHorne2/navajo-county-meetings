Chairman Joel Lawson called the meeting of the Navajo County Planning & Zoning Commission to 
order at 6:00 p.m. , and  led the pledge of Allegiance.   Chairman Lawson then explained the meeting 
procedures to the public. 
 
Item # 1 – CASE # 11-42:  SPECIAL USE PERMIT. Discussion and possible Commission action on a 
request by Disgen Marcou Mesa, LLC, for a Special Use Permit to  place wind power generating towers 
and related facilities for the purpose of developing an alternative power (wind) facility know n as the 
Disgen Marcou Mesa project, on various Assessors Parcels in the Marcou Mesa vicinity  north of 
Holbrook.   
 
Greg Loper  provided an overview of  the request, and noted that this project is proposed to be a 
“Merchant Facility”, meaning that this project will not sell its power to a specific utility company, but 
rather sell its power on the open market  and thereby negating an y Power Purchase Agreement. He noted 
that the applicant is requesting that their setbacks be as little as 110% of the total wind turbine tower 
height, which is less than the setbacks as required in the adopted “Wind Ordinance”. He also noted that 
staff has received over 200 letters regarding this request – along with numerous phone calls and emails  – 
and that of those letters, approximately 30 are in opposition, with concerns regarding setbacks, noise, and 
property values. He concluded by stating that staff supports the requested Special Use Permit, subject to 
the stipulations listed in the staff report. The stipulations include compliance with the setbacks and other 
portions of the adopted “Wind Ordinance”. 
 
Dale Osborn, President of Disgen spoke about his company, the project, and jobs it would create.  He 
noted that the project includes 207 individual landowners who have signed up as a part of the project.  He

stated that they are trying to qualify for federal grants and tax credits  which require that 5% o f the funds 
be committed by the end of 2011 , and that the project be operational by the  end of 2012. They anticipate 
12-14 million dollars will be spent in the local area, and they hope to take the power produced to the Four 
Corners area for distribution. In regard to setbacks , he stated that the requested setbacks are needed to 
ensure the viability of the project. He also noted that most property owners are out of state, that there are 
no public services available in the area, and that there are no existing adjacent residences. 
 
Chairman Lawson called for the public to speak in regard to the project.  Steen Hviid was generally in 
favor of the project a s long as the project complies with the Wind Ordinance in regard to both physical 
and sound setbacks requirements. Charles Lowery, Joseph Barreras, Terry Shaff, Jerry Paschal , and 
Bill Jeffers all spoke in favor of the project. Kay Turner spoke in favor  of the project, as long as the 
setbacks as noted in the Wind Ordinance are upheld . Kathy Hemenway , P resident of A RENA, also 
stated her support of the project as long as the Wind Ordinance setbacks are maintained. Dannette Weiss, 
with the Arizona Game & Fish Department, handed out charts to the Commissioners noting the positive 
economic impact that wildlife has on the local economy, and noted that the Game & Fish Department will 
make their full assessment of the project upon completion of studies. No one from the public came 
forward to speak in opposition of the project. 
 
Wendell DeCross made a motion to recommend to the Board of Supervisor s approval of Case #11 -42 
with the supporting stipulations  as proposed by staff , with a change to Stipulation 5 .c. to remove the 
requirement for a Power Purchase Agreement, to read “ Documentation confirming an Interconnection 
Agreement (or equivalent documentation). ” Chuck Teetsel  seconded the motion.  The motion was 
approved unanimously (7-0).  
 
Greg Loper stated to the Commission and public that this item would go before the Board of Supervisors 
on December 13, 2011, at 9:00 a.m. 
 
 
Item # 2 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF  MINUTES FROM THE COM MISSION HEARING OF  
August 18, 2011.   
 
Chuck Teetsel made a motion to approve the minutes as presented. Bob Hall seconded the motion.  The 
motion was approved unanimously (7-0). 
 
 
Item # 3 – COMMISSIONER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF:  
 
Bob Hall  reported on activities in the City of Winslow, including the Renaissance Project (Route 66)  
project and the private prison project. 
 
Greg Loper addressed the Commission and handed out information regarding Pot Ash Mining in the area 
around the Petrified Forest National Park,  and the significant impact these mines could have in creating 
jobs in our County.  He and Bill Fraley also touched on other upcoming projects, Bill Fraley’s retirement 
at the end of January, 2012, and the County Strategic Planning process.   
  
With there being no further business before the Planning and Zoning Commission, a motion was made to 
adjourn the meeting by Ruth Ann Smith .  Bob Hall seconded the motion.  The motion was approved  
unanimously (7-0). 
 
The meeting adjourned at 8:29 p.m.

CALL TO ORDER 
 
 
Chairman Chuck Teetsel  called the meeting of the Navajo County Planning & Zoning 
Commission to order at 6:04 p.m., and led the Pledge of Allegiance.  Mr. Teetsel explained the 
housekeeping rules for the benefit of the public in attendance. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE #14 -10: SPECIAL USE PERMIT:   Discussion and possible action on a 
request by HNZ Holding, LLC for a Special Use Permit for a 196 ft. Meteorological Tower – 
Weather Station, on APN:  110 -05-001D, a.k.a. a portion of Section 22, Township 15 North, 
Range 23 East of G&SRM in the Snowflake area. 
 
Trent Larson presented the Staff Report and gave an overview of the project. 
 
Applicant Comments:  Traegen Knight spoke as a representative of the applicant.  He stated 
that the tower is solely for data collection and HNZ Holding currently has no plans to further 
develop the land.  Chairman Teetsel  asked how many Met Towers Mr. Worsley had been 
involved with.  Mr. Larson stated that he would have to double check, but believed it w as about 
five.  Mr. Worsley turned the property over to HNZ and failed to mention the permitting process.  
He is not currently involved with the property   Chairman Teetsel asked if any of Mr. Worsley’s 
other towers had exceeded their permitted time limit.  Mr. Larson said he would check on that 
for the commission. 
 
In Favor:  None.

Opposed:  Del Lewis submitted a verbal request that the Commission table this issue because 
the long-term effects are uncertain.  He believes the unlit tower is a danger to low -level training 
flight routes.  Chairman Teetsel stated that the Commission has dealt with many tow ers and is 
required to stay within the FAA guidelines.  Mr. Lewis asked if the Air Base at Kirkland, NM had 
been notified.  Mr. Larson stated that Kirkland Air Base had not been notified, but Game & Fish 
had been.  He said that it would not be an issue to notify the Kirkland Air Base.  Tom Sanguigni 
stated that Mr. Worsley is now a State Senator.  Christine Clemente asked if the tower was 
erected without a purpose.  She was also concerned that the Applicant did not check to make 
sure no permits were require d before erecting the tower.  She felt that the Commission should 
assign a penalty before approving the Permit.  Chairman Teetsel stated that it was not the duty 
of the Commission to assign penalties.   Ms. Clemente  shared her belief that retroactively 
approving this permit sets a dangerous precedent.  Bill White stated that he felt this had been 
snuck in under the table.  He does not want wind towers next to his property  because he fears 
they will cause psychological harm to his animals .  The data collected  should not be accepted 
because it was collected illegally.  He does not believe this company is capable of managing 
any possible future wind farms.  Mr. Larson  explained that the company does not currently 
have any future plans for the property.  Commissioner Smith asked the applicant when the 
tower was constructed.  Mr. Knight stated that the tower was put up in March. 
 
Motion to approve:  Commissioner Murph made the motion to approve with the conditions as 
stated by staff with the additional condition that Staff check with Kirkland Air Base.  The motion 
was seconded by Commissioner DeCross.  The vote was ( 6 – 0) to recommend approval to 
the Board of Supervisors. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE #14 -12: SPECIA L USE PERMIT:   Discussion and possible action on a 
request by AT&T for a Microwave Communications Facility with a self -supporting tower not to 
exceed 190’ and a12’X20’ equipment shelter on APN:  109 -23-007, a.k.a. a portion of Section 
34, Township 18 North, Range 21 East of G&SRM in the Holbrook area. 
 
Trent Larson presented the Staff Report and gave an overview of the project. 
 
Applicant Comments:  Lyndon Vidrine spoke on behalf of the applicant.  He stated that this 
tower would provide better voice and data service to the I -40 corridor.  The company has 
reviewed the terms and is ready to accept them.  Chairman Teetsel wanted to know why the 
tower wouldn’t be lit since it was so close to the local airport.  Mr V idrine stated that the 
company was willing to go along with any recommendations the FAA made.  Commissioner 
DeCross asked if there was a time limit on a Special Use Permit.  Mr. Larson stated that time 
limits can be applied, but are not necessary.  Since this structure is more or less permanent 
Staff recommends a permanent, transferable Permit.  Commissioner DeCross stated that he 
would like to see the company notify the County if they are no longer using the  tower, so that it 
can be removed.  Mr. Vidrine stated that the company would agree to that request.  Mr. Larson 
suggested that the conditions be amended to state that the applicant shall notify Navajo County 
Public Works , or successor department,  if the t ower is no longer in use.  Chairman Teetsel  
suggested the conditions also be amended to require the tower be lit regardless what the FAA 
requires.  The tower shall follow FAA recommendations for lighting on a 200’ tower even though 
it is 10’ shy of that height. 
 
In Favor:  None. 
 
Opposed:  None.

ITEM #3 – CASE #14-14:  SITE PLAN REVIEW:   Discussion and possible action on a request 
by City of Winslow for a Site Plan Review to operate a Kennel for their Animal Care Facility, on 
APN:  103 -18-006A, a.k.a. a portion of Section 25, Township 19 North, Range 15 East, of 
G&SRM in the Winslow area. 
 
Trent Larson presented the Staff Report and gave an overview of the project. 
 
Applicant Comments:  Sandra Philips spoke on behalf of the applicant.  She stated that the 
ranch access road in the area will continue to have access and the road will be improved with 
culvert installation.  Mr. Larson stated that he has heard comments about the turn off of the 
access road being too tight for a vehicle pulling a t railer.  The applicant sated that there would 
be no problems softening the turn to accommodate a trailer. 
 
In Favor:  None. 
 
Opposed:  Charles Engels stated that if the applicant would move the proposed site 200’ to 
the North the City could avoid additional construction on the road.  He is concerned that he is 
losing access to his road.  Ms. Phillips stated that the current location had been proposed d ue 
to the topography of the property.  If the site was moved to the North there would be flooding 
concerns.  Commissioner Lawson asked if it was absolutely necessary to impede Mr. Engels 
road access.  Ms. Phi llips stated that that would require more resear ch, planning, and 
consultation with ADOT.  Chairman Teetsel  asked Mr. Larson  what power the Commission 
had in this case.  Mr. Larson stated that the Commission’s duty was to decide if the proposed 
site is a good, legal use of the land and would not be a nuisance.  Chairman Teetsel suggested 
that Mr. Engels consult with ADOT about his concerns.  Elaine Griffin-St. Germain stated that 
she lived 300’ from the proposed site and wanted to know about noise control measures that will 
be taken.  Ms. Phillips stated that she was unaware of exactly what the noise control plan was, 
only that there was one in place. 
 
Motion to approve:  Commissioner Smith made the motion to approve with the conditions as 
stated by staff.  The motion was seconded by Commissioner DeCross.  The vote was ( 6 – 0) 
to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors.

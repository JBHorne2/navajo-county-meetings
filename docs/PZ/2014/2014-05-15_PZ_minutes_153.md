CALL TO ORDER 
 
 
Chairman Chuck Teetsel called the meeting of the Navajo County Planning & Zoning 
Commission to order at 6:01 p.m., and led the Pledge of Allegiance.  Mr. Teetsel explained the 
housekeeping rules for the benefit of the public in attendance. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE #14-08:  SPECIAL USE PERMIT:  Discussion and possible action on a 
request by Sun Valley Recycling for a Special Use Permit to operate a recycling center on APN:  
105-09-167B, a.k.a. a portion of Section 25, Township 18 North, Range 21 East, of G&SRM in 
the Holbrook area. 
 
Trent Larson presented the Staff Report and gave an overview of the project. 
 
Applicant Comments:  Glen Holden spoke on behalf of the applicant.  Mr. Holden has 
spoken with the ADEQ.  The recycled materials will be inside buildings.  The owners have filed 
intent to construct with the ADEQ.  Chairman Teetsel asked what kind of materials the center 
would accept.  Mr. Holden responded that cars and scrap metal would be accepted.  Fluids will 
be drained and placed in safe storage before metal is stored.  Mr. Holden is aware of the 
dangerous chemicals that are involved in this process.  Chairman Teetsel asked if either Mr. 
Holden or the business owner had ever worked in this field before.  Mr. Holden stated that they 
were not currently involved in this field, but had done similar work in the past.  Mr. Holden also 
indicated that the owner plans to have a well installed, to provide water for fire suppression.  
Commissioner Lawson asked if the property would be visible from the interstate.  Mr. Holden

responded that the property is 3.5 miles from I-40 and is not visible from the roadway.  Trent 
Larson pointed out that the railroad tracks are between the property and the river, nullifying the 
floodplain issue.  Commissioner DeCross asked if the railroad owned their right of way, and 
was concerned if they had been notified.  Mr. Larson responded that Navajo County did not 
notify the railroad company because they do not show up in the Assessors records as a 
property owner.  The property owner has been in contact with the railroad to inquire about the 
potential of building a railway spur in the future, so they are aware of the proposed project.  
Chairman Teetsel asked what the potential clean-up cost would be if the business were to shut 
down.  Mr. Larson stated that the cost couldn’t be determined in advance.  Commissioner 
Murph stated that he believes it would be a good idea to include the stipulation of a clean-up 
bond.  Commissioner Slone stated that in working with ADEQ the property owner would be 
required to insure that no contaminates enter the surrounding land or water so there would be 
no need for a clean-up bond.  Commissioner Smith asked how many jobs the site would 
provide.  Mr. Holden stated that there would be 3 to 4 employees.  Commissioner DeCross 
asked why the City of Holbrook was notified.  Mr. Larson stated that they were notified as a 
courtesy. 
 
In Favor:   
 
Opposed:   
 
Jeff Gray owns the adjacent property and is not opposed to the project as long as it does not 
affect the environment.  He made the Commission aware that during monsoon season that area 
does temporarily flood.  Trent Larson deferred flood control questions to County Engineer Bill 
Bess. 
 
Bill Bess stated that the run-off assessment would be completed by a Civil Engineer.  The area 
would need to be assessed while flooded to determine the depth.  A recommendation could 
then be made on the permits that the building elevation be raised accordingly.  Upon his site 
visit, Mr. Bess saw no evidence of flooding. 
 
Commissioner DeCross asked if Mr. Something had a home on his property and if his 
residence ever flooded.  Mr. Gray said that occasionally there was temporary flooding on the 
property, but that his home had never flooded. 
 
Chairmen Teetsel asked Mr. Holden when he was first on the property.  Mr. Holden was first 
on the property about 2 years ago.  Mr. Holden has not been on the property during monsoon 
season. 
 
Motion to approve:  Commissioner DeCross made the motion to approve with the conditions 
as stated by staff.  The motion was seconded by Commissioner Slone.  The vote was (8 – 0) 
to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors.

ZC 23-002 PORTER CREEK RV PARK, DISTRICT IV:  Consideration of a request by BRON Industries LLC, for a Zone Change from R-3 to C-R to allow for a 54-space RV park. The property is 4.65 acres in size, and located north of Pinetop/Lakeside, along Porter Mountain Rd. APN# : 212-12-052F, T9N, R22E, S14 of the GSRM.  Owner:  Ikd Investment, LLC.  Agent:  Bruce Ironside.

Mr. Tyler Richards, Planner I, presented the item to the Commission with a quick overview of the area, including a vicinity map, and the scope of the Zone change.  Mr. Richards also shared a zoning map showing the surrounding zonings, explaining that the Zone Change would not create spot Zoning due to comparable surrounding zoning types, and fits in well with the Comprehensive Plan. Mr. Richards said that a petition with 24 signatures was received, and two comments against the zone change which focused on traffic concerns, water usage, and property value decline.  With all findings of fact, Staff reccomends approval of the Zone Change.

The Applicant, Bruce Ironside, addressed the Commission. Mr. Ironside introduced his company's experience with creating RV parks, having done several within the City of Show Low over the past 30 years. Mr. Ironside stated that he wished to develop this RV park as he saw that there was a need for temporary housing.

Chair Latham  asked Mr. Ironside if the housing would be permanent, and how long a typical stay would be.

Mr. Ironside said that they would be willing to sign up to a 21-day lease, but did not have a maximum limit for staying.  Mr. Ironside further clarified that there would not be anything affixed to the ground, and would not allow anything that was not on wheels, so 5th wheels and trailers.  Mr. Ironside also stated they would not allow anything over ten years of age, nor would they allow for unsightly junk in the lots, but that he did not envision long-term residence.

Public Comments:

None in favor.

Comments against:

Existing Water District could not support the project.

In addition to the comments opposed, Mr. Moreno requested that if the project be approved, that a solid eight-foot wall be erected as shielding.

SUP 23-017 PORTER CREEK RV PARK, DISTRICT IV:  Consideration of a request by BRON Industries LLC for a Special Use Permit to construct an RV Park with 54 total spaces. The property is 4.65 acres in size, located north of Pinetop/Lakeside, along Porter Mountain Rd. APN# : 212-12-052F, T9N, R22E, S14 of the GSRM. Owner:  Ikd Investment, LLC. Agent:  Bruce Ironside.

Mr. Tyler Richards, Planner I, addressed the Commission. Mr. Richards gave a brief history and scope of the project, which was a continuation from the previous item, a Zone Change from R-3 Multifamily Residential zoning to Commercial-Residential. This would put the project in compliance with this Special Use Permit (SUP). Mr. Richards also noted that an SUP was issued to this parcel previously for a 52 space RV Park, but the permit had lapsed as the recession had hindered development.  Mr. Richards then read out the recommendations, adding that if approved, the Navajo County Planning and Development Services staff would be reviewed and inspected every five years.

Chair Latham asked about the well that existed on the land, and if the project intended to hook into that well, and not into the Water District.  Mr. Richards confirmed that was the Applicant's intent.

Commissioner Rogers asked if there was an existing fire hydrant, or if they were planning on hooking one up to the well for their fire compliance.  Mr. Richards was unsure of fire hydrants, but did say that the Timber Mesa Fire Department had given a Will-Serve Letter to the  applicant, and the Fire Department would have to approve and sign off on their fire plans before construction during the building process.

The Chair opened for public comments. There were no new comments from the public, but they did reiterate the comments they had on the previous item:

Comments against:
Frank Moreno, Patricia Volp

Project would have a negative impact on the community.

Existing Water District could not support the project.

Project would lower property values.

Drainage issues with site.

Increase in traffic that would be dangerous to children,

Noise

Lack of support from Fire Department.

Request for 8-foot masonry fencing.

Chair Latham asked Applicant, Bruce Ironside, to elaborate on the issues brought up in the previous item.

Mr. Ironside that it was their intent to develop the well to include their fire systems, with a tank and a booster to meet fire flow requirements.  He expressed willingness to include hookups for the Water District with their development to benefit the neighborhood.  Mr. Ironside also noted that there was potential for the Water District to purchase and take possession of the well in the future if they desired.
Mr. Ironside also commented on the fencing request, stating that he had a preference for wood and metal fencing over block walls, but would be open to block if required.  Mr. Ironside also noted that they proposed the six feet to meet the code.  Mr. Ironside also stated there would be landscaping along the front of the project, and that they would use a solid fence to help dampen sound. Lighting would also follow County Ordinance.

Commissioner Booher made a motion to recommend approval, with the addition of a traffic study recommended by staff. Commissioner Howe seconded the motion.
Motion carried Unanimously, 8 - 0 .

SUP 23-015 AZ11-166 BACKHOE WIRELESS COMMUNICATION FACILITY, DISTRICT III:   Consideration of a request by Pinnacle Consulting, Inc. for a Special Use Permit to allow for the construction of a 100' monopole wireless communication tower and associated facilities in the Heber area. APN # 206-34-005, T12N, R17E, S20 of the GSRM . Owner: Charles and Teena Parham. Agent: John Katsiris.

Sondra Starke, Planner I,  presented the item to the Commission, and gave a brief overview of the project.  The Zoning is currently Light Industrial (IND-1), and the use is commercial.  Ms. Starke shared visuals with the Commission that detailed the surrounding area of the project, located directly off of Highway 277.  Ms. Starke noted that the proposed use would be in line with the current uses in and around the surrounding area, and there would be a new easement for access. Ms. Starke noted that as this was not a residential area as defined in the Ordinance, and the minimum setback would be a 1-1 height, and this project meets that minimum requirement.  Ms. Starke also shared visual simulations of the tower with the Commission, with views off of Highway 277.  Ms. Starke stated that Staff had not received any comments for or against the project, and with all the findings of fact, staff recommended approval of the project.

There was no further questions or discussion, and there were no public comments.

Chair Latham motioned to recommend approval of the Special Use Permit. Commissioner Rogers seconded. Motion passed unanimously, 8 - 0.

SUP 23-018 THELONIUS SOLAR PROJECT, DISTRICT III: Consideration of a request by Thelonius Solar, LLC for a Special Use Permit to allow for the construction and operation of a 112.5 MWdc solar energy generation facility, 20 MW Battery Energy Storage System, and associated facilities on approximately 625 acres located in the Snowflake area.  APN # 202-27-006, T14N, R21E, S07 of the GSRM.  Owner: Flake Ranch, LLC.  Agent: Thelonius Solar, LLC.

Mr. Cody Cooper, Planning Mananger, presented the item to the Commission, sharing an overview of the project, it's location and proximity to the town of Snowflake.  Mr. Cooper then went over the details of the site plan, noting that it would be subject to change as the request was for the boundary and capacity of the project. There would be battery storage, laydown areas, and various other amenities.  Mr. Cooper stated that the Applicant had already completed a number of reviews, including cultural, wildlife, stormwater, and had also worked with the State Fire Marshall for their fire safety plans. Staff has not received any comments for or against the project, and reccomended approval as it met the goals of the Comprehensive plan.
The Applicant's representative, Hanna Alford also addressed the Commission with a brief presentation that went over the capacity and projected output of the project, as well as the benefits to the community the project would bring Ms. Alford touched on the outreach to Arizona Game and Fish for potential endangered species and plants life, and the Public outreach that they had done for the project.

Commissioner Rogers asked if their company had a Power Purchase Agreement with Arizona Public Service (APS).

Ms. Alford stated that they were adjacent to an APS transmission line, and roughly a mile away from an APS substation, and were currently in their interconnection queue and once they obtained an interconnection agreement, there was an option for a Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act (PURPA) contract, and were actively bidding in an APS Request for Proposal (RFP).

Commissioner Rogers asked about the capacity of the grid, Ms. Alford stated that a PURPA contract would require their company to take on the responsibility of upgrading the grid to accommodate the output of their project.

There were no public comments, and no further discussion.

Commissioner Booher motioned to recommend approval of the Special Use Permit. Vice-Chair Smith seconded.  Motion passed unanimously, 8 - 0.

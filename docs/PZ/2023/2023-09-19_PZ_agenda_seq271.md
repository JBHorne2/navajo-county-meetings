AGENDA

Pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County, the Board of Adjustment of Navajo County, and to the General Public that Navajo County will hold a Work Session on SEPTEMBER 19, 2023, beginning at 1:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, 100 East Code Talkers Drive, Navajo County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission and Board of Adjustment will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call.

Commissioners and Board Members will participate in the meeting in-person in the Board of Supervisors Chambers in Holbrook, or via Zoom.  Limited seating will be available to the public.

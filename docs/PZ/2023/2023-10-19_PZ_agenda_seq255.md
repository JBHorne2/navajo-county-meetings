AGENDA

Pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on OCTOBER 19, 2023, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, 100 East Code Talkers Drive, Navajo County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call.

Commissioners will participate in the meeting in-person in the Board of Supervisors Chambers in Holbrook, or via Zoom.  Limited seating will be available to the public. Commissioners, staff, and members of the public may call and participate in the meeting by following these steps:

PROJECT NUMBER:  TXT-A 23-004  AMENDMENT TO ARTICLE 25 – OUTDOOR 
LIGHTING: Consideration of text amendment to Article 25, Section 
2519 of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance, regarding light 
pollution, and effective ways to prevent light trespass. 
  
 
 
 
BACKGROUND/HISTORY: 
 
Navajo County currently has an adopted Lighting Ordinance, but it has not been amended or revised 
since 1997. In the twenty-six years since its last revision, lighting practices and trends have changed, 
and many other Arizona counties have followed suit in updating their lighting ordinances 
accordingly. The update includes language that makes the overall Ordinance easier to understand, 
addresses light pollution, and  offers provisions that allow for enforcement against  complaints of 
light trespass, which have become more common in recent years. Staff is proposing a Zoning 
Ordinance Text amendment which will allow for the revision of the Light ing Ordinance to better 
comply with industry-recommended lighting standards. 
 
 
STAFF ANALYSIS: 
 
Currently, the focus of the Navajo County Lighting Ordinance is to prevent light from projecting 
upward into the night sky, which has detrimental effects on astronomical observations. There are 
few, if any, provisions pertaining to light trespass and pollution in general, however. Additionally, 
some of the language used, as well as lack of defined terms, may result in unintended violations or 
leave Code Enforcement without avenues to pursue violations. In the past seven months, Code

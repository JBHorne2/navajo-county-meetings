NAVAJO COUNTY 
PLANNING & ZONING COMMISSION 
May 16, 2019 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the 
Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that 
the Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on March 21, 
2019, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo 
County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission 
will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote to go into executive session on any item, which will not be open to 
the public. 
 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE #19-24 ZONE CHANGE, DISTRICT V:   Discussion and possible action on a 
request by TST Holdings LLC for a Zone Change from existing Special Development (SD) known 
as Starlight Ridge Estates Unit III to A-General on 20.26 & 32. 10 acres known as APN 212-05-
007K and 212-05-007L, respectively, a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 East, Section 8 in 
the Show Low area. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE#19-25 ZONE CHANGE, DISTRICT III: Discussion and possible action on a 
request by Elva & Mary Dreos for a Zone Change, from existing R U-20 zoning on a 20.15 acre 
parcel to RU-5 zoning on APN 403-70-013B, a portion of Township  14 North, Range 23 East, 
Section 20 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Concho area.   
 
ITEM #3 – CASE #19-26 SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV   : Discussion and possible action 
on a request from Stephanie Crain to operate a residential grou p home within a 0.98 acre lot;  
APN 409-10-223, a portion of Township 10 North, Range 20 East, Section 13 of the Gila and Salt 
River Meridian in the Timberland Acres area.  Removed by Staff 
 
ITEM #4 – CASE 19-27 SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT II: Discussion and possible action 
on a request from Justin Miller to expand the existing Mobile H ome park on 5.0 acres on APN 
103-30-017C, a portion of Township 19 North, Range 16 East, Sec tion 20 of the Gila and Salt 
River Meridian in the Bushman Acres area.   Postponed by applicant 
 
ITEM #5 – CASE 19-28 SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT V: Discussion and possible action 
on a request from the Town of Pinetop Lakeside to construct and operate a recycling center on a 
1 acre parcel that is zoned A-General APN 212-38-020, a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 
East, Section 35 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Pinetop area.

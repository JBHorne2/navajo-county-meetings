ITEM #1 – CASE # 19-65 SPECIAL USE PERMIT AMENDMENT, DISTRICT V:  
Discussion and possible action on a request by Whitney Beall for a Special Use Permit 
Amendment to allow for the construction of canopy coverage for 103 already existing RV 
spaces on a portion of APN 212-18-076 & 212-18-077, a portion of Township 9 North, 
Range 22 East, Section 22 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Lakeside area. 
 
Mr. Cooper presented the item to the board, noting the area was off Rainbow Lake Drive, 
adding that there were two S.U.P.’s already issued to the properties.  The applicant is 
looking to update the Permits so that they can provide canopies for the existing lots. 
The height limit is 30 feet for any structure in the area. Mr. Cooper also pointed out that 
updating existing commercial facilities to meet public needs is encouraged by the 
Comprehensive Plan. Mr. Cooper showed where the postings were located and said that 
the existing Permit resolutions will remain in full effect, including the requirements that all 
building permits be acquired, and drainage studies be done. He noted that the applicant 
was available to address the board if they had any questions. 
 
Chairman Teetsel asked if the applicant would like to speak.

<!-- page 2 -->
Applicant, Whitney Beall, greeted the board and stated U-haul is responding to client 
requests as well as looking to provide better protection for their clients’ property by adding 
coverage.  
 
Commissioner Howe asked how and if their structures were to be engineered for snow 
and wind loads.  The applicant replied that they worked with their structural engineer 
looking at ground samples, and that they had provided initial designs in their application.  
 
Commissioner Shields  and the Chairman asked if coverage was just for RV’s. The 
Applicant said it would be for items on trailers, like boats, as well as RV’s. 
 
Chairman Teetsel asked about the current conditions of the lots. The applicant replied 
that it was like a parking lot, and that the area would remain the same, with the addition of 
canopies for shade and shelter. 
 
The Chairman asked if they had submitted a drainage study with the application, to which 
Ms. Phillips said that Staff would require such studies as part of the permitting process 
with the building department.  
 
The Chairman  then asked where the retention or detention basin was located.  The 
applicant pointed out where it was, in the southwest side of the northmost property. Mr. 
Teetsel asked how it held up with the last rainstorm.  The applicant replied that it had held 
up very well, even with the previous bad snowstorm from earlier in the year. 
 
Commissioner Howe if the lot was paved asphalt. The applicant replied that it was not. 
With no more questions from the Commission, Chairman Teetsel  asked if there was 
anyone else in the audience to speak in favor of or against the application. 
 
Richard Smith approached to speak, noting that he was neither for nor against the project 
but had a concern to mention before the Commission.  He said that the retention/detention 
basin has, in his opinion, a flaw that lets runoff leak onto his property where it pools and 
is concerned that the roofing would increase the issue. He asked that the Commission 
apply a stipulation to correct the drainage from the basin to prevent damage to his 
property. Commissioner DeCross asked him to point out where his property was, which 
was shown to be to the northwest of the basin the applicant pointed out.  
 
Commissioner Howe asked the applicant if the basin was a retention basin or a detention 
basin and the applicant stated it was a detention basin. Commissioner Howe  then 
commented that he understood that it wasn’t meant to stop the flow but to slow it. Ms. 
Phillips confirmed Commissioner Howe’s statement and noted that the drainage 
requirements would be addressed in the construction permitting phase. Chairman 
Teetsel asked who determines what issues require permits, and Ms. Phillips said that she 
and the County Engineer were the ones to determine the need, and that she would walk 
the site.  
 
Commissioner DeCross commented that he knows that there is a vast need for covered 
storage for RV’s in the area. With there being no further discussion, the Chairman called 
for a motion.   
 
Commissioner DeCross made a motion to recommend Approval with stipulations to the 
Board.  Commissioner Howe seconded the motion.  Motion carried 6 - 0

ITEM #2 – WORK SESSION: The purpose of this Work Session will be to discuss possible 
amendments to the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance: A) Review of what is considered a 
subdivision within the A-General Zoning District; B) How setbacks should be defined for 
accessory buildings; C) Distances necessary for driveway separations; D) The potential 
addition of definitions and regulations for care facilities; E) Revising permissible operation 
location to be within structures for Adult Oriented Businesses; F) Addition of solar facility 
regulations. 
 
Work session held after adjourning the meeting. 
 
ITEM #3 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE COMMISSION HEARING 
OF 6/27/2019. 
 
Commissioner Smith pointed out some inconsistencies that needed to be corrected but 
was otherwise okay with approving the minutes provided the corrections were made. 
 
Commissioner DeCross made a motion to approve with the suggested corrections to 
the minutes.  Commissioner Smith seconded the motion.  Motion passed 6 - 0. 
 
ITEM #4 – COMMISSIONER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF: 
Commissioners may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on

Motion from Commissioner Smith to further amend the agenda so that the work

The Chairman then called for any public comments, to which there was none from the 
audience.

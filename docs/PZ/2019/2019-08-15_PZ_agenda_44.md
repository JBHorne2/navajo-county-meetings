PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
PLANNING & ZONING COMMISSION 
August 15, 2019 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the 
Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that 
the Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on August 15, 
2019, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo 
County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission 
will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote to go into executive session on any item, which will not be open to 
the public. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE # 19-65 SPECIAL USE PERMIT AMENDMENT, DISTRICT V: Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Whitney Beall for a Special Use  Permit Amendment to allow for 
the construction of canopy coverage for 103 already existing RV spaces on a portion of APN 212-
18-076 & 212-18-077, a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 Ea st, Section 22 of the Gila and 
Salt River Meridian in the Lakeside area. 
 
ITEM #2 – WORK SESSION:  The purpose of this Work Session will be to discuss possible 
amendments to the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance: A) Review of what is considered a 
subdivision within the A-General Zoning District; B) How setbacks should be defined for accessory 
buildings; C) Distances necessary for driveway separations; D) The potential addition of 
definitions and regulations for care facilities; E) Revising pe rmissible operation location to be 
within structures for Adult Oriented Businesses; F) Addition of solar facility regulations. 
 
ITEM #3 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE COMMISSION HEAR ING OF 
6/27/2019. 
 
ITEM #4 – COMMISSIONER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF: 
Commissioners may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on this agenda, 
or any other topic; and the Commission may direct staff to study or provide additional information 
on topics of the Commissions’ choosing. 
 
ITEM #5 – REPORT FROM STAFF TO THE COMMISSION: 
 
The Commission reserves the right to change the order of any Agenda item.

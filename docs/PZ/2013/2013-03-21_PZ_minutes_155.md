ITEM # 1 – CASE # 13-02 SPECIAL USE PERMIT FOR A METEOROLOGICAL TOWER.  
Request by Apache District Wind Farm, LLC, for a Special Use Permit to allow for placement of 
a meteorological tower with a maximum height of approximately 198 feet (60 meters) to assess 
the viability of a potential wind energy project.  Located on APN 110-02-004A; a.k.a. a portion of 
Section 14, Township 15 North, Range 20 East, G&SRM , Navajo County, Arizona; 
approximately 13 miles south of Holbrook. 
Trent Larson presented the staff report and gave an overview of the project. 
Applicant:  Judd Rogers with Juwi Wind was in attendance to answer any questions from the 
Commission or the public. 
 
In Favor: Jerry Somers of Concho Ocho Road  asked if the wind towers that are currently in 
place have been profitable.  He also asked if the proposed wind towers would provide po wer to 
Snowflake.  Staff does not have an answer for those questions.  Dannette Weiss of Arizona 
Game & Fish explained that they updated their wind guidelines in November of 2012.  They are 
requesting a minimum of 36 ” diameter aircraft markers on the gu y wires to make the guy wires 
more visible for pilots who fly low over those areas .  Commissioner Teetsel asked Mr. Larson 
how long the Commission has been approving met towers , and if the Planning &  Zoning 
Department checks to make sure that the towers are dismantled and the area restored to 
specifications once the original permit has expired.  Trent Larson said staff will research the 
data, but also explained that time extensions may be granted in c ertain circumstances.  Juwi

ITEM #2 – CASE # 13-04 SPECIAL USE PERMIT FOR SELF STORAGE FACILITY. Request 
by David Young, Concho Road Self Storage , for a Special Use Permit to allow for construction 
of a self-storage facility with indoor and outdoor storage.  Located on APN 403-81-016L; a.k.a. a 
portion of Section 31, Township 1 4 North, Range 2 3 East, G&SRM, Nava jo County, Arizona; 
approximately 5 miles east of Snowflake. 
 
Trent Larson presented the staff report and an overview of the project.   
 
Applicant:  Doug Brimhall spoke on behalf of the applicant, stating the applicant’s gratitude for 
the Commission’s consideration of the permit.   Mr. Brimhall said he would be available to 
answer any questions regarding the project. 
 
In Favor:  None 
 
Opposed:  Jerry Somers of 4932 Conch Ocho Road  stated that he was not notified of this 
permit application.  He asked if t he detention pond would be mosquito free.  Trent Larson  
responded that the water would only be allowed to stand for a maximum of 36 hours.  The 
designer, Tetra Tech, is familiar with County standards.  Bill Bess stated that the ordinance 
requires that the excess run -off created by the metal buildings be detained and released at a 
metered rate.  Trent Larson added that Mr. Somers did not receive a postcard because those 
mailings are sent to the address of record with the County Assessor’s Office.  The postcard was 
mailed to the address of record in California.  Mrs. Somers said she talked with staff, and has 
since corrected their mailing address with the County Assessor ’s Office.  Mr. Somers asked if 
the customer access areas of the property would be paved.  Doug Brimhall stated that it is the 
intention to release the detained water back into the natural area at the natural rate.  Jerry 
Somers asked how many storage units would be built, and if they would be cinderblock or wood 
structures.  Trent Larson explained, and Chairman Lawson reiterated that it is the goal of this 
meeting to determine if a storage facility is a good use of the land.  Jerry Somers asked if the 
slated chain link fence would make a lot of noise when the wind blows.  He also asked what the 
location of the security lighting would be and if it would be on 24 hours a day.  Trent Larson 
responded that there would be approximately a 400’ buffer between the fence and the property 
boundaries to Mr. Somer s parcel.  Mr. Larson explained the Navajo County lighting ordinance 
requires that the lighting be directed downward onto the property.  Stephanie Sack of 8800 
Stray Burro Trail stated that she did not receive a notice.  Trent Larson reported that her parcel 
is jus t outside the 300’ boundary.  Ms. Sac k compiled a list of 8 other storage units in the

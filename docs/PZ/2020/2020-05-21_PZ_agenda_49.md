ITEM #5 –SUP-A 20-001 HASHKNIFE ENERGY CENTER LLC, DISTRICT III:  A request for a 
Special Use Permit Amendment to expand the site area from the previously approved 3,000 acres 
to approximately 3,960 acres, an addition of 960 acres, to build and operate a 400 MW solar 
generation and storage facility.  The properties are located in the Joseph City area.  APN # 107-
01-023, 107-05-031C, 107-05-031D, 110-15-001D, & 110 -15-008, T18N R19E S30, 31, & 32, 
T17N, R19E, S05 & 06.  Owner: Hashknife Energy Center LLC.  Agent: James Williams. 
 
ITEM #6 –  SUP 20-001 AJ’s RV GETAWAY RV PARK, DISTRICT IV: A request for a Special 
Use Permit approval to allow construction and operation of a recreational vehicle (RV) park, 
consisting of 43 RV-camp sites and 30 spaces for RV and boat storage on 14.83 acres of land, 
located at the northeast corner of Highway 277 and Buckskin Road.  The property is zoned C -R 
(Commercial-Residential), addressed as 3537 Buckskin, in the Heber area.  APN#: 206-34-003A, 
T12N, R17E, S20, District IV. Owner: Aaron and Wilma James.  Agent: Painted Sky Engineering 
& Surveying, LLC.

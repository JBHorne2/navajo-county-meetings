ITEM #1 – SUP 20 -008 AZ3 KAYWOOD WIRELESS COMMUNICATION FACILITY, 
DISTRICT V:  A request for a Special Use Permit to construct a 105’ wireless 
communication facility, centrally located on APN# 212-20-147D, T9N, R22E, S22 of the 
GSRM.  Owner: Faith2Faith International Inc. – Richard Boen.  Agent: Nancy Smith.  
 
Cody Cooper, Planner II presented the case to the commission, detailing the location of 
the project as well as highlighting surrounding landmarks, focusing on the location of the 
tower on the property. The project follows the Comprehensive Plan and is compatible with 
the permissible uses of the zoning. The tower itself will be 105’ feet tall, of monopole 
“monopine” design enclosed in a 30’ x 30’ fenced in area, with an 8’ tall solid wooden 
fence surrounding the tower. 
 
Navajo County Engineering Staff requests that a 105’ non-buildable easement be provided 
around the base of the tower for public safety, that the applicant has not agreed to.  
Staff has received a mix of feedback from the public, and the applicant had done a public 
outreach meeting on March 12th before the pandemic came into full swing. The outcome 
of the public meeting was recorded to have four neighboring property owners attend with 
objections to the project but were pleased with the monopine design of the proposed tower

and withdrew their objections, with two stating support if they keep the monopine design 
shown. Another person was noted to not be in favor of the project. Given all of this, if all 
the conditions in the staff report are met staff recommends approval of this special use 
permit. 
 
 Applicant representative Nancy Smith spoke to the commission and described the call 
volume for the area, noting that the fire chief himself has sent in a letter that they had to 
“bump” the calls to Navajo County’s dispatch, delaying service. She read the rest of the 
letter that outlined the need for this upgraded tower. She then opened to the 
commissioner’s questions. 
 
Commissioner DeCross asked if she was stating that without this tower the safety and 
welfare of the community is subject to issues, to which Miss Smith replied yes. 
Chairman Teetsel called for further questions from the commission , staff, or in any in 
favor of the project, and finding none he called for comments against the project. 
 
Judy Kennedy stated that it seemed unfair that there was an existing tower on the property, 
to add in a second one  that would further obstruct her view, also noting that they were 
noisy. 
 
Chairman Teetsel called for a motion.  Commissioner DeCross made a motion to 
recommend Approval to the Board.  Commissioner John seconded the motion.  Motion 
carried, 3-1. (Commissioner Smith)

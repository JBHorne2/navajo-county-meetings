TP 25-003: STARLIGHT RIDGE ESTATES UNIT III SUBDIVISION TENTATIVE PLAT, DISTRICT V:  Consideration and possible adoption of Resolution 25-03P, approving/denying a Tentative Plat request by Starlight Ridge Unit III LLC, to allow for 49 single-family lots, 74 townhome units, and accompanying roads and infrastructure on 5 parcels totaling approximately 52.96 acres in the Wagon Wheel area.  APNs #:  212-05-007N, 212-05-007P, 212-05-007Q, 212-05-007R, 212-05-007S.  Owner:  Starlight Ridge Unit III LLC.  Agent:  Doug Brimhall

Mr. Tyler Richards, Planner II, introduced the item to the Commission.  Mr. Richards gave an overview of the project, showing the Commission its location relative to various landmarks, as well as the roads that would be dedicated to ingress and egress. He also went over its history briefly, the Board of Supervisors had approved a Master Development Plan Amendment in early 2025, which would allow for 49 single-family lots, and 74 town-home units. Mr. Richards then shared with the Commission the Tentative Plat of the project, and the 14 conditions listed in the staff report.

Mr. Richards stated that there had not been any comments for or against the project, and that a representative of the applicant was available to answer questions.

There was no further discussion of the Tentative Plat.

Commissioner Latham motioned to approve the Tentative Plat.  Commissioner Smith seconded.
Motion passed, 8 - 0 .

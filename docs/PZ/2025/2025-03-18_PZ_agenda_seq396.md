AGENDA

Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.02 and A.R.S. § 38-431(2), notice is hereby given to members of the Navajo Planning & Zoning Commission, the Board of Adjustment and to the General Public that a quorum of the Navajo County Planning & Zoning Commission and/or Board of Adjustment may be present for a work session on March 18th, 2025, at 1:00 PM in the Code Talkers Room, 100 East Code Talkers Drive, Navajo County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona, where no official action will be taken. Members of the Commission and Board Members will attend either in person or virtually.

Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Board Members' and Commission's discretion. The Commission and Board Members may vote to meet in closed executive session pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.03(A)(3) for legal consultation on any item listed on the following agenda.

A copy of the agenda background material provided to the Board Members and Commission, except items to be considered in executive session, is available for public inspection at the Navajo County website,
www.navajocountyaz.gov

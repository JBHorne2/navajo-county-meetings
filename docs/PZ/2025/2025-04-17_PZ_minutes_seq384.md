MDP-A 24-001 STARLIGHT RIDGE ESTATES III, DISTRICT V:  Consideration of a request by Steve Kohner for a Master Development Plan Amendment from an approved, approximately 80-unit, single family residential subdivision to be amended to allow for 49 single family lots, 74 townhome units, and changes to the roadways as well as open spaces in the Starlight Ridge Estates Special Development within the Wagon Wheel area. APN#: 212-05-007N, 212-05-007P, 212-05-007Q, 212-05-007R, 212-05-007S, T09N, R22E, S08 of the GSRM. Owner: TST Holdings. Agent: Steve Kohner.

Mr. Cody Cooper, Planning Manager, presented the project to the Commission. Mr. Cooper started by going over the location of the project, its landmarks, and the basic history of the project. This development has gone before the Commission previously and was tabled by the Board of Supervisors on August 27th, 2024, in order to make sure that water supply to the City of Show Low and traffic concerns for the development were acknowledged. The Applicant has now provided documentation for each of these requests and is returning to the hearing process. As a part of the tabling, the Board of Supervisors requested that this item be re-heard by the Navajo County Planning and Zoning Commission, prior to returning to their chambers. Mr. Cooper then went over the details of the primary and secondary access points along Wagon Wheel Lane. The Applicant has done a new traffic study and proposed access with substantial improvements to Wagon Wheel Lane, and the Applicant indicated that they would pave the internal roads of the project.

The proposed Master Development Plan Amendment meets all relevant goals and policies of the Navajo County Zoning Ordinance and the Navajo County Comprehensive Plan as described above. Planning and Development Services Staff have received thirteen (13) comments in opposition to this project as of August 7th, 2024. No further comments have been received since the tabling of this item at the Board of Supervisors Hearing.

Mr. Emery Ellsworth was attending as a representative for the Applicant but had no further comments to add to Mr. Cooper's presentation.

Vice-Chair Smith asked if this item would come back before the Commission, citing concerns over the increased density along with wildfires. Mr. Cooper confirmed that they would see this item again as a Tentative Plat, and there would be further studies completed at that stage. Mr. Cooper further explained the traffic study would look at the daily trips and the expected trips along the roads, as well as the current conditions of the road. The study would note any improvements needed, like additions of turn lanes or further improvements to the roadway for public use and Emergency Service use, we do require full fire approval. There is a will serve letter from Timber Mesa Fire Department for this project as is, but there will need to be full Fire Department approval before groundbreaking. Egress, hydrant location, turn radii and otherwise, and so forth, will have to be accepted by Navajo County Public Works, as well as Emergency Services for acceptable traffic levels for the Applicant to move forward appropriately.

Vice-Chair Smith asked about the project backing up against the Forest Service and Reservation land, still with concerns about emergency egress for fire safety given recent events with homeowners insurance eliminating service in the area due to the fires in California. Mr. Cooper said that they had reached out, but had not received comments from the Fort Apache Reservation, and assured the Commission that there were resources that Emergency Management could provide for residents that were seeing some loss in their coverage, but that insurance companies were not under the County's purview. There was also more egress to the north, which, while unable to be used for everyday traffic, was suitable for emergency access.

With no further discussion, and no comments from the public, the Chair called for a motion.

Commissioner Muder motioned to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors.  Commissioner Latham seconded.  Motion passed unanimously, 8 - 0 .

PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
PLANNING & ZONING COMMISSION 
June 21, 2018 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the 
Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that 
the Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on June 21, 2018, 
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo County 
Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission will attend 
either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote to go into executive session on any item, which will not be open to 
the public. 
 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE #18-11 ZONE CHANGE, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and possible action on a 
request by Kaiser Investment Inc. to change existing S.D. zoning on their 1.76 & 2.53 acre parcels 
also known as “A5” and “B8” respectively, within the Bison Ranch Final Plat to C -R zoning on 
APN 206-49-997E and APN 206-49-987, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 
34 in the Heber area. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE #18-14 SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT III: Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Joe Breeze to construct and erect a private, 1,400sq ft family mausoleum on APN 
111-02-005, a portion of Township 15 North, Range 16 East, Section 6  in the Chevelon Canyon 
area. 
 
ITEM #3 – CASE #18-06 SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV: Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Markin Allen Investments, LLC to construct a 199’ wireless communication facility 
on APN 208-03-001B, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 18 East, Section 11 in the Aripine 
area. 
 
ITEM #5 – ADDITION OF  LANDSCAPE REQUIREMENTS FOR  COMMERCIAL (C -R)/I 
INDUSTRIAL ZONING ALONG WHITE MOUNTAIN BOULEVARD IN THE ZONING 
ORDINANCE:  Item to be postponed to September hearing 
 
ITEM #6 – A-GENERAL MINIMUM LOT SIZE:  Item to be postponed to August hearing. 
 
ITEM #7 – YARD REGULATIONS FOR COMMERCIAL (C-R)/I INDUSTRIAL ZONING IN THE 
ZONING ORDINANCE:  Discussion and possible action on a request by Staff to clarify setback 
requirements on commercial properties.

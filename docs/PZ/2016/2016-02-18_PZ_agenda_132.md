AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the 
Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that 
the Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on February 18, 
2016, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo 
County Governmental Center, Holbrook , Arizona.  Members of the Commission 
will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote to go into executive 
session on any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 - CASE # 15 -34:  ZONE CHANGE – DISTRICT III:  Discussion and possible  
action on a request by Kompo Care owner Dusty DeCarlo for a Special Use Permit 
Amendment to expand facilities at the current Offsite Cultivation location to include a 
10,000 sq. ft. proces sing building along with an 8,000 to 10,000 sq. ft. green house.  
The original Special Use Permit consisted of a 6,000 sq.ft. metal building which will 
house the necessary equipment to process and refine the product.  A portion of Section 
35, Township 14N, Range 21E of the Gila and Salt River Meridian.

ITEM #3 – APPROVAL OF THE 2016 MEETING SCHEDULE: 
 
ITEM # 4 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE COMMISSION

ITEM #5 – COMMISSIONER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF:

additional information on topics of the Commissions’ choosing.

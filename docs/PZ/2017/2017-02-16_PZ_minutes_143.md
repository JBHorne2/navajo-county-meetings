ITEM #1 – CASE # 17-01:  SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT V:   Discussion and 
possible action on a request by JoDean E. Freese, Jr. & Renee A . Freese, for a Special Use Permit 
to operate a Custom Motorcycle & Repair Shop on APN 212-10-051,  Scotts Pine Meadows #2 
Lot 161, a.k.a. a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 East, S ection 9 in the Lakeside area.  
Chairman Teetsel  asked for staff comments.  Ms. Sandy Phillips  provided information and 
history on the property and the surrounding properties.  The pr oposed building was previously 
used as the Timber Mesa Fire Station which has been vacant for quite some time.  Staff considers 
this motorcycle shop to be similar to an auto mechanic shop whi ch requires a Special Use Permit 
for an R-3 zoning.  We received one letter of concern from a ne ighbor regarding the possible 
noise levels from the shop.  Engineering, Flood, and the County  Attorney, have no objection to 
the request.  Chairman Teetsel asked for questions from the commission for staff or the 
applicant.  Chairman Teetsel  asked if there is a noise ordinance in the area.  Ms. Phillips  
responded, there is no noise ordinance for Navajo County except  for Wind Turbines.  Chairman 
Teetsel invited Mr. Elkins to comment, Bryan Elkins, is with Long Reality, and is representing 
Mr. Freese.  One of the reasons they want to purchase the prope rty was in part, the exhaust 
system which is already in place.  He understands the concern o f noise but the vent system was 
what sold him on the building, and he made certain the system r emained in the building.  
Chairman Teetsel  asked if there were any further questions from the commission.

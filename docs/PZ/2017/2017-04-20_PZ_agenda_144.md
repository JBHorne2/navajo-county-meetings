AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Planning 
& Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Planning 
and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on April 20, 2 017, beginning at 
6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo County Governmental 
Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission will attend either in person 
or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote t o go into executive 
session on any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE #17-04 SITE P LAN AMENDMENT, DISTRICT V:   Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Homestead MHP LLC to Amend Site  Plan previously 
Approved in 1986 for apartments with open space to 11 lot mobil e and RV park within 
existing Homestead Rd Mobile Ranch on APN 212-31-130B a portion  of Township 9 
North, Range 22 East, Section 26 in the Lakeside area. 
 
ITEM #2 – ELECTION OF OFFICERS: 
 
ITEM #3 – WORK SESSION:  The purpose of this Work Session will be to discuss with 
possible action on possible amendments to the Navajo County Ordinances. A) A-General 
zoning to have minimum lot size of 1 acre when septic system is needed; B)Initiate $100 
fee for Flood Use Permit and/or any encroachment into floodplain; C) Review of proposed 
Land Division Ordinance wording;  D) Change definition of garage ; E)Landscaping 
requirements on Commercial; F) 5ft minimum side yard setback for accessory structures 
and not allowed in front of plane of principal residence. 
 
ITEM #4 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE COMMISSION HEARING 
OF OCTOBER 20, 2016, & FEBRUARY 16, 2017: 
 
ITEM #5 – COMMISSIONER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF: 
Commissioners may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on this 
agenda, or any other topic; and the Commission may direct staff  to study or provide 
additional information on topics of the Commissions’ choosing.

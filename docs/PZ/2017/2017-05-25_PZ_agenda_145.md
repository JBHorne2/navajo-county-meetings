AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Planning 
& Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Planning 
and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on May 25, 2017, beginning at 
6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo County Governmental 
Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission will attend either in person 
or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote to go into executive 
session on any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE #17-07 ZONE CHANGE, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Buster K Ranch, LLC to Change Zoning on 7.8 acres currently zoned A-
General to R1 -43 on a portion of APN 409 -24-001C a portion of Township 10 North, 
Range 21 East, Section 8 in the Linden area. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE #17-05 TENTATIVE PLAT, DISTRICT IV:   Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Buster K Ranch, LLC to Approve Tentative Plat subdividing 7.8 
acres into 4 lots on a portion of APN 409-24-001C a portion of Township 10 North, Range 
21 East, Section 8 in the Linden area. 
 
ITEM #3 – CASE #17-11 FINAL PLAT, DISTRICT IV:   Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Buster K Ranch, LLC to Approve Final Plat subdividing 7.8 acres into 4 
lots on a portion of APN 409 -24-001C a portion of Township 10 North, Range 21 East, 
Section 8 in the Linden area. 
 
ITEM #4 – CASE #17 -10 SPECIAL USE PERMIT, DISTRICT III:   Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Tiny Town Arizona, LLC to Approve Spe cial Use Permit 
to allow a “Glamping” Camp on APN 404-51-008 a portion of Township 11 North, Range 
23 East, Section 1 in the Concho area. 
 
ITEM #5 – AMEND DEFINITION OF SETBACK LINE IN THE ZONING ORDINANCE :  
Discussion and possible action on a request by Staff to change the definition.

AJORN MEETING 
 
 
ITEM #10 – WORK SESSION:  The purpose of this Work Session will be to discuss with 
possible action on possible amendments to the Navajo County Ordinances. A) A-General 
zoning to have minimum lot size of 1 acre when septic system is needed; B) Initiate $100 
fee for Flood Use Permit and/or any encroachment into floodplain; C) Review of proposed 
Land Division Ordinance wording; D) Change definition of garage; E)  Landscaping 
requirements on Commercial; F) 3ft minimum side yard setback for accessory structures 
and not allowed in front of plane of principal residence.

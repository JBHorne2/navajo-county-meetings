AGENDA

Pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.02(H), the public body will have physical access to the meeting place fifteen (15) minutes prior to the start of the meeting.
Pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the members of the Planning & Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the Public that the Planning and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on AUGUST 15, 2024, beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, 100 East Code Talkers Drive, Navajo County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call.

WATCH THE MEETING LIVE AT:   www.navajocountyaz.gov/660
All public comments will need to be made in person or in writing. Email Planning at  Planning@navajocountyaz.gov  OR call and leave a detailed comment at (928) 535-7155. If further accommodation is needed, please contact Planning twenty-four (24) hours prior to the Commission Meeting, so accommodation can be arranged. When leaving a comment, please identify the Planning and Zoning Commission meeting date, the agenda item number and title. Please leave your name and telephone number. Comments must be sent twenty-four (24) hours prior to the Commission meeting.

Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Commission's discretion. The Commission may vote to meet in closed executive session pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.03(A)(3) for legal consultation on any item listed on the following agenda.

A copy of the agenda background material provided to the Commission, except items to be considered in the executive session, is available for public inspection at the Navajo County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov

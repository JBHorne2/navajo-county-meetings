PLANNING & ZONING COMMISSION WORK SESSION

OCTOBER 17, 2024
5:00 p.m.

AGENDA

Pursuant to A.R.S. § 38-431.02 and A.R.S. § 38-431(2), notice is hereby given to members of the Navajo Planning & Zoning Commission and to the General Public that a quorum of the Navajo County Planning & Zoning Commission may be present for a work session on October 17 th , 2024, at 5:00 PM in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, 100 East Code Talkers Drive, Navajo County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona, where no official action will be taken. Members of the Commission will attend either in person or telephonically.

Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Board Members' and Commission's discretion. The Commission and Board Members may vote to meet in closed executive session pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.03(A)(3) for legal consultation on any item listed on the following agenda.

A copy of the agenda background material provided to the Board Members and Commission, except items to be considered in executive session, is available for public inspection at the Navajo County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov

SUP 24-003 AZ-5148 RED HAWK WIRELESS COMMUNICATION FACILITY:  Consideration of a request by Vertical Bridge for a Special Use Permit to allow for the construction of a 199' monopole wireless communication tower and associated facilities on a 49.48-acre parcel located in the Clay Springs area.  APN#:  208-18-003J, T11N, R19E, S23 of the GSRM.  Owner:  Marian L. Petersen.  Agent:  Shawn Sanchez.

Mr. Tyler Richards, Planner I, presented the item to the Commission. Mr. Richards shared some information about the general location of the tower, stating that it was not in a residential area as defined by the Zoning Ordinance for Cell Towers and would be using a 1 to 1 setback. Mr. Richards also showed the Commission photo simulations of what it would look like once constructed, from three different viewpoints. Staff received a letter from a member of the public, and Staff was able to alleviate some of their concerns prior to the hearing. The citizen did not say if they were still in opposition but appreciated Staff's response to their inquiry.

Mr. Richards stated that with all the findings of fact, Staff recommended approval of the Special Use Permit (SUP). Mr. Richards also noted that a representative of the applicant was present for questions.

Tiffany Passel, a State 48 Development Representative for the applicants Vertical Bridge, thanked the Commission and said she would be happy to answer any questions the Commission had.
Ms. Passel further gave some details regarding the improvements the tower would bring, especially for E911 uses, and that they were planning for collocation for additional carriers if they so desired.
Chair Rogers opened the Public Hearing portion, calling for anyone from the public to speak in favor.

Elizabeth Brewer spoke in favor of the project, noting that as of now not having cell service was of deep concern as she cared for her elderly mother, and saw the benefits it would have for the whole community of Clay Springs.

No one spoke against the project.

With no further questions or comments, Commissioner Muder motioned to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors. Commissioner Murph seconded the motion.
Motion passed unanimously, 7 - 0 . ( Commissioner Lister joined partway through the opening of this item.)

TXT-A 24-004 AMENDMENT TO ARTICLE 25 - WIRELESS COMMUNICATION FACILITIES:  Consideration of a text amendment to Article 25, Section 2521, regarding wireless communication facilities.

Mr. Cody Cooper, Planning Manager, introduced the Text Amendment to the Commission. Mr. Cooper gave a quick summary of the amendment, stating that they were cleaning up processes to ensure that both applicants and the public fully understood what was being presented. The change would require any variances for the 2 to 1 height setbacks in residential areas to be obtained prior to going to hearings for a Special Use Permit. (SUP)

Staff also recommended removing a few of the portions, one of which required certification of compliance with federal standards, as this was redundant and that they would be held to these standards regardless; Mr. Cooper then said that the requirement for notifying all agencies within the area would also be stricken, as that is done during the Pre-Application process by Staff.

Mr. Cooper then stated that the last amendment would be to allow wireless communications to be permitted by all government agencies, so as not to override authority by agencies that have more jurisdiction than the County.

Staff had not received any comments from the public about the text amendment, nor were there any members of the public present to comment.

Commissioner Muder confirmed with Mr. Cooper that recommended permissions for the Government agency towers would still follow County regulations, Mr. Cooper noted that commonly the towers were completely exempt, but that they would still work with and encourage their guidelines when possible.

With no further discussion, Commissioner Latham motioned to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors. Commissioner Murph seconded the motion.
Motion passed unanimously, 7 - 0 .

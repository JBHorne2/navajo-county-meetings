ZC 24-001 RIM COUNTRY RV PARK, DISTRICT V: Consideration of a request for a Zone Change from R-2 to Commercial-Residential (C-R) on an approximately 0.33-acre portion of an approximately 2.28-acre parcel, located in Lakeside, along Rainbow Lake Drive.
APN#: 212-18-035A, T09N, R22E, S22 of the GSRM.
Owner: Rim Country RVP LLC.
Agent: Gammage and Burnham.

Tyler Richards, Planner II, presented the item to the Commission. Mr. Richards gave a brief overview of the project, stating that this Zone Change is in reference to a triangular portion of Residential-2 (R-2) zoning that was located within the boundary of the project parcel, of which the remaining portion was zoned Commercial-Residential (C-R). This Zone Change will unify the parcel to prepare for the next step in the project and the following item, a Special Use Permit (SUP) to allow for expansion of the Rim Country RV Park.

Mr. Richards went on to state that staff did not receive any feedback in support of or against the project, and that Staff recommended approval of the Zone Change.

Commissioner Muder asked Mr. Richards how such a strange zoning formation was possible, Mr. Richards stated that Staff was unable to find a reason for this zoning to be like that, noting that it seemed to be left over from the overlay process that was never addressed or corrected.

With no comments from the public, and no further business to discuss, the
Chairman called for a motion.

Commissioner Latham motioned to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors with the stated recommendations.
Commissioner Muder seconded. Motion passed unanimously,
8 - 0 .

SUP 24-004 RIM COUNTRY RV PARK, DISTRICT V: Consideration of a request for a Special Use Permit to permit an RV Park with 38 existing spaces and an expansion to add a further 28 spaces, for a total of 66 spaces. The existing and expanded park will be approximately 4.76-acres in size, located in Lakeside, along Rainbow Lake Drive. APN#: 212-43-001, 212-18-035A, and 212-18-078C, T09N, R22E, S22 of the GSRM. Owner: Rim Country RVP LLC. Agent: Gammage and Burnham.

Mr. Tyler Richards, Planner II presented the item to the Commission. This Special Use Permit (SUP) is tied to the previous item, a Zone Change to unify the subject parcel as fully Commercial-Residential zoning. The project is currently legally in a nonconforming use as an RV park, and this SUP will bring the parcels into conformance with the Zoning Ordinance and expand the business. The Applicant also had a representative available to answer questions, and Staff recommended approval of the project.

Chair Rogers asked Mr. Richards to clarify access, Mr. Richards stated that there would not be improvements to the access road off of Highway 260, but that there would be improvements to the gated emergency access off of Rainbow Lake Drive, and the roadways inside the RV park would be gravel. Chair Rogers asked if this was a short-term rental RV park, Mr. Richards stated that he did not have information on how long the stays would be.

Commissioner Muder asked Mr. Richards to explain what a legally non-conforming use was. Mr. Richards explained that the parcel was in use as an RV park before the Zoning Ordinance went into effect, or "Grandfathered" in.

With no further discussion or comments from the public, Chair Rogers entertained a motion.

Commissioner Booher motioned to recommend approval of the SUP to the Board of Supervisors. Commissioner Latham seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously, 8 - 0 .

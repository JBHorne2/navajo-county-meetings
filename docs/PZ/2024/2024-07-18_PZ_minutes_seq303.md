MDP-A 24-001 STARLIGHT RIDGE ESTATES III, DISTRICT V:  Consideration of a request by Steve Kohner for a Master Development Plan Amendment from an approved, approximately 80-unit, single family residential subdivision to be amended to allow for 49 single family lots, 74 townhome units, and changes to the roadways as well as open spaces in the Starlight Ridge Estates Special Development within the Wagon Wheel area.         APN#:  212-05-007N, 212-05-007P, 212-05-007Q, 212-05-007R, 212-05-007S, T09N, R22E, S08 of the GSRM.  Owner:  TST Holdings.  Agent:  Steve Kohner.

Tyler Richards, Planner II, presented the item to the Commission. Mr. Richards started by showing the Commission a map of the vicinity and its distance from State Route 260. This project was an amendment to the original approved development, which allowed for a maximum of 80 single family dwellings, to a maximum of 49 single family dwellings and 74 townhome units. It will be developed in two phases, and has two access points along Wagon Wheel Lane, along with northern and southern access points connecting to Rim Road. Mr. Richards also shared some basic setback information for the proposed lots, comparing them to R-2, Multifamily Residential Zoning. Mr. Richards briefly went over the list of conditions and requirements, particularly for a traffic impact study, a master drainage plan, a sewer collection system design report, and a water distribution design report; In addition, that any requirements identified as a result of these studies will be required to be constructed or financially assured prior to construction. Mr. Richards said that there were 14 comments in opposition to the project, mostly concerned with traffic increases, property values, and drainage concerns. The Applicant's Engineer was available for questions.

Chair Rogers then opened for public comment, of which there was no one present to speak either in favor or against.

Commissioner Booher then asked for more information about the townhomes. Mr. Richards then clarified that there would be units that would share a wall, and that there would be four units per building, like apartments.

Mr. Cody Cooper, Planning Manager, also clarified that the Applicant was only seeking approval for the total number of units, but that number of units per building was open to change at the plating phase, provided the total number of units did not exceed those approved. Mr. Cooper further stated that the Applicant would be going before the Commission again for a Tentative Plat approval.

Chair Rogers asked about the history of the project, noting that it had been 18 years since the approval of the original project.

Chair Rogers then asked if there was a time limit on projects such as these. Mr. Richards explained that the condition 11 stated that the Applicant would need to apply for a Tentative Plat within two years or the Master Development Plan Amendment would become null and void, reverting to the original approval.

Chair Rogers then asked about the received public comments, regretting the lack of public participation, as the previous Commission meeting had been canceled due to lack of a quorum. Mr. Richards stated that many of the public were concerned about traffic impact and road conditions, along with the impact on property values and non-residential individuals disturbing the serenity of the area.

Commissioner Muder also asked about the roads, noting the current condition of Rim Road through the property is primitive at best.

Mr. Cooper explained Rim Road has been dedicated as a public Right-of-Way (ROW) and that Staff was requiring improvements on the road further to the south and the east to allow another connection into the subdivision to prevent some of the overloading traffic. The developer would be responsible for the side roads unless brought to the County standard so that the roads could be taken into the County's maintenance system.

Commissioner Muder asked what the standard was; Mr. Cooper answered that they would have to be paved.

Commissioner Muder then asked if they would have to follow the results of the traffic study, to which Mr. Cooper confirmed that the traffic study would dictate what improvements would need to be made.

Chair Rogers asked if the applicant's proposal included all the paving and to make sure the roads would be updated; Mr. Cooper confirmed that was correct. With no further questions,

Commissioner Booher made a motion to recommend approval to the Board of Supervisors. Commissioner Muder seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously, 6 - 0 .

TP 24-001 HIGH PINES ESTATES TENTATIVE PLAT, DISTRICT III/IV: Consideration of a request for a Tentative Plat to allow for a 100-lot single-family residential subdivision named High Pines Estates. The site is zoned Special Development (SD) on two parcels, which total 93.60-acres. APN# 208-03-002A & 002B, T11N, R18E, S03 & 09 of the GSRM. Owner: High Pines Estates LLC. Agent: Travis Barney.

Mr. Cody Cooper, Planning Manager, presented the item to the Commission. Mr. Cooper gave an overview of the project, noting its history before the Commission, and stating that all previous approvals had expired. Mr. Cooper also highlighted that there had been changes made to the Subdivision Ordinance; However, this project had been submitted prior to the implementation of the updated Ordinance and would not be held to the new requirements. Mr. Cooper talked briefly about the applicant's efforts to mitigate lot size concerns, including doing site perc testing for all parcels, stating that there would be nitrogen management implemented for the second phase, and that a well would be constructed. The applicant has worked with the Arizona Department of Transportation (ADOT) for their access off Highway 260. Mr. Cooper briefly mentioned the 24 conditions that Staff recommended. There were no comments for or against the project, and the Applicant and his Engineer were present to answer questions from the Commission.

The Chair opened for public comment. Mr. Doug Brimhall, the engineer, spoke in favor of the project, listing a few accomplishments, including working with the Bigler family in getting secondary access.
With no further comments in favor or against, the Chair closed the public comment period.

Commissioner Booher asked about the fire mitigation inside the subdivision, Mr. Brimhall detailed that they would have their own water improvement district and put a pump on the well to bring the pressure up to what was needed for the various hydrants in the subdivision.

Vice-Chair Smith noted several concerns, all of which centered around the density of the project, she also touched on the history of the project and noted that there were several things the applicant had not addressed, the most important being that there was insufficient documentation that gave permission to alter or improve Forest Service Road #332, or any clear indication of who would be maintaining Forest Service Road #332.

Vice-Chair Smith stated she felt this needed to be a fulfilled condition before Commission approval as it is an integral part of their development, proof of which they have been unable to produce to date despite multiple requests.
After discussion, the Commission's main concern was the lack of permissive documentation from the Forest Service allowing improvements to be made to Forest Service Road #332 for fire safety, especially when taking into consideration that equipment and personnel would be using the #332 road for fire access and suppression at the same time the Aripine area would be evacuating would also need to be considered as this could pose serious safety concerns.

Vice-Chair Smith made a motion to deny the Tentative Plat. Commissioner Howe seconded.
The motion tied, 3 - 3 . ( Commissioners Howe, Booher, Vice-Chair Smith voted in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Muder, John , and Chair Rogers , voted in opposition to the motion.) The motion died on the floor.

After some consultation, the Applicant stated they would provide the requested documentation within two months.

Commissioner Howe motioned to table the item until September 19th. Commissioner Muder seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously, 6 - 0 .
The item was tabled until the September 19th, 2024, Planning & Zoning Commission Meeting.

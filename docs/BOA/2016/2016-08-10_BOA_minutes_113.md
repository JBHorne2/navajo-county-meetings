 Chairman Bill Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of Adjustment 
to order and he then led the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
Chairman Arendell welcomed the staff and applicant Craig Walter.  He also provided 
meeting instructions and called upon staff to provide information on Item #1, Case 16-17 
for variance change in Heber/Overgaard area. 
 
ITEM #1 – Case #16 -17 – VARIANCE – DISTRICT IV:   Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Craig Walter for a variance from the ten (10) foot required side 
yard setback, (Section 1105 Item #2) to the existing three (3) feet setback, to allow the 
enclosure  of an existing carport to the dwelling on Assessor’s Parcel #206 -12-023, a Lot 
63, Timberlake Pines Pinewoods Unit Two, a.k.a. a portion of Township 12 North, Range 
17 East, Section 19 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Heber area. 
 
Mrs. Peggy Saunders  had been to the site a several years ago when Trent Larsen was 
still with Navajo County.  T he parcel & building have been in existence since 1981 and 
the fence line h as been there since that time as well .  Mrs. Saunders provided Power 
Point slides to indicate the parcel location as well as the existing structures.  When Mr. 
Walter purchased the property on August 5, 2005, the garage and portico were in place as 
shown.   He is requesting a variance from the  required 10-foot side yard setback, to  a 3-
foot setback as he would like to enclose the existing carport to allow for a great-room for 
his grandchildren.  Continuing with the slides, Mrs. Saunders indicated where the fence 
line was when he purchased the property  and indicated that there was a meter also.  Mr. 
Walter has been very compliant with regards to this property while completing the

beautiful work he has done thus far and, as the garage and fence were already in place 
prior to his purchasing the property he had no control over the fact that the set-backs were 
in place and incorrect  prior to the  purchase.  The zoning is R1-10, single family zoning 
with 10,000 sq. ft. per dwelling.  He will be required to obtain the necessary permits prior 
to enclosing the carport, should he received authorization for  this variance.  He will then 
have valid documentation  which will show he  received authorization from the board , 
albeit after the fact.  We appreciate that he is coming before the board prior to performing 
the work for the enclosure .  Mr. Walter has inquired with his neighbor regarding the 
change of variance and his intention t o do th e enclosure and the neighbor has no 
objection.  Vice Chairman  Johnson asked if the  carport was 3 -feet away from the 
property line and whether there  had been a survey done which determines that the fence 
is the property line?  Mrs. Saunders directed the question to Mr. Walter and he indicated 
the fence line at the time of the sale and for the entire time he has owned this property has 
been the boundary between the properties.  There has been no formal survey performed.  
It was a pre -settled issue betw een the previous owner and the current neighbor.  
Chairman Arendell asked if the owner of the property he purchased from had 
previously owned both properties.  Mr. Walter described the history he has had with the 
property.  Both properties we re owned by Ve nada Family.  The slide s show both 
properties as well as the garage and the home.  Both the garage and the home were meant 
to be used in conjunction to each other. Prior to purchasing, he made certain there would 
be no issues, that the fence/carport were n ot going to be a problem for him as his next 
step would be to obtain a building permit for several changes/additions.  He was able to 
obtain a copy of the original permit, of which was received back in 1981 and he had a 
Navajo County inspector come out to the property to review the permit and provide 
assurance there would be no issue on any future cha nges he may want to make to the 
property.  The inspector reviewed the original permit and the property and provided him 
with the assurance that he would have n o issues down the road.  As previously indicated 
by Mrs. Saunders, Mr. Walter’s desire to enclose the carport is primarily to provide a 
safe play area for  his 14 grandchildren, but also he believes enclosing it will prevent the 
use of the existing carport for actual vehicles.  The carport structure is under existing roof 
and current framework of existing posts.  The enclosure would require some ligh t 
electrical, heat and cool insulation as well as a few additional  posts.  Enclosing it will 
actually benefit the neighbor by alleviating any emissions caused by any vehicles should 
they use the carport  as well as noise due to starting, driving and opening/closing vehicle 
doors, etc. The enclosure will mirror the previous upgrades, cedar siding, windows from 
the same manufacturer, as well as the inside.  It will also provide additional privacy for 
the neighbor as the carport is currently open and there is a window that faces out onto the 
side yard and any activity outside in that area faces his back yard.    There would be no 
issues with fire, police, or utility response.  He did have a thorough discussion with the 
neighbor regarding his intentions a nd shared the drawings as well as had him view the 
carport and explained what he would like to do.  The neighbor did no t have any 
objections and indicated it would have no effect on him or his property.  It would further 
assist with any future property owner, should he sell the property in the future.   Vice 
Chairman Johnson said, in the Heber area, the snow load is  about 30lbs, his concern is 
that the enclosure is properly inspected for this specifically.  Mr. Walter indicated  the 
snow load is not an issue and he did  have it reviewed.  Chairman Arendell asked about

the permit that Mr. Walter had taken out previously.  Mr. Walter indicated it was for the 
garage which originally held an airplane.  The y tore it down to the studs and rebuilt it.  
Chairman Arendell asked for any questions or comments by Staff.  Bill Bess wanted to 
clarify that the roof overhang extends out 2-1/2 feet, which would mean that the distance 
from the fence/property line to the structure is only around 6 to 7 inches, but the main 
portion of the structure will be 3 -feet from property line.   Also, as the neighbor has no 
objection, staff recommends approvin g the request.  Chairman Arendell asked for any 
comments from Craig Walter.  Mr. Walter indicated based on the information from his 
drawings etc., t he overhang protrudes 1 3-14 inches.  Mr. Bess indicated he is going off

have no objections.  Vice Chair  Johnson His only objection was the issue with the 
neighbor, and that the neighbor would not come back at a later time  with an objection .  
Mrs. Saunders  could get a letter  from the neighbor if he requested so.   Vice Chair  
Johnson made a motion to approve the request.  Chairman Arendell  asked for any 
further questions or comments and as there were none, Chairman Arendell seconded the 
motion.  The variance change was approved 2/0.

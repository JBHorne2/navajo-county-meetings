PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Board of 
Adjustment of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Board of 
Adjustment will hold a Public Hearing on February 10, 2016, beginning at 10:00 a.m. 
at the Board of Supervisors  Chambers, 100 E. Code Talkers Drive , Holbrook, 
Arizona.   Members of the Board will attend either in person or by telephonic 
conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Board may vote to go into executive session on 
any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE NUMBER 15 -35: An application and suppo rting documents were 
submitted to be considered at  a formal hearing for a Use Permit.  The applicant would 
like to add permanent Caretaker’s Quarters to their parcel with an existing Children’s 
Home/Shelter and septic system.  The caretaker’s quarters would allow separate housing 
for staff, and free up space for six additional children from the ages of 0-17 years old. The 
Caretakers dwelling would have its own septic system per county specifications.

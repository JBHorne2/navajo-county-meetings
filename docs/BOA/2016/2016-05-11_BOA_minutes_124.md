Meeting held at the Navajo County Board of Supervisors Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona – Time:  10:00 am. 
 
Chairman Bill Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of Adjustment to order and explained 
the meeting procedures to the public.  Chairman Bill Arendell led the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
 
Item #1 – REQUEST:  CASE #16-07 – VARIENCE – DISTRICT V: 
 
Chairman Bill Arendell called upon the P&Z staff to provide the staff report.  Peggy Saunders read the staff 
report and provided the location of Rodeo Apartments with the pointer on the Power Point presentation.  Mrs. 
Saunders:  Staff has no objection to the applicant’s request, and we have received no objection from the 
Engineering, Flood, or the Courts Department.  The only recommendations we have, if the application is 
approved, are that they acquire building permits and any relating permits from Navajo County Planning and 
Zoning department.  Also that a registered land surveyor has verified all of the setbacks prior to construction of 
the community building and playground.  Glen Johnson would like to know what area they are in and if they 
would be serviced by the Lakeside Fire department?  Mrs. Saunders: She indicated that she believed so, as 
they are within that district.  Julie Hyatt, Manager for General Partner:  Provided a summary of the 
remodeling and updating that will be done to improve and better serve the children and families living in the 
complex.  She also provided information regarding the after school programs and family services that will be 
included as well as details on the playground which will provide play area for the children.  Chairman Bill 
Arendell is Rodeo Drive a dead end?  Ms. Hyatt Yes, it is a dead end.  Chairman Bill Arendell  Asked if the 
setbacks are encroaching on the road? Jose Cruse Crawford, Architect for the project – the road is actually 12 
to 15 feet away from the property line.  They are not close to the road, the setback is from the property line.  He 
provided further information on the setbacks and where the new building needed to be placed.  Chairman Bill 
Arendell asked if utilities would be affected, both Ms. Hyatt and Mr. Jose indicate there will be no effect on the 
utilities.   Chairman Bill Arendell asked if the County attorney had any issues, Mrs. Saunders indicated there 
were none.  Chairman Bill Arendell asked for questions, there were none.  Glen made a motion to pass Case 
#16-07, Chairman Bill Arendell seconded.  The motion passed with a vote of 2 to 0. 
 
Mrs. Saunders will update the setback to 13 Ft. and will have Chairman Bill Arendell sign the corrected 
Resolution and get a copy to the applicant. 
 
Item #2 – Possible Approval of Minutes from April 13, 2016 BOA Meeting . 
Chairman Bill Arendell made a motion to approve the minutes.  Glen Johnson seconded the motion.  Motion

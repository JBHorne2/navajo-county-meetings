Meeting held at the Navajo County Board of Supervisors Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona – 
Time: 10:00 a.m. 
 
 Chairman Bill Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of Adjustment 
to order at 10:09 a.m.  Chairman Arendell  then led the Pledge of Allegiance  and 
following that , provided the meeting procedures to the public , staff and any attending 
public. 
 
Item #1 – CASE #16-18 – USE PERMIT – DISTRICT – IV:  Mrs. Peggy Saunders  
presented the staff report  for Jeffery Ryan and Patricia Harris who submitted a Use 
Permit.  Patricia  was in attendance via speaker telephone.  Mrs. Saunders provided 
information regarding the purpose of the Use Permit request and gave details about their 
need for an additional dwelling for their mother as she is unable to care for herself.  
Using the Pow er Point presentation, she indicated the location on the map.  There were 
no objections from the neighbors, but we did receive one call to inquire about whether the 
property was in a flood zone, and th at question was answered by Sandy Phillips, Asst. 
Engr., Flood Plain Mgr., indicating that the property was not in a flood zone.  There are 
no concerns from staff, it is a straight forward case.  They have been advised they are 
able to hook the new dwelling into the sewer.  Mrs. Saunders inquired with Patricia  
Harris as to whether she had anything to ask.  Ms. Harris reiterated the need for the 
additional home to allow for space for her mother.  Mrs. Saunders added, the new home 
will become the primary dwelling and the current, existing home will become the car e 
taker, guest quarters, etc.  The zoning for this property is A -General, in which this is an 
allowed use.  Chairman Arendell asked if she was aware of the required size for the 
new dwelling.  Mrs. Saunders indicated the dimensions on her site plans are 34 ’ x 63’.  
The existing dwelling is 25 ’ x 68’.  Ms. Harris stated via telephone that t he dwelling is 
actually 14’ wide.  Mrs. Saunders requested Ms. Harris clarify the size and Ms. Harris

indicated it was 14’ x 68’  to which Mrs. Saunders pointed out that a porch was added-
on, which would account for the size difference.  Ms. Harris agreed, yes there is a deck.  
Chairman Arendell asked if we need to change anything on the condition staff has 
added because of the sewage/sewer system.  Mrs. Saunders reviewed the resolution and 
did find there was a need to change the wording.  It will be changed to read, “ Sewage 
system” or “waste system”, rather than septic.   Chairman Arendell asked if there were 
any further questions, Vice Chairman Glen Johnson indicated he had none and made a 
motion to approve the request for the Use Permit which would include the recommended 
changes, Chairman Arendell seconded the motion.  The motion passed 2/0.   Mrs. 
Saunders thanked Ms. Harris for her participation and indicated she would get  her the 
resolution as soon as it was recorded. 
 
 
Item #2 – CASE #16-19 – USE PERMIT – DISTRICT – IV:  Chairman Arendell 
called upon staff to provide the report.  Mrs. Saunders indicated we had sent out many 
more cards than were actually needed.  We did no t receive any feedback, either “ for” or 
“against”.  Using the Power Point presentation, she indicated which structures were going 
to be removed and that the septic was going to be replaced.  The applicant would like  to 
add caretakers quarters, some of the buildings currently on the parcel are going to be 
demolished.  Using the site plan, Mrs. Saunders indicated the location of the guest 
quarters, and the main dwelling. Separate septic systems will be installed for both 
locations.  We  notified the public via  the newspaper and placed the information on the 
county website.  We did go way and beyond with the notifications which included the 
post cards.  Mr.  Ron W ehe is in attendance , to represent the Dake  family.  Vice 
Chairman Glen Johnson asked what the size o f the new dwelling is going to be.  Mr. 
Wehe indicated it would be 18’ x 27’ sq. ft. of  livable in the guesthouse, with the main 
dwelling being almost 4 ,000 sq. ft.  Chairman Arendell recommended clarification that 
the property could never be rented out.  Mr. Wehe agreed and assured the Board that this 
was not a possibility for this family.   Vice Chairman Johnson  asked how far apart the 
two dwellings would be from each other.  Mr. Wehe provided that it is a 1900 acre parcel 
and the distance would be approxi mately 1000 ft .  Chairman Arendell had no further 
questions, Vice Chairman Johnson had no further questions and made motion to accept. 
Chairman Arendell seconded.  The motion was passed 2/0.

PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
December 14, 2016 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Board of 
Adjustment of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Board of 
Adjustment will hold a Public Hearing on December 14, 2016, beginning at 10:00 
a.m. at the Board of Supervisors Chambers, 100 E. Code Talkers Drive, Holbrook, 
Arizona.   Members of the Board will attend either in person or by telephonic 
conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Board may vote to go into executive session on 
any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 –CASE #16-22 - VARIANCE – DISTRICT - IV:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Jeff & Wendy Hunt for a variance from the t hirty (30) foot required side 
yard setback, to a twenty (20)  foot setback, to allow a new attached garage to be 
constructed on Assessor’s Parcel #206-35-070D, a.k.a. a portion of Township 12 North, 
Range 17 East, Section 31 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Overgaard area. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE #16-28 – USE PERMIT – DISTRICT – IV: Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Lori Rice for a 480 square foot (16X30) guest/caretakers quarters 
to be built above the garage, currently under construction on parcel #409-02-010C, a.k.a. 
a portion of Township 10 North, Range 20 East, Section 1 of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian in the Linden area. 
 
ITEM #3 –CASE #16-24 - VARIANCE – DISTRICT - V:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Robert & Claudia Thomas for a variance from the twenty (20) foot required 
side yard setback, to a fourteen (14) foot setback, to allow construction for a garage 
addition on Assessor’s Parcel #411-68-078, a.k.a. a portion of Township 8 North, Range 
23 East, Section 11 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Pinetop area. 
 
ITEM #4 – APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM AUGUST 10, 2016  & SEPTEMBER 14, 
2016 BOA MEETINGS: 
 
ITEM #5 – APPROVAL OF 2017 MEETING SCHEDULE  
 
ITEM #6 – BOARD MEMBER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF.  
Board Members may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on 
this agenda, or any other topic; and the Board may direct staff to study or provide 
additional information on topics of the Boards’ choosing.  
The Board reserves the right to change the order of any Agenda item.   
 
The Board reserves the right to adjourn into executive session when needed pursuant to 
ARS §38-431.03(A) (3) for legal consultation on the above-described agenda items.

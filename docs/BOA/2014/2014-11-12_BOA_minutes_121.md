Meeting held at the Board of Supervisors’ Chambers, 100 E. Code Talker’s Drive, Holbrook,

Chairman Bill Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of Adjustment to order 
at 10:19 a.m., and asked all present to stand for the Pledge of Allegiance.  
 
ITEM #1 CASE # 14-23 USE PERMIT: Discussion and possible Board action on a request by 
Wayne H. Flake for a Use Permit to place a primary dwelling and permanent guest quarters on a 
portion of APN: 206 -37-009, in Section 33, Township 12 North, Range 18 East, G&SRM, 
Navajo County, in the Aripine area. 
 
Mr. Larson  presented the staff report  to Members of the Board.  The  property is zoned A -
General, which permits Guest Houses as an allowed use, if granted a Use Permit by the Board of 
Adjustment, as cited in Section 302.18.b of the Zoning Ordinance. 
 
There was one (1) comment received by Planning and Zoning in favor of the project.  
 
Mr. Larson added five requirements to the Use Permit, as stated in the Staff  Report. 
 
There were no  questions from the Board, so Mr. Glen Johnson made a motion to approve the 
Use Permit with the conditions stated by staff .  The motion was seconded by Mr. James 
Maloney.  The item was approved with a vote of 3 to 0. 
 
Mr. Johnson asked about the differences in use between A -General Zoning, and RU-20 zoning,

Navajo County Public Works

BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT

JANUARY 10, 2024
10:00 A.M.

AGENDA

Pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.02(H), the public body will have physical access to the meeting place fifteen (15) minutes prior to the start of the meeting. Pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the members of the Board of Adjustment of Navajo County and to the Public that the Board of Adjustment will hold a Public Hearing on JANUARY 10, 2024, beginning at 10:00 a.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, 100 East Code Talkers Drive, Navajo County Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Board will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call.

For this meeting: If you would like to speak during the meeting, please call 24 hours ahead 928-535-7155 (except the applicants and representatives) and follow the directions.

Items on the agenda may be considered out of order at the Board's discretion. The Board may vote to meet in closed executive session pursuant to A.R.S. §38-431.03(A)(3) for legal consultation on any item listed on the following agenda.

A copy of the agenda background material provided to the Board, except items to be considered in the executive session, is available for public inspection at the Navajo County website, www.navajocountyaz.gov

10:00 a.m. Call To Order: Pledge of Allegiance

1.

CONSIDERATION OF APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE COMMISSION HEARING OF:  June 14th, 2023.

Board Member Evans motioned to accept the minutes.  Chair Smith seconded the motion.
Motion passed unanimously, 3 - 0 .
Approved

Presented By:
Kristyn Saunders

2.

2024 BOA MEETING SCHEDULE:  Consideration and approval of the 2024 Navajo County Board of Adjustment meeting schedule.

Chair Smith motioned to accept the 2024 meeting schedule. Vice-Chair Hartman seconded the motion.
Motion passed unanimously, 3 - 0 .
Approved

Presented By:
Kristyn Saunders

3.

ELECTION OF OFFICERS:

Board Member Smith  stepped down as Chair of the Board of Adjustment.

Board Member Smith  nominated Board Member Hartman for Chair. Board Member Evans seconded the nomination.
Unanimous election of Board Member Hartman for Chair of the Board of Adjustment, 3 - 0 .

Chair Hartman nominated Board Member Evans as Vice-Chair.  Board Member Smith seconded the nomination.
Unanimous election of Board Member Evans as Vice-Chair of the Board of Adjustment, 3 - 0 .

4.

BOARD MEMBERS' COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF:
Board Members may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on this agenda, or any other topic; and the Board may direct staff to study or provide additional information on topics of the Boards' choosing.
No Action

5.

ADJOURN:

Meeting adjourned at 10:10 AM.

Dated: 1/3/2024
NAVAJO COUNTY BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT

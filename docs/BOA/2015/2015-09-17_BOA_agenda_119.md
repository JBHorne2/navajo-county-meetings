AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Planning 
& Zoning Commission of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Planning 
and Zoning Commission will hold a Public Hearing on September 17 , 2015, 
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in the Board of Supervisors' Chambers, Navajo  County 
Governmental Center, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the Commission will attend 
either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Commission may vote to go into executive 
session on any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE # 15 -08:  SPECIAL USE PERMIT – DISTRICT III:   Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Raymond & Nancy Fitzgerald / Verizon Wireless for a 
Special Use Permit for a 199’ Wireless Self Support Lattice Cellular Tower and 12X26 
Equipment Shelter on  APN:  110 -17-001, a.k.a. a portion of Section 21 Township 17 
North, Range 22 East of the G&SRM in the Holbrook area.   
 
ITEM #2 – CASE # 15 -22:  ZONE CHANGE – DISTRICT II:  Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Gary and Vickey DeSpain for a Zone Change from RU-I, (Rural-1 
Zoning) to IND -I (Industrial-I Zoning), to establish a Contractor’s Yard on APN 108 -23-
015D, a.k.a. a portion of Section 9, Township 18N, Range 16  East of the G&SRM in the 
Winslow area. 
 
ITEM #3 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM THE  COMMISSION HEARING OF 
August 20, 2015. 
 
ITEM #4 – COMMISSIONER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF.  
Commissioners may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on this 
agenda, or any other topic; and the Commission may direct staff to study or provide 
additional information on topics of the Commissions’ choosing. 
  
ITEM #5 – REPORT FROM STAFF TO THE COMMISSION. 
 
The Commission reserves the right to change the order of any Agenda item.

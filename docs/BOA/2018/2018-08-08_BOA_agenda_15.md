PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
August 8th, 2018 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Board of 
Adjustment of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Board of 
Adjustment will hold a Public Hearing on August 8th, 2018, beginning at 10:00 a.m. 
at the Board of Supervisors  Chambers, 100 E. Code Talkers Drive , Holbrook, 
Arizona. Members of the Board will attend either in person or by telephonic 
conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Board may vote to go into executive session on 
any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 – CASE # 18 -16- USE PERMIT – DISTRICT – IV: Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Steven & Nancy Brimhall on their 1.9 acre A-General zoned parcel, 
to allow a use permit for construction of their new home on Assessor’s Parcel #205 -24-
008H, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 20 East, Sec tion 32, of the Gila and Salt 
River Meridian in the Pinedale Area. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE #18-21- USE PERMIT – DISTRICT – IV: Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Matt Benson  on his 6.88 acre A -General zoned parcel, to allow a use 
permit for an existing structure on Assessor’s Parcel #205-26-008B, a portion of Township 
11 North, Range 21 East, Section 32, of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Linden 
Area. 
 
ITEM #3 – CASE # 18-17 – VARIANCE – DISTRICT – IV: Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Timothy Provencher on his 0.26 acre R1 -10 zoned parcel, to allow a 
variance from the twenty (20) foot required front yard setback, to a five (5)  foot setback, 
to allow installation of a garage on Assessor’s Parcel #311-18-113, a portion of Township 
9 North, Range 23 East, Section 22, of the Gila and Sal t River Meridian in the Sky -Hi 
area. 
 
ITEM #4 – CASE # 18-20 – VARIANCE – DISTRICT – V: Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Ann Reid on her 0.28 acre R-3 zoned parcel, to allow a variance from the 
twenty (20) foot required front yard setback, to a seven (7) foot setback, to allow 
installation of a carport on Assessor’s Parcel #212-43-032, a portion of Township 9 North, 
Range 22 East, Section 22, of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Lakeside area. 
 
ITEM #4 – APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM APRIL 11, 2018.  
 
ITEM #5 – BOARD MEMBER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF.  
Board Members may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on 
this agenda, or any other topic; and the Board may direct staff to study or provide 
additional information on topics of the Boards’ choosing.

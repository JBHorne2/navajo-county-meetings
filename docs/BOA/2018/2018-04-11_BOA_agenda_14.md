PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
April 11th, 2018 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Board of 
Adjustment of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Board of 
Adjustment will hold a Public Hearing on April 11th, 2018, beginning at 10:00 a.m. 
at the Board of Supervisors  Chambers, 100 E. Code Talkers Drive , Holbrook, 
Arizona. Members of the Board will attend either in person or by telephonic 
conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Board may vote to go into executive session on 
any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 –CASE #18-03- USE PERMIT – DISTRICT - III:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Deborah Barcon on her 4.81 acre RU -1 zoned parcel, to allow a use 
permit for construction of a guest home / quarters on Assessor’s Parcel #404-42-024J, a 
portion of Township 11 North, Range 23 East, Section 31, of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian in the Show Low area. 
 
ITEM #2 –CASE #18-07- USE PERMIT – DISTRICT - III:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Fred & Krystal Parish on their 3.18 acre  RU-1 zoned parcel, to allow a 
use permit for construction of a guest home / quarters on Assessor’s Parcel #304 -71-
010F, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 22 East, Section 8, of the Gila and Salt 
River Meridian in the Show Low area. 
 
ITEM #3 –CASE #18-09- USE PERMIT – DISTRICT - V:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Stephen Evans on his 0.8 acre A -General zoned parcel, to allow a use 
permit for construction of a guest home / quarters on Assessor’s Parcel #212 -34-013Q, 
a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 East, Section 27, of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian in the Lakeside area. 
 
ITEM #4 – APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM OCTOBER 11, 201 7 & FEBRUARY 14, 
2018.  
 
ITEM #5 – BOARD MEMBER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF.  
Board Members may use this time to offer additional comments regarding any item on 
this agenda, or any other topic; and the Board may direct staff to study or provide 
additional information on topics of the Boards’ choosing.  
The Board reserves the right to change the order of any Agenda item. 
The Board reserves the right to adjourn into executive session when needed pursuant to ARS §38-431.03(A) (3) for legal consultation 
on the above-described agenda items

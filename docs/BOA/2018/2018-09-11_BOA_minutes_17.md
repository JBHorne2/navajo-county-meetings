Chairman Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of 
Adjustment to order and explained the meeting procedures to the public.  He 
then led the Pledge of Allegiance and called for a moment of silence in honor of 
the tragedy that took place on this date, 18 years ago.  
 
ITEM #1 – CASE # 19-64 – U SE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Kevin & Diana Young on their 4.42-acre A-
General zoned parcel to allow a Use Permit for a caretaker quarters, located on a 
portion of APN 205-35-011E, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 21 East, 
Section 34 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Burton area. 
 
Ms. Phillips introduced the item, a use permit for guest quarters for the 
applicant’s special-needs daughter. The pr operty is located off Lone Pine Dam 
road, just outside of the city limits. This is an allowed use for the zoning, and Ms. 
Phillips pointed out the a ccess, and the lot has a singl e-family dwelling and two 
barns existing on the property. Sandra fu rther pointed out where the postings 
were located, Staff has not received any  emails or phone call that voice either 
support or opposition from the public. Commissioner Smith  asked if the new 
building would have a new septic, and  the applicant Kevin Young and he 
responded that yes, there would be, and proceeded to show where the septic 
was located. Commissioner Smith made a motion to approve. Commissioner 
Hartman seconded the motion.  Motion passed with a vote of 3 to 0.

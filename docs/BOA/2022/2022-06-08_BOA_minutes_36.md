ITEM #3 – UP 22-005 – USE PERMIT, DISTRICT I:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Larry and Misty Benham on their approximately 37.13 -acre, RU-1 zoned 
property to allow for the construction of an approximately 2,272 square foot guest house, 
located on APN 108-23-144, a portion of Township 18 North, Range 16 East, Section 05 
of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Winslow area. 
 
Mr. Cooper presented the item to the Board.  He showed the Board an a erial of the 
project site just to the south of Winslow, the Applicant’s letter of Intent, the site plan, 
and the area of not ification. The RU -1 Zoning did allow for guest houses with no 
additional requirements for size, meaning this project was well within the allowance 
of the Zoning Ordinance. Staff had not received any comments from the public either 
for or against the project. The applicant plans to have two separate septic systems and 
have applied for the first septic at this time.  
 
Vice-Chairman Muder asked if the applicant was available to talk about the septic 
permits. Mr. La rry Benham, t he applican t’s Father was available to  speak to the 
Board, as the appli cant was unavailable.  Mr. Benham explained that he was waiting 
on a confirmation for this and was ready to run final plans up to the Building 
Department as soon as there was a confirmation from the Board. 
 
With no fu rther discussi on, Board Member Hartman  made a motion to approve, 
Vice-Chairman Muder seconded. The motion carried Unanimously, 4 – 0.

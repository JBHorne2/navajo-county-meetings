ITEM #1 – CASE # 19-108 – VARIANCE, DISTRICT V: Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Richard Montag on hi s 0.24-acre, R1-10 zoned parcel to 
allow for the encroachment fr om the twenty (20) foot re quired rear yard setback, 
to a zero (0) foot rear yard setback, fo r installation of their single-family home on 
APN 411-67-007, a portion of  Township 8 North, Range 23 East, Section 11 of 
the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Pinetop area. 
 
Ms. Phillips presented the item to the Board, noting that the Board has heard this 
item previously, however there was not a true quorum. Staff is bringing the 
project back before the Board to ratify the previous ruling and ensure all legal 
requirements were met. The two lots we re sold and treated as one lot, however 
they have two separate taxing districts, so they were unable to combine them.  
Staff is working with the Assessor’s O ffice and the Pinetop-Lakeside Sanitary 
District to try and rectify this problem for the future, as surrounding properties 
also have this same issue.  Ms. Phillip s shared a visual to better illustrate the 
situation.  Staff received some conc ern against the application from neighbors 
that, Staff felt, misunderstood which lot line they were encroaching upon.

<!-- page 2 -->
Commissioner Hartman asked if they were ever able to be sold separately, and 
Ms. Phillips confirmed that no, they were never going to be able to sell the lots 
separately. 
 
Kristi Larsen approached to speak to the Board, she is the applicant’s wife and 
explained they just want the final legal details done and over with. 
 
Commissioner Smith made a motion to approve the variance.  Commissioner 
DeSpain seconded the motion.  Motion passed with a vote of 3 to 0.  
 
ITEM #2 – CASE # 19-76 – V ARIANCE, DISTRICT IV: Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Richard Sweet on hi s 0.22-acre, A-General Zoned parcel 
to allow for the encroachm ent from the twenty (20) foot required front yard 
setback, to a fifteen (15) foot front yard setback, fo r installation of a garage on 
APN 309-41-107, a portion of Township 9 North, Range 21 East, Section 1 of the 
Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Show Low area. 
 
Mr. Cooper introduced the item , noting that the projec t was located in Summer 
Pines, he showed them the lot and explain ed what the variance was to encroach 
upon the front yard setbacks by five f eet, he explained lot dimensions and noted 
the lot was rather small considering that it was A-General zoning, but that all the 
lots in Summer Pines were similarly si zed. Staff further discussed with the board 
the difficulty of the project, noting that  even if the variance was granted, they 
would be unable to obtain a building permit because of the minimum requirement 
between buildings for fire safety.  Staff further explained that because it was a 
self-imposed hardship, Staff recommended denial.   
 
The Board asked why the applicant was not simply attaching a breezeway, staff 
explained that they would have to follow the roof line and it would be extremely 
difficult to match the garage along with the pitch of the roof.  
 
After some discussion wit h Mr. Sweet, Staff and Chairman Arendell  both 
recommended going back and working with the Ch ief Building Official to come to 
a better solution, to which the applicant agreed and withdrew.

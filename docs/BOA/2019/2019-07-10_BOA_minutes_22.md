ITEM #1 – CASE # 19-29 – U SE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Scott Johnson on his 1.56-acre A-General zoned 
parcel to allow a Use Permit for the placement  of a home that is 34 feet in height 
as opposed to the allowed 30 feet in height, located on a portion of APN 206-44-
018B, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 27 of the Gila and 
Salt River Meridian in the Overgaard area. 
 
Sandra presented the item to the Board, noting its location was just outside of the 
Mogollon Airpark. The land is currently vacant and there is a very steep south-
eastern slope on a large portion of the pr operty. As there is very little buildable 
room on the lot, the applicant would lik e to build their house above a hangar and 
is asking for a four-foot allowance abov e the height limit as the snow load

requirements raised the peak of the roof over the 30-foot lim it, though the vast 
majority of the home will be we ll below the limit. Staff further noted that they had 
not received any phone calls or e-mails in objection to the variance. Cody then

for this Use Permit as a precaution.   The board had no further questions, and 
there was no one else to speak in the audience.   
 
Commissioner Smith  made a motion to approve Commissioner DeSpain  
seconded the motion.  Motion passed with a vote of 3 to 0. 
 
ITEM #2 – CASE # 19-52 – U SE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Daniel Miller  and Sheryl Filliater on their 1.2 acre 
RU-1 zoned parcel to allow a Use Permi t for an already existing caretaker 
quarters, located on a portion of APN 206-02-047A, a portion of Township 12 
North, Range 17 East, Section 30 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the 
Overgaard area. 
 
Sandra explained that the item was gr anted a Zone Change by the Board of 
Supervisors and described where the proper ty was located.  She detailed that 
when the applicant bought the pr operty, they did no know that the lot was out of 
compliance and when presented with this information the applicants both decided 
to go through this process to be able to ke ep their guest quarters as-is. Staff has 
stipulated that although they  can rent out their entire property, they cannot rent 
the guest house separately. Staff received a few calls about the item, but once 
the situation was explained, they had no further comments or complaints. 
 
Sheryl Filliater and Dan Miller spoke to t he board and said that they for the most 
part just wanted to keep the house they  purchased the same and were looking 
forward to using the guest quarters. The Board had no further questions, and  
 
Vice Chairman Smith  called to the public, no one in the audience had 
comments or questions, so  he called for a motion to approve the Use Permit.

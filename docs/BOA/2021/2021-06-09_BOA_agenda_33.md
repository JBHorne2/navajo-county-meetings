AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38 -431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Board of 
Adjustment of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Board of Adjustment 
will hold a Public Hearing on June 9th, 2021, beginning at 10:00 a.m. at the Board of 
Supervisors Chambers, 100 E. Code Talkers Drive, Holbrook, Arizona. Members of the 
Board will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
ITEM #1 – POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM MAY 12, 2021.  
 
ITEM #2 – UP 21-005 – USE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV: Discussion and possible action on a 
request by Hal Earnhardt on their approximately 14.27-acre, A-General zoned parcel to allow 
for a Use Permit, to permit and expand an already -existing, 1260 square foot guest house, 
located on APN 205-21-002Y, a portion of Township 11 North, Range 20 East, Section 27 of 
the Gila and Salt River Meridian. 
 
ITEM #3 – UP 21-006 – USE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV: Discussion and possible action on a 
request by Pauline Pfeister on their approximately 1.87 - acre, A-General zoned parcel to 
allow for a Use Permit to permit an existing 440 square foot home as a guest house in order 
to allow the construction of a 1248 square foot home as the primary residence, located on 
APN 206-26-032, a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 32 of the Gila and 
Salt River Meridian. 
 
ITEM #4 – UP 21-007 – USE PERMIT, DISTRICT V:  Discussion and possible action on a 
request by Michael Granillo on their approximately 1.00 -acre, A -General zoned parcel to 
allow for a Use Permit to permit a 432 square  foot guest house, located on APN 212 -19-
011C, a portion of Township 09 North, Range 22 East Section 22 of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian. 
 
ITEM #5 – BOARD MEMBERS’ COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF:

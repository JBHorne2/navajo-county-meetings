ITEM #3 – UP 21-002 – USE PERMIT, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and possible 
action on a  request by Malcolm Stinson Jr. on their 9.98 -acre, A-General zoned 
parcel to allow for a Use Permit for a guest house, located on APN 205-30-025, a 
portion of Townshi p 11 North, Range  21 East, Section 20 of the Gila and Salt 
River Meridian in the Burton area. 
 
Mr. Cooper presented the project to the board . The applicant’s property is 
located on Turkey Lake Road, with Stinson Lane to the south, in the Burton area. 
The applicant has applied for a guest house to allow family and friends to stay  on 
his property while they come to visit  him. Currently, the applicant has a 2168 
square foot manufactured home on their property. The applicant is requesting the 
ability to place a 1500 square foot guest home on their property if granted a Use 
Permit. This size of guest home is within the 70% size allowance as noted in A -
General zoning requirements and all setbacks on  the site plan meet the 
requirements of this zoning district as well.  He also presented a site plan and  
displayed the area of notification. 
 
Commissioner Smith asked about the septic system, Cody noted that this will 
need a separate septic tank, and will be addressed in the staff recommendations 
for approval. Commissioner DeSpain asked about access, and how they would 
be accessing the guest house.  Cody explained that there were two possible 
accesses to the property, and it would depend on preference as both were valid. 
 
The Chairman called for a motion, and Commissioner DeSpain made a motion 
to approve the variance.  Commissioner Hartman seconded the motion.  Motion 
passed with a vote of 3 to 0.

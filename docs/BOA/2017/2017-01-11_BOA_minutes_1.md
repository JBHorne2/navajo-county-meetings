ITEM #1 –CASE #16-29 - USE PERMIT – DISTRICT - IV:  Discussion and possible action on a request 
by Boone Bryant , on behalf of James and Iona Jackson , for a Use Permit to allow a new second 
dwelling to be constructed.  The existing dwelling will be used for guest/caretakers quarters on 
Assessor’s Parcel #208 -06-002C, a.k.a. a portion of Township 11 North, Range 19 East, Section 9 of 
the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Clay Springs area.  Ms. Sandy Phillips presented the s taff 
report, to allow a new larger primary dwelling to be constructed on the parcel.  The adjacent neighbors 
were notified; no comments were received by staff .  Flood C ontrol, Engineering and the County 
Attorney have no objection.  Vice Chairman Johnson  asked how many dwellings were on the 
property.  Ms. Phillips pointed out several barns and sheds along w ith a picnic pavilion, but only one  
residence is in u se.  The old homestead is not a livable structure.  The property is zoned A -general 
which does allow a second dwelling on the parcel .  With no further questions, Chairman Arendell 
asked for a motion, Vice Chairman Johnson  made a motion  to approve the Use Permit, with t he 
recommendations and conditions stated by Staff , Chairman Arendell  seconded the motion.  T he 
motion passed 2/0.  
 
ITEM #2 – CASE #16-30 – VARIANCE – DISTRICT – IV: Discussion and possible action on a 
Variance request by Kim Heaton on behalf of Ranchero M obile Home Park to install a n eight (8’) foot 
high, 322 foot long, solid fence on parcel #210 -33-020A, a.k.a. a portion of Township 10 North, Range 
22 East, Section 32 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Show Low area.   Ms. Phillips presented 
the staff report, and indicated the location where the wall would be built.  The applicant will verify that 
the placement of the wall will not be in the right-of-way prior to granting the permit application.  The wall 
will be a decorative wall.  Staff recognizes White Mountain Boulevard as our scenic corridor, and we

<!-- page 2 -->
want to assure the area remains presentable for our  tourists and residents.  We sent out post cards to 
adjacent property owners, providing information on this request  along with posting in the newspa per 
and did not receive any objections .  Flood department does not have any issue.  No objections from 
Engineering, or the County Attorney.  Jerom Hatch provided information on the wall dimensions.  The 
columns that are a support for the wall are 19”x19” s quare and the panel is 5” thick, spans from footing 
to footing.  Compared to a site poured wall, there is no cracking after post installation because it has 
room to move within the joint of the column and due to the well-designed plan.  They have been using 
this plan since 2007.  Chairman Arendell reviewed the sketch, it looks like it is 6  foot wall; Mr. Hatch 
agreed, stating they manufactured the columns at eight foot, but the panels are manufactured as a six 
foot, and a two foot panel.  T he six foot panel sits on top of the two foot panel.  Vice Chairman 
Johnson asked whether this was the actual design .  Mr. Hatch provided actual photos for a wall in 
Sedona.  There were also several eight foot walls included in the photos.  One customer purchased 1.3 
miles of wall in Mesa in 2016.  Ms. Phillips, asked Mr. Hatch to provide other locations.  Mr. Hatch 
added that Superstitions Springs Mall, and Leisure World used the same wall panels.  Vice Chairman 
Johnson asked what direction the fence will  face, and whether  it would be crossing any private 
property lines or divide any private property?  Mr. Hatch replied no, the fence will be running parallel to 
the highway.  Ms. Phillips showed the location on the PowerPoint.  Chairman Arendell asked if there 
were any other walls in the area of the same height.  Ms. Phillips responded, not to her recollection.  It 
has been  done before , along White Mountain B oulevard.  Mr. Hatch indicated there is a wall  at 
Mountain Gate, which was installed in 2007 , along the right of way .  It is further to the south of this  
proposed wall location, past Walmart, before Lakeside on the NE side of the high way.  There is about 
1,000 lineal feet there, and it has trees in the front. Ms. Phillips asked about tree removal, Mr. Hatch 
indicated that all Ponderosa trees will stay, A spen trees will be removed per the owner.  Chairman 
Arendell expressed concern about setting a precedence in that more people will want a similar wall .  
Bill Bess  said the Mountain Gate project was in the town of Pinetop/Lak eside, outside of Navajo 
County,  This particular project is located in Navajo County on the east side of the road , with City of 
Show Low on the west side.  Ms. Phillips indicated staff will be working with P lanning & Zoning 
Commission on setting up some rules for any plans in the future .  Chairman Arendell agrees the wall 
is decorative, but expressed concern over putting up an eight foot wall along Hwy 260.  Mr. Hatch The 
owner of the property has noted the neighbors have complained about noise, the wall will act as a noise 
barrier and the highway is up higher than the wall which makes it appear shorter than it is.  Ordinances 
in Maricopa once were six foot, but now many have two feet added. It becomes a sound abatement .  
Vice Chairman Johnson asked Bill Bess about his opinion on structure soundness.  Mr. Bess yes, the 
design is complete with a wind-load design and does have engineering seal.  IBC which we are looking 
at adopting will allow a seven foot wall.  Mr. Hatch , we spent over $100,000 last year modi fying 
molding equipment so we could produce eight foot specifically.  We are a Navajo County company and 
would like to grow here.  Chairman Arendell Prior to the project being presented, the individual was 
aware of issues regarding noise.  Mr. Hatch  the RV  Park has been in place 30+ years, traffic has 
increased over the 20 years, he feels Show Low is not the same place,  it is very progressive and is 
becoming much busier.  Walls of this type are becoming  more common as highways grow along with 
populations.  It is possible an  economic impact may be caused due to the restrictions, folks may not 
want to invest in property that does not allow for the eight foot WALL sound abatements.  Ms. Phillips 
no sound study has been done which would show the impact of a six foot vs. an eight foot.  W ith the 
speed limit being 35 mph, a six foot wall may help as well as an eight foot wall.  Mr. Hatch does not 
feel the owner would be interested in doing the project.  Chairman Arendell said staff has indicated a 
seven (7) foot wall would be allowed upon adoption 2015 IBC.  Vice Chairman Johnson agreed, staff 
is very  comfortable with the Variance , as long as  all recommendations and stipulations have been 
followed, he would like to made motion to a pprove the Variance as presented by  staff.  Chairman 
Arendell seconded the motion.  The motion passed 2/0.

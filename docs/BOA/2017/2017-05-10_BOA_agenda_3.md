PUBLIC HEARING 
NAVAJO COUNTY 
BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT 
May 10, 2017 
AGENDA 
 
Pursuant to ARS §38-431.02, notice is hereby given to the Members of the Board of 
Adjustment of Navajo County and to the General Public that the Board of 
Adjustment will hold a Public Hearing on May 10, 2017, beginnin g at 10:00 a.m. at 
the Board of Supervisors Chambers, 100 E. Code Talkers Drive, Holbrook, Arizona.   
Members of the Board will attend either in person or by telephonic conference call. 
 
As indicated in the following agenda, the Board may vote to go into executive session on 
any item, which will not be open to the public. 
 
ITEM #1 –CASE #17-08 - VARIANCE – DISTRICT - IV:  Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Chris & Charlene  Garst for a variance from the thirty (30) foot required 
side yard setback, to a twenty (20) foot setback, to allow a ne w attached garage to be 
constructed on Assessor’s Par cel #206-36-118, a.k.a. Lot 118 in  Pineland Acres and a 
portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, Section 20 of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian, in the Overgaard area. 
 
ITEM #2 –CASE #17-13- VARIANCE – DISTRICT - IV:   Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Thomas & Lorinda Mills for a variance from the twenty (20) foot required 
rear yard setback, to a fifteen (15) foot setback, to allow installation of mobile home and 
garage on Assessor’s Parcel #309-41-194D, a.k.a. Lot 193 in Sum mer Pines #2 and a 
portion of Township 9 North, Range 21 East, Section 1 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, 
in the Show Low area. 
 
ITEM #3 –CASE #17-14- VARIANCE – DISTRICT - V:   Discussion and possible action 
on a request by Rob Evans for a v ariance from the thirty (30) p ercent maximum lot 
coverage, to forty-five (45) per cent lot coverage, to allow con struction of new dwelling 
and garage on Assessor’s Parcel #212-79-007, a.k.a. Lot 7 in Elk Springs and a portion 
of Township 9 North, Range 22 East, Section 9 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the 
Lakeside area. 
 
ITEM #4 – APPROVAL OF MINUTES FROM FEBRUARY 15, 2017 BOA MEETING 
 
ITEM #5 – BOARD MEMBER’S COMMENTS AND/OR DIRECTIONS TO STAFF.  
Board Members may use this time to offer additional comments re garding any item on 
this agenda, or any other topic; and the Board may direct staff  to study or provide 
additional information on topics of the Boards’ choosing.  
The Board reserves the right to change the order of any Agenda item.   
 
The Board reserves the right to adjourn into executive session when needed pursuant to 
ARS §38-431.03(A) (3) for legal consultation on the above-described agenda items.

Chairman Bill Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of Adjustment to order and 
asked all present to stand for the Pledge of Allegiance . Chairman Arendell then provided 
instructions and the meeting procedures to the staff and the public. Chairman Arende l/ excused 
himself from this item due to previous interaction with Gary Hunter who has an item on the agenda 
for board approval. Vice Chairman Glen Johnson took over and led the meeting. 
ITEM #1 -CASE #16 -31 - USE PERMIT - DISTRICT - IV: 
Discussion and possible action on a Use Permit request by Gary & Patricia H unter, to 
allow a new primary dwelling to be constructed . The existing living space above the 
garage will continue to be used for a recreation/guest room. Parcel #409-01-008C, a.k.a. 
a portion of Township 10 North, Range 20 East, Section 20 of the Gila and Salt River 
Meridian, in the Pinedale area. Miss Sandy Phillips identified the location SW of Show 
Low, that had an existing garage constructed with temporary living corridors above. They 
are requesting a Special Use Permit to allow the garage to remain as a guest quarters . 
We received one letter of concern from a neighboring property regarding a wash that has 
moved slightly. Staff has encouraged the applicant to stabilize the bank to protect from 
further erosion and for the parties involved to work together to assure there are no adverse 
impacts to either property. Staff, Flood Control, Public Works , or the County Attorney have 
no objections . No objections from any of the other neighbors. Miss Phillips pointed out that 
the applicant is here if the Board has any questions for them. Board Member Kevin Smith 
questioned Mr. Hunter on the original permit. Vice Chairman Johnson asked if both plans 
submitted at the same time. Mr. Hunter indicated yes. Vice Chairman Johnson asked if it 
was his intention to keep it for guest quarters. Mr. Hunter said the intention was for it to 
be a recreation room for the family, but it does work as guest quarters. Vice Chairman 
Johnson Are we approving guest quarters now? Mrs. Saunders Yes, as well as approving 
that he is building the primary dwelling which will be used as his home. Mr. Smith

ITEM #2 - CASE #16-28 - USE PERMIT AMENDMENT - DISTRICT - IV: 
Discussion and possible action on a request by Lori Rice to amend the Special Use Permit 
to remove the restriction to rent the primary dwelling only. Parcel #409-02-01 OC, a.k.a. a 
portion of Township 10 North, Range 20 East, Section 1 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian 
in the Linden area. Miss Phillips noted the location N of 260, the parcel is 1.72 acres and 
is zoned A-General. This Resolution was heard last month and was approved, however, 
the owner has requested that she be allowed to rent the main dwelling on the property 
and to have item # 5 revised to allow this. There are no concerns with Public Works, Flood 
Control, Engineering , or County Attorney. This was advertised in the Holbrook Tribune . 
Mr. Smith made a motion to approve the amended wording . Vice Chairman Johnson 
seconded the motion. The item was approved with a vote of~ to Q. 
Miss Phillips provided a quick overview of the next day's Planning and Zoning Commission 
meeting that will include updates to the ordinance . Mr. Smith asked about future meetings and 
their scheduling . Mrs. Saunders provided a rundown on the BOA binder which includes a copy of 
the meeting schedule and ordinance. As changes occur to the chapters, Sandy Phillips will 
provide the board with updated copies. Chairman Arendell indicated if there are any questions by 
the board to staff or vice versa. Mrs. Saunders provided further information to Mr. Smith about 
receiving each BOA meeting agenda via email so he has a chance to review it before the meeting. 
We always will bring a hard copy to the meetings via the board binders which we collect at each 
meeting and replace/update with current agenda and any pertinent information.

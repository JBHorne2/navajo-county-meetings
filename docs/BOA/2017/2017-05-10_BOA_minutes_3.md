ITEM #1 -CASE #17-08 - VARIANCE - DISTRICT - IV: Discussion and 
possible action on a request by Chris & Charlene Garst for a variance from the 
thirty (30) foot required side yard setback , to a twenty (20) foot setback , to allow 
a new garage to be constructed on Assessor 's Parcel #206-36-118 , a.k.a. Lot 
118 in Pineland Acres and a portion of Township 12 North, Range 17 East, 
Section 20 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian , in the Overgaard area. A primary 
dwelling currently exists on the lot. The garage is larger than the existing 
dwelling. There are numerous trees on the lot. Staff has received no objections 
from neighbors or the County Attorney, Engineering, or Flood Control. One letter 
from the neighbor in support of the request. Mr. Coussoulis conducted a site 
visit. No additional comments were provided from the audience. Board member 
Johnson questioned the possibility of there being a utility easement. There are 
no easements that were observed, and no power lines were witnessed overhead . 
The board did not favor the garage so close to the property line. Chairman 
Arendell asked for a motion, Mr. Smith moved to deny, Mr. Johnson seconded. 
Variance was denied 3/0.

ITEM #3 -CASE #17-14- VARIANCE - DISTRICT - V: Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Rob Evans on behalf of RL Sky LLC for a variance from 
the thirty (30) percent maximum lot coverage, to forty-five (45) percent lot 
coverage , for the construction of new dwelling and garage on Assessor 's Parcel 
#212-79-007, a.k.a. Lot 7 in Elk Springs and a portion of Township 9 North, 
Range 22 East, Section 9 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian, in the Lakeside 
area. Staff visited the site. Some houses do not meet t he 30% lot coverage 
required of the specific zoning in the neighborhood . Staff did notify neighbors and 
received one phone call objecting and one letter in support. Rob Evans 
presented information regarding his home. He stated the proposed house will be 
within the setbacks. Mr. Evans claimed out of 10 homes, 7 of the homes are over 
30%, and his model is the same model that is o n 12 and 4. He was given the 
plans by the developer and he felt it would work well as it is the same design as 
other homes in the area. He claimed his additions to the garage and patio add to 
the aesthetical appearance of the area. Mr. Smith asked if he increased the 
square footage on the plans. Mr. Evans responded, yes. Mr. Johnson asked if 
the contractor has other plans that will fit within the 30% requirement. They do, 
yet they are too small for Mr. Evans' liking. Mr. Bess commented that when the 
subdivision was in process , the 30% coverage was not looked at. Staff feels the 
area is meant to allow for outdoor enjoyment, and as other existing properties are 
already well over the allowed limit, we don't want to continue to add. Planning 
staff is unable to review changes until a revised application is submitted. 
Chairman Arendell asked Mr. Evans if he wanted to table or take a vote. Mr. 
Evans wants to table and resubmit. Chairman Arendell asked for a motion to 
table the variance, Mr. Johnson motioned, Mr. Smith seconded, motion passed 
310.

Meeting held at the Board of Supervisors Chambers, Holbrook, Arizona – Time: 10:05 a.m. 
 
Chairman Bill Arendell called the meeting of the Navajo County Board of Adjustment to order.  
With no one from the public attending Chairman Arendell asked staff to present item #1.   
 
ITEM #1  REQUEST:    (CASE # 13-12)   USE PERMIT:  Discussion and possible Board 
action on a request by Trevor & Gena Hatch for a Use Permit to maintain an existing garage as a 
guest/caretaker’s quarters upon completion of construction of new house, located on a portion of 
APN 403-20-005A, a.k.a. a portion of Section 4, Township 13 North, Range 23 East, G&SRM, 
Navajo County, in the Snowflake Area.      
 
Glen Johnson asked for clarification as to whether both existing garages will be made into guest 
quarters or just one.  Gina Hatch indicated that the garage in the middle of the property would 
remain a garage.  Mr. Johnson inquired if all the neighbors had been notified.  Trent Larson 
responded that they had been notified as required. 
 
James Maloney motioned to approve the special use permit as presented by staff.  Glen Johnson 
seconded the motion.  The Variance was approved, 3-0. 
 
ITEM #2 - POSSIBLE APPROVAL OF AUGUST 8, 20 12, AND NOVEMBER 14, 
2012 MINUTES. 
 
James Maloney made a motion to approve the minutes from the last two meetings.  Bill Arendell 
seconded the motion.  Motion carried 3-0. 
 
ITEM #3 – ELECTION OF OFFICERS. 
 
James Maloney made a motion to leave the officers as is.   Bill Arendell seconded the motion.  
Motion carried 3-0.

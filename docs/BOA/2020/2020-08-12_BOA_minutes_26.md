ITEM #1 – UP 20-002 – USE PERMIT, DISTRICT III:  Discussion and possible 
action on a re quest by Brad Mullner on their 20.00 -acre, RU-20 zoned parcel to 
allow for a Use Permit for a guest house, located on APN 404 -51-004B, a portion 
of Towns hip 11 North, Range 23 East, Section 1 of the Gila and Salt River

Cody Cooper presented the meeting, showing the area of the project, the site

not included in the graphic, but notification was given to the state. 
 
The applicant was available. The Chairman asked if it was an allowable use, Mr.

it and they were holding the permit for it until the Use Permit was granted. 
 
With no questions or comments, the Chairman called for a motion.

ITEM #2 – VAR 20-001 – VARIANCE, DISTRICT IV:  Discussion and possible 
action on a request by Paul Edward Crews on his 2.35 -acre, A-General zoned 
parcel to allow for the encroachment from the twenty (20) foo t required rear yard 
setback, to a ten (10) foot rear yard setback, for a n already existing accessory 
building on APN 212 -03-106B, a portion of Township 9 North, Range 22 East, 
Section 4 of the Gila and Salt River Meridian in the Show Low area. 
 
Mr. Cooper presented the item and gave the overview of the property, noting that 
the building was put on the property back in the 70 ’s, we have no record of the 
building being p ermitted, and the earliest the building was assessed was in the 
80’s.  Mr. Cooper also listed the basic variance requirements and noted that the 
variance would not really fix the main issues for the property , as it would remain 
out of compliance . Mr. Cooper further  noted that they had also contacted c ity of 
Showlow and that  recommended Denial. Chairman Arendell asked what would 
happen if the variance was denied, Mr. Cooper noted that he was not sure as the 
building would remain out of compliance. Commissioner Smith asked if the only 
resolution was to move the building, Mr. Cooper said they would ha ve to meet 
certain criteria as to be up to state requirements. 
 
Commissioner Smith  made a motion to deny.  Commissioner Hartman 
seconded the motion.  Motion passed with a vote of 3 to 0.
